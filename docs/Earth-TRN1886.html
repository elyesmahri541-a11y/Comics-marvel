History

Ghost Rider is no stranger to dark magic, nor is he afraid to take a chance and see what occult artifacts might be able to do for him. So when he is transformed by the evil, dark pages of the Darkhold, the Rider’s flaming skull becomes the fiery pumpkin of the legendary Headless Horseman. In the modern day, he rides the Earth now with a new purpose… [1]
