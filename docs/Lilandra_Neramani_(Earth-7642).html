History

Empress Lilandra seemingly had a similar history as her Earth-616 counterpart. Among the nations she warned about the Phoenix Force was The Citadel.[1]

Some time later, she sent a distress call to the Avengers, so they sent Captain Marvel, Hulk, and Vision to Chandilar. Unfortunately, Doctor Doom intercepted this distress call. Now knowing that the Avengers were down three heavy hitters, he planned to send his entire Doombot armada to New York, forcing the Avengers to hold auditions for replacement members, which led to many of the Muppets applying for the job.[2]
