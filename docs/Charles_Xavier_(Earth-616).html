Professor Charles Xavier is a mutant gifted with complex telepathic powers, a specialist in mutant biology and sociology, and the founder of the uncanny X-Men as Professor X.[40] With immense assistance from Dr. Moira MacTaggert, his life has become dedicated to fulfilling a dream of mutants and humans peacefully coexisting.[41][42][43] As a young man, he traveled around the world to better comprehend mutantkind, meeting a dear friend and ruthless rival, the man who would become known as Magneto.[44]

After becoming paraplegic following a conflict against the villain Lucifer in the Himalayas,[45] Xavier transformed his family's ancestral home into the Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters, a sanctuary for mutants to train and learn about their abilities.[46] Under his supervision, Professor X's students formed the first X-Men, a group who fights for a world that hates and fears them, guided by Xavier's dream. Through the years, the X-Men found themselves at odds with both humans and mutants with diverging ideologies, especially Magneto, who believed in mutant supremacy.[40] In different situations, Xavier demonstrated to be a shadowy figure, hiding secrets to achieve his goals.[47][48][49][50]

To ensure mutantkind's survival, Xavier assumed a harsher stance on the mutant-human conflict. Influenced by Moira and assisted by Magneto, he established a mutant nation on the living island of Krakoa, granting citizenship to all mutants. As the head of state of a mutant nation, he became responsible for a much larger community than solely the X-Men, which comprised thousands of mutants.[51] However, the species war prevailed, causing Krakoa to collapse.[52] Following a series of convoluted schemes and machinations,[50] Xavier sacrificed his own reputation and credibility to protect his species. As a consequence, he was placed under arrest in a catatonic state, ironically being held in the Graymalkin Prison, which was erected over the ruins of his former school.[53] After a controversial prison break, Xavier and his beloved Lilandra marched to outer space,[54] becoming involved in the political crisis affecting the Shi'ar Empire beside their daughter, Xandra.[55]

History

Early Childhood[]

Charles Francis Xavier was the child of geneticist Dr. Brian Xavier and his wife Sharon. Brian worked at a top secret facility in Alamogordo, New Mexico. There he worked with leading geneticists Kurt Marko, Irene Adler, Alexander Ryking, and Nathan Milbury.[18] The secret project Black Womb[12] was run by Milbury - secretly Nathaniel Essex - who was conducting experiments on human mutation.[18] During cellular mitosis, the embryo of Brian and Sharon Xavier split in half, forming twins. One child would eventually be born as Charles, while the other was his twin sister. Becoming self-aware in the womb, Xavier's twin sister attempted to kill him, and Charles lashed back with his budding mental powers, resulting in the twin being born stillborn; Charles was born minutes after, healthy.[56]. The other child, Cassandra Nova, survived and would resurface until decades later.[56]

Charles was experimented on by Dr. Milbury along with the other researcher's children, Carter Ryking,[18] Cain Marko, and Mortimer Toynbee among others.[12] Charles and Carter became fast friends during this time, often playing together on or near the Alamogordo facility where their fathers' worked.[57] Meanwhile, Millbury continued to experiment on Charles alone, covering his memories of these events.[58] Hi's end goal was creating vessels in which he could transfer his mind into in the event his physical body should die, a truth Xavier would not learn until many decades later.[59] Charles grew close to his mother, his budding telepathic abilities establishing a rapport with her. Unaware that he was doing this, Charles formed a strong bond with her due to the fact that he was unconsciously reading her mind.[46] Charles also developed a bond with the maid, Ms. Lafitte.[60]

When Charles was 9 years old, tragedy struck when an explosion claimed the life of Brian Xavier. Kurt Marko, having grown jealous of Brian's wealth, had a chance to save Brian's life but chose not to. He soon came to console Sharon and eventually took advantage of her grief, convincing her to marry him and giving Marko access to Brian's wealth. Hoping to provide Charles with a stable family life, his mother moved to the Xavier family's ancestral mansion in Westchester. Charles, thanks to his growing telepathic ability, began to suspect that Kurt was involved in the death of his father. Things for Charles got worse as Kurt's son Cain had moved in to live in the Xavier Estate. Cain was a bully who constantly picked on Charles.[61] Meanwhile, Kurt regularly abused Charles' mother, which Charles felt through his bond with her. But he was a silent witness as Sharon stayed with Kurt because she thought it what was best to raise her son.[46]

Living with Markos[]

However, Kurt cared for Charles, due to his intellect, much to the chagrin of Cain.[12] Eventually, Sharon Xavier died, leaving Charles to be raised by Marko. One night, Cain and Kurt got into an argument over money when Cain accused his father or murdering Brian. Overhearing this, Charles confronted the Markos for the truth. During the course of the argument, Cain threw a vial of chemicals, triggering an explosion.[61] When Kurt went to save Charles from the blaze, this caused more hatred from Cain.[62] In rescuing his child and step-child, Kurt Marko sustained fatal wounds. Dying, he revealed that he was too cowardly to save Charles' father years earlier. Revealing that he knew of Charles' telepathic abilities, he warned Charles to beware of Cain if he ever found out about his abilities.[61]

Left orphaned under the same roof, Charles and Cain lived as bitter enemies. Xavier devoted himself to exploring the full potential of his emerging psychic powers. As a side effect of his mutant abilities, Charles began to lose his hair and was completely bald by the time he was a teenager. As his mutant powers developed, his intellect increased as he was able to pull answers out of the minds of others. He excelled both academically and athletically thanks to his mind reading abilities. As Charles' grades and awards continued to grow, Cain's jealousy increased and the two often came to blows. Charles intelligence allowed him to graduate from high school at an accelerated rate. On his first day to college Cain offered to drive Charles to school and attempted to scare him by driving fast. When Cain lost control of the car, Charles used his telepathic powers to make Cain bail out while Charles was injured in the crash, although he wasn't seriously hurt and recovered. Consequently, Xavier permanently severed their relationship.[61] By the time that Charles was 16 he had graduated with honors from Harvard University, he also began attending Columbia University and went on to Oxford University in England, netting PhDs in Genetics, Biophysics, Psychology, Anthropology, and Psychiatry,[63] focusing on the complexities of mutations in society. His studies led him to envision the peaceful coexistence between mutants and humans.[64]

Meeting Moira[]

It was at Oxford that Charles met Moira Kinross when attending a fair. She approached him and sparked a discussion about his idealistic dream for the future of mutantkind. When she called Charles by name despite him not having introduced himself, Xavier inquired about her identity. Moira told him that they went ways back and invited Charles to read her mind.[43] Xavier telepathically accessed her memories and learned she Moira a mutant whose reincarnation powers had allowed her to live multiple lives, many of them alongside Charles himself. In all her lives, mutants had always been destined to perish, the revelation crushed Xavier. Moira then kissed Charles and told him that she had known both love and hate for him, and that not once did she see him change. After Xavier thanked her, Moira clarified that it was not a compliment and that in her tenth and seemingly final life cycle, they must abandon old ideals to unite mutantkind by breaking all rules.[65]

Xavier found in Moira an intellectual equal with whom he could openly discuss his theories and ambitions. He was introduced to her advisor, a revolutionary geneticist who was, in fact, Mister Sinister in disguise.[39] At that time, Moira was romantically involved with Lance Corporal Joe MacTaggert. Charles joined Moira and Joe on a tour across the United Kingdom and it seemed that Joe had all but won Moira over. However when they left a pub together, they got into an accident. Sensing the trouble with his mental powers, Xavier came to their aid, saving Moira from a raging river after a car accident caused by MacTaggert. From that moment, Charles began falling in love with Moira.[42] Eventually, the two were engaged to get married.[41] Engaged to Moira, Xavier experienced what he regarded as the happiest period of his life.[41] During a vacation on the French Riviera, Charles began sharing Moira his budding concepts of his dream where humans and mutants could live together in harmony.[66]

However, Charles vision was only academic at first, as he was only interested in lecturing and settling down to raise a family. He was confronted on campus by two mutants named Logan and Victor Creed, who upon learning that the United States government was actively imprisoning mutants, were seeking to band mutants together in a common group. Xavier flat out turned them down, something he would grow to regret in years to come.[63] After he had finished his schooling. Charles decided to prove that he was worthy to be with Moira and joined the military.[61]

Military Career[]

Charles saw action in the Siancong War,[61] where he was overwhelmed by the constant thoughts of the wounded and dying. Through sheer will power, Xavier managed to erect mental barriers to maintain his sanity and ward off outside thoughts.[67] His time in the military reunited Charles with his step-brother Cain and the two were put in the same unit. While out in a conflict zone, Cain attempted to desert his post. Seeking to prevent Marko from getting court-martialed, Xavier followed after him. The pair stumbled upon the long lost Temple of Cyttorak. Inside, Cain found the Crimson Gem of Cyttorak. Reading its inscription of power, Cain grabbed it and began transforming into the powerful Juggernaut. However this transformation started a cave in. While Xavier managed to escape, Cain was buried under tons of rubble where he would remain trapped for decades.[61]

During his tours of duty Charles received a letter from Moira notifying him that she was breaking off their engagement and asked him not to come looking for her. Charles, heartbroken and angry, burned the letter and continued his military career.[46] Charles became a highly regarded member of the military's search and rescue team, secretly utilizing his telepathic abilities to help his fellow soldiers, earning him the name "The Good Shepherd". Charles however began acting recklessly, growing too reliant on his telepathic powers to get him through danger zones, almost not caring if he lived or died.[42] During his time in the military, Charles fought beside Carmen Pryde and Logan. While Xavier disliked Logan, he and Pryde became fast friends, and when Carmen was scheduled to return to the states, he invited Charles to be the best man at his wedding. Pryde was disappointed when he learned that Charles signed on for another tour of duty after Moira ended their engagement. During a mission, Xavier lost his crew and suffered injuries that took him out off combat duty.[39]

Returning to the United States, Charles found reports of growing mutant hysteria, but the government was covering up instances of mutant activity as hoaxes. Learning that he was flagged by the military for his high I.Q. scores, Xavier used his mental powers to infiltrate the Pentagon in Washington, D.C. There he learned that the military was working with inventor Bolivar Trask to deal with the so-called "mutant menace". Using his mental powers as a influence, Xavier made his superiors give him an honorable discharge from the military. He also probed their minds to learn more about the FBI's own mutant projects.[68] Xavier was leaving base just as the military - along with their mutant cohort Virus - were launching an attack on Logan's group of mutants with an army of robotic Sentinels. When Xavier was stopped by FBI agent Fred Duncan for a ride, Xavier willingly provided it.[69] Witnessing as Logan's mutants either were slaughtered or fled, Xavier used his mental powers to help many escape. His interference in matters were partially responsible for the government pulling the plug on the Sentinel Project as being too costly, resulting in Agent Duncan being the only agent assigned to investigate mutants.[70]

His heart still broken after his break up with Moira, Xavier began travelling the world, wandering across the Mediterranean before settling on Kirinos, where he assisted the local population, finding the experience deeply therapeutic. He continued to Cairo, Egypt, where he was pickpocketed in the city's main bazaar by a young thief. Attempting to telepathically intercept her, Xavier was overwhelmed by a powerful psychic assault that drove him to a nearby saloon. There, he encountered Amahl Farouk, the Shadow King, the first evil mutant he ever met. After besting his foe in an astral duel, Xavier understood the deadly potential of mutant abilities when wielded for evil. This led him to dedicate his life to promoting peace and dream of a harmonious world in which humans were protected from those who abused their powers while innocent mutants were defended from oppression.[41]

Israel[]

Xavier began utilizing his mental powers to help those suffering from mental trauma in Haifa, Israel, reuniting with an old friend, psychiatrist Daniel Shomron, who directed a clinic for Holocaust survivors suffering from severe trauma. There Charles began working with an Auschwitz survivor calling himself Magnus. Over the following weeks, the two engaged in complex discussions about hypothetical scenarios about humanity's reaction to a super-powered race of individuals. Whereas Xavier was optimistic about peaceful coexistence, Magnus was shaped by the horrors of intolerance and believed that humanity would inevitably persecute any group it perceived as different. Xavier kept his mutant nature a secret, unaware that Magnus himself as also a mutant. Xavier also began treating a young woman Gabrielle Haller who had gone into a catatonic state due to traumatic experiences in a concentration camp. Xavier began treating her mind with his telepathic powers, eventually willing her out of her catatonic state.[44] Xavier and Gabrielle eventually formed a relationship,[44] a bond which Xavier secretly willed with his mental powers.[39] As this relationship blossomed it's future fruit came to haunt them in the past, when the medical facility took in a strange amnesic patient. Unknown to anyone at the time, this "John Doe" was really David Haller, the future son of Charles and Gabrielle. Both Magnus and Xavier learned that the John Doe had a series of mutant powers of his own when he displayed the ability to probe Magnus' mind, create psychic images, and produce pyrokinetics.[71]

Xavier and Magnus spent time in a bar where they got into a brawl with the locals when they began teasing a homeless amputee. After the fight Xavier answered a distressing call from Gabrielle calling for help, the pair arrived to find that "John Doe's" memories had returned and that he was plotting to murder Magnus.[72] David was opposed by members of the X-Men from the future. Despite the X-Men's best efforts, David managed to grab a hold of Magnus, but as he was about to make the killing blow, Xavier was poised to save his friend.[73] Before David could land a lethal blow with a psi-knife, he was stopped by yet another time-traveler Bishop. By stabbing the psi-blade in himself, Bishop showed David the future in which David accidentally killed his father, leading to the conquest of the Earth by Apocalypse. The resulting energies dissipated both Bishop and David, the future X-Men were returned to their own time and all the damage caused restored. Xavier, Magnus, and Gabrielle's memories of the encounter quickly faded.[74]

Gabrielle became the target of the terrorist organization Hydra who sought to learn the secret of long lost Nazi gold. They succeeded in kidnapping Gabby and bringing her to a remote location in Kenya, where Hydra's leader Baron Strucker was able to locate the gold. Xavier and Magnus came to her rescue, easily defeating Strucker and his Hydra minions, and thus revealing their mutant abilities to each other. Magnus considered killing von Strucker, but Xavier stopped him. In the aftermath of the battle, Magnus was convinced that humans and mutants could not live in peace together and left with the Nazi gold to fund his campaign against the human race.[44] Eventually, Charles and Gabrielle drifted apart as Charles found more desire to travel. He and Gabrielle ended their relationship unaware that Gabrielle was pregnant with Xavier's son. Gabrielle kept this a secret and quietly raised the child with Xavier's former colleague Daniel Shomron whom Gabrielle married.[75] This played into Moira's plan for her and and Xavier to conceive exceptionally powerful mutants.[65]

Paralyzed[]

In search of other of his kind, Xavier relied solely on his powers and rumors, eventually finding Namor, the avenging son of Atlantis, and convincing him to join his cause after revealing his mutant heritage. The pair had little luck, and Xavier constantly used his telepathy to temper Namor's volatile rage. Ultimately tracking down an escaped mutant named Genus, Xavier considered using his powers to forcibly transform humans into mutants, earning Namor's disapproval. Before the two could resolve their disagreement, Genus was killed by local officers. Afterwards, Xavier attempted to repair Namor's psyche, only to shatter his mind and end their alliance. Xavier continued his journey alone[76] and, after years of formative experiences, reunited with Moira to discuss using her research to advance his crusade of finding and protecting mutants. She initially rejected the proposal, warning Xavier's idealism could eventually give way to corruption.[67]

Xavier had now met a number of other mutants and had grown concerned, and began formulating his first ideas to gather mutants and train them in the use of their powers for the greater good. Tracking down Moira MacTaggert, he asked her to allow him to research her own files on mutants, but she was reluctant to do so.[67] Xavier resumed his world travels, and his telepathic powers drew him to an ancient temple where he encountered Gracie Gamble and Cuckoo of the Destine Clan while they were attempting to stop the extra-dimensional creature Synraith from crossing over into Earth's dimension. The trio combined their mental powers to send the Synraith back, and in an attempt to protect Xavier, Gracie erased his memories of the event.[77] During these travels Xavier learned of another telepath named Tobias Messenger who was recruiting mutants for his own cause, but Messenger disappeared before Charles had the opportunity to meet him.[78] Xavier's travels brought him to Tibet where he discovered a mysterious walled off city deep in the Himalayas. Upon further investigation he discovered that the city was enthralled by the alien invader Luciferthe advance scout for an alien invasion. Xavier thwarted his plans by inciting a rebellion against the would-be world conqueror. In retaliation, Lucifer dropped a massive stone block onto Xavier, leaving him trapped beneath the rubble and paralyzing Xavier from the waist down.[45] Xavier called out mentally for help and was rescued by a a young mutant rebel who had heard his telepathic cries and carried Xavier to medical aid, she would become his ally Sage.[79]

Taken to a hospital in India, Charles was told by doctors that he would never walk again. Xavier fell into a deep depression over the loss of his leg mobility. He was nursed back to health and put through physiotherapy with a young American nurse Amelia Voght.[46] Amelia pushed Xavier to the limits to rehabilitate himself and find a new will to live.[80] Three months later, the couple moved into an apartment together in Bombay. There Xavier began working on schematics for a mutant detecting computer he called Cerebro. Voght, a mutant herself, became upset when she learned of this until Xavier revealed that he too was a mutant. Although Amelia remained with Xavier, she was uneasy with the notion of gathering mutants and training them to battle for peaceful coexistence between humans and mutants, content enough to live out her life as a mutant in anonymity. Nonetheless, Amelia returned with Xavier to the United States to start up Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters.[46] Also during this period, Charles worked with Dr. Karl Lykos and Moira MacTaggart on "Project Mutant" but they eventually cut ties and pursued their separate careers. Moira maintained the facility they worked at for years.[81][82]

The First X-Men[]

Xavier was contacted by John and Elaine Grey whose daughter Jean had developed telepathic and telekinetic powers but was traumatized when she was in the mind of her best friend as she died after being fatally run over by a car. After pulling Jean out of her shell, Xavier attempted to help her control her telepathic abilities.[83] Xavier also began giving lectures on mutations, becoming one of the leading experts on the subject.[84] Having collected extensive data on mutants whose gifts had manifested, Professor Xavier shared with Moira his intentions of creating a task force to train youngsters and carry on his dream of peaceful coexistence.[85] By this point Xavier also had Moira MacTaggert working with him in tutoring Jean Grey, while also testing out his early prototypes for Cerebro.[86] Through these early tests both Charles and Jean made contact with another young mutant named Scott Summers. Jean and Scott made a connection, but Xavier chose not to reveal themselves as anymore as part of a vivid dream at the time.[87] Xavier continued honing Jean's telekinetic abilities until she developed moderate control.[88]

Voght stayed at Xavier's side, partly because she loved him, but also to persuade him to abandon what she believed to be nonsensical ideals.[46] Xavier became aware of two other mutants, Pietro and Wanda Maximoff, and offered them to the chance to return with him to America to hone their powers. Pietro refused on their behalf, but they were recruited by Magnus - now calling himself Magneto - who was also gathering mutants to his cause.[89] Learning of Magneto's intentions, Xavier and Amelia traveled to the long abandoned Auschwitz concentration camp to confront Magneto on the anniversary of his time there during World War II. There Xavier attempted a final plea to Magneto to work with him to bring about peaceful coexistence between humans and mutants. However by this time Magneto had given in fully to his hatred of the human race and refused to listen. Shielded from Xavier's telepathy by a specially designed helmet, Magneto was adamant to fight for mutant supremacy. Xavier warned that such a course would mirror the violence Magneto himself had endured during the Holocaust. Upon leaving, Magneto warned Xavier that when they next met, it would be as enemies.[90] Xavier continued to teach Jean to control her powers, making great progress. However Jean relapsed with her telepathy training when at a mall. Xavier decided to focus on her telekinetic powers until she matured more, and shut down her own telepathy for the time being.[91]

Xavier decided to take a more active role of recruiting mutants to his side and reached out with whatever resources available to him. He reconnected with Fred Duncan at the FBI's division on mutant affairs after increase reports of anti-mutant hysteria. When reports of Scott Summers' powers flaring out of control came out, Xavier convinced Agent Duncan to allow him to find and train the boy and other mutants to his cause, transforming his family's ancestral mansion home into a mutant academy, where they could master their powers and receive conventional education, in service of humanity.[92] Using his mental powers to track down Scott, Xavier was too late to stop him from being recruited by another mutant, a crook named Jack Winters.[93] Winters attempted to exploit Scott in order to access a cyclotron that would transform his body into living diamond. Xavier tracked the two rogue mutants and engaged Winters in telepathic battle.[94] Xavier lost the first bout when he was seemingly buried alive, allowing Jack to access the cyclotron and transform himself into pure diamond. Having survived, Xavier saved Scott from arriving security and convinced the boy to help him stop Winters.[95] Now calling himself the Living Diamond, Winters attacked both Scott and Xavier. Unable to stop their foe on their own, Xavier guided Scott on how to operate the cyclotron, bombarding Winters with too much energy and causing him to implode.[96]

Taking Scott back to his school, Xavier made him the first of his new team, the X-Men, giving him the code name Cyclops.[96] By this point Amelia had enough of Xavier recruiting mutants to his cause and begged him to walk away from it all to be with her. When he could not choose, she attempted to walk out on him and in a panic he tried to stop her with his mental powers. Horrified by what he had done, Xavier released his control from her, but the damage had already been done and Amelia Vought walked out of Charles life.[46] He also secretly recruited the young girl who had rescued him in the Himalayas, forging for her the identity of "Tessa" as an undercover spy placed as the secretary of Sebastian Shaw, a wealthy mutant industrialist.[97] Charles assisted the young mutant Jamie Madrox by maintaining close contact with his parents and providing them with resources to keep his mutant abilities under control.[98]

Forming the X-Men[]

Xavier became aware of another mutant, the wealthy Warren Worthington III, who had feathered wings that enabled him to fly. Unable to get to Worthington right away, Xavier telepathically guided the youth when he was being attacked by a religious fanatic who believed his "angelic" mutation was an affront to god.[99] The next mutant Charles actively sought out was young Robert Drake, who could control and manipulate ice. When Drake was arrested after freezing a bully in a block of ice, Xavier and Cyclops traveled to Fort Washington. Learning that Bobby was in jail and a lynch mob sought to hang him, Xavier sent Cyclops to rescue the boy.[100] Bobby refused to go with Cyclops and the two eventually came to blows[101] until the mob caught up with them.[101] Xavier intervened, using his mental powers to erase everyone's memories of Bobby's ice powers and invited him to join the school. Bobby agreed, and he became the second X-Man, codenamed Iceman.[102]

Xavier began focusing on Warren Worthington once again, who had now began a solo career as a costumed hero calling himself the Avenging Angel. Tracking Angel down, Xavier sent both Cyclops and Iceman to recruit him just after the Avenging Angel succeeded in recovering a stolen vial of a radioactive isotope.[103] However the isotope had an effect on the Angel's mind and he battled the two X-Men. With the isotope reaching critical mass, Xavier successfully contacted Warren telepathically and convinced him to toss the isotope high in the air where it harmlessly exploded. His sanity restored, Warren also agreed to join the X-Men, re-christening himself as simply the Angel.[104] Meanwhile, Xavier began petitioning the Grey family to allow their daughter to join his school.[91]

Charles began reading about Duffee, Illinois high school football star Henry McCoy whose enhanced agility and large hands and feet identified him as a mutant.[105] Hank's abilities also attracted the attention of the terrorist Conquistador who sought to utilize those powers for his conquering the world. The Conquistador captured Hank and his parents before Xavier and his other students could reach him.[106] Charles managed to track Hank when he was forced to break into a nuclear research facility to steal a newly invented solar power device.[107] Xavier sent his X-Men to rescue Hank and his family from the Conquistador's lair. The rescue was a success and the Conquistador was seemingly slain in the ensuing blast when the solar power device overloaded.[108] Like the others, Hank agreed to transfer to Xavier's school and join the X-Men taking the code name Beast. As with Iceman, Xavier used his mental powers to erase all knowledge of Hank's mutant powers from the people in town.[108]

With Jean Grey joining the group, Xavier's team of X-Men was complete. He began training them to hone their powers in his specially constructed Danger Room, much to the chagrin of Moira MacTaggert who questioned Xavier's motivations. Xavier also decided to look into the past of Scott Summers, suspecting that he had been in contact with other mutants prior to his joining the X-Men.[67] Early on there was trouble when Hank began to get upset that Xavier used his powers to erase all memory from the people he grew up with.[13] Hank became angry at this decision, but Xavier eventually convinced Hank that it was for the good of those he knew and loved, as well as his own personal safety.[109] In training, Xavier taught Beast to never underestimate his adversaries.[110] Discovering another mutant, Xavier and Moira attempted to convince the wealthy Winston Frost to enroll his daughter Emma into his school. When Winston would not consider it, Xavier telepathically contacted Emma and tried to convince her to join his cause. However she refused when Charles offered to alter her father's mind to change his mind, and Xavier and MacTaggert left empty handed.[111] Xavier and Moira began comparing notes, including files on foreign mutants Ororo Munroe, Peter Rasputin, and Kurt Wagner. Xavier began planning to try and recruit them into his X-Men once he had a more international reach.[85]

Early Missions[]

Now calling himself Professor X, Xavier was ready to reveal the X-Men to the world. After months of preparation, Magneto made his first move against humanity by seizing control of a military base at Cape Citadel with the intention of launching missiles against human targets. The threat of Magneto became very real, and Xavier called upon his X-Men to prepare for their first battle against his one-time friend. Through Xavier's guidance, the X-Men succeeded in stopping Magneto from taking over the Cape Citadel missile base, forcing the master of magnetism to flee.[40] Due to his exemplary work in the field, Xavier selected Cyclops to act as the field leader for the team, despite Scott's fears that he was not worth due to the lack of control he had over his powers.[67] When the mutant teleporter Vanisher began stealing government secrets, Professor X offered the X-Men's aid to the government to stop him. Xavier himself defeated the Vanisher by using his mental powers to cut off his access to his powers, allowing the X-Men to easily capture him and his minions.[14]

When the Angel had another personality shift following exposure to radiation unleashed by a test by Stark Industries, Xavier allowed Iron Man to deal with the danger posed by the Angel until he reverted back to normal.[112] Xavier agreed to return the favor when Iron Man sought the X-Men's aid in tracking down his rogue Avengers teammate the Hulk. However the Hulk ultimately evaded capture before the X-Men could get involved.[113] Xavier had the X-Men meet the supremely powerful Sentry, assisting him in defeating the General who was attempting to steal nuclear weapons to launch against the nation of France.[114]Professor X and the X-Men were later forced to forget the existence of the Sentry.[115]

Xavier located another mutant, the immovable Blob who was working as part of a carnival freak show. However Charles learned the error of welcoming the Blob in with open arms when he turned out to be uninterested in joining the X-Men and attacked the school. While the X-Men kept the Blob and his army of carnies at bay, Xavier completed a device that allowed him to wipe out all memory of the X-Men's headquarters and secret identities from the mind of the Blob and his cohorts.[116] While in a training session, Xavier and the X-Men were briefly surprised by the sudden appearance of a medieval knight, but it disappeared.[117] After more training,[118] Charles once more challenged Magneto, this time on the Astral Plane after, Magneto and his newly recruited Brotherhood of Evil Mutants conquered the South American nation of Santo Marco. Xavier deployed the X-Men who battled the Brotherhood, who were forced to flee. Xavier threw himself at a boobytrapped door to save his students from the blast, and led them to believe that he had lost his mental powers as a result.[17]

When the X-Men were lured into a trap by Magneto and were taken aboard his Asteroid M headquarters, they managed to escape without the aid of Xavier. Charles then revealed to his students that he feigned his lost powers as a final test to see if his students could handle themselves without his aid.[119] Professor X was enthralled by the Puppet Master and the Mad Thinker who used Xavier to order the X-Men to attack the Fantastic Four. Although the two teams were forced to fight each other, Xavier broke free from the Puppet Master's control and exposed the entire ruse.[120]

Learning that the Sub-Mariner was also a mutant, both Xavier and Magneto sought to recruit him to their respective sides. Magneto succeeded in summoning Namor first, ultimately leading to another battle between the X-Men and the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants. Realizing that Magneto intended to make Namor a subordinate, he refused to join. The X-Men drove Magneto and his Brotherhood away, but Namor had no interest in joining the X-Men either.[121] When the X-Men noticed the Human Torch's call to Spider-Man, Xavier ordered them to ignore it as it did not involve them.[122] Charles dispatched the X-Men to battle the Brotherhood again when they attempted to recruit Thor into their ranks, mistakenly believing him to be a mutant. When the X-Men arrived, Magneto and his minions fled.[123]

Xavier and the X-Men found themselves pulled forward in time by Access to a future where the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants had teamed up with the other-dimensional being Darkseid and his Apokoliptians who were attempting to conquer their two universes. The X-Men assisted the Avengers as well as the Justice League of America and the Teen Titans, heroes from Darkseid's dimension.[124] While Access battled Magneto and Darkseid aboard Asteroid M with an army of Amalgamated heroes, Charles, the Beast, and Cyclops remained on Earth to deal with the various threats there. When Access and his allies defeated Darkseid and Magneto, all the heroes and their foes were returned to their proper times and dimensions. Upon their return home, Charles and the X-Men's memory of events eventually faded.[125]

Professor X and his students found themselves pulled through time again by a would-be assassin named Sidestep who was hired to assassinate the group of British superheroes Excalibur on behalf of Doctor Doom. To this end, each member of the X-Men had their memories altered to fit the era in which they were placed and forced to do battle with a member of Excalibur. In Xavier's case he was transported to the apocalyptic future of Earth-811 where mutant hunting Sentinel robots dominated North America. Fused to one of these robots, he was forced to fight the Shi'ar warrior Cerise. Xavier was no match for the warrior, and they were brought back to the present. There the X-Men and Excalibur battled it out, until Xavier learned the truth by reading the mind of Excalibur's leader Nightcrawler. When Nightcrawler damaged Sidestep's time travelling harness, both teams were returned to their proper times and places, with the X-Men's memories of events being erased.[126]

First Graduation[]

After his X-Men had completed their educational and training requirements, Professor X threw a private graduation ceremony for them.[127] After the festivities, Charles showed Cyclops the secret chamber where he had completed his first version of Cerebro. He revealed that he was embarking on a secret mission and had selected Cyclops to run the school in his absence.[127] This mission was to track down Xavier's old foe Lucifer, who was active somewhere in Europe. While searching for him, Xavier was contacted by Scott who told him that the Beast was leaving the group. Xavier assured Scott that he did all he could, Beast soon rejoined the team on his own.[128] When Professor X finally located Lucifer, he called for his X-Men to join him in Europe. Quickly captured inside Lucifer's stronghold in the Balkans, Charles learned that Lucifer had armed a bomb that could blow up all of Europe and that it was specially rigged to his heart, so that the bomb would go off if his heart was stopped. When the Avengers arrived to investigate the scene, Charles had his X-Men hold the Avengers at bay until he was able to incapacitate Lucifer with a mental bolt. Charles stopped the first between the two teams and explained the situation. With the help of his students, Xavier disarmed the bomb. When Lucifer revived shortly thereafter, Xavier allowed him to go free as he had been thoroughly defeated.[2]

With the next stage of their education and training complete, Xavier provided the X-Men with alternate costumes that they occasionally wore in the field. He also developed an addition to Cerebro which included an artificial intelligence that taught his students whenever he was absent or busy. When Xavier began detecting a strange force that was taking control of plants and animals near the mansion, he and the X-Men tracked the source near Greenland. There they prevented scientists who were studying global warming from drilling into the domain of ancient creatures that used to be the dominant species on Earth and were in the final cycle of their lifespan. This discovery convinced the scientists to drill elsewhere for their research.[129] During a training session, Jean attempted to get the other members of the team motivated by offering to go out on a date with whoever won a race to the Danger Room, a competition that was quickly quashed by both the Professor and Cyclops.[130]

Charles and Scott ventured out to State University to interview students that were potentially mutants. Although this proved a fruitless endeavor, the two crossed paths with the Fantastic Four.[131] Both Charles and his X-Men were invited to the engagement party of the Fantastic Four's Mister Fantastic and the Invisible Girl. Since Charles was keeping his ties to the X-Men a secret at the time, he and he students had to act as though they did not know each other.[132] During a lull in activity, Charles allowed the X-Men to investigate reports of a savage man running loose in Antarctica. This led to the X-Men discovering the Savage Land and finding an ally in it's ruler Ka-Zar.[133] Deciding to take a vacation, Charles took his students to the Florida Everglades and decided to pay a visit to his old colleague Curt Connors. Learning that Connors had once more transformed into the Lizard, he had Angel, Beast and Iceman assist him in capturing the Lizard and reverting him back to human form. Xavier also used this opportunity to orchestrate events so that Scott and Jean could have time alone to facilitate their growing feelings for each other.[134] When the rest of the team came down with the flu, Charles sent Cyclops on a lone mission to the Blue Ridge Mountains of West Virginia to investigate disappearances in the area. With Xavier's guidance, Cyclops rescued the missing people from a cave dwelling mutant known only as Frederick.[135]

Brotherhood Disassembled[]

Charles became aware of what appeared to be a new mutant and sent his X-Men to make first contact. However, this new entity turned out be the cosmic being Stranger who had come to Earth to study mutants. During the ensuing clash, the Stranger transformed Brotherhood member Mastermind into dense matter and captured Magneto and the Toad to examine.[136] Charles had Mastermind's inert body stored at the mansion until he could find a means of dealing with it.[137] Charles' step-brother Cain finally freed himself and returned to the mansion, now calling himself the Juggernaut. Charles had his X-Men set up defenses and revealed his relationship to the Juggernaut. Their foe crashed through all the defenses and easily bowled over Xavier's students.[61]

Unable to attack the Juggernaut's mind thanks to a psi-proof helmet, Xavier contacted the Human Torch for assistance. While the X-Men kept the Juggernaut at bay, the Torch used his flame powers to remove Cain's helmet, leaving him vulnerable to attack and eventually defeat at the hands of Xavier. After thanking the Torch, Xavier erased his memories of his encounter with the X-Men and the location of their headquarters.[138] Charles contained the Juggernaut on the mansion grounds, keeping him in a state of coma.[139] Eventually, the X-Men recovered from their injuries and Charles decided that they should all take a brief break from adventuring.[140] Xavier took the time to search for a mutant he detected in Africa but fell prey to an ancient cavern trap that sapped the will of those trapped inside until they died. Calling out to the X-Men for help, they eventually located and rescued Xavier with the aid of the retired 1950s adventurer Gorilla Man.[141]

Marvel Girl had developed a friendship with the Scarlet Witch who was trying to reform herself after leaving the Brotherhood. When the Witch was secretly tapped by the spy agency S.H.I.E.L.D., Xavier was able to locate her and send his X-Men to help. In turn they learned that the Witch was assisting the Black Widow in stopping Hydra from stealing a new experimental sub. Charles used his mental powers to block Hydra brainwashing which led to the organizations defeat.[142] Charles joined his students and Thor in tracking down members of the Midgard Vanir who had kidnapped Iceman in a plot to revive the Frost Giant Ymir on Earth.[143] Mastermind's consciousness expanded allowed him to use his illusion casting powers at great distances for a short period of time. Seeking to get revenge against the X-Men, he used his powers while the Professor was away to trick the X-Men into going on a "mission" to Monster Isle.[144] When Charles returned to the mansion and found his charges missing, he managed to tack them down and expose Mastermind's plot, defeating him in mental combat. Xavier had Mastermind's inert body removed to a safer location where he could do no further harm.[145]

Charles continued to test his X-Men's abilities and eventually came to the conclusion that both Marvel Girl and Iceman had nearly unlimited potential, using the term "Omega level" to classify them.[146] Xavier also began investigating a young mutant Sonny Baredo whose mutant powers wiped out his entire family, but the boy disappeared before Xavier could officially get him into his school.[147] Both Charles and the X-Men were invited to the wedding of Mister Fantastic and the Invisible Girl, once again having to pretend to not know each other. The wedding was crashed by an army of super-villains under the control of Doctor Doom. Xavier mentally guided his students as they fought off attacks from the Mole Man and his Moloids,[148] Batroc the Leaper, the Executioner, and the monster Grogoom.[149] Eventually Mister Fantastic dispelled the villains with a time displacement device provided to him by the Watcher and the wedding went on uninterrupted.[148]

Anti-Mutant Hysteria[]

Anti-mutant hysteria had reached new highs at this point thanks, in part, to the bigoted fear mongering of inventor Bolivar Trask. Seeking to try and dispel some of the prejudice, Charles organized a televised debate with Trask. Trask in turn used the debate to unveil his mutant hunting Sentinel robots to the public. However the machines turned on him, deciding that the only way to protect humanity was by ruling it.[140] Charles summoned his X-Men, but they were too late to stop the Sentinels from absconding with Trask, although one of the robots suddenly deactivated in the battle. Before they could determine why, Charles led his X-Men out to the secret base of the Sentinels only to find it heavily fortified with powerful weapons.[140] During the initial attack on the Sentinels fortress, Beast and Iceman were captured. While the other X-Men renewed their attack, Xavier probed the base and learned that the Sentinel's leader, Master Mold was probing the Beast's mind to learn what he could about the X-Men. Xavier prevented the probes from revealing anymore before his astral form was driven back by Master Mold, who detected his presence.[105] With his X-Men still trapped inside, Charles decided to determine what stopped the Sentinel back at the TV station and used his mental powers to commandeer a ride back into the city. There he discovered that a giant crystal display at the Crystal Products Building was jamming the signal to the Sentinel, he made arrangements to have it flown to the Sentinels' headquarters. While Charles had the police and military jam the Sentinels, Bolivar - having realized the errors of his ways - sacrificed his life to seemingly destroy Master Mold. The X-Men also managed to escape, but were injured while the fled, particularly Iceman who was in critical condition.[150]

Professor X accompanied the X-Men to the hospital in order to ensure that their secret identities were not exposed, although he was incredibly careful not to led the authorities or hospital staff suspect that he had ties with the group. When they X-Men became aware that Warren's parents were going to be making an impromptu visit to the school, Charles and his students tried to return home before their arrival. This led to everyone but the recovering Iceman to be captured by Magneto, who had escaped the Stranger's world. After releasing Xavier and his charges high into the atmosphere in a balloon, he captured Warren's parents.[151] Charles used his mental powers to guide Iceman in stopping Magneto from using Warren's parents as genetic templates to create an army of mutant slaves. All the while, he also guided the X-Men into getting back to ground while also contacting the Stranger to inform him of Magneto's escape. Sure enough, after the X-Men arrived, the Stranger, followed forcing Magneto to flee.[152]

Non-Mutant Threats[]

The purpose of the Worthington's visit was to inform Angel that his Aunt Mimi had gone missing while exploring Brazil. Angel eventually tracked her down in the hidden domain Land of Mists and decided to stay there. When the other X-Men returned upset that Worthington chose to stay, Charles tried his best to console the team.[153] By this time Charles began working with FBI Agent Baker who seemed to occasionally replace Fred Duncan as liaison for the X-Men. He also got into contact with his colleague Able Stack and agreed to have Stack bring his robot X-50 to interact with his students. Xavier dispatched the X-Men and X-50 to Mount Saint Helen's where there were sightings of Lava Men.[154] Xavier warned his students when it became known that the X-series robots were proving to be unstable. The Lava Men were eventually stopped, but X-50 was destroyed in the resulting battle.[155]

Charles and his X-Men crossed paths with the former Frightful Four member Medusa. Charles offered to assist her in recovering her lost memories but found doing so difficult. Suspecting that she was a mutant, Charles invited her to join the X-Men. When her former leader, the Wizard sought to bring Medusa back into the fold, Charles realized that he had been influencing her mind with his helmet. After Cyclops destroyed it, Medusa was freed from the Wizard's influence and went on her own. Charles revealed a surprise to his students, revealing that Angel had returned to them.[156] When Jean was struggling to find a role model in the male dominant group, Charles arranged for Marvel Girl to shadow the Invisible Girl of the Fantastic Four, assisting them in defeating the Mad Thinker and his Awesome Android.[157]

The X-Men were attacked by Calvin Rankin who had the power to mimic the abilities of those in close proximity to him. Ranking attacked the X-Men in the costumed identity of Mimic after learning their identities. Charles and the X-Men followed after Mimic when he kidnapped Jean and fled to the secret cave laboratory he lived. Here Mimic hoped to use the X-Men's stolen powers to open up a buried device invented by his father that Mimic assumed would make his power duplication abilities permanent. As it turned out this was not the case and Xavier allowed himself to be taken hostage while Mimic used the device which stripped him of his powers instead. Charles erased Calvin's memories of his encounter with the X-Men and set him free.[158]

Lucifer resurfaced and manipulated the Blob and Unus to team up and commit crimes dressed like the X-Men. When Xavier attempted to learn the identity of the Blob and Unus' benefactor he was incapacitated with with a mental defense weapon. After the Beast developed a device to block the effects of the mental distorter, Xavier led the X-Men after Lucifer.[45] While Lucifer attempted to activate his Dominus computer to enslave the Earth, the X-Men attempted to infiltrate his fortress the Professor was captured by Lucifer's robots. Learning that only the robots could utilized the Dominus computer, Xavier instructed his students to destroy them, leaving Lucifer with no means of activating his computer. Unimpressed, Lucifer's superiors banished him to another dimension, ending his threat to Earth.[159] Charles and the X-Men were asked to assist the military in capturing the Hulk who was sighted in the Pacific Northwest. However they learned from the Hulk's sidekick Rick Jones that the military sought to weaponize the Hulk. Feeling sympathetic for the Hulk's alter ego of Bruce Banner, Xavier and the X-Men let him go and acted as though their search was fruitless.[160] Charles assisted Marvel Girl and the Scarlet Witch in investigating strange thefts at a nearby museum, exposing the Mole Man as the one responsible.[161]

In order to better train the X-Men, Xavier designed the robot Colosso to hone their powers. After their training session Charles allowed his students to have a brief break from their studies and training. While they were gone, Charles showed a brief moment of weakness to lament over his disability. While the X-Men were gone, they were targeted by Maggia leader Count Nefaria who enlisted the aid of the Scarecrow, Planetman, the Eel, the Porcupine, and the Unicorn in order to capture the youths. When Nefaria offered the X-Men to join his organization, they refused and were taken prisoner while he targeted Washington D.C.[162] The Count sealed Washington in an impenetrable dome and demanded a huge ransom to release it, framing he X-Men in the process. Upon learning this, Charles offered his "expertise" to the military in order to get close enough to Washington to send his Astral form. Contacting his X-Men, Charles instructed them to play along.[163]

Having developed special leg braced to allow for limited mobility, Charles infiltrated the dome when Nefaria lowered the shield to allow the X-Men to collect the ransom. While the super-villains fought over the money, Xavier infiltrated Nefaria's base of operations and deactivated his device. Tricking Nefaria and Unicorn into thinking they escaped with the ransom, Charles mentally alerted the Coast Guard as they attempted to flee the country.[163] Later, when the X-Men battled the insane entomologist Locust, Xavier put on his leg braces again and disguised himself as a "wise old man" to try and get the Locust to see the errors of his way. When this failed, the X-Men foiled his plan to destroy America's wheat supply with giant insects.[164] Back at the mansion, Charles began working to enhance Cerebro some more and got caught in a mental loop between himself and the computer. In order to save himself, Xavier pulled the sleeping X-Men into his mind where they worked to free him.[60]

After assisting his students in rescuing children from a burning orphanage, Charles returned home to what was eventually determined to be a false reading of a new mutant in New York. The X-Men were deployed and encountered El Tigre, an explorer trying to find the second half of the amulet of Ahau god Kukulkan. Xavier attempted to assist them with his leg braces, but they malfunctioned, and the team had to leave Charles behind.[165] Although El Tigre was able to now block Charles' mental probes, he managed to locate the lost city where their foe escaped to. The X-Men went there and eventually defeated El Tigre/Kukulkan, stripping him of his powers.[166]

Factor Three[]

Charles became aware of a new mutant menace that threatened them and began actively seeking out new members to boost the X-Men's ranks. Charles contacted the Scarlet Witch and Quicksilver, but they turned down Charles' offer as they were with the Avengers. He also had the Beast and Iceman attempt to recruit Spider-Man, who also turned down their offer. The Puppet Master resurfaced and attempted to take control of Xavier's mind, but Charles had already placed mental blocks to prevent this from happening again. When the X-Men found their new member, a newly restored Mimic, they were shocked when Xavier arbitrarily made Mimic deputy leader of the group. After the Puppet Master possessed Mimic, Charles sent the X-Men out to defeat the Puppet Master, freeing Calvin from his control.[167] Charles began training the X-Men and Mimic in order to prepare them for the coming battle. Their foes turned out to be a mysterious organization called Factor Three who sent their operatives Banshee and the Ogre to attack the school. As he was being forced to work with Factor Three thanks to a headdress rigged with a bomb, Banshee went out of his way to sabotage the Ogre's attack and ultimately the X-Men defeated the Ogre and freed Banshee from Factor Three's control.[168]

As Charles tried to learn more about Factor Three, the X-Men accidentally revived the Super-Adaptoid who had hidden itself on the Xavier estate. Seeking to turn the human race into androids like itself, the Adaptoid attempted to start with the X-Men. Mimic made the ultimate sacrifice when he tricked the Super-Adaptoid try to duplicate the powers he mimicked off the X-Men. While the Adaptoid was rendered inert, Mimic was also made powerless and left the team.[169] Charles, Jean and Angel were captured by the mutant Warlock who sought to make Jean his queen after a bid to take over the world. Charles channeled his mental powers to bring the rest of the X-Men to the Warlock's hideout. There the X-Men bested the Warlock's minions and while their foe was distracted, Xavier used his mental powers to force the Warlock back into a state of suspended animation.[170]

With the threat of Factor Three still looming, Charles continued to train his students. He also attempted to bring Scott and Jean closer together. While the X-Men were battling the Cobalt Man, Charles was busy trying to find a way to cure his step brother of the curse of the Juggernaut, whom he had in his custody since his last defeat at the hands of the X-Men.[171] Later, Charles and the other X-Men threw a surprise birthday party for Jean. As a gift, Charles gave Jean a new green and yellow costume which she would end up wearing as a regular costume later.[172] Returning to his attempts to cure Cain, Charles strapped himself and his step-brother into a Energo-Transformer to try and probe his mind for a means of curing him. Instead the presence ofOutcast caused feedback that caused the machine to explode. While Charles was put in a coma, Cain was revived and endowed with Xavier's mental powers. The newly revived Juggernaut was too powerful for the X-Men and left after being mentally contacted and asked to join Factor Three.[139] While Charles was in a coma, the X-Men were still able to probe his mind and learn of the origins of the Crimson Gem of Cyttorak and with the aid of Dr. Strange, Earth's Sorcerer Supreme, banished the Juggernaut to the Crimson Cosmos.[173]

However while the X-Men were busy defeating the Juggernaut, Factor Three kidnapped the Professor.[173] Charles was kept prisoner alongside Banshee in Europe. The X-Men eventually tracked Factor Three's base down learning that the group consisted of their old foes Vanisher, Blob, Unus, and Mastermind as well as the Changeling and the so-called Mutant Master who were plotting to set off a nuclear holocaust so that the human race would be exterminated and mutants could rule the world.[174] While the X-Men escaped and attempted to stop attacks on the United States and Russia, Charles began trying to convince the already suspicious Changeling of Mutant Masters ulterior motives.[92] The X-Men ultimately foiled Factor Three's plot and Xavier - with the aid of Banshee and Changeling - revealed that the Mutant Master was really a member of the Siris race who was attempting to exterminate humans and mutants so the Earth could be colonized. With his mission a failure, Mutant Master committed suicide over capture and the members of Factor Three disbanded.[93]

Xavier helped the X-Men defeat a mind controlled scientist who rebuilt a Sentinel, and although Charles sought to determine how he as manipulated he never learned the source. The X-Men's attentions were turned to Cyclops who started seeing visions of a demon running loose. With the aid of Dr. Strange they learned that the demon followed Scott back from the Crimson Cosmos and returned it there.[175] Charles helped the X-Men control the latent mutant powers of Bernard the Poet, a poet who regularly performed at Coffee A Go-Go, which the X-Men regularly frequented. Charles guided the X-Men in tracking down a strange reading picked up by Cerebro, uncovering the android Dragon Man. The creature was taken by Jean, and accompanied the X-Men on various missions for a time before Charles convinced Jean to let the dragon find a new home on Monster Isle.[176] Soon after, Xavier decided that the X-Men had passed their latest round of training and awarded them with new costumes.[93]

Z'Nox Invasion[]

Charles learned that the alien Z'nox were planning an invasion of Earth and began preparing a means to stop them. While in the early planning phase he was visited by the Changeling who revealed that he was dying of cancer and wanted to make up for past wrongs. This worked perfectly to Xavier's plans. Confiding in Jean only, Charles transferred his mental powers to the Changeling who then posed as Xavier, and unlocked Jean's own telepathic potential. Charles hid himself within the mansion in a heavily psi-shielded room to continue his work uninterrupted.[177] Meanwhile, the Changeling posed as Xavier, fooling even the X-Men.[94] Eventually, the Changeling died while the X-Men were battling the subterranean mutate Grotesk, further covering Xavier's deception.[96]

The X-Men spent a period of time fending for themselves, adding Cyclops' brother Havok and the magnetic mutant Lorna Dane to their ranks.[81]. While the senior X-Men were away from the mansion, Xavier was finally ready to fend off the Z'Nox invasion. He revealed himself to Havok, Lorna, and the rest of the team when they returned. Briefing them on his deception and the impending invasion, Charles quickly resumed leadership of the X-Men and began preparing them for the battle. As the X-Men battled the Z'Nox invaders, Charles linked his mind with every good human being on the planet and channeled these alien emotions to the Z'Nox via his X-Men, forcing them to flee. The strain caused Xavier to suddenly collapse.[177] Unknown to Charles at the time, this effort also put him in psychic rapport with Shi'ar Princess Lilandra Neramani.[178] In a coma and in ill health, Charles mind was probed for a possible solution to his condition. Xavier told them to seek out Bruce Banner and recover a gamma powered device that could cure him. The X-Men succeeded in recovering the device and curing Charles, but not until after a clash with Banner's alter-ego the Hulk and the military.[179]

The X-Men have returned to Banner's secret cave and discover that it is still intact, but buried under tons of rubble. Professor X scanned Marvel Girl's mind to learn what he could about his X-Men's battle with the Hulk so he could cure him. The X-Men finished preparations to lure the Hulk back to the cave.[180] Meanwhile back at Banner's secret lab, the Beast, Lorna Dane, and Professor X have finished preparing the lab for Bruce's return. When Banner arrived with the rest of the X-Men, he was brought up to speed on what the X-Men hoped to accomplish. Bruce was put in a telepathic matrix between his mind-healing device and the Professor's mutant powers. As the experiment began, the Hulk's persona was fighting back, dragging Xavier into Banner's mindspace.[181] Charles experienced a reality in which Jean Grey turned into the Hulk, followed by the one in which Xavier himself had done the same. When Xavier woke up, he found that the X-Men were attacked by robots and taken prisoners by the Leader, who warned Charles against using his telepathic powers as it would trigger the bombs the X-Men were fitted with.[182]

Now with Bruce strapped into the device the X-Men hoped would cure him, the Leader had modified it so it would turn Bruce into a gamma battery. Professor X tried to contact Marvel Girl telepathically, but she was unable to respond due to the sensory deprivation device strapped to her head. When the Leader demanded to know how Professor X affected the device with his telepathy, Charles only agreed to do so if Leader removed the explosives from himself and his students. In order to try and get Charles to talk, the Leader ordered one of his Humanoids to begin tearing Angel's wings off. Hearing Warren's screams of pain, Banner woke up and somehow used telekinesis to free the X-Men from their bonds. As the X-Men tried to hold the androids off, the Hulk smashed free and destroyed the mental device, despite protests from Professor X. Hidden Years[]

Secret Empire[]

Lorna and Havok were captured by the Secret Empire terrorist organization, whose bid for world domination would have an impact on the lives of Charles and his students.[223] With the rest of his team reaching the final stretch of their college education, Charles was secretly away on Muir Island, the X-Men began being attacked by a series of threats including rampant Danger Room robots, a returned Frederick,[224] and strange phantoms of the Juggernaut and an apparent "magnetic ghost" of Magneto.[225] Charles returned to find his school in shambles. He called the X-Men back and learned of the recent attacks on the school. After advising his team that he intended to expand the scope of his school to an international level and focus primarily in training new mutants to control their powers and defend themselves, Charles and the others realized that the strange phantom attacks were coming from Jean's mind.[226] Charles and his students ventured into Jean's mind and helped her cope with her fears. With the crisis averted, the X-Men were graduated from their classes.[227] Following graduation, Hank was offered a position with the Brand Corporation. Charles and his X-Men bid the Beast farewell as he went on to a new life. Unknown to all at the time the Brand Corporation was a target of the Secret Empire.[228]

Charles old colleague Hans Jorgenson had been kidnapped, and Spider-Man was being blamed. Charles sent the X-Men out to find Spider-Man. The group learned that Spider-Man was not responsible for the kidnapping, but was seeking out Jorgenson to help cure him of an mystery illness plaguing him. The X-Men and Spider-Man discovered that Jorgenson was kidnapped by Morbius the Living Vampire and rescued him. Xavier and Jorgenson worked to cure Spider-Man of his illness using the enzymes in Morbius' blood. Charles contained Morbius at the mansion for a time.[229] When the Angel became worried about Hank, Charles refused to get involved, but did not stop Warren from seeking out his friend. In tracking down the Beast, Angel learned of his friend's further mutation and reported it back to the X-Men.[230] Not long after this the Beast was also captured by the Secret Empire.[223]

Magneto resurfaced and attacked the X-Men directly, incapacitating the group and using his magnetic powers to enslave Charles and most of the X-Men. Magneto lured the Avengers into a trap, capturing the Scarlet Witch, Captain America, and Iron Man in the process.[231] Angel was captured by the Secret Empire during this attack.[232] Charles and the captive X-Men were rescued by the remaining Avengers along with the Black Widow and Daredevil. In the aftermath of the battle Charles and his students began trying to learn what happened to the Angel.[233] Charles joined Agent Duncan in briefing jungle adventurer Shanna the She-Devil on two mutants named the Mandrill and Nekra.[234] With mutants going missing, Charles assigned Cyclops, Iceman, and Marvel Girl to check in on their various allies and enemies. Iceman went missing shortly thereafter.[223] Shortly thereafter, Charles and Cyclops were unable to stop Morbius from breaking free and escaping the mansion.[235]

Taking a more proactive approach, Charles joined Cyclops and Marvel Girl in a search for their missing allies. Their search took them to the southwest where they encountered the Juggernaut in the middle of a battle with the Hulk. Professor X and his students incapacitated the Juggernaut and allowed the Hulk to go free before resuming their search.[236] They tracked their old ally the Banshee down to Nashville, where they found him in the middle of a battle with Captain America and his partner the Falcon. Believing that Xavier, Cap and Falcon were out to capture him, the Banshee fled the scene. Charles learned that Captain America and Falcon were also seeking to stop the Secret Empire and an alliance was formed.[237] The heroes were attacked by Nick Fury and a team of S.H.I.E.L.D. agents. Charles used his mental powers to shield his presence to keep his ties with the X-Men a secret and they made a hasty escape. Comparing notes, Charles suggested they try and determine the connection between the Secret Empire and the Beast's girlfriend Linda Donaldson who Charles tracked to the area. Charles had Cyclops stage an attack on her while a disguised Cap and Falcon came to her rescue, posing as men seeking work. She quickly offered them a job, revealing herself as an agent of the Secret Empire, and they were hired to steal an electro-gyro from the Brand Corporation. This theft was a success and when Cap and Falcon were taken to the Secret Empire's secret hideout, Charles and his remaining students followed.[223]

However, the plan went awry as the Secret Empire saw through the deception and attempted to capture Cap and the Falcon, Charles and his students came to their rescue. The heroes found the captured X-Men as well as Blob, Unus, Mastermind and Mesmero were also prisoners and that the Secret Empire was using their mental energies to power a weapon. Before the captured mutants could be freed, the Secret Empire seemingly slew Charles and the others with an atomic annihilator weapon.[232] However the Empire had been infiltrated by S.H.I.E.L.D. agents Gabe Jones and Peggy Carter who were controlling the cannon and fired a non-lethal blast. Getting Xavier and the others to cover, they regrouped and battled the Secret Empire when they attempted to storm the White House. While the X-Men freed their allies and defeated the Empire, Cap exposed their leader as a high ranking government official who committed suicide rather than face capture.[238] Later, Charles became aware that Russian intelligence operative Alexei Vazhin was seeking him out. Contacting Vazhin telepathically, Charles learned that Alexei was seeking Charles' aid in protecting a mutant named Piotr Rasputin away from being exploited by his government. Charles assured Vazhin that he would be in touch and would ensure Rasputin's safety.[239]

Shortly after, Magneto plagued Xavier and the X-Men by raiding the school. With the X-Men defeated, Xavier was left injured amid the devastated mansion. The Avengers came to their aid,[231] ultimately overcoming the villain and rescuing Xavier.[233] Magneto returned with the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants, forcing Xavier to seek assistance with the Defenders to confront his old adversary since the X-Men were unavailable. Under Xavier's guidance, the Defenders battled the terrorist mutants.[240] The crisis came to an end when Xavier convinced Alpha, the powerful mutant created by Magneto, that his creator's ideology was corrupt. Acting on this realization, Alpha reverted Magneto into infancy, ending the threat he posed.[241] Around this period, the X-Men were imprisoned by the cryptic Secret Empire alongside other mutants. Joining forces with Captain America and the Falcon, Xavier thwarted the organization's plans to free its captives.[242]

Second Genesis[]

Charles became aware of a mutant signature on the island of Krakoa,[243] and had his X-Men reunited and sent on a mission to investigate the island.[244] Xavier also briefed the Illuminati about Krakoa.[220] Charles became aware that Magneto had resurfaced and reformed his Brotherhood of Evil Mutants. Seeking assistance, he contacted Doctor Strange's loose-knit team the Defenders to assist him in stopping Magneto. Tracking the Brotherhood to a secret cave, the Defenders and Charles were captured and witnessed as Magneto created a being he dubbed Alpha the Ultimate Mutant.[240] The Brotherhood fled with the rapidly evolving Alpha and attacked the United Nations Building. Charles and the Defenders followed. During the course of the battle, Alpha reached the zenith of his evolutionary transformation and decided to use his vast intellect to judge whose ideologies were the most righteous. Alpha judged in favor of the heroes, and Magneto and his Brotherhood were all reverted into children before Alpha left Earth to explore the universe.[241] Charles turned Magneto and his infantile Brotherhood over to Moira MacTaggart at her mutant research center to be re-educated in the hopes they would grow to be peaceful mutants.[245]

Having lost contact with the X-Men while they battled strange creatures on the island of Krakoa, Charles became desperate to save his students. He sought out Moira and asked her to allow him to use her students to rescue his team of X-Men. Moira reluctantly allowed this when Kid Vulcan and the other students agreed to help Charles. In order to fast track their training, Charles put them through a virtual training with his mental powers, giving these students months of training in just hours. Before sending these novice X-Men on their mission to save the others, Charles revealed to Kid Vulcan that he had discovered that Cyclops was his brother. These new X-Men proved to be no match for the island of Krakoa, and only succeeded in freeing Cyclops, sending him away while the group seemingly sacrificed their lives to cover his escape. When Cyclops returned to the Mansion and told Charles what happened, Xavier realized the grave error he made. Not wishing Scott to live with the grief of losing the brother he never knew, Charles erased all memory of this novice group of X-Men from the minds of Cyclops and Moira and began planning another rescue mission. To this end, Charles altered Scott's memories to make him believe that Krakoa released him in order to lure more mutants to the island to feed off their mutant energies.[48]

Charles gathered various mutants from around the globe to help rescue the captured X-Men. The all-new, all-different X-Men included the German outcast Nightcrawler, Canadian government agent Wolverine, Irish ally of the X-Men Banshee, African villager revered as a deity Storm, Japanese atomic knight Sunfire, Soviet farmer Colossus, and Native American hothead Thunderbird. This new team of X-Men proved more successful, rescuing the original team, and defeating Krakoa by jettisoning the island out into space.[243]

Upon their return from their mission, Charles invited everyone to remain with the X-Men, however most of the original team - save for Cyclops - left to pursue their own lives. Sunfire also decided to depart from the group, having no desire to be part of the team, having only agreed to help Xavier free the X-Men. Storm, Colossus, Wolverine, Banshee, Nightcrawler and Thunderbird all agreed to stay. Charles and Cyclops began training this new generation of X-Men, who would become legends in their own right.[246]

Commanding a team of experienced adult mutants proved to be a challenge for both Professor X and Cyclops. During their first mission against Count Nefaria and his Ani-Men, Thunderbird ignored Xavier's orders to stand down and was killed in action. His death devastated the Professor.[247] Afterwards, Moira moved into Xavier's School, quickly showing that she concealed secrets about her past story with Xavier.[248]

Shi'ar Consort[]

As a consequence of his psychic projection to repel the Z'nox, Professor Xavier unwittingly established mental contact with Lilandra Neramani, a dissident princess of the magnanimous Shi'ar Empire, an interstellar civilization of colonialists spanning countless planets. Fleeing the tyranny of her brother, Emperor D'Ken, Lilandra believed that Professor X was the key to overthrow the his regime. Drawn to the mysterious telepath, she roamed the vastness of space to reach Earth.[178][249] Their psychic bond tormented Xavier, plaguing him with recurring nightmares.[250] Hoping to clear his mind, he accompanied his old friend Peter Corbeau on a voyage at sea, where they were captured by Stephen Lang's Sentinels and taken to his space station.[251] The X-Men mounted a successful rescue and dismantled Lang's plans,[252] but Jean Grey sacrificed herself to return her dearest ones to Earth and reemerged as the Phoenix. Concerned by the disturbing change in her condition, Xavier kept her company at the hospital,[253] where his connection to Lilandra continued to haunt him.[254]

After Grey was discharged, Xavier accompanied her back to her apartment.[255] Simultaneously, Eric the Red, a bounty hunter pursuing Lilandra, staged a final strike against Xavier by brainwashing Lorna Dane and Havok, restoring Magneto to adulthood, and deceiving the Herald Firelord into joining his cause. Lilandra reached Xavier, finally meeting him in person before collapsing from exhaustion. Although the Phoenix's immense power disrupted Eric the Red's plans, he escaped with Lilandra to the Shi'ar Galaxy. In desperation, Xavier urged Phoenix to transport the X-Men to the Shi'ar Empire to save Lilandra and stop her mad brother[256] Left behind on Earth, Xavier succumbed to terror, being treated by Moira. He waged a symbolic battle against the dark aspects of his psyche to recover.[257] Phoenix and the X-Men put a definitive end to D'Ken before the M'kraan Crystal, which he sought to control to conquer absolute power. Lilandra accompanied the X-Men back to Earth in temporary exile, where she could thank Xavier for having saved the Shi'ar throne.[258]

Xavier introduced Lilandra to Earth's customs and to the lives of the X-Men at his school.[259] As their bond deepened, they gave in to their passion for each other.[260] They enjoyed the paradisiacal beaches of Kirinos, when Xavier abruptly lost his psychic rapport with the X-Men. Captured by Magneto near the South Pole, the X-Men witnessed the destruction of Magneto's base during the ensuing battle. With only Phoenix and Beast escaping, the other X-Men were in fact stranded in the Savage Land.[261] Tragically, the X-Men were believed dead. Phoenix delivered the devastating news to her mentor and dear friend.[262] Overwhelmed by grief, Charles Xavier was offered the chance to depart to the Shi'ar Empire with Lilandra. Convinced that his dream had died with his students, he accepted the offer.[41] Xavier became the royal consort of Majestrix Lilandra Neramani[263] following her coronation in the galaxy-spanning realm.[264] Marginalized by the Shi'ar aristocracy, he immersed himself in the imperial archives, becoming a specialist in their history and culture.[265] Frustrated by the prejudice he faced as an outsider in Chandilar, the Shi'ar throneworld, he was able to connect with its people through the perspective of the plumber Chakra.[266]

The misunderstanding surrounding the X-Men's demise was sorted out when Xavier returned to the school and reunited with them. Resuming his search for new mutants, Professor X visited the young Kitty Pryde and her family in Illinois hoping to recruit her to his school. From the shadows, the X-Men were spied on and manipulated by the Hellfire Club. Emma Frost also sought to recruit Pryde for her dark schemes, attacking the X-Men in her attempt.[267] Rather than intervening directly, Xavier observed how the X-Men handled the crisis, stepping in only to telepathically calm Pryde's frightened parents, causing Cyclops to question his judgment.[268] Hellfire's main target ended up being Phoenix; when Cyclops had the X-Men raid the Hellfire Club, she became completely corrupted by Mastermind and turned against her friends. Xavier remained at Angel's Aerie in New Mexico, resenting Cyclops' lack of subordination.[269]

Moira informed Professor X that Jean Grey succumbed to degradation as Dark Phoenix, obliterating the D'bari System and annihilating billions of lives.[270] The atrocious massacre drew the attention of the Shi'ar, who marched to Earth to bring her to justice. At her family home, Phoenix was subdued by the X-Men and subjected to a mental scrambler designed by Beast. Risking his own life, Xavier warred a psychic battle with his most adored pupil. Although he restored her reason,[271] Professor X had to oppose Majestrix Lilandra as she demanded for Phoenix's execution. Drawing upon his knowledge of Shi'ar tradition, Xavier invoked the right of Arin'n Haelar, a duel of honor to decide the fate of the Phoenix. As preparations for the trial were made, Xavier and Lilandra lamented their painful position as opponents. On the Blue Area of the Moon, the X-Men faced the Shi'ar Imperial Guard, which triggered the rise of Dark Phoenix. Having no alternatives, Xavier ordered the X-Men to stop her by any means necessary. In the end, Phoenix herself adknowledge she could not control her evil impulses and sacrificed her own life rather than risk harming others.[272] Following her funeral, the X-Men were left shattered, and Cyclops resigned from the team.[273]

Magneto's Ultimatum[]

Professor X continued serving as the mentor of the X-Men, now led in the field by Storm with Pryde as their most recent addition.[8] They were surprised by an adult version of Pryde whose mind had been sent back through time to prevent a dystopian future where Sentinels subjugated society. This catastrophe would begin with the assassination of Senator Robert Kelly by a new Brotherhood of Evil Mutants led by the rabid Mystique. At the time, Xavier and Moira were meeting with with Kelly in the senate to discuss mutant-human relations.[274] They were subdued by Mystique and her clairvoyant partner, Destiny, but the murder attempt failed thanks to the timely intervention of the X-Men. Still, the attack was a turning point in public opinion towards mutants.[275]

Reassessing his long-time rivalry with Magneto, Professor Xavier assigned the X-Men to locate him on the Savage Land, with no success.[276] Xavier and Moira secretly visited Magneto at his secret base on Island M, where Xavier psychically entrusted Magneto with Moira's memories of her previous lives, revealing that mutantkind was doomed to failure. Despite initial skepticism, Magneto was persuaded to join Xavier and Moira in their long-term plan to ensure the survival of the mutant race.[277] Together, Xavier and Magneto arrived on Bar Sinister to meet Mister Sinister and propose the create a consistent collection of the mutant genetic material that he had acquired over the years. Recognizing that the repository could be essential to their common interests, Sinister accepted the arrangement, after which Xavier suppressed his memories of the encounter.[278] Magneto revealed himself to the world's leaderships, demanding that every nation dismantle their nuclear arsenals in the name of preserving life on Earth. His ultimatum was met with rejection, prompting him to declare open war on humanity. In response, Professor X mobilized the X-Men to invade Island M and depose Magneto, while he remotely engaged his old friend in psychic combat. During their fight, Magneto physically restrained Xavier with his magnetic powers. Realizing his hatred had clouded his judgment, Magneto abandoned his crusade. The X-Men resettled on Island M[279] because Professor X concluded that they would be safer operating from outside the country in face of escalating anti-mutant sentiment.[280]

In between these events, Xavier was contacted by Professor Power to provide psychic treatment to his catatonic son. The two engaged in a philosophical debate over the ethical use of telepathy to advance their personal goals. Power had the criminal telepath Mentallo under captivity in hopes of harnessing psychic energy to activate a machine capable of propagating a global mental war. While Xavier battled Mentallo on the astral plane, Spider-Man invaded Power's mansion and fought his operative, the Fixer. Xavier emerged victorious over Mentallo and concluded that Power's son was beyond saving.[281] Meanwhile, after the X-Men defeated Sauron on the Savage Land, Professor Xavier succeeded in curing him by restoring his personality as Dr. Karl Lykos. By isolating a viral component within Lykos' genetic matrix, Xavier freed him him from the Sauron persona.[282]

The Brood Queen[]

From Island M, the X-Men were abducted by the Shi'ar to a Dreadnought, where Chancellor Araki shared that Lilandra had been overtaken by terrorists. As the imperial consort, Xavier forbid the Shi'ar troops to enact force to recover Lilandra. Instead, he had the X-Men act furtively, telepathically locating her in New York City. The kidnapper was her renegade sister, Deathbird, who had joined forces with the vicious aliens known as the Brood to usurp the throne. The X-Men were overcome with the attack, with Professor Xavier being captured by Deathbird.[283] Reunited with his beloved, Xavier was taken by Deathbird to a mobile colony of Brood built in the body of an Acanti. The X-Men and the Starjammers, a band of space pirates led by Corsair active in the Shi'ar Galaxy, followed them. Despite being successful in defeating Deathbird, the X-Men still had to save Earth, that was at Admiral Samédàr's mercy.[284] On their way back, Professor X unexplainably was sent into a coma.[285] On Island M, the Shi'ar psychic Oracle inspected Xavier's mind, identifying a malignant presence within his soul.[286] During his abduction, Professor X had been implanted with a Brood Queen embryo.[287]

Professor X woke from his coma after resisting monstrous experiences deep within his mind. Majestrix Lilandra planned a farewell banquet for the X-Men on Earth's orbit, but Moira recommended Xavier not to attend it due to his weakened state. Deathbird and the Brood invaded the celebration, teleporting them away.[44] Despite his pupils believed dead and his dream shattered, Xavier returned to his institute, keeping it operational.[288] Moira alerted him that, although he had chosen to stop helping mutants in need, their adversaries would not. To convince him, she shared about the fate of Karma, an immigrant Vietnamese in need of guidance.[289] He resolved to tutor her at his school, with Moira introducing the Scottish lycanthrope Wolfsbane to the institute. The need to aid young mutants was confirmed when the unscrupulous cyborg Donald Pierce deployed his resources to hunt other young mutants, including Danielle Moonstar, the granddaughter of a friend of Xavier's, Black Eagle. In his attempt to outsmart Pierce, Xavier ended up being captured, but the joined efforts of Moira and the young mutants, added by Sunspot and Cannonball, got him free and Pierce apprehended. A new class of mutant students aptly named the New Mutants enrolled at Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters.[290]

Despite having a new class of students, Xavier was reluctant to have them in the field and vehemently rejected the idea of having them act as future X-Men. Using Shi'ar technology, Professor X reconfigured the Danger Room using an artificial intelligence and holographic technology.[291] In secrecy, due to being influenced by the Brood infection, Professor Xavier considered gathering and using his pupils as embryos.[292] He displayed reckless behavior against his protégés, such as trapping Moonstar inside the Danger Room to torment her.[291] Eventually, the New Mutants realized something was wrong with their mentor.[293] Wolverine identified Xavier had been compromised and, as the X-Men returned from space with such a knowledge, they raided the school. This caused the Brood Queen to reveal herself by emerging from Charles Xavier's body. Momentarily asserting control using his mind, Professor Xavier asked Cyclops to mercy-kill him, to which he refused. Moira and the Starjammer physician Sikorsky evaluated his condition as irreversible. Instead, they created a golem body through cloning and artificial aging to transplant his mind. Starjamming[]

Growing worried about violence in his classes at Columbia, Professor Xavier prepared Magneto and the X-Men to prepare to act against a potential hate crime.[317] Following this episode, Magneto agreed to join the X-Men at Xavier's request, especially due to the imminent return of the Beyonder.[318] Magneto, however, was soon placed under arrest after surrendering due to his past crimes against humanity.[319] In Paris, at the Palais de Justice, he went on trial before the World Court, with Haller and Xavier providing him defense counselling. Mutant Genesis[]

Upon returning to Earth, Xavier immediately sequestered the X-Men's former Blackbird aircraft that had been confiscated by W.H.O., which he used to land on Muir Island. Legacy Virus[]

During a Lila Cheney concert in Central Park, Xavier held a mutant rights speech. An assassination attempt was performed against him by Cable, the extremist militant leader of X-Force.[345] In fact, the murderer was Stryfe, posing as Cable to damage his reputation.[346] The shot infected Xavier with the Techno-Organic Virus, leaving him in critical condition in the infirmary of his school[347] with Moira and Beast doing their best to stabilize him and keep him alive.[348] Sharing a common enemy in Stryfe, the ancient mutant warrior Apocalypse offered to save Xavier's life, despite the X-Men's concern,[346] by expunging the virus from his organism.[349] Upon recovery, Charles Xavier was disturbed by the connection Cable and Stryfe shared with Cyclops and Jean Grey.[350] In the aftermath, Xavier temporarily regained the use of his legs, devoting this precious time to roller-skating with the youngest recruit of the X-Men, Jubilee, before returning to his wheelchair.[351]

Deep underground within his mansion, Professor X designed the Ready Room, a secluded computer chamber where he analyzed huge amounts of information where he spent a lot of his time working.[352] In his research, he discovered the Legacy Virus, a terminal disease affecting mutants that had been released by Stryfe as his last laugh before defeat. In addition to this, tensions between mutants and humans escalated with the return of the Acolytes[85] and the emergence of the radical Friends of Humanity. This prompted Xavier to debate on mutant rights on television with Senator Kelly and the intolerant Graydon Creed. Xavier and Kelly ended up finding a common ground and realizing they shared many perspectives on the matter.[353] Simultaneously, Xavier prepared the X-Men to address the situation concerning the Acolytes, by storming their castle in France after they kidnapped Moira. The X-Men exposed to the Acolytes alternative interpretations on their idol Magneto, eroding Cortez's influence over them. Amidst battle, Xavier recognized Voght as one of Magneto's followers, while concluding his and Magneto's visions shared more similarities than he believed.[85]

Onslaught[]

Following a visit to the Savage Land, Professor X and his X-Men were struck by an attack performed by the Upstart Siena Blaze that made their Blackbird crash onto the frigid lands of Antarctica during a blizzard. Xavier believed he, Cyclops and Storm could succumb to the cold, and wished them to treat him as an equal. By himself, Professor X ventured through the tempest, reaching a citadel generating a disrupting electromagnetic field. Miraculously, a mysterious benefactor saved Xavier from perishing, allowing him to return to his students and fend off Blaze. Professor X recognized his savior as Magneto.[354] Suspicions were confirmed as Magneto resurfaced to unleash his rage upon humanity from Avalon, a floating fortress he erected using Shi'ar technology stolen from Xavier. He offered mutants sanctuary away from humankind. In horror, Xavier observed one of his own, Colossus, defect, after being disillusioned with the death of his sister because of the Legacy Virus. This sparked a violent reaction in him as he attempted to stop Magneto at all costs in vain.[1]

At the same time, Xavier supervised the enactment the Magneto Protocols through the Mutant Underground, a network of informants he coordinated in secrecy.[1] He then assigned Storm to perform a heist and steal data on a cloaking technology that could give him advantage over Magneto. She complied, but expressed her discontentment with his dubious morality.[355] He was aware of corrupting himself to finally put an end to Magneto's cruelty. In the Danger Room, Xavier went through intensive training in preparation to invade Avalon.[356] With the Magneto Protocols, the planet's magnetic field was disturbed, depowering Magneto on Earth and effectively banishing him from the world. In retaliation, Magneto rendered the technology on Earth useless with an electromagnetic pulse. Wishing to prevent a global war, Professor X donned a Shi'ar Exoskeleton fed by psychic energy that enabled him to walk and lead the strike-force against Magneto. He infiltrated with the X-Men unnoticed in an attempt to telepathically force Magneto to question his judgment. In response, Magneto violently attacked Wolverine, removing the Adamantium coating from his skeleton. In horror, Professor X could not complete his mental intervention; instead, he took Magneto's mind away, erasing it to a vegetative state.[357] Wolverine left the X-Men following the aggression.[358]

In desperation to control his savage and murderous tendencies, Sabretooth invaded Xavier's School in search for telepathic treatment. Professor X subdued him and, contrary to his X-Men's recommendation, took the killer in.[359] Particularly, Jean Grey expressed her discontentment with the decision,[360] but reinforced she would not lose respect for her mentor because of it. This coincided with Xavier coping with his strike against Magneto, who emerged as a figure within his subconsciousness. In conclusion, Xavier recognized he was more similar to his rival than he believed.[46] Amidst these complexities, Charles Xavier attended the wedding of Cyclops and Jean Grey, being able to waltz with his student thanks to her telekinetic powers.[361] Meanwhile, Emma Frost awakened from her coma inside Iceman's body. Xavier comforted her about her condition, especially concerning the death of her class of students, the Hellions.[362] This coincided with the techno-organic creatures of the Phalanx attacking mutantkind. To protect the X-Men's secrets, Banshee destroyed Xavier's Ready Room and its vast database. Moreover, he and Frost were instrumental in fending off the invasion.[363] In the aftermath, Professor X agreed to transfer his School for Gifted Youngsters to Frost's Massachusetts Academy, where the Generation X was to receive education. Xavier's estate and home of the X-Men became the Xavier Institute for Higher Learning.[364]

Amidst his inner conflicts concerning Magneto's ideologies, Xavier identified Legion recovered consciousness. In a twisted attempt to help his father, Legion wished to end Magneto, contemplating what life would have been had him never undermined Xavier's dream.[365] Professor X had the X-Men oppose Legion in Israel.[366] Traveling to the past, Legion attempted to murder Magneto in Haifa,[71] but accidentally murdered Xavier instead.[73] This altered the timeline, generating a world in which the X-Men were founded by Magneto under Apocalypse's supreme rule.[367] Reality was restored,[74] but with repercussions, including a series of interlopers from the perverted world.[368] One of these was Holocaust, who destroyed Avalon, causing Voght to seek support from Xavier and the X-Men.[369] Meanwhile, Sabretooth proved to be a lost cause, immensely frustrating Professor X.[370] Simultaneously, the Legacy Virus epidemic had also became more accentuated. The disease caused panic among the public after being exposed.[368] Xavier and Moira had been working relentlessly to produce a cure as Moira herself became one of the infected.[371] He took her to Paris for some moment off, when they reminisced on their youth and shared a kiss, commenting about their strong friendship.[372] The stress of these ordeals proved to be overwhelming to Charles Xavier,[373] fracturing his identity.[374]

Amidst this tragedy, a vile entity known as Onslaught knocked the Juggernaut out, casting mysteries among the X-Men.[375] Post, a servant of Onslaught, raided the Xavier Institute, demonstrating to have privileged information about the X-Men. Gateway appeared after his defeat, informing Onslaught would inevitably surface with his full power to change the world.[376] Onslaught revealed himself to Jean Grey, attempting to recruit her by exposing Xavier as a hypocrite.[377] The Professor's behavior became bizarre to his students, with Grey only trusting Juggernaut to uncover the mystery of Onslaught.[378] From the Juggernaut's disrupted memories, she found out Onslaught was Charles Xavier as he depowered Cain Marko by stealing the Gem of Cyttorak.[374] In the process of tampering with Magneto's mind, Xavier sustained psionic damage. The dark sides of Xavier's and Magneto's psyches combined and manifested themselves as the omnipotent Onslaught.[379] He revealed himself to the X-Men, destroying the Xavier Institute, but was unable to have them join his cause of absolute rule.[380]

Inspired by the Age of Apocalypse, Onslaught planned to rule Earth,[380] while Xavier's personality remained trapped within the monster's soul. The super-hero community of Earth, including the Avengers and the Fantastic Four, mobilized to confront Onslaught.[381] Meanwhile, to oppose him, Moira and the X-Men unsealed the Xavier Protocols, secret files Xavier produced with detailed information on defeating the X-Men, including himself. This proved to be a shock to the X-Men, who were disappointed in learning their protector had plans against them.[382] Disguised as a child version of Xavier, Onslaught abducted the incredibly powerful Franklin Richards from his family of the Fantastic Four to gain additional power.[383] Another part of his plan involved dispatching a horde of Sentinels to New York City to obliterate humanity.[384] Having erected an impenetrable citadel on Central Park brought upon mayhem to the city.[385] Charles Xavier resisted, attempting to protect Richards from being used in Onslaught's scheme for power. The Avenger Thor cracked through Onslaught's psionic armor, freeing Xavier from him and effectively separating them. This, however, unbound Onslaught.[386]

Stripped from his telepathic powers, Xavier could only watch his dark side destroy any chance of realizing his dream. He conversed with Joseph, a young version of Magneto who emerged following the fall of Avalon, understanding him to be the good facet of Magneto's soul. By himself, Xavier visited Onslaught to reason with him, with little success.[387] No longer having a physical body, Onslaught's existence nurtured from mutant signatures. As such, all non-mutant heroes present seeming perished while engaging in combat with him. The creature was destroyed, though at the cost of Earth's heroes.[388] In fact, they had been gathered by the powers of Franklin Richards to another plane of existence.[389] The damaged inflicted by Onslaught gave rise to attacks on mutantkind, in the form of Graydon Creed's intolerant presidential campaign, and Bastion's anti-mutant Operation: Zero Tolerance. Professor Xavier deeply lamented the consequences of his acts.[390] From the ruined Xavier Institute, he was taken into custody by Val Cooper, as the head of mutant affairs for the government.[391] Incarcerated in the Hulkbuster Base in New Mexico, Professor X was turned over to the custody of Bastion, where he received subhuman treatment.[392]

Hunt for Xavier[]

At prison, Xavier was evaluated by psychologist Dr. Ingrid Thysson, while the child Nina, an artificial mutant resulted of the Mannite program, secretly interacted with him. With Dr. Thysson's help, Xavier conspired with Renee Majcomb, of his Mutant Underground, to break Nina out of the facility and Bastion's hold.[392] Bastion's agenda escalated as Prime Sentinels took over the country, with him tormenting Xavier about the fall of his dream[393] and incarcerating the X-Men.[394] Eventually, Bastion was put down,[395] but Xavier could not help to reconsider the disastrous situations to which he felt he had subjected his students.[396]

Bastion's attack against the X-Men corrupted Cerebro's intelligence, causing it to gain independence.[397] As the Founder, he impersonated Professor X and created a team of X-Men from his files,[398] being obsessed with cataloguing every mutant on the planet.[399] The Brotherhood of Evil Mutants broke Xavier out of prison to protect themselves from the chaos. Hidden in Alcatraz, Professor X coordinated the Brotherhood in fighting the X-Men, as they were involved in the battle against Cerebro,[400] who became obsessed with destroying his creator, also being drawn to Nina and Majcomb, who at the time hid in a monastery in Tajikistan.[401] The X-Men protected Professor X from the machine's advances,[402] but ended up at its mercy. Nina accessed Charles Xavier's mind to restore his telepathic powers, which he used to confuse Cerebro's senses and enabled the X-Men to incapacitate it. Alongside Nina, Professor X reconfigured Cerebro's programming, causing it to abandon its perverted cause. Sound and safe, the Professor resumed his role as the headmaster of the Xavier Institute with the X-Men at his side.[397]

Magneto also returned around this period, determined to subjugate the world.[403] This coincided with Cortez's Acolytes raiding the Xavier Institute in Magneto's name. Defeated, the Acolytes asked for sanctuary, which Professor X denied for he no longer believed in redemption from villains. Alternatively, he assumed the Acolytes' cause and resolved to look for Magneto himself.[404] In charge of the Acolytes, Magneto performed various terrorists attacks, with Xavier following his tracks. At the North Pole, the X-Men found him after he activated a mechanism to disrupt, once again, Earth's magnetic field and destroy humanity's weapons.[405] In vain, Xavier attempted to telepathically reason with the Acolytes, being stopped by Scanner.[406] To revert Magneto's damage, Professor X assisted Joseph in restoring the planet's atmosphere, though it came with his sacrifice. Subdued by the X-Men, Magneto was to be arrested for his crimes according to Xavier. Alternatively, the United Nations, represented by mutant specialist Alda Huxley, granted him rule over Genosha.[407][408] This outcome deeply disturbed Charles Xavier.[409] He contacted Huxley about her decision, but got no reconsideration from her part.[410]

Before Xavier could address the situation, he and some of the X-Men were abducted by an alien[410] identified as Ejulp, who enlisted them to stop the destruction caused by the Juggernaut, who had been empowered by an additional Gem of Cyttorak. During the mission, Professor X's psyche accidentally merged with Wolverine's body, leading them to share perspectives.[411] Together, Xavier and Wolverine ventured inside Cain Marko's mind, exorcizing him from the malignant influence.[412] When attempting to return the X-Men to Earth, Ejulp accidentally sent the X-Men to the past on the Skrull War-Moon, a fabricated environment mimicking New York City that served as a training facility for shapeshifting spies.[413] This coincided with Galactus, the world-eater, consuming the planet of Tarnax.[414] Professor X psychically communed with the all-mighty entity to convince him to spare the Skrulls, but failed. With no alternative, he and the X-Men fled in a spaceship as the world was destroyed. Dormant in a stasis-field, the X-Men traveled for years before returning home right before they had left.[415] Upon return, Professor X was worried about his team being compromised by the Skrull during the mission.[416]

Growing increasingly suspicious, Professor X became strict and cruel towards the X-Men,[417] ultimately disbanding the X-Men after expressing he could not trust his students.[418] Alternatively, Xavier resorted to X-Force, planning to recruit them. However, the X-Men approached him with the corpse of Wolverine, who had been murdered by Apocalypse's Horseman of Death. During the autopsy, Wolverine was revealed as a Skrull. With the infiltrator exposed, Charles Xavier shared his concerns with the X-Men and explained his previous motivation to have disbanded the team.[419] The Horseman of Death was the real Wolverine, while the Skrulls were allies of Apocalypse in his plan to gather the legendary mutants known as the Twelve to ascend to omnipotence through a prophetic ritual.[420] Having analyzed Destiny's predictive Diaries, Xavier amassed a legion of mutants to fight Apocalypse. Before the mission, Xavier was contacted by Fiz, a mutant Skrull reject who regarded him as an idol and wished to collaborate with the X-Men.[421] Since one of the Twelve was Magneto, Professor X contacted him, proposing a truce against Apocalypse.[422] They ended up being abducted by Apocalypse, however, alongside the rest of the Twelve.[423] Apocalypse was defeated by the X-Men, but at the cost of Cyclops' soul, for he became the host of the monster to prevent his ascension.[424] As a result of this tragedy, Jean Grey left the X-Men, bidding farewell to her mentor as she departed.[425] Feeling responsible for the mutant Skrulls, Xavier accompanied them to the space to philosophically guide them as a team of justice, Cadre K.[426]

Dream's End[]

Cadre K saved innocent mutant aliens across the Skrull territory, being mentored by Professor X. The Skrull leaderships became insulted by their activities, bringing the case to the Galactic Council. As leader of the Shi'ar Empire, Majestrix Lilandra interceded on her consort's behalf. In one of Cadre K's missions, they attempted to stop Ego, the Living Planet, from causing horrors across the galaxies. With the help of the telepath Z'Cann, Professor X accessed the minds of those of the Council in planet Selandiar, harvesting their psychic energies to incapacitate Ego. This and other events led the alien community to consider humanity a plague who interfered with matters of which they should not be part,[427] sentencing Earth to be politically cast out of the rest of the universe as an isolated prison.[428] Professor X conversed with Lilandra about solutions, she promised to defuse the political tension, but could not directly intervene.[429] Deathbird held access to break through the barrier that isolated Earth, leading Xavier and Cadre K to hunt her across the Shi'ar Galaxy. He secured the item with Bishop's assistance.[430] Next, Cadre K raided Selandiar to rescue Z'Cann, who had been arrested by the Council, but Professor X fell hostage as well.[431] In fact, he had planned his capture to provide Z'Cann with information to be sent to the X-Men on Earth, who managed to teleport Bishop back as well[432] to put down the barrier.[433] The situation was revealed to be a conspiracy orchestrated by the Kree, and Earth was released. In the aftermath, Professor X concluded he had to remain with the X-Men and, with his blessing, Cadre K departed to share his dream to other worlds.[434]

Xavier's return coincided with Senator Kelly's presidential campaign gaining strength. As a response, Mystique elaborated a scheme to accomplish her long-term plan of assassinating Kelly which involved the Brotherhood attacking important figures for his candidacy. Professor X commanded the X-Men to stop her. Part of her plan involved transforming the Legacy Virus into a plague to infect mutants, leading her to invade Muir Island while the Brotherhood attacked Kelly.[435] Moira was fatally wounded in the assault just before concluding the cure for the disease, but Mystique was apprehended by the X-Men.[436] Before Moira's death, Professor X retrieve the details on her research and stayed with her psychically until her final moments. He indicated he would have died with his first love as she shared she held no regrets before he was pulled back and consoled by Jean Grey.[437] In fact, Moira faked her death using a Shi'ar golem after she concluded that, as a potential liability to mutantkind's plans, she would better remove herself from the open.[65] Kelly ended up dying at the hands of an anti-mutant militant human.[437] Despite being disillusioned in grief, Professor Charles Xavier reinforced his motivations to carry on his dream.[42]

With Moira's results, the Legacy Virus was cured.[438] Meanwhile, Storm decided to leave the Xavier Institute with her own splinter team of X-Men, not trusting Professor X's idealism concerning the study of the Destiny's Diaries. With the X-Men's numbers severely diminished[439] and the mutant plague eradicated, Magneto had a huge tactical advantage over his rival.[440] To win his war against the human race, he kidnapped Charles Xavier from his school.[441] Jean Grey formed a new team of X-Men to infiltrate Genosha, rescue Xavier, and prevent a global war.[442] Her team gained access to Charles Xavier, who had been publicly crucified Charles Xavier in Magda Square.[443][442] Voght secretly broke Xavier free, which gave the X-Men advantage. Furiously, Wolverine mortally wounded Magneto, putting him out of commission. At the Xavier Institute, Professor X prepared his staff of teachers for a new era of mutantkind.[444]

When X is not X[]

An evolutionary leap occurred in the mutant population, with the emergence of a new generation of mutant activations, prompting Xavier to prepare for a brave new world by specializing his school to receive new students. At the Xavier Institute, he and Beast designed Cerebra, a new version of the mutant-locating technology with global range. Before the school term, he was contacted through Cerebra by a repugnant creature attempting to possess his mind. To protect himself, Charles Xavier considered suicide by gunshot before being protected by Jean Grey.[445] He tracked the attack to Ecuador, at a Wild Sentinel Factory. The assailant was Cassandra Nova, the perverted remains of his stillborn twin sister who recollected her form after years of her disposal as a miscarriage.[56] She was captured by the X-Men, but not before dispatching Sentinels to obliterate Genosha, causing a massacre that cost millions of mutant lives only to hurt Xavier, who she regarded as a nemesis.[446] At the Xavier Institute, Nova attacked the X-Men in full might and accessed Cerebra to murder all remaining mutants on the planet. She was stopped by a survivor of the fall of Genosha, Emma Frost, allowing Professor X to shoot his evil twin sister.[447] During this ordeal, Nova in fact switched bodies with Charles Xavier without his X-Men realizing, thus trapping him inside her moribund organism.[448]

Masqueraded as Professor Xavier, Cassandra Nova publicly revealed his status as a mutant on television, fully exposing the Xavier Institute as a mutant-oriented institution.[447] Her next step was to gain access to the immense power of the Shi'ar Empire through Lilandra, after infecting the X-Men with Nano-Sentinels and beating Beast into a coma.[449] Eventually, the X-Men uncovered her scheme, learning about her origins by psychically rescuing Xavier from her brain.[56] Meanwhile, wearing his face and telepathic might, Nova relentlessly usurped control over the Shi'ar Empire, directing the Imperial Guard to obliterate the Xavier Institute.[450] Infected by her machines, the X-Men were severely punished by the attack. As Cassandra Nova's body fell apart, Beast considered using the Sentinels to preserve Xavier's life. Alternatively, Jean Grey captured his mind, housing it within her own spirit.[451] The healer Xorn cleansed the X-Men from the infection, restoring Charles Xavier's health inside Jean Grey's body. Using Cerebra, Jean Grey scattered Professor X's mind within the psyche of all mutants on the globe. When Nova apparently succeeded in accessing Cerebra to commit mutant genocide, he was, thus, able to reclaim his body. Meanwhile, Frost tricked her into occupying the artificial brain of the alien Stuff, which had been programmed to learn.[452]

Being healed by Xorn, Professor X regained the use of his legs.[452] Despite Cassandra Nova having the intention to undermine Xavier's reputation by exposing him as a mutant, he took the opportunity to expand his dream to the entire globe through X-Corporation, an international organization to offer support to mutants. Meanwhile, he grew wary of the development of Jean Grey's seemingly limitless skills. Using Shi'ar methods, he diagnosed her in Paris, ultimately communing with the Phoenix.[453] In Mumbai, Professor X was targeted for assassination by an immensely disturbed Lilandra, being saved by Jean Grey. The after-effects of Nova's attack fractured her sanity. The Shi'ar council determined their marriage to be annulled, especially because the Phoenix was predicted to be reborn.[22] At the ruins of Genosha, Xavier gave support to the survivors who remained in the wastelands. Polaris was found in extreme turmoil, being rescued.[454] At the school, he provided her with psychic therapy, to help her heal her traumatized mind.[455] In between these events, the X-Men had a team-up with the Juggernaut after he requested for their help.[456] Afterwards, Charles Xavier offered him to stay at the institute, marking the end of their long period of discord.[457]

One of Xavier's most promising students was the telepath Quentin Quire. Gradually, however, he proved to be a challenging presence, inciting rebellion on campus.[458] Quire formed a gang and performed a riot at Xavier's inspired by mutant supremacy. They knocked Professor X out to keep him hostage with a helmet to block his telepathy in order to propagate Quire's ideas at the Xavier Institute.[459] The X-Men resisted the attack, while Xavier broke himself free.[460] It was revealed Quire had been manipulated from someone inside the school through the use of the drug Kick.[461] As he experienced delusion, Xavier assisted Xorn in putting him out of his misery. The devastating outcome made Charles Xavier reconsider his position as headmaster, motivating him to step down after the end of the semester[462] in favor of Jean Grey assuming his position.[463] He held the prizegiving ceremony soon after.[461] The mysteries inside the Xavier Institute became more complex with an assassination attempt against Emma Frost.[464] In addition to these events, Professor Xavier addressed a delicate situation when Frost provided sanctuary to a mutant boy accused of murder. Storm's X-Men believed he had to face justice.[465] The psychic predator Elias Bogan tampered with Frost's reason, making her unavailable to Xavier through telepathy.[466] Storm, Bishop, and Sage, the alias adopted by Tessa after parting ways with Sebastian Shaw resolved the situation after breaking into the Xavier Institute.[467] The philosophical differences Xavier had were made obvious, and Sage took the opportunity to express her resentment for him having put her to the degrading role of a spy.[468]

The corruption inside the Xavier Institute was revealed to be the work of Xorn, who had his Special Class subdue Professor X as he revealed himself as Magneto. Reversing his healing procedure, Xorn crippled Xavier once again.[463] Keeping his enemy restrained, tortured and sedated, he caused a massacre in New York City in retaliation for the massacre of Genosha.[469] The X-Men fought back, with Professor X ridiculing "Magneto" for his desperate and inconsequential acts. After murdering Jean Grey, Xorn was executed by Wolverine through decapitation.[470] His corpse was taken by Professor X to the ruins of Genosha, where Wolverine could not allow his memory to be respected.[471] His idealism was challenged by the heir of Magneto, Polaris, in a heated debate. Abandoned by his students, Xavier was left alone in Genosha.[472] Meanwhile, the Xavier Institute was reformed with Cyclops and Frost as its acting headmasters.[473]

Genosha[]

On the ruins of Genosha, Charles Xavier lamented the recent tragedies afflicting mutantkind. Among the debris, he was attacked by Unus' Gang, being assisted by the necromancer Wicked and the monstrous Freakshow in escaping from their ire. After parting ways with the young mutants, she shockingly met with the real Magneto, who had been undercover in his fallen nation after surviving its death.[15] They were joined by Callisto, who had been assigned by Storm as Xavier's bodyguard. A team was, thus, formed to build a community on Genosha.[474] Genoshan Magistrates proved to be their first opponents, as they had secured a canister containing Karima Shapandar, one of Bastion's Omega Sentinels. Xavier and Magneto deprogrammed her and restored her human mind. Another canister contained the telekinetic mutant Shola Inkose. The group of mutants, afterwards, asserted their right to operate peacefully before Unus.[475]

Simultaneous attacks on X-Corporation offices took place, alerting Xavier. Meanwhile, on Genosha, Xavier and his allies were incapacitated by the pirates hired by Unus, Stripmine and Appraiser, which sabotaged any investigation.[476] Depowered and incarcerated, Professor X was confused with images of Moira.[477] The team banded together to break free to defeat and arrest the looters, who had been manipulated by Dark Beast. In the aftermath, Professor X and Magneto symbolically founded the Republic of Genosha.[478] Their task was to aggregate all survivors living on the country,[479] performing rescue operations as well as providing medical treatment to the vulnerable.[480] Their mission found opposition in the form of the vile Sugar Man, who Callisto eliminated.[481] From the shadows, Mister Sinister had been responsible for the disasters undermining Xavier's activities on Genosha.[39] Around this period, at some point, the Illuminati resolved to assemble the Infinity Gauntlet to secure its immense powers. To protect themselves from corruption, they secretly became in charge each of one of the Infinity Stones. After retrieving the Mind Stone from the astral plane, Professor X became its guardian.[482]

After years of serving as the X-Men's training facility, the Shi'ar artificial intelligence of the Danger Room evolved into physical form. As Danger, she sought revenge against her creator and incarcerator, Charles Xavier.[483] Professor X could anticipate her arrival, leading Magneto to deactivate all pieces of technology on Genosha using his powers so Danger would not be able to hack into any systems. He alerted the murderous robot that, although she had vast data on fighting his X-Men, he had never battled him.[484] To survive the encounter, he designed multiple traps to slow Danger down. This gave him enough time to reunite with the X-Men, who traveled to his rescue.[485] Danger, however, gained access to the remains of one of the Wild Sentinels who had destroyed Genosha. To escape it, Beast took Xavier away, but they were followed by Danger. In a vicious fight, Beast forced her to retreat. Afterwards, the X-Men confronted Xavier about him knowing Danger had sentience and purposefully ignoring her pleas to further his students' training. In disappointment, the X-Men bitterly rejected their mentor before departing.[486]

Suffering a mental breakdown, the Scarlet Witch, wielding her reality-warping powers, launched an attack against the Avengers, leading them to disassemble and prompting Magneto to retrieve her.[487] and bring her to Xavier to repair her shattered psyche.[488] Despite his best efforts,[489] he had no success. Foreseeing she could trigger a massive mutant-related calamity, Professor X summoned the X-Men and the Avengers to address her condition, even contemplating her execution. On Genosha, her twin brother Quicksilver convinced her to tap into Xavier's powers to make the wishes of those involved in the crisis come true.[490] In the new reshaped existence, mutants ruled supreme, and Xavier and his institute did not exist.[491] Breaking through the façade, Wolverine assembled the heroes to revert the illusion and restore the world.[492] Although successful, before demolishing her construct, the Scarlet Witch cast a spell that depowered the vast majority of the mutant population.[493] This event became known as M-Day, the onset of the mutant decimation. After the ordeal, Professor Charles Xavier was nowhere to be found.[494]

Decimation[]

One of the victims of the mutant decimation, Charles Xavier lost his mutant telepathy. Despite depowered, he regained his capacity to walk. Another after-effect of M-Day was the return of Vulcan, who awakened in the remains of Krakoa. Seeking revenge for his tragic fate, he hunted Xavier, involving the X-Men in his crusade. On Muir Island, the X-Men finally found Xavier as he came out of hiding in shame. Confronted by his former pupils about Vulcan's motivation, Xavier had no option but to confess his sins as the one responsible for the death of Moira's students.[219] He also shared details about the Shi'ar's involvement in Vulcan being orphaned and enslaved. Vulcan directed his raging inferno to the Shi'ar Galaxy. The X-Men were devastated by the exposed secrets. At the Xavier Institute, Cyclops cast Charles Xavier out of the school, declaring he was no longer welcome at the mansion.[48]

In desperation, Xavier attempted to reach out to the Shi'ar Empire, but had no success for being banished from its society. Alternatively, he chose to form a team to pursue Vulcan, recruiting Nightcrawler, Proudstar, Havok, Polaris, Rachel,[495] and Darwin, who had also survived Krakoa. The team broke into a base belonging to Eric the Red hidden on Earth, which Xavier found by sharing the privileged Shi'ar technology he had with his peers of the Illuminati. After defeating cyborg bodyguards, the X-Men gained access to a spaceship.[496] Xavier disguised himself as a way not to be recognized in the Shi'ar Empire during their travel. Meanwhile, the X-Men noticed he made use of Rachel's psychic powers in questionable ways. After the X-Men confronted Skrulls shapeshifting Shi'ar, Xavier's identity ended up exposed to the Skrulls' Shi'ar hostages.[497] Betrayed by the aliens he had rescued, Xavier was abducted. However, Darwin was able to tag along the kidnappers' ship.[498] Delivered to K'Tor, who conspired to dethrone Lilandra in a coup, Xavier was brutally tortured.[499] Darwin found a very debilitated Xavier in a prison cell, while learning in horror that D'Ken had been revived and coronated Majestor with Vulcan and Deathbird at his side.[500] At D'Ken's mercy, Xavier was to publicly executed in celebration to the wedding of Vulcan and Deathbird[501] at the M'Kraan Crystal. Lilandra, the X-Men, and the Starjammers crashed the wedding with a resistance armada. Vulcan threw Xavier into the M'Kraan Crystal in hopes he would be destroyed. He proceeded to vaporize D'Ken and usurp the Shi'ar throne, ascending as Emperor Vulcan.[502] Inside the M'Kraan Crystal, Professor X had his mutant powers restored before he was retrieved by Darwin. As Lilandra tended to his wounds, she forcedly dispatched Xavier and some of his X-Men back to Earth to protect their lives.[503]

One of the Illuminati's decisions involved banishing the Hulk from the planet after one of his rampages. Xavier was not present in the meeting to agree to a vote. The plan backfired and the Hulk declared war on Earth in revenge. The Hulk directed his endless rage to the Xavier Institute. The teenage squadron of the New X-Men attacked the Hulk, until Professor X telepathically intercepted him and broadcast his hurtful memories of his exile. The Hulk demanded Xavier to inform him how he would have chosen.[504] With Xaviers' honest answer, the Hulk proceeded to have him face punishment with the remaining Illuminati, but a myriad of X-Men teams combated the green goliath to save their mentor. One of the fighters was the Juggernaut, who made a pact with Cyttorak to protect his stepbrother.[505] Despite his best efforts, the Xavier Institute was severely damaged during the battle, with Professor X begging the Juggernaut to surrender. The Hulk's rampage was swayed by the young Mercury, who desperately shared the grief and misery the X-Men had endured with the mutant decimation. Seeing the gravestones on the grounds of Xavier's school, the Hulk spared Xavier's life so that he could live his penance.[506]

X-Men's Legacy[]

From Val Cooper's mind, Professor X extracted that Magneto had survived after M-Day, which caused O*N*E to be interested in apprehending him for S.H.I.E.L.D.. As a result, Xavier obsessively looked for Magneto's whereabouts to get to him before human organizations could. Unable to locate Magneto using Cerebra,[507] Professor X and Nightcrawler searched for him investigating locals. This raised tensions between the X-Men and O*N*Em even though Magneto could not be found.[508] Extremely disillusioned by the diminished mutant population, Professor X unexpectedly sensed the birth of a mutant child in Cooperstown, Alaska.[509] The X-Men addressed the situation without briefing him, Cyclops explicitly refused his participation.[510] Alternatively, he resorted to the New X-Men, who rudely dismissed him as well, arguing he had abandoned the school during its direst moment.[511] In the midst of this, the Xavier Institute was obliterated by corrupted O*N*E Sentinels.[512]

Eventually, Cable was revealed to be the one in charge of the infant, being on the run to protect from the Marauders, Purifiers, the Reavers, and even the X-Men. Xavier questioned Cyclops about confronting his own son, but once again was reprimanded for his intrusion.[513] Having had lost hold over the baby, Cable went to Professor X as his secondary plan to recover her.[514] Xavier tended to his wounds and got explanations, learning that the baby was crucial for the survival of the mutant race in the future. Being convinced and using his telepathy, Xavier identified the child's whereabouts to be on Muir Island, to which he march with Cable.[515] After a split-up, Xavier found the messianic baby with Mystique and Gambit, next to Mister Sinister's corpse and a comatose Rogue. Gambit peacefully trusted Xavier with the infant.[511] Cable planned to take her into the future, but Bishop regarded her as a disgraceful entity. After a bloody battle had taken place, Cable vanished with the baby who he christened Hope as Bishop fired at them, shooting Charles Xavier instead. With their mentor apparently dead and the Xavier Institute destroyed, Cyclops disbanded the X-Men.[516]

Professor Xavier was rescued from the brink of death by Exodus and his Acolytes, who quietly took him to their hideout, New Avalon. There, away from the intervention of the X-Men, Exodus telekinetically dismantled Xavier's brain to work on repairing it. As a result, many of Xavier's memories were destroyed. As part of the healing process, Exodus enlisted a familiar face to help Xavier, his shadow and nemesis, Magneto.[67] Still depowered from the mutant decimation, Magneto's very existence was seen by his former Acolytes as blasphemous. As Xavier was revived from his catatonic state by Magneto and Shapandar, he was attacked by Frenzy. Magneto was able to subdue her, sparking the ire of Exodus. The amnesiac Xavier then engaged Exodus in psychic combat to protect himself and Magneto.[517] After emerging victorious, Exodus surprisingly offered him leadership of the Acolytes. He immediately declined, instead choosing to recover the missing parts of his past and his lost connections. In the end, before parting ways to a lonely journey, Xavier expressed how he believed his and Magneto's ideologies had become obsolete with time.[518]

The first step Xavier took when restructuring his life's story was investigating the life of his father by visiting Carter Ryking, who was still insane and now depowered in a mental facility.[18] This coincided with Xavier being targeted for assassination by the Assassins Guild. Protected by his former student Gambit, a member of the rival Thieves Guild, he was informed about a hit list that consisting of mutants formerly experimented on by Mister Sinister in Alamogordo, including Xavier himself, Ryking, Cain Marko, and Sebastian Shaw. When visiting Ryking a second time, Xavier found out he had suddenly passed away, supposedly of natural causes. In the ruins of the facility in Alamogordo, the Assassins Guild struck again.[58] Having investigated the mystery as well, Shaw joined Xavier and Gambit. Xavier was captured by the guild's employer, Amanda Müeller, a former researcher from Alamogordo and a subject of Sinister herself. She exposed how Mister Sinister had a secret plan to resurrect himself in the event of his death. Those he experimented on as children were part of Project Cronus, having been inserted with enough of Sinister's genetic material to be supplanted by his own personality. Müeller wanted to eliminate the potential candidates so she could host Mister Sinister's powers while suppressing his psyche. To that end, she mortally wounded Xavier, but unexpectedly caused Mister Sinister to take his body.[59] Possessing Xavier's body, Mister Sinister put Müeller down. However, as Gambit and Shaw destroyed the Cronus machine that kept his consciousness in the host body, Xavier was able to prevail and rid his mind of his enemy's influence.[519]

Worried that Mister Sinister had also hidden his essence in Cyclops, Professor Xavier finally chose to reveal himself to his former pupil. In the woods surrounding the X-Men's new base in San Francisco, Xavier telepathically probed Cyclops' mind without his full consent. In retaliation, Cyclops had Emma Frost prepare herself to psychically attack Xavier in order to investigate Sinister's possible return.[520] After being punished by Frost with his own sins, Xavier recollected some crucial lost memories. At peace, he departed, leaving the X-Men under Cyclops' leadership.[13] Wolverine realized Xavier was, in fact, alive. Enraged at Cyclops and Frost for keeping this information from him, he searched for Xavier by himself. The two old friends reunited with each other as Wolverine needed help with psychically deprogramming his amnesiac and murderous son, Daken.[521] In coalition with Sinister's reincarnated self, Miss Sinister, Shaw attempted to manipulate Daken to serve as his private assassin in an attempt to perform a coup d'etat in the Hellfire Club. Simultaneously, Xavier and Wolverine went on a mission to assess the situation.[522] Despite not approving Wolverine's brutal methods,[523] Xavier joined him in a final showdown against Shaw and Sinister, who were bested in combat. However, when Xavier accessed Daken's mind, he could not anticipate a mental backlash that put him out of commission. Free from any machinations, Daken rebelled against all present.[11] In the end, Xavier showed Daken the truth about his tragic origin to calm him down. Wolverine parted ways with Xavier afterwards, forgiving him for having manipulated the course of his life.[524]

Resuming his journey, Xavier turned his attention towards his stepbrother Juggernaut next. Wishing for reconciliation, Xavier soon realized the brutal Juggernaut, who had returned to his criminal ways to regain the power of Cyttorak, would have murdered him without a second though. As a response, Xavier trapped Juggernaut in a mental illusion, forcing him to never cross his path again.[525] His next mission involved Rogue, whom he wished to finally cure from the affliction her powers caused her. He enlisted Gambit to accompany him to the Australian Outback, where she resided in isolation.[526] Upon arrival, Xavier and Gambit found the ghost town where Rogue was supposed to be as a massive conglomerate of different environments.[527] Xavier identified the cause of the odd circumstances was Danger, who had reverted to her function as a virtual reality simulator after being targeted and corrupted by Shi'ar bounty-hunters.[528] To cease her erratic behavior, Xavier once again trapped her into a compliant program[529] with the subsequent intention of releasing her completely of any bounds. Fully free, Danger defeated the criminals, forgave Xavier, and assisted him in finding a solution to Rogue's uncontrollable abilities.[530] As his next step, Xavier returned to New Avalon, this time to fully destroy it and put an end to Exodus' activities permanently. He convinced the Acolytes to disband, with most of them joining the X-Men in San Francisco, afterwards.[531]

Lost Legions[]

After coercing Exodus to disband the Acolytes, Xavier was approached by Norman Osborn, who had ascended as head of national security.[531] Osborn incarcerated Xavier alongside other mutants in one of H.A.M.M.E.R.'s bases in Alcatraz Island, where his powers were siphoned by the Omega Machine.[532] Meanwhile, Osborn had Mystique impersonate Xavier to the public as part of his agenda to discredit the X-Men in San Francisco.[533] Osborn believed he had an ally in Emma Frost, who led his own dark version of the X-Men. In fact, she was an infiltrator and identified Xavier as one of the captives.[534] Cyclops had the X-Club repurpose the ruins of Asteroid M into an island-nation named Utopia, to which all of the mutants residing in San Francisco relocated to, outside Osborn's reach. With their people safe, Frost had her X-Men rebel against Osborn and liberate the inmates.[535] Frost and Xavier joined forces to stop Osborn's main weapon, the nigh-omnipotent Sentry. Although successful, Frost was partially infected with the essence of the Sentry's dark half, the Void, whom Xavier considered to be evil incarnate. Freed from Osborn, mutantkind resettled on Utopia.[536] In due time, Professor X performed a psychic surgery to get her rid of it. He placed Cyclops inside Frost's mind to combat the Void. However, the Void left her and transferred itself into Cyclops's mind. Frost did the same as her beloved, entering his mind to help him. With her assistance and what he had learned over the years from Jean Grey, Cyclops trapped the sliver of the Void in a psychic prison.[537] Xavier found no solace from Cyclops as part of the community on Utopia.[538] Magneto, on the other hand, having been repowered, was welcomed into the X-Men, much to Xavier's concern.[539]

The former New Mutants clashed with Legion, bringing him captive to Utopia. X-Club treated his fractured mind, with Danger recommending Xavier not to establish any contact until his son's condition improved. In the meantime, he expressed his pride for the New Mutants' maturity.[540] As Cable returned to the present with Hope, it sparked a reaction in Bastion and his Human Council,[541] who murdered Nightcrawler.[542] With Utopia sieged, Cyclops ordered Professor X to deploy Legion to attack the hordes of Sentinels raiding the mutants.[543] Through telepathy, Xavier assisted his son in selecting and controlling his numerous personalities for tactical purposes.[544] Bastion was defeated with the activation of Hope's powers, with Xavier recognizing her as his dream taken form.[545]

On Utopia, Xavier opposed to Doctor Nemesis' approach of erasing Legion's personas, claiming his son's mind could intuitively find a way to react. A dark personality assuming Moira's form emerged as a result and, as Xavier, psychically probed into the shattered mind, it revolted against him.[546] With the power to reshape universes, Moira cast an illusion on Utopia, recreating it as Fortress X, in which telepaths, including Xavier, were kept incarcerated and sedated in the X-Brig.[547] Rogue found Xavier and, after establishing physical contact with him, learned about the truth about the false reality through his memories and psychic powers.[548] Consequently, Magneto and Pryde broke him out of prison[549] allowing him to make amends with his son, share the truth with all the entrapped mutants, and expose Moira.[546] Enraged, Moira turned the world against the X-Men, while Professor X reasoned with Legion to confront his mother figure. The situation was delicate since Legion was a prestigious hero in that plane of existence, but he ultimately put Moira down and tore the farse apart. Some anomalous after-effects of the ordeal remained.[550]

Doctor Nemesis created a device for Legion to control his personalities, with Xavier supervising the procedure.[551] Through it, they realized a malfunctioning in Legion's psyches,[552] as a group of them rebelled and escaped similarly to Moira. Legion asked for his father, who assembled a team of X-Men formed by Rogue, Gambit, Magneto, and Frenzy to hunt down the fugitive personas. They planned to murder Legion to be completely freed from him, while the X-Men pursue them to be reincorporated with Xavier subduing them telepathically.[553] Thus, the leader of the rogue personalities, Styx, resolved to kidnap Xavier to interrupt their mission.[554] In the catacombs of a church in Paris, Styx mind-controlled Professor X to create tormenting visions for Legion and the X-Men as they advanced to confront him.[555] As a psychic vampire, Styx consumed Xavier's mind and, when he did the same to Legion, they met each other in an ethereal realm. In fact, Rogue had disguised herself as Legion to knock Styx out. Professor X then ventured inside his son's chaotic mind to sever his link to Styx. Afterwards, Xavier parted ways with the X-Men to take care of his child.[556]

Death of a Mentor[]

Cyclops and Wolverine had an ideological schism that split the X-Men up into two factions. While the former remained on Utopia with his militaristic army of mutants,[557] the later renovated the ruins of the Xavier Institute as the Jean Grey School, which Professor X attending its opening day and congratulating Wolverine for keeping the academy working.[558] He remained distant from both factions. However, when the Avengers went to war against the X-Men for the fate of the Phoenix, who came to Earth for Hope, Xavier and Legion sensed its arrival while enjoying the beaches of Ibiza. Due to the misguided intervention of the Avengers, the Phoenix bonded with five of Cyclops' X-Men.[559] In a few days, they completely reformed Utopia, transforming it into a paradisiacal community. Professor X visited Cyclops, worried about a potential corruption after the Phoenix Five reshaped the world, eradicating war, hunger, and poverty. The first X-Man dismissed his mentor's concerns.[560] However, the war against the Avengers escalated, prompting Xavier to telepathically ask Cyclops and the Phoenix Five to stop their crusade in severe disappointment, with no success.[561]

Rejecting an alliance with the X-Men, Professor Xavier discussed the situation with the Illuminati. Stark considered the crisis to be his fault, sparking a discussion. Ultimately, Xavier concluded he would do whatever necessary to stop his first pupil[562] and ended up siding with the Avengers contrary to his wishes, providing them with psychic cover[563] and helping them rescue their teammates from the Phoenix's prison in Limbo.[564] His priority became having the X-Men abandon their loyalty to the Phoenix, with Magneto contacting him for support.[565] He roamed the planet, gathering his former students to amass a task force against Cyclops. On the shores of Utopia, Professor X confronted Cyclops by himself, soon ambushing him with help of Avengers and X-Men. Enraged with Xavier's traitorous transgression and right before being completely corrupted as Dark Phoenix, Scott Summers lost control and assassinated Charles Xavier.[28] In the aftermath, Cyclops was incarcerated,[566] and Utopia was abandoned.[567] To remedy the negligence he believed the Avengers had for the mutant cause, Captain America proposed the creation of the Avengers Unity Division, an uncanny team of heroes that honored Xavier's dream.[568]

Red Shadow[]

Charles Xavier's funeral service was held at the Jean Grey School for Higher Learning. His grave was, however, desecrated by the S-Men,[568] servants of a clone of the Nazi villain Red Skull. Having taken an interest in destroying mutantkind, the Red Skull implanted the telepathic portion of Xavier's brain inside his own, acquiring his abilities and inciting psychic hate against mutants upon humanity.[569] The Avengers Unity Division assembled to put an end to his madness and protect Charles Xavier's honor.[570] Parallel to this, Legion also had as a mission to uphold his father's legacy,[571] while, at some point, Nightcrawler communed with Professor X's spirit in afterlife.[572]

As a secret part of his will and testament, Professor Xavier entrusted his Infinity Stone to Beast, inducting him into the Illuminati as his replacement and voice of mutantkind.[573] Parallel to this, Cyclops ran a revolutionary initiative as a fugitive, founding the secret New Xavier School in the Weapon X Facility.[574] He deeply regretted his action for having murdered his mentor. Jennifer Walters requested his presence during the reading of Xavier's will and testament.[575] During the reading, the X-Men learned in shock that Xavier had hiddenly interacted with an omnipotent mutant from their knowledge, Matthew Malloy.[576] Without Xavier, Malloy was released onto the world, obliterating the X-Men.[577] Cyclops' recruit, Tempus, travelled back in time to discuss matters with Charles Xavier and prevent the disaster.[578] Together, Xavier and Tempus rewrote history as they had Malloy's parents never meet each other. This eliminated the threat. As a result, the reading of the will only considered Scott Summers as the legitimate owner of Xavier's mansion.[579]

The Red Skull built a concentration camp for mutants on the ruins of Genosha,[580] where Magneto had been active and ended up being captured.[581] A fragment of Xavier's consciousness prevented the Red Skull from unleashing the full potential of his powers. He mentally alerted Rogue to fight back, prompting the Unity Division to march to Genosha.[582] Contrary to the Avengers' pleas, Magneto murdered the Red Skull in Genosha, which broke him free from Xavier and allowed him to ascend as Red Onslaught.[583] In disappointment, Xavier's trapped spirit begged Rogue to finish his life if necessary.[584] To stop him, the Scarlet Witch and Doctor Doom cast a spell to invert the moral axis of his mind and bring out Charles Xavier. The spell was successful, but also affected the Avengers, the X-Men, and the villains on Genosha, shifting their ethical alignments.[585] Captured by the Avengers', the Red Skull eventually vanished[586] having been rescued by Captain America.[587] As the White Skull, Xavier's sense of moral was the dominant personality who fought to restore the heroes to their proper selves. Once the inversion spell was broken, the Red Skull also resurfaced.[588]

In secrecy, and infiltrated inside the Avengers Mansion, the Red Skull resumed his vile plans[589] after gaining more control of Xavier's gifts. In his final move against the Unity Division, he trapped them in an illusion in which they believed they had recovered Xavier.[590] Cable shut down his own mind to escape the assault, while Deadpool ran away for being immune to the telepathic attack.[591] Rogue was the Skull's main soldier, brutally maiming Deadpool[592] until he protected her with Magneto's Helmet. Now immune to Xavier's telepathy, Rogue knock the Red Skull out. She brought his unconscious body to Beast in New Attilan,[593] where he performed brain surgery on him to remove the segments that belonged to Xavier. At Rogue's request, the Human Torch incinerated those parts, to prevent them from falling into the wrong hands again, and in order to finally put Charles Xavier to rest.[594]

A Man Called X[]

Professor Xavier's spirit was collected and imprisoned by the Shadow King on the Astral Plane.[595] The psychic monster planned to gain access to the physical world by amassing telepaths all over the globe. One of the affected was Psylocke, who in turn assembled a group of X-Men to combat Farouk.[596] Having waged an eternal war against the Shadow King, Xavier watched his students get over several mental traps formatted as a contest.[597] Revealing himself to Psylocke as the Shadow King started to intrude into the real world, Xavier managed to enlist Rogue, Mystique, and Fantomex as his partners,[598] which allowed him to overcome the Shadow King and finish his life. Having struck a deal with Fantomex, Professor X occupied his body, as a way for the untrustworthy living weapon to get redemption. The revived Professor Xavier called himself simply X.[3]

The Shadow King's defeat unleashed his link to power, Proteus.[599] X was a controversial figure with the mutants, who recognized their former mentor had drastically changed upon being reborn. He and Psylocke entered Proteus' mind to understand his plans, but, since X resorted to aggression, Proteus retaliated, merging him and Psylocke together.[600] To separate them, X was severely injured for he had not inherited Fantomex's self-healing capacities. While he was put out of commission to recover, the X-Men followed Proteus to Fetters Hill, a small village from which he intended to merge the real world with the Astral Plane.[601] After Proteus launched seeds into many cities of the world to transform them,[602] X convinced Psylocke to create a network between the world's telepaths to push back Proteus' influence.[603]

The Shadow King had planted a portion of himself into X's mind, which allowed him to take advantage of the network to return to the physical plane.[603] Although the X-Men were defeated by the Shadow King, X managed to recover and destroy the monster. In the aftermath, he erased the memories of all of the X-Men involved in his return, except for Psylocke as a countermeasure for a possible return of the Shadow King.[604] X revealed himself to his first students, the original X-Men, enlisting them to execute Lucifer after he had assumed control over Lago. In the process, X wished to demonstrate what a world with no X-Men could have become, despite his pupils' disappointment in his facetious methods.[49] Freed from his past shadows, X had a new dream.[604]

House of X[]

Charles Xavier set to realize Moira's proposal of uniting mutantkind by making telepathic contact with Krakoa and proposing a partnership in which the island would be transformed into a sovereign nation. Having recruited the former New Mutant linguist Cypher to Krakoa, Professor X established an interface that allowed mutants to interact with the island. In the months to come, other systems were created involving transit, defense, external technology, observation, and expansion. This allowed mutants to occupy and inhabit Krakoa.[278][605] Moreover, with the mutant circuit of the Five to create husks and Cerebro's database to link them to their souls, Xavier ended up refining mutant resurrection, which was central in populating the island. In time, the nation of Krakoa became a haven for any mutant on the universe and was introduced to the rest of the world. As its head of state, Professor X celebrated its foundation at the United Nations. Diplomatically, relationships with other nations took place through mutant pharmaceutical companies,[606] derived from technology based on the Flowers of Krakoa.[607] The governmental structure for Krakoa considered different leadership perspectives of mutantkind in the form of the Quiet Council, who composed the Laws of Krakoa.[608] Meanwhile, from the shadows, hidden within the No-Place, Moira supervised and gave advice to Xavier's and Magneto's actions. She warned them that, as a clairvoyant, Destiny was never to be resurrected for she could identify Moira's existence. She also reinforced that, in all of her past lives, mutantkind had always lost. Xavier and Magneto arrogantly tried to assuage her fears, expressing that things would be different because now they were different.[65]

Anti-mutant sectors answered to Krakoa in the form of the organization Orchis.[51] Professor X and Magneto had as a priority to stop them[43] from making the self-adaptive murder machine Nimrod operational, whose rise had doomed Krakoa in Moira's previous life.[609] Cyclops was tasked with breaking into Orchis' space station, Forge, to destroy the Mother Mold Sentinel factory. Professor X telepathically coordinated the mission, tragically experiencing the entire team's demise despite their success.[610] In a resurrection ceremony, Professor X gloriously brought his X-Men back to life.[606] Alongside Magneto, he prepared himself for humanity to oppose their initiative.[608]

Another reaction to Krakoa came in the form of the extremist organization of artificially enhanced soldiers known as XENO. Its agents raided the island, breaking through its defenses and assassinated Professor X, after elaborating a way to ambush him.[29] His death was brief as he was resurrected by Jean Grey through the Five.[30] As a consequence of this attempt, Xavier focused his efforts on consolidating Krakoa's many gains, through continued diplomacy with mankind and through his work on the Quiet Council, especially through approving the creation of X-Force, a mutant intelligence agency.[611] His attitude was met with caution by the world's leaderships, leading him to reassure that any attack on him and his people would predict a reaction.[612]

An important ally made during the foundation of Krakoa was the eternal mutant Apocalypse,[606] who shared a past history with Krakoa concerning its other half, Arakko, which had been swallowed by the demonic world of Amenth. Apocalypse managed to reach it through Otherworld, despite the disapproval of Professor X and the Quiet Council. The contact sparked a war and, with Earth threatened of getting consumed, a tournament mediated by Saturnyne was proposed.[613] The Quiet Council resolved to seal Krakoa off Otherworld, but Cyclops and Jean Grey opposed to them, proposing the reformation of the X-Men as a group of heroes independent from Xavier's rule[614] and selected through elections. Following the end of the conflict, the Arakki resettled on Earth.[615] Ill suited to life with humans, Arakko was relocated to the terraformed planet of Mars.[616]

Amidst these events, Professor X identified a dark psychic presence surfaced on Krakoa, being fed by lost shards of minds undergoing resurrection. He believed it to be Legion, sending Nightcrawler to retrieve him from Orchis.[617] In face of Legion's murder, Professor X chose not to resurrect him, but Legion performed the ritual himself, straining their already damaged relationship.[618] The creature was Onslaught, who Orchis had returned to plague Krakoa. Thanks to Legion and Nightcrawler, he was defeated, not before Professor X lamented having distrusting his son.[619][620] Another event involved Xavier's other child and daughter of Lilandra, Xandra, acting Majestrix of the Shi'ar Empire. She was murdered in a conspiracy orchestrated by Kin Crimson.[621] Sensing his daughter's dying cry, Professor X captured her psyche and resurrected her through Krakoan methods.[622]

Krakoa's Inferno[]

Mystique was a crucial agent in Professor X's plans, being an infiltrator in Orchis and a member of the Quiet Council. However, her incessant demand for the resurrection of her wife Destiny[623] went against Moira's conditions. As Mystique failed to prevent the emergence of Nimrod, Professor X and Magneto refused to attend her request.[623] With an existential conflict with Orchis and the Sentinels imminent and with their grasp over Krakoa slipping, Xavier and Magneto sought out Moira's counsel as she begun to lose hope in their plans due to her allies' arrogance and recklessness.[624] This was exacerbated when Mystique engineered her dead wife's resurrection on her own and won her a place on the Quiet Council. Enraged at Professor X and Magneto, Moira expressed her frustration with how they had failed her. As an alternative, Professor X decided to bring Emma Frost to their cabal and share with her the truth about Moira's secret role. Their plan backfired, with Frost announcing she would bear no loyalty to them. Instead, Moira indicated Xavier to select Colossus to the Quiet Council to serve their purposes.[605] Frost exposed Moira's secret to Mystique and Destiny, who kidnapped her and misled Professor X and Magneto to an Orchis Node in Terra Verde,[605] where they were ambushed and killed off by Nimrod and Omega Sentinel. Moira, meanwhile, was depowered by Mystique and Destiny, ultimately abandoning Krakoa. Frost resurrected Professor X and Magneto after sharing the complete truth of Moira's lives to the rest of the Quiet Council.[625]

Going against Krakoa, Omega Red embarked on a mission to eliminate Charles Xavier in the past using Cerebro's technology provided by Mikhail Rasputin to have his mind drift through time.[626] Simultaneously, Professor X and Jean Grey had Wolverine undergo the same process to defend Xavier's life in history.[627] During the mission, a Phalanx-infected Wolverine from a future in which Moira allied herself with the machines emerged on Krakoa to hunt her down, prompting Professor X to parallelly assign a group of mutants to stop him.[628] She made it to Forge's Armory, where Professor X spoke with her through telepathy trying to explain his position. She ultimately was murdered by the future Wolverine.[629] Meanwhile, after fending off Omega Red, Wolverine's was brought back to his body by Professor X, protecting Xavier's history.[20] Afterwards, Wolverine destroyed his future contaminated self, celebrating Krakoa's triumph with Xavier. Moira, meanwhile, revived herself as a machine.[630]

In the Quiet Council, Professor X lost his main ally, Magneto, who in face of the recent events renounced his seat to move to Arakko. His position was occupied by Hope, the mutant messiah and leader of the Five, despite Xavier being contrary to her inclusion.[631] Matters became worse when mutant resurrection was revealed to the world, causing anti-mutant sentiment to rise[632] and the immortal Eternals led by Druig to retaliate. A consequence was the awakening of the Progenitor, a Celestial who judged Earth and its people.[633] During his own personal judgment, Charles Xavier was failed by the Celestial in the form of his son, Legion. However, he ignored the inner trial as he prepared to fight the Eternals. The psychics of Krakoa hacked into the Eternal Uni-Mind to perform a coup d'état and elect the Avenger Starfox as their new leader as Eternal Prime and cease the conflict. However, Magneto fell in battle before this strategy when battling Uranos on Planet Arakko.[35] The heroes of Earth worked together to put an end to the Progenitor and save the world from its moral rule.[634] In the aftermath, Professor X summoned Storm from Arakko to get details about Magneto's end. She refused to provide explanations and rejected his authority. Xavier then expressed how his close allies lost faith in his dream, fearing he could be the next one.[635]

Mister Sinister, as an erratic influence within the Quiet Council and provider of the genetic database for mutant resurrection, harnessed Moira's reincarnation powers through cloning and replicated his genome within the resurrected. At some point, he murdered Professor X and other members at the Quiet Council.[36] Upon resurrection, he was infected with Sinister's genes.[636] During the following millennium, the universe was remade in the image of Mister Sinister. This timeline was amputated with Moira's powers being activated.[37] An interloper from this potential future, Rasputin IV, revealed the corruption in the making, causing Professor X to be sentenced to the Pit of Exile. Forge exorcised the infected from Sinister's genes, but they had their right to participate in voting impeded.[637] Deeply compromised from within, the tarnished Quiet Council's end was brought up by Professor X following Cypher's recommendation as representative of Krakoa's voice.[638]

Fall of X[]

Orchis launched an all-out attack on the Hellfire Gala, successfully murdering several mutants. With their victory seemingly secured, Professor Xavier was blackmailed into banishing every mutant to Arakko. He complied by psychically forcing mutantkind through Gateways safe for those with telepathic resistance. As an agent of Orchis, Moira was on the brink of murdering him, when Rogue saved him. Realizing that he could not sense the mutants who had left Earth, Xavier was convinced that he had shepherded them to death. Broken, he requested Rogue to get him to the deserted island of Krakoa.[52] Wandering the island, he was contacted by Frost, who informed him about her plans of resistance, but refused to take part of it.[639] He protected the lands from Orchis' intervention,[640] while Mister Sinister lingered within his body through the Project Cronus procedure, emerging when he fell unconscious during sleep. Intertwined, Xavier and Sinister were stuck in a perverted alliance against the ascension of Enigma as a Dominion.[641]

With Mister Sinister's secret assistance,[642] Professor X retreated to the outskirts of time and space, No-Place X, to be protected from Enigma, while also forming a Crew to execute his plan. After failing to prevent Enigma's ascension, he sought to travel through time and execute Moira in her infancy to prevent the rise and fall of Krakoa and, consequently, Sinister's corruption.[643] Xavier assigned Rachel to form a team to travel across the Multiverse and get information from an alternate Moira's mind. He also established contact with the White Hot Room through the sorceress Mother Righteous, who had been responsible for sequestering the mutant population he believed to have murdered. Meanwhile, in Rasputin IV, he had a warrior to defend the Crew from external threats sent by Enigma. By himself, Charles Xavier went to the past[642] finding a teenage Moira. No longer a dreamer, he held her at a gun. Rasputin IV intervened at the last time, leading Professor X to consider an alternative approach. Back to No-Place X, they got rid of Mister Sinister and, shockingly, Xavier terminated Rachel.[644]

Professor Xavier's apparent betrayal was followed by him knocking Rasputin IV out and using Mother Righteous to bring No-Place X back to the shores of Krakoa. He let himself be captured by Orchis and proposed an alliance against humanity in favor of mutantkind's survival. To ensure his loyalty, he used his telepathy to give Orchis tactical advantage in their plans under the pretense of doing whatever it took to win, including handing out Mother Righteous, exposing potentially infiltrated rebels, and acquiring nuclear weapons. This was part of an action to thwart an attack from humanity, which killed the responsible unit in the process.[4] Parallel to this, the X-Men ended up dismantling Orchis and destroying Nimrod and the Omega Sentinel. Meanwhile, Rachel's assassination and subsequent revival led to the ascension of Jean Grey as the Phoenix. In this setting, Charles Xavier could finally find Moira, revealing about his original plan of simply eradicating her existence as a child. As a machine who had joined Enigma, Moira had access to his consciousness. Xavier asked her to give the Phoenix access to Enigma to put an end to his threat. Recognizing her role, Moira complied. Gathering the psyches of all of mutantkind, the Phoenix reduced Enigma to ashes.[645]

X-Manhunt[]

Xavier's victims during his association with Orchis were brainless clones he had created using Mister Sinister's technology to avoid the death of innocents.[646] Despite this, the world at large believed Professor X to be a murderer. Thus, he surrendered to human justice for the crimes he had committed while feigning allegiance to Orchis.[645] Wolverine invaded his transport to prison to execute him for his apparent betrayal. However, a resurrected Magneto fended him off. A final debate ensued between the two facets of the destiny of mutantkind. The ruins of the Xavier Institute were repurposed as the Graymalkin Prison, an incarceration facility for mutants. From it, Xavier telepathically observed how his pupils fared in a world without Krakoa, ultimately deciding to switch his own mind off to free mutantkind from his manipulations.[53] The prison was directed by Corina Ellis, who maintained some of the X-Men captive. Cyclops and Rogue each led teams of X-Men to raid Graymalkin Prison and rescue the victims. One of Ellis' assets was the psychic Scurvy, who misdirected the X-Men into fighting each other.[647][648] Nightcrawler and Psylocke accidentally got out of the disturbance, allowing Xavier to establish contact and be freed.[649] He bested Scurvy in a psychic battle, allowing the X-Men to escape with their captive friends. However, Charles Xavier chose not to break away from prison.[24]

Professor X changed his mind about staying in prison as his daughter, Majestrix Xandra, became a target of the opposition faction of the Secret Alliance and mentally contacted him for help. Having been severely tortured, he reached out to Sarah Gaunt, a fellow inmate and former lover, to break him free. Mentally disturbed due to being affected by a brain tumor, Xavier experienced confusion and memory loss. Rogue and her X-Men were contacted by Ellis to restrain him safely, but he ended up attacking his own former students, not recognizing them.[650] From the abandoned Treehouse, he collected a Cerebro unit,[651] next seeking asylum at the Storm Sanctuary. He shared with Storm his need of performing one last resurrection. However, her fortress was attacked by Cyclops and his X-Men, who wished to make Xavier pay for his transgressions.[652] In an unexpected encounter with Quentin Quire, Xavier was surprised and defeated.[653] On the ruins of Utopia, he managed to recover amidst the chaotic fight and retrieve a secret egg,[654] from which Lilandra was reborn. Covered by Sage and Pastor Wraith, who provided him with extraction, Professor X and Lilandra got access to additional transport to leave the planet.[655] As Lilandra performed brain surgery to free Xavier from his malady, Cyclops attempted to stop and apprehend them. Professor X shared the truth about his deception and actual innocence, before bidding farewell to his X-Men.[656]

Imperial Guardsman Electron, claiming to be the illegitimate son of D'Ken Neramani, demanded his right to the throne and moved against Xandra and her bodyguard Deathbird. At the Sky Slums of Chandilar, Professor X and Lilandra prevented Electron from committing regicide against their daughter. She refused to abandon the Shi'ar Empire, however. Instead, to cover her escape, Xavier telepathically subdued the Imperial Guard and put them at Deathbird's track at her own request to protect her niece. Exiled, Xandra and her parents set course to the secret world of Urvogel to prepare to depose the usurper Electron. Professor X set to recruit other castouts from the new regimen of the Shi'ar Empire to aid them in their mission of recovering the throne.[55]

Powers

Charles Xavier is an Alpha-Level Mutant,[191][424] and later confirmed as an Omega-Level Telepath.[653] Said to possess the most powerful brain in existence,[657] and to be the world's greatest telepath[452][383][374][390][7] possessing vast psionic powers:

Telepathy: Xavier is able to read minds and project his own thoughts into the minds of others within a radius of 250 miles (on Earth). With extreme effort he can greatly extend that radius. When synced with Cerebro or Cerebra, he can connect to every mind on a planet. After he was thrown into the M'kraan Crystal, it not only restored his mutation, but made his telepathy more powerful than it had been previously, thereby making him the world's most powerful telepath once again.

  • Telepathic Illusions: Charles has the ability to create realistic telepathic illusions and cause people to experience events which are not actually occurring.
    • Telepathic Cloak: He can mask his presence from being detected by others. His abilities could at times go undetected or be counteracted by other more powerful telepaths depending on their level of skill in using their own psi abilities. He can extend these defenses to others around him as well.
    • Telepathic Camouflage: Charles is capable of masking his and/or other people's presence from those around him. He can telepathically disguise himself, making his appearance to those around him quite different (changing the appearance of clothing, as well as more involved disguising).
  • Mind Link: He has the ability to develop a mental link with any person which remains as a connection to that individual.
  • Mind Blast: He has the ability to place large amounts of information in another's mind.
  • Neural Jumpstart: While under the influence of the infectious brood, Charlie X had secretly used his telepathic abilities to momentarily unlock Mirage's true mutant potential.[658]
  • Mind Control: Xavier possesses the power to control the minds of others upon mere concentration. This power could allow him to completely shut down several people's minds, making it appear as if time has stopped.
    • Mind Possession: Xavier can possess the mind of another and use that being's body as his own.
  • Mind Alteration: He has the ability to alter the minds of others by force of will.
  • Mental Amnesia: Charles can cause loss of particular memories or total amnesia in another person or a group of people, including his own.[579]
  • Psychic Wave Manipulation: Xavier's mental talents are such that he can give his psionic power enough physical substance to affect the material world through his telepathy. Having used this faculty of his mutant power to K.O a Sentinel, knocking it a great distance away from himself with the power of his thoughts made real alone.[251]
  • Mental Shield: He has the ability to erect a psychic barrier for protection of himself and of other minds, so that he could prevent the free access to the conscious and subconscious mind of anyone.
  • Psionic Blasts: Xavier can project psionic force bolts which had no physical effects but can affect a victim's mind so as to cause the victim pain or unconsciousness and could even kill an adversary.
  • Mental Detection: He can sense the presence of another superhuman mutant within a small but as yet undefined radius of himself by perceiving the distinctive mental radiations emitted by such a being.
  • Mind Transferal: Xavier possesses the ability to transfer both his mind and powers into other host bodies if his own physical body was somehow killed.
  • Absorb Information: He has the ability to quickly process and store information, by mental transference.
  • Telepathic Learning: He is also able to telepathically teach information to other individuals. For example, when Illyana Rasputin first came to live at the X-Mansion, he telepathically taught the all of the active X-Men Russian, so that they could communicate with her.[659] He later taught the team Japanese prior to their leaving for Tokyo to attend Logan and Mariko's wedding.[660] Xavier was also able to impart months of training into the minds of four young mutants, over the span of a few hours, to insure they were better able to use their powers and work together as a team.[218]
  • Omnilingual: As a result of his telepathic abilities, Xavier is able to communicate with any individual, regardless of languages barriers.
  • Astral Projection: Charles can use astral travel and communicate with others astrally through his own will, or through contact with the thoughts and memories of others. In the astral realm, he could use his powers to create "ectoplasmic" objects. He could not engage in long range astral projection on the earthly plane.

Telekinesis: Professor X has shown low-level TK abilities.[40] Having been potent enough to disrupt high-end technological equipment with enough effort on his part.[661] Though seemingly no longer existent, they were apparently keyed to his genetics, as Cassandra Nova has the same ability and is basically a clone of Xavier.
