History

Malone was a mercenary and the leader of a group of criminals who participated in the seizure of the American Embassy in Iraq five years before his death. During the hostage crisis, Malone repeatedly subjected a Canadian nun to psychological torment, threatening to execute her and staging mock shootings until she submitted to his sexual abuse. When Delta Force stormed the embassy, Malone escaped by killing an American soldier and stealing his uniform. Unbeknownst to him, Wolverine, then operating as Weapon X, had accompanied the assault team after the Canadian government learned that its citizens were among the hostages. Before dying from a gunshot wound sustained in the crossfire, the nun asked Wolverine to make her abusers suffer as she had. He promised to fulfill her request and, five years later, tracked down Malone and his surviving accomplices, killing them one by one. Cornered at the edge of a ravine, Malone realized that Wolverine had come to exact his revenge. Rather than allow himself to be killed by the mutant, he shot himself, falling to his death.[1]
