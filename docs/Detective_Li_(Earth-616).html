History

Detective Li first encountered Brenda Swanson at Marion M. Graham Hospital, where he questioned her about the mysterious creatures involved in the recent destruction and the attacks on Pei. When the hospital was attacked by the Terror Priests and the Crane Mother's Daughters, Li attempted to maintain order and protect those present. He subsequently joined Brenda in pursuing Pei, who had been taken by Davos, but their fellow officers were killed when Davos unleashed his life-draining cybernetic weapons. Li continued helping Brenda and Iron Fist in their efforts to rescue the girl, even infiltrating Rand Tower and eventually being imprisoned alongside them in a stasis tank. After being freed, he took part in the final confrontation with Davos and the forces threatening K'un-Lun. Later, Li contacted Iron Fist to enlist his help in investigating a series of disappearances and supernatural incidents in Chinatown. He continued working alongside Rand and Pei, eventually accompanying them and Aisyi in their hunt for demons connected to Mara. Li was a determined but often humorous police officer, whose occasional bewilderment at the supernatural events around him contrasted with his loyalty to his companions.[1][2]

Personality

Li was a determined but often humorous police officer, whose occasional bewilderment at the supernatural events around him contrasted with his loyalty to his companions. He had three daughters and occasionally drew on his experience as a father when advising Rand about raising Pei.
