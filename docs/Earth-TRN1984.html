History

In this reality, Baymax wandered around counting sheep until Baymax's junk data protocol appeared in the form of a red sheep and started destroying the sheep, deeming them as "junk". After Baymax told the protocol that it was counting sheep because Hiro told him to, the protocol determines that Hero must also be junk data too and tried to delete Baymax's memories of Hiro. Baymax told the protocol that Hiro is his friend and he should not be deleted, and helped the protocol understand what friendship is and why it isn't "junk". After learning about friendship, the protocol transformed into a regular sheep and became Baymax's friend.[1]

Notes

  • This reality was seen as a "dream" by Baymax of Earth-14123.
