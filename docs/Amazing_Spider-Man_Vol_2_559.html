Synopsis for "Peter Parker, Paparazzi: Part One - The Money Shot"

Spider-Man chases Screwball, a delinquent who livestreams his crimes. When he throws a Spider-Tracer at her, however, the girl, believing that it is the sign that he has chosen her as a murder victim, turns herself in, believing him to be a serial killer. Bennett tasks Peter with finding out who actor Bobby Carr's new girlfriend or boyfriend is. Peter fails to find out who, but takes photos of Carr mistreating a waitress which please Bennett. Realizing that this is what his new boss wants, Peter begins taking embarrassing photos of celebrities using his powers. Meanwhile, a mysterious girl, she seems very interested in Carr.
