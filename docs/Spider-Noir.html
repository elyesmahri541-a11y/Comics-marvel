Spider-Noir is a superhero noir television series developed by Oren Uziel for MGM+ and Amazon Prime Video, based on the Spider-Man Noir comics. The series stars Nicolas Cage as Ben Reilly, an aging private investigator and vigilante who attempts to grapple with his past as he confronts newly emerged superpowered criminals in New York City in the 1930s. It is set in an alternate reality within the Sony's Spider-Man Universe franchise.

The series was produced by Sony Pictures Television in association with Amazon MGM Studios, and had Uziel and Steve Lightfoot serving as showrunners. Spider-Noir premiered on MGM+ on May 25, 2026, before releasing on Prime Video two days later in both black-and-white and color versions.

Notes

  • The variant of Spider-Man Noir that Nicolas Cage voiced in Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse is not the same as the variant he played on Spider-Noir.
  • A tie-in comic set before the events of the series was released as a CCXP Mexico exclusive to promote the series, and later released digitally for free reading on Marvel.com.[1] Like the show, it was published in both color and black-and-white copies.
  • Despite multiple Emmy nominations and high critic ratings, on September 4, 2026, Spider-Noir was cancelled after one season.[2]
