Marvel Action Comic Books

Marvel Action is an imprint of comics meant for readers of all-ages that tell original stories using established Marvel characters that was created as part of an ongoing collaboration with IDW Publishing.[1]

Marvel Action is one of the successors of the Marvel Adventures line of comics, and both are meant for lower-level readers. Marvel Action places an emphasis on relating these comics to viewers of the MCU film franchise among other recent popular releases.
