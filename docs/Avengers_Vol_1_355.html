Synopsis for "When Come the Gatherers...!"

The Gatherers rescue the Coal Tiger from a parallel universe, but to keep him alive they must destroy the Black Panther. Sersi and the Black Knight begin an uneasy romance, much to Crystal's disappointment. Black Widow plays the matchmaker and sets up Hercules with Taylor Madison. The Gatherers invade Avengers mansion to get information on the Black Panther, but the Avengers are prepared. Unfortunately, the Gatherers are victorious and make their way to Wakanda.

Notes

  • The Gatherers visit Earth-355 and recruit the last living person, Coal Tiger (Earth-355) into their ranks.
