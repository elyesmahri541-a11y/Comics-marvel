History

Tony Stark came to London for business and tried to talk to Captain Carter while he had the opportunity. He met at her a bad time, as she had just been ambushed by a number of S.T.R.I.K.E. agents and was on the move, though he explained that she knew his grandfather, Howard. Captain Carter kept moving, chasing a mysterious blonde woman who had been involved in her ambush. She followed her to an abandoned warehouse, where she was immediately attacked by a man with a rocket launcher. Luckily, Tony swooped in at the right moment, flying her to safety. Together, they tracked the mysterious woman further, until they found themselves ambushed by masked men.[1]

Though they fought bravely, eventually they had to admit that they were outnumbered and fled the scene to go to Tony's London lab. There, they picked up gadgets to disguise Tony and turn Peggy invisible. Afterwards, they reconvened with Peggy's neighbor Harley. After Tony found out that Peggy and her allies were investigating the prime minister, he realized that he moved in a lot of the same circle as him and could be of assistance. He met with an old friend to ask more questions, but when he pushed too far, his friend took a call from the PM and then jumped off the roof, only explaining that he made him do it.[2]

After Peggy got arrested for breaking into 10 Downing Street and attempting to attack the PM, who turned out to be vampire John Falsworth, Tony assisted in freeing her from a high security facility. In the process, a fight broke out and he came to a clash with the mysterious blonde woman, who was revealed to be John's daughter Jacqueline. She quickly overpowered him, ripping out his left arm, which appeared to be completely cybernetic. He was saved just in time by Harley, who drove a stake through Jacqueline's heart, killing her.[3]

After his adventure with Captain Carter, Tony returned to the United States, where he was seen amongst debris, facing an unknown foe.[3]

Attributes

Powers

Tony possesses a number of abilities due to being a cyborg:

  • Flight: Tony was able to propel himself forwards via boosts emerging from his feet.[1]
  • Repulsor Blasts: Tony could shoot repulsor beams from his hands and feet.[1]
  • Infrared Vision: Tony could see infrared.[2]
  • Force Field: Tony was able to project hard-light force fields from his arms.[3]

Paraphernalia

Equipment

  • Hologram Projection Manogram Clouds: Tony used these to disguise himself and others and to become invisible.[2]
