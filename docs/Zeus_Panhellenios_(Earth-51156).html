History

Zeus, the god of the sky and ruler of Olympus, was revered by the mortals of ancient Greece and Rome. An immortal being possessed by immense power and authority, he commands thunder and lightning, and together with his children, Ares, Athena, Aphrodite, and Hercules, he protects the Olympian realm.[1]
