History

When Fred was being digitalized on Baymax's head, Hiro's computer detected a glitch, what corrupted the system. In Baymax's head, Fred saw his hand all pixelated and everything he touched became corrupted, with the same effect. Baymax diagnosed Fred had a glitch and advised Hiro to install his system debug protocol. By doing so, Hiro removed the glitch from Fred's body, which manifested a body for itself, gaining some level of consciousness.[1]

Thinking she was cute, Fred named the glitch Gertrude and, against Baymax's warnings, let her loose on the robot's head. She then began corrupting Baymax's data, which would cause Baymax to cease to operate. Hiro then told Fred to hold it still for him.[1]

Fred tried to corner her against a wall, but Gertrude corrupted the wall, distracting Fred and managing to free herself and escape. Gertrude ran to a passing train and corrupted it, teleporting it onto Fred, who managed to dodge it in time. Fred managed to catch Gertrude during her fall, but she escaped and corrupted a building around Fred, almost corrupting him along with it if it weren't for Baymax's help. Unbeknownst to them, Gertrude had attached herself to the boot of Baymax's armor, corrupting it. Gertrude then rode Baymax and corrupted all the data around them. Baymax pleaded with Hiro, saying it was their last chance. Hiro then managed to aim at Gertrude and successfully shoot her, stopping her trail of destruction and restoring Baymax's data. Gertrude was then grounded by Fred.[1]

Attributes

Powers

  • Glitch Physiology: Gertrude is a glitch in Fred's digitalization into Baymax's head, who has gained a physical body due to the Baymax's system debug protocol.
    • Data Corruption: As a glitch, Gertrude is able to corrupt digital data.
    • Teleportation: Gertrude is capable to teleportate along with any digital construct she's touching.

Trivia

  • Throughout the episode, Gertrude is referred to as "she" by Fred, but as "it" by Hiro and as "that" by Baymax.
