Synopsis for 1st story

Daredevil races a motorcycle across one of the Golden Gate Bridge’s suspension cables, chasing the remote-controlled bike of kidnap victim George Smith, who has been tapped as the new Stunt-Master's latest sacrifice on his path to going viral with death-defying feats. The sound and the exhaust fog both Matt’s radar sense and his heightened ones, but he’s still able to detect an explosive device on his own bike - after all, why would Stunt-Master give him any chance at all to save George? Just before the bomb is remotely detonated, Daredevil leaps from the vehicle and grapples safely to the cable. He also hooks George’s jacket and yanks him off his bike before it careens into the water below. Daredevil lowers them both to the ground and tells the police to get George to a hospital. He turns his attention to Stunt-Master’s truck as it peels away from the scene. He makes quick work of the vehicle, hooking the steering wheel with his billy club and causing it to overturn.

Daredevil interrogates the Stunt-Master. Stunt-Master says that if Matt gets him a deal, he’ll turn state’s evidence against his boss and tell all about how he worked with Leveron, a pharmaceutical company, to distribute drugs to the homeless who were coerced into being sacrificial lambs for Stunt-Master’s videos. But before Daredevil can get anything more out of him, Stunt-Master pulls a hidden tripwire that clotheslines a passing motorcyclist. Stunt-Master steals the bike as Daredevil tends to the driver, but Matt thinks quickly and commandeers a convertible. He telescopes his club to its full length, using one end on the pedals and the hook of his club to steer the car. A thrilling chase ensues, up the steep hills of Lombard Street, veering through and between traffic and trolleys. Stunt-Master maneuvers over the top of a box truck and winds up behind the convertible. He pops a wheelie and aims the front tire at Daredevil. DD jams his club into the spokes of the front wheel and the bike flips, sending Stunt-Master flying.

Meanwhile, Kirsten McDuffie arrives at the hospital where George Smith was taken to act as his lawyer in case the police arrive. She’s told by a doctor that she has to wait as George is still unconscious, but George lays in bed listening. Sometime later, the doctors hear a noise in George’s room and enter to find him with his arm around Kirsten’s throat. He threatens to snap her neck if they don’t back up, but Kirsten stabs him in the arm with a syringe and frees herself. George shouts in frustration and leaps through the glass window of his room towards the adjoining parking garage. He comes up short and starts to fall, but Daredevil swings by and catches him, depositing them both on the roof. Matt asks George what’s wrong, but before the former Stunt-Master can spin a tale, Kirsten shouts from the open window that George has been lying to them all along.

Later, Matt interrogates George at the police station. George says he’s planned his “comeback” for years, and took drugs that altered his body chemistry to the point that he could fool Matt’s heightened senses. But these drugs also poisoned him - he has only a year or two left to live. But George says it would have been worth it - if Daredevil had failed or died during the Golden Gate Bridge race, George would have been back on top.

Back at their office, Matt and Kirsten debrief. The new Stunt-Master is still in the ICU but they’re still planning on using him as a witness against the pharmaceutical company. Matt is frustrated George fooled him so badly; he hates not knowing who he can trust. Kirsten says he can trust her. Matt says he knows he can, because he loves her. It’s the first time he’s said the “L” word. Kirsten has no objection, and they kiss.
