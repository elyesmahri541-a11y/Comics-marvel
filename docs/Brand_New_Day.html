Synopsis

With his life as Spider-Man changed after the superhero civil war over the SRA, Peter Parker resumes his life with some major changes—such as his best friend Harry Osborn returning from Europe, him no longer dating Mary Jane, and the Initiative under Stark—and with some remaining constants—rent issues, Parker luck, and a poor love life. But with the arrival of new villains and the return of some old ones, Spider-Man starts a new chapter in his heroic saga, while Peter Parker struggles with his personal life once more intersecting with his vigilante activities.
