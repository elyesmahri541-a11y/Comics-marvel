History

The Vault of Departed Spirits was an isolated, ominous-looking country house used by the Nazi agent Schultz, who operated under the guise of the medium Doctor Eternity. Schultz lured Congressman Barlow to the house by claiming that he could establish contact with the congressman's deceased brother, intending to eliminate Barlow before his Anti-Nazi Committee could uncover his activities. Barlow's chauffeur, Sam, was so unnerved by the building's sinister appearance that he suggested taking his employer to New York's Palace Theatre instead.

After Doctor Eternity and his accomplices captured Captain America and Bucky, they took the heroes to the house's underground chambers. There, the Nazis discovered the corpse of Judge Cohn, recognized him, and remarked that they now understood what had happened to him, congratulating Eternity on a job well done.[1]
