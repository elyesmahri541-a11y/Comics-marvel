History

The Air Force Drone Test Field was a military facility located in Nevada, where the U.S. Air Force stored and tested unmanned aerial vehicles. The facility also housed the Stark Drones, which were hacked and remotely activated by operatives of the Mandarin International and turned against their creator. When Iron Man confronted the rogue machines, he was forced to destroy them.[1]
