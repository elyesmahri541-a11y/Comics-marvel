History

The A.I.M. Jetpacks are armed with stud shooters, saw blades and a grappling hook shooter. They were used by A.I.M. agents against the Avengers and Detroit Steel to prevent from reaching the Stark Weather Towers, obtaining the Stark Industries Weather Machine and stopping Red Skull.[2]
