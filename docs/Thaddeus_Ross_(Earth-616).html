Thaddeus E. "Thunderbolt" Ross[16] is a decorated U.S. Air Force officer, holding the rank of three-star General.[17] Ross is best known for his relentless military campaigns against the Hulk, whom he viewed as a threat to national security and the safety of his daughter, Dr. Betty Ross.[18]

Ross became the commanding officer of Desert Base. Under the supervision of Dr. Robert Bruce Banner, he witnessed the first successful test of the Gamma Bomb, which resulted in Banner's accidental transformation into the Incredible Hulk.[19] Ross' obsession with the creature led him to spearhead numerous Hulk-hunting operations,[20] working closely with Major Glenn Talbot and often deploying elite forces such as the Hulkbusters, who operated out of Gamma Base.[21] In time, Ross' obsession consumed him, leading to his participation in covert operations that transformed him into the Red Hulk.[9] Now possessing superhuman strength, durability, and energy absorption abilities,[22] he initially operated covertly, clashing with both the Hulk[23] and other heroes[24] until his identity was ultimately exposed.[25]

Following the revelation of his dual identity, Ross operated in a more ambiguous role, aligning himself with groups such as the Avengers[26] and his own version of the Thunderbolts[27] to combat global threats, while still maintaining a firm stance on controlling superhuman activity by working with the Power Elite.[28] Despite numerous setbacks, including court-martials, exile, and imprisonment, Ross continued to re-emerge as a controversial figure, wielding power in ways that both shaped and shook the world around him.[1]

History

Early Years[]

Thaddeus E. Ross was born to Alexander and Elizabeth Ross into a New Hampshire family with a rich military heritage.[12] A Ross ancestor fought under General Sherman in the Union Army during the American Civil War, and another was made a general in the Argonne during World War I.[12] Although his grandfather was a farmer and Ross grew up on the family farm in western New Hampshire, his father pursued a career as a professional soldier and his mother was a scientist.[12] As a boy, Thaddeus befriended the neighboring Franklin Storm, and the two spent much of their time playing at war.[12] Because Alexander was away on Army duty for most of Thaddeus' childhood, their neighbor Mr. Garvin effectively became his day-to-day father figure, even beginning an affair with Elizabeth that Thaddeus never learned about.[12] Despite this, Thaddeus idolized his absent father and internalized the Ross ideal of military duty and sacrifice; his father read his son stories of valor when he was home.[12] He received a toy tank for his eighth Christmas that he played with obsessively, and by the age of 10 he had already flown solo for the first time.[12] At the age of 13, he began taking flying lessons in earnest and performed old-fashioned barnstorming shows to help pay for the costs.[12] When Thaddeus was 16, his friend Henry's father borrowed a Mustang fighter plane from the local National Guard station to fly home on leave.[12] The engine stalled over the Ross farm and the plane crashed into nearby woods.[12] Thaddeus, Henry, and their friends reached the wreck in time for Henry to see his father's body still in the cockpit, a trauma that pushed Henry into a breakdown and pushed Thaddeus further toward aviation.[12] Working for a crop duster in the next county, Ross learned more about flying and eventually bought the plane himself.[12] During a summer aerial show, he crashed and was placed in a full body cast, with doctors telling him he would never walk again, a prognosis he ultimately proved wrong.[12]

Early Military Career[]

When Thaddeus came of age, he followed his father's example and entered the United States Military Academy at West Point, where his discipline and tactical aptitude saw him graduate near the top of his class and receive his commission into the United States Army.[29] As a cadet he served under flight instructor Captain Lee, and during this period he began a relationship with Lee's daughter, Karen, whom he soon married with her father's approval.[29] Following graduation, Ross’ first posting kept him stateside, attached to the U.S. Air Force, where his barnstorming background and obsession with aviation made him a standout pilot and a promising young officer.[29] He quickly rose through the junior ranks, earning his double bars as a Captain and being assigned to a combat unit when his first major military conflict broke out in the Pacific.[29][30] In that campaign Ross proved himself a ruthless and decisive commander, leading air strikes and ground-support missions with such speed and aggression that his men began calling him "Thunderbolt" for the way he struck like a sudden thunderstorm.[29] By the end of the war Ross had been promoted to Major and decorated for bravery, earning a reputation for keeping his unit's casualties comparatively low despite the ferocity of the fighting.[29]

Stark Seven[]

While sitting at the rank of Lieutenant with the United States Special Forces, Thaddeus was approached by millionaire inventor Howard Stark, to whom Thaddeus owed a favor. Thaddeus was teamed with FBI agent Jimmy Woo, the transforming demolitionist known as the Bear, Nessa the Kitten, a Las Vegas card shark, and CIA agent "Dum Dum" Dugan in forming a group that was later referred to as the Stark Seven. Stark had the group break into Area 52, a Nevada casino owned and secretly run by aliens who called themselves the Greys in order to steal the Rigellian Recorder #451, which Stark hoped to use to save the life of his unborn son.[31] Their mission was successful,[32] but when the Greys threatened the secret of Stark's child, Howard regrouped the Stark Seven, and they attacked Area 52, destroying the Greys' operation and wiping out the aliens in order to protect the secret.[33]

Thaddeus worked a desk job for the military, and it was during this period that his wife gave birth to their daughter, Betty.[34] Thaddeus was once more called back to active duty to take part in a conflict in East Asia, where he rose to the rank of General.[35] When the conflict was over, Thaddeus returned home and helped raise his daughter, who tried to be the son that Thaddeus always wanted. Twelve years after his return home, Thaddeus' wife Karen died of cancer; it was during her funeral that Ross ever showed any sign of emotion to his daughter.[29]

Los Alamos[]

Ross was stationed at the nuclear research facility at Los Alamos, New Mexico, unless the Air Force demanded he be elsewhere due to conflict. It was here that he met nuclear physicist Dr. Brian Banner, the father of Bruce Banner with whom Ross would work years later.[36][37] Ross served as a Colonel during his first tour and eventually rose to the rank of Brigadier General in the U.S. Air Force by the time of the ceasefire. Known as "Thunderbolt" for his love of combat, Ross was unsatisfied with the Air Force’s decision to assign him to manage a research facility in New Mexico, viewing it as a mundane "desk job." Despite his reluctance, he later achieved the rank of Lieutenant General. Ross, now in command of Desert Base, New Mexico, a missile base and nuclear research site, believed that it was not the right place to raise a young girl and had Betty enrolled in a boarding school far away until she reached adulthood. After graduating, Betty returned to New Mexico in order to be close to her father, who was now her last living blood relative.[29] An attempted bombing incident at Science High School by a young Robert Bruce Banner, which was later stopped by Banner himself, claiming that his imaginary friend "the Hulk" did it, drew the interest of Ross. He would meet his aunt, Susan Banner, Bruce's guardian, who had just moved out of town with Bruce in order for him to avoid jail time. He pointed out to her that the bomb, while sophisticated, was not constructed properly, but the work impressed the military and they would invest in his education in the hopes that he could become a great weapons designer who could work for the military.[38]

Control[]

Dr. Reed Richards wrote a paper showing the need to defend the country and the planet, so the U.S. government and General Edward Harrison followed his advice and formed Control. He recruited Ross, General Cecil B. Slinkard, General Lewis Haywerth, industrialist Howard Stark, and scientist Dr. Bolivar Trask. He hoped they could monitor all future scientific progress with hopes of ending threats before they began.[39]

The Gamma Bomb[]

Ross was assigned to recruit scientist Robert Bruce Banner and utilize his research into gamma radiation to build a Gamma Bomb. Upon his first meeting with Banner, he was unimpressed with the bomb being used to destroy enemy weapons and fortifications but sparing lives; he wished a powerful weapon that would strike fear into America's enemies. Ross' first impression of Banner was that he was a weakling and a milksop. Paying a visit to Banner later, Ross was upset to find that Bruce's father Brian—a convicted murderer—was living with him and warned Banner that this could negatively impact his development of the bomb before leaving. This soon became a non-issue, as Brian Banner was found murdered.[40] As Banner began developing the weapon, the military and government were concerned about how much of taxpayer dollars were going towards the development. Ross was called to answer for the delays in design and brought Banner to assure them that the project would continue without issue. The project itself was saved, thanks to a glowing endorsement from industrialist Tony Stark, although Banner and Stark almost came to blows when Stark pointed out that the bomb could be made stronger.[41] By this time Ross was in charge of the Los Diablos Missile Base in New Mexico where he lived with his Betty. When Banner came to the site for the first time, Betty was interested in the timid scientist. Ross berated Banner for still having one of his childhood stuffed animals in his belongings.[36] Soon the Gamma Bomb project was under way and Ross oversaw the project with various other members of military brass.[42]

On the day of the Gamma Bomb test, Ross put extra pressure on Banner to ensure that the test went off without a hitch. Betty, meanwhile, took a liking to Banner, much to her father's disapproval, he hoped she would marry a military officer.[18] Moments before the bomb went off, Banner spotted a young man named Rick Jones out on the test site and told his assistant, Igor Starsky, to delay the countdown. None suspected that Igor was really a Russian spy seeking to steal the secrets to the Gamma Bomb project, and when Banner went out to pull Rick from the test site, he kept the countdown going, hoping to eliminate Banner and get a step closer to stealing the plans. Banner succeeded in getting Rick to a lead-lined trench, but the bomb went off before he could get to cover and he was bombarded with a lethal dose of gamma radiation.[18] A metaphysical barrier called the Green Door, which connected Earth to the Below-Place, brought Banner back to life in such a short time span that his death went unnoticed.[43]

History was almost changed that day thanks to the machinations of Zarrko the Tomorrow Man, a time traveller from the future of Earth-6297. He had tricked the future Hulk into going into the past to stop the Chronanarchists from changing history, as well as create an even more powerful Hulk to use as a weapon.[44] When the future Hulk arrived in this era, along with a Recording Observational Bot containing the mind of Banner, the R.O.B. tried to change history by knocking Banner into the trench before the Gamma Bomb went off. However, the future Hulk was caught in the blast and mutated into a more powerful form.[45] Ross and his soldiers tried to keep this even more powerful Hulk at bay, while the modern day Bruce Banner—given his future memories from the R.O.B.—battled the Chronararchist known as Khotto. Banner ultimately reversed the change in history, putting it back on the right course, saving the lives of Ross and all of the soldiers and erasing the super-powerful future Hulk from existence. None were left to remember that any of these events had ever occurred.[44]

Early Altercations with the Hulk[]

Unknown to Ross and everyone else except for Rick Jones — who had pledged his life to Banner for saving his life — whenever night fell, Bruce Banner turned into a powerful brute that was later dubbed the Hulk. On the night of the Hulk's first transformation, he rampaged through the military base to get to Banner's cabin and prevent Igor from stealing his Gamma Bomb plans. Ross mobilized a strike force to go after the Hulk, but when they arrived at Banner's cabin, Igor was subdued and the Hulk was nowhere to be found. Ross promised that he would track down the Hulk and destroy the creature.[18] Unaware that Banner was secretly the Hulk, Ross still began to grow suspicious of his activities outside of the base and his regular absences. When Earth was threatened with invasion by the alien race known as the Tribbitites,[46] Ross tried to destroy their ships with missiles, but their magnetic weapons proved more than a match for Earth's primitive defences. Later, when Bruce Banner was captured by the Tribbitites, he crashed one of their ships as the Hulk. When Ross and his men found Banner in the wreckage, Ross immediately assumed that Banner was a traitor and had him locked up. That night, Banner turned into the Hulk and broke out of his cell. When the Hulk kidnapped Betty, Ross was powerless to stop him. Eventually, the Hulk reverted to Banner and both he and Rick used a Gamma Ray Projector to repel the alien invaders, redeeming Banner, although Ross still found his activities suspicious.[47]

When the Hulk next resurfaced, the brute attacked a number of military missile tests, and Ross mobilized forces against him. When they finally caught up with the Hulk, it was after he defeated the Distorter, a costumed villain who used his mind control devices to bend the Hulk's will. Ross was surprised when the Distorter was unmasked and revealed to be Leon Stort, a member of the staff at Desert Base.[48] When the military sought to test out a new rocket that could escape Earth's orbit, Ross had Rick Jones brought in and played on the boy's sense of patriotism to trick the Hulk into boarding the rocket, as he was the only being with a body powerful enough to withstand the launch. Ross did not reveal until later that he had no intention of bringing the Hulk back to Earth whatsoever. When Rick learned the truth, he forced the rocket to land in order to save his friend. When the Hulk next appeared, he shut down the Ringmaster and the Circus of Crime. When Ross and his soldiers arrived on the scene after the battle, the Hulk escaped capture once again.[49] Ross later got involved in the testing of a new cobalt bomb. To assist in the tests, the military enlisted the aid of the Asgardian thunder god Thor. The test was nearly thwarted by Zarrko the Tomorrow Man. Thor prevented Zarrko from stealing the c-bomb and returned it to the military.[50] Ross then started using military resources to create weapons to capture the Hulk; ironically, in most cases they were based on weapon designs that were drawn up by Banner himself. After testing a new "Iceberg" missile, Ross ordered his soldiers to bring in Rick Jones again so he could be grilled on his connection to the Hulk and learn the whereabouts of Banner, who had been missing for quite some time. Despite threats of locking Rick up, Ross could not get any information out of the boy. Banner eventually resurfaced as he and Rick had devised a means where Banner could switch back and forth between the Hulk and his human form at will with the aid of the Gamma Ray Projector.[51]

When Ross began to realize that his daughter was falling in love with Banner, he tried to dissuade her from following those feelings, not liking the secrets that Banner was obviously keeping. Later, Betty was kidnapped by Tyrannus, a long-lived Grecian ruler who controlled one of the underground domains of Subterranea. Tyrannus ordered Ross to not impede his coming invasion of the surface world in order to ensure the safety of his daughter. Ross complied, but Betty was eventually rescued by the Hulk. Later, Ross tried to apprehend the Hulk using the Iceberg missile, but the weapon proved useless against the Hulk.[52] Ross immediately blamed the Hulk for a series of sabotages that began occurring on the base. In order to capture and destroy the Hulk, Ross hired the Fantastic Four to track down the monster, ignoring Banner and Rick's insistences that the sabotage was the work of another. Ultimately, the Hulk assisted in capturing the real saboteur, Karl Kort, another scientist who worked on Desert Base and was really a Communist spy.[53] Ross and his soldiers were equally powerless when the alien known as the Metal Master came to invade Earth, as the alien's power over metal made all their weapons useless. When the Metal Master defeated the Hulk, Ross and his men were able to finally capture the green behemoth. When the Hulk angrily rejected Rick — believing that the boy had betrayed him — Rick asked Ross if he could join the military. Ross turned Jones down, telling him that he was too young to enlist. Eventually, the Hulk broke free and, with the help of Rick and his newly-formed group the Teen Brigade, he defeated the Metal Master, forcing him to leave Earth. Ross was surprised to learn this, but was less able to believe Banner's excuse that he was on vacation in Bermuda during the crisis to explain his absence.[54] Ross later sat in on a Congressional meeting over the latest defence work being carried out by Tony Stark and Stark Industries for the military.[55] Back at the base, Ross was unable to locate Banner when he was needed to help stop the villain known as Sundown.[56] Sometime later, a giant mound was pushing through the ground near the base as part of an invasion of the surface world by the Lava Men. Ross allowed the Avengers to deal with the threat. He also insisted that Banner do something about the situation. Ultimately, Thor convinced the Lava Men to cease their invasion and the Hulk was tricked into destroying the mound.[57]

Hunting the Hulk[]

After the Hulk resurfaced in the Southwest again, Ross began devoting his energies to stopping the creature, on top of testing out new weapons being made for the military. When the Avengers Giant-Man and the Wasp tried to re-recruit the Hulk back into the Avengers, Ross mobilized his forces to capture him. Ross was almost tricked into blowing up both the Hulk and Giant-Man by the heroes' foe the Human Top, but this was thwarted by the Wasp, and the Hulk escaped. When Betty continued to show romantic interest in Banner, Ross wondered how she could love a "weakling" like Banner on a base full of soldiers.[58]

Ross soon began pressuring Banner to complete a new robotic suit of armor that would allow the wearer to withstand a nuclear bomb blast. That evening, the suit was stolen by a spy. When the spy absconded with the suit after a test, Ross assumed that Banner was piloting the suit and ordered his capture. Ross soon discovered that this was not the case after Banner was found following a battle between the Hulk and the spy in the suit of armor. Still suspicious of Banner's activities but not able to do anything without proof thanks to a presidential order protecting Banner, Ross convinced the Pentagon to send security officer Major Glenn Talbot to investigate Banner. Talbot took an immediate liking to Betty and soon became a rival for her affections, but Betty remained ever faithful to Banner. When the Hulk was knocked out after his next battle with the spy, Ross brought special restraints, ironically designed by Banner himself, to bind the Hulk and bring him back to base.[20] The base was next infiltrated by the professional spy and master of disguise known as the Chameleon, who was hired by the gamma-spawned genius the Leader to steal military secrets. The Chameleon briefly posed as Ross to get past security and dismiss the security detail around the Hulk. Ross ordered Banner's arrest when the Chameleon posed as Banner in order to steal a gamma grenade and kidnapped Betty; however, he was stopped by the Hulk and defeated. Banner was then cleared of all wrongdoing thanks to Betty confirming that the "Banner" who kidnapped her was an impostor, this still did nothing to dissuede either Ross or Talbot of any of their suspicions toward Banner.[59]

Ross next ordered Banner's latest invention, the Absorbatron, to be transported by train with Banner under tight security. The shipment was attacked by the Leader's Humanoid creation and stopped by the Hulk, although Banner was implicated in the attack and arrested.[60] Ross and Talbot did not believe Banner's claims of innocence, but still provided him with the tranquilizers that he requested, unaware that they were being used to stave off a transformation into the Hulk. However, Banner was soon released thanks to Rick Jones confiding the secret of the Hulk with the President of the United States. Banner and Talbot were then assigned to test the Absorbatron on Astra Island, but were attacked by the Leader's army of Humanoids.[61].[62] The battle with the Leader ended with the Hulk being washed out to sea and reverting back into Bruce Banner, where he was recovered by a Russian sub and taken as a prisoner to a Communist nation in Eastern Europe.[63] With intelligence reports coming in about Banner being taken to this country, General Ross immediately assumed that Banner had become a traitor to the United States. He began planning to deploy Major Talbot to covertly enter the country to recover Banner and learn the truth.[64] Soon, a coded message was received from bandit chieftain Kanga Khan of Mongolia, who had managed to capture Banner and was holding him for ransom. Ross then sent Major Talbot to pay the ransom and recover Banner.[65] When Banner returned to his home in America, he was spotted by the military, and after recovering Major Talbot, General Ross had his soldiers surrounded Banner and took him prisoner. However, thanks to the President's influence, Banner was freed once again and was again signed on to test out his Absorbatron. As with the previous attempt, the island was attacked by the Leader's Humanoids.[66] When the Hulk and the Absorbatron were captured by the Leader and taken to his secret hideout, Banner managed to sent out a distress signal bringing both the military and Rick Jones to his location. Ross, still suspecting that Banner was a traitor somehow, ordered his men to shoot to kill on sight. Although the Leader escaped, Banner was shot in the head and seemingly died.[67]

While Ross and his soldiers searched the Leader's base, Rick escaped with Banner's body, and was able to revive the Hulk in Banner's secret desert lab. Meanwhile, the Leader unleashed a gigantic Humanoid on Missile Base. Ross and his soldiers tried to keep the massive construct at bay, and Thaddeus incorrectly assumed that the arriving Hulk was in league with the Humanoid and ordered that the military's new Sunday Punch Missile be deployed to destroy both of them.[68] While the Humanoid was incapacitated by the blast, the Hulk managed to escape. Ross and his men surrounded the massive Humanoid but before they could study it, the Leader incinerated his creation. Ross then gathered his men and continued their hunt for the Hulk, tracking him back to the cave where Banner's lab was secretly hidden, where they threatened to level the cavern if the Hulk did not surrender.[69] Rick Jones tried to convince Ross and his men to stand down, but Ross refused to listen and ordered his men to fire when the Hulk refused to come out. The secret lab was destroyed and the Hulk was seemingly slain. Observing the wreckage, Ross was surprised to find all sorts of scientific equipment, finally giving Ross the proof that he needed that Banner and the Hulk were connected. However, he incorrectly deduced that the Hulk was a robot created by Banner. Demanding answers from Rick, Ross still could not get the boy to talk and ordered his soldiers to take him into custody again.[70] Following the supposed death of the Hulk, Ross was assigned to test Banner's last invention, the T-Gun, in order to find out what its function was. However, Ross was still unconvinced that the Hulk was destroyed for good and ordered Talbot to continue the hunt for him, with no luck. When the Hulk resurfaced, Ross received reports that the Hulk was heading for Washington D.C. and brought the T-Gun there. The Hulk was blasted just as he landed on the lawn of the White House and was seemingly atomized in the blast.[71]. Ross was under pressure from his superiors to find proof that the Hulk was destroyed and had not escaped.[72] Ross then attempted to question Rick again, who still refused to reveal any of the secrets that he knew about the Hulk. Ross let Rick go, but assigned Talbot to follow the boy and see what he could learn. After following Rick back to the ruins of Banner's lab, Talbot managed to discover Rick's secret: that Bruce Banner and the Hulk were one and the same.[73]

The Orion Missile Crisis[]

With Banner's secret now out, Ross hired a new scientist to work at the base, Professor Konrad Zaxon, who was creating weapons that were powered by organic energies. When the Hulk resurfaced, Zaxon devised a way for Ross and his soldiers to capture the Hulk. Zaxon proved to be a would-be world conqueror and attempted to kill the Hulk with his Organic Energy Attractor weapon.[74] However, Zaxon became victim to his own weapon, and the Hulk escaped. Ross then went with Rick and Talbot on a mission to capture the Hulk. When Betty pleaded with her father not to destroy the Hulk in case he really was Bruce Banner, Ross promised to do what he could.[75] Later, Ross allowed the X-Men to be recruited in the hunt for the Hulk, who had appeared in the northwest; Rick convinced them that the Hulk was a hounded victim. The X-Men let the Hulk go when their leader, Professor X, learned that the military intended to weaponize the Hulk.[76] Not long after this Rick, Talbot and Betty went missing, captured by Tyrannus.[77] Ross mobilized a full military search party, blaming the Hulk for the disappearances.[78] Ross later found Rick and Talbot and learned that Betty had been kidnapped by the costumed villain known as Boomerang, who was working for the terrorist organization known as the Secret Empire. They had kidnapped Betty in order to hold her for ransom so that the Secret Empire could obtain the military's new Orion Missile.[79] Ross held Talbot responsible for Betty's kidnapping and when the Hulk was spotted in the region going after Boomerang, he mobilized an attack force but ordered Talbot to stay behind with a skeleton crew to defend the Orion Missile. Meanwhile, the Hulk managed to rescue Betty from Boomerang, who gave her up in order to escape the Hulk.[78] While out on the hunt, Ross was confronted by the Hulk, who took both him and Rick Jones to where he was keeping Betty safe. Ross was surprised to learn that the Hulk had actually saved his daughter, but the Hulk eventually grew agitated and bounded away.[80]

After the base where the Orion Missile was located was attacked by Boomerang, it was repelled thanks to Talbot and his men. Ross then had the missile test moved to Florida.[81] Once the missile was ready, Ross ordered its launch, unaware that a spy named Gorki had developed a device to divert the missile and have it strike New York City.[82] The missile was stopped by the Hulk, who redirected it into the Atlantic Ocean where it harmlessly blew up, but the Hulk was blamed for the missile being diverted in the first place. It was about this time that the military located one of the Leader's former bases and his prototype Humanoid named the Hulk-Killer. Seeing this as a means of destroying the Hulk once and for all, Ross ordered the robot be activated and used to destroy the monster. However, once the Hulk-Killer was activated, it proved impossible to control and it went on a rampage until it actually crossed paths with the Hulk, who destroyed it.[83] Although Ross was bent on destroying the Hulk when he believed it was a menace, he soon realized that he was rash when Gorki was captured and his involvement in the Orion Missile situation was revealed. When the Hulk was made weak enough that he reverted to Banner, he was pulled out of the battle by Rick Jones. Ross then complied with Banner's requests for supplies to build a weapon that allowed him to destroy the Hulk-Killer when he next transformed into the Hulk.[84]

The Manhunt Continues[]

Although the Hulk was hailed as a hero, the creature was set on another rampage thanks to the secret interference of Boomerang, who was looking to get revenge against the Hulk for botching his job with the Secret Empire. This rampage came just as Ross received a letter from the President, informing Ross that he had the sole authority to grant the Hulk amnesty if he deserved it. Ross tore up the letter, determined to hunt down and destroy the Hulk.[85] When Ross returned to Missile Base, he listened to appeals from both Betty and Rick but only agreed to avoid destroying the Hulk unless there was no other option.[86] Soon, Ross had to concern himself with other matters, namely the fact that his command had been infiltrated by a Russian spy. He ordered his men to hunt down the spy, instead they found Banner, who was trying to end his life by exposing himself to a lethal dose of gamma radiation. Banner was captured and the spy, Emil Blonsky, bombarded himself with the gamma radiation instead — transforming him into a massive and more powerful gamma-spawned monster dubbed the Abomination. The Abomination easily defeated the Hulk and kidnapped Betty. With her daughter a prisoner of the monster, Ross was uneasy to come to terms with the fact that he needed the Hulk's help to get her back. But he feared the worst, as the Hulk was on the brink of death after his battle with the creature.[87] With the help of Rick, Ross revived the Hulk, who reverted back to Banner. Banner used his Infinite Weapon Ray to draw the Abomination back and weaken him enough for the Hulk to defeat him in battle. With the Abomination defeated, Ross agreed to let the Hulk go, unsure how to deal with him after rescuing his daughter.[88] It was not before long when the Hulk went on another rampage and another subsequent clash with Ross and his soldiers, but the Hulk managed to escape once again.[89]

Shortly thereafter, the Hulk and Banner went into hiding and, despite the ongoing hunt for the Hulk, Ross was unable to locate him initially.[90] Ross and his men were incapable of stopping the spy known as the Black Widow from stealing a new experimental X-76 Verti-Jet.[91] Unable to locate the Hulk, Ross called in Talbot and assigned him to Operation: Lightning, to find and shut down the terrorist organization known as the Lords of the Living Lightning.[92] However, the Lords and their leader had convinced the Hulk to attack Missile Base. While Ross was busy defending the base from the Hulk, the Lords managed to take over the base and take Betty hostage. Ross was brought to their leader and he was ordered to surrender the base or Betty would be left in a cell with the Hulk.[93] The Lords did not anticipate the Hulk's romantic connection with Betty and the creature refused to harm her and even reverted back into Banner. Talbot, meanwhile, managed to retake the base and force the Lords to retreat back to their base. With the threat of the Lords' supply of missiles, Ross agreed to allow Banner to expose himself to gamma rays in order to destroy the headquarters of the Lords of the Living Lightning. The Hulk succeeded in doing this, destroying their mountain headquarters, but the brute was buried alive.[94]

Obssession with the Hulk[]

Ross and his soldiers arrived at the scene of the ruined base and Rick pleaded with Ross to help free the Hulk. The Hulk managed to free himself. However, he was now under the mental control of the Puppet Master, who sought to use the Hulk to get revenge against his foe, Namor the Sub-Mariner. When Rick tried to approach the Hulk, the Puppet Master ordered him to swat the boy away, seriously injuring Rick. Cradling Rick's unconscious body, Ross vowed to get the Hulk, no matter what it took. When Betty arrived on the scene, Ross told her what happened, and she was devastated by the news, truly believing that she had lost Banner forever. Meanwhile, Ross and his men monitored the Hulk's battle with the Sub-Mariner across Miami, Florida, but did not engage. Ultimately, the Sub-Mariner defeated the Hulk and returned to the ocean.[95] When the Hulk went missing again, Ross and his forces tried to contain the Silver Surfer, who had decided to go to war against the people of Earth.[96] After Rick had recovered from his injuries, Ross, Talbot and Rick appeared on a television program to denounce the Hulk as a menace to society. Soon after this, the Hulk engaged in a battle with the Space Parasite in New York City. The Hulk won the bout, but was captured by the authorities when he reverted back into Banner.[97]

With Banner under sedation, Ross ordered him to be taken out of New York. Ross accompanied a convoy transporting the unconscious Banner to JFK International Airport. Along the way the convoy was attacked by the Rhino, who had been hired by Russian spies to kidnap Banner. The Rhino tried to escape with both Banner and Betty, but Banner transformed into the Hulk once again. Ross and his men recovered Betty, while the Hulk seemingly slew the Rhino. At the end of the battle, the Hulk then grabbed Betty and fled the scene, much to the horror of her father.[98] With the Hulk on another rampage through New York City, Ross appealed to the people of the city, ordering them not to leave their homes. During this rampage, the Hulk clashed with the so-called Missing Link, an irradiated caveman unleashed upon New York by Russian spies.[99] After Betty was injured in a car crash during the crisis, Nick Fury and the spy organization S.H.I.E.L.D. were pulled in to apprehend the Hulk. Ross was impressed when a massive Helicarrier pulled the two combating behemoths out of the city but was shocked when it turned out that it was not a S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier that had captured the Hulk and the Missing Link, but a Russian craft piloted by Fury's Communist counterpart, Yuri Brevlov.[100] With the Hulk missing somewhere in Asia, Ross and his command were assigned to determine what was causing a shift in Earth's axis, causing all sorts of weather disturbances. This led to Ross wondering if Banner might have something to do with it.[101] Ultimately, the crisis soon passed before Ross could learn who was responsible.[102]

With the Hulk still unaccounted for, Betty — who was still recovering from her injures — called her father daily to find out if there was any new information, much to his irritation. Soon, Ross had to defend his base from the Hulk again after the Hulk was tricked into attacking it by the Sandman. The military soon learned that the Sandman orchestrated the attack using the Hulk as a distraction while he tried to steal their new Space-Warp Ship. Not only did the Hulk ultimately defeat the Sandman, but he also saved Betty from another car crash. Ross tried to approach the Hulk to thank him, but the Hulk perceived this as an attack and fled.[103] Not wishing Betty to get into any more danger, Ross ordered her confined to her quarters. Soon Bruce Banner came to her, seeking help to cure himself of being the Hulk. The pair were confronted by Talbot and security, prompting Banner to change into the Hulk. While the Hulk tried to escape with Betty, they were both captured by the Sandman and the Mandarin, who both sought revenge against the Hulk. Ultimately, the Hulk defeated both foes—with the Sandman being turned into glass and the Mandarin escaping—but he was captured by the military, something the simple-minded Hulk blamed on Betty.[104] With the Hulk kept sedated by Neutralizer Rays, Ross scrambled to find a permanent solution to deal with the Hulk. When the Leader turned up alive and well and offered his services to contain the Hulk, Ross was convinced that the Leader was intending to right his past wrongs. This played directly into the Leader's hands, however, as he wished to get access to the base and its cache of nuclear weapons in order to trigger World War III. Despite his insidious goals, the Leader came through and built a Plasti-Thene prison to keep the Hulk subdued. This still did nothing for Betty, who insisted that they let the Hulk go free. In private, Ross lamented over his daughter being in love with Banner instead of a soldier like he wished.[105]

Soon, Betty overheard the Leader's plans for Armageddon and convinced Talbot about it. When they brought Betty's discovery to Ross, he refused to believe it, thinking that it was a trick being played by his daughter. However, Ross soon realized how wrong he was when the Leader began his opening salvo, using his vast mental powers to knock out all personnel on the base. Unaffected by the Leader's powers thanks to a special headband given to her by the Leader to wear so that she would be shielded from the Leader's mental force waves, Betty freed the Hulk,[106] who seemingly sacrificed his life to thwart the Leader's plans.[107] When the evil Inhuman Maximus the Mad and his minions, the League of Evil Inhumans, took control of the Central American island of Costa Salvador, the President of the United States initiated the mutual defence pact between the U.S. and Costa Salvador. To this end, he ordered Ross to mobilize his troops to fight off Maximus and the Evil Inhumans. As it just so happened, the Hulk was also on the island as well.[108] Ross and his men had to fend off not only the Hulk, but Maximus and the Evil Inhumans as well. Eventually, the Hulk realized that he had been tricked and turned on Maximus, incapacitating his fortress and weapons with the help of Ross and his men. Despite this, Maximus and the Evil Inhumans fled, and when Ross attempted to apprehend the Hulk again, the Hulk also evaded capture as well.[109] Ross and his men were later relocated to Florida for more missile testing. When the Hulk botched another test, Ross sent his men into the Florida Everglades to hunt him down. The hunt was complicated when Betty was kidnapped by another radiation-spawned creature called the Glob. Like most other threats, the Glob was destroyed by the Hulk and Betty was both rescued and left to be recovered by the military while the Hulk fled.[110]

Desperate Measures[]

At one point Banner gained the ability to turn into the Hulk while retaining his own personality, intelligence, and memory.[111] No longer considered a menace, Banner succeeded in winning Ross' reluctant approval for his intention to marry Betty. However, the wedding ceremony, held at the house where Betty was born, was disrupted by the Leader and his then-ally, the Rhino. Before Banner and Betty could be pronounced man and wife, the Leader used his technology to transform Banner back into a Hulk with a savage personality.[112]

Subsequently, the United States government constructed a special site for operations to capture or kill the Hulk, "Hulkbuster Base", located in the New Mexican desert. As the man most experienced with directing military operations against the Hulk, Ross was given command of the base and to the troops assigned to it, known as United States Air Force Mobile Combat Force One, but more familiarly called the Hulkbusters. The overall military operation to capture the Hulk was now known as Project Greenskin, after the color of the Hulk's body. Neither Ross nor the U.S. government now wished to kill the Hulk; if possible, Project Greenskin's mandate was to capture the Hulk and to find a means to cure him, returning Banner permanently to his human form. Ross contended that by studying the Hulk, scientists would learn a great deal about the effects of radiation upon the human body, which would bring about major medical breakthroughs for normal human beings as well.[113]

Although Ross claimed he still wanted a cured Banner as his son-in-law, he was greatly pleased when Betty married Talbot instead, since she thought that she would never be able to have Banner as her husband.[114] Around this time, Ross made an illegal bargain with the Abomination that he would have the latter cured and freed if the Abomination captured the Hulk for him.[115] The Abomination failed in this task, but Ross would return to this scheme once more in the future.[116]

With the permission of the Canadian government, Ross and a team of Hulkbusters pursued the Hulk into Canada. In the Arctic Circle, Ross was captured by the Gremlin, a Soviet agent who headed a secret base hidden in Canadian territory. Talbot and Vietnam war veteran and Air Force Colonel Jack D. Armbruster led the Air Force team that rescued Ross from the Soviet prison to which he was sent. However, Talbot was captured by the Soviets, and the Americans thought him to be dead.[117] Shocked at losing her husband, Betty blamed his apparent death on Ross, thereby causing her father great anguish. Moreover, in part due to embarrassment over his capture by the Soviets and in part because he struck a reporter, Ross was relieved of his command of Project Greenskin. Armbruster succeeded Ross as commander of Hulkbuster Base. Ross was now officially "on leave", but often accompanied Armbruster on Hulkbuster missions.[118]

Armbruster was eventually killed while saving Ross and the President of the United States from an assassination attempt. Hulkbuster Base was destroyed by the so-called Devastator, and Ross and S.H.I.E.L.D. agent Clay Quartermain led a team that rescued "Talbot" from the Soviets.[119] Together Ross, Quartermain, and Doc Samson sent the Abomination to defeat the Hulk, but once again this scheme failed.[120]

Hulkbuster Base was reconstructed as Gamma Base, and Ross was again put in command of it. Gamma Base was intended more for research into the effects of gamma radiation and a cure for the superhuman creatures this radiation had created; the capture of the Hulk was only a secondary goal. Nonetheless, Ross was still obsessed with capturing the Hulk. This obsession and Ross' continuing failure to end the menace of the Hulk permanently caused Ross to suffer a nervous breakdown. Ross was placed on medical leave and was cared for by Doc Samson, a psychiatrist. Meanwhile, Betty and Talbot were divorced since she realized that she was still in love with Banner.[121]

The Joint Chiefs of Staff promoted Talbot to the rank of Colonel and made him the new commander of Gamma Base. Talbot was determined to kill the Hulk, in part in revenge for the end of his marriage to Betty but then, due to the repeated failures to stop the Hulk, the U.S. Congress closed down operations at Gamma Base. Refusing to end his pursuit of the Hulk, Talbot committed treason by stealing the "War Wagon," a powerfully-armed flying vehicle, and used it to hunt down the Hulk himself. Soon afterwards, Talbot was killed in combat with the Hulk.[122]

Revenge[]

Apparently fully recovered, Ross returned to active duty, bent on avenging his protégé Talbot's death, as well as getting revenge on the Hulk for his nervous breakdown. The President of the United States was unable to get Congress to grant Ross manpower and funding for hunting the Hulk, but made him an adviser at the Pentagon on matters relating to the dangers posed by the Hulk.[123] Once again, Banner became capable of maintaining his own personality, intelligence, and memory when he transformed into the Hulk. As a result, the President pardoned the Hulk for all his past offenses.[124]

Ross now commanded the military's Project Earthfall, a research operation involving cryogenic facilities in which the Abomination was now held in cryogenic suspended animation. Realizing that Betty was still in love with Banner, and refusing to believe that the Hulk was no longer a menace, Ross planned to commit treason by freeing the Abomination and sending him out once more to kill the Hulk. Before Ross could do so, the Project was invaded by M.O.D.O.K., the then-former leader of the subversive organization A.I.M., who also intended to free the Abomination to use as an ally against A.I.M. itself. Ross and M.O.D.O.K. came to an agreement to cooperate in freeing the Abomination.[125]

Subsequently, the Abomination attacked the Hulk but failed to defeat him. The Hulk succeeded in getting Ross to admit that he had allowed M.O.D.O.K. to free the Abomination. Overhearing Ross' confession, Betty was appalled. She reaffirmed her love for Banner and accused her father of treason.[126] Overwhelmed by shame, Ross nearly committed suicide at his office in the Pentagon. But he decided against killing himself and instead disappeared from sight. In his absence, he was dishonorably discharged from the Armed Forces. Ross became a drifter, sinking increasingly further into insanity.[29]

Return[]

Banner, apparently permanently cured of becoming the Hulk, proposed to Betty, and she accepted. But in the midst of their wedding ceremony, the former General Ross appeared, wielding a gun and demanding that the marriage not take place. When Banner's best man, Rick Jones, tried to stop Ross, the former General shot him. Courageously, Betty confronted her father and persuaded him to let the wedding proceed.[127] Robert Bruce Banner and Betty Ross were at last married, Jones soon recovered from the gunshot wound, and Thaddeus Ross was hospitalized and placed under psychiatric care.[128]

Ross' old ally, Clay Quartermain, enabled Ross to participate in a S.H.I.E.L.D. experiment to transform him into a superhuman being by infusing some of the "living electricity" that composed the sentient electrical entity called Zzzax into Ross' body. But the experiment went awry, and Ross' consciousness and psionic energy was absorbed by Zzzax, which then broke free. Strangely, perhaps because of the strength of Ross' hatred for the Hulk, Ross' mind took control of Zzzax, submerging Zzzax's own personality. Meanwhile, Ross' original physical body remained alive, but all of that body's independent thought processes had ceased; only involuntary functions of the nervous system (such as those controlling the heartbeat) continued.[129] Controlled by Ross' mind, Zzzax tried unsuccessfully to kill both Banner and Rick, who had recently become a Hulk-like monster himself. The Ross persona had apparently again become unbalanced by its absorption into Zzzax, and when Banner forced Ross to recognize that the latter's mind now occupied a monster's form, the Ross persona was horrified. Still controlled by Ross' consciousness, Zzzax fled.[130]

Eventually, Ross' mind returned to his original physical body, bringing much of Zzzax's living electricity with it. But now a new menace had come to Gamma Base: a grotesque, unnamed mutant, which seized upon the heads of human victims and drained life energy from their bodies, killing them. Ross watched as Rick, in his Hulk-like form, tried unsuccessfully to defeat the mutant, which menaced Betty. Then, to save Betty, Banner hurled himself into the mutant's path, and thereby himself became the mutant's victim. On seeing the Hulk's bravery and Banner's self-sacrifice, Ross realized that he had gravely misjudged them both. The mutant rendered Banner unconscious without draining him of all his life energy, and then headed towards Betty. This time, Ross hurled himself into the mutant's path, and the creature took a hold of his head. But since Ross still possessed much of Zzzax's electrical energy, the mutant could not take control of Ross' mind, and Ross released all of Zzzax's energy, killing the mutant by electrocution.[14] But the strain of Ross' heroic act was too much for his aged body. Telling Betty he had been wrong about Banner and that he himself loved her, Ross died in his weeping daughter's arms.[14]

Back to Life[]

Ross' body was later stolen by the Leader, who used the powers of one of his followers to resurrect Ross, turning him into a mindless replacement for his fallen soldier, Redeemer. Ross was eventually recovered and revived by agents of the alien Troyjans, and returned to the Air Force.[131] He later came up with a more cost-effective method of confronting the Hulk when he was in his childlike stage: active non-resistance. He and his men simply did not fire on or engage the Hulk in any way. The Hulk, confused, did not smash anything, and simply leaped away. Ross made friends with the Hulk's human side, which did not last past the apparent death of Ross' daughter.[132]

Secretly Becoming the Red Hulk[]

Ross' daughter Betty was later killed by the Abomination, and the Hulk was exiled from Earth by a group of super-heroes. The grief of his daughter's death, along with lacking a purpose now that there was no Hulk on Earth for him to fight, led Ross to spiral into alcoholism and depression. On the night that Captain America was assassinated, Ross was watching the events on a television in a bar when suddenly all of the patrons in the bar except for Ross were sent into a trance, and Ross was approached by the villains M.O.D.O.K. and the Leader. The two interlopers told Ross that the Hulk would return to Earth some time soon and seek vengeance on those who had exiled him. Using revenge upon the Hulk and a promise that they would bring his daughter back to life as leverage, the two villains eventually convinced Ross to help them with their plans. Thus, when the Hulk eventually did return and initiated the event known as "World War Hulk," Ross helped fight in the battle against the Hulk while communicating with his new allies. With the help of M.O.D.O.K., the Leader, and Doc Samson, the quartet used satellites to siphon energy from the Hulk and even recover Banner's body in the aftermath of the battle using Life Model Decoys posing as S.H.I.E.L.D. agents. Transporting Banner's body away to a secret base, Ross' three allies used the body and the energy that they had siphoned from the Hulk to turn Ross into the monstrous Red Hulk — a red-skinned brute with similar size and strength to that of the regular Hulk, but with the ability for Ross to consciously transform between his human form and the Red Hulk at any time that he chose.[25]

To avoid suspicion from his disappearance, Ross and his villainous conspirators, who were calling themselves "the Intelligencia", replaced Ross with a Life Model Decoy of himself to perform his regular life responsibilities. In the meantime Ross, as the Red Hulk, went on missions directed by the Intelligencia. The Red Hulk was first officially seen by the public crossing the Canadian wilderness, where he was attacked by and ultimately killed a beastly Wendigo. The government detected the Red Hulk in the Bering Strait when a satellite detected the seismic activity of the Hulk landing on the ground. The Red Hulk made it to Dimitri, Russia, where he murdered the Abomination as vengeance for the death of his daughter.[9]

The supposed "Thaddeus Ross" (not known to be a LMD) was one of many experts called to investigate the appearance of the Red Hulk.[9] From New York, inside a newly commissioned S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier, the LMD Ross reluctantly called on the expertise of Banner to help investigate. Banner developed a report on the Red Hulk, but before he could reach the floating base, the Red Hulk attacked the Helicarrier and destroyed it in a crash landing.[133] While onboard, the Red Hulk erased all of the files on the original Hulk using a sophisticated computer virus. After this attack, the Red Hulk headed to Gamma Base in Death Valley, Nevada to stop a rogue experiment from his bosses, the Intelligencia, who had turned Rick into a giant, blue-skinned Gamma Mutate named A-Bomb. After squaring off against A-Bomb, the Red Hulk had to fight a group of robotic imitations of the Harpy as well as the original Hulk, who had all appeared on the scene. During this fighting, Uatu the Watcher came down to Earth to watch the Hulks battle, but the Red Hulk began pummeling this interlocutor until the Hulk stopped him.[134]

Super-heroes at the site of the Helicarrier crash unsuccessfully tried to figure out the true identity of this new Red Hulk, while meanwhile the Red Hulk defeated the Hulk in battle and dragged his body to the top of the Golden Gate Bridge in San Francisco. Boasting of his strength, the Red Hulk was interrupted by Thor, who came down to battle him. However, the Red Hulk managed to gain the upper hand, and the two battled all the way to the Moon, where the Red Hulk finally defeated Thor, left his body on the Moon, and leaped back to Earth in one jump. The Red Hulk caused an earthquake in San Francisco with his return impact on Earth, which led the real Hulk, the She-Hulk, A-Bomb, the Avengers, the Fantastic Four, and the X-Men to all arrive and investigate. Thor then also returned, stated his anger, and promptly defeated the Red Hulk in a few blows. Thor was about to slay him, but was stopped by A-Bomb, who told him that the Hulk must be the one to defeat the Red Hulk. The Hulk and the Red Hulk then engaged in combat, and it seemed that the Red Hulk was gaining the upper hand until he suddenly overheated and became very dizzy. This allowed the Hulk to defeat the Red Hulk. As the other heroes left, Doc Samson and Ross' LMD appeared on the scene and kidnapped Rick (just as he transformed back from A-Bomb into his human form) to keep him from nearly revealing the Red Hulk's true identity. They also approached the knocked-out Red Hulk and expressed their disappointment in his inability to kill the original Hulk, informing him that as a result he was no longer supported by the Intelligencia and was on his own from that point on.[135]

Not much time later, the She-Hulk gathered Thundra and other women to form a new team of Lady Liberators in an effort to apprehend the Red Hulk near Mount Rushmore. Their battle with the Red Hulk accidentally destroyed the sculpture of Lincoln's face, but the women were seemingly victorious. However, after most of the group left, the Red Hulk revealed that he had played possum, and forged a partnership with Thundra.[136]

The Offenders[]

To settle a bet with the Grandmaster, the Collector teamed up the Red Hulk with Tiger Shark, Baron Mordo, and Terrax the Tamer against the Hulk, Namor the Sub-Mariner, Doctor Strange, and the Silver Surfer. Eager for revenge on his counterpart, the Red Hulk agreed to take part in the fight, dubbing his team "the Offenders". He killed everyone and then went after Galactus. When the Red Hulk came to Galactus, he tried to attack him, but Galactus was merely insulted by his blasphemy and took away the Power Cosmic from him. He then sent the Red Hulk back and threatened to retaliate by destroying Earth next. Tired of taking orders from the Grandmaster, Ross killed him. Before he died, he brought the Defenders and the Offenders back to life, and the Defenders confronted the Red Hulk. The Red Hulk decided to show the Hulk the dead body of Jarella, breaking the Hulk's heart, which gave the Red Hulk great satisfaction.[137]

The She-Hulk's Investigation[]

Afterward, Banner was somehow captured by A.I.M., and was detained underneath Gamma Base to be used to create a gamma-irradiated super soldier program behind the Red Hulk, headed by M.O.D.O.K. and backed by Ross. The plot was uncovered by the She-Hulk, who was informed by a short-haired Doc Samson. The She-Hulk recruited reporter Ben Urich to help uncover the truth who, in turn, recruited Peter Parker to aid in photography. As they infiltrated the underground base, Samson revealed that he was brainwashed by M.O.D.O.K. and revealed a long-haired alter ego, who assaulted the She-Hulk and alerted A.I.M. and the Red Hulk to their presence; fortunately, Spider-Man appeared and began to fight him.

The battle soon interrupted the tank containing Banner, causing him to transform into the Hulk. The Hulk attacked his doppelganger with the aid of Spider-Man. As the battle escalated, the Hulk body-slammed his enemy into a gamma generator in the base. Tired of their endless battles, the Red Hulk totally absorbed the radiation that powered the Hulk. As the base reached critical mass, the defenseless Banner was rescued by a now-intelligent A-Bomb. The Red Hulk managed to catch up to Ben Urich and warned him that if one word of the affair saw print, he would kill everyone at the Front Line in one murderous rampage.[138]

Code Red[]

After finding out that Domino had witnessed the Red Hulk transforming from his human form, Ross and Doc Samson gave the Red Hulk his team of mercenaries dubbed "Code Red" (consisting of the Punisher, Deadpool, Thundra, Elektra, and the Crimson Dynamo XII) to hunt her down within 24 hours. When the Red Hulk ended up being blinded by Wolverine, he was rescued by the Red She-Hulk who was wearing a ripped version of Domino's outfit and wielding Elektra's twin sai. The Red Hulk then fought the Red She-Hulk when she double-crossed him. Taking the battle up to a roof, the Red She-Hulk then managed to throw the Red Hulk off the roof, postponing the battle.[139]

World War Hulks[]

The LMD Ross later confronted the Red Hulk while wearing Redeemer armor and was subsequently destroyed, an effort by Banner and the Red Hulk to remove the face of the Intelligencia's planned takeover of America. It was later revealed that the Red Hulk apparently killed Ross at the behest of Banner, with whom he had formed an alliance.[140]

The Red Hulk made an apparent meeting with Ross that concluded with Ross' death (later revealed to be a ruse) when he showed up in the Redeemer armor, intending to kill the red giant. After the memorial service, the Red Hulk met with his new ally who appeared to have orchestrated Ross' death all along, Banner. However, when the new Frightful Four attacked the Fantastic Four in the Baxter Building, the Red Hulk came to their rescue. He managed to save everyone inside, even though Mister Fantastic was kidnapped. He later went out to help the X-Men, but failed at that also. After that, he went to the Intelligencia's base and attempted to stop them. He failed when he was defeated by Hulkpool and the Hulked-Out Heroes. During this battle, Ross found himself facing off with the Red She-Hulk, where, when reverted to human form, he was revealed to be Ross to those present, with the Red She-Hulk also quickly being revealed as his daughter, Betty.[141]

After draining the Leader of his powers, Ross destroyed the Life Model Decoy of Talbot and, mad with power, took over the White House, leading into a confrontation with his old enemy, the true Hulk. Ross grappled with Banner and attacked him with the force of a nuclear explosion, but the battle ended when the Hulk knocked Ross out with a thunderclap.[142]

An Avenger[]

After his defeat at the hands of the Hulk, Ross was imprisoned in Gamma Base. Steve Rogers, who had succeeded Norman Osborn after his failed Siege of Asgard, managed to recruit Ross once his sanity returned. Banner told Ross that they had removed his energy-draining ability after discovering that it was killing him. He was released to aid against the Intelligencia's contingency plan, Scorched Earth. During this he discovered that he still possessed his absorption ability, though Banner insisted that he had not been lying when he said that it was killing Ross; in truth, it burned out his ability to transform back into his human form, and now he could only remain as the Red Hulk.[143]

Ross assisted the Avengers in their fight against the Hood, who was hunting for the Infinity Gems. Ross himself wound up wielding the Power Gem for a time. Afterwords, Rogers invited him to join the Avengers. His teammates neither trusted him nor knew his true identity, but their faith in Rogers was enough for his membership to be uncontested.[144]

Enter the Villains[]

After Ross had stopped Scorched Earth, a new threat emerged: M.O.D.O.K. Superior, a clone of the original M.O.D.O.K. designed to be free of any disadvantaging human traits that the original might have had. Meanwhile, the Red Hulk was introduced to the Life Model Decoy staff of Gamma Base, particularly Annie.[145] Ross found himself assaulted by General Reginald Fortean, his former protegé who had started to hunt the Red Hulk out of the belief that he had killed Ross. Ross was horrified, seeing his friend going down the same destructive path that he had gone down years ago. He attempted to reveal his identity to Fortean, but failed. It was then that Fortean injected him with nanites.[145]

These nanites allowed Fortean not only to track the Red Hulk, but could detect when he shifted into his human form. Upon detecting the transformation, the nanites would administer a lethal shock to Ross, killing him. This forced Ross to stay awake, as he often reverted to his human form while asleep. He then was assaulted by Zero/One and her new ally, the Black Fog. Zero/One had been present at the site of the Red Hulk's first deployment with Steve Rogers, and was transformed due to his actions. Now a human/machine hybrid, Zero/One viewed the Red Hulk as the main obstacle to her ambitions. She created the Black Fog to deal with him.[146] Meanwhile, Uatu the Watcher watched the planet destroyer named Omegex emerge in deep space. Omegex detected the traces of the Red Hulk's energy signal on Uatu from the punch he had received and tracked this signature to Earth. At the same time, the Red Hulk had been sent into space to deal with a threat. As such, Omegex did not attack Earth. Ross found himself sent through a wormhole to the planets Tiran and Randaa.[147]

Landing on Tiran, Ross found himself imprisoned and forced into gladiatorial service. Recognizing the parallel to Banner's similar experience, Ross formed an alliance with a doctor in the Tiranean king's service. He killed King Warka, and was surprised to find this made him the new king. This almost gave him rights to the royal wives, which he appeared to indulge. When the natives of Randaa attacked, Ross organized a counterattack and gained control of their government, too. He left the planets, hoping the lack of kings would allow the natives to find peace. Traveling back through the wormhole, he found the remains of his disintegrating, and a Watcher named Uravo told him it had been an illusion designed to delay Omegex; however, Ross insisted that it had really happened.[148]

While tracking energy anomalies, Ross came across a creature that he knew well: Zzzax. The energy being had been upgraded by M.O.D.O.K. Superior, who revealed himself to Ross. M.O.D.O.K. had captured Annie, and Ross fought bravely to save her. He turned Zzzax against his master using the old control techniques. M.O.D.O.K. retreated with Zzzax, and Annie was rescued and returned unharmed.[148]

Fear Itself[]

When the Serpent was freed by Skadi, he began a world-wide assault on Midgard. One of his most powerful tools was the Worthy, citizens of Midgard transformed by mystical hammers into hosts of the Serpent's greatest servants. Amongst those transformed was the Thing, who became Angrir, Breaker of Souls.[149] Angrir assaulted Avengers Tower. As the Avengers were deployed worldwide, only one remained to defend the tower; the Red Hulk. Though he ultimately failed and the tower was destroyed, his brave fight against Angrir earned him great respect. Wolverine, in particular, believed that this "proved" his Avenger status.[150]

Omegex[]

After the Serpent's invasion, Ross and Annie made their way to the former's childhood home in New Hampshire. Whilst investigating, Omegex finally found Ross and attacked him. The fight quickly carried across state lines to Boston. Despite Ross' best efforts, he could gain no edge against Omegex. His life was spared by the surprising intervention of the Black Fog.[151] Annie, meanwhile, had been mysteriously spared by Omegex. The Watcher named Uravo revealed herself to Annie and took her to Boston. She revealed that Omegex considered the Red Hulk a unique life form and, as such, it would not aim to destroy Earth. However, General Fortean entered the fight and endangered the rest of the populace. Zero/One, who no longer wished to kill the Red Hulk, introduced herself to Uravo and Annie and, at the latter's urging, took Ross out of the fight. Hoping to understand Ross, she viewed events from his life.[152]

At Ross' urging, he was returned to the fight. Attempting to absorb Omegex's energies, Ross instead found himself disabled. Zero/One took him through another trip through his past, but failed to find anything that convinced her of humanity's usefulness. Deactivating the nano-mines that Fortean had planted in his brain, Zero/One let Ross return a final time to the fight. Assuming that death awaited him, Ross made Uravo promise to keep Annie safe from Omegex. Uatu arrived to observe the event. Ross reverted to his human form, intending to die a human man; instead, Omegex dissipated.[153]

Uatu explained that the transformation made the Red Hulk's energy signature vanish, convincing Omegex that his target had perished. His task completed, he returned to where he had came from. This had been Uatu's plan all along, and by exploiting loopholes he had broken none of the Watchers' non-intervention laws. Though this failed to convince Zero/One of humanity's value, it allowed her assistant, Jacob, to deliver a journal on Zero/One to Annie. Ross, overjoyed, embraced Annie happily.[154]

The Hulk of Arabia[]

After a fight with Fortean, Ross returned to the camp that he had with Annie. Sorting through Fortean's transmissions, Ross discovered the death of Will Krugauer, an old friend who had left the military to work with the private security group Skytower. Krugauer had been killed fighting in the Middle East. Enraged, Ross charged right into the war-torn region where he had died; an area of Qatar which was in the midst of a revolution. There, Ross discovered that the rebels had super-advanced technology.[155]

The Secret Avengers arrived, intending to take Ross home to avoid an incident. Ross resisted and they clashed. During the fight, the hero known as the Arabian Knight entered the fray. Steve Rogers appeared and ended the conflict. The Arabian Knight revealed that the source of the weapons was the arms dealer and separatist, Dagan Shah. Ross was assigned Machine Man as a partner and was allowed to infiltrate Sharzhad, Shah's domain. They were soon attacked by what appeared to be the mystical Manticore.[156] The Red Hulk and Machine Man dispatched the Manticore, but it poisoned Ross, forcing him to revert to human form to heal. Ross and Machine Man briefly discussed Dr. Abel Stack before hitting an invisible force wall. Machine Man removed the illusion cast on the wall, allowing Ross to see beyond it. He behold a flourishing and beautiful city. Sharzhad had been terraformed.[157]

The Arabian Knight arrived and guided them into Sharzhad. He revealed himself as Dagan Shah, the Sultan Magus, and began to encapsulate Ross and Machine Man in crystalline structures as he had done to the real Arabian Knight. After Shah left to negotiate Sharzhad's status with the Arab League, Machine Man was able to free Ross and himself. Machine Man detected a power source below them, and so Ross broke the floor open.[158] As they fell, Ross questioned Machine Man on the minds of androids; particularly, if they could dream or not. They reached an otherworldly complex, a portal to a microcosm of another cosmos. An AI system revealed the origins of the Sultan Magus' powers. A failed Rigellian colonization attempt left a great source of power dormant on Earth. This power was transferred to Shah, transforming him and giving him an understanding of Rigellian technology. Ross determined that Dagan Shah was worth taking out just as the Sultan Magus himself arrived.[158]

Shah easily overpowered Ross and sent him spirally toward a portal to Rigellian space. He was only saved by the timely intervention of Machine Man. Shah once again gained the upper hand, but was interrupted by the Arabian Knight, who proceeded to cut off Shah's arms. As he reattached them, Machine Man was able to engineer an escape from the inner sanctum. While the Red Hulk and the Sultan Magus fought, General Fortean arrived. He revealed that Sharzhad had been formally recognized as an independent nation. The Sultan Magus taunted the Red Hulk before forcing him out of his country.[159]

The Circle of Four[]

Captain America ordered Ross to track down Agent Venom, who was a fugitive. At Las Vegas, both fought until Blackheart, the son of Mephisto, managed to bring part of Hell to Earth.[160] The Red Hulk then made an alliance with Agent Venom, X-23 (who was there seeking vengeance on whoever had stolen her blood), and the Ghost Rider (who had noticed the presence of Blackheart). They each fought their Antitheses until they were finally killed. Ross was killed by the Evangelist. In Hell, Mephisto offered the heroes a new chance to live in exchange for defeating Blackheart. They managed to get the Spirit of Vengeance, which had been trapped by Blackheart, and wanted to get it to Johnny Blaze so that he would become the Ghost Rider again. During the battle, the Red Hulk and Agent Venom were thrown away by Blackheart; Flash gave the Venom symbiote to the Red Hulk and it bonded with him as well as the Spirit of Vengeance, thus becoming the new Ghost Rider.[161]

Blackheart was defeated when the Ghost Rider used a mirror which created the Antithesis, being defeated by him. Meanwhile, Flash and X-23 were trying to help Johnny Blaze to return Hell from the dimensional portal and destroy it. When Las Vegas was returned to normal, the Red Hulk returned the Venom symbiote to Flash and the Spirit of Vengeance to Alejandra. When the Secret Avengers arrived, the Red Hulk convinced Captain America to make him trust Flash to control the Venom symbiote. However, Hellstorm noted a Hell-Mark that seemingly only he could see on the chests of the Red Hulk, Venom, and X-23.[162]

Clash with the Eternals[]

While fighting a genetically engineered Hydra created by Zero/One, the Red Hulk was being watched by the Eternals to know what to make of him, a heroic being or an enemy. After the battle was over, Sersi, who was on site, asked about what was going on; however, the Red Hulk could see that she was not a human and thought that she was a spy sent by Zero/One. Sersi assured him that she was not and as he was letting her hand go, he was attacked by Ikaris, who believed he was attacking Sersi. Following a short battle that was stopped by the volcano activating, the Eternals returned to their city after being summoned by Zuras, who now seemed to view the Red Hulk as an enemy.[163]

De-Powerment[]

After being cured from brain damage with the Extremis virus, the Hulk developed a new super-genius persona called "Doc Green". He set out to eliminate all the other Gamma Mutates in the world, and devised a cure.[164] Once Doc Green de-powered Rick Jones, Skaar and Betty Ross, Ross started monitoring the Hulk's moves. He found Doc Green in San Francisco and engaged him in combat. Doc Green tried to inject Ross with the cure in the middle of the fight, but the Red Hulk broke the needle. After having his arm broken by Ross, Doc Green lured him to a portal to his base, the Beehive. There, Doc Green took the time to scan the Red Hulk for data before teleporting him elsewhere.[165]

The following days, Ross made the Yucca Mountain Nuclear Waste Repository his base, so he could be ready and absorb its radiation in case Doc Green found him. Doc Green ultimately found Ross with Deadpool's help.[166] He also used the mercenary's services for another purpose; when Doc Green confronted the Red Hulk, Deadpool shot Ross' ear with a bullet that delivered a compound that interfered with his ability to absorb energy.[167] After a furious battle, Doc Green was finally able to subdue the Red Hulk and inject him with the cure, reverting him to his human form. The Army was alerted to the confrontation, and when they arrived, they arrested Ross for deserting his country.[168] Ross was subsequently jailed in a classified military prison.[169]

Working with the Power Elite and the Daughters of Liberty[]

During Hydra's takeover of the U.S. government, Ross led a cell of resistors against Hydra. Because of this, he was paroled following Hydra's downfall as part of an entire program set up for Hydra resistors with shady pasts. He started working for the government again,[170] but additionally became part of a secret cabal of influence brokers called the Power Elite.[28]

After the collapse of S.H.I.E.L.D., Ross was brought in to ensure that its assets did not fall into the wrong hands and obtained an advanced LMD, modeling it after himself. Suspicious of fellow Power Elite member Alexa Lukin, Ross began investigating her, leading to Lukin hiring the Foreigner to kill him. Ross used the LMD as a decoy and continued his investigation,[171] while Captain America was framed for his apparent murder.[172]

Because Ross had briefly been bonded to the Venom symbiote, Carnage - who was trying to accumulate as many symbiote codices as possible in order to revive the dark god Knull - dug up the LMD and devoured its spine.[173] The Hulk - concerned that Ross had been resurrected by the malevolent cosmic entity called the One Below All - located the LMD in a mass grave along with the similarly-mutilated corpses of countless other people who had been bonded to symbiotes and called it in, ensuring that the LMD was returned to its grave.[174] His survival known only to a few individuals such as the Black Widow, Ross was content to let the world believe him to be dead and began covertly aiding the Daughters of Liberty and their allies in their fight against Selene Gallio,[171] though he eventually revealed his survival to the team.[175]

The Return of the Red Hulk[]

When Ross was captured by the Power Elite, he was tortured by Crossbones. During the attack by Captain America and his allies, Crossbones and Alexa Lukin left Cutthroat to finish the job. Ross begged for Cutthroat to continue hurting him. Cutthroat did and to his surprise, Ross suddenly regained his ability to become the Red Hulk while maintaining his normal hair color and moustache. The Red Hulk defeated Cutthroat and downed many of the Power Elite's aircrafts. When he confronted Captain America, the Red Hulk saluted him.[1]

One World Under Doom[]

While doing some undercover missions as a one-man army, Ross was captured by Doctor Doom at some point, and brought to a underground prison where he strapped with a power inhibitor and guarded by numerous Doombots. He spent a unknown amount of time there, and discovered other super-powered individuals that were also made prisoners, those being: Deathlok, Machine Man, General Simon Ryker, Hugo Perez of the Hook Cartel, and Professor Li Wang Tang of Harvard University. They managed to communicate with each other through Morse code, and Ross learned that they were being used as Doom's personal "think tank" to run run different military scenarios. Eventually, Machine Man managed to free both himself and Ross, but during the escape, a damaged Doombot critically injured Machine Man, while Ross fought using a Doombot's severed arm. Machine Man helped remove Ross' power inhibitor, allowing him to slowly transform back to Red Hulk.[176]

The Red Hulk unleashed his rage and destroyed the Doombots. However, his stored gamma energy was depleted, forcing him to revert to his normal human form. As more Doombots arrived, Ross, disguised in Doombot armor, rescued Deathlok by delivering the microchip that removed his power inhibitor. Machine Man then saved Ross by destroying another Doombot that was about to execute him. With his full abilities restored, Deathlok helps fend off the attacking Doombots. Despite their efforts, the Doombots executed Hugo Perez and killed Li Wan Tang by filling her cell with poison gas, though Deathlok managed to rescue Ryker before the gas reached him. The survivors fled through an elevator shaft, escaping by clinging to Machine Man as he flew them to the surface. Emerging into the countryside at night, they discovered they were in Latveria, identified by a colossal cliff side monument of Doctor Doom. Their escape was short-lived, however, as hundreds of Doombots combined into a gigantic Doctor Doom, who confronted them with an ultimatum: surrender or die.[177]

Opposed by countless Doombots and one giant Doombot, the group were unable to fight them as Machine Man's batteries had been drained and Ross was depowered. Ross informed Deathlok and Ryker that there was a nuclear power plant nearby that they could use it to jump-start his Red Hulk form. Ryker decided to jump off the cliff first, only to get zapped by a Doombot, while Deathlok caused an avalanche that trapped the pursuing Doombots. He then told Ross that his military contacts couldn't help them due to an ongoing treaty. After evading more Doombots, Ross jumped off the cliff into the river near it with Deathlok and Machine Man not far behind. Hearing about what Ross is planning, Doom planned to wipe the nuclear facility off the map.[178] Ross, Machine Man, and Deathlok were carried away by the river to a shore, with Machine Man having lost more of his parts. Ross and Deathlok killed two nearby deer to use their fur to hide. They saw several Doombots in the sky above their location, meaning that Doctor Doom knew where they were going. Thankfully they came across a house near the power plan where they could rest for a bit. Ross got some new clothes and entered a computer in which he learned that Doctor Doom had become the ruler of the entire world. Deathlok repaired and recharged Machine Man, just in time as the Doombots caught up to them. Machine Man then set off a weapon satellite on the nuclear power plant for it to explode. Deathlok evacuated Machine Man from the scene before the explosion occurred and recharged Ross' Red Hulk form.[179]

Back to being the Red Hulk, Ross fought the Doombots, leaving Machine Man and Deathlok to work in establishing contact with the U.S. Army, and on Ross' behalf asked for help as they had been illegally imprisoned in Latveria. The U.S. Army agreed to extract them and instructed the group to meet them at the border. Ross, Deathlok and Machine Man escaped, and made their way to the border where they were confronted by the U.S. Army who informed them that they were in violation of the treaty America had with Doctor Doom and were now under arrest. Red Hulk attempted to fight back, only to be hit by a flying vehicle's weapon which disrupted his gamma energy. Ross reverted back to human, leaving him, Deathlok and Machine Man to be arrested by the United States for treason.[180] Brought back to America, specifically to a facility in Langley, Virginia, Ross was forced to put on a power inhibitor as Sgt. Tamika Bowden, equiped with her steel exoskeleton to keep him in line, took him outside for two minutes before going to meet her boss. Ross was put into a machine where he found a still living Ryker. After Bowden left, Ryker told Ross that they were in Project Alpha, a facility devoted to the bleeding edge of biological bio-warfare. While telling Ross how he had been able to survive, Ryker transformed into Warwolf, and demanded that Ross obey and fear him.[181]

Ross tried to defend himself from Warwolf, but it was futile as Ryker subdued him and had him restrained for experimentation. While preparing for the experimental tests, Ryker told Ross that following his arrest for violating the U.S.-Latverian treaty he had been stripped of his rank. As part of the procedure, Ryker used some controls to mess with Ross' Red Hulk form with pulses towards the gamma radiation in Ross' body so that he can learn more about his physiology. Once the study finished, Bowden went to check in on Ross, and he took the opportunity to try convince her that Ryker was secretly working with Doctor Doom. Perplexed by Ross' words, Bowden entered Ryker's office where she found evidence that confirmed Ross' claims; that being a mirror Ryker used to communicate with Doctor Doom.[182]

Ryker resumed his experimentation on Ross, but was interrupted by an alarm in his office and transforming into Warwolf went to see what it was about. In his absence, Bowden arrived to free Ross and help him to his feet, now retaking the name of Wildstreak. Meanwhile, Warwolf went to deal with the two of them, but thankfully he was knocked out when the tank containing Shark Men he was passing by erupted by Machine Man and Deathlok breaking out of it, having fallen inside it while breaking in to rescue Ross. With Ryker and the Shark Men unconscious, Wildstreak carried a weakened Ross, and led Deathlok Machine Man outside where they took down some soldiers. As they escape the facility in a stolen helicopter, Wildstreak injected Ross a dose of adrenalin to reawaken him. Ross then ordered Deathlok and Machine Man to head to the Pentagon and to call United States Department of Defense as he wished to have a conversation with the Secretary of State. However, at Washington DC, their helicopter was attacked by the aircraft that had took Ross down previously, forcing him to jump out and transforms into Red Hulk. He landed neae the Washington Monument and preparing to confront the aircraft, Ross broke the monument with the intent to spear the attacker with it.[183]

Ross dealt with the aircraft, and with that obstacle out of the way, he went to meet with a senator who thanks to Wildstreak and Machine Man was informed of Ryker's hidden projects with Doom. The senator advised Ross stop causing any more destruction outside of Latveria as Ryker was needed alive to be trialed and for Emperor Doom to be renounced. Three days later, Ross, Wildstreak, Machine Man, and Deathlok were traveling on a train in Hungary, when they came under the attack of Doombots posing as civilians. Red Hulk managed to destroy some of them, buying his group time to flee to a bunker he secretly set up.[184] Ross, Wildstreak, Machine Man and Deathlok made their way into Latveria where they worked to locate Ryker, and eventually tracked him down to Mount Doom, a mountain which had been hollowed-out to house a facility where Ryker continued his experiments. Upon entering the mountain, they were immediately attacked by the deformed clones of Red Hulk, made by Ryker from Ross' DNA. While his allies dealt with most of the clones, Ross shot Ryker in the left shoulder, before both he and Ryker transformed into Red Hulk and Warwolf respectively. The two monsters fought, but in the end Red Hulk defeated Warwolf and came up on top. With their master defeated, the Red Hulk clones stopped the fight and let Wildstreak, Machine Man, and Deathlok go before leaving as Latveria wasn't their country. With no one standing on his way, Red Hulk took up the United States flag and, going against Wildstreak's protest, planted it on top of Mount Doom as he declared his intent to conquer Latveria.[185]

However, Emperor Doom, thanks to his title of Sorcerer Supreme, captured Ross and had him imprisoned within Latveria. Once Doom perished and his rule over the world fractured, Ross was freed by the U.S. Marines, but was forced to wear a power-suppressing collar to ensure things went smoothly. The Marines caught Ross up to the current events following Doom's rule, informing him that some Americans were scared of going back to how it was before, much to Ross' dismay. Still the Marines were on Ross' side as they refused to take down the American flag he had planted on Mount Doom as ordered by the government. Ross arrived to the White House where he was cleared and his post as general reinstated, though he was still forced to keep the power dampener on as the government couldn't trust due to announcing his invasion of Latveria without their authority, with the Secretary of State Brooke condemning Ross for painting America in a bad light. Ross countered with the fact that now that Doom was gone, his country was unstable and opportunists would attempt to take advantage of it and get their hands of the advanced technologies hidden there. The discussion was soon interrupted by the arrival of Reed Richards and Ben Grimm, and Secretary Brooke turned his ire to them, baffled that Mister Fantastic did nothing to stop Doom and lamenting how the super-powered beings acted above the law. In that moment, they all received an alarm that most Doombots around the world were right now attempting to take over all of the world's governments, and the White House got attacked soon after. Mister Fantastic and the Thing attempted to fight them, but seeing that were clearly being overwhelmed, Richards was forced to remove the collar from Ross, allowing him to transform back to Red Hulk and destroy the Doombots. This event changed Brooke's mind and realizing that Ross was right about Latveria, assigned him as a consultant on the country, giving him full authority to overtake it and claim any possession located there.[186]

Thaddeus E. "Thunderbolt" Ross originally possessed no superhuman abilities; however, he allied himself with M.O.D.O.K., the Leader and Doc Samson to extract the Hulk's energy and his DNA to give himself gamma-spawned powers, transforming him into a red-skinned being similar to the Hulk called the Red Hulk.[25]

The Red Hulk:

  • Conscious Transformation: Ross will not transform into his Red Hulk form and vice versa without consciously willing it; for example, when he was knocked out by the Hulk, A-Bomb, and Thor, he did not revert for hours even though he was unconscious,[22] though he has sometimes changed back into Ross when asleep.
    • Superhuman Strength: As the Red Hulk, Ross possesses vast superhuman strength and can normally lift 100 tons.[22] He can augment it by absorbing radiation.[141] The Red Hulk has performed some extraordinary feats of strength which include over-powering and killing the Abomination with relative ease, defeating an Odinforce-powered Thor in a one-on-one fight (though it should be noted that Thor was unprepared for him, as he defeated Ross immediately after returning to Earth and later in a subsequent fight when the Red Hulk sought his aid and did not fight in earnest), punching out Uatu the Watcher, killing an Elder of the Universe and matching Beta Ray Bill's strength.[187] In a fight with the original Hulk, the narration describes the Red Hulk striking with the force of a nuclear explosion. In his fight with Thor he was able to leap past the Earth's atmosphere, requiring Mjolnir only to cover the rest of the distance to the Moon and then leaped from the Moon back to Earth unaided.[192]

However, when he reactivated his Red Hulk form after being temporarily de-powered, his hair and facial hair, which usually rejuvenate to a younger form, stayed the same as his human form; whether this means he now ages or not remains unknown.[1]

The Punisher was able to easily stab him with a knife through his chest, causing excessive bleeding, and later Wolverine's cutting him open in the arm, back, chest and across the eyes both weakened and temporarily blinded him.
    • Superhuman Speed: The Red Hulk can move at superhuman speeds as well. Once, he moved too fast for an unprepared Iron Man to reach.[9]
    • Gamma Radiation Emission: The Red Hulk constantly gives off gamma radiation. The rate of emission is increased the angrier he gets.[22] This radiation sometimes burns what even Ross touches, and at high levels, makes Ross appear to be on fire. If Ross emits too much radiation, he overheats and can explode in immense levels, which could be dangerous for his opponents.
    • Energy Absorption: The Red Hulk is capable of absorbing energy.[194] He said in his confrontation against the Hulk and his Defenders that he absorbs energy, mostly gamma, but that he has a real taste for cosmic power.[22] He has also absorbed (and taken for himself) the Silver Surfer's Power Cosmic, leaving him powerless on the ground while the Red Hulk flew away on his board.[195] However, overuse of this power left the Red Hulk unable to revert back to his human form.[citation needed]
