Synopsis for "Shattered"

As Jean found Scott having an affair with Emma in the latter woman's mind, she kicked her husband out of her mind then locked herself and Emma away from everyone else while using the Phoenix Force to pry into her memories, including declining to be her father's heir, joining and rising up the ranks of the Hellfire Club, and the deaths of the Hellions. Jean attempted to pry into Emma's memories to learn what happened between her and Scott in Hong Kong but Scott blasted the door open and told her to read his mind instead.

Afterwards, Cyclops left and drove away while Wolverine comforted Emma. Later, Beast came for Emma with roses, a bottle, and a book but found her shattered on the floor.
