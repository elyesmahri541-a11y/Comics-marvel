History

Bullseye is an Irish assassin with perfect marksmanship, hired by the Kingpin to kill Nikolas Natchios. In a attempt to kill Nicholas he struggled with Daredevil. He managed to take Daredevil's billy club, and kill Natchios with it, causing Elektra Natchios, Nikolas' daughter, to believe Daredevil killed him. Later, Elektra attacked Daredevil, seeking revenge but soon realized that Bullseye was really her father's killer. Elektra and Bullseye battled, but he slit her throat with a playing card, then stabbed her with her own sai, killing her.[1]

Bullseye chased Daredevil to a church, and they battled until Daredevil maneuvered Bullseye's hands to be shot by a S.W.A.T. sniper, leaving him unable to use his hands. With Bullseye wounded, Daredevil grabbed him and threw him out of a window, crashing onto the hood of a car. A later scene showed him hospitalized but still able to fling a hypodermic needle with enough force and accuracy to impale a fly.[1]
