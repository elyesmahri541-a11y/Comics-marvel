History

Lord Odin was the prehistoric ruler of Asgard on Earth-72630, a new timeline created when Miles Morales traveled back in time. This Odin also Said that he had fought a younger version of Godzilla one million years ago. He was found by Miles roasting meat by a campfire before agreeing to help the heroes from the other universe.[1]
