Solicit Synopsis

Alternaverse tale revealing the final fate of the Avengers! When Ultron slaughters the futures heroes, an elderly Hank Pym must assemble all the crimefighters he can to re-earn the name Avengers! But can the daughter of She-Hulk, a blind Hawkeye, an aged Human Torch and the mutant called Cannonball defeat the villains that killed the Earth's Mightiest Heroes?
