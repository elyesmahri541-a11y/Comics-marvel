History

Scrooge McDuck is the adventurous billionaire owner of the Chronicle.After Minnie Mouse saved his Number One Coin from Magica De Spell, he hired her as a reporter for the Chronicle. When the mysterious heroine Captain Marvel appeared, Scrooge designated Minnie to interview her, but she was unable to bring him results, as she was unconsciously the heroine herself. That day Scrooge was targetted by several villains hired by Scrooge's rival Rockerduck and Captian Marvel saved him every time; when the heroine was about to end her fight against the Beagle Boys, Scrooge yelled for Minnie, who he had called before in the hope she would met Captain Marvel, that caused Minnie to regain her memories understanding who he truly was and so she was finally able to interview the masked heroine.[1]
