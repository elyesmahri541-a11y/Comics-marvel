Trivia

  • The Chrysler Building is absent from the map of this game after having been present in Marvel's Spider-Man.
  • The webswinging system introduced on the Marvel's Spider-Man is updated, as it is now merged with the acrobatics system, which means that the player can swing and perform stunts without interruption in the animations.
  • JJJ theorized about the possibility of Spider-Woman, Spider-Punk, and Spider-Pig, a reference to Spider-Ham.
  • The game features to homages to the late actor Chadwick Boseman, who played Black Panther in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. In the map, West 42nd Street is renamed to Boseman Way, and a homage message is displayed after the end credits.
  • Braithwaite Bridge replaces the George Washington Bridge.
  • The list of Miles' neighbors were references to characters from other mediums. Waldo, Waldo from Where's Waldo, Andy + Buzz, Andy Davis and Buzz Lightyear, Cthulhu, and Fred and Jason, Freddy Krueger and Jason Voorhees, with 13f standing in for Friday the 13th.
  • One of the loading screens is a homage to Ultimate Comics Spider-Man #28.
  • Phin's wheel miniature is identical to Big Wheel, with the figure representing the driver, Jackson Weele.
