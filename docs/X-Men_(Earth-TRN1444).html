History

The X-Men are a team of super heroes from the Present Timeline led by Cyclops & Emma Frost.

Timestream Entanglement[]

At the start of the Timestream Entanglement, the team, alongside the living islands Krakoa & Arakko were swept up by a temporal storm. Storm, using her powers, guided the sister islands to the 2099 Timeline. Admist the chaos, Cyclops was lost in the storm and displaced to Nueva York instead of staying on the Island upon its arrival in the 2099 Timeline.[3]

Upon settling into the 2099 Timeline, the X-Men quickly carved out their place, with the team's lineup changing signficantly. Magik, Psylocke and Namor joined the team, while Jubilee left to find a solution for her son's condition.[4] Gambit & Rogue also left during this time to go on their intergalactic honeymoon.[5]

Hellfire Gala[]

During the 2025 Hellfire Gala, Krakoa was attacked by Ultron. The X-Men and their allies banded together to stop Ultron from taking over Cerebro, but were unable to prevent him from corrupting Arakko. Together with an avatar of Krakoa and with help from their allies, the X-Men were able to purge the island of Ultron's corruption.[5]

Sins of Alchemax[]

Believing Cyclops to be lost in the time-stream, the X-Men learned that he had instead been captured by Alchemax, who had been using his optic blasts to power their time-displacement technology. The X-Men were attacked by Alchemax's Corporate Raiders. Deadpool and Devil Dinosaur later recruited them to storm Alchemax to rescue Moon Girl and Cyclops.[1]

As part of the plan, Jean Grey led a group of X-Men to Alchemax Headquarters, freeing Cyclops and using the Phoenix Force to absorb the excess energy Cyclops' powers were generating due to Alchemax's experiments.[6]

The Mystery of Thebes[]

Upon sensing a spike in energy and the subsequent disappearance of a powerful mutant energy signature, Emma Frost called the X-Men to Thebes in the Collapsing Timeline to investigate Apocalypse's death. They were among a group of heroes who became trapped in Thebes when Apocalypse's Faith Harvesting Engine began to spiral out of control, trapping those inside and cutting them off from the outside worlds.[7]

Notes

  • The X-Men in Marvel Rivals are based on the X-Men during the Krakoan Age.
