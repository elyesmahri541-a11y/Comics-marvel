History

Origins and early years[]

The Sanguine Tree is a tree of divine origins, directly made from the blood of God (The One Above All).[1] It supposedly laid very deep within the southern-southwest of the Requiem Plain, a primordial warfield where the Mother of Horrors was imprisoned for her actions against the One Above All's design.[2]

The Sanguine Tree was known to an old race of creatures inhabiting the Requiem Plain, known as the Rutlings, who seemingly were kept alive by its healing properties.[1]

Modern Age[]

After the "Infernal Hulk" (Eldest) declared a new Age of Monsters upon Earth, Reed Richards and some of the Avengers began looking for a weapon that could kill Hulk. With Bruce Banner on their side, all of them ventured into the Requiem Plain. The Vision sensed life near, below them. There, they were quickly ambushed by a threatened Skaar, but the Rutlings joined them as the heroes tried to explain themselves to Skaar. The Rutlings noticed the wounds on Iron Man after his deadly clash with Hulk. Recognizing their pure intentions, the Rutlings asked the heroes to follow them, but Skaar attempted to stop them, believing that it was too risky and that the heroes would abuse them, take the Tree and cause the Rutlings' death due to their old age. The Rutlings told Skaar that they were meant to help the heroes and took them to the Sanguine Tree. Skaar then asked Tony to go closer to the Tree and let its light touch him, and the Tree healed him. The Tree was directly grown from the blood of the author of creation. Witnessing its magnificence, the heroes believed the Tree to be capable of defeating Hulk and stopping his new Age of Monsters. This was also seen by Eldest through a vision, prompting her to intervene and stop the heroes.[1]

The Tree was taken to the Baxter Building, and Reed crafted a machine known as the Immaculate Machine, a supposed quantum projector, with the Sanguine Tree at its center. He intended to connect the Tree to all of Earth's biome and release the purifying hormone from the tree throughout the atmosphere, which would, in theory, undo all of Hulk's corruptive effects across Earth, a major risk considering the Tree's extreme potency in purification. The Fantastic Four brought in one of Eldest's mutates, and with a tissue sample, Reed believed he could adjust everything in a way that could cure every corrupted being. But they were quickly distracted from their work by a loud noise, the power being taken out, and the emergency power being insufficient to run the Immaculate Machine. Taking the Sanguine Tree down with them (as Reed didn't want to leave the mutate with it), the F4 went underneath the Baxter Building and, to their horror, were confronted by Hulk, who also happened to have been building a corruptive garden down there. Reed tried throwing the Tree over to the Hulk to see the effects it would have on him, and it did harm the Hulk a little, but it was quickly brushed off. The F3 had a deadly clash with Hulk, with Reed tasked with protecting the Tree, but he was also quickly stopped after the others were also corrupted, and the Sanguine Tree fell into the hands of Hulk.[3]

Properties

The Sanguine Tree produced an hormone that acted as a purifying and a functional opposite to the Infernal Hulk.[3] The hormone also had healing properties,[3] seemingly preserving the old Rutlings, instantly healing Iron Man's wounds when he basked in its light.[1] Though, its effects weren't strong enough to allow blind people to see or deaf people to hear.[3]
