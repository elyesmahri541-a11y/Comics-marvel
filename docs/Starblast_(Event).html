Synopsis

In search of the Star Brand, Skeletron and his crew traveled to Earth's moon and captured The Watcher, forcing him to give them information. Planting a thruster on the moon as a distraction, two of his crew, Threkker and Codabac are sent to capture the bearer of the Starbrand, Kayla Ballantine. The thruster on the moon captures several Earth heroes, though they eventually destroy it. Nova attempts to stop the Starblasters from capturing Kayla, but is defeated.[1] Quasar gathered a group of heroes to find Kayla, and they headed into space, only to encounter Gladiator and a Shi'ar ship.[2] On Earth, a group of Secret Defenders stopped a creature left behind by the Starblasters.[3] In space, Quasar and his group have comandeered the Shi'ar ship, leading to a confrontation with members of the Imperial Guard. Eventually, a compromise is reached, and the groups work together to reach the the Stranger's planet.[4] Teleporting to the surface, they engage the Starblasters in battle. During this, Skeletron is working with the Stranger to examime the Star Brand.[5]

After the Starblasters left Earth, their ally, the Fomalhauti Xlyym stayed behind. His goal was to flood the surface, making a new home for his people. Triton discovered his base and was attacked by enslaved Banari, including Trieste. Namor, meanwhile, was working to recreate Hydrobase with Newell and allies including Carrie Alexander, with help from the Navy. Namor encountered the now mind-controlled Triton and fights the Banari Sisterhood, who are incensed upon learning the Atlanteans accidentally slew the Banari Brotherhood[6]. Fighting them off and freeing Triton, Namor and Stingray head to the Mariana Trench, while Triton, Tamara Rahn and Tiger Shark head to the mouth of the Amazon. Namor and Newell's submarine is damaged, trapping them inside, while Tamara, Tiger Shark and Triton are pulled beneath the sea floor.[7]
