Venom

Venom Symbiote
Venom is a symbiote who bonds to a host and becomes a lethal protector, commonly acting as an unconventional anti-hero. The Venom Symbiote is an alien symbiotic organism who originally merged with the amazing Spider-Man, enhancing his spider-like powers. Their connection was cut short. After this, Eddie Brock has ever since become the main partner of Venom, experiencing many adventures with its powers both as a hero and as a villain and sharing a special connection with the symbiote. Mac Gargan, the vicious criminal known Scorpion usurped the Venom name and powers, becoming a member of the Thunderbolts and the Dark Avengers under its name. When the symbiote was weaponized by the government, Agent Venom was the alias of Flash Thompson. Another villainous Venom for a time was Lee Price. Brock's son, Dylan Brock also used the symbiote under the codename Codex. As of recent, Mary Jane Watson has covertly embraced the position of an all-new Venom in a forced connection with the symbiote she explores to combat crime.
