History

Ash was the park manager of Star City, a science fiction amusement park located in Los Angeles, State. Unknown to the public or to law enforcement, the park was secretly owned by an unspecified criminal organization ("the mob") who had invested a lot of money in it and had made in the Los Angeles terminus of a top=secret network of locations that served as an "underground railroad" for criminals that needed to be hidden from the authorities or moeved without being detected.[1]
