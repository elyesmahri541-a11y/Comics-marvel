Synopsis for "My Funny Valentine"

On Valentine's Day, Peter Parker goes back to the Brooklyn Bridge, the place where Gwendolyne Stacy (Earth-616) died. He leaves her a red rose, and then the flashback about how he and Gwen met begins.
