History

Relampago (whose name means 'Lightning' in Spanish) was a paranormal operative of the Medusa Web who was sent in to capture the members of Psi-Force. She was accidentally killed by Stasi Inyushin, who tripped her while she was moving at super speed, causing her to break her neck.[1]

Attributes

Powers

Superhuman Speed.

Notes

Relampago was apparently regarded as a hero in her homeland.
