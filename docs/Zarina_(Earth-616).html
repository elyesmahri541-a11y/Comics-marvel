History

Zarina was an Atlantean that resides in underwater city of Atlantis. She was a member of the royal family and aunt to Namor, King of Atlantis.[1]

Attributes

Powers

  • Atlantean Abilities: Zarina possesses the conventional attributes of Atlanteans (Homo mermanus): gills that extract oxygen from water, superhuman physiology to withstand extreme pressures and temperatures, and acute vision. She can swim at approximately 30 miles per hour. She can remain out of water for approximately nine minutes before beginning to suffocate. However, she can operate on the surface by wearing a water-filled helmet or using a chemical that enables him to draw oxygen from the air.
