History

Frog was one of Prince Ba'rahm's henchmen and participated in the fraudulent operation through which the self-proclaimed mystic preyed on wealthy women. Ba'rahm and his associates staged elaborate ceremonies in which the victims were led to believe that they were being sacrificed to Decka, the supposed master of fire. In reality, the women were taken to a secret chamber beneath a warehouse near the docks, where they were persuaded to surrender their fortunes before being drowned.

Frog was among Ba'rahm's men when Captain America and Bucky intervened to rescue Mrs. Dale. He was defeated along with the other members of the group during the ensuing confrontation.[1]
