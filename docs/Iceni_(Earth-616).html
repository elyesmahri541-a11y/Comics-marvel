History

An ancient sect of Wicca belonging to the Celtic tribe of the Iceni who in the past under the command of Boudicca challenged the Roman Empire. Overwhelmed by the Roman Legions, the sect sacrificed themselves but placed their spirits inside the ancient Londinium awaiting the arrival of a powerful matriarch who would awaken them.[1]

Whitechapel Murders[]

When the spirits felt the presence of Satana, who was traveling in the past alongside the Thunderbolts, they took possession of the corpses of indigent victims, including the victims of Jack the Ripper. Clashing with the Thunderbolts and Satana's refusal to join their struggle, the Iceni were soundly defeated and all were eliminated.[2]
