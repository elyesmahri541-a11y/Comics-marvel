History

Spider-Monkey was a spider monkey, and the Earth-8101 counterpart of Spider-Man. Unlike his human counterpart, Spider-Monkey was more egotistical, naive, and a bit more accepting of the world around him. Spider-Monkey also had no problem with killing those who did not want to reform, as the justice system of his world said that if a villain did not reform, they had to be killed. Spider-Monkey was seen fighting against Doctor Ooktavius, who Gibbon helped to defeat.[3] Spider-Monkey was later seen at Doctor Ooktavius' trial, and he was one of the Ape-vengers who beat him to death after he refused to reform.[4]

When the spider-heroes from across the Multiverse became aware of the existence of the spider-hunter vampiric Inheritors, Spider-Monkey jointed the so-called Superior Spider-Army assembled by the Superior Spider-Man.[5] Spider-Monkey later moved to Earth-13 along with the rest of Otto's army to join a bigger faction of spider-heroes gathered by Spider-UK.[6] When the Inheritors attacked Earth-13, Spider-Monkey was killed by Jennix.[1]

Somehow returned to life, Spider-Monkey joined yet another assembly of Multiversal spider-heroes, the Spider Society founded by Spider-Man 2099. As a member of the Spider Society, Spider-Monkey participated in the chase for Miles Morales.[2] At one point he tackled Miles and this caused them to crash into Spider-Therapist's session.
