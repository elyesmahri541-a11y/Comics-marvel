History

When his firstborn son Luther was killed by the second-born Victor, Mr. Creed saw it as an act of the devil. He immediately locked up the younger boy in the family cellar, where he routinely visited to pull out what he believed were "Satan teeth". He believed that if he pulled out enough of them, young Victor would be cured of his evilness. Instead, Victor one day escaped and murdered Zeb.[1]
