History

Dohertz's history is identical to that of his Earth-14123 counterpart. In June 1963 he slipped an inappropriate image into Issue 188 of Captain Fancy, getting the run pulped upon the image being discovered by readers, except for one copy which was smuggled over the years and eventually made its way to an auction. Frederick Frederickson IV would've won it, but his rival Richardson Mole cut the power to his house and placed the winning bid. [1]

Personality

Like his Earth-14123 counterpart, Fred described Dohertz as a disgruntled artist.
