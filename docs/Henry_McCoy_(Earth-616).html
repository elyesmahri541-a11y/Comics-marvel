The intelligently gifted Dr. Hank McCoy,[27] being born with an enhanced physique, strength, and muscular structure, has adopted the identity of the Beast.[3] As a member of the first class of X-Men,[3] he has fought for the peaceful coexistence between mutants and humans for most of his life, under the tutelage of Professor Xavier.[28] McCoy's mutation further developed when he worked at the Brand Corporation and developed a serum that triggered his transformation into a blue-furred creature with sharp fangs and claws.[27]

As a cheerful and bouncing super hero, Beast established a renowned reputation as a veteran Avenger,[4] a Defender,[29] and a founding member of X-Factor.[30] Having found himself back to the X-Men[31] and the Xavier Institute, Beast was one of the most engaged X-Men in carrying on Xavier's dream.[32][33][34] However, under the leadership of his longtime teammate Cyclops, the X-Men turned to extremism, leading Beast to defect.[35] His opposition escalated with the schism between Cyclops and Wolverine, for he joined the latter in building the Jean Grey School for Higher Learning. The war between the Avengers and the X-Men consolidated Beast's allegiances, as he supported the Avengers[36] and focused on opposing Cyclops' operations in any possible way.[37][38] Additionally, he also assumed Professor Xavier's legacy in the shadowy organization known as the Illuminati.[39]

When Mutantkind was united by Professor Xavier on the mutant nation of Krakoa.[40] Beast became a central part in planting the first seeds of the Krakoan civilization.[41] In order to deal with external threats to the mutant nation, Beast took the position as the chief of the intelligence agency X-Force.[42] He descended to increasingly questionable authoritarian actions to guarantee the Krakoan sovereignty.[43] Ultimately, his corruption led him to go rogue and later brutally attack his former allies,[44] resulting in his apparent demise.[24] In reality, he secretly preserved his consciousness in duplicate bodies and has been acting as the mysterious Chairman of the gene terrorist organization 3K.[1][2]

History

Early Life[]

Henry McCoy was born and raised in Dunfee, Illinois, the son of Norton and Edna McCoy. His father worked at a nuclear power plant where he was exposed to massive amounts of radiation when attempting to fix a short circuit.[45] The ordeal had Edna McCoy demand her husband to leave the nuclear plant and search for another town for them to live. The radiation affected Norton's genes, and he retired from the nuclear plant shortly thereafter and became a simple farmer living off the land. When Edna gave birth to Hank, his mutant nature was made obvious due to his enlarged hands and feet. As a baby, Hank also showed above normal strength and uncanny agility.[46] A regular presence in young Hank's upbringing was his uncle, Bob McCoy.[47] When Hank was a young boy, he didn't realize his own strength and accidentally crushed his uncle Bob's hand. Hank became upset, and his father informed Hank why he was different from others, but Norton reassured his son that he loved him all the same.[48]

Hank proved to be highly intelligent and very inquisitive young boy, focusing on studying and enhancing his education. As a result he developed a large vocabulary at a very young age and his curiosity of all things often got him into trouble. In elementary school, Hank earned the ire of his principal as well as the grounds keeper due to the fact that he frequently disassembled any mechanical object he could get his hands on. Although he meant well and managed to fix things, such as the school bus, it was much to the frustration of school administrators.[48] Despite his physical aptitude as an unusual kid, McCoy resorted to developing his intellect, inquisitively devoting himself to specialize in different scientific and artistic fields.[12] During his childhood, McCoy was ostracized for his appearance and skills.[45]

College Football[]

Hank attended Bard College,[49] where he began dating a young girl named Mindy.[48] Although Hank cared for her, his curiosity often got out of control, such as when he started dismantling the animatronics in a tunnel of love ride at the local carnival, much to Mindy's embarrassment.[19] Hank's over sized features made him a target for bullies,[9] one day when he was being harassed by some of his classmates, Hank was shoved into traffic. He was saved from a fatal accident thanks to his agility.[45] Witnessing this, the coach of the local football team became determined to get Hank to play for the Bobcats, the school's team.[50] During this time, Hank began tutoring Jennifer Nyles and the two became fast friends, working together on projects. One of his chief bullies at this time was the local football star, Steckler.[50] When the coach petitioned Hank to join the team when he kicked the football so hard that it broke, but still managed to score a field goal.Uncanny Origins #6.

Jennifer began to fall for Hank, and when he was dateless for his junior prom, she asked him out. When Hank realized that her interest was genuine, he accepted. As they began dating, Jennifer stuck up for McCoy, defending him from bullies and getting the coach to make Hank a quarterback on the field.[10] Hank became an instant star on the field, winning game after game and becoming a rising star.[45] During one game, Hank witnessed a pair of masked thugs trying to rob the box office, and used his mutant abilities to stop them.[48] However, it was not very long that the press began questioning the fairness of Hank's mutant nature. Early reports of Hank's abilities were read by Professor Charles Xavier, as well as the terrorist known as the Conquistador.[51] Hank's potential became so great was he was looking at getting both academic and athletic scholarships to further his education.[9] On the day of the state championship, Hank received a phone call from Jennifer and the two expressed their love for one another. However, their relationship would soon be cut short, as she eventually left to pursue an education in genetics in London, though they kept contact.[10]

Joining the X-Men[]

The Conquistador decided to capture Hank and convince the youth to join his quest for world domination. The Conquistador had taken Hank's parents hostage in order to assure that he complied with the Conquistador's commands.[51] By this time Professor Xavier was now gathering a team of mutants to bring about peaceful coexistence between humans and mutants, the X-Men. Learning of Hank's kidnapping, he sent his students Cyclops, Iceman and the Angel on a rescue mission.[51] The Conquistador agreed to let Hank's parents go if he broke into a nuclear research facility and steal a new prototype reactor for him. Hank broke into the facility and recovered the item, using his mutant abilities to slip past the security fences and slip past the guards but had to flee after setting off a silent alarm.[28] He also used his father's identification from his former nuclear plant job to get past security but was stymied by a palm scanner and had to break into the holding room and fight past the guards. Hank managed to escape with the help of the Conquistador's minions.[50]

Hank gave the reactor over to the Conquistador, who then refused to release his parents, vowing to use them as hostages to make Hank do more work for him.[28] Hank then fought back against the Conquistador, when the X-Men suddenly arrived to lend a hand. During the fight the reactor began to overload. Hank then freed his parents and fled with the X-Men.[52] In another account, Hank didn't actually steal the reactor, but built a device to blind the Conquistador when he opened the container containing what the villain thought was his prize. Hank then defeated the Conquistador by himself, tearing his foes weapons with his bare hands. With the Conquistador defeated, Hank freed his parents who were frightened by these shocking developments. Hank was convinced to join Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters, and Xavier used his mental powers to erase all memory of Hank and his powers from the people in town.[50]

Hank then joined the X-Men, taking on the codename Beast.[52] Before leaving town, Hank went through one last time and was saddened to see that Jennifer - now with no memory of Hank - began dating Steckler.[50] With Jean Grey joining the group,[3] Xavier's team of X-Men was complete. He began training them to hone their powers in his specially constructed Danger Room.[53] Despite his superficially brutish attitude, McCoy presented himself as a highly educated and eloquent man with the X-Men, being a bookworm constantly focused on his advanced studies.[12] Early on there was trouble when Hank began to get upset that Xavier used his powers to erase all memory from the people he grew up with.[54] Hank soon became angry at this decision, but Xavier eventually convinced Hank that it was for the good of those he knew and loved, as well as his own personal safety.[55] In training, Xavier taught Beast to never underestimate his adversaries.[56]

Early Missions[]

The threat of the mutant terrorist Magneto became real, and Xavier called upon his X-Men to prepare for their first battle against his one-time friend. Through Xavier's guidance, Beast and the X-Men succeeded in stopping Magneto from taking over the Cape Citadel missile base, forcing the master of magnetism to flee.[3] Later when the mutant teleporter Vanisher began stealing government secrets, Professor X offered the X-Men's aid to the government to stop him. Xavier himself defeated the Vanisher by using his mental powers to cut off his access to his powers, allowing the X-Men to easily capture him and his minions.[57] When Angel had a personality shift following exposure to radiation unleashed by a test by Stark Industries, Hank and the X-Men stood back to allow Iron Man to deal with the danger posed by the Angel until he reverted back to normal.[58] Beast and his teammates met the Sentry, assisting him in defeating his foe the General who was attempting to steal nuclear weapons to launch against the nation of France.[59]The X-Men were later forced to forget the existence of the Sentry.[60].

Xavier soon located another mutant, the immovable Blob, who was working as part of a carnival freak show, and sent the X-Men to recruit him. The X-Men soon learned the error of welcoming the Blob in with open arms when he turned out to be uninterested in joining the X-Men and later attacked the school. While the X-Men kept the Blob and his army of carnies at bay, Xavier completed a device that allowed him to wipe out all memory of the X-Men's headquarters and secret identities from the mind of the Blob and his cohorts.[12] Later while in a training session the X-Men were briefly surprised by the sudden appearance of a medieval knight, but it soon disappeared.[61] Magneto resurfaced, this time with his own team of the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants he conquered the South American nation of Santo Marco.[62] Xavier deployed the X-Men who battled the Brotherhood. When the Brotherhood was forced to flee, Xavier threw himself at a booby trapped door to save his students from the blast, and led them to believe that he had lost his mental powers as a result.[62]

Later the X-Men were lured into a trap by Magneto and were taken aboard his Asteroid M headquarters. Beast and the X-Men managed to escape without the aid of Xavier. Charles later revealed to his students that he feigned his lost powers as a final test to see if his students could handle themselves without his aid.[63] Professor X was enthralled by the Puppet Master and the Mad Thinker who then used Xavier to order the X-Men to attack the Fantastic Four. Although the two teams were forced to fight each other, Xavier broke free from the Puppet Master's control and exposed the entire ruse.[64] Not long after this, Beast and the X-Men were chased by an anti-mutant mob. Cornered in a blind alley the mob started throwing rocks. Scott told his teammates that the bigoted mob were not worth it and they fled into the night.[65]

Across Time[]

The X-Men were later pulled forward in time by the Access to a future where the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants had teamed up with the other-dimensional Darkseid and his Apokoliptians who were attempting to conquer their two universes. The X-Men assisted the Avengers as well as the Justice League of America and the Teen Titans, heroes from Darkseid's dimension.[66] Access used his powers to amalgamate heroes from both universes together and brought them to battle Magneto on Asteroid M, while on the Earth, Professor X, the Beast, and Cyclops remained to deal with the various threats there. When Access and his allies defeated Darkseid and Magneto, all the heroes and their foes were returned to their proper times and dimensions. Upon their return home, Charles and the X-Men's memory of events eventually faded.[67]

Later, Beast and the X-Men found themselves pulled through time once again by a would-be assassin named Sidestep who was hired to assassinate the British superheroes the Excalibur on behalf of Doctor Doom. To this end, each member of the X-Men had their memories altered to fit the era in which they were placed and forced to battle a member of Excalibur. Hank was transported to the 5th century during the fall of Rome and led to believe that he was a warrior called Gladiator Hank. He was pitted against Nightcrawler, the leader of Excalibur. Nightcrawler wun the bout, and both were placed into stasis until the contest's outcome. Ultimately, the more experienced members of Excalibur defeated the X-Men. With Doom's plot ruined, the original X-Men were restored to normal, had their memories of these events erased, and were returned to their own time.[68]

Learning that the Sub-Mariner was also a mutant, both Xavier and Magneto sought to recruit him to their respective sides. Magneto succeeded in summoning Namor first, ultimately leading to another battle between the X-Men and the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants. Realizing that Magneto intended to make Namor a subordinate, he refused to join. The X-Men then drove Magneto and his Brotherhood away, but Namor had no interest in joining the X-Men either.[69] Hank later joined the X-Men in battle against the Brotherhood again when they attempted to recruit Thor into their ranks, mistakenly believing him to be a mutant. When the X-Men arrived, Magneto and his minions then fled.[70]

Once his X-Men had completed their high school educational and training requirements, Professor X threw a private graduation ceremony for them.[71] During this time Hank had become close friends with his Bobby Drake. The two began frequenting Coffee A Go-Go in Greenwich Village. There Bobby took a liking to the works of Bernard the Poet and began romancing a barista named Zelda Kurtzberg. Later, without Xavier, the X-Men succeeded in stopping the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants from recruiting the Blob into their ranks.[71] Despite his efforts to be a heroic figure, the Beast was not protected from anti-mutant prejudice. When attempting to save a boy from danger, he was attacked by an angry mob. Disturbed by what he considered to be ingratitude, he rebelled against humanity, choosing to resign from the X-Men, which sparked a debate about Xavier's ideals with team leader Cyclops.[72]

All-New X-Men[]

Beasts of Tomorrow[]

In face of his disillusionment with human bigotry, Beast was surprised by a mutated version of himself from decades in the future. The adult Beast requested help from the first class of X-Men in saving mutant society from collapse. He declared that Cyclops had been committing genocide,[37] with his first victim having been their mentor Charles Xavier. The original X-Men agreed to accompany the other Beast to the future, where the academy was named the Jean Grey School in honor of her death and run by the ferocious Wolverine. Upon arrival, future Beast fell into a coma, dying from the changes in his body being brought on by his Secondary Mutation. Headmistress Kitty Pryde assisted Beast in providing medical care to his future version.[73] Amidst the commotion of being displaced from the timeline and shocking the newer generations of mutants, the original X-Men ran away to confront future Cyclops, learning in shock he had Magneto as his ally. They retreated as Jean Grey, with her recently activated telepathy, was overwhelmed by the psychic tensions.[74] Back at the Jean Grey School, McCoy stabilized his future self's organism and saved his life. Under Jean's leadership, the team decided to stay in the future to prevent the alarming and imminent tragedies. Pryde took up the role of mentoring the time-displaced X-Men.[75]

Battle of the Atom[]

Stranded in a future he could not comprehend, McCoy delved into researching his own mutative genetic makeup. Meanwhile, Jean Grey that declared she was aware of his attraction for her, an information she had collected from his older self's memories. They shared a kiss following a heated discussion.[76] Recognizing that their presence in the future could have catastrophic ramifications, future X-Men decided to send the original X-Men back in time. However, upon activating the Time Platform, they were astonished by X-Men from a further future, of which an old Beast was a member.[77] Jean Grey could not trust the X-Men from the future and secretly reached out to Beast using telepathy to discuss running away with him. He hesitated and proposed waiting. Disappointed, she turned to Cyclops instead and escaped to prevent being sent back to the past.[78]

The future X-Men hunted them down, with Beast and Iceman being sidelined.[79] One of adult Cyclops' X-Men, the demon sorceress Magik, contacted them to visit the future and confirm the truth.[80] They learned the interlopers were in fact the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants, while the true X-Men thrived at the Jean Grey School.[81] The Brotherhood attempted to force the original X-Men to time travel after subduing them, but failed.[82] Instead, they were taken to Cape Citadel, where past and present Beast duelled their corrupted future doppelgänger.[83] S.H.I.E.L.D. intervened in the fight deploying mutant-hunting Sentinels, which killed future Beast. In the end, the original X-Men defeated the Brotherhood.[84]

Disillusioned with how the X-Men reacted during this incident, Pryde took her pupils to Cyclops' revolutionary institute.[84] At the New Charles Xavier School, secretly hidden in the Canadian wilderness, Hank McCoy continued his studies on the history of mutantkind. He conversed with Magneto. Additionally, he expressed his anger at Jean Grey for rejecting him after he disagreed with her.[85] Amidst these personal dramas, the original X-Men fought the religious extremists known as the Purifiers.[86] When studying Cerebro, Beast found a mutant signature of an extradimensional teleporter. Upon contact, she teleported the X-Men to Earth-1610.[87]

Ultimate Road Trip[]

Separated from his teammates, Beast emerged in Latveria and became a prisoner of Doctor Doom, who wished to learn about Beast's home dimension.[88] Infected with a truth serum, Beast answered all questions Doom had for him. In addition to this, he also lamented his broken relationship with Jean Grey.[89] After locating Beast with Cerebro, Jean led the time-displaced X-Men and the X-Men of Earth-1610 to Latveria to rescue him, preventing Doom from forcing him to help in conquering other universes. After returning home from the dystopian reality, Beast shared with Jean Grey that he could no longer tolerate not changing a future in which mutants struggled.[90] Jean proposed disbanding the X-Men afterwards, claiming they should explore the future with tranquility. Feeling guilty for the actions of his future self, Beast was distressed. Jean Grey comforted him, declaring that, although the X-Men were no more, she wanted to be with him and they embraced in a kiss.[38]

Beast's relationship with Jean Grey did not progress and they broke up with her permanently leaving the X-Men.[91] Beast adapted a microbus with spatial technology that turned its interiors into comfortable accommodations that could teleport using the powers of the Bamf named Pickles. Alongside former Jean Grey School students, Idie Okonkwo and Evan, he laid low by roaming the country as the world was engulfed by the M-Pox epidemic. The Inhuman Terrigen Mists that had been released onto the atmosphere proved to be toxic to mutants, escalating hysteria. Adult Cyclops was one of its victims, prompting Beast to find his teenage counterpart. After recruiting Iceman, Angel, and the all-new Wolverine, Beast reformed the X-Men after identifying Cyclops' signature in Chicago.[92] Following fights and difficulties with the X-Men,[93] Beast became overwhelmed by his inability to solve his friends' problems. Determined the X-Men should return to their proper moment in history, Beast contemplated the possibility of harnessing magic to accomplish his plans.[94]

Learning Magic[]

Introducing himself to Sorcerer Supreme Doctor Strange, Beast declared that where science failed him, magic could be an alternative. However, magic had been rendered inert by the Empirikul as Strange demonstrated to Beast using the Third Eye of Horus which allowed him to observe magic.[95] McCoy then focused on studying time-travel technology, considering the Time Diamonds and the T-Gun, with no success in activating a teleportation. However, when Evan interacted with the Third Eye of Horus, both him and Beast were sent to the distant past in Ancient Egypt. Happening upon the Sandstormers, Beast was mortally wounded and captured. Evan, on the other hand, was rescued by a teenage version of Apocalypse, his genetic template.[96] At the encampment of the Sandstormers, a blind mystic tended to Beast's wounds, feeling mercy for his condition. Apocalypse's father and leader of the Sandstormers, Baal, tortured Beast to blackmail Apocalypse into reuniting with the Sandstormers.[97] Rescued by Evan, Beast ended up questioning whether saving Apocalypse from the Sandstormers was valid, since he would become one of the greatest villains in history. He returned to the mystic's tent to recollect the Eye of Horus, striking a pact with the old magician. Through a revelation provided by the Eye of Horus, Beast managed to return himself and Evan to the present, ensuring Apocalypse's inevitable fate.[98]

In secrecy, Beast continued investigating magic solutions. Cyclops grew suspicious of his activities. When trying to discover what Beast was up to, he was hurt by demonic creatures.[99] The monsters, who accessed Beast's microbus' systems, broke out of their hellish dimension and went out of control, raiding Miami. Cyclops confronted Beast about his apparent corruption. At the cost of their headquarters, they teamed up to stop the invasion from the infernal realm, with Beast resolving to abandon tampering with sorcery.[100] However, murderous fiends destroyed Miami, since the Goblin Queen from Battleworld took advantage of Beast's investigations to taint his equipment and put out the demonic forces. While the X-Men did their best to abate the Goblin Queen, Beast performed a ritual using the Orb of Zarjul, which merged with his body and enhanced his mystical potential. As a powerful sorcerer, Beast single-handedly banished the demons while their conductor made a hasty retreat.[101]

Cross Time Capers[]

After his return from ancient Egypt, Hank McCoy was able to solve the problems related to time travel.[100] By himself, he visited the past using his magical abilities to shockingly learn that the timeline had apparently repaired itself by restoring the original X-Men in the past. Believing they could not coexist with their duplicates, Beast transported his teammates to demonstrate what they thought were the younger X-Men of this timeline following the destruction of the Terrigen Mists. As a result, the time-displaced X-Men decided to look forward to their future, which would be no longer tied to the fate of their concurrent older selves. In this context, Jean Grey returned to the team as their leader, immediately growing concern about Beast's corruption.[102] Believing the Xavier Institute was not for them, the original X-Men went missing.[103] Alternatively, Jean Grey turned to Magneto, joining their cause and setting base on the pirate island-nation of Madripoor as he mentored them.[104] In action, Beast started to resort more frequently to magic, using it to banish the unstoppable Juggernaut. This course of action deeply worried his teammates.[105] Drastic changes in his personality were recognized by his fellow X-Men as he became morbid and serious.[106] In secrecy, he had been communing with the Goblin Queen, who tutored him in sorcery.[107]

On Madripoor, Hank McCoy took a chance to get to know the local culture. The X-Men learned that Mutant Growth Hormone was being trafficked on the island. When tackling dealers, they met a group of mutant heroes who operated on Madripoor, the Raksha. He was tormented by the psychic Gazing Nightshade, being forced to confront his reckless decisions of recent. After understanding they served the same mission, the Raksha became allies of the X-Men.[108] Beast became close to Gazing Nightshade, conversing with her about his struggles. The Goblin Queen expressed disapproval for his relationship with Gazing Nightshade. She tricked him into casting a portal to assemble her Hex-Men to Madripoor.[107] Reverted to his demonic version, Beast attacked the X-Men and the Raksha under the Goblin Queen's control.[109] One of the Hex-Man, Bloodstorm, intervened and convinced Beast to betray his mistress, casting her away. Bloodstorm recommended him to abandon sorcery to recover his soul and no longer be possessed by a devilish spirt, which he did. The X-Men forgave him for his actions.[110]

The X-Men learned Magneto's actual plan was to return them to the past to prevent the collapse of the timeline. Since they witnessed faults occurring before them, Beast activated Magneto's time machine, sending the original X-Men to the year of 2099,[111] in a dystopian reality in which the original X-Men were revered as great saviors. With help from that year's X-Men, they made another jump, reaching Generation X.[112] Emma Frost informed them the original X-Men had been ruthless tyrants who exterminated their opponents, prompting Beast to perform a last jump in order to confront themselves in their proper period in time.[113] Upon arrival, the X-Men learned their doppelgängers were actually the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants from the future in disguise. Allying themselves with their nemesis from the past, Magneto,[114] the X-Men cast their impersonators away. In the end, Professor X informed the X-Men that, sooner or later, they were supposed to return to the exact moment they had left before Beast teletransported them back to present-day Madripoor.[115]

Extermination[]

Having broken their association with Magneto, the X-Men became independent[116] and confronted him to stop his blood-fueled agenda of punishing his enemies. Beast used magic, allowing himself to be possessed, but Magneto knocked him out. Expressing his disappointment with the potential of the X-Men remaining in the present, Magneto parted ways with them.[117] Determined to return, the X-Men prepared themselves for the trip. Beast talked with his adult version, declaring he had turned to magic to keep up with his mature version. He was worried about undoing all the changes that had occurred with the original X-Men, including his aptitude for sorcery.[118] On Madripoor, Beast bid farewell to Gazing Nightshade, who shared that him going away was for the best in order for him to become the man he had always supposed to be.[119]

The original X-Men's presence in the future proved to be a bigger problem when the time-traveling mutant-hunter Ahab targeted them to bring forth a dystopian future without Xavier's team. In opposition to him, Cable, an extremist military warrior from the future, started to hunt down the X-Men and captured them to ensure their survival and return.[120] At the Xavier Institute, Cable sedated Beast, who was treated by his older self.[121] Ahab took the opportunity to ambush Beast, who ended up being saved but also kidnapped by Cable.[122] Understanding the urgent need to protect history, Jean Grey teamed up with Cable, traveling back in time with her teammates. She sealed off the memories of the X-Men concerning their time-displaced adventures. The memories were triggered to resurface on their older selves after the young X-Men's departure. Fabricating the original setting of their removal from the timeline, the X-Men resumed their lives at Xavier's School, with Beast reacting to an attack he had experienced from an angry mob of humans.[123]

First Graduation[]

In face of disillusion with humanity's ingratitude, the Beast thus abandoned the X-Men. Maintaining his mutant identity, the Beast became a professional wrestler, considering to profit from his gifts. One of his opponents in the ring was Unus the Untouchable, a mutant who could cast a protective force field and was unbeatable in fight. Understanding his teammates might need his assistance since Unus could align himself with Magneto, the Beast returned to his friends and developed a device that boosted Unus' force fields so that he could not even touch food forcing him to surrender to have the effects reversed.[72] The X-Men participated in an investigation of a supposed mutant calling himself the Menace and his Mutantmen army. With the aid of Spider-Man, the X-Men revealed that "Menace" was really a group of criminals exploiting anti-mutant hysteria to commit robberies, pinning the blame on mutants.[124]

The X-Men joined the Avengers, Fantastic Four, Daredevil, Doctor Strange, and Spider-Man in battling the super-powerful Sundown.[125] Charles soon contacted his students and revealed that he was searching for the alien Lucifer, who was responsible for crippling him. Charles called for his X-Men to join him in Europe. Quickly captured, Charles learned that Lucifer had armed a bomb that could blow up all of Europe and that it was specially rigged to his heart, so that the bomb would go off if his heart was stopped. When the Avengers arrived to investigate the scene, Charles had Beast and the X-Men hold the Avengers at bay until he was able to incapacitate Lucifer with a mental bolt. Charles then stopped the first between the two teams and explained the situation. Satisfied that the X-Men had the situation under control, the Avengers then departed. When Lucifer revived shortly thereafter, Xavier allowed him to go free as he had been thoroughly defeated.[126]

With the next stage of their education and training, Henry and his teammates were provided with alternate costumes that they occasionally wore in the field. When Xavier began detecting a strange force that was taking control of plants and animals near the mansion, he and the X-Men tracked the source near Greenland. There they prevented scientists who were studying global warming from drilling into the domain of ancient creatures that used to be the dominant species on Earth and were in the final cycle of their lifespan. This discovery convinced the scientist to drill elsewhere for their research.[127] During a later training session, Jean attempted to get the other members of the team motivated by offering to go out on a date with whoever won a race to the Danger Room, a competition that was quickly quashed by both the Professor and Cyclops.[128]

The X-Men were invited to the engagement party of the Fantastic Four's Mister Fantastic and the Invisible Girl. Since Charles was keeping his ties to the X-Men a secret at the time, he and he students had to act as though they did not know each other.[129] During a lull in activity, Charles allowed the X-Men to investigate reports of a savage man running loose in Antarctica. This led to the X-Men discovering the Savage Land and finding an ally in it's ruler Ka-Zar during a clash with the primitive Maa-Gor and the native Swamp Men.[130] Deciding to take a vacation, Charles took his students to the Florida Everglades and decided to pay a visit to his old colleague Curt Connors. Learning that Connors had once more transformed into the Lizard, he had Angel, Beast and Iceman assist him in capturing the Lizard and reverting him back to human form.[131]

Brotherhood Disassembled[]

Charles became aware of what appeared to be a new mutant and sent Beast and the X-Men to make first contact. This led to another battle with the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants. This new entity turned out be the cosmic being known as the Stranger who had come to Earth to study mutants. During the ensuing clash, the Stranger transformed Brotherhood member Mastermind into dense matter and captured Magneto and the Toad to examine.[132] Charles' step-brother Cain Marko, transformed into the avatar of the demon Cyttorak, attacked the mansion.[133] Unable to attack the Juggernaut's mind thanks to a psi-proof helmet, Xavier contacted the Human Torch for assistance. While the X-Men kept the Juggernaut at bay, the Torch used his flame powers to remove Cain's helmet leaving him vulnerable to attack and eventually defeat at the hands of Xavier. While the Juggernaut was defeated, the X-Men were seriously injured as a result and went through a period of healing.[134] Eventually, the X-Men recovered from their injuries and Charles decided that they should all take a brief break from adventuring.[135]

While the Professor was away, Hank and the others took time to relax, spending a lot of time at their regular hangout, Coffee A Go-Go, unaware that they were being observed by young Skrulls on a mission to infiltrate the Earth. When the Skrulls later posed as the X-Men to free various creatures that escaped from Monster Isle, Beast and the others helped recapture the beasts. Realizing how selfless the X-Men were, the Skrulls later saved Henry and the others when their latest monster hunt went south, compromising their mission enough to get sent him leaving the X-Men confused about the entire ordeal.[136] Having developed a crush on the Scarlet Witch, Warren convinced Hank to hack into Cerebro and use it to find her location so that Warren could ask her out on a date. This was kept secret from the others, and when Angel and the Scarlet Witch both appeared to have gone missing, Iceman and the X-Men joined Quicksilver in searching for the pair. After a brief confrontation they learned that Warren and Wanda were briefly dating in secret until they realized that they came from very different walks of life and decided to amicably end their relationship.[137] The X-Men soon were contacted by Charles who had gone missing while on a mission to Africa. Hearing his telepathic cry for help the X-Men hired the retired 1950s adventurer known as Gorilla Man to help them rescue Charles from a mysterious cave that sapped the wills of those trapped inside.[138]

The X-Men later assisted Thor in tracking down members of the Midgard Vanir who had kidnapped Iceman in a plot to revive the Frost Giant Ymir on Earth.[139] Mastermind's consciousness expanded and allowed him to use his illusion casting powers at great distances for a short period of time. Seeking to get revenge against the X-Men, he used his powers while the Professor was away to trick the X-Men into going on a "mission" to Monster Isle.[140] When Charles returned to the mansion and found his charges missing, he managed to tack them down and expose Mastermind's plot, defeating him in mental combat.[141] Hank and the X-Men were invited to the wedding of Mister Fantastic and the Invisible Girl, once again having to pretend to not know each other. The wedding was soon crashed by an army of super-villains under the control of Doctor Doom. Beast and his fellow X-Men stopped attacks made by the Mole Man and his Moloids,[142] Batroc the Leaper, the Executioner, and the monster Grogoom.[143] Eventually Mister Fantastic dispelled the villains with a time displacement device provided to him by the Watcher and the wedding went on uninterrupted.[142]

Non-Mutant Threats[]

Anti-mutant hysteria had reached new highs at this point thanks, in part, to the bigoted fear mongering of inventor Bolivar Trask. Seeking to try and dispel some of the prejudice, Charles organized a televised debate with Trask. Trask in turn used the debate to unveil his mutant hunting Sentinels to the public. However the machines soon turned on him, deciding that the only way to protect humanity was by ruling it. Charles summoned his X-Men, but they were too late to stop the Sentinels from absconding with Trask, although one of the robots suddenly deactivated in the battle. Before they could determine why, Charles led his X-Men out to the secret base of the Sentinels only to find it heavily fortified with powerful weapons.[135] During the initial attack on the Sentinels fortress, the Beast and Iceman were captured. While the other X-Men renewed their attack, Xavier probed the base and learned that the Sentinel's leader, Master Mold was probing the Beast's mind to learn what he could about the X-Men. Xavier prevented the probes from revealing anymore before his astral form was driven back by Master Mold, who detected his presence.[45] With his X-Men still trapped inside, Charles discovered that a giant crystal display at the Crystal Products Building was jamming the signal to the Sentinel, he made arrangements to have it flown to the Sentinels' headquarters. While Charles had the police and military jam the Sentinels, Bolivar - having realized the errors of his ways --sacrificed his life to seemingly destroy Master Mold. The X-Men managed to escape, but were injured while they fled.[144]

Professor X accompanied the X-Men to the hospital in order to ensure that their secret identities were not exposed, although he was incredibly careful not to led the authorities or hospital staff suspect that he had ties with the group. When they X-Men became aware that Warren's parents were going to be making an impromptu visit to the school, Charles and his students tried to return home before their arrival. This led to everyone but the recovering Iceman to be captured by Magneto, who had escaped the Stranger's world. After releasing Xavier and his charges high into the atmosphere in a balloon, he captured Warren's parents.[145] Charles used his mental powers to the X-Men in to getting back to ground while also contacting the Stranger to inform him of Magneto's escape. Sure enough, after the X-Men arrived, the Strange soon followed forcing Magneto to flee.[146]

By this time Charles and the X-Men began working with FBI Agent Baker who seemed to occasionally replace Fred Duncan as liaison for the X-Men. He also got into contact with his colleague Able Stack and agreed to have Stack bring his robot X-50 to interact with his students. Xavier later dispatched the X-Men and X-50 to Mount Saint Helen's where there were sightings of Lava Men.[147] Xavier warned his students when it became known that the X-series robots were proving to be unstable. The Lava Men were eventually stopped, but X-50 was destroyed in the resulting battle. The X-Men recovered X-50's memory which Able Stack was later used to create X-51 later known as Machine Man.[148] The X-Men later crossed paths with the former Frightful Four member Medusa. Charles offered to assist her in recovering her lost memories but found doing so difficult. Suspecting that she was a mutant, Charles invited her to join the X-Men. When her former leader, the Wizard sought to bring Medusa back into the fold, the X-Men fought him. During the fight Charles realized that the Wizard had implanted a device on Medusa to control her mind. After Cyclops destroyed it, Medusa was freed from the Wizard's influence and went on her own.[149]

Bobby and Zeldato introduced Hank the like minded Vera Cantor. Vera previously dated Calvin Rankin had the power to mimic the abilities of those in close proximity to him. In getting into a fight with Bobby and Hank, his mimicking powers revealed to him that they were members of the X-Men. Following each the team and duplicating their powers, he attacked the X-Men in the costumed identity of Mimic after learning the location of their headquarters. During their initial clash, Mimic captured Jean and fled to the secret cave laboratory where he lived. Here Mimic hoped to use the X-Men's stolen powers to open up a buried device invented by his father that Mimic assumed would make his power duplication abilities permanent. As it turned out this was not the case and Xavier allowed himself to be taken hostage while Mimic used the device which stripped him of his powers instead. Charles then erased Calvin's memories of his encounter with the X-Men and set him free.[150]

Lucifer resurfaced and manipulated the Blob and Unus to team up and commit crimes dressed like the X-Men. The X-Men attempted to apprehend them and clear their names, but Blob and Unus managed to escape. When Xavier attempted to learn the identity of the Blob and Unus' benefactor he was incapacitated with with a mental defense weapon. After the Beast developed a device to block the effects of the mental distorter, Xavier led the X-Men after Lucifer.[151] While Lucifer attempted to activate his Dominus computer to enslave the Earth, the X-Men attempted to infiltrate his fortress, and the Professor was captured by Lucifer's robots. Learning that only the robots could utilized the Dominus computer, Xavier instructed his students to destroy them, leaving Lucifer with no means of activating his computer. Unimpressed, Lucifer's superiors banished him to another dimension, ending his threat to Earth.[152] Charles and the X-Men were later asked to assist the military in capturing the Hulk who was sighted in the Pacific North West. However they learned from the Hulk's sidekick Rick Jones that the military sought to weaponize the Hulk. Feeling sympathetic for the Hulk's alter ego of Bruce Banner, Xavier and the X-Men let him go and acted as though their search was fruitless.[153]

After another training session Charles allowed his students to have a brief break from their studies and training. While the X-Men were gone, they were targeted by Maggia leader Count Nefaria who enlisted the aid of the Scarecrow, Planetman, the Eel, the Porcupine, and the Unicorn in order to capture the youths. When Nefaria offered the X-Men to join his organization, they refused and were taken prisoner while he targeted Washington, D.C.[154] The Count sealed Washington in an impenetrable dome and demanded a huge ransom to release it, framing he X-Men in the process. Upon learning this, Charles offered his "expertise" to the military in order to get close enough to Washington to send his Astral form. Contacting his X-Men, Charles instructed them to play along. Charles then infiltrated the dome when Nefaria lowered the shield to allow the X-Men to collect the ransom. While the super-villains fought over the money, Xavier infiltrated Nefaria's base of operations and deactivated his device. Tricking Nefaria and Unicorn into thinking they escaped with the ransom, Charles then mentally alerted the Coast Guard as they attempted to flee the country. In the aftermath of the battle, Jean got a letter from her parents telling her that they enrolled her at Metro College.[155]

Although Jean was leaving Xavier's school, she still kept her ties with the team. When the X-Men battled the insane entomologist known as the Locust, the X-Men foiled his plan to destroy America's wheat supply with giant insects.[156] Back at the mansion, Charles began working to enhance Cerebro more and got caught in a mental loop between himself and the computer. In order to save himself, Xavier pulled the sleeping X-Men into his mind where they worked to free him.[157] After assisting his students in rescuing children from a burning orphanage, Charles returned home to what was eventually determined to be a false reading of a new mutant in New York. The X-Men were deployed and encountered El Tigre, an explorer trying to find the second half of the amulet of Ahau god Kukulkan. The X-Men followed El Tigre and his minions to the American Museum of Anthropology but failed to stop him from recovering the second half of the amulet, endowing him with the powers of Kukulkan.[158] Although El Tigre was able to now block Charles' mental probes, he managed to locate the lost city where their foe escaped to. The X-Men went there and eventually defeated El Tigre/Kukulkan, stripping him of his powers.[159]

Factor Three[]

Charles became aware of a new mutant menace that threatened them and began actively seeking out new members to boost the X-Men's ranks. Charles had the Beast and Iceman attempt to recruit Spider-Man, who turned down their offer. The Puppet Master resurfaced and attempted to take control of Xavier's mind once more, but Charles had already placed mental blocks to prevent this from happening again. When the X-Men found their new member, a newly restored Mimic, they were shocked when Xavier arbitrarily made Mimic deputy leader of the group. Soon after the Puppet Master possessed him instead. Charles then sent the X-Men out to defeat the Puppet Master, freeing Calvin from his control.[160] Charles then began training the X-Men and Mimic in order to prepare them for the coming battle. Their foes turned out to be a mysterious organization called Factor Three who sent their operatives Banshee and the Ogre to attack the school. As he was being forced to work with Factor Three thanks to a headdress rigged with a bomb, Banshee went out of his way to sabotage the Ogre's attack and ultimately the X-Men defeated the Ogre and freed Banshee from Factor Three's control.[161]

The X-Men accidentally revived the Super-Adaptoid who had hidden itself on the Xavier estate. Seeking to turn the human race into androids like itself, the Adaptoid attempted to start with the X-Men. Mimic made the ultimate sacrifice when he tricked the Super-Adaptoid try to duplicate the powers he mimicked off the X-Men. While the Adaptoid was rendered inert, Mimic was also made powerless and left the team.[162] Soon after this, Charles, Jean and Angel were later captured by the mutant known as the Warlock who sought to make Jean his queen after a bid to take over the world. Charles channeled his mental powers to bring Beast and the other X-Men to the Warlock's hideout. There the X-Men bested the Warlock's minions and while their foe was distracted, Xavier used his mental powers to force the Warlock back into a state of suspended animation.[163]

With the threat of Factor Three still looming, Charles continued to train his students. Hank helped his fellow X-Men prevent the Cobalt Man from attacking a Stark Industries facility.[164] Later, the X-Men threw a surprise birthday party for Jean. As a gift, Charles gave Jean a new green and yellow costume.[165] Returning to his attempts to cure Cain, Charles strapped himself and his step-brother into a Energo-Transformer to try and probe his mind for a means of curing him. Instead the presence of the Outcast caused feedback that caused the machine to explode. While Charles was put in a coma, Cain was revived and endowed with Xavier's mental powers. The newly revived Juggernaut was too powerful for the X-Men and left after being mentally contacted and asked to join Factor Three.[166] While Charles was in a coma, the X-Men were still able to probe his mind and learn of the origins of the Crimson Gem of Cyttorak and with the aid of Dr. Strange, Earth's Sorcerer Supreme. He sent Cyclops and Marvel Girl into the Crimson Cosmos where they battled Outcast and recovered a Crimson Gem and returned to the Earth dimension. McCoy and the others kept the Juggernaut at bay until Scott and Jean returned with a gem that banished Cain to Crimson Cosmos before he could join Factor Three. However while the X-Men were busy defeating the Juggernaut, Factor Three kidnapped the Professor.[167]

The X-Men began trying to search for Charles, but were sidetracked when Jean received a call from Ted Roberts asking for help. They soon learned that his brother Ralph was captured by the subterranean ruler Tyrannus who sought to use Ralph to build a robot to battle his rival the Mole Man. While below the surface of the Earth, Angel, Iceman, and Marvel Girl were enthralled by the Mole Man with the mist of forgetfulness and sent to battle Tyrannus. They were later rescued by Cyclops and Beast, and the battle was ended when they exposed both Tyrannus and Mole Man to the mists, making them forget who they were. The X-Men then rescued Ralph and returned him to the surface.[168] Back at Xavier's School, the X-Men received a partial distress signal from Banshee warning them that Factor Three was sending a "spider" after them. This turned out to be a spider robot, but the machine was destroyed by Spider-Man who happened to be in the area. Detecting the robot's arrival, the X-Men tracked it down and found the wall-crawling hero. Mistaking Spider-Man as the menace that the Banshee warned them about, the X-Men attacked him. However they realized their error when cooler heads prevailed they realized their error and let Spider-Man go. In the aftermath of the battle, the X-Men were able to track the location of Banshee's signal to a mountain range in Europe. However there was just one problem: the X-Men had no money to get there.[169]

Travelling into New York City to try and find a means of earning the money to fly to Europe, the X-Men ended up in a battle with Mekano, a disgruntled youth who used a powerful exoskeleton to terrorize the city. After stopping Mekano from trashing a new library, they unmasked him revealing him to be Tom Reagle, son of wealthy philanthropist Andrew Regal. Tom, as it turned out, was acting out because he felt his father ignored him. In thanks for stopping his son, Andrew rewarded the X-Men with the money they needed to make their trip.[170] The X-Men were attacked mid-flight and were forced to abandon their plane. They were soon attacked by more spider robots and taken prisoner in the secret headquarters of Factor Three with Charles and Banshee. The young mutants learned that the group consisted of their old foes Vanisher, Blob, Unus, and Mastermind as well shapeshifter Changeling. Under the leadership of the so-called Mutant Master, Factor Three were plotting to set off a nuclear holocaust so that the human race would be exterminated and mutants could rule the world.[171] The X-Men managed to break free and escape from Factor Three. They then split up into two groups to disarm the bombs Factor Three planted to trigger this war. While Marvel Girl, Angel and Beast went to Russia, Cyclops and Iceman tried to disarm the bomb in America. However, the respective armies of both countries put up opposition.[172] The X-Men ultimately foiled Factor Three's plot and Xavier - with the aid of Banshee and Changeling - revealed that the Mutant Master was really a member of the Siris race who was attempting to exterminate humans and mutants so the Earth could be colonized. With his mission a failure, Mutant Master committed suicide over capture and the members of Factor Three disbanded.[173]

The "Death" of Xavier[]

After their return home, Xavier helped the X-Men defeat a mind controlled scientist who rebuilt a Sentinel, and although Charles sought to determine how he as manipulated he never learned the source. The X-Men's attentions were later turned to Cyclops who started seeing visions of a demon running loose. With the aid of Dr. Strange they learned that the demon followed Scott back from the Crimson Cosmos and returned it there.[174] While going to their usual hang-out, Coffee A Go-Go in Greenwich Village, the X-Men discovered that regular Bernard the Poet was a latent mutant. With the help of the Professor, the X-Men shut down his reality altering powers before anyone could get harmed. Charles later guided the X-Men in tracking down a strange reading picked up by Cerebro, uncovering the android Dragon Man. The creature was taken by Jean, and accompanied the X-Men on various missions for a time before Charles convinced Jean to let the dragon find a new home on Monster Isle.[175] Soon after, Xavier decided that the X-Men had passed their latest round of training and awarded them with new costumes.[173]

Beast and the other X-Men soon began new training but noted strange changes in the Professor and Marvel Girl. The X-Men were sent on a mission to investigate reports of the discovery of the Frankenstein's Monster, to determine if there was a mutant connection. The X-Men battled what they thought was the monster, but soon destroyed it, exposing it as a robot. The group were later able to learn that the robot was sent to Earth over 150 years prior to act as an ambassador for an alien race, but it soon malfunctioned.[176] Charles went into hiding to prepare for an invasion by the Z'nox and had the Changeling pose as him, confiding only in Jean Grey regarding this truth.[177] While on a date with Zelda and Vera, Hank and Bobby had to slip into costume to face by yet another subterranean dweller, Grotesk, who sought revenge for nuclear tests that killed his people and mutated him. When Grotesk threatened to use a device to destroy the surface world, the X-Men fought back against him.[178] During the final battle against Grotesk, the Professor seemingly sacrificed his life to destroy the creature's machine, seemingly slaying Grotesk as well. When Changeling died, he remained in Xavier's form leaving the X-Men to believe that Charles was dead.[179]

A funeral was arranged for the man whom the X-Men believed to be their mentor, but the mourning was disturbed by the arrival of Quicksilver who had come to pay his respects. Later they watched what they thought was video confession of the Professor telling them that he had a terminal illness and before his passing endowed Marvel Girl with telepathic powers. The Professor also warned that Magneto had returned, and soon the X-Men were out hunting their old foe. Tracking down his new hideout, the X-Men were unprepared to fight Magneto and his reformed Brotherhood of Evil Mutants and they were quickly captured.[180] Although the team was shackled, Marvel Girl was able to free Angel and he fled Magneto's base to seek out the Avengers and get their help.[181] While the Angel managed to make it to the Avengers, Cyclops also broke free and battled Quicksilver when suddenly the Avengers arrived.[182] Magneto had prepared for this and planted a bug on Angel for the Avengers to find, making it seem like he betrayed the Avengers. He then used a mind control device to turn the X-Men into his slaves and ordered them to attack the Avengers. However the Avengers found the bug, and realizing Magneto was leading them into a trap only pretended to incapacitate the Angel. Free once more, the Angel freed the X-Men, and the Brotherhood was forced to flee setting their base to explode. The X-Men, Avengers and most of the Brotherhood escaped.[183]

Growing Pains[]

Back at home, the X-Men continued to mourn the apparent loss of their leader. They had Xavier's will read to them by attorney Foggy Nelson who informed them that Xavier's school and financial assets were being given to his students. The X-Men's mourning was interrupted once again by the Juggernaut, brought back from the Crimson Cosmos by one of the Professor's machines. The X-Men battled the Juggernaut who could not believe that his step-brother was dead and thought it was a trick. However the Juggernaut's return to Earth proved only temporary and he was shunted back to the Crimson Cosmos. In the aftermath of the battle, FBI agent Fred Duncan told the X-Men that in order to handle mutant menaces, the X-Men had to be broken up across the country.[184] Before leaving, Bobby and Hank went on one last date with their girlfriends Zelda and Vera. This led to another clash with the Warlock who was posing as a stage hypnotist calling himself the Maha Yogi.[185]

Soon Bobby and Hank were sent out to the west coast,[186] not telling Zelda or Vera where they went.[187] The government mandate that the X-Men remain apart proved to be short lived when the mutant Mesmero used his powers to awaken latent mutants across America and gather them in his Mutant City on the west coast. One of these mutants was a young girl Lorna Dane who was saved from traffic by Bobby. The X-Men soon discovered that Lorna was a mutant. While the X-Men began trying to track down Mesmero and his mutant army, Iceman and Lorna were captured.[47] The X-Men managed to track down Bobby and Lorna and were shocked when Mesmero announced that Lorna was the daughter of Magneto, and that their old foe had seemingly survived their previous encounter.[46] This was not the true Magneto but an android created by Samuel Saxon.[188] The X-Men were no match for Mesmero and Magneto, and worse Lorna was conflicted as to where her loyalties were. The X-Men were then forced to flee and come up with a new plan. Cyclops then developed the identity of Eric the Red, and sought to infiltrate the Mutant City from the inside.[51]

While Cyclops succeeded in infiltrating the group, the X-Men's cover was blown by Iceman who had dug up information on Lorna's past and went back to Mutant City to reveal to her the truth. In the ensuing battle, Bobby convinced Lorna to renounce her alliance to Magneto after revealing documents that proved that he was not her father. The X-Men then destroyed the Mutant City, fleeing from their captors.[28] Back in New York, the X-Men were invited to attend the wedding of the Avengers Yellow Jacket and the Wasp.[189] Testing out some of Xavier's abandoned machines, Marvel Girl later accidentally pulled the Negative Zone tyrant known as Blastaar to their dimension. The X-Men proved no match for Blastaar, but eventually the very energies that brought him to Earth shunted him back into the Negative Zone.[52]

Having kept in touch with his younger brother over the years, Scott introduced the X-Men to Alex on the day he graduated from university. The reunion was marred when Alex was kidnapped by the Living Pharaoh. The Pharaoh managed to kidnap Alex while framing Scott for murder at the same time by faking his own death.[190] Cyclops later captured as well and brought to Egypt with his brother. There he learned that his brother was a mutant as well, able to control cosmic energy, but his powers were tied to the Pharaoh. The Pharaoh kidnapped Alex and planned to used a device to boost his own power. When the X-Men finally tracked them down to Egypt a battle broke out and ultimately the tide of battle was reversed when Alex manifested his mutant powers for the first time.[191] Alex was horrified by the level of power he commanded and while Cyclops tried to calm him down, they were attacked by the Pharaoh and his minions again. Alex was captured again and placed in a device to channel his powers into the Pharaoh transforming him into the gigantic super-powerful Living Monolith. However the Monolith was easily defeated when Alex managed to overload the device causing a massive explosion. The destruction of the machines caused the Pharaoh to revert back to human form, but Alex was still terrified of his powers.[192]

Havok and Lorna[]

While the X-Men had to deal with the Egyptian authorities, Alex fled into the desert. The X-Men learned that Lorna Dane had been kidnapped from her home. While Bobby and Hank returned to the States to search for her, Cyclops, Marvel Girl and Angel remained in Egypt to look for Alex. From Lorna's trashed apartment, Hank and Bobby soon learned that Larry Trask - son of Bolivar - and federal judge Robert Chalmers had reactivated the Sentinels and actively sent them out to capture mutants.[193] Iceman and Beast were then attacked by Sentinels, while Bobby was captured Hank managed to escape and warned the others who rushed back to the United States. Soon Larry's Sentinels kidnapped Angel, Mesmero, and other mutants. Cyclops, Beast and Marvel Girl located Larry's base and began to infiltrate it. Realizing that Larry intended to execute all mutants without a fair trail, Judge Chalmers removed a necklace from around Larry's neck that nullified his own mutant powers. However this was too late as his last orders to the Sentinels were to destroy all mutants.[194] The remaining X-Men battled the Sentinels, tricking them by switching costumes with the recently captured Quicksilver, Scarlet Witch and Toad. Ultimately, Scott tricked the Sentinels into defeating themselves by convincing them to attack the source of mutations, radiation, and nearly the entire Sentinel fleet flew into the sun.[195]

With Larry Trask's plot in shambles, the X-Men fled, and Scott sought out one of the Professor's old colleagues, Karl Lykos to treat Alex's injuries. What the X-Men were unaware of was that Lykos was a non-mutant who required the life forces of others to live. Absorbing some of Alex's life force caused Lykos to transform into the pterodactyl-man Sauron who then went on a rampage across the city.[196] The X-Men went after Sauron and fought him, but as the creature's powers began to run low and a reversion back to human form began, Sauron hypnotically controlled Angel and had him bring Lykos back to his lab. The Return of Professor X[]

By this time Xavier was finally ready to fend off the Z'Nox invasion. He revealed himself to Havok and Lorna, who had been waiting at the mansion while the X-Men were away. Hidden Years[]

Finally back in full health, Charles had the X-Men brief him on their activities while he was in hiding.[212] First they showed him the Sentinel ships that they had captured in his absence. Charles used this opportunity to test their abilities by creating a mental battle between the X-Men and illusions of their greatest foes.[213] Learning that his students failed to confirm if Magneto had died in their last encounter, Charles sent them back to the Savage Land to investigate. Meanwhile, the male members of the X-Men awoke to learn that Marvel Girl was sent to the land of the dead, and assumed that she had died in the crash.[212] However, they soon learned that the natives were literal and that Jean was sent to an actual land. Cyclops and Hank fought their way to the underground river where those who were dead or dying were sent to this land while Angel tried to locate it from the air. While Hank and Scott located an ancient and advanced city, Angel was knocked out by the strong wind currents.[187] Havok and Lorna arrived in the Savage Land and began searching for their comrades, gaining the aid form Ka-Zar. Meanwhile, Scott and Hank evaded capture from the Nhu'Ghari while Marvel Girl awoke to find that the Nhu'Ghari served the apparent ghost of Magneto.[214]

Magneto began collecting the restorative volcanic gas found in the region as part of a plot to enslave humanity and had a number of balloons filled with it ready to be launched. Cyclops, Marvel Girl, Beast and Angel were reunited and battled Magneto, however the fight caused volcanic instability. Although they managed to flee the slaves, the X-Men found themselves trapped. Taking Magneto and Avia with them, they attempted fled the destruction in Magneto's blimp, but found themselves caught in a massive storm.[215] As they bucked the storm, Magneto jumped ship with Avia following after him. Angel tried to follow her but the storm was too powerful. With the blimp breaking up, each of the X-Men latched themselves to a different balloon in the hopes that they could ride the storm out and safely return to the surface. Cyclops, Beast and Marvel Girl found themselves crash landing in Africa where they were found by the young Ororo Munroe.[216] The X-Men soon learned that the massive storm was being gathered by an albino mutant Deluge who sought to get revenge against the world for the life of prejudice he had to endure.[217] The X-Men managed to defeat Deluge. Having recovered from the crash, Cyclops and the others returned to the mansion to find the Fantastic Four waiting for them.[218]

The Fantastic Four had come to Xavier to learn why he tapped their minds during the Z'Nox invasion, and when Mister Fantastic pointed out that the Z'Nox were still a threat the X-Men joined them on a mission into space to stop the alien invaders.[219] Ultimately, the Fantastic Four and the X-Men incapacitated the Z'Nox to ensure they would be a threat to no other race in the universe and returned to Earth.[220] Charles later returned to the mansion where Cerebro detected a new mutant in Illinois. Taking Hank with him, Charles discovered the new mutant was a young girl named Ashley Martin whose powers allowed her to take control of inanimate objects, including a Sentinel.[221] Seeing that the girl could control the Sentinel, Charles and Hank also met Ashley's mother Teri who was shocked to learn her daughter was a mutant. When the Sentinel attempted to destroy them, Ashley ordered it to destroy itself,[222] and angered at Xavier's interference threatened to turn her powers against him.[223]

With Ashley a dangerous threat, Charles used his mental powers to place mental blocks that prevented the girl from using her abilities and made her forget about the incident.[224] Charles then sent Hank back to the mansion while he remained behind to help Teri clean up the mess caused by the Sentinel and deal with the authorities.[225] Also rejoining the team were Iceman, Havok and Angel.[225] Angel learned that Candy sought him out because his uncle Bert - secretly the villain known as Dazzler - was attempting to marry his mom. The X-Men rushed to the Worthington estate to try and hal the wedding.[225] While the X-Men managed to stop Burt from marrying Warren's mother, he had been secretly poisoning her and she soon died as a result.[226]

Cyclops, Marvel Girl, Iceman, and Beast returned to the mansion while Warren stayed behind to see to his mother's funeral arrangements. Learning that Havok and Lorna went out seeking the signal picked up by Cerebro, Cyclops and the others went after them. In the mountains they saved Havok and Lorna from the Inhuman Yeti. The Yeti was stopped by the Inhuman royal family, and the X-Men learned that Lorna was captured due to her resemblance to Yeti's long deceased Skrull lover. When the X-Men returned to the mansion they soon found it had been infiltrated by Kraven the Hunter.[227]

Kraven the Hunter broke into Xavier's School demanding the Beast to play part in one of his sick hunting games. To guarantee his participation, the Hunter poisoned Avia, a protégée of the X-Men from the jungles of the Savage Land, as a form of blackmailing. The Beast evaded different traps set in the field by Kraven, who wished to hone his skills. Slowly, the Beast had drugs administered into his organism, which start to cloud his reason. The intoxicated Beast could no longer resort to his intellect, losing control and regressing to a berserker rage. He brutally maimed his hunter, being stopped by the X-Men's intervention. Jean Grey erased Kraven's memories about the encounter, something the Beast asked her to do for him. She reassured him his merit was placing himself in danger to protect Avia and he should not worried about losing his gentility when under pressure.[228]

It was also around this time that a group of mutants calling themselves the Promise came out of a decade of cryogenic suspension and began seeking new mutants to join their cause. As the X-Men were occupied with Kraven the Hunter, the Promise enthralled Lorna and called her to their secret headquarters.[228] The X-Men went after Lorna but were easily incapacitated thanks to the various mental powers of the Promise. Unable to convince the other X-Men to join them, Tobias forced Lorna and Havok to be put in cryogenic suspension with them. In the aftermath of this crisis, the Promise were freed from their cryogenic stasis and Avia was returned to the Savage Land. The X-Men later celebrated Hank's 20th birthday.[233]

Finals[]

Following their trials and tribulations, the X-Men went on another vacation. Bobby initially decided to stay home, but Xavier convinced he and Hank go on a road trip. The two traveled across America in Warren's convertible, accidentally flooding it when they were caught in a heavily rainstorm.[234] While arguing over Lorna, Alex accidentally blasted Bobby with his powers. Horrified he left the X-Men, with Lorna fleeing after him.[235] Lorna and Alex soon went missing, kidnapped by the Secret Empire, who was secretly kidnapping mutants for their latest attempt to conquer the world.[236] Charles was later asked by NASA to mentally probe a passing comet. In doing so, it caused a chunk of alien matter to detach itself and land near the mansion. When the X-Men returned home, the alien matter stripped them of their powers just as their home was attacked by an army of Sentinels. Although powerless, the X-Men used their combat training to defend themselves and keep the Professor safe.[237]

Realizing that the X-Men no longer had mutant powers, the Sentinels ended their attack and instead targeted Quicksilver and the Scarlet Witch. The X-Men came to their rescue, and they soon found their powers were not only returning but becoming more enhanced at a rapid rate. Hank's agility and strength were increased to greater levels. Charles soon detected the alien matter thanks to his increasing mental talents, and realizing that this increase in power could be dangerous had his X-Men eject the alien matter ejected into space, causing the team to revert their normal power levels and limitations.[238] However the alien matter had an impact on the Nexus of All Realities located in a Florida swamp causing a disruption in time in space. Charles sent his X-Men to investigate and they were sucked into various portals to possible future realities. Hank was briefly trapped in a future where he was transformed into a feral, more beastial version of himself before he was rescued by Cyclops and the others.[239] The X-Men were later called into New York by the Continuiteens who detected a disruption in reality. The X-Men uncovered that it was the work Mysterio who had stolen swamp water from the Nexus zone to alter reality.[240]

Also around this time, Moira MacTaggart was locating and recruiting new mutants to be trained at her mutant research center on Muir Island. With the rest of his team reaching the final stretch of their college education, Charles was secretly away on Muir Island, the X-Men began being attacked by a series of threats including rampant Danger Room robots, a returned Frederick,[246] and strange phantoms of the Juggernaut and an apparent "magnetic ghost" of Magneto.[247] Charles returned to find his school in shambles. He called the X-Men back and learned of the recent attacks on the school. After advising his team that he intended to expand the scope of his school to an international level and focus primarily in training new mutants to control their powers and defend themselves, Charles and the others soon realized that the strange phantom attacks were coming from Jean's mind.[248] Charles and his students then ventured into Jean's mind and helped her cope with her fears. With the crisis averted, the X-Men were soon graduated from their classes.[249] Following graduation, Hank was offered a position with the Brand Corporation. Charles and his X-Men bid the Beast farewell as he went on to a new life.[27]

Transformation[]

Hank moved out of Xavier's School to pursue his career at the Brand Corporation. There he made quick impressions with the company. However he earned the ire of his co-worker Carl Maddicks who demanded that Hank stay out of his way. McCoy was also smitten by employee Linda Donaldson who seemingly was interested in Hank as well. Unknown to Hank was that both worked for the Secret Empire and Linda was only pretending to be interested in him in order to try and steal company secrets. Left to his own devices, Hank began isolating the chemical cause of mutation. The resulting serum acted as a catalyst for activating latent mutations for short periods of time. After overhearing Maddicks plotting with a security guard to steal material from the genetics lab, wishing to stop the theft but worried he would be recognized, Hank consumed his chemical discovery causing him to mutate into a hairy form. This enhanced Hank's strength, agility, endowed him with enhanced healing, but also affected his personality making him less verbose and prone to anger and acts of savagery. Hank managed to stop the security guard from making his theft and fled security when he was spotted. However, the Beast failed to keep track of the time. Not having administered an antidote within an hour, the metamorphosis became permanent. Furious, McCoy lashed out at Maddicks, almost choking him to death before stopping himself and fleeing from security.[27]

When Jean Grey and Professor Xavier tried to later contact Hank, he rejected their call for help.[250] He briefly returned to his hometown in Dunfee to meet his family and friends, but could not reveal his condition, except for Jennifer Nyles.[9] Seeking to find a way to cover up his transformation, Hank then studied makeup effects creating a latex mask and gloves to hide his new beastial forms, and constructed a body harness to force him into a normal posture. As such, Hank McCoy and the Beast became two sides of a secret double life, usually manifesting frustration and stress. Hank then began working on a possible cure, becoming a bit of a terror in the lab. Hank later met with Stark Industries owner Tony Stark, but blew him off to have dinner with Linda when Tony's fiancee Marianne Rodgers sensed Linda's evil nature. However when Linda attempted to get intimate with Hank, he had to blow her off.[251]

Piqued with curiosity of the beast sightings in the area, Tony returned to the Brand Corporation as Iron Man. When he found the Beast, the two fought it out. The battle came to an abrupt end when the Beast was manipulated by one of Mastermind's illusions, leaving him to believe he slew Iron Man. Horrified, Hank then fled the scene.[250] Mastermind and his new Brotherhood of Evil Mutants were under the employ of the Secret Empire as well. The trauma of thinking he killed Iron Man caused Hank to be stricken with a bout of amnesia. Thus when the Brotherhood tracked him down, neither recognized each other. Hank was convinced to join their cause and his abilities were exploited to steal Rahmur Diamond. Eventually Hank's memory was restored and he defeated the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants and recovered the diamond, leaving his foes for the authorities.[251]

Hank's absence from the Brand Corporation was noticed, and Hank's cover was almost blown by a surprise visit from Linda. Hank had to use a prototype mask and a trench coat to cover himself up. Needing his real disguise, Hank broke into the Brand Corporation and recovered it. Trying to sneak off the property, he ran into Linda, as well as the new Air Force security liaison Lt. Buzz Baxter and his wife Patricia. When Hank tried to cover for himself, Baxter demanded to meet with Hank to explain his absences after Linda refused to cover for him. Later in his lab, Hank discovered it had been infiltrated by the living computer Quasimodo who had sought out the Beast in order to absorb his life energy and apparently obtain his long sought humanity. He was challenged between keeping his fantastical powers or be an ordinary human. When Hank fought against impossible odds, Quasimodo appeared to commit "suicide" rather than steal Hank's life. However, Hank found that his enhanced healing was no longer working as he was seriously wounded by a rivet gun.[252]

Seeking cover, Hank instinctively sought cover at the Baxter home where he was helped by Patricia. While in a coma, he spoke in his sleep and unintentionally revealed his true identity to her. As he healed, Hank's fur change from grey to a blue-black color. When he revived, Patricia vowed to keep Hank's secret.[253] Walker had long idolized super heroes and elicited him to promise that to keep his secret he was to introduced her to the super-human community.[254] Hank was later attacked by another member of the Secret Empire, the Griffin. During the battle, Hank's past came back to him when the Angel came seeking out his friend out of concern. The pair easily defeated the Griffin and then sought cover. This proved fortuitous, as Warren assisted Hank in covering for him during his interview with Buzz Baxter. Hank's made up story was also corroborated by Patricia, Buzz's wife. Hank was attacked by the Griffin again, but both he and the Angel beat him again and left him to the authorities. Convinced that Hank had everything under control, Warren returned to Xavier's. Not long after Warren's departure, Hank was visited by his old girlfriend Vera Cantor who needed his help.[253]

Vera refused to tell him what the problem was until they arrived. They passed through Rutland Vermont, getting stranded there during the annual Halloween Parade. The mystical nature of Rutland drew the Juggernaut to the region, however he had to struggle to remain free of the Crimson Cosmos. When the Juggernaut attacked Hank, he fought back as the Beast, removing the Juggernaut's helmet and leaving him open to weakness. Succumbing to fear, the Juggernaut began to age rapidly and was drawn back into the Crimson Cosmos.[255] Arriving in Canada, Hank and Vera had to slip past customs after an alert for the Hulk was raised in the area. Vera took Hank to remote cabin in the Canadian wilderness where he learned that the Mimic's powers were growing out of control. Not only were they back stronger than before, he was draining the energies of people near him and that the range of his powers were growing exponentially and that they threatened to wipe out the entire world. Hank began working diligently to try and cure Mimic, but the examination was interrupted by the Hulk who was drawn to Mimic as he was unintentionally draining the Hulk's strength. Hank tried to battle the Hulk, but the gamma spawned monster proved too powerful. Realizing that his friends were in danger, Mimic sacrificed his life to save them by mimicking the gamma radiation in the Hulk's body, killing himself with radiation poisoning. With the power drain stopped, the Hulk fled the scene.[256] Amidst these events, McCoy grew immensely frustrated by learning Brand Corporation had rights over all of his research.[257] While Hank attempted to return home he was captured by the Secret Empire.[258]

Hank was held prisoner with Iceman, Havok, Lorna, Warren, as well as the Blob, Unus, Mastermind and Mesmero. The Secret Empire was using their mental energies to power a weapon. They were found by Cyclops, Professor X, and Marvel Girl who had teamed up with Captain America and the Falcon to shut the Empire down. Before the captured mutants could be freed, the Secret Empire seemingly slew heroes with an atomic annihilator weapon.[236] However the Empire had been infiltrated by S.H.I.E.L.D. agents Gabe Jones and Peggy Carter who were controlling the cannon and fired a non-lethal blast. Getting X-Men and the others to cover, they regrouped and battled the Secret Empire when they attempted to storm the White House. While the X-Men freed their allies and defeated the Empire, Cap exposed their leader as a high ranking government official who committed suicide rather than face capture.[259] Although rescued by the X-Men, Hank did not rejoin the team, deciding to continue to operate on his own, he eventually ended up joining the Avengers.[4] Depressed about his appearance, Hank returned home for a time, thanks to Cyclops, but he left again, after reassurance of his importance from his old girlfriend, Jennifer Nyles.[9]

Avengers[]

Earth's Mightiest Heroes, the Avengers, publicly announced their need for expanding their roster. The Beast applied for membership, declaring at the Yankee Stadium that he had fully abandoned his civilian identity after leaving Brand Corporation and chose to live the life of a super-hero.[4] During his interview, the Avengers were attacked by terrible Toad, impersonating the cosmic entity known as Stranger using his technology. The Beast joined the team in striking back, being the one responsible for recognizing they were dealing with an imposter. Afterwards, he was accepted on a probationary status alongside the psychic Moondragon.[260] A chain of events untangled from this battle, with the Wasp and Hank Pym sustaining injuries.[261] The Beast broke into his old laboratory at Brand Corporation to recollect his serum and continue his research to recover them, which proved to be a success.[257]

As a public Avenger, the Beast was recognized by Patsy Walker.[261] At Avengers Mansion, she confronted the Beast about his forgotten promise. Now divorced, she attempted to blackmail him using his civilian identity with no avail, since he had abandoned it. In the midst of this, the Beast learned Brand Corporation had ties with the criminal activities of Roxxon as one of its subsidiaries, leading the Avengers to investigate in a mission to which he decided to bring Walker. At Brand, the team was surprised and defeated by the Squadron Supreme, with Captain Baxter imprisoning them under orders from Hugh Jones, Roxxon's president.[262] In one of Brand's warehouses, Walker found and donned the Cat Suit, becoming heroine Hellcat, which fulfill the Beast's promise. Jones sent the Avengers to the Squadron Supreme's reality,[254] where they discovered that the President of the United States was under the control of the Serpent Crown.[263] The Beast impersonated him, exposing the machinations of that world's corporate conglomerates, after which he and the Avengers regained access to their home reality.[5] Returned, they defeated Jones and Baxter, putting an end to their conspiracies to control the planet.[264]

When selecting their new roster, the Avengers accepted the Beast as a full member, while Hellcat departed with Moondragon to receive additional training.[265] His inclusion coincided with the return of Wonder Man from the dead, which the Avengers investigated.[266] The two engaged in a hand-to-hand fight, leading to Wonder Man's defeat and apprehension. The Beast helped him recollect his reason and break free from the influence of the Serpent Crown used by the Living Laser.[267] He also brought Wonder Man to the field, vouching for him during missions to assist the Avengers.[268] As their association with the Avengers went on, the Beast and Wonder Man developed a close friendship.[269][270][271] In between battles the Avengers had against formidable foes such as Graviton,[272] Ultron,[273], Count Nefaria,[274] and Thanos,[275] the Beast felt diminished to his teammates in comparison to his previous role as an X-Man.[276][277] On the other hand, he soon came to enjoy the popularity of being a prestigious super-hero.[278]

The Beast received a distress call from Polaris stating that Havok had been kidnapped, but the X-Men were nowhere to be found.[279] In his investigation, he found Xavier's School deserted and the X-Men enthralled by Mesmero and acting as circus attractions in Texas. When confronting his friends Jean Grey and Cyclops, the Beast was violently attacked. Wolverine was the only X-Man who could resist the brainwashing, snapping the X-Men out of it to rescue a defeated Beast.[280] Magneto took advantage of the assault to kidnap the X-Men to his base in Antarctica after preventing Mesmero to enchant Beast. He kept the X-Men caged under the care of the psychotic robot Nanny.[281] In fighting back, the X-Men caused Magneto's base to collapse and got split up. In the frigid landscape, the Beast was left alone with Jean Grey, with them believing the other X-Men had died.[282] Back to the school, they shared the dire new with Professor Xavier.[283] In fact, the X-Men had survived the ordeal. Upon their arrival to the school some time later, the security systems were activated, prompting the Beast to check the place. He blissfully celebrated his friends' return.[284]

Despite being a charismatic personality and a sensation in the city's nightlife as a celebrity, the Beast still suffered anti-mutant prejudice. His torment and doubt was exploited by the Manipulator, who brainwashed the Beast through one of his dates. Following a heist, the Beast was released with no recollection of the ordeal.[269] Following a massive cosmic crisis involving the omnipotent Korvac,[285] the Avengers had their roster extremely restrained by Henry P. Gyrich. While the Beast remained with the team, his dear friend Wonder Man was decommissioned.[270] The two were some of the Avengers participating in the Thing's first Super-Hero Poker Game.[286] Gyrich's authoritarianism eventually led him to disband the Avengers,[287] but his strategy to control them failed as the government reestablished the team with him no longer being in full charge of ther charter. The Beast became thrilled with this outcome,[288] which enabled the reinstatement of Wonder Man's membership.[289] McCoy drafted his friend to his bohemian lifestyle,[290] and they had adventures on their own, such as investigating the bizarre Crawlers in the sewers of New York City.[291] In another adventure, while attending a scient exhibit at the Empire State University, the Beast teamed up with Spider-Man in battling Killer Strike and the Modular Man.[292]

The X-Men targeted the secret cabal of villains of the Hellfire Club, who corrupted Jean Grey's powers leading her to descend as Dark Phoenix. Endangered Species[]

Meanwhile, mutantkind suffered a major crisis incited by the Scarlet Witch, which Beast accompanied with the Avengers and the X-Men.[473][474] Ultimately, this resulted in the deactivation of the majority of the mutants on the planet, the onset of a mutant decimation. Beast was one of the few dozens of mutants who preserved their powers,[475] and immediately focused on dealing with the consequences of it. Trish Tilby attempted to contact him, but he rejected her. The Xavier Institute became a refugee camp patrolled by Sentinels commissioned by O*N*E.[476] Tensions raised between the refuges, self-called the 198, and O*N*E agents, with Beast attempting to intervene in their contacts.[477] The conflict escalated, and Beast alongside the original X-Men composed a secret team to tackle the conflict.[478]

Given the decline of the mutant species, Beast became alarmed to reverse the situation. The ordinary death of Matt Landru, one of the few surviving mutants after the decimation, sparked desperation.[479] Having explored all the possible analyses with his allies, Beast made contact with unscrupulous scientists he would usually regard as enemies.[480] Among these, he only was welcomed at first by the High Evolutionary, prompting him to visit Mount Wundagore.[18] Their meeting was not fruitful, however, since the Evolutionary had little hopes in restoring the mutant race.[481] Beast next had access to Dr. Kavita Rao's research on the mutant cure,[482] being led to the ruins of Neverland, a concentration camp formerly run by Weapon X. Tragically, he had access to information about the victims who were brutally tortured and merciless executed in the location. There, he was met by his shadow, the Dark Beast from the Age of Apocalypse timeline.[483] Reluctantly, Beast saw in his doppelgänger a resource to approach and revert the mutant extinction.[484]

Together, the Beasts collected data for their research. Dark Beast provided a memory serum, which upon administration gave Beast all of the scientific knowledge from the macabre reality ruled by Apocalypse, including the atrocities Dark Beast performed in the name of science. Overwhelmed by the shared experience, Beast arrived on the research center in Alamogordo, where researchers such as Xavier's father and Mister Sinister carried out illicit experiments on mutant babies decades before.[485][486] From there, they dissected mutant corpses on the ruins of Genosha, only to learn the dimension of the decimation, which had eradicated the mutant genome even in dead tissue.[487] In District X, Beast teamed up with Bishop to look for traces of mutant DNA in the drug supplies of Mutant Growth Hormone. Similarly to the material from the corpses on Genosha, it had also been rendered inert.[488] Beast realized his search went to a dead end when he discussed with Forge the possibility of the mutant genome being present in other realities, only to find, through Forge's results, that the repercussions of the mutant decimation were multiversal.[489]

Having no hope, Beast returned to his extradimensional twin, regarding him as the only one who could actually provide valuable help. They went to the Guthrie Farmhouse in order to assess the potential for mutant birth from the members of the Guthrie family. Dark Beast indecently risked the life of the young Lewis Guthrie in a forced attempt to jumpstart his genomic potential by infecting him with carrion material from Genosha.[490] Beast knocked out his evil double and permanently cut ties with him, although severely worried about his own corruption.[491] Being taunted by Spiral, the sorceress from Mojoworld who regarded the mutants as entertainment, Beast decided to search for a solution in the realms of magics, beyond science. He visited Doctor Strange in his Sanctum Sanctorum, only to learn the Scarlet Witch's spell could not be reversed.[492] In desolation, Beast abandoned his mission and marched to his last resort:[493] searching for the Scarlet Witch herself. Back to Mount Wundagore, he found her amnesiac and living an ordinary life. Accepting the tragedy that surrounded his race, Beast concluded his crusade.[494]

X-Club[]

Alongside Professor X, Beast witnessed the birth of the first mutant since decimation.[495] Different factions hunted the infant down, with the Xavier Institute being infected by O*N*E Sentinels,[496] with Beast treating the wounded[497] and taking care of the New X-Men.[498] A massive battle took place on Muir Island, leading to Professor X went missing and Cyclops disbanding the X-Men.[499] Beast visited the ruins of the Xavier Institute in order to destroy any records left behind, especially those on deceased mutants.[500] Cyclops reformed the X-Men in San Francisco by Mayor Sadie Sinclair's suggestion.[501] Around this period, Beast underwent a discreet body change and regained some of the finger dexterity he had lacked since transforming into a feline creature.[502]

The city's police identified a mutant murder on which the X-Men provided counsel. Beast recognized both victim and murderer to be manufactured mutants.[503] Understanding the mutants came from an alternate dimension, McCoy contacted Agent Brand, who identified the X-Men dealt with interdimensional portals known as Ghost Boxes. In the Chinese mutant city of Tian,[504] the X-Men found artificial mutants created by an insane Forge.[505] The X-Men confronted him at Mount Wundagore, with Beast expressing disappointment with his actions. Through Forge's Ghost Box, Beast instructed Brand to eliminate the threat of the dimensional interlopers.[506] When S.W.O.R.D. intercept a Brood invasion of Earth, Brand's spaceship fell onto the atmosphere, with Beast and the X-Men rescuing her before a crash.[507] The creatures they confronted were revealed to be living weapons engineered with genetic material from deceased mutants. The procedures took results from Beast's work on the decimation[508] stolen by Kaga through Paradigm's corpse.[509] Cyclops reprimanded Beast for developing science that could be weaponized, sparking a debate between the two friends about the ethics of censoring academic advancement.[510] Kaga was put down and placed under Brand's captivity.[511] Soon after, the X-Men received reports of mutant births in Mbangawi, Africa.[512] Working with Mutantes Sans Frontières, Beast recognized radiation from a Ghost Box causing the mutations.[513] He confronted Doctor Crocodile, regent of Mbangawi, about investigating the case instead of merely murdering the infants, who were Warpies.[514] An alternate Jim Jaspers acted as a living Ghost Box, summoning a legion of Furies, Beast and the X-Men battled the Furies and ended the crisis.[515]

As the X-Men familiarized themselves with San Francisco, Earth was subjected to an invasion by Skrulls. Acting as heroes for the city, the X-Men fought the alien invaders. Cyclops had Beast hack into the Skrulls' technology with Prodigy's assistance.[516] While battling the Skrulls in the open, Cyclops had Beast prepare a contingency plan in secrecy by providing him with Super-Skrull tissue.[517] As Beast performed an autopsy to find a weakness in the Skrull genome, Nightcrawler presented him with a biblical device which claimed mutants and Skrulls both were creatures of change. This inspired Beast to modify the Legacy Virus to target Skrulls.[518] Despite Beast arguing about the ethics of genocide as tactics, Cyclops led the infection to commander H'kurrek and his soldiers. In order to save mutantkind from imminent extinction due to the repercussions of the decimation, Dr. McCoy decided to assemble a team of specialists to act for the benefit of mutantkind, the X-Club.[520] During recruitment, Beast was accompanied by Angel. During the mission, they fought a rudimentary version of a Sentinel created by the Hellfire Club, inadvertently causing the San Francisco earthquake. Upon returning to the present, their DNA evidence had been buried on where the Dreaming Celestial ended up landing.[526]

During the formation of X-Club, Beast realized that Cyclops and Emma Frost, as leaders of the X-Men, were hiding secrets concerning their activities and associations.[520] He confronted them about their shadowy allegiances and expressed he would leave the X-Men in case they got corrupted.[527] Cyclops' main plan for mutantkind's survival involved securing the messianic child who had been sent to the future with Cable. He demanded Beast to replicate Forge's time-travelling technology and locate them across the timeline, ultimately exposing his secret operation formed by mutant killers, X-Force. Despite Beast's opposition to the plan, his work had X-Force be teleported into the future to find the first mutant baby born after the Scarlet Witch's spell.[528][529]

No Utopia[]

Frost's secrets, meanwhile, concerned her association with Norman Osborn, who had ascended as the leader of the Initiative and the Avengers through his law-enforcing organization, H.A.M.M.E.R. Having resettled on Utopia, Beast could not feel at ease with the X-Men, who he started to regard as an extremist militia. As Dr. Yuriko Takiguchi passed away due to natural causes, the way Cyclops casually dealt with the funeral displeased Beast.[535] Moreover, Magneto's proposal to associate himself with the X-Men on Utopia was accepted.[536] Harrowed by the torture he had experienced in Osborn's custody as part of Cyclops' plans, Beast concluded he had to cut ties with the X-Men.[537] Despite Cyclops' protests, feeling that his friend had gone too far in trying to defend mutantkind, Beast left Utopia and the X-Men.[35]

Gyrich to act as S.W.O.R.D.'s co-commander alongside Brand, much to her despise. Once again, he left, this time being insulted by Namor as a deserter of his own people.[546] Before leaving San Francisco once again, he gained alternative perspective regarding the mutant extinction from Molly Hayes, a mutant kid who was part of the Runaways.[547]

Secret Avengers[]

Following the fall of Osborn's reign, Commander Steve Rogers reformulated the Avengers, of which Beast returned to be a member.[548] Rogers ran a black ops team of Secret Avengers to combat terrorism from the shadows. Searching for intelligence about the Serpent Crown from Roxxon, Beast learned they ran operations on Mars.[549] On the red planet, the team learned about the cryptic group known as the Shadow Council being interested in collecting Martian crowns of power. Rogers and Beast were approached by Archon, protector of the Crown of Thorns, who informed them about the dangers of harnessing the crowns and secured it.[550] The Shadow Council, led by the Life Model Decoy Max Fury, became the main enemies of Rogers' Avengers,[551] who Beast coordinated with intel during missions.[552] He also provided the team with tech support, such as when he designed a memory inspector to recondition John Steele from the Shadow Council's brainwashing.[553]

At some point, Beast was recruited by Mister Fantastic alongside other geniuses to assess Bruce Banner's condition after becoming unable to change into the Hulk. McCoy ran tests and reported no mutate cells in Banner's bloodstream. Banner, however, fled from the group of scientists,[554] leading Beast to send Wolverine to hunt him down as he provoked the unstoppable Juggernaut into entering a fight.[555] Later, the super-villain scientist cabal known as the Intelligencia kidnapped some of the world's smartest people, including Beast, to remove their opposition.[556] Banner released them as several heroes were corrupted by Gamma Radiation. With the help of the geniuses, he reengineered the Cathexis Ray Generator to absorb the radiation into himself and be restored as the incredible Hulk and destroy the Intelligencia Helicarrier.[557] By identifying planet K'ai emerging on the Solar System directed to Earth, Beast contacted his fellow peers to consider a solution.[558] Commander Rogers' Secret Avengers engaged in battle against the Hulk due to his connection to Hiro-Kala, monarch of the planet part of the imminent collision.[559] Unable to stop the Hulk, Beast and the Avengers attempted to reason with him about his methods to stop Hiro-Kala with no avail.[560]

When the world was thrown into immense fear by the attacks of Sin, the Avengers were mobilized to resist her multiple attacks. Mutant congressman Lenny Gray was trapped inside the Capitol Building as Sin's agents raided Washington, D.C. since he refused to evacuate the place. He personally requested for Beast's attendance. As old friends, the two discussed the importance of resisting the attack without running away, which was followed by Gray's sacrifice.[561] Meanwhile, Rogers' Secret Avengers kept their silent war against the Shadow Council. Below Cincinnati, the Shadow Council repurposed an abandoned hidden base that once belonged to the Secret Empire and had Time Platform technology that could obliterate the city above. With no alternative, Beast took the difficult decision of transforming an atomic flying car the Avengers drove into a neutron bomb to destroy the threat. In the process, several Shadow Council agents were killed in action. Rogers consoled Beast about the necessity of such a decision.[562] In a following assignment, in which O*N*E was infiltrated by the Shadow Council, Beast refused to resort to lethal alternatives again.[563]

The Jean Grey School[]

When S.W.O.R.D.'s satellite and research facility Pandora's Box was seized by the Brood, Agent Brand was abducted during the mission to free it. Beast enlisted help from the X-Men on Utopia for assistance considering the Avengers were not the best diplomatic alternative. Upon arrival, an infected Brand informed that the Brood planned to use their facility to repopulate their decreasing species.[564] Brand betrayed the X-Men and held Beast at gunpoint, arguing the Brood had to be preserved as an important part of the cosmic food chain. Beast proposed to be purposefully infected by the Brood alongside the other X-Men to experiment on the Brood offspring they would generate and mutate their evil tendencies. In contact with the Brood's hivemind, Brand informed Beast that such a mutant Brood had already been born inside Pandora's Box. She suggested the infant to be raised by the X-Men and instructed with their values of peaceful coexistence.[565] Despite being inoculated with Brood embryos, the X-Men resisted and expelled them from the station. Afterwards, Beast lamented his departure from the X-Men.[566] This coincided with Wolverine having an ideological schism with Cyclops as well. In the end, Wolverine returned to Westchester to repurpose the ruins of the Xavier Institute being accompanied by several X-Men and students.[567] Through their mutual association with the Avengers, Wolverine contacted Beast to help him, who promptly accepted the offer.[568] Sacrificing most of his time for the project, McCoy designed and built the new academy, baptized as the Jean Grey School for Higher Learning. The facilities were erected with extensive use of Shi'ar technology. One of the new students was Broo, the mutant Brood infant rescued by S.W.O.R.D., who became a promising pupil Beast mentored.[569]

Despite his best efforts, Beast could not understand defects the building manifested, including an infestation of teleporting gremlin Bamfs and unusual tectonic activity. On the brink of losing his sanity due to overworking as the school collapsed, he acted erratic in face of the state's inspectors during inauguration day.[569] The cause of the earthquakes was revealed to be the action of the new Hellfire Club, who wished to weaponize Sentinels for profit and saw the school as an ideological threat. As such, they implanted a clone of Krakoa, the walking island, on the grounds of the school Beast had constructed. With Krakoa pacified, the Jean Grey School was safe to start its activities with Beast as its vice principal and teacher of different subjects.[570] After a few days of running the school, the X-Men found several difficulties.[571] Amidst these, Dr. Xanto Starblood, an intergalactic biologist and terrorist, attacked the academy with a legion of Brood drones to meet the mutant Broo. While Beast, reduced to microscopic size with Pym Particles, fought the Brood inside the bloodstream of Kitty Pryde, Broo had to resort to brutal violence to stop Dr. Starblood. In the end, Beast consoled him.[572] Next, Beast himself got lost in violence and ferocity as the Hellfire Club hired Sabretooth to harm the X-Men. He planned to break Beast's spirit by massacring the personnel at the S.W.O.R.D.'s Peak, including Agent Brand. Beast viciously mauled Sabretooth to stop his assault and preserve Brand's life.[573]

Parallel to his contributions to the Jean Grey School and the X-Men, Beast remained with the Secret Avengers. Alongside Hank Pym, he built the Lighthouse, a covert and tiny space station that functioned as the team's headquarters. The team, now led by Hawkeye, fought the Descendants, a group of artificial Adaptoids infiltrated on Earth.[574] In the subterranean mechanical city of the Core, Beast was severely wounded by a Deathlok of Ms. America and kept captive alongside Hawkeye. Thank to the timely intervention of Ant-Man,[575] Beast and Hawkeye escaped the Core with the rest of the Avengers.[576]

Avengers vs. X-Men[]

The Avengers identified that the Phoenix Force marched to Earth. Afraid of the potential danger it represented for the planet, they planned to combat it. Beast and Wolverine sided with them, being consulted by Steve Rogers about the situation. This included them preparing themselves to confront the X-Men in the crisis. Beast was also enlisted to a secret mission to outer space aimed at stopping the Phoenix.[577][578] For that purpose, Beast devised a Phoenix Cage using the same technology from the mnemonic scrambler he deployed against her in the past, being particularly unsettled during the mission. War Machine wore the device, but the attempt was a disaster as the Phoenix quickly overwhelmed his systems. Additionally, Minister Marvel lured the Phoenix to Hala, diverting its route.[579] Stranded on Hala and attacked by a brainwashed and revived Captain Marvel, Beast and the Avengers ultimately freed him. To stop the Phoenix, Beast suggested Ms. Marvel to negate its energies with her powers. In the end, Captain Marvel sacrificed his life to fend the Phoenix off.[580] During this incident, Beast was particularly offended by Noh-Varr's loyalty to the Kree Supreme Intelligence, personally banning him from the Avengers and Earth.[581] Ultimately failing, the Avengers could not prevent the Phoenix from arriving at the Blue Area of the Moon, where it was supposed to bond with the mutant messiah, Hope, as the Avengers waged war against the X-Men.[582] Due to the intervention of Iron Man, the Phoenix bonded with some of the X-Men instead.[583]

The hosts of the Phoenix aimed to transform Earth into a utopia, stopping wars. The Avengers plotted to stop them, believing that they would eventually lose control. Beast and Wolverine remained with them, initially resisting the X-Men by hiding in Wakanda. However, he could not tolerate fighting those he considered family and left the Avengers. As he left, he considered he had no more alliances.[584] He relocated to K'un-Lun, where the Avengers provided asylum to Hope. As Cyclops and Emma Frost showed signs of corruption as the remaining Phoenixes, the X-Men started to abandon their cause.[585] Supported by the Avengers and led by Professor Xavier, Beast returned to the X-Men to stop the Phoenix.[586] During the ensuing battle, Cyclops usurped the entire power of the Phoenix and, as Dark Phoenix, murdered Professor Xavier before the eyes of the X-Men, including a horrified Beast.[587] Causing destruction over the globe, Cyclops was put down by the joint efforts of Hope and the Scarlet Witch, who ended up dispersing the Phoenix. As a result, mutants started to emerge once again, undoing the lasting effects of the decimation, while Cyclops was placed under arrest.[588] In a final conversation with Beast, the incarcerated Cyclops declared that, despite all tragedy, restoring mutantkind's potential was a necessary evil.[589]

In the mission against Dark Phoenix, Broo was mortally wounded by gunshot by the Hellfire Club at the Jean Grey School. Beast attempted to revive him using all his resources, but there was no records of Brood medicine for research.[590] Meanwhile, the Descendants struck at the Lighthouse, destroying it. As some of the Avengers were in Earth-666 to retrieve the Orb of Necromancy that originated the Descendants, Beast teleported to their location to help them.[591] By tricking the Avengers of the Undead, Beast and the Avengers made their way back to their reality, only to witness it being consumed by the Descendants' legions of machine life. Beast used a localized electromagnetic pulse to recover Pym from being controlled as a cyborg Deathlok.[592] In the end, the Descendants were deactivated by the destruction of the Orb of Necromancy.[593]

As part of his will, Xavier buried a telepathic prompt in Beast's mind to be activated upon his demise. Inside a hidden room in the mansion, there were records of Xavier's membership in the Illuminati and the Mind Stone to which Beast gained access. Collected by the Illuminati, Beast joined their cabal to assemble the Infinity Gauntlet in order to remedy the multiversal crisis of the incursions, in which worlds collided and universes ended. Wielding the Infinity Gauntlet, Captain America was able to prevent Earth from being destroyed during an Incursion. However, the item was destroyed in the process, prompting the Illuminati to erase his mind about the event so they could deal with it on their own terms in secrecy.[39] With the Illuminati, Beast studied the Incursions, including through interactions with the prisoner they held, the Black Swan. Eventually, they designed world-ending devices to protect their planet by destroying the alternate Earths if needs ever came to be.[594]

Mutant Revolution[]

The emergence of a next generation of mutants once again triggered a metamorphosis in Beast's organism. Believing he could not survive the process, he resolved to set out a chance for mutantkind to thrive. Upon learning an escaped Cyclops led a nascent mutant revolution that could harm his perspective, Beast was inspired by a comment made by Iceman about the original X-Men never agreeing with Cyclops' extremism. Using the Time Platform, he travelled to the past gathered the original X-Men, hoping to have them make Cyclops reconsider his actions.[37] After bringing them to the present, Beast collapsed due to his advancing mutation and fell into a coma, being treated by his own version of the past.[73] With her telepathy running unchecked, the teenage version of Jean Grey probed into Beast's mind to guide him in his rehabilitation. Deciding to give up on his own life, Beast shared his memories about the history of the X-Men with the Jean Grey from the past. In the end, Beast survived the process after being stabilized, changing into an ape-like form. However, Jean Grey refused to return to the past and the certainty of a doomed life. As such, X-Men present and past coexisted at the Jean Grey School.[75] Soon, they questioned Hank McCoy's actions and vision, as it was made evident Cyclops was not merely an unscrupulous terrorist. Having founded the New Charles Xavier School in a hidden location, Cyclops presented himself at the Jean Grey School, entering a debate with Beast about their diverging ideologies.[595] Moreover, the X-Men from the present also started to confront Beast about the consequences of disrupting the timeline and history.[596]

Meanwhile, Beast focused on restoring Broo's health at the Peak. His pupil ended up returning without his intelligence.[597] In desperation, Beast sought for Dr. Starblood, who claimed Broo was healthy and following his true nature. During the conversation, Beast and Brand were attacked by the Philistine, and agent of the Hellfire Academy run by the new Hellfire Club.[598] To track their location, Beast resorted to Krakoa, being able to find the staff of the Hellfire Academy with the X-Men.[599] The Hellfire Club was dismantled and, back home, Beast examined Broo's condition as he returned to his gentle self after interacting with a Bamf.[600] This was followed by Beast finding out the Bamfs that vandalized the school had created a portal that transported the X-Men to the Brimstone Dimension.[601] Subdued by Azazel and his demon pirates, Beast was corrupted by Azazel's scimitar and went feral.[602] The weapon was removed as he battled the undead Nightcrawler, whose soul resided in the hellish dimension.[603] With Azazel defeated by the X-Men, Nightcrawler accompanied them back to the land of the living.[604]

In between these tensions, Hank McCoy was reprimanded by Uatu, the Watcher, for his reckless transgressions against the timeline. Uatu tormented Beast using Xavier's image in his dreams.[605] He was then visited by Cyclops and his team of X-Men, who at the time were being targeted by new generation Sentinels in a crisis linked to S.H.I.E.L.D. and recognized McCoy's designs. At the Jean Grey School, Cyclops accused Beast of being behind the machines.[606] S.H.I.E.L.D. interrupted the confrontation as Cyclops and Magik demonstrated signs of having their powers acting erratically. Beast refused to obey S.H.I.E.L.D.'s commander Maria Hill and took Cyclops and Magik to his laboratory, finding Nano-Sentinels within their bloodstream. Meanwhile, the systems of the Helicarrier were hacked, turning against the Jean Grey School.[607] One of Cyclops' students, Hijack stopped the attack by commanding the Sentinels. Meanwhile, Beast traced the signature of the Nano-Sentinels back to their creator. Cyclops and Beast teamed up against the mysterious mastermind, a moribund Dark Beast from the Age of Apocalypse, who wished to punish them as his body decayed. In his last moments, Dark Beast accused Hank McCoy of murdering himself.[608]

As an Illuminatus, Beast continued his exchanges with the Black Swan.[609] At some point, Earth was invaded by Thanos and his Black Order, with Beast facing Corvus Glaive and Supergiant at the Jean Grey School.[610] It was revealed Thanos hunted his heir, an Inhuman. The king of the Inhumans, Black Bolt, destroyed the floating city of Attilan and scattered his people, detonating a bomb in the process that released the Terrigen Mists that catalyzed the transformation of Inhumans across the globe.[611] Thanos was stopped and kept captive by the Illuminati, while the Black Swan mocked them for their arrogance.[612] The Illuminati observed alternate Earths as the Multiverse died and time eroded. Beast recommended using the Bridge to investigate the role of the Black Swan, revealing the Black Swan was extremely dangerous in the process.[613] Eventually, the Illuminati found their planet colliding with Earth-4290001, a world protected by the honorable heroes of the Great Society.[614] Beast discussed the situation of potential genocide with the most recent Illuminatus, the incredible Hulk. Not seeing an alternative, the Illuminati fought the heroes after failing to reach common ground.[615] In the end, the Illuminati were responsible for eradicating a planet and billions of innocent lives. Understanding their corruption, they chose to disband.[616] Engulfed by guilt, Beast took matters to his teenage version, who condemned him for having deeply lost his ways.[617]

Storm confronted Beast about the repercussions of removing the original X-Men from the past. They were interrupted by Magik at Kitty Pryde's request to deal with an intergalactic crisis in Spartax concerning the celestial item called the Black Vortex.[618] While the X-Men debated about the fate with the Guardians of the Galaxy, Beast communed with the Black Vortex, ascending as a cosmic being and expanding his intellect.[619] Corrupted, he joined Gamora to harness the Black Vortex to evolve the universe.[620] Attacked by the Kree Empire, they obliterated Hala,[621] being opposed by Ronan the Accuser. With his expanded sense, Beast realized the harm he inflicted upon the fabric of time, shattering his mind.[622] Deeply disturbed, Beast regret his last actions.[623] He joined the X-Men and the Guardians in protecting Spartax from the infectious Brood,[624] which were destroyed by Pryde. In the end, by admitting his errors, Beast rejected the Black Vortex and returned to his usual self.[625]

While Beast served the X-Men to the best of his abilities, his obsession with proving Cyclops wrong and his unrepentant attitude towards his many mistakes, primarily bringing the original X-Men to the present under false pretenses, made the X-Men greatly concerned. Storm had the X-Men hold an intervention for him, trying to make him see the error of his ways. However, Beast responded negatively, accusing them of being ungrateful and of putting him on trial. Acting dismissively, Beast refused to accept responsibility for his actions and claimed his teammates could not understand the dimension of his motivations, ultimately leaving the X-Men. As Cyclops promoted a peaceful rally in Washington D.C., uniting mutantkind as part of his revolution, Beast was informed by the time traveller Tempus about the importance of him accepting his fate.[38]

The truth about the incursion events broke out to the public as Steve Rogers recovered his memories and directed the Avengers to hunt the Illuminati down.[626] Beast went missing,[627] secretly working with the Illuminati in Rome.[628] He also sought refuge in Nation X, a mutant sanctuary run by Cyclops, where he forged an alliance with Sunspot, who had acquired A.I.M. and founded his own New Avengers.[629] Sunspot had his team cease the fight between S.H.I.E.L.D.'s Avengers commanded by Rogers.[630] Meanwhile, Namor assumed the role of destroying Earths to preserve the universe with the Illuminati's prisoners, who formed the unscrupulous Cabal.[617] Beast proposed Rogers a truce to stop the Cabal, using A.I.M.'s advanced scientific resources for so. Inhumanity[]

Months later, the Terrigen Mists were revealed to be toxic to the mutant genome. From Muir Isle, Cyclops contacted Beast to inform him about the contamination. During their conversation, Cyclops succumbed to the infection, becoming one of the first victims of the M-Pox.[638] Frost telepathically concealed his death.[639] Beast turned investigating the effects of Terrigen his research priority, relocating to New Attilan, home of the Inhuman Royal Family, to gather data.[640] Tensions escalated between mutants and Inhumans, with Frost's telepathic intervention leading the world to believe Cyclops died in battle against the Inhuman monarchs, Black Bolt and Medusa.[639] Under Medusa's supervision, Beast resided in New Attilan, having Iso as his assistant. In the months that followed, Beast could not find a solution.[641] The queen of the Inhumans exerted pressure on his work and feared a potential betrayal.[642]

Having had his share of affliction due to tampering with the timeline, Beast immediately recognized the course of history was being rewritten as the royal Inhuman Gorgon ceased to be. He identified the culprit to be Kang the Conqueror, who had hold of the Inhuman heir Ahura. Beast suggested travelling in time to specific points to counter Kang's actions. In their mission, they found Ahura to have become a new Kang.[643] In the field, Beast coordinated Iso, helping her hone her air pressure-controlling abilities.[644] In the distant past, Beast and the Inhumans retrieved Ahura before he could be corrupted by Kang.[645] A precognitive Inhuman named Ulysses represented a problem in the superhuman, tearing it between those who believed in the preventive justice or in the freedom.[646] Beast sided with the Inhumans as Iron Man kidnapped Ulysses to assess his abilities.[647] Following one of Ulysses' visions, Hawkeye executed the Hulk, who was prophesized to kill several superheroes. Beast was convoked to speak during the trial of Hawkeye, who ended up being absolved.[648] Iron Man and Captain Marvel escalated their schism, with Beast ensuring Iron Man's research was correct about the uncertainty of Ulysses' powers.[649] In the end, Captain Marvel knocked out Iron Man, who ended up being brain dead. Beast assessed his condition, declaring he had altered his biology to the point of trapping him in stasis.[650]

Despite maintaining an unwavering optimism, Beast eventually discovered that the Terrigen saturation levels in the atmosphere would soon reach a point that would make the Earth permanently uninhabitable to mutants.[641] Fearing the Inhumans could go to war against the X-Men, Beast called together several key mutant leaders to inform them of his findings after paying respects to Cyclops' grave on Muir Island. He proposed that the best course of action was to evacuate mutantkind from Earth and to begin again somewhere else, as he believed that a war against the Inhumans would spell mutantkind's end. The other mutant leaders objected and revealed they had already begun preparing for such a war. As Beast intended to leave and inform the Inhumans, Storm incapacitated him. He was then imprisoned for the duration of the ensuing conflict.[651] On Muir Island, the incarcerated Beast discussed the imminent tragedy with his younger self as the X-Men rebuked his traitorous position.[652] Despite Beast's fears, the X-Men's gave them advantage against the Inhumans, giving them advantage for most of the conflict. After learning that Terrigen exterminate mutants, Queen Medusa herself neutralized the compound in the atmosphere. After the racial war, Storm released Beast. He forgave his friends, blaming himself for the conflict and choosing to remain apart from the X-Men.[653]

Disassembled[]

Beast visited New York City as it recovered from Hydra's control, meeting Wonder Man in the region they used to hang out in the past. He lamented being a failure in all teams of which he had been a part. Krakoan X-Force[]

From the shadows, Xavier set out to form a mutant-exclusive nation on the living environment of Krakoa.[40] Beast was one of his former mentor's emissaries in creating the technological systems necessary for Krakoa to function as a country, directly being in charge of data analysis and overwatch.[690] Among the wonders of Krakoan society there were mutant resurrection and habitats. Beast explicitly oriented the linguist Cypher to manufacture flower-based drugs to help mutants buy recognition and sovereignty from humanity, expressing his excitement with the possibilities of the new era mutants were to experience.[41] Accompanying Xavier and Frost during a meeting with the United Nations, he witnessed Krakoa recognized as an independent state.[691] This independence was not well received by external factions. Krakoa suffered a major attack by XENO, causing Professor Xavier to be assassinated. During the incident, Beast questioned how security operated in the new mutant society.[692] Jean Grey guided Beast in bringing Cerebro online to revive Xavier, urging him about the need of blindly believing in Krakoa.[693] The procedure was a success, and a resurrected Xavier saw imperative to found an intelligence service for his nation, X-Force.[42]

Based on the Pointe,[42] Beast was appointed as the head of X-Force, being in charge of coordinating their operations.[694] He worked close with Jean Grey during his missions, such as when they interrogated an anti-mutant agent linked to XENO.[695] Soon, McCoy demonstrated to be seduced by the amount of power given to him as the one controlling all intelligence regarding Krakoa, seeing himself as the conscience behind the nation. When X-Force identified anti-mutant plant-based technology in the country of Terra Verde, Beast chose to neutralize his telefloronic technology that were being used to perform a coup d'etat. For this purpose, he secretly kidnapped the president's son, Hadwin Cocom, and lobotomized him while eliminating the telefloronic infection that turned him into a botanical cyborg.[696] His intervention led the technology to run wild instead and forced him to assign X-Force back to Terra Verde, which had succumbed to a local deity. He purposefully kept the crisis a secret from Jean Grey.[697] Upon learning about his mistake, she confronted him about his reckless intervention in a sovereign human nation. The botanical infection was purged by Jean Grey and X-Force with the cost of several innocent lives. After the tragedy, Jean Grey chose to quit X-Force.[43]

The corpses of the XENO agents in Krakoa spawned off infiltrated soldiers, engineered as a form of "nesting dolls," who incapacitated Beast and the medic Cecilia Reyes before securing remnants of a Cerebro unit and delivering them to XENO's associate, Mikhail Rasputin, Colossus' brother.[698] Beast linked XENO's operations to Colossus and to the erratic Krakoan citizen Omega Red. Both were arrested and interrogated in a controversial display, which led Wolverine to attack Beast. Despite their disagreement, Beast and Wolverine enlisted Jean Grey to psychically probe into their hostages' minds.[699] Colossus was read as clean, while Omega Red was regarded by Beast as an asset who could be used as a double agent through the use of a Carbonadium Synthesizer built by Forge. Jean Grey once again expressed her disgust at Beast's corrupting and arrogant views.[700] Meanwhile, alongside Reyes, Beast identified a tumorous entity created within Krakoa's organism, which led him to dispatch X-Force to neutralized it, despite his intentions of weaponizing it.[701] Around this period, Beast designed the Shadow Room within the Pointe to holographically replicate crime scenes.[702] During this period, he established some secret agreements with his colleague, Sage. One of those included eliminating their own lives upon a sign of corruption. This was enacted when Quentin Quire used his telepathy on a brain-damaged Beast to investigate the degradation of his own constant resurrections.[21] However, at some point, Beast no longer trusted Krakoan resurrection and decided to carry out cloning research by himself in secrecy.[703] Growing suspicious of his intentions, Wolverine declared he would keep an eye on the head of X-Force.[22]

During the Hellfire Gala, a mutant worldwide event with diplomatic purposes, X-Force was assigned as the security crew. Beast developed telefloronics to control politicians from Terra Verde as puppets for his purposes. His plan was uncovered by Emma Frost, the host of event, who confronted Sage and Beast about the potentially disastrous outcomes of their actions. Meanwhile, the plan backfired as the technology was erratic and caused the diplomats to become monstrous creatures.[704] Beast directly attacked ambassador Gabriella Gomez, who accused him of manipulating her country. Sage demonstrated her frustration with X-Force's path and forced Beast to stand down as she deactivate his telefloronics and worked on a truce with Terra Verde.[705] Telefloronic-related infections linked to XENO kept causing incidents around the world, being countered by X-Force. Through the creature known as Man-Slaughter from Weapon Plus, Beast oriented X-Force to incapacitate the responsible, Doctor Bloodroot.[706]

Fear the Beast[]

Secretly keeping one of XENO's corpses in his laboratory, Beast ended up being infected by one of their miniature agents. In desperation, he had Black Tom create a similar minuscule replica to free him of the invasion.[707] Inside McCoy's body, the creature multiplied itself as Tom attempted to exterminate it. After having Black Tom eliminate all creatures but one, Beast found the remaining interloper inside his left eye. In a gruesome display, he excised his left eye to reclaim his body.[703] Following Mikhail Rasputin's attempt to tamper with the timeline through Omega Red and eliminate Professor X from history,[708] Beast spoke before the Quiet Council of Krakoa to brief them about X-Force's decision of banishing the responsible ones from resurrection. Sage once again indicated to be an opposition to Beast within X-Force, as she exposed Beast's manipulations in the form of turning Omega Red into an unwitting double agent. This caused Beast to worry about X-Force's reputation in the eyes of the Quiet Council. He visited Planet Arakko, where Abigail Brand comforted and reassured him about his methods and plans.[709]

He also squirreled away funds to open a space station prison with the help of Maverick and interrupted one of Wolverine's resurrections to attach an obedience collar to send him on clandestine operations, all of which was kept secret from the rest of X-Force.[citation needed] This behavior led Wolverine to question Hank's morals, which greatly annoyed him to the point of sending Wolverine on a mission only to be captured for an auction for which Hank attended and where he murdered Wolverine.[710] This was in order to resurrect Logan in a primitive state and turn him into Weapon X, where Beast sent him on missions assigned to him by himself.[711]

When Logan was freed with the help of Sage and the island of Krakoa and at the request of Maddie Bannister, the daughter of Jeffrey Bannister who said that Hank was holding captive.[712] After Maddie was allegedly attacked by a mosquito sent by Hank, Sage and Wolverine went looking for Jeffrey while Maddie was left in the care of Dr. Cecilia Reyes and Black Tom. When Logan caught up with him, Wolverine confronted Beast for his actions before killing him. However, Hank was resurrected in a secret laboratory, and as he came out of the tank where he was, he mentioned that it was time to move on to the second part of his plan.[23]

After resurrecting in a new body, Beast began the next phase of his plan, which was to take his entire laboratory and the X-Force headquarters out of Krakoa in the form of a colossus. Once far away he was sighted by a UN submarine that passed through that place, and without remorse, Beast proceeded to destroy it, killing the entire crew, to later create several clones of himself. At the same time he proceeded to communicate with the rest of the X-Force just to say goodbye and tell them that everything he did was for the good of the mutantkind, and then proceeded to the laboratory where some of his clones managed the creation of Wolverine clones, calling this project Weapons of X.[713] Beast and his clones would eventually be defeated, forcing Beast to go into hiding,[714] although he would reappear a few months later, attacking X-Force's new compound in the North Pole to steal a Krakoan battlesuit.[715]

In order to put a stop to Beast's actions, X-Force tried using one of his clones against him, but the clone escaped, hoping to fix things by himself. He met with Wonder Man and convinced him to go after the original Beast with him, hoping to remind him of his once noble goals. When the duo caught up with Beast underwater, off the coast of California, he removed Wonder Man's breathing apparatus to force him to go back to the surface and give him some time alone with his clone. He showed him his Black Hole Gun and explained his plan to send Arrako to a cosmic vault for safekeeping. However, the clone wasn't moved by his words and, when the weapon was deployed, they got into a fight. The original Beast tried to end his clone's life, but Wolverine's surprise appearance allowed the clone to free himself. Wolverine then attempted to kill Beast, but the clone was adamant no one should die and tackled Wolverine. Without losing more time, the original Beast escaped, but was shocked to find Wonder Man attempting to stop his weapon. As the gun was about to fire with Wonder Man in front of it, Beast made one final selfless act and sacrificed himself to save his friend's life.[716]

The Chairman[]

Somehow, Hank managed to upload his consciousness to a synthetic body and continued down his path to madness. Now calling himself the Chairman, he joined the gene terrorist organization 3K,[2] alongside Cassandra Nova, Astra, and Wyre.[717] After his mind was transported into the Age of Revelation and returned back, his experiences there made him believe it was best to embrace his emotional side, and thus cloned for himself a new organic body, resembling his white-furred counterpart.[20]

Personality

Henry McCoy wondered why he wasn't one of the "lucky ones." If he had to remain a mutant, why did he have a power that made him look and sometimes feel like a beast? He continued to struggle for the acceptance of others. As a man of arts and letters and the sciences, Beast was very much the Renaissance man, and while his peers in the intelligence community knew this, they still considered him very much a mutant. He even knew what it was like to be jilted by the proverbial Beauty, his recent fear of human emotion had caused him to turn away from longtime lover Trish Tilby, who had finally seemed to find the error in her ways as far as an attitude towards his secondary mutation.

Despite his ongoing battle with the demons of loneliness and rejection, he was an inveterate prankster and wit, and besides his brutish exterior, Beast was the most literate and eloquent of the X-Men, possessing a million-dollar vocabulary. Henry was every bit a human being plagued by duality: man/beast; genius/sports star; gentle giant/feral aggressor. Equal parts biochemist and activist, Beast focused on the X-Men's goals of building better relationships between man and mutant. In immersing himself in this campaign, Beast held hope that in the world he and the other X-Men dream of, he'll finally be known more for the genius within than for the creature outside.

Although a prankster at heart, Beast's bitterness towards humans and will to protect mutantkind no matter the cost would corrupt him. By the time the mutant nation of Krakoa was founded, Beast was violent, secretive, and extremely paranoid, committing horrific acts to protect Krakoan sovereignty and national security.

Powers

Beast is a mutant. His powers have varied at times due to ongoing and subsequent mutations.

Genetic Atavism: It is possible that Beast's mutation is a result of genetic atavism (or genetic traits that resurface in a species after many generations of dormancy). He also possesses neotenous (or traits in a descendant that resemble those of an ancestor organism) characteristics, which would explain why he has a big modern brain with an ape-like physique.

Due to Secondary Mutation,[718] Beast transformed from his original ape-like physique to a more cat-like physique.[33] A subsequent mutation affected him, and he required the help of his past self, who was able to stabilize him in a new appearance.[75]

  • Superhuman Strength: Originally in his teen years, Beast was able to lift about 1 ton. From breaking chains to headbutting through a solid metal wall, he always made full use of his physical prowess by combining muscular strength with agile kinetic buildup. After his mutation into a blue simian form, which eventually stabilized afterward, his strength was increased to the point where he can now lift about 10 tons, which is sufficient for him to smash through a brick wall with a single punch, toss a smaller car, and to tie an iron barbell into a knot. When enhanced after Infectia´s kiss, he was strong enough to smash a tank with his bare hands and punch the ground to create a massive shock wave. Even before these extreme levels though, he has lifted part of a full-grown oak tree made of solid gold, with effort. His strength also extends to the muscles in his legs, allowing him to leap a height of 32 feet high and 50 feet in a standing broad jump.[719]
  • Superhuman Speed: Beast can run and move at speeds that are slightly beyond the natural physical limits of the finest human athlete, despite his abnormal size. He can run at speeds up to 40 mph for short distances, particularly if he runs on all fours. It also allows Beast to attack opponents before they can react and pull their trigger fingers and keep up with opponents who can move at low-level superhuman speeds.[719]
  • Superhuman Stamina: Beast's highly advanced musculature produces fewer fatigue toxins during physical activity than that of an ordinary human. He can exert himself at peak capacity for several hours before the buildup of fatigue toxins in his blood begins to impair him.[719] He also commented that after his mutation into a cat-like form that he no longer needs a full night of sleep, although it is unknown if he still retains this side effect of his stamina after becoming a new ape-like form once again.
  • Superhuman Durability: Beast's bodily tissues are harder and more resistant to certain types of injuries than the bodies of normal humans. His physiology can withstand great impact forces, though he can be injured by many types of conventional weapons such as standard firearms and bladed weapons. However, he can withstand physical impacts, such as falling from several stories by landing on his feet without suffering any broken bones or sprains. Also, he is able to be physically struck by many superhumanly strong beings like Colossus and The Hulk that would either severely injure or kill a normal human with little to no discomfort.[719]
  • Superhuman Agility: Beast's agility, balance, and bodily coordination are enhanced to levels that are beyond the natural physical limits of the finest human athlete. While in his simian form, he has the agility of a great ape combined with the acrobatic prowess of an Olympic level gymnast. He can walk a tightrope or a slack rope as easily as most people can walk on a sidewalk. He can also walk on his hands for many hours or perform a complicated sequence of gymnastic stunts such as flips, rolls, and springs. He can easily match or surpass any Olympic record in gymnastics.[719]

His training with the X-Men, as well as his experience battling enemies, has granted him the reaction time to evade gunfire. He can react at low-level superhuman speeds, even catching smaller missile mid-air with his feet. His reflexes are a partial reason why the Beast is able to be so agile and fast, despite the abnormal bulk of his ape-like form.
  • Superhuman Dexterity: He can perform many tasks with his feet as easily as a human could do with his hands. His manual and pedal dexterity are so great that he can write using both hands at once or tie knots in rope with his toes.[719]
  • Regenerative Healing Factor: Beast's metabolism enables him to repair mild to moderate injuries much faster than a normal human. For instance, he can recover from minor ailments, such as a severe cold or fever within a few hours. More severe injuries, like broken bones, used to heal within a few days, whereas normal humans might take many weeks or even months. However, his healing abilities have increased to the point where he recovers from mild to moderate injuries within the span of a few hours. However, he cannot regenerate destroyed organs or severed limbs.[719]
    • Contaminant Resistance: Hank possesses a highly advanced immune system, which is a partial reason why he is able to recover from mild ailments, among other forms of sickness, within a few hours. Hank also possesses a high immunity to foreign chemicals, such as tranquilizers and certain types of poisons used by Kraven the Hunter in their encounter during his early years with the X-Men.
    • Temperature Resistance: Due to a combination of his own healing abilities and his fine coat of blue fur, Beast is resistant to freezing temperatures. This was evident even before his ape-like form, to the point of being frozen in solid ice by Iceman for a short time and survived with a moderate case of shivers once freed during his early years with the X-Men.[719]
    • Telepathic Resistance: Hank possesses moderate resistance to telepathic manipulation and was able to resist Cassandra Nova's high-level telepathic powers for a short time until she used his own fears of devolving into a mindless animal against him.
    • Slowed Aging: Beast also possesses a slight degree of slowed aging due to his own healing abilities. Despite being 40 years of age since the time of Cassandra Nova's attack, he still retains the physical appearance and vitality of a man in his prime.

He gained his initial combat training from Professor Xavier, and then continued his tutelage in combat gymnastics from Captain America. He typically uses a freestyle form of combat, similar to that of Spider-Man, that allows him to make full use of his great strength and agility. During times of anger, however, the Beast is known to resort to brawling and street fighting tactics.[citation needed]
  • Multilingual: He is fluent in several languages including English, German,[299] French,[730] Latin, Italian,[731][541] Spanish,[732] Japanese,[730] Arabic, and Russian[733] as well as Latverian. The Black Swan has also been teaching him a few extinct languages.[609][719]
  • Expert Tactician: He is a capable leader as he has led his own team of Defenders with fellow X-Men Iceman and Angel as teammates. Even when he is not the leader of a team, he has been a key member in some of the greatest team accomplishments, such as helping to rescue Nova on Mars with the Secret Avengers. He is also a great team player.[citation needed]
  • Indomitable Will: Hank's dedication to science and coexistence between humans and mutants is unwavering. He never gave up on finding a cure for the Legacy Virus in which he eventually did find a cure. He has great compassion for both humans and mutants alike, and his unflagging courage allows him to protect both of them at any cost, even with his own life.[citation needed]

Notes

  • Beast doesn't have blue skin but a fine coat of blue fur.[734]
  • Beast's original furry transformation was an idea that originated from Roy Thomas, with input from Stan Lee, in an effort to make the character more visibly striking. He also became more werewolf-like to capitalize on the success of Werewolf by Night.[735][736]
  • Hank's pheromone abilities should be considered apocryphal as they're only mentioned in supplementary material and have never been depicted in any media.

Trivia

  • It was revealed that Hank had a crush on Betsy Braddock when she was a supermodel. They flirted with one another.[732]
  • The Beast has a weakness for Twinkies. Evidence can be found in his New Year's Resolutions revealed that one of his resolutions was to "Eat fewer Twinkies"[424] (the other resolutions were to "read more 15th century texts" and "cure Legacy Virus", the latter the only one which it is evidenced that he did).

  • Henry McCoy claims that he does not care for the identity of the Beast, and that he feels relieved when removing his X-Uniform. He preferred his civilian identity as an honor student.[63]
  • Beast was once captured by S.H.I.E.L.D and imprisoned in "The Cage". His prisoner number was: 412076.[citation needed]
  • Hank identified himself as an agnostic.[602][75] Despite this, he has prayed to God on occasion[37] and affirmed his belief.[737] He ultimately expressed strong belief in God's existence during a conversation with Spider-Man, who, during the conversation had declared his atheistic beliefs.[738] Recently however, he has expressly stated his atheism.[713]
  • Hank played obstacle chess with Nightcrawler frequently.[603]
  • Beast has had at least 1143 websites devoted to him.[739]
  • Beast favors Chamomile tea.[670]
  • Beast has been a MacArthur Fellows Program recipient, a Nobel Prize winner, and a nine-time recipient of the Presidential Medal of Freedom.[740]
  • In Avengers (Vol. 3) #14, Beast and Wonder Man shared the first on-panel kiss between two male superheroes in mainstream Marvel comic book history, 10 years before the more romantic gay kiss shared by Rictor and Shatterstar in X-Factor (Vol. 3) #45.
