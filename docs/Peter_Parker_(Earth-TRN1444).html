History

Bitten by a radioactive spider, young Peter Parker gained the ability to cling to almost any surface, a "spider-sense" that warns him of danger, and the proportional speed, strength, and agility of an arachnid. Now, he strives to use his great power responsibly as the wall-crawling wonder known as Spider-Man.

Since the Timestream Entanglement, Spider-Man has been swinging between two battlefronts. When he's not rallying New York's heroes to fight against ancient forces of darkness, he's busy protecting the Web of Life and Destiny with his fellow Web-Warriors in Tokyo Webworld.

Timestream Entanglement[]

At the start of the Timestream Entanglement, just before Dracula's takeover of New York City, the Green Goblin and several of Spidey's rogues teamed up to attack Manhattan. Spider-Man—knowing these were his villains—figured he could handle them, but didn't know that Osborn had enhanced their powers with the Goblin Formula. Peter was easily overpowered and vulnerable, but before his villains could kill him, Captain America sacrificed himself to save Spider-Man's life. With his dying words, Cap told Spider-Man to continue fighting. Spider-Man mustered up his remaining strength to defeat his villains and his arch-nemesis, though his inability to save Captain America would continue to haunt him.[1]

The Abyss Awakens[]

When Spider-Man ventured to Klyntar with the other heroes to stop Knull, he was bonded to Kerfuffle.[5] Though was separated from the symbiote when Blade defeated the Queen in Black.[6]

The Hunt Is On[]

During Kingpin's crackdown on vigilantes, Spider-Man was one of the many heroes who took cover at Empire State University, returning to his civilian identity Peter Parker.[7]

Mary Jane Watson later texted Peter, noting that he had been quieter than usual. The pair had a lengthy conversation about the recent happenings in New York City. Including the death of their reality's Captain America, Tiger Division, Fisk's vigilante ban, a chance to save another reality's Captain America, and the Black Cat's plan to start a prison break. While he couldn't be talked down from going through with the jailbreak, Watson told him to be safe for her.[8]

Spider-Man later met up with Black Cat to deliver the Tablet of Life and Time to Fisk as a ruse to free their allies from the Vault.[9] He was later part of the rescue team, riding in a S.H.I.E.L.D. transport van.[10]
