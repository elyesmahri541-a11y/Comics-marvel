History

Congressman Barlow was an American politician who chaired an Anti-Nazi committee during World War II. In January 1943, he became the target of a Nazi spy posing as the medium Doctor Eternity, who lured him to a séance by claiming that the spirit of his deceased brother wished to contact him. During the fraudulent séance, Barlow saw an apparent apparition of his brother warning him that he would soon join the world of the dead, whereupon he collapsed and died. His death was part of the spy's scheme to prevent Barlow's committee from uncovering the location of his operations.[1]
