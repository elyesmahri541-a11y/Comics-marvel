History

The Avengers from this timeline initially had a history that was identical to that of their Earth-86501 counterparts. At a time when Iron Man, the Wasp, Captain America and Hercules were active members, the team decided to induct two new members. The first to join was Storm, the mutant who could control the weather. After that, Colossus became the newest member, but he could only join after Iron Man had helped him smuggle his family out of Siberia so that the Kremlin could no longer control him.

To celebrate their newest member, a reception was scheduled to be held at Avengers Mansion so that President Ronald Reagan could publicly welcome Colossus to the team. Learning of this, Kang the Conqueror decided that the reception would provide a good opportunity for him to infiltrate the grounds and bring with him a nuclear bomb that he planned to use to kill the Avengers.

On the day of the reception, Captain America, Hercules and the Wasp were outside Avengers Mansion with some reporters, all waiting for Colossus to finish conferring with President Reagan. By this time, Kang had somehow acquired a suit of Iron Man armor and was already wearing it over his battlesuit so that he could impersonate Iron Man. When Storm arrived, the Wasp immediately welcomed her home and congratulated her for doing a great job of blunting a Gulf Coast hurricane. When Storm thought that the presence of the reporters indicated that it was a more lavish reception that she had expected, the Wasp told her that the reporters were waiting for their newest member.

At that moment, Colossus exited the mansion with President Reagan who immediately began taking questions from the press. However, before he could answer more than a single question, "Iron Man" flew in for a landing, carrying a metal box in which he had placed the nuclear bomb. After the president introduced him to the ladies and gentlemen (of the press) as the man who was chiefly responsible for bringing Colossus to their shores, "Iron Man" approached President Reagan and the two men shook hands. At that point, "Iron Man" offered the metal box to the president, claiming that his employer, Tony Stark, had asked him to deliver it. Believing that the box contained something that he had been expecting but not so soon, the president immediately took it and began trying to open it, but initially couldn't get it open, prompting "Iron Man" to encourage him to just tug a little harder. As the president continued his efforts, Tony Stark arrived, calling out for the president to stop, but was grabbed and wrestled to the ground by Secret Service agents who didn't recognize him. Before Stark could explain, the president finally managed to yank the box open, setting off the nuclear bomb. Protected by his force field, "Iron Man" was unaffected by the awful heat and radiation but the Avengers and everyone else at ground zero were vaporized, and little was left standing on the island of Manhattan.[1]

Despite having killed his enemies, Kang's victory was short-lived because he had no sooner used his force field to tear off the Iron Man armor when he was abruptly transported away to Limbo, a location that was unknown to him. Once there, Kang was confronted by a shadowy tribunal who called him a fool and showed him an image of the smoldering ruin that was the world from which he had been taken. They explained that there was nothing left on that Earth to conquer because the American armed forces, believing that the explosion of his bomb had been a Soviet attack, had retaliated, triggering a global thermonuclear war between the United States and Russia that had killed everyone except him. After showing Kang that his counterpart in a divergent reality had been even less successful, having died during his failed attempt to kill the Avengers, the trio revealed themselves to be other Kangs just before they executed Kang-267 for his incompetence.[1]

Notes

  • Since Earth-267 and Earth-86501 are divergent counterparts of each other, the teams of Avengers who existed in each reality must have had histories and memberships that were identical up until the moment of divergence. As such, despite not appearing in Avengers #267, counterparts of this timeline's Iron Man and Storm exist in the Earth-86501 timeline.
  • On the last page of Avengers Forever #11, a Storm and a Colossus appear among the horde of good Avengers who had been summoned through time to help fight the corrupt Avengers serving the Time-Keepers. The Notes in Avengers Forever #12 identify them as "Alternate versions of Storm and Colossus from AVENGERS (vol. 1) #267" and this wiki has assumed that that meant the Storm and Colossus from Earth-267 who had been plucked out of time at some point before their deaths and then returned, possibly with their memories erased, to the point from which they had been taken. However, there's nothing that says that they couldn't have been the Storm and Colossus from Earth-86501 instead.
