Paladin is an enhanced human mercenary, private investigator, and former soldier whose true identity and the source of his superhuman abilities remain unknown.[3] His enhanced abilities have made him a valuable operative for private clients, governments and intelligence agencies.[4] Paladin first became involved with the superhuman community while hunting Purple Man, bringing him into repeated conflict with Daredevil before the two eventually joined forces against Killgrave and his agents.[5] He later established himself as a professional gun-for-hire, bodyguard, and investigator, and became romantically involved with Wasp while using the alias Paul Denning.[6]

Paladin has worked both alongside and against superheroes throughout his career. He became a frequent associate of Silver Sable, serving with the Wild Pack, Outlaws, and Intruders,[7][8] and later fought alongside Captain America, with the two developing a degree of mutual respect.[9] During the Civil War, Paladin joined the Heroes for Hire, while secretly accepting a separate contract from S.H.I.E.L.D. to capture Captain America.[10] He later joined Norman Osborn's Thunderbolts, but became increasingly opposed to the team's methods and eventually turned against Osborn during the Siege of Asgard.[11]

After Osborn's fall, Paladin continued working as a mercenary.[12] He later joined Misty Knight's reorganized Heroes for Hire, and over time the two became increasingly close.[13] Paladin eventually began taking assignments without demanding payment, despite his long-standing insistence that he worked for money.[14] Their relationship later became romantic after Paladin infiltrated Purple Man's Villains for Hire under the identity of Scourge.[1]

History

Early Life[]

Little is known about Paladin's life before becoming a professional mercenary. His real name and the source of his superhuman physical abilities remain unrevealed.[3] Paladin formerly served in the United States Army. During the early stages of his mercenary career, one of his mentors was an old Army associate who later operated as a mercenary called the General. The General was eventually killed by Elektra, causing Paladin to hold a grudge against her for years.[15] Later Paladin participated in the liberation of a prominent Russian nuclear physicist. As a consequence of the operation, he was warned never to return to Russia.[16] Paladin subsequently developed a reputation as an expensive private investigator and gun-for-hire whose services were generally available only to wealthy clients.[17]

Meeting Daredevil[]

Paladin was hired by Mort Chisholm to locate the Purple Man, who had previously used Chisholm while under the influence of his powers. Paladin initially demanded $500,000 for the assignment, although he accepted $450,000 when Chisholm arrived short of the agreed amount.[17] Knowing that Daredevil had recently encountered Killgrave, Paladin followed him. The two fought across the rooftops until Paladin revealed that he was also searching for Purple Man.[17] Paladin continued pursuing Killgrave's associates and later encountered Daredevil while tracking one of Purple Man's henchmen in Central Park. Frustrated by Daredevil's interference, Paladin stunned him and continued the pursuit.[18] Paladin was subsequently hired as a bodyguard by Marsha Connors, whose former boyfriend Phantasm was threatening her. After Marsha appeared to have been killed, Paladin furiously pursued Phantasm before discovering that she had survived.[19] Paladin eventually tracked Purple Man to his headquarters, where Killgrave was holding Heather Glenn hostage and controlling Gladiator, Cobra, Mister Hyde, and Jester. Purple Man attempted to control Paladin, but Paladin fired first and sealed his armor against Killgrave's pheromones before joining Daredevil in defeating the controlled villains.[20] When Purple Man apparently disappeared following the battle, Paladin complained that the loss of his quarry had cost him his fee.[20]

Defenders for a Day[]

After seeing a television advertisement announcing that the Defenders were seeking new members, Paladin travelled to the Richmond Riding Academy. He joined the numerous heroes assembled there as the informal "Defenders for a Day ", but soon decided to leave.[21]

Thermo[]

Christine Michaels hired Paladin to locate her husband Walter Michaels, who had been transformed into the energy-draining criminal Thermo following a laboratory accident. Spider-Man initially mistook Paladin for a criminal while both were searching for Thermo, but the misunderstanding was resolved and the two began working together. Christine supplied Paladin with a nullifier designed to counter Thermo's powers.[22] With the assistance of Dazzler, Paladin and Spider-Man eventually located Thermo and his captives. Paladin repeatedly used the nullifier during the confrontation, helping defeat Thermo before collecting his payment.[23] Paladin was later transported with numerous other superhumans during the Contest of Champions, although he was not selected as an active contestant.[24]

Wasp[]

Paladin was hired to protect valuables at a casino on Utopia Cay. While there, he encountered Wasp and introduced himself under the alias Paul Denning. The two became romantically involved during their stay.[6] When Baron Brimstone and his men attacked the casino, Paladin revealed his costumed identity and fought beside Wasp. Afterward, he admitted that Paul was not his real name.[6] Around this period Paladin adopted a modified version of his costume and continued his relationship with Janet, who was then separated from Hank Pym. Businessman Vince Granetti later hired Paladin to force Janet to surrender her business interests. Although Paladin publicly appeared to carry out the intimidation, he covertly warned Janet about Granetti's intentions. He ultimately sided with Wasp and Spider-Man against his employer.[25] After completing another lucrative assignment, Paladin accompanied Janet to question Yellowjacket. When members of the Masters of Evil intervened, Paladin assisted Wasp and the Avengers against Grey Gargoyle and Screaming Mimi.[26] Paladin remained openly flirtatious toward Janet during their association, while Black Knight, who was himself attracted to Wasp, regarded their relationship with jealousy. Paladin and Janet eventually went their separate ways.[27]

International Investigations[]

Newspaper publisher Emmette Edison hired Paladin to locate his supposedly kidnapped daughter Dixie Edison. The apparent kidnapping eventually led him into a much larger conspiracy involving toxic waste, an international crime syndicate, the KGB, and extraterrestrial manipulators. Paladin travelled through the United States, Italy, Russia, and Antarctica during the investigation. He sought assistance from a Russian physicist whom he had previously liberated and ultimately bluffed the extraterrestrial conspirators into retreat by claiming several superhero teams were approaching.[16] Later Paladin was hired to investigate a bomb threat against a baseball stadium. While examining the threat, he survived an armed attack and uncovered the stadium's own security officer, Burke, as the man behind the conspiracy. Paladin defeated the attackers, prevented the destruction of the stadium, and exposed Burke's involvement.[28]

Silver Sable[]

Silver Sable hired Paladin to investigate the foreign operative Chakane and a conspiracy involving the Life Foundation that th ened Symkaria. Spider-Man initially interfered with the investigation before learning that Paladin was working for Sable.[29] Paladin and Spider-Man subsequently infiltrated Life Foundation operations together, gathering information before joining Silver Sable against Carlton Drake.[30] Silver Sable later accepted a contract from the newly democratic government of Bosqueverde to bring former mercenary Marc Spector to justice for actions from his earlier career. Paladin accompanied Sable and the Wild Pack on the assignment. Although Paladin questioned why so many operatives were necessary to capture a single man, Spector initially evaded the group before voluntarily surrendering.[31]

The Punisher[]

Crime boss Vincent Mangano offered Paladin ten million dollars to kill Punisher. Despite initially claiming that he did not perform assassinations, Paladin accepted the contract. His attempts to kill Castle ultimately failed. U.S.Agent intervened during the conflict and broke both of Paladin's legs with his shield. Paladin nevertheless managed to escape, concerned both about losing the payment and the damage the failure would cause to his reputation.[32]

Diamondback and Superia[]

Diamondback hired Paladin to help her raid the Serpent Society's headquarters and rescue her allies. Paladin was captured during the mission but was eventually freed by Captain America.[33] When Diamondback, Asp, and Black Mamba later disappeared, Captain America approached Paladin for assistance. Paladin cancelled another assignment and accompanied him in the search.[34] Their investigation led them into conflict with Superia and her army of female superhumans. Paladin proved resistant to M.O.D.A.M.'s mental control and later saved Captain America's life before infiltrating Superia's vessel alongside him.[35] After being captured, Paladin and Captain America were subjected to Superia's attempt to transform them into women. They escaped with help from Asp and Black Mamba and eventually located Diamondback.[36][37] The experience produced genuine respect between Paladin and Captain America despite their very different approaches to heroism.[38]

Outlaws and Intruders[]

Silver Sable later assembled Paladin, Sandman, Prowler, and Rocket Racer into a group called the Outlaws. They were hired to recover Dr. Walshe and stolen Symkarian technology from Darkmoor Energy Research Centre. The mission initially brought them into conflict with Excalibur before both teams joined forces against the larger threat.[39] Paladin later became one of the superhuman operatives associated with Silver Sable's Intruders. He investigated Dmitri Petrovich and exposed the activities of the Genesis Coalition, later helping Silver Sable attack their operations.[40][41] As an Intruder, Paladin also participated in operations involving Viper, Hydra, and the Bio-Genes, including humanitarian missions in Somalia.[42] When accountant Jonathan Lee embezzled money from Silver Sable International, Paladin tracked him through an international laundering network and intimidated him into admitting that the Foreigner was behind the scheme.[43] He remained associated with Silver Sable through later missions in Bosnia and Herzegovina and Antarctica.[44]

Hellfire Club[]

Donald Pierce hired Paladin to defend a Hellfire Club estate in Boston while the Club was pursuing its secretive Tomorrow Agenda. Cable infiltrated the estate while searching for information concerning the project. Paladin attempted to apprehend Cable but was defeated.[45]

Wild Pack and the Heroes for Hire[]

Silver Sable later hired Paladin, Sandman, Cat, and a disguised Madcap to protect scientist Wolfgang Hessler, who possessed knowledge concerning a dangerous biological weapon. The assignment brought Sable's Wild Pack operatives into conflict with the Heroes for Hire. During the battle, Deadpool repeatedly shot Paladin.[46] Paladin subsequently fought Misty Knight, becoming impressed by both her strength and marksmanship. He defeated her and helped Silver Sable's operatives capture the Heroes for Hire. The opposing groups eventually joined forces after Luke Cage came under the control of the Master of the World.[47]

Generation X[]

Paladin periodically worked for Adrienne Frost, who hired him to retrieve a samurai sword held by the Rising Sons in Madripoor. After studying the group, Paladin attempted to retrieve the weapon but was badly wounded and left for dead.[48] He managed to return to the Massachusetts Academy, where Jubilee found him collapsing outside.[49] After recovering, Paladin returned to Madripoor accompanied by members of Generation X. He and Jubilee pursued the Rising Sons aboard a speeding train and helped recover the sword.[50] Jubilee developed an attraction to Paladin during their adventure, although her interest faded after Generation X and Paladin went their separate ways.[51] Paladin turned down an invitation to join the Great Lakes Avengers.[52]

S.H.I.E.L.D.[]

Nick Fury of S.H.I.E.L.D. later hired Paladin and Diamondback to recover a biological weapon from Genetassist. They infiltrated the company but encountered the Hellions.[53] During the battle, Paladin resisted Tag's psychic powers and turned Rockslide's fist against him. He tried to explain that the Hellions were stealing a biological weapon, but Hellion knocked him unconscious and escaped with it.[54] Paladin and Diamondback later reported back to Fury, with Paladin characteristically remaining concerned about receiving his payment.[55]

The Murdock Papers[]

The FBI recruited Paladin during its campaign against Daredevil. In exchange for having his criminal record expunged, Paladin accepted an assignment to take Murdock down. From a helicopter and at considerable distance, Paladin shot Daredevil with a sniper rifle. The wound was serious but not fatal and allowed federal authorities to arrest him.[56]

Government Sting Operation[]

Paladin later accepted employment from a corrupt government while posing as an ally of rebel forces as part of a sting operation. During the mission, however, Paladin became sympathetic to the rebels and helped them plant explosives inside a government installation. After the rebels were captured, Paladin collected his payment from the government but declined to prevent the explosives from detonating, maintaining that doing so had not formed part of his agreement.[57]

Civil War[]

During the Civil War, Misty Knight and Colleen Wing recruited Paladin into a newly organized Heroes for Hire. Paladin's initial interest in the organization was openly financial.[58] Unknown to the others, S.H.I.E.L.D. separately hired Paladin to locate Captain America. When Misty arranged a meeting with Captain America following Goliath's death, Paladin betrayed the team in an attempt to capture Rogers for S.H.I.E.L.D. He released paralytic gas, used an electromagnetic device against Captain America's shield, and attacked Luke Cage before activating a tracking beacon.[59] Shang-Chi avoided Paladin's trap and knocked him unconscious. Captain America and Shang-Chi then switched Paladin's costume with Rogers', causing arriving S.H.I.E.L.D. personnel to unknowingly take Paladin into custody instead.[60]

Despite this humiliation, Paladin continued taking S.H.I.E.L.D. work. He was later offered ten million dollars to organize a mission into the Savage Land to retrieve Moon-Boy. Paladin returned to the Heroes for Hire and offered each member one million dollars to participate.[61] The group successfully acquired Moon-Boy and returned to New York City during World War Hulk. Their previous experiences left Paladin distrusted by most of the group.[62] Paladin later participated in a covert S.H.I.E.L.D. training operation involving Scorpion, during which he deliberately allowed her to believe she had killed him.[63] He eventually rejoined the Heroes for Hire long enough to help rescue Colleen Wing and Tarantula, but ultimately claimed Moon-Boy for S.H.I.E.L.D. despite the objections of the team.[64]

Last Defenders[]

After completing an assignment for a foreign ambassador, Paladin was recruited by Nighthawk alongside Atlas and Junta to join the 50-State Initiative version of the Defenders based in New Jersey.[65] They battled U-Man, the Brand Corporation, and the Quintronic Men before Nighthawk revealed that his team had never received government authorization. The group consequently disbanded.[66]

Nasim Rahimov[]

Paladin remained a S.H.I.E.L.D.-contracted mercenary after leaving the Heroes for Hire, but accepted a more lucrative offer from Nasim Rahimov. Rahimov sought revenge against the government of Kirikhstan after his wife was murdered and he himself was tortured and imprisoned. Because Rahimov had previously worked with Tony Stark, he possessed detailed knowledge of the microprocessors used in Iron Man's armor and modified Paladin's stun weapon accordingly.[67] Paladin successfully used the weapon to disable Iron Man, but Stark used Extremis to restart his armor and defeat him.[68] During questioning afterward, Paladin surrendered everything he knew about Rahimov in exchange for six weeks of S.H.I.E.L.D. "career counselling" and the possibility of additional employment.[69]

Elektra and Norman Osborn[]

Paladin later learned that a two-million-dollar contract had been placed on Elektra while she was imprisoned aboard a H.A.M.M.E.R. Helicarrier. In addition to the money, Paladin remained motivated by his old grudge over Elektra's killing of his former mentor, the General. Disguised as a doctor, Paladin infiltrated the Helicarrier and reached the restrained Elektra. He revealed his personal grievance and prepared to kill her, but Elektra escaped and stabbed him through the side.[15] Norman Osborn subsequently took custody of Paladin. In exchange for his life, Paladin surrendered access to his financial assets and intelligence resources. Osborn thereafter incorporated him into his covert Thunderbolts program.[70]

Dark Reign[]

Paladin joined Norman Osborn's covert incarnation of the Thunderbolts alongside Ghost, Ant-Man, Headsman, Mister X, Scourge, Black Widow, who was posing as Yelena Belova. During one of the team's early operations, Paladin served as pilot of the Thunderstrike.[71] Paladin subsequently helped the team pursue Deadpool and became disturbed when he learned that Osborn had weaponized medical technology for use against him.[72][73] While discussing the team with Ant-Man, Paladin admitted that he could accept killing as part of a job but was disturbed by Mister X's enjoyment of murdering civilians. The two developed an unlikely friendship based partly on their shared distrust of the more unstable members of Osborn's organization.[74] When Osborn captured Black Widow and former Thunderbolt Songbird, the team was ordered to execute them. Paladin initially attempted to regard the situation as simply another assignment but ultimately refused to allow their murder. He shot through Black Widow's restraints and joined other members of the team in resisting Scourge and Mister X. Mister X drove a sword through Paladin's shoulder before Ghost intervened. Paladin remained with the Thunderbolts afterward and secretly began undermining Osborn's operations while maintaining the appearance of obedience.[75]

When Osborn subsequently had Iron Fist forcibly recruited and brainwashed, Paladin participated in the capture of Luke Cage, attacking him with a neural disruptor. Paladin later released Cage from his restraints under the pretense of seeking revenge against him. Cage badly beat Paladin before escaping with Iron Fist, allowing Paladin and the other conspirators to sabotage Osborn's operation without openly exposing their disloyalty.[76] Paladin also participated when Osborn dispatched the Thunderbolts to apprehend Spider-Woman. Paladin warned Drew to surrender, but she resisted the team and escaped after destroying their aircraft.[77]

Agents of Atlas[]

Before attacking the Agents of Atlas, Paladin studied the organization and concluded that the Thunderbolts were inadequately prepared to fight them. During the battle he used a heat weapon against Namora before being captured by Gorilla-Man, but refused to reveal his employer.[78] Venus later forced the Thunderbolts to experience their deepest fears. Paladin envisioned himself permanently controlled by Norman Osborn and denied the opportunity to become a hero, revealing his growing conflict over the course his mercenary career had taken.[79]

Siege[]

Osborn deployed the Thunderbolts during the Siege of Asgard, ordering them to retrieve Gungnir, the spear of Odin. Paladin and Ant-Man increasingly acted as reluctant participants in the operation. After fighting Asgardians and encountering the Mighty Avengers, possession of Gungnir shifted repeatedly between members of the two groups.[80] Scourge eventually seized the weapon. Paladin openly turned against him, shooting Scourge and taking Gungnir for himself. Declaring that he was finished following madmen such as Scourge and Osborn, Paladin fled with the spear. Osborn's Thunderbolts effectively collapsed with the end of the Siege. Paladin and Ant-Man were among those who escaped the immediate aftermath, with Paladin still in possession of Gungnir.[81]

Shadowland[]

During the events surrounding Shadowland, Paladin returned to New York as a freelance mercenary. Mikey Fortunado hired him to kill his brother Bobby. When Mikey himself was murdered before Paladin could complete the contract, Paladin suspected Bobby and continued pursuing the matter in part because the unfinished contract threatened his payment.[12] His investigation brought him into conflict with Shroud, who was independently investigating the murders. After fighting, the two concluded that the killings did not fit Daredevil's usual methods. Paladin subsequently encountered Misty Knight and Silver Sable, both of whom were following related murders. Realizing that all four were investigating the same conspiracy, Paladin, Shroud, Misty, and Silver agreed to work together.[82] Following one theory, Paladin investigated whether Mister Negtative was responsible, but the lead proved false. The investigators eventually discovered that corrupt New York police officers had been disguising themselves as Hand assassins and murdering criminals who had avoided conviction. Their impersonation enraged the genuine Hand, producing a confrontation between the police, the Hand, and the investigators. Paladin used his sniper rifle and holographic technology during the battle, and the corrupt officers were ultimately exposed and arrested.[83]

Misty Knight's Heroes for Hire[]

Misty Knight subsequently established a new Heroes for Hire organization in which heroes were anonymously directed toward specific assignments. Paladin became one of the organization's most important operatives, performing intelligence work, surveillance, investigations, and field clean-up.[84] While investigating military-grade weapons connected to several murders, Paladin discovered that firearms had fused themselves to their victims. He called in Satana, who identified the weapons as demonic Soulcutters.[85]

Paladin gradually realized that Misty's behavior was abnormal and discovered that the control room from which she supposedly directed the operation was empty. His investigation ultimately helped expose Puppet Master's manipulation of Misty and the other heroes.[86] Following Puppet Master's defeat, Paladin encouraged Misty to continue running Heroes for Hire legitimately. The two rebuilt the organization, with Paladin undertaking much of its field work.[87] During another operation Paladin was attacked by ninjas and assisted by Spider-Man. Batroc later severely injured him, but Paladin continued pursuing the case despite Misty's attempts to make him seek medical treatment.[88] Satana subsequently suggested to Misty that Paladin's reasons for remaining involved had become more personal than financial.[89]

Paladin's increasingly close partnership with Misty Knight began to extend beyond their professional relationship. Iron Fist questioned whether Paladin had developed feelings for her, while Satana later independently reached the same conclusion.[90] His changing priorities became particularly apparent when he admitted to Silver Sable that he was performing Heroes for Hire assignments without payment, something highly unusual for Paladin. Sable interpreted this as evidence of his feelings for Misty.[14]

Spider-Island[]

During the Spider-Island crisis, the Heroes for Hire were employed to help contain the outbreak in Manhattan. Paladin fought alongside the team's other operatives against spider-powered criminals and infected civilians.[91][2] Paladin himself became infected and developed enhanced spider-like strength and agility. His condition rapidly worsened as additional spider limbs emerged from his body, eventually transforming him into a monstrous spider-mutate. Paladin isolated himself as the transformation progressed, but Misty Knight found him and remained with him while she too experienced the effects of the infection. Both were eventually cured.[2]

Fear Itself[]

During Fear Itself, Paladin and Gargoyle worked to rescue civilians following the devastation of Yancy Street. They encountered Brady Briedel, whose abilities caused him to assume the appearance of a person's greatest fear. Against Paladin, he became an idealized version of Paladin himself stronger, faster, and seemingly superior in every respect and quickly defeated him.[92] Misty later found Paladin and Gargoyle severely injured.[93] After recovering, Paladin joined Silver Sable against another Hook facility and admitted that he had been doing the work without payment.[14]

Villains for Hire[]

Misty and Paladin subsequently began an elaborate undercover campaign involving Purple Man and his new criminal organization. After weeks of planning with Misty, Paladin took the identity of Scourge, wearing a mask that protected him against Purple Man's pheromonal control.[94] Misty assembled an apparently competing team of criminals while Paladin infiltrated Purple Man's operation. Only Misty knew that Scourge was actually Paladin. To protect the deception, the pair staged an encounter in which Misty appeared to kill Paladin while Purple Man observed remotely. Paladin then resumed the Scourge identity, convincing Purple Man that Paladin was dead.[95] Paladin maintained his cover throughout the ensuing confrontation between the rival criminal groups before revealing himself to Purple Man and Headhunter. The operation ended with the criminal organization dismantled and its members arrested. Afterward, Paladin and Misty acknowledged their growing attraction and kissed.[1]

Later Mercenary Work[]

While receiving treatment at Night Nurse's Clinic, Paladin encountered Spider-Man shortly after the death of Marla Jameson. Paladin taunted the grief-stricken hero over the deaths caused by Massacre, provoking an enraged response from Spider-Man.[96] Paladin later accepted another ten-million-dollar contract to kill Deadpool. He attacked Wade while emphasizing that the assignment was impersonal and broke S.H.I.E.L.D. agent Scott Adsit's wrist when Adsit attempted to intervene. Deadpool and Adsit escaped.[97] Paladin later attended Deadpool's bachelor party.[98]

Doctor Doom[]

After Latverian missiles destroyed the Antlion Control Center on the Moon, the Symkarian government hired Paladin and Orb to hunt Doctor Doom. They intercepted Doom and Kang the Conqueror in Texas. Paladin opened fire, but Kang easily defeated both mercenaries using a weapon that caused them to question their life purpose.[99]

Freelancers for Funds[]

Paladin later joined a mercenary team associated with Deadpool known as the Freelancers for Funds, alongside Terror, Annabelle Riggs, Ren Kimura, She-Hulk (Lyra), and Hit-Monkey.[100]

Venom War[]

During the Venom War, Alchemax hired Paladin to apprehend Eddie Brock and his temporal duplicates after their appearance disrupted a wrestling event. Paladin participated in the ensuing confrontation but was severely wounded when Bedlam slammed him into the wrestling ring.[101]

Attributes

Power Grid[106]

Powers

  • Superhuman Strength: Able to press one ton.
  • Superhuman Speed: Paladin is capable of running and moving at speeds greater than even the finest human athlete.
  • Superhuman Stamina: Paladin's musculature produces less fatigue toxins during physical activity than the musculature of an ordinary human. He can exert himself at peak capacity physically for several hours before fatigue begins to impair him.
  • Enhanced Durability: Paladin's bodily tissues are somewhat harder and more resistant to injury than those of an ordinary human. While not invulnerable, his body can withstand blunt and impact forces that would severely injure or kill and ordinary human with mild to moderate discomfort.
  • Enhanced Agility: Paladin's agility, balance, and bodily coordination are enhanced to levels that are beyond the natural physical limits of even the finest human athlete.
  • Superhuman Reflexes: Paladin's reaction time is enhanced to a level that is beyond the natural physical limits of even the finest human athlete.[4]

Abilities

Skilled Martial Artist Paladin is a skilled martial artist and hand-to-hand combatant, blending boxing with Judo and Taekwondo.[4]

Skilled Espionage Agent Paladin has proficiency in espionage methods and marksmanship. He was also a former bodyguard.

Multilingual: Paladin speaks several languages, including Somali.[102]

Paraphernalia

Equipment

Paladin wears a body-suit of synthetic stretch fabric, and his boots, gloves, helmet, torso, and knee and elbow guards are made of bulletproof composite materials. Paladin's helmet is equipped with a belt activated faceplate which slides down to make the helmet airtight, and has its own one hour air supply. The lenses in Paladin's helmet can be adjusted for infrared vision.[4]

Weapons

Paladin typically uses his Stun-Gun, which generates enough force to disrupt the nervous system sufficiently to render most people unconscious. However, he has been known to use more conventional firearms.[103]

After Siege, Paladin escaped the fall of Asgard with Gungnir, the spear of Odin.[104]
