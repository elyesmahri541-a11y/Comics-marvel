Lycana was the favorite daughter of the Eldest of the Mother of Horrors, conceived by desecrating the dead body of the Asgardian god Agmundr while in the form of a raven.[4][13] Lycana was also the sister of Vârcolac,[7][6] father of the Wolf Lords[5] and progenitor of the Wolf-Men.[14][15]

Known as the Valravn to the Norse, she was slain by Thor.[4][13] Her body fell near a cave[4] near modern-day Las Vegas Strip[1] whose body was found by cavemen. They ate her flesh and one of them wore her pelt, and respectivelly became Skinwalkers and the vessel of Lycana.[4]

For ages, Lycana's vessels, who donned her pelt, the Godskin, for a Moon cycle, hunting and feeding, before returning to the altar to be sacrificed to the pack,[4] the House of Lycana,[1] to strengthen the pack.[4]

During the Modern Age, Lycana chose Charlie Tidwell, the sidekick of the Hulk, as her vessel.[3]

History

Origins[]

Lycana was the favorite daughter of the Eldest of the Mother of Horrors, conceived by Eldest by desecrating the dead body of the Asgardian god Agmundr while in the form of a raven.[4][13]

Lycana was the sister of Vârcolac,[7][6] father of the Wolf Lords[5] and progenitor of the Wolf-Men[14][15] (see notes).
It is unclear if Lycana's relationship to Vârcolac is literal or metaphorical.

Upon her death, as spawn of man and wolf, Lycana was possibly taken to her brother's Den of Thorn and Flame hell to be tormented for eternity.[7]

Legacy[]

The Skinwalkers[]

Lycana's body was found by a tribe of cavemen that had been driven off by a stronger and more war-like people. Starving, the tribe ate her flesh, wore her pelt, and burned her entrails for warmth. This caused them to be granted parts of Lycana's powers, with the one wearing her godskin becoming her host to allow Lycana to operate on Earth once again. As the mortal wearing the pelt prayed to Lycana, thanking her and promising to honor her sacrifice with victory over their enemies, the next morning, they awoke to find the bones of their enemies in their camp. They were transformed into human flesh-eating beasts referred to as the Skinwalkers, and began worshipping Lycana as a goddess.[4]

The cave of the tribe became the Temple of Lycana,[4] among caves sacred to the Natives,[1] and the pelt became known as the Godskin, their object of worship.[4]

They became known as the House of Lycana.[1] In time of need, the tribe chose a champion to wear the godskin, allowing Lycana to return and devour their enemies. The champion then returned to the altar to sacrifice their flesh to the tribe, strenghtening them.[4] Along the years, the ritual shifted towards tormenting unwilling victims,[1] or fulfilling the members' hunger for worship.[5]

Modern Age[]

During the Modern Age, the Temple of Lycana was set among sacred caves beneath modern-day Las Vegas, beneath the Luxor.[1] The deepest, forgotten part of the cave held a well of blood served to sacrifice to Vârcolac.[5]

After Charlie Tidwell's body was destroyed by Nephele,[16] her soul was trapped in a purgatory of her own nightmare, tormented by skinwalker Teelget.[17] Hulk went to the Temple of Lycana to save her[1] intending to use the Godskin on Sumanguru the Flesh-Weaver's advice.[18] Katie, a recent and reluctant skinwalkers who loathed her new companions and refused her fatal fate as she was next to don the Godskin, freed Charlie. As they were discovered by Skinwalkers, Katie turned on them to allow Charlie to flee. Charlie hid under the altar and the godskin of Lycana, and prayed to God[19][20] but Lycana answered, allowing her to slaughter some the pursuing skinwalkers. Brought before Eldest, Charlie stood her ground to her, turned into Lycana, devoured Teelget after learning he had killed her father. Charlie and Eldest fought, allowing Hulk to break free, and Charlie and Hulk escaped.[3]

After the Godskin was stolen, Nico learned from Old Gabe of a well of blood in the deepest part of the cave. Wishing to retrieve the power stolen from them, Nico led a group to the well, where he sacrificed David to Vârcolac to summon him and ask him to return to them the godskin and bring vengeance upon Charlie and Hulk[5]

Now worshiping Vârcolac (despite Vârcolac lacking any care for the Skinwalkers or the Godskin), Nico tracked Hulk, who easily killed him, thus summoning Vârcolac himself.[5] Hulk was dragged in Vârcolac's Den of Thorn and Flame dimension, but was saved by Charlie and returned to the Earth plane.[7]

Attributes

Powers

  • Godly Physiology: As the daughter of the Eldest and the Asgardian god Agmundr,[4] Lycana possesses vast powers.
    • Winged Flight: Lycana possessed wings that enabled her to fly.[3]
    • Superhuman Strength: Lycana was incredibly powerful, being able to battle Thor for a long time.[4]
    • Superhuman Durability: Lycana was extremely resistant to conventional forms of physical damage, but not invulnerable as Thor in the end managed to kill her with Mjolnir.[4]
    • Shapeshifting: Lycana could take the form of those she had consumed.[4]
    • Possession: Lycana can possess the body of those who wear her skin.[4]

Notes

  • The werewolf Jack Russell mentioned that his telepathy with Charlie Tidwell (who wore the Godskin) was due to them being "linked by the blood of Lycana."[7]
  • Lycana (daughter of Eldest and Agmundr)[4] and Vârcolac (Elder God transformed by Chthon,[15] considered an Elderspawn of Chthon)[14] are mentioned as siblings.[7] Whether this relation is literal or metaphorical remains unrevealed.
    • An origin that would reconcile all accounts regarding the relation between Lycana and Vârcolac would be the following:

"Lycana had a half-brother, Vârcolac,[7][6] born an Elder God,[15] spawned by the Elder God Chthon[14][21] and by Eldest."

As Lycana is already born of Agmundr and Eldest, it simpler to assume that if the relation between her and Vârcolac is literal, then it's as half-siblings (with Eldest as Vârcolac's mother), rather than to introduce Chthon as a third parent to Lycana.
  • It unclear whether the valravn was a race of Asgard (to which Lycana belonged, or was confused with), if the valravn is the Norse equivalent for skinwalkers, or if Lycana was the sole valravn.
