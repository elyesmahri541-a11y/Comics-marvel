History

The Chitauri Chariots are chariots used by the Chitauri during the Battle of New York. During it, Loki rode one of them and Black Widow got on one of them to take out the Chitauri piloting and riding it.[1]

Bestman Salvage were originally contracted to clean up after the Battle of New York, with Herman Schultz sawing one and Adrian Toomes using a Chitauri Staff to pry out the Chitauri Energy Core from them at the Grand Central Station. Their contract was ended and instead assigned to the newly formed United States Department of Damage Control. However, they had received a final shipment of a chariot at their new base of operation, with Tinkerer using it to create weapons.[2]

Alternate Reality Versions[]

New York Time Heist (Earth-11131)[]

The chariots were used by the Chitauri during the Battle of New York.[3]

Cosmic Time Heist (Earth-TRN734)[]

The chariots were used by the Chitauri of the past at the future during the Battle of Earth, housed within the Sanctuary II. They were all Snaped out of existence by Iron Man.[3]

Captain Carter's Reality (Earth-82111)[]

The chariots were used by the Chitauri during the Battle of New York, having the same history as the main timeline, with Loki riding of them and Black Widow getting on one of them.[4]

LEGO Marvel Universe (Earth-13122)[]

Thanos and the Chitauri allied themselves with Red Skull and Hydra. The Chitauri had their chariots parked at the Hydra Research Base. When the Avengers infiltrated the base, the Chitauri rode their chariots and used them to fight against them. In the end the villains were defeated by having the entire island be shrunk and placed in a bottle, including chariots present at base.[5]

LEGO Marvel Brickverse (Earth-0937)[]

Numerous Chitauri riding their chariots participated in a battle between heroes and villains orchestrated by Josh.[6]
