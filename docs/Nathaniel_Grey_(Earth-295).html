Nate Grey, a.k.a. X-Man, is a powerful mutant genetically engineered by Mister Sinister using the DNA of Cyclops and Jean Grey to be used as a weapon against Apocalypse in the Age of Apocalypse timeline.[11] With a shard of the M'kraan Crystal, Nate was transported to Earth-616.[12]

Although he and Cable technically have different mothers, they are treated as alternate reality counterparts of each other.[23] Cable's mother is Madelyne Pryor, a clone of Jean Grey. When Nate entered Earth-616 and reached out for Jean, he inadvertently brought Madelyne back to life as a psionic construct,[24] though the Red Queen replaced her.[25] Later, he accidentally freed Onslaught from Professor X's mind,[26] and he crossed paths with Mister Sinister[27] and Apocalypse of Earth-616, particularly the latter's plan involving the Twelve.[28][29] During this time, he became intimate with the mutant known as Threnody.[30]

Nate's vast psychic powers were slowly killing him,[11][31] but he got them under control as the barefooted, X-tattooed "shaman."[32] After merging with everyone on Earth in an act of self-sacrifice,[33] he returned during Norman Osborn's reign with H.A.M.M.E.R.,[34] but he lost the majority of his powers due to the Omega Machine.[35] He joined the New Mutants for a while[36] and briefly dated Dani Moonstar.[37] With his powers restored and increased with a Life Seed,[38] he presented as the "Second Coming" and formed the Horsemen of Salvation to make the world "perfect."[39] When the X-Men resisted,[40] he and the Life Seed created a new plane of existence for them to live in, but in time, he returned them home.[41]

History

Age of Apocalypse[]

In the reality ruled by Apocalypse, Mister Sinister secretly combined genetic material from Cyclops and Jean Grey to create a mutant powerful enough to destroy his master. The child, whom Sinister named Nathaniel Grey, was grown rapidly in a laboratory and engineered without the limitations that restrained his Earth-616 counterpart Nathan Summers. During one of Cyclops' raids on Sinister's pens, Scott unknowingly released his own genetic son, allowing Nate to escape before Sinister could complete his conditioning.[11]

Nate was found by Forge and his Outcasts, a group of mutants who traveled as a theater troupe while protecting humans from Apocalypse's forces. Forge became Nate's surrogate father and tried to teach him restraint, warning that the uncontrolled use of his immense telepathic and telekinetic powers would expose him to Apocalypse and eventually consume his body. Nate nevertheless revealed himself while helping the Outcasts liberate a trainload of prisoners, during which he rescued Sonique and persuaded Forge to accept her into the group.[42]

Disguised as a wandering performer named Essex, Sinister joined the Outcasts and encouraged Nate to ignore Forge's cautions. His manipulation led the group to one of Apocalypse's factories, which Nate destroyed with a massive display of power.[43] The display alerted Apocalypse, who sent Domino, Caliban, and Grizzly to capture or kill him. Domino's team slaughtered most of the Outcasts, but Nate overwhelmed Domino and erased her mind; while Nate was occupied, Sinister murdered Forge.[44] Sinister then disclosed his identity, Nate's parentage, and the purpose for which he had created him. Enraged, Nate attacked his creator and left him for dead before setting out to confront Apocalypse.[45]

At Apocalypse's citadel, Nate briefly encountered Scott and Jean and immediately felt a psychic bond with Jean, although there was no time for them to form a relationship. During the X-Men's final attempt to repair their damaged reality with the M'kraan Crystal, Nate rescued Magneto and fought Apocalypse's son Holocaust. Nate impaled Holocaust with a fragment of the crystal, but the resulting discharge transported both combatants out of their universe.[12]

Arrival on Earth-616[]

Nate fell into the Swiss Alps of Earth-616 like a meteor. His arrival released a psionic shock wave detected by telepaths throughout the world, including Professor Xavier, Jean Grey, Psylocke, and Blaquesmith.[46]

Confused and unconsciously searching for the mother he had barely known, Nate used his power to give physical form to Madelyne Pryor, Jean's clone. Madelyne helped him begin adjusting to the new world, but Selene recognized Madelyne's unusual nature and separated her from Nate for her own purposes.[47]

Soon after the original Age of Apocalypse ended, a group of survivors led by Forge crossed into Earth-616 while attempting to preserve what remained of their reality. Nate confronted them and warned that they did not belong in the restored timeline.[48] He initially opposed their willingness to endanger Earth-616, but his reunion with Forge persuaded him to hear them out and join the mission.[49] Their pursuit carried Nate and the displaced X-Men through earlier periods of X-Men history, including encounters with the original teenage team and with the X-Men in the Savage Land.[50] The crisis was revealed to have been manipulated by Sinister and Holocaust, whose forces included an army of Holocaust clones. Nate fought beside the survivors of his world and the X-Men of Earth-616 to stop their assault on humanity.[51]

A New World[]

While working aboard a freighter, Nate encountered another refugee from his reality, Dark Beast. Dark Beast attempted to exploit Nate's isolation and recruit him, but Nate rejected him after recognizing his connection to Sinister and the experiments of the Age of Apocalypse.[52] Professor Xavier subsequently tried to examine Nate's mind. Interpreting the intrusion as an attack, Nate instinctively pulled Xavier's astral form into the physical world and fled believing that he had killed him; the forced separation later helped Xavier's suppressed psychic darkness manifest independently as Onslaught.[53]

Rogue found the frightened Nate and gained enough of his trust to direct him toward Moira MacTaggert on Muir Island.[54] Moira's examinations confirmed that his powers were still increasing and were consuming him from within, making it unlikely that he would survive beyond early adulthood. Nate reacted to the prognosis and the secrecy surrounding it with anger, reinforcing his suspicion that every authority figure intended either to control or study him.[31][55]

Nate next rescued Threnody, a mutant able to sense and absorb the psychic energy released by death, from Sinister and the Marauders.[56] The conflict brought him face-to-face with Cable, his closest genetic counterpart in this reality. Their nearly identical psionic signatures caused painful feedback whenever they approached one another, while Cable recognized that Nate possessed the unrestricted power he himself might have developed without his techno-organic infection. Despite fighting a common enemy, Nate distrusted Cable and refused to be placed under his supervision.[57][58]

Onslaught[]

Onslaught became interested in Nate as a source of virtually limitless psychic power and sent Holocaust to force him into an alliance. Nate refused and defeated Holocaust, but the encounter drew him closer to the conflict surrounding Xavier's emerging alter ego.[59] In the aftermath, Nate again encountered Sinister, who attempted to present himself as the only person capable of understanding Nate's origin and deteriorating condition. Nate rejected his creator's claim over him, but remained burdened by the knowledge that both Sinister and Onslaught had viewed him primarily as a weapon.[5]

When Onslaught openly attacked the X-Men, he captured Nate and the young Franklin Richards, intending to combine Nate's psionic might with Franklin's ability to reshape reality.[16][60] Nate struggled against Onslaught from within while X-Force and the X-Men attempted to reach him.[61][62] The heroes eventually freed Nate and Franklin before Onslaught was defeated by the assembled Avengers, Fantastic Four, X-Men, and their allies.[63]

Threnody and a New Life[]

Nate settled in New York City with Threnody, who became both his guide to Earth-616 and his lover. Her dependence on death energy steadily worsened, however, and she began secretly feeding on people near death. When Nate discovered what she was doing, he left after telling her that he could help only if she chose to resist the addiction.[64] Madelyne later confronted Threnody and apparently killed her when she refused to reveal Nate's location. Nate, unaware of Madelyne's involvement, returned out of remorse but could not find Threnody.[65]

Nate developed a close friendship with Spider-Man, who treated him as a troubled young man rather than a weapon. Together they contended with Morbius and searched for Madelyne, whose behavior had become increasingly unstable.[66][65] With Jean Grey's help, Nate concluded at the time that Madelyne had been sustained as a psionic construct through his own power and emotional need for a mother. Confronted with that apparent truth and her murder of Threnody, Madelyne disappeared from his life.[24] Spider-Man later called upon Nate to help contain a greatly empowered Electro, further establishing Peter as one of the few heroes Nate consistently trusted.[67]

While pursuing Sugar Man, Nate followed him through a temporal portal into the early days of the Age of Apocalypse. There he met younger versions of Forge, Magneto, Morph, and Mastermind. Nate discovered that Forge had always known he would one day train the boy because an older Nate had told him so; to preserve that history, Forge compelled Nate to return himself and Sugar Man to Earth-616, completing the causal loop.[68]

After temporarily suppressing his telekinesis, Nate accepted an invitation from Havok to join his Brotherhood. Havok promised to help restore Nate's abilities if Nate aided the group in freeing Aurora from Department H, but the Brotherhood instead exposed him to Coldsnap-9 as part of its own agenda.[69] Nate recovered his telekinesis and rejected the group after learning that Dark Beast was among its members and that Havok had deceived him.[70]

During Operation: Zero Tolerance, Cable telepathically warned Nate that Sara Grey's children, Gailyn and Joey, were being hunted by Prime Sentinels. Nate rescued the children and Roust, then placed Gailyn and Joey in the care of their grandparents.[7]

Nate was subsequently drawn to a boy who appeared to be hunted by the X-Men and initially believed him to be another persecuted mutant. The boy was actually Hybrid, a human–Dire Wraith hybrid who used Nate's psychic energy to rebuild himself and intended to conquer Wraithworld. Nate reached the remnant of Jimmy Marks' human personality within the creature and used it to destroy Hybrid again.[71]

Public Hero and Psi-Ops[]

As Nate began using his abilities more openly, he accidentally awakened latent psychic powers in Jackknife, a former follower of the Abomination. Unable to endure the voices in his mind, Jackknife blamed Nate and murdered people who had encountered him. Nate defeated him in public, and the witnesses protected Nate from the police by identifying him as a hero.[72] Around the same period, Nate befriended the terminally ill Jerome and traveled to Transylvania in the hope that the immortal Countess Absynthia von Mort could save him. The encounter forced Nate, whose own powers were slowly killing him, to confront the difference between extending a life and making it worth living.[73] Soon afterward, physician Marcus Arlington acquired healing powers through contact with Nate and regenerated Jam's severed arm. The miracle transformed Nate into a public sensation surrounded by followers who treated him as a messianic figure.[74]

Purple Man exploited the movement around Nate, using manipulated supporters and mercenaries to turn public opinion against him and frame him as a terrorist.[75] Overwhelmed by the hostility, Nate considered erasing New York's memory of him, but Spider-Man persuaded him not to violate millions of minds simply to escape his problems. Nate instead confronted the forces manipulating the crisis and abandoned his attempt to control how the public perceived him.[76]

The Shi'ar also targeted Nate and the other surviving refugees from the Age of Apocalypse because they regarded their continued existence as a threat to reality. Nate resisted their effort to remove him and once again defeated Holocaust when his old enemy attempted to steal his identity and energy.[77] During another independent adventure, Nate joined the Hulk in preventing Thanos from re-entering the mortal plane through a psychic gateway.[78]

Nate's growing fame made him a target of Psi-Ops and brought him into contact with the enigmatic Ness. Madelyne reappeared and helped him replenish his strength when the Great Beasts Tundra, Tolomaq, and Somon threatened the world, enabling Nate to defeat beings whose power was tied to the planet itself.[79] He subsequently withdrew to Ireland in search of stability, but Psi-Ops continued to pursue him and Holocaust impersonated him while committing murders, destroying what little trust Nate had gained from the local population.[80]

An encounter later revealed to have occurred during this period brought Nate to Cable for help when the techno-organic predator Metus attacked him. Cable transported Nate to a refuge at the end of time inhabited by alternate versions of himself, and together they prevented Metus from using Nate as another means of tormenting Cable across history.[81][22]

Ness foresaw a future in which Nate and Stryfe destroyed the world in battle. Stryfe captured Nate in Latveria and siphoned his psionic energy, intending to make that future inevitable.[82][83] Cable and Madelyne helped Nate recover his power, and Nate used Doctor Doom's power siphon to overload and defeat Stryfe. The ordeal allowed Nate and Cable to move beyond their initial hostility and recognize one another as family, while Nate began developing finer applications of his telekinesis, including phasing through matter.[84]

The Second Coming[]

In London, Nate prevented Colleen O'Shaughnessy from committing suicide. The rescue revived press claims that he was a divine "Second Coming" and drew the hostility of the religious vigilante Crusader, whom Nate defeated.[14] After Emma Frost contacted him, Nate worked with Generation X against Dark Beast and Gene Nation, rejecting another attempt by his former world's scientists to define the future of mutantkind.[85][86]

Psi-Ops' Strike Squad Gauntlet attacked Nate's home in Dublin, forcing him, Madelyne, Ness, and Fontaine into a final confrontation with the organization. Nate and Fontaine destroyed Psi-Ops' command vessel, but the battle cost Nate his home and again turned the surrounding community against him.[87] Madelyne left afterward, while the resurrected Threnody was revealed to be carrying a child.[17]

Family and Apocalypse's Host[]

Nate sought out Scott Summers and Jean Grey, who were recuperating in Alaska, and began forming a genuine bond with the people he regarded as his parents. When a displaced processing complex from the Age of Apocalypse threatened the area, the three fought together to contain it.[88] Nate remained with Scott and Jean when the M'Kraan Crystal's guardians Modt and Jahf came for him, and Scott gave Nate one of his old X-Factor uniforms as a sign that he belonged to their family.[89]

After the X-Men disbanded, Scott and Jean called upon Nate, Cable, Archangel, and Wolverine to protect the newly emerged Mannites. The group discovered that the Horseman Death was Wolverine himself, conditioned by Apocalypse, and helped prevent him from taking the children.[90][91]

Mysterio later imprisoned Nate in "Greyville," an illusion in which Nate possessed the family and ordinary life he had always wanted. Nate recognized the artificial world, escaped it, and turned Mysterio's own method against him by trapping the illusionist in a fantasy of successfully ruling Earth through Nate's power.[92]

Threnody then returned, explaining that her connection to death energy had revived her after Madelyne killed her but now caused corpses to rise around her. She sought Nate while pregnant and gave birth as he recovered from exhaustion. Nate destroyed the pursuing undead and apologized for having abandoned her, but Threnody refused his offer to remain with her because she feared that her addiction would endanger him.[93]

While searching for other survivors of his native reality, Nate was captured by Apocalypse's agents.[13][94] Apocalypse intended to channel the collective power of the Twelve through the Living Monolith and transfer his consciousness into Nate's body, whose vast potential would grant him a perfect new host.[8][29] Cyclops intervened at the moment of transfer and merged with Apocalypse in Nate's place, saving Nate but disappearing with the villain.[95] Still connected to the Twelve, Apocalypse used Scott's body to force the heroes through a succession of distorted realities known as the Ages of Apocalypse before they broke his control and restored their world.[96] Nate was later among those who learned that Apocalypse's consciousness had survived within Scott as Jean and Cable began searching for him.[97]

The Shi'ar later designated Nate "19X" and confined him on a labor world, where he discovered the exploitation of the native Krillians. Nate exposed the colony's abuses to Lilandra, forcing the Shi'ar government to intervene rather than allowing the prisoners to be quietly discarded.[98]

Shaman of the Mutant Tribe[]

Returning to Earth, Nate abandoned the search for a conventional life and began acting as a "shaman" for mutantkind, investigating attacks on scientists, artists, and innovators whose work could shape the future.[99] A woman resembling Madelyne had been entering Nate's dreams and using his power while he slept to cause destruction.[100] She revealed herself as the Red Queen, an alternate Jean Grey who had secretly replaced the psionic construct Nate knew as Madelyne several months earlier. She taught Nate to travel between realities and took him to Earth-998, intending to turn him into the weapon that would secure her rule. Nate refused and escaped from her forces.[101]

Nate encountered another Nate Grey, a less powerful counterpart who had previously suffered under the Red Queen. The other Nate explained that she had searched multiple realities for a version of Nate capable of powering her Engines of the Gods.[25] Before sending Nate home, the alternate shaman stabilized his otherwise self-destructive power and marked him with an X-shaped genetic insignia.[102] With full control of his abilities for the first time, Nate returned to Earth-998, destroyed the Engines, and ended the Red Queen's threat.[4]

Nate then traveled the world as an openly interventionist protector of mutants and humanity. His work drew him into the conflict with Qabiri, who was purging alternate Earths along the multiversal Spiral; Nate united with displaced counterparts of familiar heroes and prevented Qabiri from continuing the destruction.[103]

On a Japanese island, Nate freed a young mutant named Kiza from the financier Lord Yaru, who had imprisoned her and exploited her ability to turn anything she touched into gold. When Kiza transformed herself into a gold statue, Nate entrusted her to local Shinto monks for protection.[104]

When the alien Harvester arrived to collect Earth as a crop seeded by its species at the beginning of life, Nate recognized that simply destroying the creature would not stop its people from returning. He converted his body into psychic energy, merged with the Harvester, and distributed both of their essences through every living thing on Earth, making the planet unusable to the harvesters at the apparent cost of his own individual existence.[33]

Return and the Dark X-Men[]

Months later,[105] Nate began reconstituting a physical body in a small town, where the psychic energy surrounding his return caused residents to dream-walk while repeating that they were X-Men. Norman Osborn sent his Dark X-Men to investigate; Mimic and Weapon Omega were overwhelmed by the energy, while Nate initially mistook a Jean Grey disguise worn by Mystique for his mother.[106] After draining the psychic knowledge of H.A.M.M.E.R.'s PSI Division, Nate learned what had happened to mutantkind and identified Osborn as the power controlling both the Avengers and X-Men.[107]

Nate formed a new body and attacked Avengers Tower, overcoming Venom and exploiting Sentry's fragile mental state before Mimic pulled him into another plane to face Ares. Nate appeared to be destroyed in the battle, but had instead transferred his consciousness into Osborn's mind to dismantle his regime from within.[20] Mystique detected the possession and led the Dark X-Men into Osborn's psyche, where Nate's attack also weakened the barriers restraining the Green Goblin personality.[2] Osborn forced Mimic to duplicate Weapon Omega's absorption ability, turning the two mutants into a siphon powerful enough to drain Nate. Dark Beast then confined the powerless Nate in the Omega Machine as an energy source.[108]

Following Osborn's fall, Sugar Man found Nate still attached to the abandoned machine and modified it to use Nate's power to open a portal to the Age of Apocalypse.[109] Cyclops reorganized the New Mutants under Dani Moonstar and sent them to rescue him. Nate exhausted almost all of his remaining energy opening the portal, but the team defeated Sugar Man before he could escape and brought Nate to Utopia. Nate survived with only a greatly diminished degree of telekinesis.[110]

New Mutants[]

Nate joined the New Mutants and struggled to redefine himself without the near-absolute power on which he had always relied. Hope Summers trained with him in the Danger Room and made him confront the need for ordinary combat and tactical skills rather than measuring his worth solely by his abilities.[36]

During the Serpent's attack on Earth, Nate accompanied the New Mutants when they tried to follow Dani into Hel. A mistranslated spell instead brought the group before Mephisto, who transported them to Hel only after Magma agreed to owe him a date.[111] Nate fought beside the team and Hela's dead against the Serpent's forces until Dani was recovered and Hela's realm was secured.[112]

Following the X-Men's schism, Nate remained aligned with Cyclops, both because he wanted to know Scott better and because he agreed that young mutants could not be completely sheltered from danger. He nevertheless moved with Dani's team to 1128 Mission Street in San Francisco, where they attempted to maintain independent lives while continuing to assist mutants. Their first missions included helping Blink end a series of disasters linked to a rock band.[113]

On Paradise Island, the team uncovered the Ani-Mator's attempt to resurrect himself as a techno-organic virus. Nate developed a focused X-shaped telekinetic technique despite being able to move only what he could physically lift. While infected and fused together by the virus, Nate and Dani acknowledged the attraction that had been developing between them.[114] After the mission, Blink took the team to Madripoor to recover, where Dani helped Nate through his discomfort with ordinary social life and the two began a romantic relationship.[37]

When a spell cast by the ancient hero Sigurd transformed several Asgardians into mortals and erased their memories, Nate and the New Mutants joined Kid Loki in restoring them and confronting the Dísir.[115][116][117] The team was then displaced into a reality ruled by future versions of Cypher and Warlock. Nate's experience with alternate universes helped the New Mutants recognize the false history and return home.[118]

During the conflict between the Avengers and X-Men over the Phoenix Force, Nate initially helped defend Hope and later supported Cyclops' Phoenix-backed rule.[119][120] As the Phoenix Five became increasingly authoritarian, the New Mutants concentrated on protecting civilians rather than enforcing their rule, and the team disbanded after Cyclops' defeat.[121][122] Nate remained close to Dani, and the pair later openly confirmed their relationship when they encountered her former boyfriend Cannonball.[123]

X-Men Disassembled[]

Nate eventually came into possession of a Life Seed, which restored his diminished abilities and elevated them far beyond their former level, but left his body rapidly deteriorating beneath the strain. Believing that he had little time remaining and that Earth-616 was moving toward the same ruin as his native reality, Nate adopted a messianic role and resolved to impose peace before his death.[124][38] He abducted Kitty Pryde, anti-mutant senator Ashton Allen, and Apocalypse, forcing them to serve as a council representing competing visions of the world's future.[125][39] Nate also transformed Magneto, Angel, Blob, and Omega Red into his Horsemen of Salvation, overriding their wills and enhancing their powers.[39]

With the Horsemen acting in his name, Nate ended armed conflicts, created a lake in the Kalahari Desert, and restored dinosaurs to the American Midwest, treating each intervention as proof that only his unrestricted power could save the planet. At Apocalypse's suggestion, he also eradicated religious sites around the world, regarding organized faith as another source of division and an obstacle to humanity accepting him as its savior.[126] The X-Men opposed both his methods and his disregard for free will, and Psylocke succeeded in breaking Nate's control over Angel.[127] Legion then imprisoned Nate and several young X-Men within a psychic recreation of the Age of Apocalypse, but Nate recognized that the world existed inside Legion's mind and seized control of his body instead.[128] Combining his own power with Legion's, Nate overwhelmed the X-Men and replaced Angel with a mind-controlled Storm as his Horseman.[129]

Facing an opponent whose power now eclipsed their combined strength, Jean Grey sent a telepathic summons to every available current and former X-Man.[130] Psylocke freed Storm, whose attack gave Jean, Psylocke, Sage, the Stepford Cuckoos, and No-Girl the opening needed to separate Nate from Legion.[38] Jean nevertheless remained trapped within Nate's mind, where she tried to reach him as his genetic mother and forced him to experience the suffering produced by the world he claimed to be saving. Although her appeal exposed the fear and desperation beneath his certainty, Nate still insisted that lasting peace was impossible while the X-Men continued to oppose him.[38] He consequently used the Life Seed to remove Jean and most of the assembled mutants from Earth-616, making them appear to have been killed while transferring them into a plane shaped by his own consciousness.[38]

Age of X-Man[]

Using the Life Seed, Nate constructed the Age of X-Man, a plane of existence that was both his creation and an extension of his mind. He rewrote the displaced mutants' memories and fabricated a history in which mutants had always formed a peaceful global society, inserting himself into that history as one of the original X-Men.[131][132] Because he believed personal attachment inevitably produced jealousy, possession, and violence, Nate abolished romantic love, families, and natural reproduction; children were instead produced in hatcheries and taught that every individual existed without biological relatives.[131][133] Nate publicly served beside Jean Grey, Magneto, Storm, Colossus, Nightcrawler, Nature Girl, and Bishop as the celebrated Marvelous X-Men, using the team to embody and defend his supposed utopia.[131]

Nate's restrictions were enforced through compulsory reconditioning, the erasure of offenders from public memory, and confinement in the psychic penitentiary known as the Danger Room. When Jean and Bishop gave in to their suppressed attraction, Bishop was removed from the Marvelous X-Men and imprisoned, while Jean's memory of their relationship was erased and X-23 was installed as his replacement.[131][133][134] Department X similarly persecuted citizens who formed intimate bonds, but encounters with a naturally conceived pregnancy and other forbidden relationships demonstrated that Nate had not eliminated love, only driven it underground.[135] Maintaining the false history increasingly exhausted him, allowing Magneto and Storm to notice missing memories and inconsistencies that Nate could no longer immediately erase.[136]

Resistance spread as Apocalypse and his followers openly preached love and family, moving beyond the controlled opposition Nate had intended them to provide and directly challenging the foundation of his society.[137] The Marvelous X-Men recovered memories of Earth-616 and confronted Nate, who admitted that he had created their reality and that the plane existed through him.[138] The Dani Moonstar within the plane was revealed to be a manifestation of Nate's subconscious, created from his memories of the woman he had loved and embodying the doubts he had tried to suppress. She urged him to accept that a world without choice could not be a true utopia.[41] Nate finally opened portals with the Life Seed and allowed every displaced mutant to return to Earth-616.[41] The original Magneto returned with them, but a copy of him remained behind to help Nate rebuild the plane without secret police, compulsory reconditioning, or the prohibition of personal bonds.[41]

Powers

Nate was created to be the most powerful mutant on Earth and the most powerful psionic imaginable.[139] He was classified as an Omega Level mutant[107] a psi of the first order,[53][140] an Alpha Psi,[141] and an Omega Level Threat.[107] He was described as the most powerful[142][143][31][56] and the most dangerous[142][56] being on the planet, the ultimate mutant,[144][145] the most powerful psionic in any reality,[24][63] as near omnipotent,[63][106][107][108] and as a living god.[107]

As he grew, he possessed enough power that he could destroy entire worlds if he wished.[4] Even a small trace of his powers led to the almost instantaneous overload of Omega and Mimic.[107] His power is such that upon examination by Moira MacTaggert, she suggested his psionic powers rivaled that of a Phoenix Force-imbued Jean Grey[31][55] even though at that time he just started to learn how to use his powers. It was also stated that he can fight on par with the Sentry.[107] Nate even stated that he and the Sentry fought Galactus.[20]

His powers were burnt out by the Omega Machine,[110] and he lost access to almost all of his abilities. He was left with limited telekinetic and telepathic abilities, but slowly regained control over more advanced powers.

Eventually, thanks to a Life Seed, Nate fully recovered and became even more powerful than ever before,[38] and is now stated to be more than Omega Level.[39]

His powers include:

Psionic Entity: After Nate transformed his body into energy and dissolved it into every living creature on the Earth,[33] his consciousness actually continued to exist on the Astral Plane,[106][20] evolving into a psionic being and was no longer restricted by the boundaries of the physical world.

  • Physical Reconstitution: Nate later learned how to rebuild his body from psionic energy.[106] He can recreate his body anywhere.[20] He can also turn himself back to psionic energy at will, and can survive his physical body's destruction by becoming psionic energy again.[20]
    • Self Detonation: He can explode his body while reversing back to psi-energy.[20] This leaves a corpse behind which can fool opponents into believing he is dead.[20]
  • Possession: Along with learning to rebuild his physical form, X-Man's heightened state of existence has allowed him to possess people.[20] While possessing someone, he can explore their mind from the inside.[146] It is possible that if he possesses a mutant, he can use their powers along with his own.[citation needed]

Psionic Energy Manipulation: Nate is able to manipulate psionic energy for various purposes.

  • Psionic Energy Absorption: He is able to absorb psionic energy from other living beings or psi-attacks, and use it to amplify himself.[84][17] He is able to almost instantly siphon psionic energy from a few dozen psychics without any effort, that left them all unconscious on the brink of death.[107]
  • Psionic Energy Solidification: He is able to make psionic energy solid and shape it to any suitable form.
    • Astral Projection Materialization: He is able to pull entities from the astral plane into the physical world.[53][60] He did this to Professor X's astral form, which accidentally unleashed Onslaught. According to Onslaught, Nate is the only mutant to have ever done this, and Onslaught's escape was only possible thanks to Nate.[26] Even Jean Grey was unable to do so, according to Moira MacTaggert.[54]
    • Psionic Armor:[84][147][13] He is able to form psi-armor around himself, that greatly increased his strength and durability. While armored he was able to counter-force Holocaust's strength,[148] push the Hulk miles away with just one punch,[78] and withstand the grip strength of Thanos.[78] He was also able to form psionic armor around other people.[78]
    • Psionic Weapons: Nate can shape psi-energy into various weapons like hammers,[55] scythes,[76] claws,[149][150] lashes,[58] etc.
    • Psionic Energy Materialization: He is able to create perfect replicas of normal matter by materializing psionic energy. He created a psionic construct of the arm of Jam,[74] that looked, felt and worked like a real arm;[6] the copy was so perfect that even a professional doctor could not determine that it was not real.[74] These psionic constructs are independent from Nate and continue to exist even without any support from him, they cease to exist only if Nate decides so.[149]
      • Psionic Body: He later vastly improved this power and was able to create an entire human body out of psi-energy, that even had the consciousness, memories and temper of the original person.[139] After his return he created a few such bodies for himself,[106][20] including his current one.[108]
        • Resurrection: Nate can potentially bring people back to life by psionically recreating them (as a psionic entity). He subconsciously brought Madelyne Pryor back to life.[24] Regretting this, he later tried to undo Pryor's existence but she had become independent of him.[24]

Astral Plane Tap: Ability to tap into the virtually unlimited psychic resources of the astral plane in order to manipulate matter and energy (even down to the molecular level) and up to a potentially unlimited scale.

Telepathy: Nate is considered the world's most powerful telepath,[71][151][152] but not the most skillful, as he himself admitted.[61] Nate was able to converse telepathically, read minds of dozens of people simultaneously,[55] read thoughts across vast distances,[55] surface scan up to several millions minds to find a specific person,[153][6] etc. He can even communicate across the Multiverse.[154] Nate considers telepathy to be his sixth sense and his most important one.[155] After Nate recovered from the power loss with a Life Seed, his telepathy reached unprecedented level as he was able to project a message into the minds of everyone on Earth with little effort (a feat that Jean Grey doubted Charles Xavier at his most powerful could have done).[39]

  • Astral Projection: Capable of forging psi-links to travel in astral form alone[42][141] or with others.[11][43]
  • Telepathic Defense: He can use telepathy in a number of defensive ways:
    • Mind Cloaking: Nate is able to mask himself and his abilities from being detected,[149] even by powerful telepaths like Charles Xavier[142] and Exodus.[58]
      • Physical and Technological Undetectability: By tweaking the senses of others, Nate was able to make himself completely imperceptible, even by those with superhumanly acute senses.[156] He is also able to psionically conceal his own life-signs, to convince everyone around him and even the high-tech equipment of the Psi-Ops' agents that he was dead.[76] He is able to hide himself and others from the sensors of Prime Sentinels,[7] and even from Cerebro, which is extremely difficult, thought to be impossible, for even the strongest of mutants to accomplish.[53]
    • Mental Shield: He can protect his mind from mental intrusions, illusions, mind control, mind manipulation, etc. His telepathic defense is so great that even while inexperienced and weakened, he easily blocked such powerful telepaths as Charles Xavier.[53] Even when his telepathy was non functional, Nate was able to sense when someone tried to read his mind and trace it back to the telepath.[85] He is also able to see through illusions created by powerful telepaths like Emma Frost.[147] Nate can escape from illusions created by such technical specialists like Mysterio.[157] His mental defense is so impervious, that it even protected him from being affected by Sentry's powers, as he was apparently the only person on the planet who kept his memories about the Sentry, when the entire world was made to forget about him.[20]
      • Automatic Psionic Defense: After Nate studied and absorbed the memories of Sunspot about the teachings of the Askani, he learned how to thoroughly protect his mind from intrusions and overcome psi-dampers placed on his powers.[61] He also created a special automatic psionic defense mechanism that rerouted the synaptic links and neural relays in his brain every time someone tried to read, manipulate, mentally intrude or control his mind; making all attempts eventually ineffective.[5]
      • Mental Shield Removal: Nate can easily overcome psi-shields created by other powerful telepaths, such as Charles Xavier,[140] and even shields created by joint efforts from several telepaths.[158]
  • Illusions / Psi-Projections:[153] Nate is able to create realistic illusions, that cause people to see and experience events, that are not actually occuring in reality.[56][159][13] His illusions can fool even Prime Sentinels[7] and trap a technical specialist in illusions like he did with Mysterio in the illusory world, Greyville.[157] It's not known how many people Nate can affect at the same time, but it's probably a few thousand or even tens of thousands.[17]
    • Telepathic Camouflage / Psi-Skin:[10] Illusions also help him disguise himself and others, by altering his appearance to the perceptions of those around him,[71][149] or make himself and others mentally invisible.[160] However his camouflage can be broken, if he does something physically superhuman.[10]
    • Mental Projection:[156] Nate can telepathically broadcast his semi-real image halfway around the world from Switzerland to New York.[156] Through this projection, he could see, hear and talk like a normal person, but had no tangible form.[156] Eventually he could do this across the Multiverse, as he projected himself to Qabiri many realities apart.[154]
  • Mind Control: Nate is capable of overriding a person's will, and telepathically control their thoughts and actions.[42][56][13] He is shown to be able to control dozens of people simultaneously.[145] He can control Magneto, even when the latter is wearing his helmet that protects him from psionic attacks.[38] Even more impressive, he is powerful enough to take over Legion's mind, a feat Legion did not expect to be possible.[161]
    • Suggestion / Persuasion: Sometimes he is able to control people just by simple verbal commands.[53][20][162]
    • Psychosomatic Suggestion: He is able to implant strong thoughts and feelings in the minds of others, which caused a corresponding physical response of the body. He was able to instantly freeze Venom, just by reminding him that it had happened to him in the past.[20]
    • Mental Paralysis: He is able to "brain-freeze" one or more people, effectively stopping their physical and mental activity for a while.[10][149]
  • Memory Manipulation:
    • Memory Reading: He is able to read the memories of those around him,[163][153][71] and project himself into a person's memories[45][164] or place others into his memories[66] to explore it from the inside. He can read memories of several people simultaneously and combine them to form a full picture.[165] He was able to read the entire life story of a person.[55][71]
    • Memory Erasure: At first he was capable of instantly erasing the memories of himself from the minds of a dozen people.[53][10] Later he developed this power to an even greater degree, and was able to simultaneously manipulate the minds of more than 7 million citizens of New York, erasing every memory about him and his life in New York.[149]
    • Memory Implantation: He is able to place psychic implants in the minds of others, that replaced their memories with false ones. He could also make these implants deadly, so that if they were removed the person could die.[56]
    • Memory Projection / Telemorphic Manifestation:[5] He is able to project someone's memories in order for them to be visually seen.[163][5][165] Mr. Sinister said that this was theoretically impossible to do.[5]
    • Memory Recall: He is able to remind himself as well as other people of the memories that they forgot.[20]
      • Memory Overload: He is able to flood the minds of others with an abundance of information of traumatic memories of their past, which overloaded their consciousness,[44] emptying their mind and soul, and placing a person into a coma-like state.[45]
    • Memory Exchange: He is able to temporarily put the memories of one person into the mind of another, including his own.[160]
      • Memory Absorption: He can absorb information, memories, knowledge and skills from others and use it as his own,[16][61][140] and even absorb the entire consciousness of a person.[71]
    • Memory Restoration: By reading the deep subconscious of a person, Nate was able to restore their fractured and/or lost memories.[71]
  • Mind Link: He is able to create a permanent mental link with another person which allowed them to share thoughts, memories and feelings,[44] and he was able to sense the whereabouts of said person.[42][45]
    • Hive Mind / Telepathic Web:[100] Nate can interconnect several people to a hive mind of sorts, with him as leader, which allow all members to share thoughts, memories and feelings with each other and maintain a permanent mental link with each other.[166][100]
  • Mental Blasts: Nate can project stunning bolts of psionic energy, which have no physical effects, but cause pain to the victim's mind[139] or render them unconsciousness.[43]
  • Intuitive Multilingual: Capable of intuitively translating and learning new languages,[142][167][78][73] including alien languages.[3]
  • Psychometry: He can read residual thought imprints left on objects touched by people. By touching an object, Nate is able to see past events that happened with the object and around it, days[71] or even decades ago.[11] Sometimes he did not even need to touch an object, only enter the room to see what happened there many years ago.[11] Psychometry also allows him to see the possible future of an object.[160]
    • Precognition & Retrocognition: Nate is also a powerful precog and retrocog. At first he was able to see visions of the future during a trance[168] or through precognitive dreams.[169] Later he learned another way to see, by displacing himself into a time-stream that shows every version of every possible future, as well as past and present events.[107][20] Nate possibly used his precognition powers to concoct his plan against Norman Osborn.[20] He can also trigger visions of other precogs and share them.[170]
  • Technopathy: Nate is able to communicate with computers[100] and control cyborgs.[7]
  • Power Manipulation: Nate is able to directly activate and control the superpowers of other beings, without taking control of their body or mind.[171][170]
    • Power Bestowal: Nate can grant superhuman powers to others as he did with Marcus Arlington III.[74] Nate gave Blob (his Horseman of Bounty) and Omega Red (his Horseman of Wellness) the ability to transform an oil rig into a jungle.[126]
    • Power Amplification: He can increase other mutants powers to incredible levels.[18][145][158]
    • Power Negation: He can place mental blocks to prevent people from using their powers.[67][15][39]
  • Telepathic Tracking: Nate is able to find any person by isolating their psi-signature on the Astral Plane and tracing it all around the globe.[172][166]
    • Telepathic Tracer: He can also place a special "tracer" on a chosen person, which allowed him to track that person's movements.[160]
  • Dream Inducement: He is able to induce dreams into other sleeping beings.[66]

Telekinesis: Nate is the most powerful telekinetic on Earth.[25][151] Nate is capable of very fine control over objects down to the molecular and even subatomic level. He could telekinetically move the chemicals in someone's brain around.[15] He can disassemble and reassemble complex devices within minutes. On larger scales, Nate can levitate vast weights, but his upper limit remained undisclosed. Nate telekinetically destroyed the city of Quito in his sleep, while being thousands of miles away in Buenos Aires.[101] He can move the tectonic plates and he once threatened to sink a continent.[129]

  • Telekinetic Flight: By telekinetically levitating himself, Nate is able to fly at amazing speeds. His maximum speed is unknown, but Nate was able to fly across the Atlantic from London to the Massachusetts Academy overnight.[147] Later his speed drastically increased, exceeding hyper-sonic speeds many times over, as he was able to fly out of the planet's atmosphere in a matter of seconds.[155][33] Nate can also remotely levitate other people.[171][10][160][100][66]
  • Force Field: Nate is able to create nigh impenetrable[171] protective force shields, that could protect him from the physical attacks of Captain Britain,[31] Colossus,[31] Thing,[13] a rocket fired at close range,[68] corrosive acid,[19] hurricane winds,[53] point blank explosions,[145] lava,[24] a direct lightning strike,[53] atmospheric re-entry,[155][33] Electro's electric discharge,[67] Psi-Ops' blasts,[17] Prime Sentinel's laser beams,[7] Fever Pitch's plasma beams,[147] Holocaust's plasma beams,[173] Human Torch's flames,[13] and even from Qabiri's blasts (that are powerful enough to destroy a planet),[174] and punches from overwhelmingly strong beings like Modt.[89] His force fields activate instinctively,[142] sometimes he is not even aware of it.[23] He can also form shields around dozens of people simultaneously.[171][160]
    • Telekinetic Bubble:[145] Normally his force fields are skin-tight, but he can also expand them to form spherical shields to extend protection to several people at the same time,[14][147] cover vast areas,[175] or hold oxygen to breathe in inhospitable environments.[9][19]
    • Molecular Filter: Nate is able to fine-tune force fields for specific types of matter to either block said specific matter or allow only it through his shields. For example he was able to stop oxygen from reaching a person,[104] separate smoke from air,[176] block pheromones from entering the body,[149] etc. He even later stated that pheromones are ineffective against him since he sampled every possible chemical.[20]
    • Thermal Adaptation: He is also able to use force fields to protect himself from extreme temperatures.[23][24][177][147][13]
    • Force Fields Removal: Nate can easily destroy the force fields of other powerful telekinetics, such as Cable.[23]
    • Telekinetic Deflection: Nate is able to deflect and/or redirect attacks, like projectiles, away from himself.[68][169] His control and power is so great and finely tuned, that instead of stopping an explosion like most telekinetics tend to do, he simply curved the energy of the shock-wave and the exploding shrapnel around him.[178]
  • Telekinetic Holograms:[147] His mental fine motor skills were so acute that he could mentally manipulate dust and dirt to create realistic holograms.[148][147]
  • Concussive Blasts: Nate can project telekinetic energy as powerful beams from his eyes,[42][44][45] hands[61][169][179] or entire body,[180] that could affect matter with concussive and/or explosive[44] force. These blasts vary from a hard push,[169] to punching through the walls,[42] robotic bodies of the Infinites[42] or Prime Sentinels,[7] and up to total destruction of Hybrid[71] and Tanaraq of the Great Beast.[180] He was even able to crush the entire city of Quito in Ecuador, in just a few moments, in his sleep.[101]
    • Telekinetic Flare: He is also able to create devastating omni-directional blasts of energy,[42] powerful enough to instantly blow up entire buildings,[44][149] and even destroy the special house created by Cable that was considered to be able to withstand the strongest of attacks.[23] One of his strongest flares created a huge rift in the ground a few miles in size.[168][180] Nate could potentially create so powerful a flare that could destroy half the planet, or more.[168]
  • Intangibility: Can phase through solid matter by mentally shifting his molecules around another object's as he moves forward.[144][6][84] Nate can selectively phase only certain parts of his body, while the rest of his body remains solid.[160] He can also phase other people and does not require physical contact to do so.[10]
  • Energy Manipulation: Nate was able to telekinetically manipulate the various types of energy of the electromagnetic spectrum.
    • Electromagnetic Pulses: He can use telekinesis to channel the Earth's magnetic field through the electro-current of his brain and generate electromagnetic pulses that disrupt electronics.[6] He can channel enough energy to generate so devastating an electromagnetic pulse, that it affected an area of a few miles in size, and left behind a residual electromagnetic charge, powerful enough to disrupt electronics even a few hours later.[180]
    • Electricity Generation: He can also channel the electromagnetic field of the planet into directed discharges of high-voltage energy from his eyes, hands and entire body,[76] and possibly even discharge lightning.[145]
    • Light Manipulation: He is able to control visible light for various effects:[31]
      • Flash Light: Nate is able to create flashes of visible light, powerful enough to blind a group of people for a short time.[149]
      • Invisibility: Nate can telekinetically deflect light around himself to become invisible.[149][147][181]
  • Matter Manipulation: The ability to alter molecular and atomic structures in various substances.[169]
    • Weather Manipulation: By hyper-agitating the molecules in clouds, Nate was able to bring forth rain and lightning.[176]
    • Air Manipulation: Nate is able to create whirlwinds[139] and even create a vacuum.[148]
    • Water Manipulation: He is able to part the sea,[31] control and shape snow[24] and clouds.[85]
    • Earth Manipulation: Nate is able to open and close fissures in the earth,[58] cause[58] and stop[176] earthquakes, and even form hollow mounds of earth as protective 'armor'.[17]
    • Heat Generation: By agitating the motion of water molecules, he was able to heat and boil water around his enemy while both of them were underwater.[76]
      • Fire Generation and Manipulation: Nate was able to ignite a subterranean gas pipeline,[145] creating fire by igniting oxygen molecules in the air[157] and then extinguish the flames,[176] momentary incinerate a person[83][25] and even several people at the same time.[100]
    • Inanimate Disintegration: He can disperse objects down to its subatomic molecular state.[145][14]
    • Gravity Manipulation: Nate is able to manipulate the gravitational field of the planet. He was able to negate gravity even on very huge objects,[68] and he himself said that it did not pose any challenge for him.[24] He was also able to isolate the gravitational field around certain objects or people, and since objects are held on the surface of the planet by the gravitational field, while the planet is constantly moving in space, removing the field of gravity would cause objects to fly out the planet's atmosphere almost instantly.[180][181]
  • Teleportation: X-Man even learned that he could use his telekinesis to transport himself and other people to any location[74] and/or create portals for teleportation.[170]
    • Interdimensional Teleportation: He is also able to travel into other dimensions and realities. At first he did so accidentally,[68][101] but quickly learned how to control this ability[4] and mastered it.[166][100][170][174][19][154] Nate is labelled a "world-walker" by Qabiri because of his ability to traverse the Multiverse with ease.[19]
    • Remote Teleportation: Nate is also able to remotely teleport objects[6] and people[45] without requiring any physical contact and across vast distances.[125] He even can teleport them into other dimensions or realities without transporting himself along.[100]
    • Space Manipulation: Nate can distort and/or destroy the dimensional barriers that separate dimensions and realities from one another, allowing residents of these dimensions travel to the main reality and vice versa.[168][180]
    • Spatial Perception: Capable of sensing dimensional rifts and/or anomalies,[180] sense if someone traveled between realities[158] and see the Spiral of Realities.[101][25]
  • Tactile Telekinesis: Nate can use telekinesis to enhance his own physiology:
    • Superhuman Strength: He can use telekinesis to increase his physical strength to such a degree that he was able to literally beat Sinister to death despite his ability to rapidly regenerate the cells of his body,[45] lift large stones,[3] leap to heights and distances greater than that of an ordinary human,[3] break Holocaust's armor,[171] tear through Psi-Ops' shielded ship,[17] etc. Combining telekinetically increased strength with his force fields, Nate was able to fight with Rex,[143] X-Cutioner,[54] Psi-Ops,[17] Thing,[13] Holocaust,[45][173] Apocalypse[45] and even Ares.[20]
    • Superhuman Speed: He can use telekinesis to increase his running speed, though it is unknown to exactly what degree.[3]

Healing: He can heal himself and others.[177][4] Marcus Arlington III described Nate's powers as a gift and a healing power that could cure the likes of cancer, but it is limited by Nate's lack of knowledge.[6] When Nate first encountered Arlington, he accidentally created a link between them; Nate's powers and Arlington's medical knowledge allowed them to completely regenerate a brand new arm for Jam.[74][6] Nate can also give other people healing abilities: Arlington retained some of Nate's residual healing powers, and he could perform miraculous feats such as healing gunshot wounds instantaneously.[74] However, Arlington admitted that he is not a mutant, he cannot replenish the powers he was given, and he is not sure how long they will last.[6] Later, after Nate declared himself a "shaman" of mutants, he travelled around the world healing people in need.[4][170]

Time Manipulation: Nate can step out of the time-stream and enter the "Planck Length" between moments. Within it, he can see the time-stream, past and present events as well as future possibilities, and he was also able to manipulate both the time-stream and past, present and future events within it.[20]

Reality Warping: Nate Grey is a reality warping mutant.[182][165] When Nate was attached to the Omega Machine, it used his reality warping abilities to conceal them. Danielle Moonstar thought it was an illusion but Victoria Hand corrected her by stating it was all real.[110] Nate was able to erase all houses of worship around the world from existence with a single thought.[126] He can also create miniature stars.[4]

  • Age of X-Man: With a Life Seed, Nate created a new plane of existence.[41] He molded reality/history to make it a mutant paradise.[132] He can manipulate anything there. For example, when Bishop got excommunicated, not only did Nate alter everyone's memories, he altered pictures as well, replacing Bishop with Laura Kinney.[131] However, he has to constantly maintain the plane as psychic "fissures" began to appear which leaked memories of the main reality into this one.[183][184] Nate can create these fissures himself and load them with false memories.[184] Nate is aware of everyone and everything in the plane at all times, as if they are a part of him; when people take a step, he feels it as if they are walking on his chest.[41] Nate and the Age of X-Man are one, so if Nate dies, so does the Age of X-Man and everything in it.[41]
  • Reality Warp Immunity: Nate was able to see through the reality warping of powerful beings such as Jahf and Modt.[89]

Phoenix Force (potential avatar): According to the Shi'ar, the carriers of the Grey Genome,[185] Omega Level Mutants,[186] and telepaths[187] have the potential to bond with the Phoenix Force, which makes Nate a potential host. His strength was compared to the Dark Phoenix,[31][55][188] and he was once surrounded by an aura similar to the Phoenix raptor.[155]

Former Powers
Pseudo-Vampirism: By unknown means Nate was infected by Morbius, and had been transformed into a being similar to a vampire.[65] In this form he had red eyes, fangs and claws, and possibly possessed other vampiric powers, as well as weaknesses, like Morbius. Nate eventually cured himself of his vampirism (possibly through his telekinetic ability to induce vitakinesis), as according to Spider-Man, Nate either isolated and destroyed the vampirism virus with his telekinesis (possibly through his ability to molecularly disintegrate things) or he simply learned to live with it.

Abilities

Charisma: Nate possess natural charm and charisma, that easily attracts people to him to such an extent that it even impressed Purple Man.[74][149]

Meticulous Mind: Although Nate has relatively little combat experience, he however is very observant and easily able to analyze his enemies and find their weaknesses. He often finds creative ways to use his abilities in extreme circumstances, even on the verge of death.[76] Even in situations when his abilities were blocked or shut off entirely, he repeatedly found interesting ways around it.[139] Even when he was just a child, he was already able to easily overcome and/or completely ignore psi-blocks placed by Sinister specifically to block his powers.[11] Later he, without much effort, overcame psi-dampers developed by the Shi'ar Empire, who thoroughly investigated his abilities beforehand.[77]

Weapons Specialist: Nate does not usually use weapons, but he absorbed a profound amount of knowledge about various firearms and armed combat techniques from Domino.[16]

Expert Survivalist: Having lived for many years in the wastelands of Age of Apocalypse, he was taught by Forge how to survive even in the most extreme surroundings.[42][149]

  • Weapons Expert

Weaknesses

Overconfidence: Nate is very confident in his abilities, and sometimes acted too hastily and emotionally, often underestimating his enemies, which led to his defeat against weaker opponents.[108] His overconfidence also led to the fact that he unintentionally lessened his mental defenses against the Red Queen, allowing her to take control over his mind and body without him realizing it at first.[25]

Mind's Interconnection: His bio-signature[143] and psi-signature[58] are almost identical to Cable, so their minds are interconnected, causing them great pain and spontaneous exchange of memories, when they're in close proximity to each other.[23] Strangely enough he had not had such a problem with Cable's clone[83] or his own counterpart from another reality.[25]

Former Weaknesses
Genetic Time Bomb: After testing Nate's power levels, Sinister was so frightened, that he encoded a special genetic sub-program into Nate's D.N.A. that was supposed to kill him eventually.[11] Dr. Moira MacTaggert later conducted a study of Nate's body and came to the conclusion that he is unlikely to survive up to 21 years.[31] His powers were slowly killing him, until his counterpart gave him his genetic X-Gene insignia, created by Red Queen to stabilize his powers.[4]
