Notes

  • Marvel Age was a promotional comic book-sized magazine from Marvel Comics in the 1980s. It contained previews of upcoming Marvel comics, as well as interviews with comics professionals and other features. Its initial cover price of $0.25-0.35 USD was much cheaper than regular Marvel comics of the era.
