History

The Death Illusion Spell of the Shadow was a magical spell used by Doctor Strange against Victoria Hand while the New Avengers were questioning her loyalty. Strange made Hand believe that Wolverine had thrown her out of her apartment window, causing her to plummet several stories before crashing onto the roof of a taxi. The apparent fall was actually an illusion, intended to frighten Hand into cooperating with the Avengers.[1]

The spell is detailed in the Book of the Vishanti at page 234.
