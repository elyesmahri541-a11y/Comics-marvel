Synopsis for "Assemble, Part One"

In the Howley household, John watches his wife Julie get ready for election day, where she is supporting Amanda Spring’s campaign for mayor of New York City. Their daughter Paige declines the offer to intern.

Wolverine approaches Gambit and Rogue, requesting Gambit use the Eye of Agamatto to help him track a prey, but he refuses to elaborate on who it is or why.

Mephisto comes to New York City to cancel deals he made previously.

Peter and May Parker volunteer at a shelter, but Peter’s spider-sense alerts him that something is off, so he leaves.

In a Hell's Kitchen bar, Carol Danvers stares at a glass of alcohol, debating if she should drink it as she reflects on the trauma she experienced seeing Red Hulk explode. Daredevil walks in and convinces her to walk away.

As Amanda Spring makes her final case to the electorate that they must illegalize superpowers, Luke Cage finds that one of his biggest donors has had her heart ripped from her chest. He calls Jessica Jones who believes that the culprit was something supernatural. As they wrap up their conversation, Spider-Man appears to tell Cage that he knows that something is wrong, but can’t figure out what it is.

He exits on rooftops and encounters Miracleman, who is searching for Tony Stark to get clarity on why he has developed bizarre powers. The two heroes come to blows, with Spider-Man blaming Miracleman for the catastrophe in Russia. Wolverine interrupts them, saying that Miracleman is not responsible and he has been keeping an eye on him since his transformation.

The skies turn red and at the Howleys’, John tells Paige to run and gives her a blank, sentient book named Grimsby whom he tells her will guide her. John’s eyes turn red and brandishes a supernatural sword to fight a host of angels led by the Adjudicator. He calls his wife to tell her what has happened and implore her to stay away.

The angels have come to reverse a variety of contracts made by Mephisto. In the huge fracas, the six superheroes spontaneously encounter one another and coordinate fighting back the invasion. Although she does not understand how, Paige’s life is in danger due to a deal that her parents made and she is narrowly saved by Daredevil and Spider-Man.

She explains that Grimsby has explained that Spider-Man has also made a deal with Mephisto and his deal is now being undone, so anyone under the protection of the terms of the contract are in danger. Spider-Man has no memory of this, but he assumes that this means that Aunt May is under attack.
