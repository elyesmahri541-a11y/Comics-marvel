History

This reality was known to a variant of Loki known as the "God of Stories," who watched over the story of the reality's Avengers on their road to facing Doctor Doom. Notably, the Avengers were first assembled by Nick Fury to thwart the attempt of this reality's Loki to invade Earth.[1]

Later, they faced Iron Man's creation Ultron, who was aided by Wanda and Pietro Maximoff. After Pietro's death, Wanda joined the Avengers and Ultron was defeated once and for all.[2]

The Avengers later fought Thanos and lost, resulting in the Mad Titan eliminating half of all life in the universe.[3]

Five years later, the surviving heroes hatched a plan to borrow infinity stones from other timelines to bring everyone back. In the final moments of the battle, Iron Man snapped his fingers and eliminated Thanos and his forces.[4]

Notes

  • This reality is depicted through Marvel Rivals' Path to Doomsday event and is inspired by the Marvel Cinematic Universe, specifically Marvel's The Avengers, Avengers: Age of Ultron, Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame, which features a story event every couple seasons based on each of the four Avengers films in preparation for Avengers: Doomsday.
  • Unlike the Avengers and Age of Ultron game modes, the actual gameplay of the Infinity War game mode is unrelated to the lore surrounding it.
