When the Avengers and Fantastic Four sacrificed themselves to stop Onslaught, Franklin Richards saved them by sending them to a pocket universe. Many elements of this universe mirrored the Prime Universe, though some details such as the membership of the Heralds of Galactus were slightly different.

History

After the Silver Surfer's first encounter with the Fantastic Four,[1] Terrax came to Earth in order to eliminate the cosmically-empowered beings he saw as a potential obstacles to their master Galactus, but the other Heralds Firelord and Plasma arrived to collect their "brother" so that they could begin the process of harvesting the planet.[2] Each Herald was tasked with placing one of Galactus' bio-spheric energy capacitors: Firelord and Plasma established theirs near Attilan and on Monster Island, respectively, but were stopped by the Inhumans and Avengers, and Thor destroyed the Surfer's device in New York City; however Air-Walker's stealthy placement of a capacitor on Antarctica allowed the process of harvesting to begin.[3]

After Doctor Doom's time travel to prevent Earth's destruction, the capacitors' locations were seemingly changed: this time the Surfer traveled to Moscow, where his capacitor was destroyed by the Fantastic Four; Plasma set up a capacitor in Los Angeles, which was destroyed by the S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier; Firelord instead traveled to Antarctica but was thwarted by the Hulk, Scarlet Witch and Vision; and Terrax established his capacitor in Hong Kong, only for it to be destroyed by the remaining Avengers; each resulting in many casualties. Airwalker informed the survivors that the destruction of all four capacitors had in fact proven that their world was ready for harvesting, however, and the capacitors reformed in new locations and began again their task. Though the Avengers sacrificed the Earth in a gravity warp that engulfed the solar system, including Galactus, Doom once again traveled back in time to save his planet.[4]

This time, the Fantastic Four identified and intercepted the Heralds before their arrival on the planet, but were each killed in the struggle - Terrax killing the Thing, Firelord the Human Torch, Plasma Mr. Fantastic and Air-Walker the Invisible Woman. Continuing on to Attilan, Terrax was able to kill Black Bolt before being taken down by Thor and She-Hulk, while the Wasp joined the Scarlet Witch and Vision in freezing Plasma in Antarctica and Captain America took Doc Samson and Hawkeye helped Namor beat Firelord on Monster Island. Still, this was not enough, and Doom altered the timeline once more.[5]

In this final timeline, the Heralds were put down quickly, with Thor and the Hulk trouncing Terrax, Scarlet Witch and the Vision taking down Firelord, and Iron Man and Hawkeye putting an end to Plasma. Still, this was not enough to defeat Galactus for good, and the Silver Surfer eventually sacrificed himself, with the Avengers' help, to overload Galactus' own energy conversion.[6]

Notes

  • As with their Prime Universe counterparts, each Herald is associated with one of the four classical elements: Air-Walker with air, Firelord with fire, Plasma with water, and Terrax with earth. Though the Prime Silver Surfer was also associated with water,[7] here he may be thought of as representing aether, occasionally considered a fifth element.
    • As the Fantastic Four are also connected with the four elements, they are each considered to have an equivalent or "corollary" among the Heralds, with Mr. Fantastic's being Plasma.[2]
