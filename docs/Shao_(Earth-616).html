History

Shao was a dwarf who served as one of Prince Ba'rahm's henchmen and assisted him in carrying out his fraudulent religious ceremonies. He took part in the operation through which Ba'rahm deceived wealthy women into believing they were being sacrificed to Decka, while actually robbing and murdering them.

During Captain America and Bucky's attempt to stop Ba'rahm from killing another victim, Shao overpowered the two heroes and knocked them unconscious. They were subsequently taken to a chamber where Ba'rahm intended to drown them, but managed to regain consciousness and escape. Shao was still among Ba'rahm's associates when the heroes returned to the hideout and brought their operation to an end.[1]
