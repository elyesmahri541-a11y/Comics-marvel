History

Zmey Gorynych (or Zmey and Gorynych) was the son of Naga and Maralith, and as such the brother of Merro, Bolla, and Pressyne,[3] and a great-great-grandchild of the Elder God Set.[2]

Notes

  • In his entry in Marvel Zombies: The Book of Angels, Demons & Various Monstrosities #1, Set is stated to the great-great-grandfather of, among others, "Zmey" and "Gorynych". It is unknown if Zmey Gorynych is indeed two entities, or if it is an error.
