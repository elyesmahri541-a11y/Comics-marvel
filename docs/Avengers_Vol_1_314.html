Synopsis for "Along Came a Spider..."

Spider-Man accompanies the Avengers as they investigate a strange inversion effect. A disguised Nebula has taken control of a research project, with which she hopes to gain the ultimate power. However, her latest attempt seems to have obliterated almost the entire universe, leaving a room from the Avengers subterranean headquarters and its five occupants, all protected by Sersi's power, floating alone in an empty white void.

Notes

  • The Avengers have begun rebuilding Avengers Mansion in Manhattan after the sinking of Avengers Island in Avengers #311.
  • Captain America's private Quinjet is stated to have been repaired so well that no one would know that it had been wrecked "just a few days ago" in Avengers #313.
  • Sersi joins the Avengers.
