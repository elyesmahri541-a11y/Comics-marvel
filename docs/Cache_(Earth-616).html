History

Captain America found Protocide at an AIM station and the two immediately continued their battle. Cache, Billups, and other agents of AIM saw this as an opportunity to get rid of Captain America once and for all. They opened fire on the two combatants. Protocide and Cap joined forces to defeat Cache, Billups and AIM.[1]
