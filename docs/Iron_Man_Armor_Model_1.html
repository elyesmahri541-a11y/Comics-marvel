History

This suit of armor was created by Tony Stark, with the help of Ho Yinsen, when he was captured by a terrorist cell in Siancong while overseeing the testing of some weaponry.[6] It worked as a pacemaker to keep Stark's heart beating after shrapnel from the detonation of a land mine became lodged in his chest. The armor doubled as a means for Stark to escape captivity and avenge Yinsen's death at the hands of their captors.[7]

The suit was later upgraded into its MK II version by Stark when he returned to the United States. The most notorious change was present in the chestplate. After realizing he would need to wear that piece of the suit at all times, Stark made it lighter and sleeker.[8] During one of his heroics, Tony soon realized the dull gray appearance of the armor terrified not only his enemies but also the innocent people he was trying to protect. Stark decided to paint its shell gold, after his date Marion Rodgers commented how Iron Man would look better with a paint job that could make him look like a knight in shining armor.[9] The gold-plated version became the MK III,[4] and Iron Man soon gained the nickname of "The Golden Avenger."[citation needed] Tony was forced to create a partial duplicate of the suit after being kidnapped to Netherworld without access to the parts of original armor other than the chestplate that was keeping him alive.[10] In order to specifically fight the Melter, Stark built a version of the armor that replaced any iron with aluminum, so the armor could resist the villain's Melting Ray. In a previous encounter with the Melter, the armor's left arm had been easily destroyed.[11]

Following a disastrous battle with Mister Doll which nearly killed him, Tony realized the suit was too heavy, a disadvantage that caused the suit to consume more energy than necessary to sustain its own weight. Additionally, the chestplate appeared to need recharging more often. As soon as a new lighter armor finished being assembled, Stark stopped using the Model 1.[12] Stark briefly returned to this armor when the Model 2 was stolen by small-time criminal Weasel Wills, requiring Stark to use the older suit against the criminal. Despite the other armour being faster and more powerful, Stark was able to use the Model 1's superior endurance to wear Wills down until he could drain off the Model 2's power.[13]

The MK II version of this suit of Iron Man armor was far from the only significant upgrade created by Stark. After losing Stark International to Obadiah Stane and subsequently joining Circuits Maximus as he recovered from alcoholism, Tony built a replica of the Model 1 as a sort of rehabilitation project.[14] While built largely with spare parts and physically inferior to the Model 5 suit being worn by James Rhodes at the time, this version would incorporate some ideas that would later be used in the Silver Centurion suit. The most distinct difference between this "rehab" armor and the original Model 1 was the elimination of the hip-mounted power pods, a weakness Tony himself was able to exploit against the Model 5 when Rhodey, due to headaches induced by the armor, went on a rampage.[15] Tony used this suit again to aid the West Coast Avengers against Doctor Demonicus,[16] though he was forced to ditch the armor in the Pacific Ocean.[17] Demonicus recovered the suit and briefly used it himself, but his lack of experience allowed him to be defeated quickly by Tony wearing components of the incomplete Model 8 and a makeshift costume.[18]

Another version, code-named "MK 0," was secretly built later on, which incorporated repulsor weapons, a type of weaponry not implemented in Stark's suits until the Model 3. It was kept in Wong-Chu's abandoned base in case of emergency. When Tony Stark was on the run from Norman Osborn and was slowly wiping out his brain, he traveled to Siancong in order to recover the "MK 0," which was the only armor simple enough to be used in his state.[19] Stark tried to get to Dubai, but he was intercepted in Iran by Norman Osborn, the Iron Patriot, who nearly destroyed the suit and almost killed Stark. However, Stark's allies managed to leak the coordinates of the fight to media outlets, granting the fight the media coverage necessary to force Osborn to not kill Stark in cold blood.[20] The "MK 0" was one of the only nine suits that were recovered after Norman Osborn's fall,[21] and its materials were ultimately scavenged to create the Rescue Armor Model 2 for Virginia "Pepper" Potts.[22]

The Model 1 was recreated and kept in exposition in the different Halls of Armor owned by Stark. During the War of the Serpent, when Stark Tower was struck down by Skadi, Protector tapped into Stark's mainframe and controlled an army of armor that had survived the collapse, including the Model 1, to fight against Skadi, but they were easily dealt with.[23] Another Model 1 armor was kept in the Hall of Armor at the Avengers Mansion,[24] and one later at the rebuilt Stark Tower.[25] When Stark temporarily moved his base of operations to Stark Island, a Model 1 was displayed there.[26] The Hall of Armor in the Stark Tower remained there, and it even used one Model 1 as a security drone.[27] A different Model 1 armor was brought to Petoskey Stone Diner by numerous LMDs in hopes of being reverse engineered.[28]

When a digital copy of Tony Stark confronted the real one, who had had his morality inverted with magic, it commanded an army of Iron Man armor, including the Model 1 in both its MK II and MK III versions. Iron Man hacked into the MK III version in order to learn the location of the digital Stark's mainframe.[29] During battle against the sorcerer dragon Sadurang, the advanced technological systems of Stark's suit became infected by magic and it transmuted into a magical armor. Unable to control the ability to cast spells the new suit granted him, Tony opted to discard the armor and return to the Model 1. The bulky suit's focus on brute strength combined with its lack of technology advanced enough for Sadurang to infect gave him the edge in battle, and he defeated the dragon.[5]

Following the discovery that Tony's use of a bio-engineered new body with his mind downloaded into it to overcome a coma following the Second Civil War meant he was purportedly a copy of the real Tony Stark who was since then dead, Stark lost his legitimacy as a real person and was deposed of his belongings.[30] Having embraced his nature as an artificial being, Tony became the leader of the A.I. Army, a liberation movement for artificial lifeforms, among them androids and robots, fighting for their rights in the light of increased oppression. Tony donned a makeshift recreation of the Model 1 Armor and started going by the name "Mark One."[31]

Properties

Overview[]

The armor's primary function was to act as a pacemaker which electronically stimulated Tony Stark's heart to keep it beating after the shrapnel from a land mine's detonation had entered his chest.[7] This ability was achieved through the use of a defibrillator circuit and flat antenna that directly stimulated the S-A signal of Stark's vagus nerve as needed with the use of a supercutaneous high-frequency electric signal.[4] This function was made obsolete once Stark had been cured of his condition.[32]

The suit's shell is made of threads of mono-crystalline iron coated with tetrafluorethene plastic, knitted together using a 3D mechanical loom. Integrated circuits called "micro-modules" were created from sheets of silicon using an e-beam writer; this circuitry, that placed 200 transistors in a square inch with wiring and associated components, could be used both as a switch and amplifier. This allowed the creation of tiny power amplifiers that could generate the amounts of electricity needed for the DC motors that provided the suit with mobility. Each small motor generated around a horsepower of power. The use of pure iron protected with tetrafluorethene allowed a 200-inch per second speed. A gauntlet alone, for example, required a dozen motors.[4] The circuits of the armor are coordinated with its user's brain waves, which allow the suit to duplicate every action of the human body. But the system is not perfect, and it requires some time to get used to it.[7] It also employed a negative feedback body motion sensing system.[4]

The armor's first versions were powered by a flat array of miniature batteries built into its chest plate. The batteries could be recharged by plugging the chestplate into any electrical source.[7] Additional high-density storage batteries were later molded into shapes that fit almost all over the interior surface.[4] The "MK 0" version of this suit, an armor hidden in Wong-Chu's abandoned base in case of emergency, was powered by a R.T. node.[19] An automatic self-lubrication system was built into the armor, this could be used to squirt low-pressure streams of oil.[7] The suit also possessed an internal air supply.[33]

To help with the armor's comfortability, lamb's wool was used for interior padding. The wool was later replaced by a foamed plastic that wicked moisture away from the skin and osmotically pumped it into collection cells. There it was cooled and recirculated around the outside of the body to transport heat away from any hot spots.[4] The combination of hard and soft parts allowed the bulky armor to be folded and unfolded like clothes, without losing its durability.[9] However, it mandated a certain procedure in unpacking the suit so that it would fit inside Tony Stark's briefcase.[4] The armor's alloy prevented the suit from being magnetized.[34]

Jet boots were the armor's main transportation system. The boot turbines made use of ordinary air, drawn in through a loose weave of iron fabric, which was highly compressed and created a jet that enabled its user to fly,[4][7] or make assisted leaps.[7] The Model 1 Mark III featured the first successful sub-2-inch diameter turbine. However, the thrust-to-mass ratio was too low to allow the suit to be able to hover if its user carried something as insignificant as pocket change. The sheer size of the turbine compressor produced an ear-splitting high-frequency noise which was "about a notch higher than a mosquito and millions of times as loud," making the suit gain the nickname of "Screaming Mimi."[4] A series of small silicon levers allowed the armor to keep flying straight and level; its wearer had to use body English to accomplish steering while flying.[4] The "MK 0" can make repulsor enhanced jumps.[20]

Other transportation systems included jet-powered roller skates for fast travel by land at 200 mph,[35] a miniature jet engine and four casters that could be adhered magnetically to the armor to allow for a horizontal aerodynamic travel, like a luger,[36] and a propeller which could be clamped into the boots for travelling on water.[36] When he returned to the United States, Stark modified the suit into what is known as the MK II version of the Model 1 Armor. The new version included onboard O.S. (with a translator program), lenses, and heating and cooling systems.[19] Most of these changes were also featured in the "MK 0" version of the suit.[19] A MK III added improvement in the surface, with gold plating and a metal kilt to cover groin segments. Communication systems were also improved.[4]

Because the armor's helmet was made of solid iron, it had almost no flexibility. Its wearer could not breathe hard, shout due to reduced jaw movement, or even puff his cheeks. The MK II version of the helmet added water-cooled padding, directional speakers which fed sounds to its user from wherever they impinged on the suit sensors, and a head-up display; Stark called this "Environment+Information." The neck piece was a very complex assembly. A series of flat, circular motors were stacked around the user's neck, and they were surrounded by thin metal sheeting which would follow their motion. Having several motors in each neck segment motivated helical screws that allowed for very quick movements and still be very strong.[4]

Stark and Yinsen provided the armor with additional offensive and defensive capabilities to double its use as a means to escape captivity.[7] Miscellaneous equipment includes miniature versions of different handy tools, including a buzz saw[7] and a drill,[9] public-address speakers,[9] powerful magnets,[9] detachable suction cups that allowed the wearer to adhere to smooth surfaces and are strong enough to support the weight of the armor for a few moments,[7] an "electronic reverse-energy beam" that could redirect rays of energy,[37] chemical crystals that could change the composition of substances,[37] image inducers,[37] nuclear-powered clippers,[37] diamond-edged digging tools,[36] a miniature "furnace" that generates heat waves,[35] a fluoroscope,[36] audio recorders,[33] radio communications (enhanced by a telescoping antenna,[2])[35] ear plugs,[33] and miniature jet blowers.[11] Some of these items appear to be stored in the suit's accessory belt,[7][9] while others, such as the buzz saw, appear to be incorporated into the armor's fingers.[7] Using the belt's panel, the armor could access the systems of Stark Industries.[11]

Offensive Powers[]

The suit enhances its user's strength to superhuman levels, allowing the user to pick up a man in the air easily,[7] break through metal doors,[9] destroy brick walls simply by walking into them,[9] crush a cannonball with ease,[10] tear a wall of two floors in height with one hand,[10] and break the fall of a missile.[33] Tony Stark also stated that the suit was capable of performing such feats like ripping the bottom of a ship with ease, knocking supersonic-speed bombers with a single punch, and breaking apart space missiles. However, the veracity of that statement is dubious, as Tony was trying to intimidate Queen Kala of Netherworld to prevent her from invading the Earth.[37] Weaponry in the armor includes a miniature flamethrower[7] and tear gas bombs.[36] The armor can also redirect its electrical currency to its shell, being capable of electrocuting objects by touch.[9] A circular device in the center of the chest is known as the "Monobeam," a portable proton beam[4] that served the purpose of a powerful search light,[9] but it could also be adapted for a heat ray.[35] It only had a useful range of several yards.[4] The "MK 0" version of the suit was outfitted with energy projection capabilities via repulsor weaponry,[19] and can also unleash a flamethrower from a seam on its chestplate.[20]

Defensive Powers[]

The armor's shell provides a degree of superhuman durability, as it is strong enough to resist gunfire,[7] the bites of animals like wild felines,[9] the impact of a cannonball,[10] heat, cold, energy, and acidic attacks.[2] However, it was not protected against potential rusting from sea water.[10] The suit is also equipped with an array of miniature electromagnetic generators that allow its user to manipulate magnetic fields for a variety of effects, including jamming and disruption of electrical equipment,[38] and reverse magnetism, to repel metallic objects like bullets and enemy weaponry.[7] An electrical force field could also be created to protect the armor from energy damage.[33] The "MK 0" is durable enough to take several repulsor blasts, missiles, physical blows from the Iron Patriot and strong enough to stop its momentum powered blows.[20]


Sons of Yinsen[]

The Sons of Yinsen built drones that were based on the 1st model of Iron Man Armor. Which had the same appearance and capabilities, with some of the same capabilities and at least one upgrade. They had flight capability and fired repulsor bolts, and were upgraded to protect them from an electromagnetic pulse. Drones which were filled with air and constructed of steel.[39][40][41]

Alternate Reality Versions

Startling Stories: Thing Night Falls on Yancy Street (Earth-36)[]

In this universe, Iron Man was seen as a statue created by Alicia Masters.[42]

Coal Tiger (Earth-355)[]

In this universe, Iron Man is one of many Avengers of his world who were killed by an imbalanced Sersi.[43]

Zombieverse (Earth-2149)[]

Following the zombies devouring almost all humans on Earth before consuming Galactus, surviving X-Men member Forge broke into the ruins of Stark International and spent the next 40 years improving on Tony's original design up until the Zombie Galacti returned to Earth and New Wakanda.

Ultimate Universe (Earth-1610)[]

Main article: Iron Man Armor MK I (Earth-1610)

In this universe, the first Iron Man armor (or a suit simply similar in appearance) was being displayed in Tony Stark's office.[44]

A version of the suit was stolen by the vampire named Anthony, who used the armor to protect himself from sunlight. When Anthony died at the hands of his subordinate the "Nerd Hulk",[45] Stick started wearing the armor up until his death.[46]

Iron Maniac's Reality (Earth-5012)[]

In this universe, Iron Maniac utilized a suit almost identical to the Iron Man Armor Model 1 before when he was stranded on Earth-616.[47]

New Ultimate Universe (Earth-6160)[]

In this universe, The Model 1 was the first armor built by Howard Stark, being made of gold. Following Stark's betrayal of the Maker and the ensuing availability of Stane/Stark's corporate assets, Midas would seize the opportunity to appropriate the Model and repurpose it as a collector's item to wear, utilizing America Chavez as a power source.[48]

Bereet's Techno Art Movie (Earth-7711)[]

In this universe, Iron Man was impersonated by a Krylorian, their battle has attracted the attention of the real Iron Man.[49].[43]

Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes! (Earth-8096)[]

When Ultron hacked into Iron Man's suits to confront the Avengers, Tony Stark was forced to don his first and most primitive armor, the MK I.

Tony first used it to assist his teammates against the rogue armory, and later to confront Ultron in the S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier. The MK I was heavily damaged and subsequently destroyed in the ensuing battle.[50]

When the Enchantress magically depowered Iron Man, Captain America and Thor, Iron Man's armor was turned into the MK I. While the armor was not too complex to control, Tony's intellect had also been magically diminished. When the spell was broken, the armor returned to its advanced form.[51]

Iron Man became a traitor (Earth-8861)[]

Iron Man was forced to betray the U.S.A.[52]

Ruins (Earth-9591)[]

Tony Stark's first armor, built in Vietnam to survive injuries during a visit to help end the war, it earned him the name "The Man in the Iron Mask" yet was eventually replaced by a new suit of armor when Stark formed the Avengers.[53]

Amalgam Pocket Universe (Earth-9602)[]

In the Amalgam Universe, after being injured by shards of metal stuck in his chest, Hal Stark built his first armor with the alien technology he acquired from the dying alien Rohmann Sur, powered by a battery he found in the wreckage, which resembles a lantern. The suit of armor not only allowed him to survive, but also gave him incredible powers, allowing him to create any object he pleased out of green energy, due to the battery he used being powered by Oa the Living Planet. He battled and defeated the aliens that had shot down Rhomann Sur's spacecraft. This version of Armor Model 1 is green and has a Green Lantern emblem on the chest.[54]

Marvel's Avengers Assemble (Earth-12041)[]

Main article: Iron Man Armor MK I (Earth-12041)

In order to fight the forces of the Cabal, Iron Man assembled the Iron Legion, an army of his past suits, with the MK I being one of them.[55]

Marvel Adventures (Earth-20051)[]

Tony Stark developed proto-armor as a child, when he went to show It to his dad he got reprimanded for interrupting his business discussion with Obadiah Stane. Howard went on to equip his S.E.R.V.A.C. with mass produced automatons of his son's proto armor.[56]

Darkhold: Iron Man (Earth-21129)[]

In this reality, Tony Stark equipped his earliest Iron Man Armor with machinery capable of repairing organs and mending bones, akin to a mobile hospital, with the intention to protect himself and help the world at large. His invention went awry when the armor's systems concluded that the suit's own iron skin was superior to human skin, and tried to replace it with itself.

Before Tony and Pepper Potts could fix this issue, Tony was enticed by the suit's analgesic effect and unconsciously slipped back into the armor, accelerating the process. The suit dissolved all of Tony's skin, broke down his entire body and lodged its wires deep inside his brain. Deluded by the epiphany that his new body was safer and stronger, Tony built countless copies of the armor and placed them all over the city, sowing chaos as unwitting bystanders climbed inside the suits only to have their bodies mutilated by it. Stark also created one copy of the armor specifically tailored to the female physique for Pepper.[57]

The Fantastic Four ended (Earth-49487)[]

In this reality, Iron Man was wearing the Model 1 armor when Dark Raider murdered Mister Fantastic and Invisible Woman.[58]

The Operatives (Earth-53523)[]

In this reality, Iron Man was a founding member of the Operatives.[59]

Kobik's Timeline (Earth-61311)[]

In this reality, Iron Man was a member of the Avengers which was founded by Hydra.[60]


Bullet Points (Earth-70105)[]

On Earth-70105, a suit similar to the Iron Man Armor Model 1 was developed to supplement the loss of the Super Soldier Program under Dr. Erskine. Steve Rogers was selected to undergo a long painful surgery to integrate the operating systems of the armor with his body.[61]

Spider-Man Heroes & Villains Collection (Earth-83066)[]

On Earth-83066, Tony was forced to wear his first armor.[62]

Super Hero Squad (Earth-91119)[]

On Earth-91119, the Model 1 Armor was one of the many suits used by Iron Man.

The Invincible Iron Man (Earth-199673)[]

In this universe, rather than being the first armor he built, the suit Tony Stark used to escape captivity from the Jade Dragons was a crude recreation of Stark's most advanced suits that he had created as part of a secret project.[63]

Marvel Cinematic Universe (Earth-199999)[]

Main article: Iron Man Armor MK I (Earth-199999)

Similarly to its Marvel Universe counterpart, the first Iron Man armor was created by Tony Stark with the help of Ho Yinsen when both inventors were captured by the Ten Rings in Afghanistan.

Instead of a 3D-knitted surface, the suit's shell was composed of scrap metal recycled from Stark Industries weaponry, making it more crude. The suit was powered by a miniature arc reactor that doubled as an electromagnet to keep shrapnel from reaching Stark's heart. Offensive capabilities included increased strength, a flamethrower and a single-fire missile launcher.

Boost thrusters could catapult Stark into the air, but not sustain flight, for which the armor shattered upon crash-landing into a sand dune after escaping the Ten Rings. The remains of the armor were recovered by the terrorists, and subsequently retrieved by Obadiah Stane. The rebuilt suit was used by Stane as a base to create his own armor.[64]

Doc Iron's Reality (Earth-200781)[]

In this reality, a suit almost identical to the Iron Man Armor Model 1 was created by Victor von Doom. The young inventor built this protective suit of armor in order to counteract the abilities of the Frightful Four, and he became Doc Iron.[65]
