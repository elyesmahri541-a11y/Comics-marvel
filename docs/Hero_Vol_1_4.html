Synopsis for "Chapter 4: Revelation/Revolution"

Cody awakens on the sidewalk of a futuristic Earth-like world and inadvertently causes a traffic accident. When a young girl is struck by a vehicle, he discovers that she and the surrounding population are machines. An android police officer identifies Cody as an "Organic" and orders his arrest, but Ivory Darc and Cody's other companions suddenly arrive and drive off the attackers long enough to escape to a nearby place of sanctuary.

Later, in Al's Air Taxi warehouse, Ivory explains that the Triad has enslaved humanity on this world and replaced humans with mechanical replicas. When Cody refuses to help unless he gets some answers, one of the mysterious gods who have been watching him appears and gives him a vision of Golgotha, home of the demon Malevelos. The vision shows Cody that Malevelos experiences pleasure when things die and seeks Devilhorn because the weapon can open a gateway allowing "Death itself" to enter the worlds of the living. Cody also learns that his repeated deaths will not permanently kill him and that when he dies at a gate between dimensions he will resurrect on a different world than the one on which he died. However, Cody still refuses to help.

As Cody's companions prepare for their mission to destroy the Triad, they are approached by Ambrose-7, a prototype droid who claims that he was created when a human brain was transferred into a droid body and that he wants to destroy the Triad because they were responsible. Ambrose-7 leads the four adventurers down into the drainage tunnels but they are all soon captured by more android police officers and Kilobots. The Triad then arrives and reveals that Ambrose-7 had been followed because he was suspected of disloyalty and that they will now all be punished.

That evening, at the taxi warehouse, a televised announcement that the renegade organics and their accomplice will be executed at dawn upsets Wafloon's barge bearers but Cody refuses to help, saying that they got themselves into trouble so they can get themselves out.

Early the next morning, at the governmental palace, the Triad visits the five prisoners who have been shackled to the roof under a group of lenses that will focus the rays from the rising sun into heat rays that will dissect them. As the sun rises, the killing rays approach the condemned but the execution is interrupted when Cody arrives in one of the Al's Air Taxis, smashes the lenses and frees the prisoners. During the ensuing battle, Gaelen uses a soul-arrow to weaken the Triad and then, despite being warned against it by Ivory, he fires another soul-arrow at the Triad, resulting in an explosion that destroys the Triad but also reduces him to a smoking skeleton. With the Patrollers now confused without the Triad to give them orders, Ambrose-7 leads Cody and the others down to the laboratory level where he shows them a platform that he claims will transport them to the slave pens where they can free the humans. However, once they're all on the platform, Ambrose-7 pulls a lever and the adventurers find that they can no longer move. Ambrose-7 reveals that the platform, instead of being a transport device, it actually a stasis beam, and that he only helped them defeat the Triad so that he could reach the lab. Declaring that he doesn't care about the other humans and only wants to be human himself, Ambrose-7 then reveals that he serves the only one who can restore his body, the Dark One...Malevelos!
