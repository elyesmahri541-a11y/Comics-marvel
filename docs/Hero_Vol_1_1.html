Synopsis for "Chapter 1: A Hero Born"

In his apartment in Hoboken, New Jersey, bonded security courier Cody Pace is dumped by his girlfriend Darleen after he breaks a third date with her in a month. Cody isn't that upset because he'd broken the date so that he could ask Judy-Ann out on Saturday in hopes of picking up some financial tips from her since her father is a big-time stockbroker. Now running late, Cody speeds to Manhattan to receive his new assignment. In an office, he meets with two members of the Petroleum Mediation League who give him a briefcase which they handcuff to his wrist and which they claim contains documents that must be hand-delivered to Al Hassan Fabib at the United nations by eleven o'clock. However, after arriving at the U.N. Plaza, a positive reading by the metal detector learns to a security scan which reveals that the briefcase actually contains a bomb. Cody tries to unlock the handcuffs but discovers that the key he was given doesn't fit, so two security guards, in order to protect the countless diplomats on-site if the bomb squad can't arrive in time, forcibly escort Cody to an underground vault designed to contain detonations of any explosives that might be found on the premises and lock him in with the bomb. Seconds later, at eleven o'clock, the bomb detonates, killing Cody by blasting his body to pieces.

Following the explosion, Cody unexpectedly awakens on the world of Peregrine Magi where he finds himself wearing unfamiliar clothing and with a strange staff-like weapon lying beside him on the ground. Seconds later, he is greeted by a young man named Ort who, after hearing about Cody's situation, suggests that he ask for help from the Slivigoth, three rather nasty wizards who rule that world. When the two of them are then attacked by strange creatures, Cody jumps to the conclusion that he didn't die and is actually in a hospital having a drug-induced dream. Believing that he's dreaming, Cody decides he might as well have some fun by joining Ort in fighting their attackers. Despite having some initial success, Cody is soon knocked down and forced to pick up the weapon that was beside him to defend himself, and the surviving attackers are quickly forced to flee. Ort then reads an inscription of the battlepike that identifies it as Devilthorn. When it then starts raining and Ort uses his individual deflection spell to keep from getting wet, Cody just thinks that his dream is getting weird.

Elsewhere, on another plane of existence, four gods who have been observing these events in a giant crystal ball discuss how the only hope to defeat Malevelo and the fate of the entire universe rests on the shoulders of a man who won't even take his own plight seriously.

Back on Peregrine Magi, the two travelers spot a building through the rain which Ort identifies as a gambling den with a bad reputation and advises against going there, However, as Ort had secretly expected, this only causes Cody, who is cold, wet and tired, to insist on going there. Once inside, Cody finds himself in a bar full of non-human creatures that reminds him of a George Lucas movie. When they are greeted by a four-armed four-limbed creature with eight eyes on its face, Cody panics and cuts off the greeter's head with Devilthorn, but the being remains alive, picks up its still-talking head and escorts them to a table where they're soon served some dragon brew. Soon afterwards, Ivory Darc arrives and demands blood rites on the Owkon clan. Ort explains that this means she's seeking retribution for the death of a member of her hive and points out a number of large and strong-looking Owkons who have risen to face her. Ort suggests that the lady might be in trouble, but Cody just replies that it's better that it's her trouble instead of his. Once the greeter, whose head is back in place, accepts pre-payment for damages from both Ivory Darc and the Owkons, the battle is about to begin. Ort again suggests that maybe they should help but Cody still refuses, so Ort secretly signals Ivory who then makes sure that her first blow causes an Owkon to fall backwards so that Ort can covertly trip him into falling against their table. When that Owkon gets up and accuses Cody of siding with the hiver, Cody, who still thinks he's dreaming, allows himself to be run through with a sword. The pain Cody feels causes him to realize that his nightmare is really happening so he begins fighting alongside Ort and Ivory Darc, but it soon becomes clear that there are too many Owkons present for them to defeat. Following Cody's suggestion that they get out of the gambling den, the trio leave through the back door which Cody jams shut. However, Ort then begins to look unwell and, while apologizing to Cody for not telling him about a little "problem" that he has, turns into a giant dragon-like monster. With the Owkons now at the other end of the alley, Cody and Ivory Darc are trapped between them and the dragon.

Solicit Synopsis

Meet the most unlikely hero of all in this saga of a man's unending obligation to combat evil — an obligation that begins after he is killed!

Trivia

  • The series was conceived many years before its actual realease. For example, back in 1987's Amazing Heroes Preview Special #5 the series was described as "long-promised" and "in complete limbo in present" because of Michelinie being busy with the Amazing Spider-Man and Iron Man series.
