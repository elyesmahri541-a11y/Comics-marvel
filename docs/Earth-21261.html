History

In this reality, upon being created by Hank Pym, Ultron killed his maker. The rest of Earth's heroes soon fell to an army of Ultron Drones, and Ultron took over the world. After Earth-21261 was destroyed by an incursion, its remains were salvaged by Doctor Doom and used to build Battleworld, becoming the realm of Perfection, ruled by Ultron.[1]
