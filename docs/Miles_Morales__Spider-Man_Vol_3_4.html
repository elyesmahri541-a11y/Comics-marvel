CAUGHT IN A TRAP!

SPIDER-MAN knows a trap when he sees one, but this time, he has no choice. His new foe, REVENANT, forged by the KINGPIN, found a way to hurt him by targeting someone that Spidey may trust — MILES MORALES! And that means collateral damage. To beat this super villain, Spidey will have to think like a super villain. But can Spider-Man stay in control, or does he risk losing himself in this darkness?
