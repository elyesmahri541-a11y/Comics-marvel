History

Lockdown noted that it was a busy night as the authorities also picked up the Ghul-Guys as well as Gyre of the Twisted Sisters. Using her invisibility powers, Stormy broke the rest of her team and Gyre free. Grateful for the rescue, Gyre offered the Tetrad safe haven with the Twisted Sisters. They were taken to a nightclub where the Twisted Sisters hid. There they were offered an alliance between the two groups. Reed's recollection ended as his "Tetrad" and the Twisted Sisters were about to break into Astra Techtronics. With his team calling him a traitor and the Twisted Sisters vowing revenge, Reed silently reflected on the fact that this was the only way he could think to potentially save them all.[1]

Elsewhere in the women's section of Bedlam, the Twisted Sisters broke free from their cells and vowed to get revenge against "Richard Reed" for betraying them. The rest of the Fantastic Four got to the women's section but were too late to stop the Twisted Sisters from utilizing their powers to transform Sue into one of their own. Sue was transformed into a new form they called Torque. However instead of fighting alongside the Twisted Sisters "Stormy Sue" turned on them because she missed her man.[2]
