History

Gladstone-Loki sought revenge against his cousin Donald-Thor after being banished to the Island of Silence to tidy up his prizes from the Nine Realms lottery. Loki witnessed his cousin on Midgard on a date with Daisy-Sif and decided to control local hero Goofy-Hulk making him ruining Duckburg's bicentenary anniversary. When Donald heard the news, he started looking for Goofy, but he found an illusion of him instead; understanding Gladstone was responsible for that, Donald went to the Island of Silence where Loki attacked him, but Donald eventually defeated him. Gladstone was brought to Scrooge-Odin who forced him to polish his treasures.[1]
