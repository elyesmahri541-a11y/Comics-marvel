History

The Burglar shares a similar history to his Earth-616 counterpart until he broke into the Parker residence. Instead of killing Ben Parker, he killed Aunt May and escaped into a neighboring warehouse. He was pursued by Spider-Man and Uncle Ben. Spider-Man confronted him in a rage and freaked out the Burglar so much he stumbled out a window and seemingly died from the fall.[1]

Trivia

  • The burglar's death is similar to his Earth-96283 counterpart's death in the Spider-Man 2002 film. This is even pointed out by the narrators.
