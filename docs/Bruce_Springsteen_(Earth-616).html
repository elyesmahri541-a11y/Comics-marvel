History

Bruce Springsteen was a renowned rock musician whose songs remained widely recognized and appreciated.

On one occasion, a melancholy Drax the Destroyer accompanied a cassette recording of Springsteen's "The River" by playing along on the saxophone. Despite his transformation into the immensely powerful Drax the Destroyer, he recalled that before his new existence he had been an accomplished saxophonist, a talent he had never entirely lost.[1]

Springsteen's music also found its way into everyday life through Kate Bishop, who used "Born in the U.S.A." as the ringtone on her smartphone whenever she received a call from Captain America.[2]
