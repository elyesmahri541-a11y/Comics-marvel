X-Men Unlimited Comic Books

X-Men Unlimited was the title of three comic book series published by Marvel Comics.

The second run, which ran from early 2004 to early 2006, is more of a collection of short stories (2 in each issue) that fit in between the other X-Men titles currently published. Like an anthology title, these stories focus less on the main continuity and more on little side things that the X-Men characters go through, like Jubilee in a mall with a crush on a boy or Wolverine going through a particularly rough healing process. The series' final issue was in April 2006, although no one realized for another month or two due to lack of publicity or mention.

The series was revived as an Infinity Comic in 2021, now set in the Reign of X era.
