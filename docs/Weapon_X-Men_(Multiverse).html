History

After Onslaught-696 escaped into the Multiverse, destroying his Earth in the process, Phoenix-empowered Jean Grey from Earth-80777 assembled a team to hunt him down and willing to do what's necessary. To that end, she recruited five Wolverines from different realities and different points in their respective timelines: Weapon X from Earth-295, Old Man Logan from Earth-807128, Earth X Wolverine from Earth-9997, Jane Howlett from Earth-1281, and Zombie Wolverine from Earth-2149, whose undead hunger she temporarily suppressed with the Phoenix power. Explaining the danger that Onslaught posed to every reality and stressing that the Wolverines needed to make the hard choice when the time came, Jean send them to Earth-93068 where she believed Onslaught to be hiding.

The Wolverines met that world's Uncanny Avengers and explained to them the threat Onslaught posed to their world. Jean-93068 used the Cerebro to scan every mind on Earth, but found no trace of Onslaught. It was all too late found that Onslaught was not hiding in any one mind, but in almost every mind of the planet, having already taken over over billions of humans and superhumans alike. Jean-93068 ordered Spider-Man to initiate the Badoon Protocol: in the event of an extinction-level event, the magic users of Earth-93068 would create portals to rapidly evacuate the civilian population to a safe world through the Nexus of All Realities. Onslaught revealed itself, and the Wolverines battled the possessed heroes of Earth-93068 until Zombie Wolverine decided to use his Hunger Virus as a weapon, creating a horde of zombies that attacked and infected Onslaught-possessed heroes. With the aid of Phoenix-80777, Magik, Doctor Strange and others sorcerers of Earth-93068 were able to evacuate over a billion people in a matter of minutes before their entire planet was destroyed by Onslaught.[1]

Phoenix-80777 had extracted the Wolverines and revealed to them that Onslaught was next targeting her homeworld of Earth-80777.[1] Accompanying the team this time, she led them to the home of her family, where the engagement party of Scott and Madelyne's son Nathaniel Summers and Logan's daughter Amiko was taking place. Recruiting the support of the Sorcerer Supreme Illyana Rasputina, Phoenix and the Wolverines tracked Onslaught to the Savage Land, but walked into a trap, and Illyana was incapacitated. Realizing that Onslaught was targeting her family, Jean rushed to the engagement party, where she suspected that Onslaught was hiding inside Madelyne. However, Nathaniel was the one who was actually possessed by Onslaught, who managed to get a hold of Jean and started absorbing her Phoenix power. He was only stopped when Earth-X Wolverine sliced off Nathaniel's arm, prompting Onslaught to flee once again. Earth-80777 was saved, but Nathaniel was mortally wounded and had to be infected with Warlock's Techno-Organic Virus in order to survive, leading to Jean relationship with her family strained even further.[2]

Continuing the hunt for Onslaught, the Phoenix send the Wolverines to Earth-31974. This time, Onslaught immediately revealed itself to them, and after effortlessly fighting off their attacks had proposed a truce. Onslaught explained that had to this world to realize the dream of his foundational persona Magneto and Jean Grey: make all mutants immortal, so that they could inherit the Earth. He performed a spell using the Lifeline Tablet, but the Tablet was seized and the spell went wrong, turning every human and mutant immortal but not invulnerable nor immune to pain. The pain of those who were suffering but could not die had crippled and debilitated Onslaught, who reached out to the Wolverines for help, knowing they would not leave an innocent world to endless suffer. Jane Howlett proposed a deal: they would only help if Onslaught promised to never interfere with any of their home world, including Phoenix's, and Onslaught accepted. He gave them a fragment of the Lifeline Tablet, which the Wolverines tracked to the Bloodstone Manor. There, they found Ulysses Bloodstone firmly possessed by Ulluxy'l Kwan Tae Syn who had entered into an alliance with Dracula and Selene. The three of them had interfered with Onslaught's ritual and stole the Lifeline Tablet to create a world full of undying prey to endlessly feast upon. The Wolverines attacked the villains, and during the battle Jane Howlett smashed the Tablet and broke the spell, restoring Earth-31974 to normal.[3]

Onslaught made a secret offer to Old Man Logan: remove the others from his path in exchange to being taken back in time to before he had killed all of his friends.[3] Making a hard choice of his own, the Old Man accepted. On yet another Earth he had incapacitated his teammates and send a mental distress call to the Phoenix. She arrived to back up her team, only to be literally stabbed in the back. Onslaught gave the wounded Jean an Ultimatum: surrender her Phoenix Force willingly, or he would have to destroy the world they stood on to rip it away from her. Backed into a corner, Jean accepted and passed the Phoenix Force she spend decades saving lives with over. This had an instant effect on Onslaught, negating the pain of his Jean Grey and Magneto and helping him realize the horrors of what he was doing. Old Man Logan confirmed that this was his plan all along, seeing as there was no way for them to beat Onslaught in direct confrontation. Jean-80777 commended him on his choice and said goodbye to her Phoenix Force, who departed with Onslaught to explore the Multiverse. With her remaining power, Jean send Jane, Weapon X and Earth-X Wolverines back home, send Zombie Wolverine to Earth-91 where his bites could not hurt anyone and stuck around with Old Man Logan for a while to explore the world in which they two and their friends all had their happy endings.[4]

Notes
