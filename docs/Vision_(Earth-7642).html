History

Vision was a member of the Avengers.[1] One day, Empress Lilandra of the Shi'ar Empire sent a distress call to the Avengers, so they sent Vision, Captain Marvel, and Hulk to Chandilar. Unfortunately, Doctor Doom intercepted this distress call. Now knowing that the Avengers were down three heavy hitters, he planned to send his entire Doombot armada to New York, forcing the Avengers to hold auditions for replacement members, which led to many of the Muppets applying for the job.[2]
