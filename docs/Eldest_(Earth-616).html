History

Origins[]

The being known as Eldest is the firstborn of Vinruviel, the Mother of Horrors, an ancient being that was not created by the One Above All. When her mother tried tò change the Natural course of earth by corrupting his children and filling it with her creations (Udru,Ngathil,ecc...) the One Above All split Earth in half in order to see what was going on, so Eldest's mother, Vinruviel, took this opening to infect him with Her corruption. He manifested as the One Below All, later destroying Udru[10][11] and trapping the Mother of Horrors in a prison that could only be opened by its own power.[3]

Ancient Times[]

Lycana[]

Eldest survived and eventually had a daughter named Lycana, after desecrating the body Asgardian god Agmundr while in a raven form.[12][13]

Lycana was later slain by Thor. Her body was skinned and devoured by a tribe of cavemen, leading to the creation of the Skinwalkers. She was worshipped as a goddess by them in a temple[12][13] underneath what would one day become Las Vegas, Nevada.[14]

Nineveh[]

By 1600 BC, Eldest had moved to Assyria and had taken control of the city of Nineveh with the help of many of her siblings.[citation needed] One account stated that she swept into the city during the night, and killed the king and his soldiers.[2] Eldest herself mentioned she had laid siege to the city, and that Nineveh's greatest warrior, Sennacherib had come out to challenge her, thinking invincible thank to his sword and breastplate of finest iron.[7]

She summoned the Living City of Gologolthia,[1] whose blood and spirit imbued the stone of Nineveh, watching over its monstrous dwellers.[15] After enslaving the population, she took a child as a host, as it amused her, and was also known as the Child God.[2]

Enkidu, who was looking for a way to die, stumbled upon a group of humans in distress and, upon learning of a "child god," decided to battle it, looking for a new challenge.[2] Enkidu carved a path of blood and destruction to Eldest, who swiftly defeated him and took control of his body in order to free her mother. Moments before Eldest could free the Mother of Horrors, however, the One Below All manifested itself and burned them with Gamma flames, forcing Eldest to leave Enkidu's body, who took the chance to burn Eldest and himself in the flames, sealing their fates. Eldest's remains were later buried with Enkidu's remains and remained undisturbed for thousands of years.[3]

Modern Age[]

Hunt for the Fractured Son[]

During the Modern Age, a group of looters stumbled upon Enkidu's tomb, Eldest took her chance to possess the body of a woman who was with them and killed them all.[16] Upon learning of the Hulk's existence, she begun to kidnap Gamma Mutates,[17] such as Betty Ross,[18] and began to impersonate her, trying to convince Banner to give up being the Hulk, saying that Eldest would create new human bodies for them.[19]

Eldest kept baiting the Hulk by sending monsters to fight him and eventually a Nephilim named the Frozen Charlotte turned Charlie Tidwell, the Hulk's new companion, into a statuette[20] and Eldest, impersonating Betty, managed to convince Banner to seek her help in order to save her.[21] Once she arrived in Las Vegas, Eldest quickly defeated the Hulk and "Betty" convinced Banner to agree to Eldest's conditions,[14] but she could not possess the Hulk due to a protection spell that Doctor Voodoo had given to Banner.[22] Charlie was resurrected from the dead as part of their deal, but she was possessed by Lycana. Feeling betrayed by her daughter, she directed her pet, Teelget, to kill Charlie with him masquerading as Charlie's father, as he had previously consumed him. However, after being encouraged by Banner, Charlie killed him. Out of sheer brute force, the Hulk destroyed the Chains of Uthl, forcing Eldest and her minions to retreat.[23]

She then proceeded to kidnap other Gamma Mutates, including the Absorbing Man,[17] Skaar,[24] Doc Samson,[24] and the Abomination,[25] possessing each one of them and then forcing them to try to open Vinruviel's door, but all of them failed. So instead, they had all their gamma channeled by a Arachnis midwife, fueling the Abomination, whose corpse Eldest would use to fight the Hulk once he arrived in the Requiem Plain.[18]

Once Banner’s protection spell had weakened, Eldest took this opportunity to teleport the Hulk to New Orleans, Louisiana in order to lure him to the Strange Academy, having allied herself with the spirit of Doctor Voodoo's dead brother, Daniel Drumm. He was accompanied by the ghost of former Pinkerton agent Francis Bergeron, though Drumm quickly banished him, luring the Hulk into a room where the Arachnis midwife awaited him, devouring him to transport him to the Requiem Plain.[26] Eldest impersonated Betty again, showing him the origin of Vinruviel the Mother of Horrors and Her children. The Hulk found a weakened Doctor Voodoo as he came to rescue him. However, the Hulk saw Betty's corpse in one of the Arachnis midwife's paws, realizing immediately that the one accompanying him was not her.[18]

The Hulk then proceeded to confront Eldest, who claimed that by draining the Hulk's blood she would become powerful enough to free the Mother of Horrors. The Hulk killed her, but Eldest then possessed the Abomination's corpse, facing off against the Hulk. She then actually, physically ripped Banner out from inside the Hulk, possessing his flesh and finally opening Vinruviel's door, which Eldest reluctantly recognized that only the Hulk could open. However, Vinruviel was unresponsive, having been discouraged by the torture that the One Below All had inflicted upon Her back then and the centuries of waiting. In a rage, Eldest proceeded to both tear out and eat chunks of Vinruviel's corpse, assimilating the Mother of Horrors' powers. Deciding to carry on Her legacy, she became the Infernal Hulk.[27]

New Age of Monsters[]

Now as the Hulk, Eldest next emerged in Red Creek, Kentucky, being monitored by the National Guard commanded by David Bridger. Meeting with them, Eldest decimated the soldiers. Recognizing Jinni Dagaal behind Bridger's eye, she decided to take him with her, along with Travis Dorsey (whom Bridger shot dead as Eldest was changing him). She then summoned the Living City of Gologolthi and made it both her new base of operations[1] and her Sanctuary for the new Age of Monsters.[15]

After establishing her presence, Eldest travelled to Atlantis, intending to awaken her long-dormant brother Ngathil the God-Eater. After succeeding in doing so, her brother questioned her authority and tried to devour her alive. Eldest made short work of Ngathil's insides and, after impaling him with an Atlantean column, she said that she did not awaken him to kill him, but to make him stronger. She empowered him with the corruptive seed of the Mother of Horrors, asked for his obedience once again, and ordered him to gather the Icthari, reclaim the oceans, attack the mortals and their ships, and trap them on land. When congratulated by Namor the Sub-Mariner, thinking she was the normal Hulk, Eldest replied that she only sent Ngathil away because she didn't cared for Atlanteans yet, but that she would return when their deaths would interest her.[6]

Next, Eldest targeted the skies, notably attacking an U.N. plane transporting an ambassador. Air travel was consequently closed down over the North Atlantic.[28]

As she was attacking Cincinnati, Ohio, and as the Avengers were deployed to save as many civilians as possible, Eldest was attacked by a vessel of the One Above All. Despite being extremely powerful, the vessel was torn apart by Eldest.[29]

Personality

You think this stripling mortal form is all that i am? This flesh is but a garment. See my true form, little thing. There be stronger things in the dark than thee.

—Eldest[source]
Eldest is a cruel being that sees humans as little more than clothes to be worn and she only feels genuine affection for her fellow monsters, especially her mother Vinruviel, her pet Teelget, and her daughter Lycana.[23] She is truly a misanthrope and seeks only to free her mother from her prison, wanting nothing more than to destroy both humanity and the One Above All. In spite of this, she can recognize when a mortal was more of a monster then any of them. She ultimately respects Bruce Banner, recognizing the suffering that he had endured and the Lovecraftian rage that he had felt his entire life, in addition to committing such horrendous acts of which, according to Eldest, even her mother, Vinruviel Herself, would be real proud.[25]

Attributes

Powers

As the first born of the Mother of Horrors, Eldest is a powerful being gifted with many superhuman abilities.

  • Immortality: Eldest has lived for thousands of years and can survive even without a body.[3]
  • Possession: Eldest can take control of another being's body either through spiritual possession[16] or by entering it like a parasite.[3]
  • Pyrokinesis: Eldest can generate and project flames from her body.[2][3]
  • Energy Projection: Eldest can project powerful blasts of energy from her hands capable of harming even the Hulk.[14]
  • Flight: Eldest can move through the air at great speed.[3]
  • Superhuman Strength: Eldest possesses incredible physical strength and could easily punch through Enkidu's chest.[3]
  • Invulnerability: Eldest possesses incredible resistance to physical damage and was completely unhurt by the blows of an enraged Hulk.[14]
  • Shapeshifting: Upon entering a body, Eldest can reshape it at will.[3]
  • Elasticity: Eldest can stretch her body like rubber.[3]
    • Size Alteration: Eldest can grow to gigantic size with ease.[14]
  • Telekinesis: Eldest can manipulate matter with her mind.[30]
  • Magic: Eldest has magic of her own, as she was able to actually, physically tear Bruce Banner out from inside the Hulk.[25]

Gamma Mutate Physiology: After possessing the body of the Hulk Eldest obtained all of his superhuman abilities.[25]
