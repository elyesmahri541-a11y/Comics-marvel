History

He attended Prince Thor's party in Las Vegas. And after another party in Sydney, Australia, Thor wanted everyone to help him clean up the mess, to which Nebula refused, claiming that her father was calling her. She and the guests eventually changed their minds when Thor warned them that his mother Frigga was coming, and helped clean up the mess on Earth.[1]
