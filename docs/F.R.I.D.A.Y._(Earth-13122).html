History

F.R.I.D.A.Y. was Tony Stark's new artificial intelligence program following the upload of J.A.R.V.I.S. into the body of the Vision. Stark used her during the final battle between the Avengers and Ultron in Sokovia.[1][2] Since then, F.R.I.D.A.Y. has been loaded into the computer systems of Stark's Malibu mansion.

At the Malibu mansion, F.R.I.D.A.Y. witnessed Justin Hammer's dance battle with the player, in which she was impressed by the player's moves and said she wished she had legs to dance with. She also witnessed Amadeus Cho and the player destroy Iron Man's out of control armor, instructing them not to break anything.[1]

During the fight against the hydra, Tony asked F.R.I.D.A.Y. to play music.[3]

Notes

  • Elle Newlands voices F.R.I.D.A.Y. in LEGO Marvel's Avengers.

Trivia

  • During Free Roam in Stark's Malibu mansion, F.R.I.D.A.Y. occasionally talks to the player. In one instance, she jokingly offers to tell them what "F.R.I.D.A.Y." stands for before admitting it doesn't actually stand for anything.
