Trivia

  • Many of the suit styles were references to other costumes.
    • Style 1 of Peter's Advanced Suit 2.0 had the color scheme of the Earth-51838 Peter Parker's advanced suit.
    • Style 1 of the Black Suit and Style 2 of the Symbiote Suit were both based on the color scheme of Randy Schueller's original design for Spider-Man's Black Suit.
    • Style 1 of Peter's Classic Suit matched that of Web-Man.
    • Style 1 of Peter's Spider-Man 2099 Black Suit had the suit's original color scheme: black and red.
    • Style 2 of Peter's Scarlet Spider Suit was similar Chasm, another of Ben Reilly's codenames.
    • Style 3 of Peter's Superior Suit was based on Superior Octopus' Suit.
    • Style 3 of Peter's Iron Spider Armor had the black and gold look the armor had during Sinister Six Reborn.
    • Style 2 of Peter's New Blue Suit resembled the Spider-Man of Earth-808122's Fantastic Four uniform.
    • Style 1 of Classic Black Suit had the purple tint of the Venom Symbiote of Earth-1610, while style 3 was shaded blue and red like the Venom Symbiote of Earth-92131 had when bonded to Eddie Brock.
    • Style 4 of the Last Stand Suit had the color scheme of Arachno-Man.
    • Style 3 of Miles' Family Business Suit resembled the design of the Iron Spider Armor during Carnage Reigns.
    • Style 3 of Miles' Sportswear Suit had the color scheme of the Earth-1610B Miles Morales' suit in Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse.
    • Miles' Forever Suit was based on the Panther Habit of Earth-199999. Style 1 had the color scheme from Captain America: Civil War, Style 2 had gold highlights, like N'Jadaka and Shuri's suits, while style 3 had purple highlighhts, like T'Challa's suit.
    • Miles' Best There Is Suit had the style of Wolverine's other suits. In order: classic brown and gold, X-Force, and classic yellow and blue.
