Synopsis for "Winter Carnival"

Reprint of the 4th story from
Bizarre Adventures #27
During Dartmouth College's Winter Carnival, visiting student Bobby Drake helps stop a robbery and enjoys acting as a solo hero.

Solicit Synopsis

• The final explosive issue in Brian Michael Bendis’ epic X-Men saga!

• Scott Summers – savior or destroyer? Questions will be answered!

• The Jean Grey School, the New Xavier School – it’s all going to change.

• The X-Men are at a crossroads. And not everyone will make it through in one piece.

• Featuring 40 pages of a host of popular X-Men creators!

Notes

  • Illyana states that she is now training with Stephen Strange, and regrets not having formally trained as a sorcerer as a child. Strange began teaching her in Uncanny X-Men (Vol. 3) #7.
  • "Winter Carnival" was originally published in Bizarre Adventures #27. Pinup by Paul Gulacy
