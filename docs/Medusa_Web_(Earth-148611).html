History

The Medusa Web is a covert group operating internationally, led by a man named Thame Panagitis (Codenamed Babel) which employs several paranormal agents as well as an army of mercenaries. It began operating some time before the White Event which granted paranormal abilities to many humans and boosted the abilities of those already existing, and Thame has claimed that despite its appearing to be a mercenary group, it actually exists to save the world by uniting warring nations against itself. The paranormal youths known as the Psi-Force were hunted by the Medusa Web on the orders of the US Government, but later became freelance operatives for the organization.
