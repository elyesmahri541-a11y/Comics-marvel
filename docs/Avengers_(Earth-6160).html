History

All Power to the People[]

When Iron Lad launched the Ultimates 3.0 initiative, Luke Cage started supervising a rehabilitation program aimed at transforming the various prisoners under his command into super heroes, whom he dubbed the Avengers.[1] This program was conceived in response to the riots caused by Cage in the Midas Group's private prison network across the North American Union,[1] which later became self-governing collectives known as the Ultimate Archipelago.[4] Although volunteers were shared, independence was maintained regarding the analogous Project 4, which would evolve over time to become the group of cosmic-empowered teams known as the Fantastic Force.[5]

Trained by Cage, Captain America, and Doom in a wide variety of subjects to maximize their efficiency for the benefit of the movement, most of the volunteers had been imprisoned for going against the status quo. Once training was complete, they were provided with a super-heroic catalyst[1] stolen from H.A.N.D. installations[5] or given superpowers by other means.[3]
