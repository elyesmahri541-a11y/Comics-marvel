History

Necroblasters are a guns wielded by Sakaarans.[2]

Alternate Reality Versions[]

Cosmic Time Heist (Earth-TRN734)[]

The Necroblasters were wielded by the Sakaarans of the past, who travelled to the present with them. As the Sakaarans were Snaped out of existed by Iron Man, the necroblasters also got snaped.[3]

Star-Lord T'Challa (Earth-21818)[]

The Necroblasters were wielded by Sakaarans working for Ronan. Korath was sent along with the Sakaarans to Morag to retrieve the Orb which contained the Power Stone. The Sakaarans fought against Star-Lord using them. After Korath joined T'Challa, the Sakaarans began working for the Collector. As Star-Lord and other stormed Collector's Museum, looking for the Embers of Genesis, the Sakaarans fought against them using the guns.[4]

Sanctum Infinitum Prisoners (Unknown Reality)[]

The Sakaarans along with their Necroblasters from an unknown reality were kidnapped by Strange Supreme and imprisoned at the Sanctum Infinitium. They along were the other prisoners were intended to be fed into the Forge by Strange in order to restore his reality. However, they along with they rest of he prisoners were broken out by Captain Carter and ended up fighting against each other with the Necroblaster-wielding Sakaarans using them to fight. During Strange's fight with Carter and Kahhori, he summoned all the prisoners to Forge to be consumed by it. However, he was stopped by Carter using the Time Stone, which was in turn stopped by Strange with his Time Stone within the Eye of Agamotto. They were all eventually sent back to their reality of origin by Kahhori including the Sakaarans along with their Necroblasters.[5]

Notes

  • They are powered by Endoplasm.[6]
