History

The Avengers of the Undead were the undead counterparts of the Avengers who served the will of the Undead Celestial on Earth-666. Revering the cosmic being, they became the guardians of the Orb of Necromancy after Captain Britain entrusted the artifact to them to keep it out of Father's hands, fearing it could be used to grant true life to additional Descendants. The Orb was ultimately concealed by Brother Voodoo within Shuma-Gorath's Ego Maze, where it rested under the Undead Celestial's protection.[1]

When Captain Britain and Hawkeye traveled to Earth-666 seeking the Orb to stop Father's plans, they were met with open hostility by the undead Avengers. Brother Voodoo nevertheless escorted them through the Ego Maze, explaining that the Undead Celestial would never heed the requests of the living. As the visitors reached the final chamber, the Avengers of the Undead turned on them, with Thor striking Captain Britain from behind before the rest of the team joined the attack. During the confrontation, Wolverine revealed that the Undead Celestial intended to use the Orb of Necromancy to spread undeath throughout reality.[2]

Despite resistance from both the Undead Celestial and its champions, Hawkeye succeeded in retrieving the Orb with one of his arrows, allowing Captain Britain to escape with it. Brother Voodoo sealed their attempted route through Otherworld, forcing the fugitives deeper into Earth-666, where Thor's attack sent them crashing into a vampire nest. Reinforcements from the living universe eventually arrived, leading to further clashes in which Captain Britain defeated the Punisher while Hawkeye used an arrow containing synthetic sunlight to reduce Wolverine to a skeleton before escaping once more with the Orb.[3]

Back at their headquarters, the Avengers of the Undead discussed the consequences of losing the artifact, acknowledging that without it the dead of their world would remain forever at rest while life continued to spread unchecked. As they prepared to recover the Orb, Captain Britain, Hawkeye, and Beast unexpectedly returned, apparently offering to surrender it in exchange for safe passage home. When Brother Voodoo reached for the Orb, Captain Britain activated its power, restoring Voodoo to mortal life, while Hawkeye imprisoned him within a force field. Deprived of Voodoo's magic, Captain Britain reclaimed the Orb, opened a portal back to his own reality, and escaped with his allies before the Avengers of the Undead could stop them.[4]
