History

Smith was an operative of the Brand Corporation who met with Miguel Rodriguez, also known as the Tarantula, at a restaurant in Miami Beach. Smith hired Rodriguez to assassinate Nose Norton, offering him $250,000 for the job. Norton had obtained information about the Brand Corporation and was believed to possess documents that the organization wanted to recover. Smith's involvement in the arrangement was part of the corporation's efforts to silence Norton and prevent the information from coming to light.[1]
