History

She was one of the Squadron Supreme clones made by Dr. Mortimer Dauoi. She came into conflict with the Guardsman Alpha Squad. After destroying Mainframe in the main entrance of Project P.E.G.A.S.U.S. Facility, she died when Jack of Hearts pulled the zero energy out of her.[1]
