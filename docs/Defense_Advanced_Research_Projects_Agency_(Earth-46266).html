History

Agent Hamilton was contacted by Monica Rappaccini who passed on to him the data she had obtained on the DNA of Bruce Banner and the chimpanzee who survived the detonation of the Gamma Bomb, but Hamilton chilled his spirits by replying that the funding for the new Super-Soldier Serum had been given to Dr. Ted Sallis.[1]
