History

Toocheetsch (Russian: Тушич) is a Soviet citizen who worked for the mercenary organization called the Medusa Web in order to help mankind and earn money to help his family leave his homeland. Toocheetsch, a kindly man, hated the violence of his job and also disliked causing property damage.[citation needed]

Attributes

Powers

Toocheetsch's great mass makes him almost impossible to move if he doesn't want to be moved and he is extremely resilient to harm.
