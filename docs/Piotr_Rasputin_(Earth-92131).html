History

A Russian farmer who left his home country after a regrettable incident with his powers and their government's equipment. He travelled to America and was mistakenly arrested for robbing a bank after he tried to stop the thief, Juggernaut.[1]

Realising that they might need his help in stopping Juggernaut, Rogue and Storm broke him free. He and the X-Men defeated Juggernaut, and Colossus refused their offer to join the X-Men, opting to return to Russia instead.[1]

Upon his return, he found that a tyrant called Omega Red had overrun the country, and he returned to America to ask the X-Men for help. He, Jubilee, Rogue, Storm and Wolverine managed to freeze the villain. Wolverine again offered him a place in the X-Men, but he had too much work to do at home.[2]

Colossus was present when a Wild Sentinel attacked Genosha, along with his sister Illyana. They tried to save civilians from the Wild Sentinel, only to witness his sister being caught in its blast, killing her.[3] Six months after the disappearance of the active X-Men roster, Colossus was listed on Forge's roster of X-Men.[4]

A few months later, Colossus has joined the Acolytes led by Exodus, and kidnapped Graydon Creed in the midst of his presidential campaign, and took him as a prisoner on their headquarters on Savage Land. The X-Men consisting of Rogue, Beast, Nightcrawler, Bishop and Polaris arrive at Savage Land to free Creed. After their jet crash-lands, Colossus finds the X-Men who shows them the Acolytes' home in an effort to foster understanding. Nightcrawler persuaded Exodus to put Creed on trial rather than execute him and promises to join the Acolytes if he failed. With Colossus presiding as judge and Exodus and Nightcrawler as counsel, Nightcrawler has Creed recount the abuse he received from their mother Mystique and his father Sabretooth for being human, swaying both the crowd and Colossus. When Colossus hesitates to deliver a verdict, Exodus attacks, prompting a battle after Polaris and Bishop destroy the power-dampening tower which restores the X-Men's powers. During the battle Colossus fought on the side of the X-Men. Following the battle, Colossus decided to return to the X-Mansion, as Nightcrawler asked him about his verdict, Colossus answered that he was never convinced but Illyana would have been.[3]

Attributes

Powers

Metal Mimicry: Colossus can transform his entire body into organic steel.[1]

  • Superhuman Strength: Due to his metallic muscles while transformed, he has enhanced strength to the point where he can bend steel.[1]
  • Superhuman Durability: His body has the toughness of steel, and he was able to withstand being hit with a truck.[1]

Notes

  • Developed for television by Larry Houston and Frank Squillace.
  • The voice of Colossus was provided by Rick Bennett in the episode "The Unstoppable Juggernaut" and by Robert Cait in the episode "Red Dawn" and X-Men '97.

Trivia

  • While Colossus never joined the X-Men during the original show, X-Men '97 implies that he eventually joined at some point between the year long gap between series, as Morph's transformation into Colossus features him in his comic counterpart's X-Men costume, also implied by his inclusion in Forge's roster later in the series.[4]
