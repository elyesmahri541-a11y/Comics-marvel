Professional History

Brian Michael Bendis is a writer who has worked on many titles for Marvel Comics. He is best known for writing Ultimate Spider-Man, Alias, New Avengers and Ultimate Comics Spider-Man. He is notable for creating the characters Jessica Jones, Spider-Man (Miles Morales), and Ironheart.

In November 2017, Bendis announced that he would be writing comics exclusively for DC Comics. He wrote Action Comics, Superman and Legion of Super-Heroes, among other series, before moving on to mostly creator-owned work. He then went on to work on Murder Inc.: Jagger Rose with artist Michael Avon Oeming for Dark Horse Comics.
