History

Mr. Brady was an attorney and insurance agent who secretly collaborated with Prince Ba'rahm in a scheme targeting wealthy women. Brady arranged insurance policies for his female clients and subsequently directed them to Ba'rahm, who used his fraudulent religious ceremonies to convince the women that they were being offered eternal life. In reality, he and his accomplices murdered the victims after persuading them to sign over their fortunes. Their bodies were later recovered from Snake River, allowing Brady to collect the insurance payouts and share the proceeds with Ba'rahm.

When Captain America and Bucky investigated the deaths, Brady pretended to have no connection with the mystic and directed them to his hideout. He subsequently attempted to conceal his involvement by setting the warehouse ablaze during the heroes' confrontation with Ba'rahm. However, Captain America struck him with his shield, causing Brady to burn his hand against an incense brazier. The injury later exposed his identity when the heroes confronted him in Manhattan. Realizing that he had been unmasked, Brady drew a pistol, but stumbled and crashed through a window, falling to his death.[1]
