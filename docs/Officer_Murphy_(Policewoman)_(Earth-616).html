History

Officer Murphy and her partner, Officer Weissman, were patrolling in squad car Unit 18 when a radio dispatch interrupted their casual conversation to report an armed robbery. Upon arriving at the scene, they found an elderly woman weeping as she held her apparently injured husband, who had been attacked by the perpetrators. While Weissman stayed with the couple, Murphy called for medical assistance. During the call, she learned over the radio that the robbers had already been apprehended and wrapped up in a web by someone who could only have been Spider-Man.[1]
