Solicit Synopsis

Scarlet Witch has fallen under the control of an ancient, mutant manipulator. Captain America and the She-Hulk are pulling out all stops to save her from the menace. The trouble is, the Scarlet Witch loves what's happened! And once you see who the manipulator is, you'll know why! Written and penciled by John Byrne and inked by Mike Machlan.[1]

Notes

  • Although Saunders had previously been identified as the town where Absolom College was located, this is the second issue in a row in which it is stated in-story that the college is in Absolom, Texas. However, while that previous story had identified "Saunders College" as being in Absolom, the Omniscient Narrative in this issue identified it as Absolom College.
  • One of humans within whom That Which Endures had already awakened was identified as Ms. Klein in this issue. A woman who looked exactly like her appeared as one of Jeremiah Random's aides in West Coast Avengers (Vol. 2) #47 but, despite having some dialogue, she wasn't named and, as such, may not have been meant to be the same character.
  • In this issue, Starfox (Eros) makes a mental reference to how he had given up his quest for his grandniece Nebula after the Avengers told him that she had been lost in time. The first in-story indication that the "Kang Nebula" who had been lost in time in Avengers #298 was going to be retconned into actually being someone else who had been posing as the space pirate Nebula did not appear until Avengers #317.
  • The ancient alien planet where Starfox spied on Nebula and Gunthar was identified as Omicron Seti in Avengers #311, the next issue in which those three characters appear. The "stones" that were found on that planet in this issue weren't identified as the Stones of Halkor until Avengers #316.

Trivia

  • Although the title on the covers of this series changed to "Avengers West Coast" beginning with the previous issue, this was the first issue whose indicia named the series as "Avengers West Coast Vol 1."
