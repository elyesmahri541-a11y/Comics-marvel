History

Spider-Man was one of the super-powered beings who somehow became a human-flesh-eating werewolf. With his fellow super-heroes, they ravaged their world, ate Galactus, and headed for Ash when he came to their world.[2]

Peter was among the heroes summoned to Earth-31913 by Anansi the Spider God. Banding together as the Web Warriors, they aided the native Web-Slinger in defending his reality from a symbiote invasion. Web-Slinger; however, didn't trust that Spider-Man could control his werewolf instincts and tried to kill him. Peter proved himself when he saved Patrick's life and they were able to work together to save the world.[1]

Parker was recruited by the S.H.I.E.L.D. of Earth-41940 into the Spider-Men, a group of Spider-Man analogs assembled to combat the Multiversal Sinister Six. He was also joined the Spider-Force and became the liaison for The Pacifists.[1]

In some point in time, Peter became apart of Miguel O'Hara's Spider Society. He was among the many spider-totems who chased down Miles Morales of Earth-1610B.[3]
