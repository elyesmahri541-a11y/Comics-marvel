History

In the era of twenty-third Ragnarök, Balder and the Warriors Three arrived to Niffleheim to assist Thor and Brunnhilda in their fight against a Frost Giant. They later celebrated together, so loudly that the locals have complained, attraction the attention of Odin.[1]
