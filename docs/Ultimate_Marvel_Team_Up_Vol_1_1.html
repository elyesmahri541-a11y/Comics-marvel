Synopsis for "Spider-Man & Wolverine"

Weapon X sends three agents, including Sabretooth to track down Wolverine and bring him back to the project. Spider-Man happens upon the mutants as they confront each other. The fight spills over into Times Square, but Spidey isn't sure who the good guy is in this situation. When the police get involved, Spider-Man flees, taking Wolverine with him. Spidey tries to get Wolverine to talk about what happened, but he refuses to talk and leaves. Peter appears to have been influenced by Wolverine's mannerisms and adopts some of his style at school, which only ends in humiliation.
