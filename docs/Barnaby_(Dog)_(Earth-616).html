History

A poster indicated that Barnaby was missing and the Thrilling Three decided that finding him was a good start to their heroic deeds.[1]

The Tsum Tsum imprinted on super-heroes found Barnaby and delivered him to Dunk's brother to take him back to his owner.[2]
