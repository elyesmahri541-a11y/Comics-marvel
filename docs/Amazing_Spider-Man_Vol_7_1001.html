Synopsis for 1st story

Following the intense aftermath of issue #1000, Peter Parker struggles to pick up the pieces of his life while facing a new, catastrophic threat in New York City. Cormac Crane, operating under the dangerous alias Ravage, launches a destructive assault across Manhattan, targeting key points in the city. Amidst the chaos, Spider-Man leaps into action to stop Ravage's rampage near Oscorp Plaza. However, the situation escalates rapidly when Aunt May becomes unexpectedly caught in the crossfire of the battle. Desperate to protect his aunt while holding off Ravage's overwhelming force, Peter is forced to push his limits, receiving unexpected magic-based assistance and guidance from the Scarlet Witch and Norman Osborn in the fray. Despite Spider-Man's best efforts to minimize the destruction and secure the safety of those around him, the encounter leaves him questioning whether he truly managed to save the day, as Ravage's attack lays the groundwork for a far more sinister conflict ahead.
