Synopsis for 1st story

After the incident at Stamford, Tony Stark recorded a message, calling for other heroes to register. Later, when Secretary Kooning offered him command of S.H.I.E.L.D., he refused. At the office of Karim Mahwash Najeeb, planned to hire the Spymaster. While planning to celebrate his anniversary with Pepper, Happy was attacked by the Spymaster and while fighting back, they both fell of a platform.
