Solicit Synopsis

Based on the story that introduced the world to the All-New, All-Different X-Men in the pages of Giant Size X-Men #1, this tale features the very first adventure as team of X-Men of Storm, Colossus, Nightcrawler, Sunfire, Banshee, and Wolverine as they unite to try and save the original X-Men from the clutches of the monstrous living-island known as Krakoa! Will the X-Men escape the island monster?

Notes

  • Part of the X-Men Digest Novels line of books.
  • This book is an adaptation of Giant-Size X-Men #1
