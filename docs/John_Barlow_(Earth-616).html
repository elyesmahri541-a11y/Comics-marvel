History

John Barlow was the deceased brother of Congressman Barlow. His supposed spirit was invoked by the Nazi spy Doctor Eternity during a fraudulent séance intended to frighten the congressman. The apparition warned Barlow that he would soon join the world of the spirits, shortly before the congressman collapsed and died.[1]
