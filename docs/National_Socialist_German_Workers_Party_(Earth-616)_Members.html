Preface[]

Below is a full list of members of the Nazi Party on Earth-616, along with a brief explanation of their roles in the war and their current status (if available). Whenever possible, profiles are linked. Otherwise, every effort is made to list the issue in which a character appeared.

For the history of the Nazi Party on Earth-616, see the abridged or unabridged histories.

High Ranking Officials[]

  • Adolf Hitler: Leader of the Nazis. Killed in his bunker by the Human Torch in 1945. Various clones survived to act as his successor.
  • Hermann Göring: Hitler's second-in-command, commander-in-chief of the Luftwaffe. He was put on trial for war crimes after the war and committed suicide before his execution.
  • Joseph Goebbels: Minister of Propaganda during World War II. Committed suicide to avoid capture in 1945.
  • Edmund Heines: Ernst Röhm's deputy in the Sturmabteilung.
  • Heinrich Himmler: Oversaw the concentration camps during World War II. Committed suicide in 1945 to avoid capture. He once transformed into the monstrous Zyklon by Satannish to battle the West Coast Avengers in the modern era.
  • Reinhard Heydrich: High-ranking German SS and police official and a main architect of the Holocaust. Visited and inspected the Sobibor extermination camp.[1]
  • Josef Mengele: A Nazi scientist who performed ghastly experiments on prisoners in the Nazi concentration camps. Avoided capture during the war and lived in Brazil until he died in 1979.
  • Ernst Röhm: Co-founder and leader of the Sturmabteilung.
  • Johann Shmidt (Red Skull): Trained by Hitler, one of the most feared Nazis. Put in suspended animation during the bombing of Berlin, revived in the modern age. Has been killed and cheated death many times. He is currently listed as deceased.
  • Arnim Zola: The Nazi's chief geneticist. Survived the war, currently at large.
  • Baron Wolfgang von Strucker: High-ranking official. Fell out of Hitler's favor, formed the terrorist organization Hydra. Survived the war and has cheated death numerous times. He is currently listed as dead, shot in the head by his arch-nemesis Nick Fury.
  • Baron Heinrich Zemo: High-ranking scientist for the Nazis, invented many weapons. He was responsible for putting Captain America in suspended animation until the modern era, and for the events that led to his partner Bucky becoming Winter Soldier. He evaded capture by relocating to South America and survived until the modern age, when he was killed in battle by a revived Captain America.

Costumed and Super-Powered Operatives[]

  • Nathaniel Essex (Mister Sinister): Operated out of Auschwitz, experimenting on the prisoners there to further his own research into human mutation. He evaded capture, has cheated death many times, and has recently resurfaced.
  • Otto Weiss: Telepath, clashed with the Sub-Mariner.
  • Wax Man: Nazi operative who held the position of mayor of a city near Camp Lehigh. Was apprehended by Captain America and Bucky.
  • George Maxon: Nazi spy who posed as the Red Skull for some of his early US operations. He clashed with Captain America and Bucky twice and was killed in their second encounter.
  • Mad Violinist: Could hit notes on his violin that would wither and kill adults. Was slain battling the Patriot.
  • Cat's Paw: Betrayed her country to work for the Nazis. Assassinated many high-profile Americans before the Angel convinced her to turn against her employers. Apparently committed suicide out of shame over her actions.
  • Python: German contortion expert who the Nazis hired to kill American targets. Clashed with the Angel twice and was incarcerated in Alcatraz. Broken out by the Nazis, he was strangled to death by the Sub-Mariner.
  • Dame Kackle: Was a Nazi smuggler who helped Imperial Japanese spies smuggle themselves into the United States. Was thwarted by the Patriot and Rusty.
  • The Fiddler: Another Nazi operative who could kill with the music played off a violin. He died battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Queen Jarna: Member of the Lavorites, an alien race that apparently lived on the planet Venus. Allied with the Nazis and helped plan an invasion of the United States. She was thwarted by the Sub-Mariner and set free following the destruction of a Nazi submarine base. Her fate is unknown.
  • Fritz Sternberg (Black Claw): A Nazi naval captain. Died battling Captain Terror.
  • Gustav Hertz (Armless Tiger Man): After losing his arms in an industrial accident, Gustav Hertz came to hate machines. He was sent to the United States by the Nazi Party and captured by the Angel. He later died and was seen among the dead souls holding court during Pluto's trial against Zeus.
  • Kurt Hausner (Yogi Zamor): Posed as a fortune teller in the United States to eliminate high-ranking American officials. Was killed battling Father Time.
  • Black Carlo (The Gargoyle Gypsy): Posed as a Gypsy to steal a new camouflage paint formula. Was apprehended by Captain Terror.
  • Pascal Horta (Black Talon): Captain America foe who joined the Nazis for the promise of rule over America should the Nazis win the war. Charged with capturing New America, clashed with the Young Allies and was apprehended.
  • Rod Elliot (Yellow Claw): Posed as American soldier Captain Rod Elliot. Killed American officials with poisoned flowers, attempted to romance Betty Ross. Died battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Hans Knutte (Spook): Attempted to turn the recently trapped Sub-Earthmen against the United States. Was stopped by Captain America, Bucky, and the Sub-Earthmen's Queen Medusa.
  • The Owl: Master of disguise. Tried to simultaneously help Imperial Japanese forces invade California and eliminate the Young Allies by using a movie production about the young heroes' adventures as cover for his plans.
  • Albri Leiricgrie (The Cobra): A costumed Nazi operative who attempted to capture the ambassador of a South American nation who was visiting the United States. He was thwarted by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • John Lissom (The Creeper): Handpicked by Hitler to cause chaos in the United States, apprehended by Captain America. Later broke out and posed as the American ambassador to Alslavia in an attempt to get them to side with Germany. Was defeated once again by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Anton Harvey (Medusa): University Professor Anton Harvey. Was a Nazi spy who used the Medusa identity to convince Americans that the Medusa of myth was real. Died battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Edward Elmgren (Doctor Crime): An adversary of Captain America. He was broken out of prison by Nazi operatives in exchange for the use of his shrinking formula. Was apparently killed in battle when a bird swooped down and carried off his miniature body.
  • Gunther Strauss (The Reaper): Rabble-rouser hired by Hitler to demoralize Americans. Was killed in a clash with Captain America and Bucky after he was outed as a Nazi spy.
  • The Eel: Destroyed American ships by having his operatives slather them with fish oil, making them targets for his massive octopus. Was killed battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Eraser: Deformed Nazi agent who had a rubber-like body. Attempted to steal the model for a prototype flying submarine. Was thwarted by Captain America and Bucky, but managed to evade capture.
  • Sol Harriman (Blue Diamond): Attempted to steal a new prototype "Puff Gun", but was apprehended by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Richardo Bennett (Ambassador of Terror): Infiltrated the United States government and attempted to sour relations between the United States and Boronia. Was exposed and subsequently died battling the Young Allies.
  • Peter Blakemann (Phantom Engineer): Caused various train wrecks along the Peconic & Western lines using his guise to leave the locals to believe it was the work of a haunted train. Was exposed as a Nazi operative by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Rabbit: Deformed spy who was hired directly by Hitler to capture Russian war hero Sonia Petroya and brainwash her into speaking well of the Nazi regime while on an American lecture tour. He was apprehended by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Ottokar Meltzer (Vulture): Developed a formula that allowed him to fly, used it to amass an army of Batmen and eliminate American officials. Defeated and left for dead by Captain America and Bucky, his ultimate fate is unknown.
  • Purple Terror: Were multiple Nazi agents who used this guise to ruin property and frighten Americans from small towns to the larger cities to sow more panic during later attacks. They were stopped by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Triple Destruction: Costumed saboteur who attempted to thwart the Whizzer by attacking three defense plants simultaneously at midnight Western Union time. The Whizzer defeated him and his men by pushing the clocks back in each building.
  • Fungi: A living biological weapon that attempted to unleash a swamp gas that would swallow American farmland up in muck and mire. Was destroyed by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Herman Schmidt (Masked Fiend): A handicapped Nazi agent who used special electrodes in his wheelchair to kill American officials while posing as a charity collector. He attempted to frame the Human Torch for the murders, but the hero ultimately exposed him, leading to the Fiend's death.
  • Mole-Man: Planted bombs beneath London that went off during air raids. Attempted to blow up 10 Downing Street but was thwarted by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Ali Baba: Was charged with assembling a group of Middle Eastern terrorists to destabilize the situation in Iraq to get the country to turn against the Allied Forces. Was slain battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Mongoose Master: Attempted to steal government secrets from WAC's stationed in Africa. Was apprehended thanks to Captain America, Bucky, and Betty Dean.
  • Slate (Glowing Death): Infiltrated the crew of a supply ship. Attempted to signal it to his fellow Nazis by coating it with illuminating paint. Was defeated by the Angel.
  • Agent of 1000 Faces: Master spy, wore many different masks. Clashed with Sgt. Fury and his Howling Commandos.
  • White Vampire: Killed her targets by dousing them with a perfume that caused her vampire bats to attack. She was apparently killed in an explosion while battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Cobra: Master of disguise who attempted to assassinate President Franklin Delano Roosevelt, Prime Minister Winston Churchill, and Josef Stalin. Was foiled by the Sub-Mariner.
  • Doctor Heinman (Zombie Master): Attempted to assassinate President Roosevelt with an army of zombies in Haiti. Was slain battling the Sub-Mariner.
  • Mad Mechanic: Nazi super-scientist who built weapons in the Swiss Alps. Was slain battling the Young Allies.
  • Black Snake: Loaned to the Japanese to assist in using a massive flying ship to brainwash American troops into thinking they were Japanese. Was slain battling the Sub-Mariner.
  • Georges Tarragh (Gargoyle): Wealthy but deformed, the Gargoyle joined the Nazis and attempted to abduct Sir Anthony Hutchins, but was apprehended and thwarted by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Man in the Steel Mask: Broke into an Incan tomb and forced a scientist to help him activate a mechanical idol. Attempted to coerce Mexican citizens into believing he was their master and launch an attack on the United States. Was thwarted by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Beast of Frankenstein: Created by Anna Frankenstein and her lover Von Rottz to eliminate Captain America and Bucky. The creature failed and was destroyed while Anna and Von Rottz lost their lives.
  • Bran Kunze (Madame X): Cross-dresser. Blackmailed female factory workers to blow up their place of employment. Was stopped by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Baron of Horror Castle: Wore armor and attempted to capture General Haywood and Sgt. Duffy. Died battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Master of Death: Used electric eels to attack ships. He was slain battling the Sub-Mariner.
  • Karl Shaffer (Shadow Monster): Posed as a painting come to life to keep people away from a Zorite mine. Committed suicide rather than face capture at the hands of Captain America and Bucky.
  • Herr Swastika: Used one-man subs disguised as turtles to destroy American supply ships along the Great Lakes; was stopped by Sub-Mariner.
  • Baron Krugg (The Green Death): Transformed by Radium into a green-skinned creature that could shoot lethal bolts of Radium from his hands, was destroyed by the Young Allies.
  • Thing From the Swamp: Was a co-pilot for Major von Kaulus on a mission to destroy an American aircraft factory. Seriously wounded when their plane was shot down in a nearby swamp, the elements in the water transformed the pilot into the Thing. Kaulus used the creature to attack the aircraft factory, but the creature was destroyed by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Kurt Bach (The Black Demon): An international terrorist hired by the Nazis to kidnap government officials and cause acts of sabotage in the United States. Was stopped by the Sub-Mariner.
  • Fencing Master: Attempted to kill high-ranking American officials in Washington, D.C. with his poison-tipped fencing sword; was stopped by Miss America.
  • Wilhelm Lohmer (Master Man): American-German Bundist who was transformed by a variation of Abraham Erskine's Super-Soldier Serum.
  • Julia Koenig (Warrior Woman): German sadist who was also exposed to a variation of Erskine's super-soldier serum.
  • Star Gods: Axi-Tun aliens brainwashed by Nazi scientist Brain Drain to support the Nazi cause.
    • MCM-XLI (Brunhilde)
    • Satorymho (Donar)
    • Xepdgin (Froh)
    • Frobbank (Loga)
  • Meranno (U-Man): Atlantean exile who joined the Nazi cause.
  • John Falsworth (Baron Blood): Brother of the original Union Jack and a vampire who betrayed his country to join the Germans during World War I and the Nazis during World War II.
  • Werner Eisen (Face): Commanded the Polish ghettos, briefly captured the Invaders.
  • Jacob Goldstein (Golem): Harnessed the power of the Golem of Jewish myth. Was forced into Nazi service by Battle-Axis.
  • Helmut von Schuler: A vampiric officer who made many victims in Belgium at the beginning of WWII.

Military Officials[]

  • General Grüber Brinkhaus: Clashed with the Sub-Mariner.
  • Major Bauman: Met Wolverine around 1942 at the Sobibor extermination camp.[1]
  • Major Von Lutz: Nazi official whom Sky-Devil's member K-4 posed as.[2]
  • Jerry Alton: A British spy who infiltrated Nazi forces. Helped jungle hero Trojak eliminate Nazi presence from his domain.
  • Kro: Member of the Deviants, briefly posed as Hitler until he was exposed and ousted by the Eternal Makkari during his tenure as Mercury.
  • Fredrich Roth (Black Ace): Member of the Luftwaffe, shot out of the sky by the Flying Flame.
  • Lt. Heinrich von Betz: Was captured while carrying out spy activities in the United States prior to America's entrance into the war. Being extradited out of the country, he attempted an escape but was stopped by the Patriot.[3]
  • Pepo Laroc: A Frenchman who became loyal to the Nazis following the occupation of France. He operated a Nazi prisoner of war camp on Devil's Island. He clashed with Captain America and Bucky when they succeeded in freeing a prisoner.
  • Lt. Kurt Mueller: A Nazi spy who convinced the Dhaka Tribe that he was their god. Attempted to steal secrets from General Haywood. Died clashing with Captain America and Bucky.
  • Muller: Major Bauman's second in command.[1]
  • Captain Ollendorff: Convinced Ali Hamud to lead the Nazis in using his palace in the Belgian Congo as an airfield. He was slain battling Ka-Zar.[4]
  • Captain Frederick von Banger: Chief of the Gestapo. Failed to stop the Destroyer during one of his earliest adventures.[5]
  • Major Pierre Dumort: Nazi operative who posed as a French resistance fighter coming to Camp Lehigh to share military secrets and switched ammunition for war-games into live rounds. He was captured by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Barracuda: AKA Captain Erich von Wolff. Took pleasure in destroying passenger ships and gunning down the innocent people aboard until he was slain by the Fin.
  • Von Maus: Member of the Gestapo and expert man-hunter and apparently Hitler's only friend. Was delivered a humiliating defeat at the hands of the Destroyer.
  • Heinrich Bungler: Aka Bungler the Butcher, a commanding officer in the Gestapo responsible for torturing and executing prisoners. Failed to stop the Destroyer from rescuing outspoken Nazi critic Father Mueller.
  • Cramm: A Gestapo agent who smuggled himself into England to assist Herr Horner in acts of sabotage was exposed by Headline Hunter.[6]
  • Heinrich Tode and his ally Dr. Drew attempted to force defected German scientist Hans Stiller to reveal his new explosive formula. They were apprehended by Major Liberty. Tode was apprehended, and Drew was killed. [7]
  • Captain von Spitz: Was a Nazi operative who helped smuggle Nazi spies into the United States. They operated from a secret hideout on Coney Island. He and his men were apprehended by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Marshal Doering: A Naval commander who was apparently Hitler's "right-hand man". Was killed battling the Sub-Mariner off the coast of West Africa.
  • Captain Achhimmel & Inga von Leche: Gestapo agents who both attempted to apprehend the Destroyer by having an actor pose as the hero to draw him out. They were fooled by the Destroyer, who posed as the German actor they hired. After Achhimmel was killed by Inga, she took his position in the Gestapo but still failed to capture the Destroyer.
  • Von Ratter & Von Rommel: Two Nazi naval captains who patrolled the waters of Africa. Both men were trounced by the Sub-Mariner, who shattered their forces and then turned the two men over to the American military.
  • Le Bull: French citizen loyal to the Nazis. Sent to the United States to steal Professor Clement Mott's Cosmic Depressor Device. Died battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Herr Gruber: Ordered by Adolf Hitler to build an underground munitions factory using slave labor. Died battling the Destroyer.
  • Captain Von Blech: AKA the "Canadian Führer", launched attacks on Canada from a secret base along the Canada/US border. Apprehended by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Herr Demon: Tortured the captured General Spencer to try and learn secrets, clashed with Captain America and Bucky.
  • Commander Kornstalk: Attempted to force Namor to ally with the Nazis by flooding Atlantis with copper particles, making his people deathly ill. Killed by Princess Fen.
  • Captain von Nieusance and Brander: Attempted to force German dissidents to reveal the location of an underground press. Were defeated by the Destroyer.
  • Colonel Schutter: Attempted to invade Switzerland and was killed in action battling the Destroyer.
  • Admiral von Roeder: Commanded an army of robots in an invasion of Atlantis, killed by the Sub-Mariner.
  • Oskar Kessler: Created an army of robots to invade Atlantis. Killed by the Sub-Mariner.
  • Captain Sturm: U-Boat captain, commanded an underwater Nazi base. Died clashing with the Sub-Mariner.
  • Captain von Sar: Smuggled himself into the United States and used American passenger planes to bomb American targets. Brought to justice by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Colonel von Steibel: Organized the creation of the Killer Beasts, biologically engineered dogs with poison glands in the mouth.
  • Captain Brool: Was posted in Nazi-occupied France to eliminate Free French resistance fighters. Defeated by the Destroyer.
  • Erwin Krause: Attempted to stop spies from fleeing Nazi Germany with stolen plans. Thwarted by the Destroyer.
  • Von Kropp: Aka "The Phantom of the Air", he and his men took over an Allied weather station in Greenland to submit false reports to the Allies. Defeated by the Sub-Mariner.
  • Felix Pavlek: Polish citizen, betrayed his country by joining the Nazis. Attempted to slay his brother during the invasion of Poland in 1939. Was slain battling the Vision.
  • Mashall Kronin: Attempted to get to the Sub-Mariner by capturing his friend Betty Dean. Defeated by Namor.
  • Colonel von Bitters: Commanded Nazi forces in the town of Blanc in France. Attempted to smash Free French resistance fighters by capturing the daughter of Frank Pireaud. His plans were foiled, and he was driven out by the Destroyer.
  • Von Broot: Attempted to destabilize relations between the United States and Barabia. Stopped by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Captain von Lontz: Battleship captain. Killed battling the Sub-Mariner.
  • Captain Kraust: Posed as Lord Mood in Britain to slay government officials. Slain battling the Angel.
  • The Vulture: Commanding officer in Nazi-occupied France. Clashed with Free French guerrilla fighters. Was slain battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Herr Klutz: Led Nazi forces into Russia in a failed invasion, killed battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • The Hawk: Airship captain, built the massive airship called "Der Tag" to launch attacks on the United States. Slain by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • General von Savage: Attempted to build an invasion tunnel to England under the Channel. Killed by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Max Bovar: Gestapo agent who attempted to force chemist Hans Dreden to reveal a secret chemical formula. Run down by a car while attempting to flee the Angel.
  • Schenker: Major Bauman's assistant.[1]
  • Colonel Strauheim: Diminutive Nazi operative, commanded a base in Brazil, captured American planes. Killed while battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Baron von Ritter: Aka the Butcher of Istanbul. Attempted to steal surveying plans from an American engineer. Committed suicide to avoid capture at the hands of the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Baron von Wetzel: Placed in charge of the "Battle of the Atlantic", designed a massive submarine that used paralyzing gas to incapacitate enemy ships. Killed while battling the Sub-Mariner.
  • General Eric Kesselwolfe: A Prussian loyalist who had no choice but to support the Nazi regime to preserve his own life. After the war, he fled to Brazil, where he was eventually captured by Miss America.
  • Wilhelm Schultz: A Nazi officer, later a fugitive (still wearing a swastika) who was tracked and executed by the Wild Pack.
  • Oberstleutnant Erik Lonnroth: A morally conflicted officer of the Luftwaffe (the German Air Force). While opposed to Hitler and the Nazi rule, he is forced to fight for the German cause. Traveled to the future to fight Harald Jaekelsson.
  • Captain Bore: A captain in the German Gestapo who disguised himself as Lieutenant Werner in order to steal a message from the Sub-Mariner. His fate after he was knocked out is unknown.

Teams[]

  • Unholy Legion: A group of Nazi spies operating in the United States who posed as homeless men to avoid detection. Used panhandling to earn money for their operations. Were exposed and dismantled by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Nazombies: A group of Nazis who posed as Zombies while operating in Bermuda to frighten the locals. They were exposed and shut down by the Sub-Mariner and the Angel.
  • The original Circus of Crime (Fritz Tiboldt, Zandow, Trapeze Trio, Omir, and Tommy Thumb) was sent to the United States to eliminate high-ranking military officials. They were thwarted by Captain America and Bucky. For his failure, Fritz Tiboldt was killed.
  • Druids of Rockhenge: A group of Druids who hid from society in a secret underwater domain. Discovered by the Nazis they were convinced to let them use their home as a staging ground for attacks on the United Kingdom. They soon realized the Nazis were exploiting their resources and assisted the Sub-Mariner in ousting them from their domain.
  • A group of Nazis posed as Martian in an attempt to create the same sort of panic caused by the radio broadcast of War of the Worlds had. This plot was exposed and foiled by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Followers of the Yellow Hoods: A Nazi group that impersonated and infiltrated their way into allying themselves with a group of amphibious underground dwellers called Lai-Sonians from the Island of Lai-Son. They briefly took over the town of Valley Port and were seemingly wiped out by Captain America, Bucky, and Betty Ross.
  • Cult of the River God: A cult that worshiped a "River God". Formed by a Nazi sympathizer named Dolvin who wished to affect American morale by making it appear as though a water god was flooding the Ohio River. The cult was smashed, and Dolvin drowned at the hands of the Sub-Mariner.
  • Carnival of Doom: Nazi spies who posed as actors in an anti-Nazi play mocking the Nazi regime in order to murder the cast and crew. They were slain by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Batmen: Minions of the Vulture. Forced to do his bidding due to the lethal withdrawal from his flying formula. Left to die by Captain America and Bucky.
  • The Hours (I, II, III, IV, V, VI, VIII, VIIII, IX, X, XI, XII, and Der Tag): Clock-themed saboteurs who attempted to blow up every clock tower in New York. They were thwarted by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • The Gargoyles: Insane asylum patients from Vicy, France dressed as gargoyles and set upon the people to make them loyal to their German oppressors. Freed during a battle between the Nazis versus Captain America and Bucky, which lead to them being slaughtered by Nazi soldiers.
  • League of Hate: Handpicked by Hitler, these men posed as wounded American soldiers to sneak into the United States and stir up racial tensions in the country. The group was exposed by Captain America (Nasland) and Bucky (Davis).
  • Blitzkrieg Squad: A group of Nazi soldiers hand-picked by Baron Strucker to be a counterforce to Nick Fury and his Howling Commandos.
  • Super-Axis: A team of mostly Nazi operatives gathered by Lady Lotus to battle the Invaders.
  • Battle-Axis: A team of failed American super-heroes who were convinced to join the Nazi cause by Dr. Death. Their attempt to cause an earthquake of enough force to sink the American west coast was foiled by the Invaders.
  • Ubermadchen: A group of female Nazi loyalists. They battled Miss America during World War II. Escaping capture during the war, they were recruited into ICON in the 1950s until they were all slaughtered by Sabretooth.

Spies and Saboteurs[]

  • Dr. Vee: Was a saboteur active in the United States. He was apprehended by the Dynamic Man.
  • Herr Broen, Stohl, and Shultz were spies that were stopped by Marvel Boy.[8]
  • Major Albrecht Kerfoot: Impersonated Prof. Hamilton.[9]
  • Pinhead: Brilliant Nazi saboteur, active in New York City. Killed battling the Human Torch.
  • Karl Schwab: American criminal who allied with the Nazis after being released from prison. Died battling the Angel.
  • Heinz Kruger: the legendary spy that managed to infiltrate Operation Rebirth, killing scientist Abraham Erskine, was instantly killed by Steve Rogers following the success of the Super-Soldier formula.
  • Sando and Omar were saboteurs who posed as fortune tellers to lower American morale. They were captured by Captain America, Bucky, and FBI agent Betty Ross.
  • Craig Talbot: A member of a Nazi Bundt and Hollywood actor. Attempted to sabotage an anti-Nazi film but was brought to justice by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Eric von Himmel and his cohorts sabotaged American munitions plants but were apprehended by the Young Avenger.[10]
  • Lord Wotonby: A British citizen who betrayed his country. He used his own munitions plant to build weapons for the Nazis for an attack on British soil. He was killed clashing with American reporter Headline Hunter.
  • Lens: Spy active in the United States attempting to smuggle poison laced gems into the United States to kill Americas wealthy. He was brought to justice by the Whizzer.
  • Herr Huss: Attempted to cause acts of sabotage within the United States by posing as a Nazi defector. Exposed by the Patriot, and died battling the hero.
  • Countess Mara: Led Nazi operatives in attempting to steal a rapid fire grenade launcher. She was stopped by Captain America and Bucky and apparently extradited back to Germany.
  • Wilhelm von Logor: Spy who posed as British reporter Will Jenks. Attempted to assassinate government official Reginald Whitney but was foiled by Headline Hunter.
  • Saint: Master archer charged infiltrating the Raleigh Tank Factory to steal plans. Was apprehended by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Baron Boche: Hired the Black Talon to secure a source of minerals for the Nazis. Was killed in a clash with the Young Allies.
  • Guy Stanford: Publisher of the Tattler Newspaper in Britain. Betrayed his country to assist the Nazis in a plan to flood England with a toxic gas. Attempted to frame Deadline Hunter with giving secrets to the Nazis. Deadline cleared his name, alerted the people of the poison and how to protect themselves. Stanford was killed in the subsequent clash.
  • Dr. Felix Sak: Immigrated to the United States from Germany to get an education. Allowed the Nazis to build a secret submarine dock beneath his island home off Long Island. Was killed battling the Sub-Mariner.
  • Herr Horner: Attempted to commit acts of sabotage in Britain by posing as a defector, was caught by Headline Hunter.
  • Gondor: Operated as a mortician in California. Helped Imperial Japanese agents smuggle themselves into America in the coffins of dead American soldiers. Was exposed and brought to justice by the Angel.
  • Baron Girbel: Attempted to smuggle Nazi spies into the United States through South America but he was stopped by El Gaucho who turned him over to the American authorities.
  • Friz Krone: Fifth Columnist. Attempted to demoralize Americans by staging a phony invasion of "Sub-Earthmen" who were really Nazi agents in disguise. His plans were foiled by Captain America and Bucky who turned him over to the authorities.
  • Grandma: Was the nickname for an elderly Nazi operative who attempted to stop the Secret Stamp in order to counter the inspiration he was giving young Americans. The Stamp put a stop to her plans.[11]
  • Von Brukk: Attempted to destroy the Sky City Plane factory. Was killed during this operation while clashing with the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Kurt Weidner: Smuggled information out of Washington D.C. Clashed with the Black Avenger.
  • Herr Mongrel: Attempted to stop food supplies from reaching New York City. Died battling the Fighting Fool.
  • Von Gotch: Owned a cargo company in New York City, purposely loaded ships so that the cargo would shift, sinking the ships. Was captured by the Sub-Mariner and apparently sent off to a firing squad.
  • Great Astro: Phony astrologist who "foretold" Nazi attacks on American ships, was brought to justice by the Patriot.
  • Felix von Shlecht: Hired an organ grinder and his monkey Gertie to steal defense plans of the Panama Canal. Was exposed by Terry Vance, Deadline Dawson and Dr. Watson.
  • J-97: Operated a Nazi spy network in New York City. Was apprehended by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Krauss: A blind Nazi killer who used his parrot Polly to assist in his crimes. Died battling the Angel.
  • Hermit: Local hermit who planted a bomb at a Freetown munitions plant. Was exposed by the Secret Stamp.
  • Joe Grupper: Owned a food manufacturing plant. Poisoned food rations going to American soldiers overseas. Swallowed a cyanide pill to avoid being captured by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Mr. Thos: Attempted to sabotage American relay towers that he himself owned. Captured by the Whizzer and his sidekick Slow Motion Jones.
  • Dr. Eternity: False medium attempted to slay an American Senator. Killed battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Beak: Secretly replaced supplies going from the United States to the Russian front with junk to sour relations between America and Russia. Captured by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • The Ring (Hans Reimann): Sent poisoned rings to high ranking American officials. Suffered death by one of his own weapons while fighting Captain America and Bucky.
  • Baron and Baroness Kidersky: Russian ballerinas who supported the Nazis. They stole secrets from American bases and used television broadcasts to transmit their messages the Nazis through interpretative dance. They were exposed by the Human Torch, Toro and the Sub-Mariner.
  • The Knife: Infiltrated the American Rangers and attempted to slay soldiers while battling Japanese forces on Guadalcanal. Was exposed and killed while battling the Sub-Mariner.
  • Baroness von Kampf: Spy located in New York City, made a number of shady dealings. Clashed with Miss Fury.
  • Bruno Beitz: Spy located in New York City, constantly clashed with Miss Fury as well. Lost his arm in a bomb explosion.
  • Doctor Izan: Nazi operative who had spies surgically altered to resemble terminally ill patients who suddenly had a "miraculous" recovery. Had them pose as air raid wardens to compromise American security. Dies battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Herr Brunder: Attempted to ruin a USO show through acts of sabotage. Exposed and apprehended by the Sub-Mariner.
  • Drums Toggins: Communicated stolen government secrets via Morse Code hidden in his drumming. Exposed by the Patriot.
  • Eric Ward: Nazi sympathizer who attempted to blow up Mount Rushmore after its completion. Killed battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Von Boom: Attempted to blackmail reformed criminal "Thick Lips" Scarbo into helping in sabotage operations by keeping his son hostage. Was captured by the Patriot.
  • Kurt Schnell: Attempted to obtain an highly potent explosive being smuggled out of Europe in a suit of supposedly cursed armor. Clashed with the Angel. Killed when the explosive went off as he attempted to escape with it.
  • Von Blubber: Attempted to sabotage the Philadelphia subway system. Apprehended by Marvel Boy.
  • Here Blutcher: Deformed insane asylum patient who was hired to lead a group across the frozen Channel to invade Britain. Killed battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Bull Neck: Attempted to photograph the New York Harbor patrol. Was shot in the head for his failure when the Sub-Mariner replaced the film with an instructional film on swimming.
  • Heinrich Himmelman: Disguised himself as an ape and hid in a zoo in order to be smuggled out of the United States. Discovered by Dr. Watson, Terry Vance, and Deadline Dawson.
  • Fritz von Bock: Posed as Dr. C.J. Noon and quarantined government officials to cover up their murders. Was exposed by the Angel.
  • Herr von Richter: Hitler look-alike who planted bombs in New York Cities biggest banks until he was killed by one of his own bombs while attempting to flee the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Ferdinand Jones: Infiltrated the United States army and attempted to reduce the amount of recruits by putting irritants in trainees' uniforms. Was exposed and apprehended by Sgt. Dix.
  • Baron von Widemouth: Escaped a South American prisoner of war camp and attempted to sabotage defenses at the Panama Canal. Died battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Elanor von Drei: Attempted to obtain a Yashmak that would have made a band of Turkish warriors loyal to her. She was thwarted by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Albel von Gukk: Used money to entice troubled teens to commit acts of juvenile delinquency. Defeated by the Patriot.
  • Von Schmeer: Passed Heinrich Himmler's sabotage school with flying colors. Sent to the United States to personally inform spy cells there of a new radio frequency. Was apprehended by the Sub-Mariner.
  • Franz Papp: Smuggled stolen plans that were tattooed on his body, prompting Nazi spies to try and recover it after he was killed in an FBI shootout. This was prevented by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Carl Wertz: Escaped his trail after being captured by the authorities. Recaptured thanks to Terry Vance and Deadline Dawson.
  • Double Beard: Worked in an air force kitchen. Drugged female pilots responsible for flying recently completed aircraft to various bases, causing crashes. Thwarted by the Angel.
  • Great Varda: Used a puppet show at New York's Black Cat Lounge to seemingly kill American officials. Took their death-like coma-state bodies to learn government secrets. Killed battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Moonface: Deformed face. Attempted to blow up government buildings in Washington D.C. Killed battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Claw: He poisoned food supplies and then attempted to blow up Boulder Dam. Killed while battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Fritz Bruck (Nazi Cleaver): He tainted meat being sold on the black market, killing many. Killed while battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Karl Gluten: Attempted to frame Norwegian engineer Morton Valnik of sabotaging a ship salvaging operation to sour relations between the United States and Norway. Was exposed and apprehended by the Sub-Mariner.
  • Herr Wolf: Forced Swiss marathon runner Kaspar Gregg to smuggle newly developed "War Water" into the United States. Was captured by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Zomber: Forced planes to crash using super-powerful electromagnets. Died battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Herr Radder: Moved marker lights in the sea to force American ships to crash. Killed battling the Sub-Mariner.
  • Count Lust: Attempted to assassinate the British Royal Family. Killed battling the Angel.
  • Felix von Tont: Attempted to kidnap Bernard Hix, an American official who held evidence that would be used in War Trials after the war. He died battling the Angel.
  • Frank Craben: Spy who infiltrated a Free French resistance cell in order to capture them for the Nazis. Was thwarted by the Destroyer.
  • Kriss Lout: Posed as a janitor at the Defense Department and put poisoned pins on phone earpieces. Attempted to blow up the White House, was captured by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Coughing Killer: Used miniature blow darts that were set off by his coughing to kill his targets. Died battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Mr. Gaines: Nazi who posed as a quiz show host who used the script as a way to send coded messages to his allies. Was exposed by Terry Vance and Deadline Dawson.
  • Lucky Wilson: was hired by Nazis to sabotage a local mine. Opposed by the Vision, who led a group of angry minors to his hideout, where he was apparently beaten to death.
  • Mad Boar: Attempted to poison canned fish supplies being sent to soldiers overseas and died ingesting his own poison while battling the Angel.
  • Mole: Attempted to blow up a missile plant. Captured the Human Torch and Toro. Before he could kill them, he and his men were gunned down by the FBI.
  • Ratter: Stole local dogs promised to the military to test out a new explosive. Died battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Herr Schmutz: Tainted blood supplies being shipped out by the Red Cross. Apprehended by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Fritz von Arnheim: Most wanted Nazi spy. Broke out Nazi prisoners of war from a prison along the Mississippi River while posing as Civil War veteran "Herman the Hermit".
  • Pinhead Killer: Nazi assassin thwarted by Miss America.
  • Wilbur the Great: Betrayed his country by stealing American oil in order to turn it over to the Nazis. Killed while battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • The Wisp: Attempted to stir up relations between the United States and Basra with a doctored document promising that the United States "would" attack Mecca. She was thwarted by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Brain: Senator Doaks betrayed his country by providing the Nazi secrets. He disguised himself as the Brain and died battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Hans Thysenn: Attempted to send spoiled meat to soldiers overseas. Killed battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Fritz Mueller: Undercover as a member of the New York Blue Socks baseball team. Provided information to Imperial Japan. Apprehended by Toro.
  • Baron Schluss: Operated a Nazi spy operation out of an aquarium. Died battling the Sub-Mariner.
  • Karl Kraft: Murdered and posed as American scientist John Barnes to steal a Poison Detective Barometer and kill high-ranking military officials. He was apprehended by the Angel and his reporter friend Nan Carter.
  • Siegfried Kunz: Posed as a flower woman selling poisoned flowers to his targets. Burned alive while attempting to flee the Angel.
  • Von Wolheim: Kidnapped French radio broadcaster Jules Durrian to stop his Anti-Nazi broadcasts. Was stopped by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Carl von Brummel: He launched long-range torpedoes at New York with a specially built sub. Was killed battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Von Rottz: Blind Nazi spy and lover of Anna Frankenstein. Assisted her in creating her own monster to slay Captain America and Bucky. Was killed at the monster's hand.
  • Mistress of Death: Murdered merchant sailors who had shipping secrets smuggled on their dog tags. Drove her car off the Brooklyn Bridge fleeing the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Wolf: Attempted to ruin the American economy with counterfeit money. Died battling the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Flying Falcon: Posed as a circus acrobat to kill American soldiers back from the war due to injuries who were attending a charity performance. Was strangled to death by a gorilla attempting to flee the Angel.
  • Herr Bat: Died attempting to bomb an American glider school after flashing with the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Jackal: Attempted to sabotage America's railway system and eliminate military officials traveling to Washington by train. Was apprehended by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Krauss and his pet parrot Polly: Were spies in America. Clashed with the Angel, but was killed when he was swarmed by his own collection of starving birds of prey and was eaten alive.
  • Dr. von Helstog: A spy and master hypnotist who used his powers to steal American secrets until stopped by Miss America.
  • Von Kropf: Was a Nazi scientist with photographic memory who was sent to memorize the plans of a newly designed Allied rocket. He was defeated by the Patriot.[12]
  • Rex Herr: Was a Nazi spy who fled to Brazil following the Nazi's defeat in 1945. He attempted to obtain the plans for a sun-ray device in order to continue the Nazi cause in 1946 and start off World War III. His plans were foiled by Miss America.
  • Tinfish: An American defector who went to Europe to support the Nazis in 1930. Evading capture in 1945, he returned to the United States in 1948 and attempted to kill his brother in order to inherit money to revitalize the Nazi Party. He was thwarted by the Sub-Mariner and Namora.
  • Alfie: Formed the Crusaders, a group of British superheroes, in order to trick them into assassinating the King of England. His plans were foiled by the Crusaders and Invaders.
  • Lem Snipe: A Nazi spy who was captured by Captain America (Steve Rogers) in 1940. He was released from prison in 1949 and clashed with Captain America (Jeff Mace) before being institutionalized.[13]
  • Herr Krauss: Herr Kraus was head of a team of Nazi spies stationed in the US who tried to steal the secrets of the Atlantean technology.[14]
  • Von Richt: A Nazi spy who was executed by Hitler's order after he failed at his job to demoralize America.[15]
  • Herr Schnitzel: A Nazi spy who discussed with his colleague about Captain America and how they would defeat him.
  • Herr Snupp: Nazi spy and leader of the Unholy Legion. His plans were foiled by Captain America and Bucky, who handed him to the authorities.
  • Herr Krantz: Nazi spy who chased Agent Zero around the globe until he was defeated by the Young Allies.
  • Herr Kranz: Nazi spy who managed to gain full control of a Nazi prison camp to let them loose and take over America. Was defeated by the Patriot.
  • Herr Schnapps: Nazi spy who freed German POWs from a Canadian prison camp, including Herr Kopf. Was defeated by Captain America.
  • Peter Niven: Ex-saboteur, became a wealthy stockbroker in New York and a member of the Gotham Game Club.
  • Herr Stoltz: Spy
  • Hans Schmidt: Spy

Scientists and Inventors[]

  • Professor Barzak was a spy who developed a shrinking potion to steal American defense secrets. He was shrunk down to size by his own potion by FBI agents.[16]
  • Lord of Death: Turned a group of Bowery bums into an army of Zombies to attack the Brooklyn shipyards. Was apprehended by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Scar: Nazi scientist who invented devices to help the Gestapo quell German citizens who were against the Nazi regime. He was killed battling the Destroyer.
  • Doctor Dragon: Attempted to build an invasion tunnel from Nazi-occupied France to Britain beneath the Channel. Was killed when the Destroyer caused the tunnel to flood.
  • Dr. Eric Karlin: Created mindless zombies to try and steal Nazi assets frozen in the Federal Reserve. Was killed battling the Blue Diamond.
  • Dr. Strasser: Used his medical skills to get a position as a surgeon in the United States. Used his credentials to "treat" high-ranking American officials, but instead turned them into bloated floating corpses. Suffered the same fate at the hands of the Patriot.
  • A Nazi Mad Scientist stationed in New Jersey attempted to destroy New York by creating a massive biologically engineered octopus. He died clashing with the Vision.
  • Professor Holz: Biologically engineered an army of massive sea creatures to attack American ships. Killed battling the Sub-Mariner.
  • Dr. Grant: Violin enthusiast and explosives expert. Attempted to blow up a hydro dam at Niagara Falls, killed battling the Sub-Mariner.
  • Professor Karl Schultz: Discovered a preserved dinosaur body in Antarctica, placed the brain of his assistant Olaf Olsen into it, and sent it to destroy American munitions plants. After a clash with Captain America and Bucky, Olsen forced himself and Schultz off a cliff to their deaths.
  • Professor Jurg: A botanist working out of Pasta Guerra, South America. Tricked American soldiers into shipping plants carrying incendiary devices that would sink the ships. Turned over to the authorities by the Sub-Mariner.
  • Professor Fear: Designed a sonic device that could wipe out the minds of men, making them obedient enslaved people. Defeated by the Human Torch and Toro.
  • Dr. Necrosis: Worked with the Red Cross in Melbourne, Australia. Tainted blood supplies with "white gangrene" that were intended for soldiers on the front line. Died battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Dr. Agony: Torture expert, attempted to find a chemical immunity to pain by torturing his suspects. Killed while battling Captain America and Bucky.
  • Anna Frankenstein: Member of the legendary Frankenstein family. Used her ancestors' notes to create her own monster. Killed herself out of shame for betraying her country to the Nazis.
  • Dr. Luville: Operated on St. Lazare Island in the West Indies. Developed an aging serum to kill his targets. The Sub-Mariner subjected him to his own formula, and he rapidly died of old age.
  • Dr. Groitzig: Captured Mary Morgan and experimented on her with a newly developed formula. Led to her obtaining the powers she briefly used as Miss Patriot.
  • Professor Todt: Also a spy, attempted to blow up American ships by converting corpses into bombs, and was brought to justice by Captain America and Bucky.
  • Professor Hans Ludwig: Created a sun-ray device more powerful than the atomic bomb. When the Nazis lost the war, he fled to New York City as a refugee, where he attempted to smuggle his formula to his colleague Rex Herr in Brazil. This plan was ultimately foiled by Miss America.[17] His fate is unknown.
  • Professor Heinz Rheint: A Nazi scientist who developed a deadly biological weapon. When the Nazis lost the war, he fled to Brazil, where he hid his formula before he died in 1946. OSS agent Mark Mason and the Blonde Phantom prevented the formula from falling into the wrong hands.[18]
  • Brain Drain: Had his brain removed and placed in a jar. Created various advanced weapons for the Nazis during the war.
  • Johann Goldstein: A Jewish scientist was forced to use his Blue Bullet armor against the Invaders to save his brother's life; later, he was forced by Battle-Axis to help build a device to cause a massive earthquake on the American West Coast.

Others[]

  • Anson Dictor: A Nazi purchasing agent who attempted to buy a new prototype engine from inventor Don Gorman. He was apprehended when he attempted to steal the engines.
  • Chester Orsen: Betrayed his country when his employee Don Gorman refused to sell his new prototype engine to Anson Dictor. He was apprehended when attempting to steal the engine.
  • Betty Dean: Working undercover, she joined an American cell of Nazi spies in order to flush them out for the Sub-Mariner.
  • Ali Hamud: A Middle Eastern prince who fled to the Belgian Congo. He allowed the Nazis to use his palace as an airfield. He died clashing with Ka-Zar.
  • P.J. Evans: Was a weapons manufacturer in the United States. He betrayed his country to provide weapons to Nazi operatives in Mexico as "Herr Leader". He was exposed by the Defender and Rusty.
  • Charles Reigel: Was the leader of the Nazi Service Organization in America. Later created the Gotham Game Club with other ex-criminals and mercenaries.[19]
  • James Logan: Shipping magnate and former gunrunner for the Nazis.
