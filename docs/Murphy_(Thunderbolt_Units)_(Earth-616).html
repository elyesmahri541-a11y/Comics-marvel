History

Murphy was hired by The Rose to capture Spider-Man. Pretending to be a member of Mayor Wilson Fisk's Thunderbolt Units, after Spider-Man was arrested by his accomplice Gibbs, Murphy drove the van that would take him to the Rose's lair. However, Spider-Man realized they weren't cops and escaped their captures, much to The Rose's chagrin.[1]
