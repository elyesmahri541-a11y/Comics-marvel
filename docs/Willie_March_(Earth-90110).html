History

Willie March joined the Avengers as a replacement for the slain Irondroid (Bill Hogan). He served with the Cosmic Avengers as they battled Korvac and the mind-controlled Guardians of the Galaxy over the safety of Ultra-Vision. After Korvac and Ultra-Vision expired in cyberspace combat with each other, the Whisperer recruited Irondroid to be his agent.[2]

Attributes

Powers

Willie March has no powers of his own but they are derived from his Irondroid Armor.

Notes

  • Irondroid's design is based on Iron Man 2020.
  • Given his last name and the status of the previous Irondroid as a descendant of an ally of Iron Man, it is entirely likely that William is a descendant of Eddie March.
