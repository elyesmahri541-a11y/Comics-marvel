History

As Daredevil and Cloak were teleported right into an arena during the Hyborian Age, the wild men ruling the nearby city sent Devil Dinosaur against them, but Cloak absorbed it inside his darkness. Later, the hero, unable to keep Devil inside him more, freed it in the prison he had been brought, allowing the imprisoned Savage Avengers to escape.[1]
