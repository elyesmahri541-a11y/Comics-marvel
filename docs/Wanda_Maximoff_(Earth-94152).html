Lore is the nexus being of a reality where necromancy was common and a dark counterpart of Wanda Maximoff.[2][1] In her hunt for nexus beings, Lore attacked Earth-616's Scarlet Witch but was slain by her.[4] In death, Lore found a new calling in ruling the Land of the Dead, and attempted to stop her prime counterpart from escaping to the land of the living, but she would be once again defeated.[1]

History

Hailing from a dark, necromantic reality, Lore was the embodiment of her world’s corruption as its nexus being. In time, she consumed the total mystical and existential energies of her home reality, an act that left it functionally destroyed and uninhabitable. Unable to manifest independently beyond her own universe, Lore turned to dimensional conquest, invading other realities by possessing their nexus beings. Through this process, she fed upon both the nexus host and their reality itself, collapsing entire universes in the process and killing the possessed nexus beings.[2]

Disguised as a monstrous entity, Lore later assaulted the reality of Amazar by possessing its nexus being, the Witch-Queen Eleyne. This incursion and the resulting destabilization of the reality were sensed by the Scarlet Witch of Earth-616, herself a nexus being and Lore’s intended next victim. Lore manipulated Master Pandemonium and manifested demonic versions of the Avengers to weaken Wanda before directly attacking her spiritually. Wanda countered by using her hex powers to forcibly anchor Lore into physical form. During their confrontation, Lore drew upon the souls and residual power of the nexus beings she had already destroyed, but this proved disastrous when the vengeful spirits turned against her. Wanda ultimately stabbed the witch with a sword, freeing the trapped souls and ending Lore’s multiversal threat.[4]

As a witch, Lore’s soul was cast into the Witches' Underworld, where she embraced damnation and ultimately rose to rule the realm.[1] When the Scarlet Witch herself died and sought resurrection via the Witches' Road, Lore obstructed her passage. Although Lore initially held the advantage within her own domain, Wanda ultimately escaped, returning to life and thwarting Lore once more.[1]

Attributes

Powers

Lore is a Nexus Being that possess vast magical powers and feared among other nexi to the point of being nicknamed "Scourge of the Nexi". She is capable of traveling between dimensions, possessing people's minds and bodies, raising and controlling the dead, and firing mystic energy blasts. She could drain energy from entire planets to replenish her reserves and from her words, destroy countless realities as a result of her actions. [5][3]

Nexus Being: Belonging equally to all possible timelines, Lore is a nexus being, agents of pure possibility created by the Queen of Nevers. As a nexi, Lore is untethered to fate, unaffected by predetermined forces that governs existence. As a living focal point for Earth's energies, she also has potential access to any form of magical energy in the dimension, including forces such as the Life Force.[6]

  • Witchcraft: Lore is a human of magical birthright, which means she possesses the ability to manipulate magic since the very beginning.[2][3]
    • Possession: Lore has demonstrated the ability to possess others when needed, though prefers other nexus beings. [2]
    • Energy Absorption: Lore was capable to absorb and channel energy. She has been shown to be able to siphon the life-force of entire worlds to replenish herself. [2]
    • Energy Projection: Lore was capable to release and project powerful energy blasts of various shapes and/or intensities and annihilate her targets.[2][3]
    • Flight: Lore has also proven to be able to fly at will.[2][3]
    • Necromancy: Lore can communicate with the deceased and raise them.[2]
    • Interdimensional Teleportation: She was able to travel interdimensionally, traversing universes with seemingly minimal effort.[2][3]
    • Summoning: Lore can call upon demons to attack her enemies. In their battle against the Scarlet Witch, they were able to decay and seemingly drain her of life energy, [1]
    • Illusions: Lore can create illusions as shown when she impersonated Phoenix and her powers to mock Wanda. [1]
    • Immortality: Lore appears to not age beyond her prime. While still alive, she claimed to have drained her own world eons ago, implying she has been alive for thousands of years. [7]

Notes

  • The idea that multiple nexus beings cannot exist in the same reality is only applied in Scarlet Witch (and is circumvented in Scarlet Witch #4). However, this is ignored elsewhere, especially in the many, many cases of Kang, another nexus being,[8] interacting with Wanda Maximoff and Earth-616. Later, Lore is seen existing in Earth-616 despite Wanda still being alive, so this rule seems to have been abandoned.[3]

Trivia

  • Lore's appearance in Scarlet Witch (Vol. 4) #3 seems to be inspired by the MCU's Scarlet Witch, with the paler skin, redder hair, and an outfit that resembles Wanda's in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness.
