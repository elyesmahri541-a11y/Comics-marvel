Reprint of the 1st story from
Marvel Universe Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes #11
Hercules pays a visit to the Avengers to restart his friendly rivalry with Thor with a destructive fight that breaks through the walls of the mansion and onto the streets. Hawkeye calls in Hulk to break them up then the Wasp gets them to work together to clean up the mess they made.
