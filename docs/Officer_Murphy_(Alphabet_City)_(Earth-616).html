History

Officer Murphy was a police officer assigned to patrol duty in Alphabet City alongside his partner, Charlotte Jones, when they encountered the injured Forge, who was being pursued by Genoshan Magistrates. After Forge warned them about their pursuers, Murphy reached for his gun, prompting the Magistrates to shoot him dead. Forge managed to drag Jones to safety behind their patrol car, and the two subsequently joined forces to fight off their attackers. Although they eventually helped X-Factor defeat the Genoshan forces attempting to abduct Jenny Ransome and Phil Moreau, Murphy's death went unpunished, as the United States Government arranged for the Magistrates to be repatriated to Genosha.[1]
