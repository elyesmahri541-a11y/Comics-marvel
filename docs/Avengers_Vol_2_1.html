Synopsis for "Awaken the Thunder!"

Loki materializes over the Earth, his memories hazy. When he attempts to return to Asgard in search of Thor he finds that the Rainbow bridge is missing and upon searching mystically realizes that the nine worlds of Asgard are seemingly missing. Believing that Thor is the cause of all this he goes searching for his brother.

In Norway, Loki is drawn to a S.H.I.E.L.D.-funded archaeological dig led by Dr. Donald Blake, a name which sounds familiar to Loki. One of the team leads Blake, followed unseen by Loki, to a block of amber with a man inside it. Both Blake and Loki realise the figure in the amber is Thor. Blake’s team are surprised as they thought they were looking for a Norse war hammer. Blake wants to contact HQ to tell them of the discovery.

Loki attempts to destroy Thor then and there and finds that his magics are unable to do so. Blake, seeing the strange spectacle, is persuaded by Jane Foster to call in S.H.I.E.L.D. and the newly formed team featuring Captain America, the Avengers. Loki finds the name of the Avengers familiar too and feels almost the same hatred for them as he does for Thor. Loki decides to go and seek them out and use them to break through the spell protecting Thor and destroy him.

Seeking them out he finds the newly built Avengers Island headquarters in New York Harbor. Peering inside he witnesses the Scarlet Witch unpacking her room with her teacher Agatha Harkness. Wanda is complaining to Agatha about being here and wanting to return to Salem. Agatha thinks that being in the outside world and working with S.H.I.E.L.D. and the Avengers will be good for Wanda. Wanda shows an attraction to Captain America and is reprimanded by Agatha as Cap is old enough to be Wanda’s grandfather. Agatha suggests that Wanda will learn to control her power as part of the team. Loki detects that the Scarlet Witch is a powerful sorceress, and she manages to pick up Loki's presence even though Agatha cannot. Not wishing to be seen yet, Loki takes his leave realising he needs to be careful around Wanda.

He enters the room of the Swordsman, and notes how the hero is a narcissistic braggart. In the next room, he finds Hawkeye working out in a rigorous training schedule. In another room he finds an animalistic-looking Hellcat watching television until she is interrupted by the Vision. Hellcat offers to try out a new massage technique on the Vision.

In yet another room he finds the living legend of World War II, Captain America, training. As Cap trains he displays anger towards an unnamed person who took everything from him that he loved and held dear, apart from his soul. A myriad of images fills Cap’s mind. Images of the past, present and his hopes for the future.

Elsewhere in the complex, Colonel Nick Fury is showing government liaison Henry Gyrich around the island, listening to Gyrich's complaints that super-heroes should be outlawed not government sanctioned. A S.H.I.E.L.D. agent, Sellars, has been monitoring the Q-ratings for the Avengers. Whilst Captain America has great approval from the public, the Avengers are not as popular with them as the Fantastic Four, but the hope is that will change once the team has had its first mission. Sellars suggests that the team needs more minority members in order to gain further public approval. Gyrich insists that what the team needs is control and boundaries.

Overhearing that the Avengers are waiting to go on their fist mission, Loki begins to realize that Fury controls the Avengers. He wonders if Fury, with his stern attitude and missing eye, could be an aspect of Odin. Loki thinks that Sellars may be the one to manipulate in order to be able to use the Avengers to destroy Thor. Suddenly Fury receives a video call from Dr. Blake who informs them of what his team has found and the fact that man in amber seems to be Thor.

Meanwhile, in another part of the complex, the Scarlet Witch reflects that she wishes that Agatha would allow her more independence, although she still feels grateful to Agatha for raising her like a daughter and teaching her how to use her powers. Wanda encounters the Vision for the first time. She finds it a little creepy that he was deactivated part way through her conversation with him. He tells her it’s a way for him to recharge his cells, analogous to the human sleep function. Vision shows her around, and introduces her to his creator Dr. Henry Pym and his girlfriend Janet van Dyne both part of the Avengers support staff. Janet offers to take Wanda shopping since she likes Wanda’s outfit except for the boots. Janet is startled by Pym's newest creation Ultron-1, his new lab assistant since the Vision has joined active duty with the Avengers. The alarm sounds and soon the Avengers are deployed to Norway to investigate Blake’s discovery.

In Norway, Blake shows the Avengers a Viking war hammer bearing an inscription. Blake informs the team that nobody has been able to lift it and he believes it to be Mjolnir, the hammer of Thor. He goes on to tell them that he believes the man in the amber is Thor himself. Blake tells Captain America that his team have tried everything they can think of to try and break Thor free from the amber. Cap instructs Hawkeye to fire a blast arrow at the amber. Then he gets the Swordsman to create a hairline fracture after which Cap throws his shield into the fracture. He then tells the Vision to dematerialise the shield and himself until they’re halfway through the amber. Blake is amazed to see Vision executing this ability. Cap tells Wanda to throw a hex at the amber, which she does despite worrying that it will affect the circuitry of the Vision. After Cap tells Vision to solidify both himself and the shield, Loki realises the team is going to succeed. As the Vision solidifies and the hex hits the amber it shatters. Thor, now freed, takes deep breaths of air and announces himself as Thor.

Blake is happy that the effort was successful, but Captain America orders the Avengers to be ready for anything since Thor may behave in a barbarian way that modern society would find unacceptable. Whilst Thor becomes slightly disoriented Loki chooses this time to appear and tries to convince the confused Thor that he has been trying to free him as well as the fact that Loki standing by his side in battle against the Avengers when they attacked Asgard. Thor attacks Captain America who is unsure how long his shield can protect him against the strength of Thor. Meanwhile, Loki turns his attention to the Scarlet Witch. Despite Hawkeye and the Swordsman trying to assist her they are knocked away by Loki’s magic. Hellcat tries to help Cap by leaping onto Thor. He hurls her from him and she inadvertently lands on Wanda taking them both out. The Vision changes his density in order to hit Thor with a punch as hard as a diamond before Loki fires magic at Vision causing him to short circuit.

Thor returns to the attack on Captain America. Cap still has doubts that this man really is Thor, since he believes Thor is a myth, but tries to reason with him. Loki tries to strike down Thor with a bolt of magical energy, and is saved by Captain America. Finding his hammer, Thor picks it up and his memories return. He remembers that his brother is a trickster god and turns against him. Loki attempts to cast a spell which will send Thor to Limbo forever. Captain America instructs a recovering Scarlet Witch to use her hex power against Loki. Despite being unsure if she can help, Wanda casts her hex power at Loki causing his spell to backfire and send him to Limbo instead.

With the battle over, Captain America establishes that the Avengers are heroes and asks Thor what happened to him. Thor cannot recall, he remembers an onslaught, a great reckoning, and sacrifice. Thor remembers Jord possibly sought to protect him from Ragnarok, but he can’t remember much else. Captain America still believes this is just a delusional man rather than really Thor, but decides to play along. Realizing that having Thor's raw strength on the team could be a great asset to the group he invites the thunder god to join them, an offer which Thor agrees to take until he can find Asgard. The team has a small moment to celebrate their first mission being successful.

While in the limbo realm that Loki has been banished too, the trickster god is confronted by Amora the Enchantress who tells Loki that Thor can never be defeated on Earth. Loki agrees to team-up with her to destroy Thor and the Avengers. Amora says she has no interest in the Avengers except for the Scarlet Witch, someone that the Enchantress claims is her daughter.

Notes

  • Plot by Liefeld, script by Valentino.
  • Pencils (story pages): Yaep pages 1-19, Liefeld pages 20-41.
  • Loki believes that he is the Loki of Asgard however as it is made clear in Heroes Reborn: The Return #1 he is in reality a creation of Franklin Richards. It is notable that both he and Thor have recollections of events prior to Onslaught.
  • It's notable that Hawkeye's unmasked face is not revealed in this issue. Originally, Hawkeye was intended to be someone other than Clint Barton in the Heroes Reborn universe, as will be realized in later issues.
  • Enchantress refers to the Scarlet Witch as her daughter, implying that in the Heroes Reborn universe she is part Asgardian instead of being a mutant. The issue is skirted around and ultimately dropped without any resolution.
  • Dr. Donald Blake mentions a Dr. Horton who has written a paper on the Vision. Since the Earth-616 Phineas Horton, who created the android Human Torch on which the Vision’s body is based, was a Professor rather than a Doctor, whether this is the Counter-Earth version of Phineas Horton or a relative is unknown.

Trivia

  • Loki is the first enemy faced by the Avengers in this issue. Loki was also the first enemy faced by the Avengers in Avengers Vol 1 1, albeit a different version of Loki and none of the Earth-616 Avengers from that issue are part of this team.
  • Captain America is present when Thor is rescued from within a block of amber. In Avengers Vol 1 4 the Earth-616 Thor was present when this Captain America was rescued from within a block of ice.
