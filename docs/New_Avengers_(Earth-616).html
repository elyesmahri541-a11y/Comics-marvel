In a chaotic sequence of events executed by the insane Scarlet Witch, Earth's Mightiest Heroes, the Avengers, were destroyed and forced to disassemble permanently.[3][4] A group of New Avengers assembled by fate during a prison breakout in the maximum security installation for supervillains known as the Raft. Initially motivated by recapturing the fugitives, the team filled the vacant position their predecessors had left.[5] The team was torn apart however, during the Superhuman Civil War, becoming outlaws who evaded the Registration Act and rivalled the government-sponsored Avengers.[6][7] After the decommission of the act, super-heroes were free once again, and the New Avengers, led by Luke Cage, became a parallel unit of Avengers with full independence from the main team.[8] The team ceased their operations after Cage and his wife Jessica Jones opted for retirement.[9]

History

Avengers: Disassembled[]

In the wake of personal tragedy regarding the heartbreaking loss her twin children, Scarlet Witch's grip on her volatile reality-warping powers unraveled. She unleashed a catastrophic surge of chaos that devastated her own teammates, the Avengers. Some perished, others were left irreparably scarred, and the Avengers Mansion was obliterated in the resulting maelstrom.[3] Amid the ruins of their very existence, the Avengers lamented their losses, which also included the destruction of the realm of Asgard and the death of the mighty Thor. The shattered Mansion was transformed into a solemn memorial, a grim testament of their interrupted history. Tony Stark, the invincible Iron Man and financial pillar of the team, announced he could no longer bankroll their operations. With their resources depleted, their numbers diminished, and their spirit broken, the Avengers had no choice but to disband. Thus came the end of an era: Earth's mightiest heroes disassembled.[4]

New Avengers, Assemble![]

A new group of Avengers assembled by chance during a breakout at the Raft, an island high-security facility designed to detain the world's most dangerous super-criminals. The incident was set in motion during a calculated power outage engineered by the villain Electro. During the blackout, private bodyguard Luke Cage was on-site escorting attorneys Foggy Nelson and Matthew Murdock, secretly Daredevil, on a visit to the enigmatic and omnipotent Sentry, who had been imprisoned by his own demand. Accompanying them was the original Spider-Woman, who served as the S.H.I.E.L.D. agent overseeing the Raft. As chaos erupted, reinforcements came in the form of the friendly neighborhood Spider-Man, the star-spangled hero Captain America,[10] and the invincible Iron Man, who joined forces with the other heroes to help restore order.[11] Unbeknownst to those involved, Spider-Woman was in fact the alien Skrull Queen, an imposter who had infiltrated Earth as part of an elaborate plan of invading the planet.[12] The Skrulls, who had deeply compromised S.H.I.E.L.D. and many other organizations, had actually orchestrated the prison break.[13]

Believing the assembling of heroes was a fateful sign more than a fortunate coincidence, Captain America convinced Iron Man to form a new team of Avengers. Free from the government's bureaucratic involvement and the financial burdens that had plagued their predecessors, this new incarnation would exist solely to take on threats too great for any single hero to face alone, starting with recapturing the forty-two inmates of the Raft who had managed to escape into the world. Captain America reached out to the heroes present at the Raft riot to invite them to the New Avengers. Worn down by a career of failures, Spider-Man accepted the offer. Grounded by a sense of duty, Luke Cage joined in hopes of making his loved ones proud. On the other hand, Daredevil declined, fearing that his convoluted life would compromise the team. Having been displaced from her role within S.H.I.E.L.D., Spider-Woman accepted ase well. The Sentry had vanished without a trace. The newly formed Avengers established their base in Stark Tower, a skyscraper owned by Iron Man at the heart of Manhattan. The loyal butler Jarvis resumed his role as caretaker of the team's penthouse headquarters.[1] In a sinister parallel to Spider-Woman, Jarvis had also been replaced by a Skrull infiltrator, further deepening the deception seeded within the group.[14]

The team was put into contact with the Director of S.H.I.E.L.D., Commander Maria Hill, who had assumed the position following Colonel Nick Fury's unsanctioned invasion of Latveria -- a covert mission that had involved several of the Avengers-to-be. Unlike her predecessor, Hill held little enthusiasm for a non-government-sanctioned Avengers unit, but Captain America asserted his authority by invoking his Full Champion License, which allowed him to assemble any team hfor any mission he deemed necessary. With Spider-Man identifying Electro as the perpetrator of the breakout, the Avengers apprehended him and followed the trail of his intended target, the mutated Sauron, to the inhospitable Savage Land. Wolverine, the feral X-Man, had arrived in the area following a tip from the Scorcher and joined the investigation.[15] Wolverine was controversially added to the team roster[16] in case a last and lethal force was required by the Avengers.[17] In the dense jungles, the team confronted both the Savage Land Mutates associated with Sauron[18] and S.H.I.E.L.D. rogue operatives led by Yelena Belova, the Black Widow. Having uncovered a hidden Vibranium mining operation manned by enslaved laborers, the Avengers witnessed a S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier obliterate the area with cruise missiles, under Hill's orders, which escalated the tensions between them and the spy agency. In addition to this, by considering that several of the escaped convicts they hunted had been listed as deceased, the Avengers concluded that S.H.I.E.L.D. might have gone backsliding without the watchful eye of the recently deposed Fury and should not be trusted.[16]

In between intercepting escaped criminals, among them the destructive Wrecker, the Avengers turned their attention to the potentially perilous figure of the Sentry. More specifically, Tony Stark shared detailed information with the Illuminati, a secretive cabal of leaders within the super-hero community that manipulated events to protect the planet. As such, the Avengers gained influential allies in their mission, such as the Fantastic Four, the X-Men, the Inhuman Royal Family, the Sorcerer Supreme Doctor Strange and the King of Atlantis Namor.[19] Once regarded as a golden hero, the Sentry had his existence erased from the memories of those who had known him. Internally, he waged a battle with his dark alter-ego, the Void, who sabotaged his every heroic act. With help of the superhero community, the Sentry reclaimed his place in the world and joined the Avengers. Although he agreed to assist the team when he could, his mental instability often rendered unreliable in the field. As a peculiar consequence, the Sentry's Watchtower was restored over the top of Stark Tower, a surreal confirmation of Captain America's suggestion that the team had been assembled by fate.[20]

One of the Raft's former prisoners was the warrior swordsman known as the Silver Samurai, an influential member of Clan Yashida. Intelligence reports indicated that he returned to Japan for a potential alliance involving the Hand and Madame Hydra's forces. With Wolverine unavailable, the Avengers lacked an expert familiar with the clandestine criminal underworld they were about to enter. Although Daredevil turned down a request from Captain America to help, he recommended the mysterious Ronin in his place for being an agent experienced with the Hand affairs. In fact, Ronin was the deaf martial artist known as Echo. Taking on the assignment, she traced Madame Hydra, but was ultimately chased down by the Hand's undead ninjas. The Avengers engaged in a fierce battle, during which Spider-Woman confronted Madame Hydra in secrecy to discuss her cryptic allegiance to Hydra. The Silver Samurai claimed he had been abducted by S.H.I.E.L.D. to the Raft unjustly, adding another layer of mystery surrounding the prison break. The Avengers departed with Madame Hydra captive, but Spider-Woman furtively staged the prisoner's escape while en route.[21] When confronted by her teammates over the baffling betrayal, she revealed her true role as a triple agent, hidden inside Hydra and the Avengers as an agent covertly guided by Nick Fury.[22]

After operating in the shadows for some missions, the Avengers prepared to officially reintroduce themselves to the world. In a press conference covered by the Daily Bugle, editor-in-chief J. Jonah Jameson vocally expressed his criticism of super-heroes, particularly Spider-Man. He wasted no time in launching a tirade against the new roster of the Avengers, denouncing and highlighting unsavory aspects of their stories. Amid this controversy, the team counted with direct support from the former Avenger Ms. Marvel.[23] At the same time, the Avengers assisted Luke Cage when his partner, the private investigator Jessica Jones went into labor by taking her to Doctor Strange's Sanctum Sanctorum,[24] where the baby girl was delivered.[25] Next, they celebrated Cage and Jones' wedding. However, the ceremony was violently interrupted by vengeful Yelena Belova, who had been transformed by A.I.M. into a Super-Adaptoid as part of Hydra's schemes. The Avengers assembled to confronted the enhanced assassin. In the end, Belova was eliminated by her own handlers, who chose to sacrifice her rather than risk exposing their larger conspiracy. The Avengers were finally free to commemorate the union of two of their own in peace. [26]

In between the events that consolidated the team, the Avengers faced diverse challenges. They battled with a team of four Avengers doppelgängers created by a faction of Hydra which was put down after Spider-Man infiltrated their ranks.[27] The team also supervised the emergence of a group of Young Avengers, who had adopted tribute mantles honoring the champions they admired. Though initially disapproving their activities,[28] the Avengers reevaluated their stance after fighting joining forces with them to fend off aKree and Skrull incursion. Representing their blessing, the Avengers permitted the Young Avengers to restore the grounds of the Avengers Mansion to serve as a memorial site.[29] Another encounter with teenage vigilantes unfolded when Cloak, wrongly accused of harming his partner Dagger, turned to the orphans known as the Runaways to clear his name. The Avengers hunted Cloak down[30] only to clash with the band of youngsters in the a confrontation marked by miscommunication. The Runaways eluded any captures and exposed Cloak's imposter.[31] A major assault happened after the Thunderbolts were assigned by the Commission on Superhuman Activities to target the Avengers Tower, a plan envisioned by Henry P. Gyrich, Carol Danvers, and Hank Pym. The Thunderbolts tricked the Avengers into leaving the building and defeated them. It was then revealed that Baron Zemo was the actual mastermind behind the scenes, working with the Commission to monitor super-heroes, including the Avengers.[32]

House of M[]

The fate of the Scarlet Witch was still an unresolved crisis. She was isolated in the ruins of Genosha under the care of Professor Xavier and Doctor Strange, and any possibility of recovery seemed impossible. In light of that, Xavier contacted both the Avengers and his X-Men to discuss a course of action. The first step was to reason with the Scarlet Witch directly on Genosha, but, upon arriving, the heroes were ensnared in a chaotic trap.[33] Tapping into their deepest wishes using Xavier's telepathy, the Scarlet Witch crafted an alternate reality built from their dreams. In the fabricated world, mutants were a ruling caste. Yet, the Avenger and X-Man Wolverine had his greatest wish of accessing all of his memories granted and could see through the illusion.[34] The disturbed Wolverine was met by Cage,[35] who had been approached by Layla Miller, another mutant who had recollection of the truth and could awaken others.[36] Wolverine and Cage embarked on a mission to restore the memories of the Avengers and the X-Men to dissipate the Scarlet Witch's doing.[37] Having reassembled the Avengers, they launched an assult on Genosha alongside the X-Men.[38] In a climactic moment, the distressed Scarlet Witch, overwhelmed by desperation, restored reality.[39] As the world was restored, the majority of the mutant population found themselves depowered.[40]

The mutant energy released by the Scarlet Witch's spell catastrophically detonated in Alaska. S.H.I.E.L.D. deployed Canada's premier team Alpha Flight to assess the phenomenon, resulting in their extermination. With no alternatives, Director Maria Hill reluctantly contacted the Avengers for aid.[41] During this incident, the Avengers focused on community-level outreach under Luke Cage's suggestion, for he believed the group should connect with the people they protected. As the omnipotent entity arrived in Cleveland, the Avengers called in Ms. Marvel for reinforcement; the intercepted creature was identified as the Collective in a fight with destructive consequences.[42] Through the Vision's database, S.H.I.E.L.D. discovered that the Collective was constituted by the combined signatures of the mutants who had got depowered.[43] Hill took the opportuniy to investigate the truth behind the crisis, psychically extracting the information from Spider-Man.[44] Arriving on Genosha, the Collective searched for Magneto. Being followed by the Avengers, he had his energies entrapped and dispersed into the sun with the covert intervention of the agent of S.H.I.E.L.D. Quake. In the aftermath, Magneto went missing, but the Collective no longer posed a threat.[45]

Civil War[]

Amidst tensions surrounding unchecked super-human activity, the congress proposed a Superhuman Registration Act to regulate the activity of costumed heroes in the country. Foreseeing a crisis, Iron Man decided to endorse the proposition.[46] The act was enacted after a disaster protagonized by the junior heroes and reality show stars New Warriors had hundreds of victims in Stamford, Connecticut. Iron Man, alongside figures such as Reed Richars and Hank Pym supported registration, forcing vigilantes to reveal their secret identities and receive training in law enforcement. Captain America vehemently opposed the emerging law on the grounds that it violated civil liberties. The debate evolved into a superhero Civil War, forcing every hero to choose sides.[47] The schism shattered the Avengers, Captain America formed a band of renegades, the Secret Avengers, who evaded S.H.I.E.L.D.'s squadron of cape killers,[48][8] while Iron Man recruited subjects for the Initiative, a federal program meant to train and deploy superhumans across every state.[49]

Luke Cage and Spider-Woman rejected the act and joined the underground resistance led by Captain America.[50] On the opposing side, Ms. Marvel and the Sentry supported Iron Man and S.H.I.E.L.D..[49][51] Spider-Man initially supported registration, but defected after witnessing ethical compromises within the system.[52] The X-Men, including Wolverine, remained neutral.[49] The Civil War intensified, until culminating with Captain America surrendering after a huge battle in Times Square, New York, after he realized that superheroes were causing the very horror they were supposed to protect innocents from.[6] While in custody, Captain America was assassinated when entering a federal courthouse, as part of a plot orchestrated by his longtime foe, the Red Skull.[53] Iron Man lamented the death of his rival and dear friend Captain America.[54] The outlaw Avengers went underground without Captain America, while Iron Man formed a new team of Mighty Avengers as a government-sanctioned unit of registered heroes who operated under the Initiative.[6]

Outlawed[]

In the aftermath of Civil War, the New Avengers found asylum with Doctor Strange in his mystically protected and unreachable Sanctum Sanctorum. Under Cage's leadership, the fugitives Spider-Woman, Spider-Man, and Wolverine were joined by Iron Fist, who had supported Captain America during the crisis. Spider-Woman had been informed by Ms. Marvel that Captain America was, in fact, alive and kept in the Raft, which was confirmed by Strange. The Avengers raided the Raft only to immediately realize it was a trap set by Iron Man, who had his own Avengers ambush them with power-dampening technology.[55] Doctor Strange prevented a confrontation by teleporting his allies away. Despite Iron Man attempting to capture them using the magic abilities of Brother Voodoo,[56] he failed. Before departing, he ultimately led the outlaw Avengers question their purpose as a resistance after the war.[57]

Iron Fist received a call for help from Echo, as well as her Ronin suit and gadgets, since she had once again clashed with the Hand, now led by the vile Elektra.[56] As the Avengers prepared to rescue their ally from being corrupted by the assassins, an old-time member of the Avengers, Clint Barton, the marksman Hawkeye, visited Sanctum Sanctorum. He was invited to join the team, as an opposer of Iron Man, but refused to reassume his identity of the archer Hawkeye. As such, he adopted the mantle of Ronin.[57] The Avengers rescued Echo and sought refuge at Silver Samurai's palace. However, they were followed by Elektra and her army of assassin ninjas, sparking a violent conflict.[58] The brainwashed Echo mortally wounded Doctor Strange amidst battle.[57] With help from his disciple Wong, Strange escaped death and exorcized Echo. Seeking vengeance, she slew Elektra. Oddly enough, the Hand retreated and the the Avengers watched Elektra revert to her true form, a Skrull impersonator.[59] This proved to be the first evidence of the impending Skrull invasion Earth faced, Spider-Woman retained her cover as paranoia set in, since the Avengers believed anyone could be an undercover infiltrator. On their way back to New York City, Iron Fist's plane was affected by Ultron's attack to Earth and crashed. Spider-Woman managed to escape unscathed and betrayed the team by taking the Skrull corpse to Iron Man.[60]

Doctor Strange was able to confirm all Avengers and their associates in Sanctum Sanctorum were not shapeshifters, strengthening the trust within the group. Parallel to this, Wolverine learned about the emergence of the Hood in the criminal underworld, as he took advantage of the constant conflicts between heroes to gain influence and assemble a legion of super villains, the Hood's Gang.[61] Worried about the Hood making a move against Iron Man and his Avengers, the group decided to take action.[62] However, the team was caught in a symbiote infection in Manhattan, with many Avengers, from both active rosters, being bonded with alien monsters. Due to his impenetrable skin, Cage was clean and joined forces with Iron Man's Avengers to revert the situation. After the alien parasites were neutralized, both Avengers teams called a temporary truce to help afflicted civilians, which allowed the outlaw Avengers to retreat.[63]

Tracking the Hood's Gang down, the Avengers managed to beat them into submission in an illusory setting cast by Doctor Strange that summoned an apparent army of heroes. The Hood ran away in the middle of the fight, and S.H.I.E.L.D. arrested most of his Gang. He broke them all out and elaborated a revenge attack against the Avengers.[64] Using his magical abilities, the Hood easily broke through the defenses of Sanctum Sanctorum. The Avengers were severely beaten, and Doctor Strange was murdered.[65] Using dark magic, he revived himself and knocked all villains out, which cost him his position as Sorcerer Supreme and forced him to leave the team. The commotion alerted S.H.I.E.L.D., but Ms. Marvel ultimately chose to cover her old allies' retreat. As a consequence of the disastrous attack, Jessica Jones sought asylum in the Avengers Tower with her infant child, abandoning her husband Cage and his Avengers.[66] Iron Fist relocated the Avengers to one of his corporation's apartments that was once occupied by the Leader.[65]

Skrull Invasion[]

The Skrull invasion to take over Earth finally came to a boiling point when a Skrull ship landed in the Savage Land. Spider-Woman tipped the rebel Avengers about the situation and they stole a Quinjet the official Avengers team was about to use. Upon reaching their destination, both Avengers teams found the Mutates' citadel in ruins. The Skrulls corrupted Iron Man's technology all over the globe with a virus through his armor's systems, not only were Iron Man and the Avengers affected, but S.H.I.E.L.D., the Initiative and many others. The landed spaceship opened, revealing a group of many costumed adventurers claiming to have escaped Skrull captivity, including Captain America, the Scarlet Witch, the Vision, and Ronin's ex-wife Mockingbird.[67] Confused, the Avengers were not sure who was a shapeshifter and who was legit. The Avengers cautiously recollected their team in the chaotic situation, with Ronin vouching for Mockingbird. Meanwhile, Manhattan was invaded by thousands of Super-Skrull soldiers.[68] Spider-Woman reached out to Iron Man, who recovered from the infection in his systems. She played with his mind, revealing to be Queen Veranke, the liege of the Skrulls and mastermind behind the infiltration and invasion of Earth. The Avengers put aside their differences and join forces against the common enemy,[69] being assisted by Ka-Zar and Shanna, the She-Devil.[70]

The veil of uncertainty ended when Reed Richards unleashed a device that could reveal the Skrulls' true nature. Mockingbird was exposed as an alien and murdered alongside the other imposters.[71] In the climactic battle against the Skrulls, the Avengers fought alongside a slew of comrades, most prominently Thor, the new Captain America, Nick Fury and his Secret Warriors, the Thunderbolts, Noh-Varr, and even the Hood's Gang.[72] As a last resort, the Wasp was transformed into a giant living bomb by the Skrulls, forcing Thor to end her life to prevent a detonation. On a happy note, on the other hand, the real heroes replaced by the Skrulls were found to be alive and well, including Spider-Woman and Mockingbird.[73] During the final stand, the director of the Thunderbolts, the unscrupulous Norman Osborn, who used to be the villain Green Goblin, killed the escaping Veranke. Using his technology from OsCorp, Osborn was not affected by the corruption of Iron Man's Starktech.[73] S.H.I.E.L.D. was decommissioned and replaced by H.A.M.M.E.R., with Osborn as its head in charge of the Initiative.[74] The Avengers remained outlaws. However, instead of old friends, they were now opposed by enemies. A dark reign descended upon Earth's Mightiest Heroes.[75]

Dark Reign[]

In the aftermath of the Secret Invasion, with Norman Osborn officially in charge of Earth's superhumans, the Avengers found out Luke Cage and Jessica Jones' baby had been kidnapped by the Skrull who had posed as Jarvis.[76] The Avengers reassembled themselves in Captain America's Apartment, now in possession of his successor, Bucky Barnes, the new Captain America. Barton was accompanied by his ex-wife, the resurgent Mockingbird, while Echo disappeared without notice. The face of the Skrull invasion, the real Spider-Woman was also offered asylum. In desperation, Cage and Jones, assisted by Ms. Marvel, reached out to the Avengers and the Fantastic Four for the sake of their child, with no avail.[77] Seeing no alternative, Cage contacted Osborn for help. Using his vast resources, Osborn easily tracked and assassinated the Skrull, bringing the baby back to safety. However, Cage betrayed Osborn and escaped Avengers Tower, returning to his outlawed group of renegade Avengers.[78]

In charge of the official Avengers' team, Osborn reformulated its roster, revamping many super-villains with the legacy mantle of classic Avengers. His dark iteration of Avengers, which counted with the mentally unbalanced Sentry in it, was received with disgust by its predecessors. Spider-Woman was sent to Osborn in another attempt to trick him, in an attempt to ambush his Avengers with the power-dampening technology at the abandoned Hellfire Club Mansion. Osborn sent the Hood's Gang instead, since he had forged a partnership with the Hood. Severely outnumbered and having the power dampener immediately destroyed, the Avengers were easily subdued by the Hood and his syndicate of villains, being forced to retreat. Clint Barton was profoundly offended by what Osborn had done with the reputation of the Avengers and publicly denounced him on live TV.[79] Despite their differences, the two opposing Avengers teams joined forces, being guided by Thor, to protect the world from a frigid nightmare caused by the frost giant Ymir, who got defeated by the Twilight Sword. After victory, Thor did not allow Osobrn to tackle the outlawed Avengers.[80] Back to hiding, the team discussed their new directions, with Barton being appointed as leader with Ms. Marvel as second-in-command. Around this time, Iron Fist distanced himself from the group to deal with his own adventures.[81]

The group was surprised by Doctor Strange, who happened upon their hideout seeking help. No longer the Sorcerer Supreme, Strange searched for his substitute, with the Hood being on his track in a hunt for the vacant position. The Avengers immediately offered help in opposing the Hood, who weirdly operated alone. In a stolen Quinjet, the team followed the path of chaos to New Orleans,[82] where they were attacked by Madame Masque, the only partner the Hood had. In the commotion, the Hood demonstrated signs of demonic corruption, while Brother Voodoo emerged as the new Sorcerer Supreme.[83] Strange and Voodoo were able to reveal the demon who had possessed the Hood as Dormammu and, with the Avengers' and Daimon Hellstrom's assistance, exorcized him.[84]

Abandoned by its leader, the Hood's Gang realized they had been working as Osborn's mercenaries without their consent. Wishing to get Osborn's attention, they caused trouble in the city, alerting the Avengers instead. Having reverse-engineered the power-dampening technology, the Hood's Gang incapacitated the Avengers.[85] As a regular human with no special abilities, Mockingbird was unaffected and resisted the villains. Simultaneously, Osborn arrived with his Avengers on the scene[86] and made a deal with the Hood's Gang to kill the Avengers. Piloting their Quinjet, Mockingbird was able to retrieve her teammates and flee. However, Luke Cage underwent cardiac arrest due to the effects of the dampener and was taken by the Avengers to the Night Nurse's clinic.[87] Osborn was able to arrest him in it, but was legally forced to provide him with medical treatment.[88] The Avengers assembled a legion of friends of Cage to break him out of Avengers Tower and Osborn's clutches. While the Avengers attacked the Initiative installations in Camp Hammond, their friends raided Avengers Tower to rescue Cage. Osborn was not unprepared, having installed a microscopic bomb on Cage's heart during surgery.[89] With Hank Pym's assistance, the device was removed and it detonated one of Osborn's luxurious houses after the Avengers went into hiding again.[90]

Siege of Asgard[]

Going against the other Avengers, Clint Barton ultimately decided to assassinate Osborn as the only viable solution he could fathom.[85] As a one-man army, Barton infiltrated Avengers Tower. Despite successfully knocking most of Osborn's Avengers out, he was ultimately defeated and incarcerated.[91] As Cage recovered from his condition, the Avengers assembled to rescue Barton. Osborn was able to learn about their hideout, but found the place evacuated, for the Avengers had foreseen the attack. This distracted the villainous Avengers. In the mission, which involved retrieving Barton from a Helicarrier, Jessica Jones reassumed her heroic identity to assist her allies. In one of Nick Fury's secret bases, the Avengers were met by a resurrected Steve Rogers, the original Captain America.[92] One of Osborn's associates, the God of Mischief Loki, had been manipulation many of the recent crises involving the different teams of Avengers.[84][93][94] He re-empowered the Hood using the Norn Stones, a source that extended to the entire Hood's Gang, that now operated in a legit way under Osborn's Initiative.[90] The Avengers resisted multiple murder attempts from different members of the Hood's Gang. The Avengers not only joined forces with Rogers, but also with Nick Fury and his Secret Warriors.[95]

Loki's ultimate move in his machinations involving Osborn was to convince him about the danger of having the magical realm of Asgard on Earth. He claimed that this was a threat to Osborn's influence and suggested that a disaster should be crafted to justify a siege to the golden city.[96] Together, Loki and Osborn managed to trick Volstagg of the Warriors Three into fighting the U-Foes in a stadium full of people, who became casualties. This gave Osborn reason to move against Asgard, mobilizing all of the forces under his command such as the Avengers, H.A.M.M.E.R., the Thunderbolts, the Initiative, and the Hood's Gang. The mighty Thor defended his home, but was put down by Osborn.[97] Deciding Osborn should be stopped, Steve Rogers reassembled the Avengers to join the war, being backed by other parties, such as Maria Hill, Nick Fury's Secret Warriors the Young Avengers, and Tony Stark.[98]

After Osborn was taken down by the Avengers, he ordered the Sentry to destroy Asgard, leaving nothing but rubble. Regaining advantage, Osborn had Loki teleport the Hood and his Gang empowered by the Norn Stones to oppose the Avengers. However, the Sentry proved to be an uncontrollable force.[99] In regret, Loki realized that he had gone too far and used the Norn Stones to empower the heroes fighting on the side of Asgard. Noticing the trickery, the Sentry killed Loki to reverse the process. As a last resource, Tony Stark managed to take control of the H.A.M.M.E.R. Helicarrier to drop it on the Sentry which made him revert to his human form. Powerless, the Sentry was mercy-killed by Thor, who disposed of his remains in the Sun.[100]

During the final stand against Osborn, the Hood and Madame Masque were able to vanish.[101] In desperation to get the Hood repowered, Madame Masque sought help in Los Angeles with her father, Count Nefaria, an all-powerful crimemaster and old nemesis of the classic Avengers. In order to track down the Hood, the formerly outlawed Avengers blackmailed his cousin and associate, John King, being able to track down his location. The Avengers brutally assaulted Count Nefaria to incapacitate him. Emerging victorious, they were able to put all three villains into custody.[102] In the aftermath of the siege, the Registration Act was decommissioned and H.A.M.M.E.R. was dissolved. Steve Rogers stepped in as head of security and the responsible for the superhuman community and the Avengers.[100]

Heroic Age[]

With the return of the Avengers under Steve Rogers' supervision, Cage was not comfortable with operating in this condition after such a long time hiding from justice. As an alternative, Rogers provided Cage free jurisdiction to assemble his own team of New Avengers to operate in his terms. Moreover, Tony Stark ceded the reformed Avengers Mansion to serve as their headquarters. Rogers' only condition was having Victoria Hand, Norman Osborn's former scond in command, to act as a liaison, because he felt she could provide an important insight to the team. With Iron Fist and Jessica Jones at his side, Cage gathered most of his former allies in the team, with the new addition of the Thing from the Fantastic Four.[8]

The New Avengers faced their baptism of fire when the Eye of Agamotto happened upon and possessed Cage, prompting him to be sought by Doctor Strange and Daimon Hellstrom, who had also been corrupted by demonic forces. While Doctor Strange regained control, Iron Fist took the Eye of Agamotto and got banished to another plane after the intervention of the Sorcerer Supreme, Doctor Voodoo. This unleashed a rupture in reality, causing Earth to be vulnerable to a hellish invasion.[103] After a convoluted series of disasters,[104] the New Avengers realized the spirit of Agamotto himself reclaimed the item. The Avengers challenged Agamotto for a mystic duel, sending a mystically empowered Wolverine as their warrior.[105] Doctor Voodoo intervened and sacrificed his life to ensure the defeat of a power-hungry Agamotto. Reality was protected[106] and the Avengers returned to the Mansion, which was reformed by Damage Control. Moreover, Strange joined the team and invited his associate Wong to serve as a housekeeper, while Cage and Jones hired the unbeatable Squirrel Girl to be their daughter's nanny.[107]

New H.A.M.M.E.R.[]

The extremist Superia gathered former agents of H.A.M.M.E.R. in preparation for resurgence. Hand had intelligence about her activities and dispatched the New Avengers to investigate. In the ensuing conflict, Mockingbird was mortally wounded by gunshot.[108] As some of the Avengers tended to their injured ally, Superia was able to outpower them and escape.[109] The incident led the New Avengers to suspect Victoria Hand could still be loyal to H.A.M.M.E.R.,[110] but she proved her allegiances to the Avengers when exposing Superia's whereabouts after being contacted. Superia was in possession of a mixture of the Super-Soldier Serum and the Infinity Formula that Fury had contact with in the late 1950s. The substance, although relatively unknown, was administered to save Mockingbird's life. Meanwhile, the incarcerated Superia demonstrated her plan had been influence H.A.M.M.E.R. from jail all along.[111]

When the Asgardian god of fear, the deadly Serpent, resurfaced with the help of the Red Skull's daughter Sin, a group of super humans was empowered by magical hammers similar to Mjolnir. As the Worthy, they spread fear around the world to venerate their master in his plans of becoming the All-Father of Asgard. Part of Sin's attack involved raiding America with an army of Nazi soldiers, her own Blitzkrieg.[112] A corrupted Thing caused the Avengers Tower to fall in a deadly combat against the Red Hulk.[113] The Avengers Mansion was also attacked by Sin's forces. As Squirrel-Girl struggled to keep her protégée safe, Daredevil assisted her in fending off the illegitimate soldiers. Out of gratitude, Cage and Jones invited him to join the Avengers, which he accepted.[41] Moreover, as one of the Serpent's generals, Sin stormed Washington, D.C., with a Nazi army, causing Captain America's apparent death, which prompted Steve Rogers to step back as the star-spangled soldier.[114] All over the globe, the Avengers kept resisting the destructive Worthy.[115] Wielding Uru weapons manufactured by Iron Man in the furnaces of Nidavellir, many Avengers were empowered as the Mighty to oppose the Serpent's army. Thor bravely slayed the Serpent at the ultimate cost of his own life. The war-torn Avengers had a hard work to rebuild the world ahead of themselves but were able to do it without fear.[116]

Hand had the New Avengers supervise the transition of Norman Osborn from the Raft to another prison. The transference failed, as Osborn was freed alongside Superia by corrupted Raft officers and returned as the head of a revived H.A.M.M.E.R., which now counted with forces from A.I.M., Hydra, and the Hand.[117] The paranoic Osborn also reformed his version of Earth's Mightiest Heroes, an iteration of the New Dark Avengers.[118] At Stark Resilient laboratories at Paramus, New Jersey, the New Avengers joined Iron Man to fight a new version of Ultimo, controlled by H.A.M.M.E.R., that was trying to steal Iron Man's armor. Though the giant robot fell, a smaller robot attacked Wolverine, obtained a blood sample and escaped.[119] Back to Avengers Mansion, the New Avengers founded it surrounded by protesters who rebelled against them, having a dispiriting effect on the team. As Victoria Hand dispatched the team to deal with Atlantean terrorists in Miami, they received wrong coordinates. By the time the New Avengers lately arrived at the correct place, Osborn and his New Dark Avengers had already solved the problem in a glorious display.[120]

The two Avengers teams battled each other. However, the heroic Avengers were caught unprepared, being also rejected by civilians who blamed them for the crisis. Doctor Strange aborted the mission and teleported the team away, only to be surprised by the sudden appearance of Ragnarok, the cyborg clone of Thor who had been rebuilt by A.I.M.[121] The artificial creature was destroyed by the Avengers after a difficult fight. However, when returning to their base they experienced a bitter victory, as S.W.A.T. wished to detain them.[122] Outnumbered by the authority forces, the New Avengers were teleported to Sanctum Sanctorum to prepare a plan. Afraid of Osborn and of the violent public opposition, Jessica Jones disappeared with no trace with her child and Squirrel-Girl, leaving Cage in turmoil. Meanwhile, Hand was exposed as a triple agent, who was in contact with Osborn under the orders of Captain America, who happened to be in Osborn's custody. Another mole within H.A.M.M.E.R. was the Dark Avenger Skaar, who betrayed his team by attacking them[123] and freeing Captain America. The New Avengers arrived on the scene and defeated all the New Dark Avengers.[124] Osborn succumbed to his own greed for power.[125]

New Avengers: Disassembled[]

After the war between the Avengers and the X-Men, Luke Cage decided to leave the team in order to raise his daughter in peace,[126] which incited the New Avengers to disassemble. However, the defunct team became a target of the spirit of Daniel Drumm, who had vowed revenge for the death of his brother, Doctor Voodoo. Having possessed the body of Victoria Hand, he attacked magicians to empower himself. This attracted the attentions of authorities, who investigated Avengers Mansion.[127] Daniel Drumm assassinated Hand and an FBI agent to bring trouble to the Avengers, as S.H.I.E.L.D. determined Doctor Strange was the murderer.[128] In extreme confusion, the other Avengers team arrived on the scene. Drumm took the opportunity to randomly possess different members of both groups to spark a battle. To solve the situation, Doctor Strange called upon a challenge between sorcerers.[129] Strange battled all the Avengers by himself, resisting a plethora of different attacks. Knowing his friends faced danger, Cage decided to return to Avengers Mansion in his last act as an Avenger. Drumm possessed his body only to find himself permanently trapped, being unable to corrupt others. This enabled Doctor Strange to perform an exorcism.[9]

As a result, Strange's former mentor, the Ancient One, appeared in the battlefield and bestowed upon his disciple the mantle of Sorcerer Supreme again due to his valiant efforts in protecting the dimension without it being his duty anymore.[9] In the aftermath of the battle, the Avengers honored the memory of Victoria Hand and remembered their moments together. Cage returned the possession of Avengers Mansion to Tony Stark.[9] The building became the headquarters of the mutant-centered team known as the Avengers Unity Division,[130] some of the New Avengers became part of other Avengers teams,[131][132] and Cage planned to revive the Heroes for Hire.[133]
