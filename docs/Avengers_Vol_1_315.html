Synopsis for "Doomsday Plus One!"

A mysterious energy flux has apparently left a room in the Avengers mansion, one that contains Sersi, Thor, Jarvis, Spider-Man and Captain America, as the only remnant of the universe. However, they soon discover that another structure exists in the white void, the PolyDyne Industries building, and they make their way there. When they find Nebula there, she attacks them. During the battle, Harker tries to tell the Avengers how to deactivate his convertor before it's too late but Nebula disintegrates him for betraying her before he can tell them what to do. While Thor keeps Nebula busy, Captain America learns from another scientist how the Atomic Compressor could be shut off but, when he's unable to do it himself, he tells Spider-Man what has to be done. Using his webbing, Spider-Man succeeds in pulling the machine's plug before it could reach 100% efficiency and become self-sustaining, thereby annihilating the universe. With the machine no longer generating the Genesis Pulse that had thrown the universe into a state of complete neutrality, the universe and everything in it is restored, with virtually no time having passed for the universe while it had been neutralized. After discovering that Nebula has escaped, Captain America reminds everyone that Iron Man and the Vision had been on their way to her ship when the universe blinked out and so he suspects that she might find a surprise awaiting her there.

Notes

  • When Thor questions Nebula about how she escaped the time vortex into which she and Dr. Druid had fallen in Avengers #298, Nebula seems confused by what he's saying. This is the first sign of a retcon that will establish that, despite what was presented in two storylines written by Walt Simonson, the "Kang-Nebula" who appeared in Avengers #291-298 and Fantastic Four #337-341 was actually not the space pirate Nebula after all. Two years later, in Avengers Annual #21, the Nebula imposter will be revealed to be Princess Ravonna.
