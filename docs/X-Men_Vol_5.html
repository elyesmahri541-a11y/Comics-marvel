Accolades

  • Jen Bartel won Eisner Award in 2022 in "Best Cover Artist" category for Women's History Month Variants for X-Men (Vol. 5) #19 and other Marvel books, and also covers for DC books. Clayton Cowles earned a nomination in "Best Lettering" category for his work on issues of this and other comic volumes.[1]
    • Eisner Award is given each year for the works of the previous year, so, while Clayton Cowles lettered all issues of this volume, only X-Men (Vol. 5) #17–21 — released in 2021 — could have been nominated for the Award in 2022.
