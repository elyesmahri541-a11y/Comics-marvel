History

Mole Pete was a thief who was never taken seriously. One day he broke into Gyro Gearloose's lab, but he was able to steal only his radio because of the alarm, he then ran inside a cave and fell into a hole that brought him between two giant monsters, the radio accidentally turned on and Pete found out that he could use the music to control the monsters, forcing them to hide into Earth's depths. The underground inhabitants called Terrafirmians crowned him king of their realm and Pete used them to cause earthquakes to get revenge of the ones who used to mock him. When the Fantastic Four found him, he played the song in reverse, calling the monsters back, but during the fight the Terrafirmians decided to help the heroes and the monsters were eventually imprisoned. Mole Pete was then taken away by the Four adventurers.[1]

Trivia

  • The reason behind Terrafirmians' decision of helping the heroes came from the fact that they're annoyed by Pete, who always told them the same story of how he had came down there.
