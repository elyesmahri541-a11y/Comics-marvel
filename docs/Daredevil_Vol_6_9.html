Synopsis for "No Devils, Only God: Part 4"

After reaching out to Reed Richards and playing chess with him, Matt asked him if he believed that God exists and Reed revealed himself to be an agnostic before winning the game. Later, Mindy bought Matt a drink and apologized to him for setting him up during dinner with her family, claiming that she wanted to remind Tom that they were "never safe." When Matt talked to Sister Elizabeth at the church, he told her that he needed a sign and asked her how he would know when God sent one. North was locked in a room with an injured Higgins by officers who beat him up and were working for the Owl. The officers threatened to kill anybody he cared about unless he left the city.

After leaving the church, Matt overheard a man looking for his missing son Leelo before hearing his name elsewhere. Leelo joined a group of criminals who were attempting to "ransom" him to his father to get the money to travel to Texas. Daredevil appeared and defeated all but one in a fight. He blocked punches from the last criminal before Leelo knocked the latter unconscious with a baseball bat. Matt told Leelo that his father missed him and that he should go home. Then, he walked through the rain to Libris Books, where he kissed Mindy before having sex with her.
