History

X-Man and the Life Seed created this plane during the battle against the X-Men and numerous other mutants on Quadra Island.[1] The Life Seed was the "spark" that initiated the process and Nate Grey was the "kindling" whose energies created and merged with the world;[2] Nate essentially became the world.[3] Nate transported the mutants from the battle to this plane, erased their memories, and molded this reality based on a "collective vision" of a mutant-only paradise that he pulled from everyone's minds.[3] Eventually, the X-Men learned the truth and confronted Nate, who agreed to return them home.[2]
