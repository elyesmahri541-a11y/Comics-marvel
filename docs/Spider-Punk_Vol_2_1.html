SPIDER-PUNK’S GOING SOLO!

The Spider-Band is MISSING, and Hobie Brown’s in L.A. to unravel the mystery of what happened to his friends. But he’ll have to confront his inner demons along the way, and those of a new adversary — MZ. NEGATIVE! And if that weren’t enough, she’s teamed up with SCORPION to be the ultimate thorn in Hobie’s side… Cody Ziglar and Justin Mason reunite AGAIN to make even more musical magic with your favorite rock ’n’ roll Spider-fam!
