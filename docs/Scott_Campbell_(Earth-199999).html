History

Whiplash #1 was sent to destroy an unknown bogey, Iron Man, after his activity in Afghanistan. During the altercation, his wing was destroyed, and he ejected. His parachute was jammed, but he was saved by Iron Man.[1]

Notes

  • Tim Rigby portrayed Scott Campbell, while David Zyler did the voice of the character in Iron Man.
  • Despite being verbally referred to as "Whiplash #1," this character is credited as "Viper #1" in the film's credits.
