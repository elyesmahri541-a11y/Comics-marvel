Beaming with the power of one million exploding suns, Bob Reynolds was once the shining paragon known as the Sentry. A drug addict who stumbled into an experimental super-soldier formula called the Golden Sentry Serum that gave him inmeasurable powers,[27] Bob used his new-found abilities to become an exemplary and beloved member of the blooming super hero community with his own sidekick named Scout. Reynolds was eventually pushed beyond his limits by the Void, a dark entity that manifested from the hero's fractured psyche; his nemesis and the counterbalance of all the good Bob did as the Sentry. With the help of Mister Fantastic and Doctor Strange, Bob erased the memory of the Sentry's existence from the entire world—even himself—to prevent the Void from resurfacing.[28]

Despite previous attempts to rebury the Sentry when it surfaced as he lived a mundane life,[7] Reynolds eventually came to the attention of the New Avengers. Fully recovering his memories and driving away the Void, the Sentry came back to light once and for all.[29] After the ascension of Norman Osborn as the head of the super hero community, he drafted the Sentry into his own twisted Avengers.[30] Osborn's manipulations caused the Void to return and possess the Sentry in a catastrophic fashion during the Siege of Asgard, resulting in both of their deaths.[31] Through a Life Seed and a Death Seed, Bob would later be returned to life as a Horsemen of Death by the Apocalypse Twins.[22]

With the help of Doctor Strange, Bob set up a pocket dimension inside his own mind called Sentry World, where he could become the Sentry once a day to keep the Void at bay in the real world. A resentful Scout broke into Sentry World in an attempt to steal his old mentor's power, but the Sentry emerged triumphant, in the process absorbing the Void into himself.[32] The Sentry met his end again at the hands of Knull, the God of the Symbiotes, in an ill-fated attempt to stop his invasion of Earth.[23] With the help of Valkyrie, the Sentry's spirit moved on to the afterlife,[33] leaving his powers to manifest in six people back on Earth, one of whom—Mallory Gibbs—became their sole possessor as Solarus.[34] Sentry eventually returned to life and began operating once again on Earth.[25]

History

Early Years[]

In March 1947, Canada's Department K and remnants of the United States' Operation: Rebirth pooled their resources to become Project Sentry, an attempt to not only recreate the Super Soldier Serum, but in post-war hubris, magnify its effects one hundred thousand times over.[35] Unfortunately, within thirty years it had been divided into nearly a thousand isolated sub-projects, with lab work farmed out to private contractors. The overall administration of the Project was virtually nil.[35] Precisely where all of its resources ended up over the decades, what they might have begun, and where they ultimately led, is currently unknown.[35]

At seven years old, Robert Reynolds learned about Laika, the little Russian dog who survived two harsh winters on the streets of Moscow and was sent into space by the Soviet Union to advance space exploration. Reynolds thought about how she suffered on the mission, which made him want to save her and love her for the rest of her life. Learning of her death saddened him greatly, resulting in a year of cry. This was the first time the young boy had felt such a devastating emotion. Later, Reynolds would recognize this as, in a way, the first time the Void entered his heart.[25] Over time, the Void would only grow as Robert was met with numerous tragedies in his life that he could not fix.[36]

Having reached a certain age, Reynolds, now a meth addict, broke into the laboratory of an unnamed professor. It was there that he discovered the Golden Sentry Serum. Consuming the compound, Robert Reynolds gained the power of a million exploding suns. This act killed his partner and the lab's guards. He then asked the Professor to make him more of the compound.[27] Project: Sentry, appalled at what their irresponsibility had wrought, persuaded Reynolds to let them test his powers at their facility, but they could not contain him for long, nor curb his desire to use his powers for the greater good of all, and the Sentry debuted before a world that had almost forgotten superhuman Samaritans. After trouncing the school bully who had tormented him, Reynolds sewed together a costume and made his debut as the heroic Sentry.[35]

The Sentry was an optimistic and socially accepted hero who stood in marked contrast to the mostly freakish nature of the other heroes. He had connections to Mister Fantastic, Iron Man, the Hulk, Spider-Man, and Professor X. One of very few super heroes active during the years just prior to the Fantastic Four's emergence, Sentry gained new importance when the new wave of heroes rose to prominence. Almost instantly deducing Spider-Man's secret identity, he became a role model for the young hero, as well as an ally to the X-Men, an equal to Reed Richards, and a friend to the outcast Hulk. The Sentry even fought Doctor Doom and dealt with a Cosmic Cube alongside the Fantastic Four, briefly stabilizing the immensely powerful cube by holding it with his bare hands.[37] With the help of the X-Men, the Sentry defeated his enemy, the General.[38] On one occasion, he also pursued Doctor Octopus, encountering Spider-Man for the first time after the hero intervened. The Sentry left Spider-Man uneasy for months by revealing that he knew his secret identity. Furthermore, since Spider-Man was still early in his crime-fighting career, The Sentry did not fully trust him.[26] In one of his greatest displays of power, he fought Galactus, the Devourer of Worlds, to a standstill.[26]

During this period, the Sentry married the love of his life, Lindy and took the young Scout as his sidekick. Lindy got swept up in it all, excited at the prospect of meeting other heroes. She eventually learned of Robert's addiction but did nothing about it. She even feared that taking the serum must have awakened something of biblical proportions. She later stated that she should have killed him on their wedding night.[27]

The Void[]

The arrival of the Void, a shadowy monster that exploited its enemy's greatest fears, ended the Sentry's charmed life. The Void nearly killed Scout, drove Hulk into a rampage, and murdered over one million people in Manhattan.[39] The Void also triggered a crime wave in New York's underworld by allying with Wilson Fisk, the Kingpin. Realizing this, the Sentry sought Spider-Man's help, given the hero's experience dealing with Fisk. The duo found Fisk in the company of the Void, but the situation was not what they had expected. Fisk was impaled by one of the Void's intangible tendrils. The Void was not working with the Kingpin, but rather using its powers to manipulate his mind. Fisk was saved by Spider-Man, who freed him from the tendril.[26] Discovering that the Void had been the dark aspect of his own powers, the Sentry teamed with Reed Richards and Dr. Strange to create a system which made Earth's entire population—including Richards, Strange, and the Sentry—forget his existence. With the Sentry inactive and forgotten, the Void vanished.[39]

In reality, the Void was Reynolds' own repressed persona, powered by the Sentry's own powers. The General had captured Reynolds and brought him to mutant illusionist Mastermind, who implanted a "mental virus" in the Sentry's mind. This virus created delusions that if the Sentry used his powers, a devil would appear, intent on destroying the world.[40] As a subconscious cry for help, Reynolds implanted his memories into the mind of comic book writer Paul Jenkins, who wrote the Sentry's repressed memories into a series of comic books.[40]

Years later, Robert's memories returned, and along with them the Void, who rampaged across Europe, murdering many members of the Super Heroes of Europe (SHE). The Sentry contacted his former colleagues, but the only one who recalled their shared history was the Hulk. When the Void returned to Manhattan to continue its goal of destroying the entire universe and more, the pieces began to fall in place and the Sentry's former friends assembled to defend him and the city. Remembering the dual nature of his powers, Sentry and Reed Richards reactivated the Watchtower, dispelling the Void and making the world forget the Sentry once again.[41][7]

New Avengers[]

Later, however, Robert's memories of the Sentry again returned, and he demanded to be placed in the Raft, S.H.I.E.L.D.'s high-security prison for super-villains, claiming that he had killed his wife. Lawyers Matt Murdock and Foggy Nelson, along with bodyguard Luke Cage and S.H.I.E.L.D. agent Spider-Woman, traveled to the Raft to visit the Sentry in order to find out what had happened to him and his wife. Soon after their arrival the supervillain Electro staged a massive jailbreak, freeing almost ninety supervillains who then attacked Murdock, Nelson, Cage, and Drew, along with the newly arrived Captain America and Spider-Man. During the ensuing melee the group was confronted by the alien symbiote, Carnage. Foggy Nelson, thrown into the cell containing Robert Reynolds, attempted to convince the Sentry to help them. Without saying a word, the Sentry blasted through nine levels of the complex carrying Carnage into space, where he ripped the alien symbiote in half.[42]

Inspired by this adventure, the other heroes soon formed the New Avengers, but Sentry vanished before they could invite him to join them. The Sentry stayed in hiding but remained a person of great interest to S.H.I.E.L.D. and the Avengers, both of whom were fascinated and worried by his powers.[43] Eventually, S.H.I.E.L.D. and the New Avengers confronted the Sentry with several disturbing facts: the wife he claimed to have killed was alive, and the only reference in the world that could be found of the Sentry was in comic books, stories invented and written down by the imagination of a comics writer. Unable to face these shocking events, the Sentry fled. Resurfacing as Robert Reynolds at his home, the Sentry was again confronted by the Avengers and S.H.I.E.L.D., along with the Inhumans, the Fantastic Four and the X-Men. He cried, saying that he had warned them of the Void's coming and that it was now too late. The group was suddenly attacked by the Void. Reed Richards showed Reynolds a recording he had made himself as a plea for help. He had asked that, should Mister Fantastic believe Reynolds was beyond saving, as a precaution against his powers spiraling out of control due to his current condition, Richards take measures such as sending him to the Negative Zone, launching him into space toward Skrull Empire territory, or aiming the Ultimate Nullifier at his head and using it. Hoping that he would eventually view the recording alongside Richards, Reynolds had instructed his future self to do whatever Richards and his allies said. Robert, along with the help of Emma Frost, discovered that the reason no one remembered him was not because of his own attempts to erase the Void, but because of Mastermind's manipulation. With this new-found knowledge, the Void disappeared, and Emma Frost resurrected the Sentry. The New Avengers offered him membership, partly to keep an eye on him and partly because they would need his power, which he accepted. The Sentry's headquarters the Watchtower reappeared on top of Stark Tower, where it supposedly had been originally built.[29]

Yelena Belova attacked the Avengers and absorbed the Sentry's powers. After Belova defeated each of the Avengers, she was defeated by the manifestation of the Void, which enveloped and incapacitated her. The Sentry told Belova that absorbing his powers had exposed her to the Void, but if she answered his questions, he could send the Void away. A.I.M. liquefied Belova before she should divulge any info.[44]

When the Scarlet Witch used her powers to alter reality and create a world where mutants rose to power, the Sentry, along with many others, had his life and mind changed to fit the new reality. He was an ordinary man with a wife, Lindy, and a son. Reynolds began to question his sanity after mutants came to power. Having partially resisted the reality alteration, he remembered flying and possessing incredible superpowers, but this seemed to be a symptom of "Dead-End Syndrome"—a condition becoming common among humanity due to a sense of inadequacy following the rise of Magneto and mutantkind across the world. Unable to keep his job, he developed agoraphobia. At his wife's insistence, Reynolds began seeing a psychologist, Dr. Stephen Strange, who, prior to the reality shift, had been the great magic-user Doctor Strange. The two discussed his psychosis, but no significant progress was made. Like the Sentry, his dark counterpart, the Void, also resisted the reality shift and began to manifest as a wall of blackness that followed Robert everywhere. Reynolds believed this blackness was real but remained uncertain, unable to distinguish between reality and madness. Eventually, the Scarlet Witch altered reality once more, restoring everything to the way it had been before.[45][46]

The Sentry fought the Thunderbolts alongside the New Avengers. Photon confronted him directly, dissipating his unfathomable, escalating energies into a pocket universe. Sentry found Photon's power familiar, believing he had known him father, Captain Marvel. Sentry described him as a noble man but was interrupted by Photon, who said he had heard such things about him father before. Sentry stated that Mar-Vell would not jeopardize the entire planet over some grudge match. Photon asked who said their fight would take place on Earth. Along with Captain America, Sentry was sent to the Microverse by Photon. There, the Sentry and Photon battled, both holding back, while Captain America could only try to survive the destruction caused by the fight. Photon extracted everyone, causing them to emerge from Iron Man's armor. The resulting damage took him out of the fight. The Sentry finally decided to fight in earnest, but aware of the risk, Photon sent him back to the Microverse, noting that it would take him some time to escape on his own.[47]

When various mutants lost their powers due to what the Scarlet Witch did at the end of the House of M event, she did not destroy the energy they possessed. Instead, the energies were removed from their bodies and went out into space. After leaving orbit and entering the atmosphere, these energies were absorbed by Michael Pointer, a mutant capable of absorbing energy from others mutants, transforming him into an immensely powerful and dangerous being known as the Collective.[48] However, Michael was not even aware that he was a mutant.[49] Captain America called upon the Sentry, but as the hero was going through a difficult time, shaken by something the Void had said, it took some persistence on the Captain's part to get him out of bed and into the fight.[50] He arrived at the battlefield and saved Ms. Marvel from the Collective by carrying the entity into space. After trading a few blows, the Collective managed to fend off the Sentry and headed toward Earth.[51] Iron Man intercepted the Collective and slowed its advance. The Collective lunged at the armored hero, but the impact was avoided by the Sentry, who struck it at high speed. The two fought together, but when combat proved ineffective, Iron Man accepted a suggestion to let the Collective reach its destination, while he dragged the Sentry away from it at high speed.[52] The Collective arrived at Genosha, an island that had become a desolate wasteland where millions of mutants had once lived before being massacred in a devastating attack. The Collective briefly restored Magneto's powers and took control of him, revealing itself to be Xorn, the impostor who had previously posed as Magneto and trashed New York. Upon the arrival of the New Avengers, Xorn reanimated the corpses at the site to attack them. The Sentry suggested sinking the island as an option, but Captain America vetoed the idea. The Captain instructed the Sentry to keep Magneto on the island. Iron Man told the Sentry to remove his belt due to the metal in it, a request the hero complied with. Sentry suggested throwing Magneto into the Sun, but Captain insisted on keeping him on the island. Sentry flew toward Magneto but was hurled away, landing near a confused Michael, whom he greeted. Sentry grabbed Magneto by the neck and was surprised when the restored Master of Magnetism asked for death. Thanks to a joint effort led by Iron Man, Magneto was freed and lost his powers once again. Iron Man contained the energy within a energy field and told Sentry to do what he had previously suggested, hurl it into the Sun. Sentry did so, and was surprised to see the energy take the shape of a face as it dissipated.[49]

Later still, the Sentry was sent by S.H.I.E.L.D. to subdue a rogue Iron Man. During the course of the battle, Iron Man hacked into C.L.O.C.—the Sentry's robot prioritizing and warning system—and fed alerts of multiple false emergencies into the Sentry's head, leaving him incapacitated for a few hours.[53]

Looking for the Truth[]

Having returned to the world, the Sentry captured the hearts of the public, with newspapers referring to him as "the Golden Guardian," and he saved hundreds of lives on a daily basis. He easily thwarted a large-scale attack on Manhattan led by Attuma, whom the Sentry killed by tearing his head from his body after striking him while flying at high speed. He also stopped Terrax from achieving his goal of conquering Earth and wiping out humanity when the villain launched his attack in Ukraine. The Sentry easily defeated Terrax, avoiding all his attacks, injuring his right hand, and shattering his cosmic axe. Reynolds' psychological problems, however, had worsened. Unable to reconcile that Robert Reynolds, the Sentry and the Void were the same being, the Sentry contained the Void in a vault in the Watchtower. At C.L.O.C.'s urging, Reynolds' psychiatrist Dr. Cornelius Worth entered the vault and found only a chair and a mirror. When Cornelius confronted Reynolds with this, Reynolds became confused and ran to the fairgrounds where he first gained his powers.

Cornelius followed Reynolds to the fairgrounds where the Sentry and the Void had been fighting. The Void claimed that Reynolds transformed into the Void rather than the Sentry, and that the Sentry was merely a guilt-borne byproduct of that transformation. With only half of Robert Reynolds' memories (the Void possessing the other half), the Sentry was not sure the Void was wrong. The Sentry chased the Void away and then explained to Cornelius why Reynolds had hired him in the first place. Reynolds subconsciously wanted someone to reveal the Void's existence to the world so he would no longer have to hide that aspect of himself. The Sentry told Cornelius how Reynolds really gained his powers as a teenager: by stealing the Professor's Golden Sentry Serum and ingesting it to get high. He also told Cornelius that he knew all along that the Void was never in the Watchtower's vault. He knew that if Reynolds believed that he believed the Void to be in the Watchtower, the Void would be there. This kept the world safe from the Void for as long as the Sentry could keep up the charade.[54]

The Sentry then visited Dr. Strange to determine if he was real or not. Strange tricked the Sentry into traveling to his old Professor's research facility where the two men confined the Sentry in an illusionary realm where he was a mental patient and super heroes did not exist in an attempt to prevent any further destruction. The Sentry broke free of the illusion and after confronting the Professor who made the original serum, he traveled to Antarctica to confront the Void and discover what memories of Reynolds the Void has in his mind.[55] The Void claimed that Reynolds had actually ingested a super-saturated, exponentially more potent, version of the Super-Soldier formula that created Captain America. This was considered dangerous by the government because the Sentry's blood could be used to create more of the serum, perhaps enough for the entire world. Several failed attempts were made to kill him. Enraged by this revelation, the Sentry threw the Void into the Sun, telling his enemy that he no longer needed him to balance his own actions of good. The Void promised to return.[35]

Civil War[]

When the heroes had to decide whether or not to register, the Sentry was conflicted about his choice. While weighing his options, he found himself in a confrontation with the Absorbing Man. Throughout the fight, the Absorbing Man absorbed the Sentry's power, and the two debated whether possessing power necessarily entails an obligation to use it. The Absorbing Man insisted on seeing his opponent's full power, which the Sentry ultimately revealed. The Sentry had been right to hold back, a something the Absorbing Man learned the hard way. Held by the throat, the Absorbing Man suffered excruciating pain as his nerves burned from the intensity of the Sentry's unleashed power. As his atoms dissolved into photons, neutrons, and solar wind, the Absorbing Man was disintegrated, as his body suffered a catastrophic failure when it proved unable to absorb such a vast amount of power. The Sentry knew he would eventually return from the dead. Following the confrontation, the Sentry decided to register.[56]

During the Superhuman Civil War, the Sentry sided with Iron Man's Pro-Registration program. While trying to escape from a battle between the New Avengers, believing that every path he could choose would ultimately lead to the death of people he knew, the Sentry flew to the moon. There he was confronted by the Inhumans; believed a threat, he was ordered to follow them to Black Bolt's presence in the so-called "Blue Area" on the Moon. Then, after a discussion of the Civil War events with the (still unaware) Inhumans, he rekindled his friendship with them and almost resumed his past relationship with Crystal. He was then confronted by Iron Man himself, who finally convinced a still reluctant Sentry to join him, stating that he'll help Captain America, who is opposed to registration, understand that the world is changing.[57] He later accompanied a S.H.I.E.L.D. squad to battle Wolverine and told him that he did not want to get involved but saw no choice—he claimed that he had to stop the ugly business even if that meant becoming part of it for a while. He then knocked Wolverine unconscious and handed him over to S.H.I.E.L.D.[58] After the conflict ended with Captain America's surrender, the Sentry assisted in managing the damage caused. Three days after the war concluded, the Sentry, to the surprise of many, publicly announced his support for the Registration Act. This, along with other factors, led to a thirty-eight percent increase in recruitment for the pro-registration cause.[59] Two weeks after the climactic battle of the Civil War, the Sentry appeared in public alongside other heroes, including Iron Man, Ms. Marvel, and Wonder Man.[60] He was recruited by Tony Stark to be part of the Mighty Avengers, the newest incarnation of the Avengers team.[10]

Mighty Avengers[]

While at first there was some dispute between the Sentry and his wife, Robert joined the team while Tony Stark and Ms. Marvel offered him assistance to battle his mental issues. He was seen as the most powerful member of the team, but, lacking in proper training on how to use his abilities, he (consequently) frequently apologized for his mistakes (such as causing damage to the city building and then again when he was thrown into a blimp). When Iron Man morphed into the new female Ultron, she analyzed the Sentry, stated his high-power level and pointed out he was very difficult to terminate. He battled Ultron, with neither one seeming to be able to win, until Sentry had to break off to stop the S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier from crashing into Manhattan.[61]

Ultron then initiated a "Plan B" which involved killing Lindy, the Sentry's wife. An enraged Sentry attacked Ultron once more, but in an exchange of blows was knocked away.[62] His teammates Ares and Hank Pym in his Ant-Man costume proceeded to infest Ultron with a virus intent on destroying it and allowing Iron Man to reform himself. Sentry however, assaulted Ultron in retaliation for murdering his wife and almost compromised the Avengers plan by nearly tearing Ultron's head off. Before he could finish, he was knocked away by Ms. Marvel. After Ultron's defeat he returned to the Watchtower to find his wife, Lindy, alive and well—having apparently revived her himself after touching her. Robert was later shocked when he heard his wife, in a terrified state, secretly requesting that Iron Man find a way to either power him down or kill him before he would one day kill them all.[63]

The next day, a symbiote attack infected everyone in New York, including a few of the Mighty and Secret Avengers, with the exception of Sentry, Wonder Man, Ares, Ms. Marvel, Luke Cage and Iron Man. Sentry was one of those who could not be bonded with symbiotes. Sentry eventually had no choice but to knock out Janet, who had grown to giant size and was infected. When Iron Man managed to create an antidote, they track the symbiotes from Latveria, so the Avengers and S.H.I.E.L.D. led an attack against Victor von Doom. After Sentry helped Iron Man breach Doctor Doom's magical defenses during the fight, the three were accidentally transported to the New York of the past by Doom's time platform. After witnessing his past self battle the Void, he encountered Iron Man and Doom. The trio briefly joined forces to use the Fantastic Four's time platform at the Baxter Building to return to the present, a feat accomplished after Sentry easily defeated the Thing. Since the world was destined to forget Sentry's existence, none of this would be remembered by the people of the past. Doom would return roughly half an hour prior using dytherean spells. Upon returning to the present in Latveria, Sentry used his powers to recharge Iron Man's armor, which was malfunctioning following a massive explosion. The battle concluded with Doom's imprisonment after Sentry tore his armor apart.[64]

When the Terrigen Mists fell into the possession of the United States government, the Inhumans attempted to infiltrate the Pentagon to retrieve them. They tried to gain entry by teleporting, courtesy of Lockjaw, but a deflector shield redirected them to a frigid location. The Sentry was waiting for them, having been alerted by Maria Hill that they were on their way. The Sentry remarked that they had brought war to Earth. The sight of the Sentry immediately alarmed the Inhumans, as Earth’s most powerful warrior had been mobilized against them. Karnak instructed Somnus to induce a dream, but the Sentry easily deflected the attack, causing Karnak to fall into a dream state instead. The Sentry expressed hope that Karnak hadn't been injured, a point confirmed by Crystal. Recognizing the extreme danger posed by the Sentry, Crystal was advised by Queen Medusa to let King Black Bolt handle the situation. The Sentry, however, had no desire to fight, claiming he was among those trying to avert the war. After asking if Black Bolt understood the extent of his capabilities, he struck the ground, creating a massive fissure in the ice beneath them. The Sentry had pushed for S.H.I.E.L.D. to allow him to speak with the Inhumans, and as agents were soon to arrive, the conversation began. The Sentry understood what Black Bolt was going through and acknowledged that he, too, wished to avoid this war. He explained that the government would never return the Mists, as they granted the Inhumans too much power. The Sentry suggested that they retreat, leading the government to believe they were weaker than they actually were. He noted that, through negotiation, it might be possible to secure the release of their prisoners. The alternative was unthinkable. The Sentry compared both himself and Black Bolt to nuclear weapons. Their immense power served as an effective deterrent, but a war between them could be best described by the term "mutually assured destruction". The Inhumans departed, with the Sentry hoping that Black Bolt had taken his words to heart. S.H.I.E.L.D. agents arrived, and when the Sentry was asked what had happened, he claimed that the opening in the ice had been caused by global warming.[65]

When american scientists decided to test Terrigen Mists on humans, even knowing that humans could only survive for a short period after exposure, the Inhumans attacked the Pentagon to put an end to this sacrilege. This led to a confrontation between them and the Mighty Avengers. Watching the battle from above, Reynolds reflected on his role as the Sentry: to guard and protect mankind. He pondered what he, an Avenger, was actually avenging. The deaths of civilians in New York were one possibility, but he wondered if that truly mattered. If they even needed a reason to go into battle. They were called heroes, yet they were soldiers following orders. They were neither diplomats nor thinkers. Their talent lay in destruction. The Sentry asked C.L.O.C. to find out what was happening at the Pentagon. An Inhuman, Dewoz, had broken into the facility and freed the Inhuman prisoners. C.L.O.C. asked if it should notify Iron Man, to which the Sentry replied, "No". When Ares was disabled, C.L.O.C. suggested that the Sentry join the fight, as the removal of Ares from the conflict, according to its calculations, increased the Inhumans' chance of victory to fifty-nine percent. The Sentry asked what would happen if he did so, but C.L.O.C. explained that there were too many variables. The "Golden Dragon"[]

When Reed Richards decided to move the machine, which had been used to erase the Sentry (and consequently the Void) from everyone's memory, to a safer location, he entrusted it to the Black Panther, the king of Wakanda. As the machine was being transported from the Baxter Building, the vehicle came under attack by the Punisher, Daredevil, Elektra, and the Hulk. The four of them sought the device because a vision of the future had altered their perspective on the world. The vision revealed a world devastated by the chaos resulting from the actions of heroes and crime-fighters like themselves. They concluded that people like them inadvertently strengthened evil, as evil ended up gaining strength in order to compete with good. Seeking to cut off this source of strength and prevent the vision from becoming reality, the four stole the device to replicate what had been done to the Sentry, but on a much larger scale. This time, the goal was to erase all heroes from everyone's memory. The Black Panther agreed with their plan, as did the Sentry. The latter, retaining his own memories, swore to guard the device until the Sun exploded. The Sentry seemed the ideal guardian, as he did not age, sleep, or require food, water, or air. Furthermore, he was one of the most powerful beings in existence. Thus, he remained confined within the Baxter Building alongside the device, becoming known as "Dragon" to those aware of the situation, such as Doctor Doom and the Kingpin, or, more specifically, as the "Golden Dragon". Doom intended to acquire the device for his own ends, but recognizing the danger posed by the Sentry, he needed time to prepare. However, the five of them regained their memories on six separate occasions and attempted to undo what had been done. Each time, the Sentry explained the reasons behind their decision, and they all reluctantly agreed to forget everything once more. Yet, the seventh time was different. The Punisher shot Banner, triggering his transformation into the Hulk. Hulk charged at Sentry, taking both of them out of the Baxter Building through a wall. While Sentry and Hulk fought, the others deactivated the device, restoring the world to the way it was before.[108]

Sentry World[]

Using a device called the Confluctor, Bob gained the ability to access the pocket universe Doctor Strange had set up inside his mind. In this "Sentry World," Bob could freely act as the Sentry, without risking the Void manifesting in the real world. By day, he trudged through a mundane life as Bob Reynolds, and each night he used the Confluctor to become the Sentry and fight crime with the Sentry Family: Scout, Sentress, and Watchdog. In order to keep the Void at bay, Bob needed to return to Sentry World once every 24 hours. After returning from the Sentry World, Bob discovered that the Confluctor had been stolen, and that someone had entered the Sentry World and killed Scout.[109] Discovering that the real Billy Turner was behind this, wanting to take his place as the Sentry with help from Cranio—an old enemy of the Sentry's—Reynolds snapped, merged with the Void to become a dark version of the Sentry, killed Billy, and then flew off into space after a brief confrontation with the Avengers.[110]

Annihilation - Scourge[]

Struggling to understand his new state of self, the Sentry returned to Earth to ponder about it. While meditating, he unintentionally started to warp reality around himself, engulfing the entire town of Pawhuska in a field of bad energy that attacked whoever entered it both physically (through monsters created by the Void) and mentally (through vivid illusions). Said energy was eventually detected by Black Panther and the Agents of Wakanda, who went on to investigate it. With the help of his fellow agents, Fat Cobra managed to purge part of the Void from Sentry's body and rid himself of it. Ashamed by his own actions, the Sentry fled.[111]

Soon after, the Sentry went after Mister Fantastic and begged him to set him free of the Void forever. After extensive tests, Richards discovered there was no way to separate the Sentry from the Void under the laws of their universe, so he sent him to the Negative Zone in the hopes that he would succeed there.[112] Upon soaking up the negative cosmic rays, the Sentry separated from the Void but was left in the powerless form of Bob Reynolds, unable to transform into his super hero alter ego. The Void fled, stranding Bob in the Negative Zone until the Silver Surfer found him weeks later and merged with him in order to defeat the Void, who became the apparent leader of the Cancerverse forces in the meantime and was leading an invasion through the Negative Zone.[6]

After Reed Richards opened a portal to assemble the galaxy's greatest heroes against the Cancerverse, a group of heroes held the Void still so the Silver Surfer could merge Bob Reynolds to him again. They hoped that Bob would call off the invasion once in control, but that was not possible as the Void was not truly the Cancerverse's leader, and it was now hellbent on getting Nova back to the fold. With no other option, Nova attracted the Cancerverse's infection back to the tear it was coming from and swallowed it whole. The merged Sentry then killed Nova, thus ending the scourge and closing the rift. While all the other heroes returned to the Positive Zone, the Sentry remained behind to watch over the universe he almost killed.[17]

King in Black[]

In light of the impending invasion of Earth by Knull—the primordial god of darkness who had created the Symbiotes—the Sentry was recalled to Earth by the Avengers, who told Knull that they would watch his demise. Killing one of the symbiote-controlled Celestials, the Sentry grabbed Knull and attempted to fly him into orbit and rip him in half as he once had Carnage. Instead, Knull reversed their positions and ripped the Sentry apart before assimilating the Void into himself.[23]

After his death, the Sentry became a ghost and remained in Earth's orbit near his shattered body until he received a visit. He had a conversation with the Valkyrie Jane Foster about everything he had lived through until his death at the hands of Knull. The fact that his death hadn't helped Earth's heroes defeat Knull troubled the Sentry. He felt he would have accepted it better if he had taken Knull with him. His failure in the fight with the primordial god of darkness, one he believed he was made for, was something the fallen hero pondered as he watched Earth, now shrouded in darkness, with Foster. As they made their way to Valhalla, they encountered a headless Celestial in the Realm Between. Corrupted by All-Black since its death, the Celestial assimilated the Sentry's soul despite attempts to stop it.[113] Brunnhilde, Ivanir and Dani Moonstar later performed a ritual that severed the headless Celestial's connection to All-Black, and Jane convinced the Celestial to release the souls it had imprisoned through the years. The Sentry, finally feeling complete, moved on to the afterlife.[114]

In order to get some good publicity for himself, Mayor Fisk assembled a new Thunderbolts team to find both halves of the Sentry's body to use as a bomb against Knull, who had nested in New York City during his invasion. The team was supposed to die in the explosion; however, Taskmaster, the team's field leader, decided to betray Fisk and had Figment fake the explosion with her illusory powers. The team later appeared alive before Fisk and with the Sentry's corpse in their possession, demanding the mayor to hire them as the city's official super hero team, to which Fisk complied.[115]

Revenant Prime[]

Eventually, the Sentry's corpse was acquired by the Blasphemy Cartel through unknown means, and then used as a vessel for one hundred million insane ghosts, thus turning it into their "Revenant Prime."[19] It took the combined might of Clea (the then-current Sorcerer Supreme of Earth) and Doctor Strange (at the time Death's Sorcerer Supreme, the Harvestman) to eliminate the creature, its destruction resulting in the complete annihilation of Emerald City, the Blasphemy Cartel's headquarters.[24]

Following his departure, Sentry's power would be split and transferred to various individuals who were shot by a member of the Blasphemy Cartel. These included: Mallory Gibbs, Farhad Anand, Ryan Topper, Marvin Chow, Rosalinda Bruno, and Deniz Derya, who beside receiving fractions of Sentry's power, also began seeing his memories. Topper would go on to kill most of those who had received the fragments of the power in order to claim the full power just for himself and be the successor of Sentry. However, he momentarily spared Gibbs, allowing the latter to embrace her status as a super hero, defeat Topper and use her newfound powers for the greater good.[34]

Return[]

Sentry eventually returned to life, and went to explore the galaxy for some time. However, the truth was that as the Void came back as well, Robert was forced to finally find a way to deal with it and coexist together. With a heavy heart, Sentry decided to travel through space and willingly allow the Void to devastate various alien civilizations, because as part of the equal exchange they had, for every life Sentry saved the Void took, doing so would allow him to go around Earth help countless people unimpeded, though it still filled him with heavy guilt.[116] After decimating numerous worlds, the Sentry returned to Earth, reuniting with his wife, pet dog, and his super hero friends. He then held a press conference to announce to the world his official return.[25] When a crystalline plague began to spread, infecting many, including the Hulk (whom the Sentry fought and helped cure by tearing the crystals from his body),[36] he was initially slow to answer the heroes' calls for help. This was partly due to the grief he felt over the terminal illness of his dog, Watchdog, who was dying of cancer. The Sentry considered using his powers to heal and save the dog, but refrained from doing so due to the cost associated with his heroic actions involving the Void. After the dog's death,[116] the Sentry helped the heroes put an end to the crystalline plague, yet while doing so, he sensed that something bad was coming. He borrowed the special spaceship from Mister Fantastic and traveled to the past to prevent Laika's death in space, bringing her to the present to become the new Watchdog, thus freeing himself from a pain he had carried since childhood.[116]

Personality

He gave more than his life. He gave up the power of a god for the sake of us all. He allowed us to forget him all over again. The ultimate act of heroism is to be a hero without recognition.

—Doctor Strange[source]
From a young age, Reynolds showed a tendency towards heroism. When he learned about Laika, a Russian dog sent into space on a Soviet Union mission, he wanted to save her and love her for the rest of her life.[25] After gaining his extraordinary powers, Reynolds used them to help and protect all who needed him as the Sentry, the hero with the power of a million exploding suns.[35] He became widely accepted, including by other super beings such as Reed Richards of the Fantastic Four,[117] the Hulk and Spider-Man.[11] With the emergence of the Void, his greatest and most relentless enemy, who was also a part of himself, the Sentry dedicated himself to preventing it from achieving its purpose: to cause destruction, suffering, and death wherever it went, even being willing to sacrifice his life to contain its evil side.[21] This constant struggle often led Reynolds to mental exhaustion, which could result in a psychotic episode.[118] In combat, the Sentry could be brutal, even tearing off the heads of his enemies[82] or splitting them in half.[119] This brutality, along with his unparalleled powers for many, made the Sentry question whether he should take action, as he could injure or kill those nearby, including his allies. This thought came to his mind when the Civil War began, pondering the possibility of ending up doing things like ripping off Spider-Man's arms or killing Captain America.[57] Many times, he held back his power because of this. This need to restrain himself could be suffocating. When the Sentry faced the Hulk after his return to Earth to make those who banished him from the world and supposedly tried to kill him pay, he seized the opportunity to unleash himself more than usual, as the Hulk's extraordinary resilience allowed him to withstand attacks from the Sentry that would easily kill many.[18]

Powers

In effect, your powers are limitless, my boy. There is nothing you cannot do. You've conquered the time and space itself. You are omnipotent. Immortal.

—The Professor[source]

Enhanced Physiology: The Sentry's superhuman powers and abilities derive from Golden Sentry Serum. The experimental serum creates a phase-shift in his molecules, leaving every atom of the Sentry an instant ahead in the timeline.[1] The change was so extreme that Sentry's molecules became fascinating even to Molecule Man, who stated he had never seen molecules like them.[120] The serum induces a photosynthetic reaction, resulting in a hyperstate of consciousness.[1] His powers are seemingly based in Reality Warping.[101] Although the Sentry is described as having the power of a million exploding suns, his powers have the potential to reach even further.[1] He is so powerful that Karla Sofen theorized in front of Norman Osborn that what happened after Scarlet Witch broke down would be nothing compared to what Sentry could do.[90] He was able to briefly contain a Cosmic Cube, stabilizing it while holding it with only his hands.[37] The Sentry was powerful enough to face the Green Scar, a version of the Hulk stronger than ever before at that point, and fight him to a near-draw when he unleashed more of his power than usual. The Sentry also faced Galactus, the Devourer of Worlds, fighting him to a standstill.[2] He was stated to be an Omega-Level Threat by Mister Fantastic,[121] and Nick Fury's intel classified him as Power Level 10+.[122] During a meeting with Professor X, Emma Frost openly stated that the Sentry's powers are godlike,[123] and during his time as a member of the Dark Avengers, Sentry's profile note that there is no limit to his powers and that he is nigh-omnipotent, thus making him the strongest member in the Dark Avengers Team.[93] Since Sentry's powers fluctuate based on his mental state and emotions, he rarely manages to reach that potential due to the constant variation in his confidence.[35] The Sentry's powers are so vast that when the Skrulls launched an invasion to seize control of Earth, they were unable to devise a precise countermeasure to combat him. The best plan was a trick to make him avoid confrontation. The Skrulls knew that if this failed, the Sentry would likely kill them, perhaps even his own friends, due to his already unstable mind, affected by the invaders' trick.[74] Iron Man stated that the Sentry is the most powerful human being in the universe.[9] Following his return later on, the Sentry finally accept his dark self, the Void and merged with the entity, creating a being far more powerful than all his past incarnations, when considering all the impressive feat throughout the Sentry and the Void lifetime of struggle, the all-new merged Sentry can be said to be arguably the most powerful superhuman on Earth.[124] Though that state did not last long, as Sentry and Void eventually split again and Sentry returned to his golden form. [23]. Due to his immense powers and mental instability, a combination that could result in the end of the universe, the Sentry believed that the risk of using the Ultimate Nullifier, a weapon capable of causing destruction on the same scale, against him, was worthwhile.[125]


  • Molecularkinesis: Following a confrontation with the Molecule Man, Robert discovered he possessed molecular manipulation powers, which he used to rebuild himself after being destroyed by him. The extent of these powers are not yet known, but he was able to defeat the Molecule Man.[90] It is worth noting that Molecule Man, who possessed far greater experience in manipulating molecules, was subdued by the inexperienced Sentry, whose power was also affected by his mental state, in addition to his lack of experience. Since The Void and the Sentry are essentially the same person with differing personalities, The Void's own Shapeshifting might be a result of the Sentry's molecular manipulation. Additionally, Reynolds's molecularkinesis ability theoretically would give him potential regenerative healing abilities as well despite his nigh invulnerability. It has been theorized that the vast majority of his powers are derived from his ability to alter matter and energy and that he could simulate almost any superpower with enough practice and control. He seems to do this unconsciously as the Void when he changes shape and power sets. It is also possible that he had survived from being disintegrated by Morgan Le Fay due to the same power. Since losing his agoraphobia, and therefore gaining full control over his powers, he has been able to utilize his power to manipulate molecules to even greater degrees than ever before.[101]
    • Materialization: Sentry learned he could craft and build various constructs or even sentient effigy's by rearranging molecules by force of will. Either conjuring a miniature werewolf in the palm of his hand, erecting a force field able to deflect Terrax the Tamer's energy waves with relative ease, and when at his full potential he could even use it in psychokinetic ways such as stopping a full-speed Mjolnir throw directed at him by Thor[8] and create massive Sand Golems capable of downing a giant-sized Wasp after he become a Horsemen of Death.[101]
    • Biokinesis: The Sentry has an unknown or rarely used ability to affect biophysical phenomena to an unknown but seemingly limitless degree. Both his Sentry and Void persona's having used this aspect of their shared ability for various purposes. Robert once using his power to cure his therapist's daughter of a vegetative state and spinal problems seemingly just by being in the same room as her, while the Void bestowed a young unwed mother with an incurable breast cancer disease from states away.[126]
    • Transmutation: The Sentry has already demonstrated the power to transform things into other things—such as the time he crushed a rock and transmuted it into a Healing Orb to give to Hulk.[11]
    • Resurrection: The Sentry appears to be able to resurrect himself and to bring the dead back to life under extreme emotional distress. After Ultron murdered his wife, Sentry was able to resurrect her by simply touching her. It does not appear he has control over this power, as he was shocked by his ability to do such an act. This may also extend to simply healing others' injuries.[127]
  • Retroactive Immortality: Reynolds is chemically mixed with the sentry serum, thus allowing him and the void to be immune to aging and inevitably, dying.[35] Even if he does get killed off, be it in his human or empowered state, neither Sentry nor the Void can be permanently disposed of.[21][23] As they always return in some form or another.[22][128] For a brief time, Sentry paid his life as a sacrificial price for Doctor Strange in exchange to allow him to perform large scale magical feats from the Yggdrasil Tree's power. Considering he could not permanently die; Sentry described the experience as nothing more than taking a short nap.[107]
    • Regenerative Healing Factor: The Sentry can heal himself from virtually all injuries. Such as when he was shot dead in the face by his wife Lindy with an alien ray gun and it only took a minute to heal.[27] He twice even came back from having his molecularity dispersed, during his fights with the Molecule Man[129] and Morgan Le Fay.[87] When Ares wounded the Sentry with his axe, the Sentry continued the fight with part of his small intestine projecting from his body, but the wound healed moments later.[119]. His most prolonged death so far came when Knull ripped him in half, possibly because Knull then absorbed Void[130] though eventually, through currently unknown means, Sentry returned to life[25]
  • Superhuman Strength: The Sentry's strength varies greatly and depends on his mental stability. The Sentry possesses vast superhuman strength, granting him the ability to lift (press) far in excess of 100 tons easily. He is one of the strongest beings in the Universe. He has demonstrated several astonishing feats of strength during his career, including easily lifting tremendous weights, ripping Carnage in two,[131] taking Terrax the Tamer's cosmic axe and shattering it with his bare hands,[132] moving a cruise liner effortlessly,[132] and his unrestrained power overloaded the Absorbing Man.[92] The Void was even capable of breaking the Hulk's bones with relative ease[133] and forcing a group of extremely strong beings, including Captain Marvel, Gladiator, and Beta Ray Bill, to struggle immensely just to contain him for a few moments.[17] The Sentry was able to easily kill Ares by ripping him in half.[119] He can also absorb solar radiation for additional strength.[citation needed] The Sentry has yet to demonstrate an upper-limit to his strength, but he has shown that it can rival the likes of the Hulk, although this depends on his mental state.[18] He was able to easily overcome Namor in physical combat.[123]. The Sentry has also shown enough physical strength to be able beat the Void into submission more than once with pure force when he was relatively stable, which shows a high degree of power.[35] During his most recent battle with the Void inside of the Sentry World, Sentry demonstrate devastating feat in shattering the entire moon to reveal the Void' whereabouts and then kill him easily by ripping him apart afterward.[109] In a fight against the Sentry, Wolverine compared his opponent's strength to that of Galactus.[58] Following his fusion with the Void that granted him access to the combined might of both beings, Sentry has demonstrated an even greater degree of super strength, managing to easily overpower Scout, who had injected himself with the Golden Sentry Serum, to the point of effortlessly crushing his fist and breaking his neck. During an encounter with the Avengers, with Thor, Captain Marvel, and She-Hulk in their ranks, the merged Sentry manhandled them and knocked them all aside with ease.[124]
  • Superhuman Speed: The Sentry possesses the ability to think, move, run, and react at superhuman speeds. He has been observed catching bullets and has also been seen moving far in excess of supersonic speeds; his speed on earth is enough to be gauged as fast enough to move at orbital velocity (5 miles a second).[citation needed] It should also be noted that the Sentry has numerously been depicted as able to fly to the sun in a matter of moments (where it takes light over 8 minutes). This shows that his speed during inter-stellar travels is several times in excess of the speed of light.[27] He has recently shown the ability to travel an indistinguishable amount of light years in a very short of time in flight. Sentry's flight was described to be bending space and time through pure speed, and that the speed he was flying at was so fast that it was disorienting Thor.[100] Sentry has even been able to occasionally match Thor's combat speed in battle.[101][134] (Thor has routinely matched beings with extreme combat speed, such as Gladiator[135] and Silver Surfer[136])
    • Superhuman Agility: Sentry's natural balance, agility, and body coordination are enhanced to levels that are far beyond the natural physical limits of even the finest human athlete.[citation needed]
    • Superhuman Reflexes: The Sentry can react at superhuman speeds exceeding the equivalent of several times the speed of Sound. As such, Sentry was able to catch a sniper bullet from a highly advanced Skrull gun.[80] Sentry has in the past saved Cornelius Worth from a point-blank gunshot wound to head from being inflicted on him by the Void. With him taunting Sentry, asking if he was "faster than a speeding bullet." Upon Sentry saving Dr. Cornelius, he says to the Void that he himself knew he could catch the bullet because the Void knew how fast the Sentry was to the nearest millisecond.[137] Sentry has also reflected that he must exert control over himself for even a millisecond in battle, indicating that he operates in these time intervals.[67]
  • Nigh-Invulnerability: The Sentry is, for all intents and purposes, virtually indestructible, unless he wills himself to be killed, he has shown no direct weakness. He has been seen surviving extremely harsh atmospheric conditions, Survives a nuclear explosion at point blank range and including the vacuums of space. Nick Fury has stated that so far S.H.I.E.L.D. has yet to find a way to kill him,[15] and even Iron Man's scanners found no physiological defects in the Sentry's body.[138] Sentry has also consistently shown ability to tank punches from enormously strong beings like Hulk, though he can be damaged by them. Attacking the Sentry has been compared to striking a mountain.[139] Like all of his abilities, his durability depends on his mental state. He can go to completely no-selling Class 100-level beings, but as soon as he wishes himself dead, he could be killed in an instant, notably in the Siege of Asgard, where he asked Thor to kill him.[21] The Sentry possesses an incredibly high resistance to magic. When the Doctor Strange transmuted an entire dimension, a hell dimension, more specifically, during a fight against Loki Laufeyson, Loki asked how he remained alright after using such a large amount of magic, being a mortal. This was because the Sentry was paying the price for the magic Strange was using, a price he paid with great ease.[107] He also partially resisted the Scarlet Witch's use of chaos magic when she altered reality to create a world where mutants were dominant.[45][46]
    • Self-Sustenance: Sentry has admitted to Lindy that he does not need to ever use the bathroom.[13] He also does not appear to have the need to eat either, as shown when his wife was eating a meal with him, and he only had a glass of water in comparison.[13]
  • Superhuman Stamina: Sentry's enhanced musculature is far more efficient than that of a human. As a result, his muscles produce no fatigue toxins. However, his stamina, along with the rest of his powers, greatly varies depending on his mental and emotional state.[35]
  • Superhuman Senses: The Sentry possesses vastly enhanced senses. He is able to hear almost any sound at any decibel, pitch, and frequency. The only Earth creature who can detect sounds at the frequencies he can is a blue whale (0.01-200,000 Hz). He has shown enough control to block out ambient sounds to focus on a specific source/frequency (He has remarked that he was able to hear a butterfly's heartbeat in Africa).[13] The range of his eyesight is also far greater than that of a human being. He is able to detect a single person from hundreds of miles in the air and above cloud level. The full extent of Robert's other senses superhuman capabilities is unknown.[citation needed]
  • Extrasensory Perception: The Sentry once detected the energy of a dead Celestial at the heart of the multiverse,[103] and managed to sense Noh-Varr's aura.[140] Sentry was also able to recognize Genis-Vell immediately just by sensing his energy.[47]
  • Flight: The Sentry possesses the ability to float by defying gravity, using his superhuman speed to fly far above hypersonic speeds (above Mach 10). He has demonstrated travelling to the Sun and back in a matter of moments which would require him to fly at speeds faster than light.[27] And during the Skrulls secret invasion he flew to Saturn (about 746 million miles distance) almost immediately, which required him either to move at many times the speed of light (still thought to be impossible in this space-time) or to have bypassed space altogether thus giving the impression that he had moved faster than light-speed. He has also been stated to travel at many times the speed of light. One plausible explanation is that Sentry uses his absolute control of his molecular structure to mitigate and or prevent his body from obtaining additional mass as he moves/flies at speeds far greater that light-speed, although this has yet to have been proven or shown.[citation needed] And has also been remarked to fly at such speeds not even a god of Asgard could withstand.[100] Sentry has also been able to fly with such force to behead and kill Attuma.[132]
  • Teleportation: On at least one occasion, Sentry has shown an ability to teleport in a blinding flash of golden light.[141] Sentry teleported away, after meeting Nate Grey,[142] he was also able to teleport from the Sun back to the Earth,[27] and managed to get into an area which was protected by Reed Richard's force fields.[117]
  • Energy Absorption and Manipulation: The Sentry has been stated to be capable of drawing energy from anywhere and everywhere including the Microverse,[47] but his main source power was Earth's Sun, which gave him the power of a million exploding suns. Although external factors, such as the Sun, intensified his powers, they did not originate exclusively from these elements. The effect of the Sun on the Sentry's powers was similar to the effect of the Negative Zone on those of his dark counterpart, the Void.[1][133]

    • Power Sharing: A couple of times the Sentry has utilized a unique ability to bestow portions of his own power onto others or using it to augment existing powers in those of his friends.       • Light Projection: Having absolute control over the light he produces, he can control its direction, frequency (color), amplitude (intensity), and duration. The Sentry can produce numerous effects with the light he manipulates. He can simply cause a bright glow all about his body. He can create a pulse of light on the order of several tens of thousands of lumens of power per second (the sun produces at most 10,000 lumens of brightness at noontime), which temporarily blinds people with its brilliance.
      • Calm Inducement: Sentry's ability to project light has been proven to be able to calm down the Hulk though it also reduces him to a childlike mentality who believes the Sentry and his wife to be his mother and father.[14][133]
      • Invisibility: Sentry can turn invisible by deflecting light and radiant emissions.[56]
      • Energy Blasts: The Sentry can generate light, heat, kinetic force, and other forms of energy as powerful blasts and explosions of a golden color. Although these blasts are generated from the hands and eyes, he prefers them as infrared heat-beams from his eyes or hands. The maximum power of these blasts is unknown. It has shown previously to be capable of burning Hulk's skin and levelling whole city blocks in the past.[18] He has also shown the ability to emit omnidirectional blasts that were able to eliminate a large group of Attuma's soldiers.[132] He killed a Cancerverse-infected Nova during the Scourge in the Negative Zone, [17] blasted a group of Thunder Guard who had assaulted Asgard,[105] and once destroyed Iron Man's Hulkbuster Armor, a very durable piece of technology.[144] When merged with the Void, these blasts become crimson red in color.
  • Umbrakinesis: After merging with the Void, Sentry gained the Void's darkness manipulation abilities. He could generate tentacles from his darkness that could restrain someone as well as knock them away.[124]
  • Psionic Abilities: The Sentry possesses powerful psychic and mental powers, including telekinesis, used primarily to prevent objects from shattering under the weight of their own mass. The few psychic abilities he has displayed so far was implanting his memories inside Paul Jenkins' mind[29] and being able to erase himself from the memories of every being on the planet, with the help of C.L.O.C., Dr. Strange, and Reed Richards.[28] The Sentry's psionic abilities make his mind remarkably protected against mental attacks. Emma Frost had great difficulty dealing with his mind when she helped him break free from the mental manipulation of Mastermind, who admitted that the Sentry's mind was strong.[125][40] When the Sentry clashed with the X-Men and Cyclops realized that his best, and perhaps only, chance of dealing with the Sentry was to attack his mind, Emma needed to combine her powers with those of Professor X to re-enter the Sentry's mind. After that, Frost not only had some difficulty dealing with the Sentry's mind, but her own mind was attacked by the Void, placing her in great danger, from which she narrowly escaped by remaining in her diamond form. This event demonstrated that the Sentry's resistance to mental attacks is not only due to him own power, but also to him connection with the Void, since the dark entity can cause the mind of who invades the Sentry's mind to collapse.[123] When the Scarlet Witch rewrote the history of reality, the Sentry was able to resist and retain some memories of his life in the original reality as the hero with the power of a million exploding suns.[45][46]

Genius Intelligence: Robert is very intelligent. He is capable of using his powers to detect all substances in a single object and thence determine how much of what substances make up an object. He is adept with mechanics, architecture, machinery, tracing energy sources, cybernetics, robotics, and computer science. He used his great intelligence to construct his Watchtower as well as everything inside of it. Since he has never been very wealthy, it is likely he recovered all of the substances required for his Watchtower's construction from many different areas of the Earth or (perhaps) other planets as well. Weaknesses

Antimatter: Since Sentry has the power to manipulate molecules, he is notably susceptible to antimatter, as seen in his fight against Anti-Man. Due to his condition, Robert suffers from many weaknesses:

Psychic Assault: The Sentry has a fragile psyche and is readily susceptible to mental manipulation. Although if he is relatively stable he can resist such manipulation, as he has in the past when Dr. Strange tried to convince him that the Sentry was not real and that it was an illusion, stating that "he was always too powerful for this type of magic."[citation needed]

The Negative Zone: The Sentry is at his weakest in the Negative Zone. His powers and health gradually deteriorate the longer he is inside the zone itself and even worse, his counterpart/nemesis The Void's powers increase to maximum.[145] However, in his new state while merged with the Void, Sentry was able to retain all his superpowers in the Negative Zone without weakening at all.[17]

Lack of Control: Sentry does not seem to have complete control over all of his abilities, as he did not even know he was capable of Molecular Control until his battle with the Molecule Man and was genuinely shocked after reviving his wife from the dead.[citation needed]

Vulnerability to Chi: Spiritual energy based attacks, such as Chi, have been shown to be capable of injuring Sentry especially when inflicted by someone powerful.[139]

Notes

  • While part of H.A.M.M.E.R., he was granted Security Clearance Level 2.[147]
  • Originally, it was claimed that the character was created in the early 1960s, preceding the Fantastic Four into print in Startling Stories #17. As excerpted from Broken Frontier: "Wizard ... magazine reported that following (Artie) Rosen's death, his widow discovered a box labeled "Marvel Comics" containing comics, papers and files. She returned the box to Marvel, where it was promptly lost until writer Paul Jenkins accidentally picked it up. Jenkins discovered an old comic in the box, Startling Stories #1, featuring the debut of a Superman-like hero called The Sentry. Moderately intrigued by the forgotten hero, Jenkins dug deeper in the box, discovering old sketches and character outlines. Dated 1961, the developmental sketches were signed by Stan Lee and Artie Rosen." It was later revealed to be a simple, albeit, elaborate hoax developed as a marketing ploy for the new character.
  • The Sentry's origin has been altered numerous times. Originally in Sentry, the vague origin portrayed "Robby" Reynolds as the teen lab assistant of "the professor," who has consumed the formula simply in order to get its powers to fight the school bully. In Sentry (Vol. 2), the origin was once more altered, claiming that Robert Reynolds was a teenage junkie who had stolen the formula in order to get high. At this time, the formula and the nameless Professor were tied into both the Weapon X program and Project: Rebirth. The junkie story seems to be the correct one however, as it was confirmed by his wife in Dark Avengers #13.
  • He was created by Paul Jenkins and Jae Lee for the Marvel Comics Marvel Knights line.
  • The Sentry not being in his dark form in King in Black #1 is a continuity error owing to Donny Cates and Ryan Stegman not having been aware of his fusion with the Void at the time the issue was written and illustrated, and Marvel's editorial department not correcting it prior to publication.[148]
  • When asked if Lifebringer One was the source of the Bob Reynold's Sentry powers, Al Ewing responded that he would rather leave such matters up to interpretation than provide a boring answer that would just be retconned if he changed his mind or a later writer wanted to do something different.[149]
  • According to the co-creator himself, Jenkins, Sentry's powers are the manifestation of his own mental state, and all the serum did was essentially just unlock the potential he already possessed.[150]
