History

Thame Panagitis is the leader of the Medusa Web, an organization which appears to be a mercenary group, but which he claims exists to safeguard the world by giving other nations a collective foe to fight. Little is known of his history, though he presumably gained his paranormal ability from the White Event. Devious and manipulative, he nonetheless sometimes exhibits genuine sincerity and concern for his operatives (he was genuinely distressed at the accidental death of Relampago) and appears to believe he has the best of motives.[1]

He recruited Psi-Force into his organization after first being hired to hunt them down by the US Government.[2]
