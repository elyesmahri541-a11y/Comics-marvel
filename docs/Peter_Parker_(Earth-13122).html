History

Origin[]

When he was kid, Peter was left with his Aunt May and Uncle Ben when his parents died. As a teenager, still in school, Peter was bitten by a radioactive spider in a science exhibition and discovered the superpowers it gave him. And, after failing to prevent a crime that culminated in the death of his Uncle Ben, Peter realized that becoming a superhero and protecting the innocent was his duty, because he had the power to do so.[9]

Black Suit Saga[]

After attempting to repair a damaged suit, Peter was engulfed by an alien symbiote, forming a new black suit and giving him enhanced powers and abilities. Peter abandoned the symbiote in a church, where it engulfed Eddie Brock.[9]

Universe In Peril[]

Grand Central Station[]

On his way to the Daily Bugle, Spider-Man found Iron Man and the Hulk fighting the Sandman by Stark Tower, deciding to help the two. The two incapacitated Sandman by solidifying his form with water and cement. As the two villains were taken into custody, Peter returned to his everyday life.

A Work Day Interrupted[]

Upon Doc Octopus storming through the Daily Bugle office, Peter decided to help Captain America and Mister Fantastic pursue him. The trio pursued Doc Ock to Times Square, the three defeated him on combat, only for the brick he was holding to be retrieved by the Green Goblin.

Oscorp Visit[]

With Goblin having been tracked by Hawkeye and Black Widow to Oscorp, Spider-Man rendezvoused with them to retrieve the brick, the three were attacked by Green Goblin. The three pursued Goblin, working their way through the Oscorp labs until Goblin triggered Oscorp's security systems, shutting down various parts of the lab and turning any nearby scientists hostile against the three. After getting through the security lockdown the three engaged Goblin in combat, resulting in Goblin destroying a nearby elevator shaft to hinder their progress. The three made their way through the elevator shaft, meeting with Goblin again. However, at that point Goblin triggered a trap door, sending them down to the lower levels of Oscorp labs. Turning their attention away from Osborn and instead on escaping the lower labs, the three encountered Venom, who had manage to pervert the lower labs by infesting it with other Symbiotes, infecting both the environment and the Oscorp Scientists who worked down there. After working their way upwards, the three managed to subdue Venom by exploiting the symbiotes' weakness to sound and fire. However, the time that was wasted by their encounter with Venom gave Goblin enough time to escape with the Cosmic Brick. Their mission failed, Spider-Man then requested the two Avengers to track down Norman Osborn while he went to take care of his everyday life - a request that Black Widow was all too willing to accept.

Another Day at the Office[]

Peter, along with much of his social circle, was present at the Bugle when it was cleaned up by Phil Coulson and Doctor Octopus.

A Trip to the Ocean[]

Taking a ride in the Spider-Copter courtesy of S.H.I.E.L.D., Spider-Man joined Iron Man and Thor, who were faced with an A.I.M. submarine that had taken a S.H.I.E.L.D. research sub hostage. The three broke into the sub, fighting their way through various A.I.M. Agents, the three were faced with Doctor Doom. Following him, they were ambushed by M.O.D.O.K. who they defeated M.O.D.O.K. as a result of him overloading his power. After M.O.D.O.K. was defeated, Doom fled in the stolen S.H.I.E.L.D. research sub. The A.I.M. ship then began to break down and sink, and Thor was unable to summon any lightning, which left them trapped. The three were rescued, however, when Jean Grey arrived, immersing them in a protective forcefield. The four made their way to the stolen research sub, which was lodged on an ocean cliff due to falling debris, as Iron Man, Thor and Spider-Man protecting Jean from oncoming Doombots. Reaching the sub and making their way inside, they confronted Doctor Doom, Thor knocking him unconscious with Mjolnir and dislodging the ship in the process. Doom was not brought into S.H.I.E.L.D. custody, however, as Magneto then rescued him by controlling the Statue of Liberty. [6]

Battle with Magneto[]

Breaking out of the submarine, Jean left the three to report back to Xavier. Meanwhile, Tony, Peter, and Thor learnt of Asteroid M, Magneto's newly powered ship, and resolved to stop it. After Tony and Peter uncovered the Stark Jet, the three arrived at Asteroid M's hangar, where they were greeted by Magneto who locked them in the room with a group of Acolytes. Utilizing the airlock to blow the door open, the three pursued Magneto. Chasing Magneto throughout the Asteroid, they eventually confronted him. Magneto proved a worthy opponent, fashioning a metal Avatar for himself and throwing all manner of objects at them. Despite this, however, the heroes were able to defeat Magneto by fashioning the same large objects out of debris and destroying his avatar, with Thor subsequently launching Magneto back to Earth afterwards.

Battle on Asteroid M[]

The three then confronted Doom and Loki. Being supercharged by Loki's scepter, Doom blasted the three, knocking Iron Man and Thor back into the lower areas but failing to take out Spider-Man. Thing, Captain America and Storm arrived to help Spider-Man, prompting Doom and Loki to retreat to the upper levels. Fighting Magneto Acolytes and Doombots along the way, the four were able to activate an elevator to the outer corridors of the spacestation, where they were met by minions and security measures, including several security turrets. Opening a door to a joining connection bridge despite the securities' systems warnings, the quartet were sucked out into space, eventually landing back in the spacestation where they confronted Doctor Doom. With Doom channeling his newfound power into a forcefield and Loki out of reach, the team fought a wave of standard Doombots, V-Series Doombots and Magneto Acolytes before Doom channelled his powers into a laser cannon, allowing Captain America to deflect it, destroying the cannons and allowing them to fight him directly. After working through the rest of his defences, the heroes were finally able to defeat Doom, with the Thing punching him into space. After Iron Man and Thor rejoined the group Loki revealed his master plan, use the Doom Ray to take control of Galactus and have it devour Earth and Asgard. Loki demonstrated this, having Galactus destroy Asteroid M, causing the 6 heroes to crash-land on the helicarrier.

Last Stand on the Helicarrier[]

Nick Fury had all the heroes and villains team up to stop Galactus from eating the Earth. Spidey was on 'Team One' with Thing, Green Goblin and Mystique. Team One worked to repair one of the S.H.I.E.L.D. rotos, eventually directing the subsequent wind to Galactus. Eventually, the earth was saved, with Team Three summoning a Black Hole and Team Four knocking Galactus and Loki into it. As per Fury and Doom's earlier agreement, the villains were given a thirty second head-start to escape, an idea that was unintentionally sabotaged by Hulk's inability to count to three, allowing the villains to escape.[6]

Afterparty at Stark Tower[]

Celebrating their victory over Galactus, Tony Stark held a wrap party at Stark Tower. Spider-Man attended the party and, much like the other guests, found it lifeless and boring. However, Peter was able to get into the party once Tony and Rhodey were able to assemble a DJ station, a disco ball and repair a speaker Hulk had broken.[6]

Face-Off at the Bank[]

Upon a Bank in the Downtown Financial District being attacked by Electro and Shocker, Spider-Man went to the scene with Iron Man and Captain America. After Spider-Man knocked Shocker into a bank vault, Electro was able to protect himself by electrifying the water in the bank. After the heroes rescued nearby civilians, Electro engaged them directly, the team quickly defeating him. They then opened the Bank vault, where they were able to defeat Shocker. Both villains were then taken into custody.[6]

Investigating Fisk[]

With Wilson Fisk offering cheap dental checkups, Spider-Man and Captain America found someone strapped to a chair being assaulted with a laser. After freeing the man from his restraints, the duo were joined by one of Spidey's allies, Daredevil, causing Fisk to show himself and retaliate, sending a group of his minions, including, Elektra to take care of them. After the heroes defeated Elektra, Kingpin sent Bullseye to dispose of them. After Bullseye too was defeated, the trio forced Fisk out of his office, knocking him out with chandeliers.[6]

A Trip to the Park[]

Taking a day off in the park with Thing, Spider-Man's fun was cut off when his Spider-Sense alerted him to danger going on in the reptile house. The two investigated the house, finding Curt Connors conducting his experiments and transforming into the Lizard as a result. Pursuing Connors throughout the lab, Thing was ultimately able to physically subdue Lizard, allowing Spider-Man to wrap him up in webbing.[6]

Hostages at Marvel[]

When Vulture and Howard the Duck held the Marvel writers hostage to gain more comic coverage, Spider-Man and Mr. Fantastic arrived to deal with the situation. Working their way through the Marvel security system, the heroes confronted the duo. Howard put himself out of the equation almost immediately, knocking himself back with his rocket launcher and leaving him out of the rest of the fight, while Vulture proved more of a challenge. The two ultimately took out Vulture through a combination of traps, exploiting his bird-like instincts and placing several decoys of Avengers members, saving the Marvel writers in the process.[6]

Maximum Overload[]

At Nick Fury's request, Peter goes to a S.H.I.E.L.D. base in New Jersey to fight Doctor Otto Octavius, who was under Loki's control. Otto would be defeated electrocuted.[14] He would later be attacked by Venom, who was also under Loki's control. The two fought at the Daily Bugle, where Peter defeats him in a newspaper machine.[7] In ways unknown, Peter ends up on an oil drilling platform after Tony, Pepper, and Falcon had already defeated Trevor Slattery [15] but he was soon found by Thor and taken to Malibu, where the S.H.I.E.L.D Helicarrier had crashed and countless villains were fleeing. Thor would soon discover that Loki was behind it all, so he, Peter and Tony go to Loki's hideout and manage to get him to release the villains from his control. After all, S.H.I.E.L.D. presented Peter with the Spider-Cycle.[8]

Return of Ultron[]

While training on the Helicarrier with Amadeus Cho, Peter notices that Darren Cross has caught himself in one of his webs. After interrogate the villain, Peter meets with the Avengers in the Helicarrier and warns them of Darren's plan with Ultron.[16]

Kang's Invasion[]

Avenger's World Tour[]

At the Avengers Mansion during the celebration of Loki's defeat, Peter quietly enjoys the food until a call from Nick Fury comes to interrupt the festivities.

At the Brooklyn Bridge, Spider-Man helps Ms. Marvel and White Tiger fight escaped inmates from the Raft, including Shocker, Mysterio, Vulture, Kraven the Hunter, and Doctor Octopus. After he defeats them he says farewell for now and swings off to Manhattan the sees a white light then. When the white light clears, Spider-Man has a run in with Spider-Gwen, who he helps chase after Goblin 2099. After he defeats him and Vulture, a group of knights ride through the area, enabling Goblin 2099 to get away.

Exploring Chronopolis[]

He doesn't appear again until he is on a mission with Ms. Marvel, and She-Hulk in which he has to Klaw through the portal to Wakanda, where they eventually run into Black Panther (how he got to Wakanda from Manhattan is unknown or - for that matter - what he was doing for the time he didn't appear). Spider-Man then helps and successfully defeat Man-Ape. Pursuing the Klaw along with Black Panther and Spider-Man, they end up stopping at the Hydra Empire, where they meet Captain America, Doctor Strange and Peter Quill. Upon discovering that Klaw was on one of Hydra's airships, Captain America orders him, Black Panther and Lord Star to go after him while the rest of the group returns to the mansion.

The Quest for the Nexu Fragments[]

Later at the mansion, the Avengers have their signal jammed by Cosmo, who was trying to communicate with them. The team hatches a plan to open a portal to Knowhere to access Chronopolis, which requires the Nexus Fragments. Peter is then sent on a mission Doctor Strange and She-Hulk when they all Arrive at the Sanctum Sanctorum. He then helps Doctor Strange and She-Hulk search for his Book of the Vishanti that he lost as Wong then claims that Doctor Strange should look after his things. He then finds out along with the others that Baron Mordo has the book then Spiderman helps Dr. Strange and she hulk defeat Baron Mordo, and stop his plan of having Kang the Conqueror bend to the will of Dormammu as well as having Chronopolis be absorbed into the Dark Dimension. Doctor Strange then claims the Book of the Vishanti. Spider-Man then goes on his next mission along with Ms. Marvel, and She-Hulk to K'un-Lun and when they arrive there where a mysterious voice states that they can only enter the Cave of the Dragon if they can find the one with the iron fist in the "monochrome" city. Then Spider-Man, Ms. Marvel, and She-Hulk go to Manhattan Noir where they find Iron Fist. Before he can help them, Iron Fist states that they must help him first as he needs backup because Kingpin has taken control of the area and they must get into Fisk Tower to take him down. He suggests that Spider-Man, Ms. Marvel, and She-Hulk meet up with Daredevil and Luke Cage who have already made preparations for the sneak attack. He then meets up with his Noir counterpart who helps them on there mission after they defeat Hammerhead, Elektra and later Kingpin. Iron Fist then helps them open the door of K'un-Lun and then Spiderman must help the rest of the team defeat Steel Serpent in which he successfully does.

He then sent on another mission in Nueva York, where he finds out that the Alchemax building has been charged up by Electro 2099. After defeating Electro 2099 on the outside of the Alchemax building, Spider-Man and Ms. Marvel enter the building where they run into She-Hulk and Spider-Gwen where they both individually had traced Green Goblin 2099 to Alchemax. Unbeknownst to the others Spider-Man 2099 was in the corner on his web bed, listening in before describing to them what Alchemax does. Green Goblin 2099 speaks on the monitor to them before unleashing some symbiotes and Symbiote-controlled workers on them. Using the Nexus Shard, Green Goblin 2099 combines the samples of Venom and Carnage to form Carnom. Spider-Man, Ms. Marvel, She-Hulk, Spider-Gwen, and Spider-Man 2099 fight Carnom and his second form of Maximum Carnom. When Carnom is defeated, Green Goblin 2099 tries to attack them only to be defeated by Ms. Marvel and webbed up by Spider-Man, Spider-Gwen, and Spider-Man 2099. Ms. Marvel claims the Nexus Shard while Spider-Man 2099 and Spider-Gwen deal with subduing Carnom who is chasing Green Goblin 2099.

Fight Against Kang[]

After the plan was successfully boarded, with Kang's defenses down, he is then asked to go to Knowhere with Iron Man and Star-Lord to work to fire the EMP around Kang's Citadel. When that is done, the Nova Corps begin their attack only for Damocles to shoot them down when Kang summons it from outside Chronopolis. Riding the Milano, Iron Man, Spider-Man, and Star-Lord make their own entrance on the Damocles and fight their way through Kang's forces in order to get to the bridge. Arriving at the bridge, Iron Man, Spider-Man, and Star-Lord confront Kang and attack him only to find that they are attacking a hologram. Upon deactivating Damocles, Kang leaves Iron Man, Spider-Man, and Star-Lord with a parting gift in the form of Korvac. After helping to defeat Korvac, Iron Man, Spider-Man, and Star-Lord escape from Damocles before it can crash into the ground.

Peter was one of the heroes who later fought Kang's army for Captain America, Doctor Strange and Captain Marvel to enter Kang's tower. Following Kang's defeat, Spider-Man can be seen at the victory party talking to Star-Lord and Gamora while listening to Quill's favorite music tracks, even learning that he has every single album made by The Beatles.[9]

Gamora's Party[]

Peter Quill planned to throw Gamora a big birthday party, since she never had one. Spider-Man and Captain America would help Peter Quill in this mission, building a dance floor, a food table and a drum set for the Hulk to play music. The party actually works and Gamora is happy.[9]

Vexed by Venom[]

While riding Spider-Cycle, Peter bumps into Venom and the Green Goblin stealing a Roxxon truck. The three begin to fight and Peter manages to arrest the Green Goblin.[10] Now that the Goblin was trapped, Peter turns his lab into the Spidey Lab. Now in his new base, Peter creates numerous new equipment and manages to track Venom, discovering that he is in a Roxxon building.[11]

Arriving at the building, Peter fights Venom and uses his new Spider-Crawler.[12] Venom manages to escape, and Peter meets the Ghost-Spider, who gives him a device to call her in case he needs it. After interrogating a Roxxon scientist, Peter discovers that Venom was after a key to access any Roxxon power source.[17] Locating Venom in roxxon's renewable energy source, Peter uses his Spider-Mech to thwart villain's plans to overload the power station and blow up the city. Venom would also use a mech to fight the Spider, which makes him ask for Ghost-Spider's help to defeat the villain and manage to stop his plan. After the Venom's defeat, Peter goes to his college and gets detention for not doing his science project, but he doesn't go to detention to fight Doctor Octavius.[18]

Climate Conundrum[]

Tony Stark ends up calling Peter during his confrontation with Octavius, in which he asks Peter to borrow the Spider- Jet.[13]

Across the Spider-Verse[]

Peter later would get recruited to join Miguel O'Hara's Spider Society to help protect the Multiverse from anomalies. He took notice of the collateral damage left behind by The Spot and contacted O'Hara to alert him of the anomaly. He later returned to their HQ and was assigned by Jessica Drew of Earth-332 to a team consisting of Spider-Mobile, Spidercide, Tarantula and Last Stand Spider-Man so they could aid in dealing with the Spot situation.[3]

Peter would also end up joining other versions of himself at a Spider-Man dance party across the Multiverse, and among the variants alongside him were the Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man and the Amazing Spider-Man.[19]

Strange Tails[]

While walking through the city, Peter found a bag of money stolen by Black Cat that had been thrown from the Daily Bugle building, and when he tried to take it back to the building, he was surrounded by Daily Bugle helicopters.[20]

Notes

  • James Arnold Taylor voices Spider-Man in the video game LEGO Marvel Super Heroes. Drake Bell voices him in the mini-series LEGO Marvel Super Heroes: Maximum Overload. Martin T. Sherman voices him in the video game LEGO Marvel Super Heroes 2. Ben Diskin voices the character in the mini-series LEGO Marvel Super Heroes: Avengers Reassembled. Robbie Daymond voices him in the mini-series LEGO Marvel Spider-Man: Vexed by Venom. Cole Howard voices him in LEGO Marvel Avengers: Climate Conundrum. Nic Novicki voices him in the film Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse. Benjamin Valic voices him in LEGO Marvel Avengers: Strange Tails.
    • Taylor had previously portrayed the character in the video games Spider-Man: Battle for New York, Spider-Man: Friend or Foe and Marvel Avengers: Battle for Earth, Drake Bell was voicing the character in the then-concurrent Ultimate Spider-Man animated series, Robbie Daymond voiced the character in the animated series Marvel Disk Wars: The Avengers, Marvel Future Avengers and Marvel's Spider-Man, Cole Howard voiced the character in the animateds series Marvel Super Hero Adventures and Marvel Video Comics, Ben Diskin had voiced Spider-Man in the video game Marvel Ultimate Alliance 2, and Benjamin Valic voiced the character in Marvel's Spidey and His Amazing Friends.
    • Spider-Man is the only LEGO character who has a different voice actor in all his appearances.

Trivia

  • Their attires resembles that of some alternate versions.
    • In LEGO Marvel Super Heroes, his suit resembles that of his Earth-12041 counterpart.
    • His "Civil War" costume in LEGO Marvel's Avengers is the same as an unused version of the costume in Captain America: Civil War.
      • But in LEGO Marvel Super Heroes 2 it looks like the final version used in the movie.
    • In LEGO Marvel Spider-Man: Vexed by Venom, it resembles the Spider-Armor MK IV that his Earth-616 counterpart used in Amazing Spider-Man (Vol. 4).
  • Miguel O'Hara of Earth-928B considers this version of Peter to be one of the best operatives in the Spider Society.[3]
  • LEGO enthusiast and fan animator Preston Mutanga animated the segment in Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse that featured LEGO Spider-Man.[22] He was approached by producers Phil Lord and Christopher Miller after they caught notice of a fan project of his that consisted on recreating the main trailer for Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse as a LEGO animation.[23]
    • Phil Lord and Christopher Miller have a good relationship with the LEGO Group and Universal Studios, which allowed the LEGO Marvel Universe (Earth-13122) to officially appear in the movie.[24]
