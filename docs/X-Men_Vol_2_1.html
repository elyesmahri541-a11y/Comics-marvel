Synopsis for "Rubicon"

A group of Mutants have stolen a shuttle, intending to find Asteroid M, and are being pursued by some S.H.I.E.L.D. agents. One the three pursuing S.H.I.E.L.D. shuttles is destroyed by a weapon fired from the Mutant’s shuttle. The leader of the S.H.I.E.L.D. agents, Harry Delgado, requests permission from the command station on Earth to return fire. A sudden electromagnetic spike and a flash of light brings the pursuit to an end.

Magneto arrives, floating in a force field bubble in space, and tears the ships apart. Magneto expresses his lack of interest in Earth’s status, citing environmental damage and violence amongst Humans, but will not allow these people to bring violence to his home. One of the Mutants that sought Magneto out begs him not to leave them to die. Magneto replies that he will merely return them to Earth with a warning that he does not wish to be disturbed again.

The female Mutant assures Magneto that he is the man her group came to find, with the intention of joining his cause. Magneto suggests that those days are behind him. The woman says that Magneto’s people need him now more than ever. She asks if he’s able to turn his back on his fellow Mutants or deny his destiny.

On Earth, at the Star City Cosmodrome in Baikonur, the Director-General of the Cosmodrome is informed of the destruction of the American shuttles in space and the cause of their destruction. He asks if there has been confirmation of Magneto as the cause. His colleagues confirm that it was Magneto and that he’s maintaining Asteroid M’s orbit 250 kilometres above them. They also report that his power levels have increased. The Director-General gives the order to inform Moscow and Washington that he’s initiating Stage One of the Magneto Protocols.

In Washington, at the White House, President Bush is briefing Colonel Nick Fury about the message from the Cosmodrome. The President understands their reaction and suggests that he’d probably do the same. He wonders if the runaway Mutants might be able to sway Magneto to their cause. Fury worries that if Russia acts rashly against Magneto it might make matters worse. The President asks what Fury would advise.

At Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters, forty miles North of New York City, the X-Men are testing the building's defenses. They split into two teams, with the objective being to breach the defenses, locate, and then capture Professor X. In one of the rooms of the mansion, Charles talks to Jean Grey about his return to the team and how he needs to learn the X-Men’s individual abilities and how they work as a team.

Charles wonders if he should have never left since his dream that Humans and Mutants could coexist peacefully seems further away than ever. As Jean glances at a photo of the New Mutants, Charles wonders if he’d have been able to prevent the deaths of some of his students. Ultimately, Charles questions whether his dream still has any validity. Jean assures him that it does or the X-Men would not still be a team.

Outside the mansion, the team of Rogue, Iceman, Colossus and Archangel are attempting to breach the defenses installed by Forge. Missile launchers prove to be a problem until Rogue, pursued by the missiles, flies back towards the launchers causing the missiles to destroy them. With that taken care of, Archangel launches Colossus at the mansion. Piotr changes to his steel form and smashes through a window into a room, where he is telepathically attacked by Jean.

Banshee informs Cyclops that the other team of Wolverine, Psylocke and Gambit are making their way past defenses under the mansion. Cyclops sends robots to attack this team to thwart their efforts in finding Xavier. Whilst Wolverine and Psylocke take care of the robots, Gambit splits off from them and finds his way to Jean’s location. He throws a playing card charged with kinetic energy at the wall behind Jean, kisses her and finds it was a trap as the robotic Jean and Professor X explode. This takes Gambit out of the contest.

In the control room, the team is startled by Wolverine, masked from Jean’s psi-power by Psylocke, cutting his way through the floor and tagging Xavier, ending the contest. As the Danger Room session ends, revealing that the mansion the team was attacking was a hologram, Cyclops reprimands Wolverine for being careless with his claws around Xavier. Cyclops also wants to continue running training sessions. Wolverine insists that he wouldn’t have harmed Xavier and, after suggesting he’s spent enough time involved in teamwork, heads off to spend time alone.

Later, Nick Fury contacts the X-Men and informs them of events involving Magneto. Some of the team feels that, after his time as an ally, that Magneto poses no threat and should be left alone. Others feel that there may be reason to worry about Magneto again. Professor X assures Fury that they will share any information from their files that may help with the situation. He also agrees with Cyclops to split the X-Men into two teams, doubling their effectiveness and flexibility when dealing with situations.

Aboard Asteroid M, the two different crews regain consciousness. They become aware of their surroundings and argue amongst themselves. An inhibitor field stops most powers and weapons from working. However, the armor that the S.H.I.E.L.D. agents wear isn’t affected by the field. Deke, one of the agents, subdues Anne-Marie Cortez and tries to take control of the situation.

Magneto appears and demands that the fighting cease and weapons be lowered. Anne-Marie runs to him, but is shot in the back by Deke. As she collapses in his arms, Magneto uses his magnetic powers to point Deke’s own weapon at the man and makes it shoot him. One of the people who wish to join Magneto, Fabian Cortez, cautions Magneto that the authorities on Earth will react to events here and has a suggestion for Magneto to deter them from any actions towards him.

Back at the mansion, Professor X receives signals from Cerebro telling him that Magneto has arrived on Earth. He sends the X-Men Blue team to investigate. Magneto is in the Atlantic Ocean and has raised the same nuclear submarine, Leningrad, that he once sank after it was sent to attack him.[1] Much of the structure has been damaged, but many of the missiles that were within the submarine are intact. Magneto uses his magnetic power to remove them for his own use.

The X-Men confront him. Half the team face him while the other half approaches from behind in the new Blackbird built by Forge. Magneto is aware of their presence and traps the Blackbird in a magnetic field. Beast and Gambit leap from the Blackbird to distract Magneto, allowing Cyclops to pilot the Blackbird out of the magnetic field.

Beast gets taken down by Magneto and a blast from Gambit’s kinetically charged cards is blocked by a metal barricade, causing them to explode near the X-Men instead. Psylocke knocks Magneto down, dislodging his helmet. She prepares to use her psychic knife to subdue Magneto, but he uses his power to choke her with metal.

Wolverine then attacks and wounds Magneto in what Cyclops recognises as a killing attack. Cyclops tries to defuse the situation by knocking Magneto through the wall of the submarine with an optic blast. Inside the submarine, Magneto is confronted with the skeletons of the dead crew who were inside the Leningrad when Magneto sank it. Magneto had never given much thought to these men before, but seeing them now reminds him of his own family’s fate in Nazi death camps.

As Cyclops and Wolverine enter the submarine to face Magneto again, he knocks them away with a blast and flies off with the missiles. Rogue tries to follow him to talk, but is shot down by a Russian plane. Magneto makes sure Rogue lands safely and sets off a nuclear missile, causing an electromagnetic pulse and escapes. After returning to Asteroid M, Magneto is healed by Cortez, just as Cortez healed his own sister. Magneto is dismayed by the savagery shown by Wolverine, after the time they spent as allies. Cortez insists that the only way for Magneto to become the leader of Mutantkind is by eliminating the X-Men.

Professor X telepathically contacts the Blue team and informs them that, through Cerebro, he has located Rogue. He directs them to Genosha. Rogue awakens in a hospital in Hammer Bay. She is greeted by Chief Magistrate Tam Anderson who tells her about Mutant terrorists attacking Genosha. The hospital is suddenly attacked by the newly formed Acolytes wanting her to join them. She disagrees and fights them, and the X-Men arrive to help her.

Magneto turns up and declares that the Acolytes acted without his permission. He will decide their punishment for this, but also informs the X-Men that Asteroid M will be a haven for all Mutantkind. He also says that any harm against Mutants will be considered harm against himself, which he will respond to accordingly. Meanwhile, at Xavier’s School, Banshee checks in on Moira MacTaggert. She tearfully informs Banshee that events currently unfolding are her fault.

Notes

  • First issue of the series. Advertised as a "Mutant Milestone".
  • First appearance of the two Blue and Gold X-Men teams lead by Cyclops and Storm.
  • Most of the X-Men wear new costumes and Professor X is seen using his new hoverchair.
  • This issue came with five variant covers by Jim Lee that were each released a week apart over five weeks from August 13, 1991 to September 10, 1991. The first four covers formed individual images that were combined to form a four-page gatefold cover for the final variant released.
  • First appearance of The Acolytes.
  • References to the Soviet Union and George H. W. Bush being President of the United States of America here would presumably be affected by the Sliding Timescale.
  • Only one of the following featured pinup posters were shown in each of the variant editions of this comic:
"A Villains Gallery"
"A Blast From the Past"
"Wish You Were Here!..."
"Things to Come"

Trivia

  • This Comic Book (using the Magneto Variant Cover) was referenced in Marvel: Avengers Alliance and is one of the Comic Book Covers required in order to recruit Magneto in the game.
  • According to Guinness World Records, this issue is still the best-selling single edition comic book of all-time, with sales of over 8.1 million copies.[2]
  • The estate that Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters is on predates the American Revolutionary War.
  • When Anne-Marie Cortez is shot, her brother, Fabian suggests that he shares Magneto’s grief, which would indicate she was dead. Later, however, Fabian says that he healed her. So perhaps he wished Magneto to believe she was dead in order to ramp up Magneto’s hostility towards Humans and force him to take action.
