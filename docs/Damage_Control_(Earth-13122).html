History

A long-suffering organisation dealing with superhero clean-ups, Damage Control frequently collaborated with S.H.I.E.L.D.[1][2]

Rebuilding S.H.I.E.L.D. Base[]

Damage Control was put in charge of rebuilding the Project P.E.G.A.S.U.S. Facility after the explosion when Loki arrived, and one Damage Control worker with the help of another entity cleaned up some of the debris.[2]

Cosmic Brick Crisis[]

Damage Control collaborated with S.H.I.E.L.D. and Roxxon to help clean up Grand Central Station after Sandman and Abomination attacked it, with Hulk helping them.

Later, they worked with S.H.I.E.L.D. to clean-up Times Square after Mr. Fantastic, Captain America and Spider-Man's fight with Doctor Octopus. During this clean-up, one of the workers' shovels were stolen.

On the Hellicarrier, two Damage Control workers were plagued by a fire until a passerby managed to put it out.[1]

Rebuilding Sokovia[]

Damage Control was put in charge of rebuilding Sokovia, one worker used his drill called "Doris" to destroy nearby debris while passerby's helped protect him from Ultron Drones.[2]

Mission Demolition[]

Damage Control was tasked with cleaning up the damage caused in the fight between the Avengers and the Frightful Four. Due to messing up a lot, Dennis Dunphy was put to do everything with Terry, who would soon trick him into using one of the items that Damage Control kept stored in its HQ: GALACTUS.[3]
