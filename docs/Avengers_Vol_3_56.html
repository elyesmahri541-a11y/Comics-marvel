Synopsis for "Lo, There Shall Come... an Accounting!"

With most of the active Avengers unavailable, an emergency call sends a reserve team into action when chaos erupts in downtown St. Louis. Iron Man joins Beast, US Agent, Wonder Man, and Silverclaw in answering the distress signal, but their arrival is cut short when an airborne ambush forces their Quinjet to crash into the city below. In the crowded streets, the heroes come face-to-face with the Elements of Doom, artificial beings made of various chemical and metallic substances who are rampaging through the city and endangering countless civilians. As the Avengers struggle to contain the destruction and protect innocent bystanders, Iron Man recognizes the creatures from old Avengers files and transmits data back to Avengers Mansion.

A second team—Thor, She-Hulk, and Triathlon—soon arrives to reinforce them, only to have their Quinjet torn apart before it can land. The battle escalates as buildings are damaged, explosions erupt, and civilians are repeatedly caught in the crossfire. Thor swiftly destroys several of the creatures, She-Hulk saves trapped civilians from a collapsing structure, and USAgent accidentally triggers a devastating explosion while trying to stop one of the monsters. Meanwhile, Captain America and Jack of Hearts pursue another lead and travel to a federal prison to question Dr. Vassily Khandruvitch, the scientist responsible for originally creating the Elements of Doom. During their visit, another Element attacks the prison in an attempt to silence its creator. While Jack keeps the creature occupied, Captain America learns that A.I.M. recreated the monsters using Khandruvitch’s old research notes.

Khandruvitch provides Captain America with a schematic explaining how to destabilize the Elements’ crystalline brain structures—the only way to destroy them. Jack of Hearts races the plans back to St. Louis at incredible speed, where Iron Man, Beast, and Wonder Man quickly assemble an improvised weapon based on the design. Jack fires the device and successfully dissolves the Elements into inert sludge.

With the crisis over, the Avengers return to the Mansion to face a very different challenge: a financial audit from the Maria Stark Foundation. Chief accountant Maxwell Caton and the newly hired Janice Imperato conduct individual interviews with each Avenger involved, demanding explanations for every expense, damaged vehicle, and questionable decision made during the mission.

Beast gives a theatrical account of his “emergency landing,” Iron Man defends his unauthorized use of a communications satellite, and USAgent refuses to cooperate. Thor offers Asgardian gold to cover the cost of a destroyed building, She-Hulk calmly discusses a lawsuit filed by one of the civilians she rescued, and Captain America provides a perfectly organized report of his own actions.

After the interviews are complete, Maxwell assures the Avengers that the paperwork will be handled. As he and Janice leave the Mansion, he quietly explains that much of the report will be “fudged” to stay within acceptable limits. After all, their real job is not to make the Avengers’ lives harder—but to help keep Earth’s Mightiest Heroes in business.
