History

Judge Cohn was a judge who was found dead in the underground chambers of the Vault of the Departed Spirits, a secluded country house used by the Nazi agent Schultz, who operated under the guise of Doctor Eternity. When Schultz's Nazi accomplices discovered Cohn's corpse, they recognized him and remarked that they now understood what had happened to him, congratulating Schultz on a job well done. The circumstances of Cohn's death were not fully revealed.[1]
