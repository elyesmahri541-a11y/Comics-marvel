History

Background[]

One of the many daughters of the sorcerer and crimelord Zheng Zu, Esme was raised in the House of the Deadly Dagger outside of Paris, France, eventually becoming its Champion, "Sister Dagger".

Brothers and Sisters[]

When Sister Hammer named herself as the new Supreme Commander of the Five Weapons Society over its rightful successor, Shang-Chi, Sister Dagger and Brother Sabre approached their half-brother to usurp Hammer. Shang-Chi reluctantly joined them to free his remaining family from his father's cult.[3]

Although initially cold and hostile to him, Sister Dagger eventually warmed to Shang-Chi and told him her birth name, Esme.[4]

Sister Dagger helped Shang-Chi defend London from Sister Hammer and her Jiangshi army. After their victory, Shang-Chi was named the new Supreme Commander of the Five Weapons Society and offered Sister Dagger a place at his side, who happily accepted.[5]

Shang-Chi vs. the Marvel Universe[]

While investigating a rogue Society-operated drug ring in Manhattan, Sister Dagger interrupted Shang-Chi's date with Delilah Wang by delivering a captured drug dealer to the restaurant they were dining at. While Shang-Chi and Sister Dagger argued, the dealer attempted to kill the two with his clawed hands but was defeated. Discovering that the dealer had a Spider-Tracer on him, the two were approached by Spider-Man, who was tracking the criminal while investigating a weapons ring. Believing the two groups were connected, Spider-Man offered to help Shang-Chi with his investigation. Shang-Chi reluctantly accepted but did not tell Spider-Man about the Society, much to Sister Dagger's chagrin. The three discovered that the two rings were being led by King Wild Man, a loyal follower of Zheng Zu who manufactured and trafficked Yeren Root that transformed users into monstrous Yeren. Refusing to accept Shang-Chi as the new Supreme Commander, King Wild Man attached a root onto Spider-Man which transformed him into a Yeren. Shang-Chi and Sister Dagger were able to free Spider-Man from the root while King Wild Man escaped during the confusion. Feeling guilty for Spider-Man's injuries and Sister Dagger's accusations that he was ashamed of his family, Shang-Chi revealed his family's history and his new title to Spider-Man and summoned the Warriors of the Deadly Dagger to remove all traces of the Yeren root from King Wild Man's warehouse.[2]

When footage of her exiled half-sister Zheng Zhilan resurfaced on social media, Sister Dagger traveled with Shang-Chi and Brother Sabre to Ireland to bring her back to the Society. The reunion escalated into an all-out confrontation, which was interrupted by Wolverine, who revealed that Zhilan was a mutant and believed the siblings were trying to kill her. Shang-Chi proved that his intentions were pure to Wolverine and Zhilan, who accepted Shang-Chi's offer to rejoin the Society as its new Sister Staff.[6]

Sister Dagger accompanied Shang-Chi and their siblings when they broke into the Baxter Building to gain access to the Negative Zone. In the Negative Zone, Sister Dagger and the others met Jiang Li, Shang-Chi's mother, for the first time and brought her back to Earth, where they got into an altercation with the Fantastic Four. Sister Dagger briefly fought against the Human Torch before using one of her smoke bomb-equipped knives to help her escape with her family back to the House of the Deadly Hand.[7]

While at the House of the Deadly Sabre with her siblings, Sister Dagger saw Shang-Chi get confronted by several of his superhero allies who demanded he hand over a Cosmic Cube they believed he stole and came to his aid when the confrontation turned violent. While he fought Thor, Shang-Chi realized Brother Sabre had stolen the Cube and lied to him about it, prompting him to hand Takeshi over to the heroes as a prisoner along with the Cube. Shang-Chi's actions greatly upset Esme, who tearfully told Shang-Chi to never speak to her again when he attempted to console her.[8]

Family of Origin[]

Despite her anger towards him, Sister Dagger came to her brother's aid when assassins sent by Chieftain Xin attacked him at the New House of the Deadly Hand. Sister Dagger was able to defeat and capture them with the rest of the Society. Unfortunately, the would-be assassins took Jiang Li hostage, forcing Shang-Chi to release them and escape with her and Xin to Qilin Island.[9]

Blood and Monsters[]

When Shang-Chi agreed with Xin to exchange his life for Jiang Li's, Sister Dagger accompanied him and Sister Staff to the Mauna Kea summit in Hawai'i for the exchange. Xin and the Qilin Riders without Jiang Li and attacked the three siblings. When Shang-Chi was able to fight him off, Xin resorted to using some of Shang-Chi's blood to create three taotie before fleeing back to Ta-Lo. Sister Dagger and her siblings defeated one taotie and saved Falo from the second one. While researching the taotie, the siblings realized that Xin created them to hunt anyone in Zheng Zu's lineage and that Falo was a descendant of their father. When Falo attempted to make a pass at Sister Staff, Dagger threatened him, revealing that Staff was related to him as his ancestor was her father.[10]

Sister Dagger traveled with her siblings to the Vault where they saved Takeshi from the third taotie. When Shang-Chi received a vision of Shi-Hua getting attacked by Xin, he freed Brother Sabre from the Vault's guardsmen, much to Sister Dagger's delight and the four traveled to British Columbia to rescue Sister Hammer after she was left for dead by Xin. The five Champions of the Five Weapons Society took a constructed gateway from the New House of the Deadly hand to Ta Lo to rescue Jiang Li and to stop Xin.[11] Shortly after arriving in Ta Lo, the siblings were confronted by the Jade Emperor's guards for trespassing, which escalated to a brawl. The Jade Emperor arrived and used the Ten Rings to capture the siblings and had the sent to his dungeon. Under the influence of a Taotie Mask, Xin attacked the siblings in the dungeon to destroy Zu's bloodline once and for all. Shang-Chi took the Emperor's Ten Rings from their vault to save his family from his grandfather.[12]

Empowered by the Rings, Shang-Chi initially gained the upper hand against Xin but began resisting their power when Zu attempted to influence his thoughts, allowing Xin to claim the Ten Rings, which he used to bury Shang-Chi and his siblings underneath the dungeon. However, four the Rings remained with Shang-Chi, which Shang-Chi used to protect them from rubble and to dig themselves out of the dungeon, only to be confronted by the Jade Emperor and his guards. The Emperor tasked Shang-Chi and his siblings with apprehending Xin and recovering the Ten Rings. Revealing that Xin was attacking the House of the Deadly Hand with the Qilin Riders and that Jiang Li was helping defend it with the Warriors of the Deadly Hand, the Emperor provided Shang-Chi and the others with Qilin to return to Earth. At the Society headquarters, Shang-Chi confronted his grandfather again, but lost the remaining Rings to Xin, who then ordered the Riders to destroy New York City. Shang-Chi gave in to his dark desires, reclaiming all of the Ten Rings from Xin and unlocking their full potential but also gave him the appearance and personality of Zheng Zu. Shang-Chi used the Ten Rings to easily defeat Xin and the Qilin Riders, destroying their masks in the process. Before a corrupted Shang-Chi could cut off Xin's hand for what he did to Sister Hammer, Sister Dagger, their siblings and Jiang Li talked him down, freeing him from Zu's influence and sparing Xin. After the Society repaired the damage done to the city and Xin and the Ten Rings were taken back to Ta Lo, Esme returned to the House of the Deadly Dagger.[13]

Master of the Ten Rings[]

Sister Dagger reunited with Shang-Chi, Sister Staff and Brother Sabre, who was released early from prison, for a mission to prevent a gang of disgruntled Society members from resurrecting their father. The four arrived at the ritual site at a history museum in China, where the loyalist's leader revealed his plans to summon Zheng Zu at the height of his power from the past by using Falo as a sacrifice. During the fight a portal opened, forcing Shang-Chi to jump through and close it but left him trapped in early 19th century China. The ringleader threatened to leave Shang-Chi permanently trapped in the past if his demands weren't met, but Sister Dagger incapacitated him with her knives before he could fire his gun. Sister Dagger and her siblings defeated the remaining traitors and created another portal to summon Shang-Chi back. Since Shang-Chi's experience in the past got him to realize Zu was once good and honorable, Shang-Chi agreed with Takeshi to save their father's bones and had them buried next to Zheng Yi's grave. The siblings gave him full funeral rites and to honor the good man he once was and vowed to uphold his old legacy.[14]

Daggers and Claws[]

While Sister Dagger rescued tourists being terrorized by a plant monster at Tuileries Garden, she encountered Wolverine, who recognized her from their previous encounter in Ireland. The two worked together to subdue the monster but after hearing a voice from within the monster, Wolverine prevented Sister Dagger from killing it; both of them realized the voice was saying "tasukete" (Japanese for "help me") and extracted a Japanese tourist from the monster. While waiting at a nearby cafe, Sister Dagger inquired Wolverine about his knowledge of Japanese while he advised her to listen and wait before attacking. Sage contacted Wolverine and told him that the plant monster was created by XENO's Man-Slaughter Serum and gave him the coordinates where the creature originated from. Sister Dagger and Wolverine found XENO's mutated plant that was trapping tourists and transforming them into monsters, which the two took down.[15]
