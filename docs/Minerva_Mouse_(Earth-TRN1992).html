History

Minnie Mouse used to be a blogger, but one day she went to Scrooge McDuck's Money Bin asking for a job at his newspaper, The Chronicle. When she arrived there, she was introduced to Scrooge while he's battling Magica De Spell, who wanted to get Scrooge's Number One Dime; the witch shot a magic blast to the Dime, Minnie tried to save the coin and was hit by the spell instead. An exhausted Magica retired and Scrooge hired Minnie as a prize for saving his precious Dime. Once back at home, Minnie experienced a headache that resulted in her transformation into another person with no memory of who she was. She began a superhero career and newspapers called her Captain Marvel.

At a certain point Scrooge asked the Chronicle to interview the new heroine and he personally designated Minnie to be the interviewer. Minnie had no idea she herself was the one she should interview, so when Scrooge came for results, he was disappointed towards Minnie; however, in that moment, Peg Leg Scorpion attacked the newsroom looking for Scrooge, Minnie suddenly disappeared and Captain Marvel soon intervened, defeating the Scorpion, even if Scrooge blamed her for the damages occurred. Minnie reached McDuck later telling him she hadn't interviewed Captain Marvel as she hadn't even saw her, an angry Scrooge returned to his Money Bin. There he was attacked by the Beagle Boys on an enormus robot; believing Captain Marvel would have arrived soon, Scrooge called Minnie at his Bin, but on her way to there, she turned into the marvelous heroine and attacked the Beagle Boys; when the battle was about to end, Scrooge started yelling for Minnie and that caused Captain Marvel to regain her memories, understanding who she really was. After bringing the enemies to their boss, Rockerduck, Minnie was finally able to interview "Captain Marvel".[1]
