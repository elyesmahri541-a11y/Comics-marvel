History

Sim-Max is a holographic training room created in Big Hero 6's secret hideout by Roddy Blair. Sim-Max can transform the room into locations from all over San Fransokyo and is also equipped with training robots that can turn into Big Hero 6's enemies and mimic their powers. It can be controlled remotely and can also be operated by Basemax. Sim-Max was created to help the team train stronger and was first used by Fred in order for him to become strong enough to beat Major Blast.[1]

Later, Fred opened a scam email containing a virus that infected Basemax, causing her to believe she is the "Queen of Spamibia". Sim-Max, alongside Baymax, Mini-Max, Skymax and Mega-Max subsequently also got infected with said virus. The training robots of Sim-Max were dubbed the "Knights of Spamibia" and attempted to kill Big Hero 6, before Fred was able to disable the program.[2]
