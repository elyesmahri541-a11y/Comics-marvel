History

Before Karn came to this Earth, Superior Spider-Man of Earth-616 warned Spider-Cyborg about Karn. Later, Karn really came and fought Spider-Cyborg. But then Superior Spider-Man appeared and he defeated the villain.

Then he joined the Superior Spider-Man's team to defeat Karn once and for all,[1] but was destroyed by Daemos when the Inheritors invaded their home base on Earth-928 and had retreated to Earth-13.[2]
