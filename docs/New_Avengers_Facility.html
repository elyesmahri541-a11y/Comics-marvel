The Avengers Compound, also known as the New Avengers Facility, located on coast of Hudson River in Upstate New York, served as a headquarters and residence for the Avengers after the Ultron attack in Sokovia[1] until its destruction by Thanos[3].

History

After defeating Ultron in Sokovia, Tony Stark adapted an old Stark Industries warehouse in Upstate New York into a state-of-the art facility to act as the New Avengers Facility.[2][1]

With Iron Man and Hawkeye both formally retiring, Thor traveling to Asgard to investigate the appearance of multiple Infinity Stones, and the Hulk being A.W.O.L., Captain America and Black Widow were left with the task of training a new group of Avengers based in this new facility.[1]

When Hank Pym was planning to infiltrate Cross Technological Enterprises' main facility to steal the Yellowjacket's Suit, he sent Scott Lang to retrieve a piece of technology from the warehouse, unaware of its current status. Fortunately, most of the team were currently absent, but Scott was still forced to fight with Falcon to enter the facility and retrieve the needed component without alerting the Avengers to his agenda.[2]

After the Sokovia Accords were drawn up, Wanda Maximoff was forced to remain under house arrest in the facility due to her role in tainting the public's perspective of the Avengers when she unintentionally destroyed a Wakandan relief effort while fighting Brock Rumlow. Although she spent this time bonding with the Vision, when Hawkeye came to ask for her help, Wanda agreed to come with him, and when the Vision tried to stop her, she used her powers to take external control of the Vision's density, making his body so heavy that he crashed through the floor of the facility and was sent hurtling down several more stories while Clint and Wanda escaped.[4]

Although known active Avengers are now restricted to Stark, Rhodes, and the Vision, the facility remains in operation. Tony Stark had plans to host a press conference at the facility to introduce Spider-Man as the newest member of the Avengers, but when Peter turns down the offer of membership to focus on protecting man on the street and gain experience, Tony adapts the planned conference into proposing to Pepper Potts.[5]

After the Black Order attacked Earth in search of the Time and Mind Stones, abducting Iron Man, Spider-Man, and Doctor Strange while wounding the Vision, the 'rogue' Avengers- Rogers, Romanoff, Wilson and Maximoff- returned to the compound to treat the Vision's injuries and be briefed on the situation by the returned Banner. Although General Ross insisted that the rogue Avengers be arrested, Colonel Rhodes chose to focus on the threat at hand and terminated the connection. The Avengers later travelled to Wakanda to get the aid of the Wakandans in removing the Mind Stone from the Vision so that it could be destroyed without killing the Vision.[6]

After Thanos claimed all six of the Infinity Stones and killed half the universe, the surviving Avengers returned to the compound, where they met the original Avenger, Carol Danvers, called back by a message sent by Nick Fury before he was killed in the Snap [7]. Danvers was able to find Stark and Nebula abroaded the powerless Benatar and bring them back to Earth. After tracking down Thanos, the Avengers used the Benatar to travel to The Garden to confront him. Unfortunately the team arrived after he had destroyed the Stones to prevent anyone undoing what he had done.[6]

Over the next five years, the remaining Avengers attempted to pursue their own methods of rebuilding the universe post-Snap, with Natasha remaining at the Compound to coordinate the Avengers' efforts to protect what was left, extending their remit to include the wider universe as Danvers, Nebula, and Rocket also reported to her. When Scott Lang returned 'from the dead', he travelled to the facility to explain his time in the Quantum Realm to the other Avengers, along with his theory that the Realm could be used as a means of time travel. With the aid of Stark and Banner, Lang was able to adapt the quantum realm technology to send the ten Avengers (Rogers, Stark, Thor, Banner, Romanoff, Barton, Rhodes, Lang, Nebula and Rocket) back in time to various points where they could retrieve the Infinity Stones from a point before their destruction and bring them to the present. Unfortunately, Nebula's presence in the past resulted in her cybernetic components linking to the components of her past self while she was in 2014 to retrieve the Power Stone, alerting the Thanos of that era to her actions and allowing him to devise a means of following the Avengers back to the future. Thanos's arrival in the present decimated the facility, leaving Stark, Rogers and Thor to face Thanos while Barton tried to protect the Stones and Lang, Banner, Rhodes and Rocket attempted to escape the collapse. After the heroes who had been restored from the Snap joined the other Avengers, they defeated Thanos's forces in a final battle, but the facility remained destroyed, and it is unclear if it shall ever be rebuilt after the Avengers have essentially disbanded.[3]

Various objects have been scavenged from the wreckage of the compound and sold in black market auctions.[8]

Alternate Reality Versions[]

Marvel Animated Universe (Earth-12041)[]

An old S.H.I.E.L.D. facility, that became the new Avengers' base after Avengers Tower was destroyed.[9] This facility didn't last long, as it was destroyed, leaving the current roster of New Avengers to find their next base of operation at within the Wakandan Embassy under the co-leadership of Black Panther.[10] Although, later the facility had been completely rebuild, which then became the Avenger's main base once again.[11]


LEGO Universe (Earth-13122)[]

This version of the Avengers Compound is located in New York City. The Avengers built it after Age of Ultron, and not only does the team reside there, but S.H.I.E.L.D. itself also resides there. Captain America and Black Widow were left with the task of training a new group of Avengers based in this new facility.[12]

After the Avengers were mind-controlled by Sphinx, Black Panther, Hawkeye, and Iron Man fled to the Compound, and while the first two sought mystical aid to confront the villain, Iron Man created a new Hulkbuster Armor to defeat her.[13]

Trivia

  • The Sainsbury Centre for Visual Arts at the University of East Anglia in Norwich, United Kingdom served as the real world filming location for the "New Avengers Facility".[14]
  • Porsche North American headquarters in Atlanta, USA also doubled as the "New Avengers Facility".
  • SANY America Headquarters[15] in Peachtree City, USA also doubled as the "New Avengers Facility".
  • Additionally, according to the “Avengers: Endgame” commentary, before the Avengers Compound is destroyed by Thanos at the end of the movie, Christopher Markus points out that the sequences in the building were actually shot in an abandoned hotel in Atlanta. This converted soundstage is located off Sullivan Rd in College Park, GA, near the airport.
  • According to CineFex Magazine issue #147, in Captain America: Civil War, an aerial plate was shot of an actual plot of land along the Hudson River from the west side of the river in Esopus, NY. And later in Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame, aerial shots of plot move to the east side, and a bit north, in Staatsburg, NY, using Mills Mansion and the Staatsburgh State Historic Site grounds were used[16].
