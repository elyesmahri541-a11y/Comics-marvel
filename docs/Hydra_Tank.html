History

The Hydra Tanks are Russian-made BMP armored fighting vehicles, upgraded with armor panels, sensor arrays, EMP emmittors, and powerful Chitauri pulse weapons by Hydra.[1] After the Battle of New York, the Hydra agents within S.H.I.E.L.D. obtained numerous Chitauri tech. Using them, they upgraded the tanks. The tanks were used against the Avengers when they stormed their base looking for Loki's Scepter.[2]
