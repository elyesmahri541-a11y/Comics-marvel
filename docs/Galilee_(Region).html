History

Galilee was a region in the Roman Empire. Mary and her husband Joseph travelled from Nazareth in Galilee to Bethlehem in Judea.[1] On the shores of Galilee, Jesus preached.[2] Jesus preached at Capernaum where he healed the son of a nobleman.[3] Yahweh had a brief psychotic episode in Galilee.[4]
