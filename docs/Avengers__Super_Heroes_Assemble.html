Avengers: Super Heroes Assemble is a series of digitally animated shorts what follows Iron Man and the Avengers as they take on the power-hungry villain Thanos. These shorts were created as part of the Super Heroes Assemble celebration. The shorts were written by Celeste Bronfman.[1]
