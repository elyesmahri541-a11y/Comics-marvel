Synopsis for "Whiplash's Revenge!"

Mark Scarlotti recalls a delusional version of his past, claiming he became Whiplash to punch up against the rich like Tony Stark on behalf of the common man, until he died. As Iron Man tries to explain that it was his Sentient Armor who murdered him, the villain pummels Stark with his whips.

One blackout later, Iron Man finds himself suspended by cables in the basement of Stark Tower, the wires plugged into his partially destroyed armor. He is accompanied by Citizen V, restrained and hanging upside down by whips. Stark strikes up a conversation with his fellow hostage, who warns that he is trying to stop Tony from hurting himself or others, prompting Stark to reminisce about the friends and loved ones he has lost over the years. As the duo tries to make sense of their surroundings, the Whiplashes—Scarlotti and Vanko—arrive to explain they will overload Tony's armor and trigger a gigantic explosion. Mark brushes off Vanko's concern that they are about to kill rather than merely distract their target, against their employer's wishes. Meanwhile, Adam Ware and Madame Masque pay M.O.D.O.K. a holographic visit inside the Vault. M.O.D.O.K. agrees to join Ware's side, but Adam insists he remain imprisoned to play a role in his plans for Tony. He warns M.O.D.O.K. that he is creating something new to help save the world and expects Stark to be grateful.

Inside the Iron Man Workshop, Tony's students clash over their differing worldviews and what they want from the school. Melinda May arrives to give Jim Rhodes a sitrep on her investigation into A.I.M. When May catches Melrose Morrison using stealth tech to eavesdrop, Melrose offers to build her a stealth armor of her own. Back at Stark Tower, the building's employees have taken shelter on a terrace; Pepper Potts is among them, along with Luna Lucia and her son Hugo, both frightened by the unfamiliar situation. Pepper realizes the Whiplashes are using a signal blocker since her phone gets no service, so she sneaks back inside hoping to find a way to call for help. Hugo decides to use a different door to return inside, but he is intercepted by Scarlotti, much to Luna's horror.

Vanko is finishing the device meant to overload Iron Man's armor when Citizen V begins to taunt him. He tries to remove Citizen V's mask, only for a booby trap to electrocute and knock him down. Pepper arrives, confusing Tony shen she first helps free Citizen V. Tony notices V calling her "Freckles." As the three figure out their next move, Scarlotti arrives with both Hugo and Luna as hostages. He dares Stark to attempt an escape that could trip the explosion, or else he will kill Hugo. Citizen V offers to surrender in exchange for the boy's safety and removes his mask, arguing that Tony will care about him. Stark is shocked as Citizen V reveals himself to be Happy Hogan, back from the dead.

Solicit Synopsis

WHO IS WHIPLASH?

Whiplash is one of Tony Stark's greatest enemies, but why does he hate Tony so much? As the mysterious Citizen V and Iron Man must work together to stop Whiplash's dangerous plans to destroy everything Tony has built, we take a rough trip down memory lane to learn the true history of WHIPLASH! Also, Madame Masque and Adam Ware use Whiplash's distraction to make a move on the rest of A.I.M. that will change the secret organization forever!

Notes

  • The deluded version of his backstory that Whiplash narrates—that of selflessly joining Stark Industries to help the world only to become disillusioned by Tony Stark—is a reference to the events of Iron Man #62, in which Whiplash infiltrates Stark Industries under the identity of "Mark Scott" to get close to Tony Stark to enact his revenge on Iron Man.
  • Throughout the flashback montage of his origin, Whiplash sports the black-and-purple version of his uniform that corresponded to his alias of "Blacklash", as opposed to his original purple-and-orange suit. Scarlotti adopted the black-and-purple color scheme in Iron Man #146.
  • As stated by an editor's note when Iron Man tries to clear things up with Whiplash, the villain's death at the hands of his sentient armor ocurred in Iron Man (Vol. 3) #28.
  • Scarlotti mentions that his death left his wife a widow. However, Trudi Scarlotti was murdered in Elektra (Vol. 2) #7 by Shatterhead, prior to her husband's fatal confrontation with Iron Man in Iron Man (Vol. 3) #28.
  • When talking with Citizen V, Tony Stark sorrowfully reminisces about many friends and allies he has lost throughout his life, these include:
    • Ho Yinsen, who sacrificed himself to give Stark more time to boot up the very first Iron Man Armor in Tales of Suspense #39.
    • Morley Erwin, Tony's partner at Circuits Maximus, who died when Obadiah Stane had its base of operations bombed in Iron Man #199.
    • Kathy Dare, Stark's psychotic lover who shot and paralyzed him, and committed suicide out of guilt in Iron Man #286.
    • Ayisha Ashirov, a Siberian freedom fighter and love interest of Tony. She was once presumed dead in Iron Man (Vol. 3) #50.
    • Captain America, specifically alluding to his apparent death in Captain America (Vol. 5) #25 in the aftermath of Civil War.
    • Rumiko Fujikawa, Stark's girlfriend, killed by Clarence Ward in Iron Man (Vol. 3) #87.
    • Cly Erwin, the sister of Morley Erwin and a fellow member of Circuits Maximus who turned against Tony following her brother's death, blaming him for it and allying herself with A.I.M. to enact her revenge. She died in Iron Man #216, after a downed plane she caused to be shot down by A.I.M.'s weapons crashed into her.
    • Slag, a mentor of Tony in his college years, who mutated into the ferrous Slag and Tony had to mercy-kill in Iron Man #318.
    • Finally, Happy Hogan, who seemingly died in Invincible Iron Man #14 after being beat up and put into a coma by Spymaster.
  • This issue includes an installment of the "Printed Circuits" letters page. It features a singular letter from editor Danny Khazem. He provides a breakdown of the deceased allies of Iron Man that Tony reminisces about, acknowleding that Sal Kennedy and Maya Hansen didn't make it into the page. Khazem also recaps the news from San Diego Comic-Con 2026 about Iron Man double-shipping in the lead-up to Iron Man (Vol. 7) #700 with the intertwining story arcs "The Incarcerated Iron Man" and "War Machines."
