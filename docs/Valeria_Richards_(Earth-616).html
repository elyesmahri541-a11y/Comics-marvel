Valeria Richards is the second child of Dr. Reed Richards and Sue Storm of the Fantastic Four.[9] Due to her parents being exposed to cosmic radiation and being conceived in the Negative Zone,[9] it caused her birth to be traumatic.[10] Thanks to her "Uncle"[11] Doctor Doom, she was saved through a combination of science and magic. Allowed to name her because of this, Doom chose "Valeria," the name of his old flame.[12] Despite the enmity between Doom and Reed, Valeria has a very close relationship with Doom.[11][13][14] Like her father, she developed a genius-level intellect,[15] and she later gave herself the alias Brainstorm.[2] Her intelligence is contrasted with the vast imagination possessed by her brother, Franklin.[16]

History

A false start at life[]

Valeria Richards, the second child of Mister Fantastic and the Invisible Woman, had a challenging entry into the world. Conceived in the Negative Zone, as the unborn infant neared term she began unleashing bursts of lethal radiation that endangered both mother and child. Despite enlisting fellow scientists and radiation specialists Bruce Banner, Michael Morbius, Walter Langkowski, and Otto Octavius, Reed Richards proved unable to save his daughter, who appeared to be stillborn.[10]

Life as Marvel Girl on Earth-99315[]

In fact, Franklin Richards, her older brother who possessed near-unlimited reality altering powers, felt profound grief over losing the sister he never got to know. Unconsciously guided by Omniversal Guardian Roma, Franklin secretly used his immense abilities to journey back through time and rescue his sister, taking her away from her native reality-616 and sending her "somewhere else" — into an alternate future of Earth-99315 where she was raised by another Invisible Woman who had wed a heroic Doctor Doom following the death of her first husband. Whether Earth-99315 Invisible Woman somehow bore Valeria herself, or received her as a newborn infant, is unclear; regardless, she raised Valeria as their own, and she grew up regarding Doctor Doom as her father. Roma, who harbored her own designs for the child, apparently kept a watch over Valeria.[1]

Among Valeria's childhood companions were Caledonia, a member of Captain Britain Corps and this future's Fantastic Four, and Lancer, her father's mutant bodyguard. Her stepbrother Franklin Richards, whom she nicknamed Sparky, would irritate their mother by exploiting his powers to go "time-dancing." As Valeria grew and her abilities and adventurous nature began to surface, Doom crafted armor for her protection, and she adopted the alias Marvel Girl. At age twelve, she was formally installed as Doom's heir in the throne room of the Baxter Building. Not long afterward, her father and her Ben and Johnny perished saving the world. Later, while visiting the Blue Area of the Moon to view Alicia Masters' memorial statue for the fallen heroes, both Valeria and Franklin briefly communicated with a temporally displaced echo of Earth-616's Reed, Ben, and Johnny.[1]

Meeting the Fantastic Four of Earth-616[]

When the Fantastic Four of Earth-616 were in Paris, Valeria time-traveled — apparently by accident — into the modern-day Pier Four, meeting the younger Franklin and his babysitters Caledonia and Alyssa Moy. When the interdimensional hunter Bounty attacked, seeking to capture Caledonia, Valeria immediately rallied to her defense. The battle was interrupted by the arrival of the Bacchäe, warrior women serving the Amazon Hippolyta. Intent on settling a score with the Fantastic Four from a prior encounter, the Bacchäe seized everyone present and dragged them before their mistress and her lord, the Olympian god Pluto. Caledonia dueled the Amazonian queen while Marvel Girl attempted to shepherd the others to safety. When Caledonia bested his champion, the wrathful Pluto banished her to Tartarus; Marvel Girl and the others gave chase, utilizing the teleportation powers of Franklin's pet Puppy, arriving in the Olympian underworld after a detour through Asgardian Hel and a run-in with Hela. They liberated Caledonia and together fought their way past the combined forces of Tartarus, until Marvel Girl confronted Pluto himself and demanded their peaceful release. Though initially resistant, the Lord of Hades relented upon the arrival of the Fantastic Four, accompanied by Hercules and Persephone, Pluto's wife.[1]

At first Susan Richards struggled to accept Valeria as her daughter, shocked by the idea that she would one day marry Doctor Doom and reliving the painful memories of her daughter believed to be stillborn. Hearing her mother rebuff her, Valeria fled and wandered the streets, eventually stumbling upon the Absorbing Man, Titania, and a mind-controlled She-Hulk clashing with the police. Marvel Girl attempted to intervene, but was overpowered by the combined strength of her adversaries. When the Thing and Bounty appeared, they too were swiftly placed under mind-control. Arriving to find Valeria in danger, the Invisible Woman embraced her daughter and delivered swift retribution to her attackers. Valeria assisted Reed in identifying the microbial nanites used to brainwash their allies, and the hunt for a cure drew the group into conflict with the Mad Thinker, whose meticulously laid schemes were derailed because he had failed to account for Marvel Girl's presence. Later, after a magical assault left Alyssa Moy in a coma, Valeria proved pivotal in connecting Selene and the Hellfire Club to the attack. She, Franklin, and Caledonia were taken hostage by another arcane adversary, Jihad, who coerced the Fantastic Four into retrieving four mystical artifacts on his behalf.[1]

An impending Chaos Wave heralded Doctor Doom's return from Counter-Earth and led to Valeria facing off against this malevolent, younger incarnation of the man she regarded as her father. But when Reed Richards was trapped within Doctor Doom's Armor while Doom was apparently slain, it appeared as though Valeria's future was unfolding — with Richards as her true father. For their protection, Valeria and Franklin were sent with Caledonia's care to Haven, a school for gifted children on the far side of the universe. In their absence, Richards was freed from Doom's armor, while Doom himself was discovered to have survived, once again casting doubt on Valeria's future timeline.[1]

The two children returned from Haven shortly before the emergence of Abraxas, a cosmic entity who coveted the Ultimate Nullifier and sought dominion over the multiverse. While the Fantastic Four scoured other realities for the Nullifier, Abraxas set his sights on Valeria as a temporal anomaly. Franklin and Roma whisked her away, but Abraxas soon tracked them down, apparently slaying Roma and seizing the Nullifier. As Abraxas seemed on the verge of triumph, Roma's scheme bore fruit; Franklin and Valeria combined their powers to resurrect Galactus, the sole being capable of wresting the Nullifier from Abraxas. Galactus surrendered the device to Mister Fantastic, who activated it, annihilating Abraxas and resetting reality. The act drained Franklin's cosmic powers, and Valeria reverted to an unborn child within Sue's womb.[17]

Second Birth[]

Sue soon came to term, but her life was once again threatened by radiation bursts from the child within her. With Mister Fantastic taken captive by the Hidden Ones, the Human Torch desperately sought out Doctor Doom, who successfully employed a fusion of science and sorcery to safely deliver the infant. In exchange he was granted the right to name her and chose "Valeria," after his childhood love. Cradling the newborn, Doom seized the opportunity to weave an enchantment, transforming Valeria into his familiar, granting him sight through her eyes and the ability to channel her as a conduit for spellcasting.[12] Several months later, after sacrificing Valeria's namesake in exchange for heightened magical power, Doom launched his assault. As Franklin was condemned to Hell, Valeria uttered her first word: "Doom." The villain taunted his enemies through the young girl's voice, and when the Fantastic Four traveled to Latveria to confront him, he subdued them with ease, teleporting Valeria into his grasp. With the aid of Doctor Strange, Reed ultimately managed to turn the tide and reclaim his children, leaving Doom ensnared in Hell and his bond to Valeria seemingly severed.[1]

Due to the numerous super-villain attacks on the Baxter Building, Child Welfare questioned the safety of Franklin and Val. After much reluctance, Reed and Sue decided to give up their children to Welfare. However, an initial condition was that a "dummy" safe-house was to be set up, and a press release to be announced that the children had already been moved. The decision to actually move Franklin and Valeria was rescinded after the safehouse that they were to be placed in was reduced to a crater (along with everything within a half-mile radius). It is uncertain exactly which of the FF's enemies carried out the attack, or why (there is speculation that Reed carried out the attack himself to allow him and Sue to keep the kids), but it was enough to convince Child Welfare.

A few months following the Civil War, Valeria's intelligence took a quantum leap, increasing to a level that would match her father's in only a matter of time. Val decided to keep her new abilities a secret, feeling the dynamic of her family would be altered for the worst. She was helped to embrace her new nature by her nanny Tabitha Deneuve, who was secretly a future version of Val's own mother.[18] She ultimately came clean to her family, and even started working with her father in his lab.[19]

Dark Reign[]

Franklin found himself along with his sister under siege by Norman Osborn, Venom and a high number of H.A.M.M.E.R. agents. The siblings were on their own due to their father's experiment which left him unreachable and the other members of the Fantastic Four stranded in alternate realities.[20]

Valeria managed to separate Osborn from the rest of the H.A.M.M.E.R. agents by using a bureaucratic technicality and by having them underestimate her. Osborn is led into a room where he faced Franklin who was wearing a Spider-Man mask and called him a villain. Osborn then chased the Richards children down a hall, getting ready to shoot them.[21]

The Fantastic Four had resurfaced by this point with an angry Invisible Woman telling Osborn "Step away from the children." Angry, he snapped that she should try to make him. Sue obliges by smashing him against a wall by using one of her invisible force fields. Osborn retaliated by summoning his H.A.M.M.E.R. agents and Venom. None, however, manage to pass through Sue's invisible force field barrier.[22]

Mr. Fantastic informed Osborn to leave the Baxter Building and not to come back, dismissing him. Infuriated, he attempted to shoot Reed, only to be shot by Franklin in turn. Although all are shocked, none understand how Franklin managed to shoot Norman since Franklin was only wielding a toy gun (both Johnny and Ben examine the gun once it was shot), hinting that perhaps some of Franklin's reality altering abilities have resurfaced.[22]

The Three[]

As her intelligence grew vastly, Valeria was met in the Baxter Building by a strange invader, that she of course realized was her brother Franklin from the future. He warned her of a pending crisis known as the 'War of the Four Cities' and that to save their father "All hope lies in Doom." Valeria discovered the portal her father secretly created and goes through it, finding The Council, a collection of many alternate reality versions of her father. They are being destroyed by the Celestials and three of them manage to escape through with Valeria to her home.

Realizing the threat they pose and making the connections to what her brother warned her of, Valeria secretly went to get Doctor Doom's assistance, only to find him in a state of brain damage (from the World War Hulks story arc). She made a deal with Doom - help her save her father and she would help restore his intelligence.

Around this time, Reed brought Valeria and a group of other gifted youngsters together to help solve the problems of the world - which would be known as the Future Foundation. But even their great minds could not solve the problem of the invading Annihilation Wave and they could not close the portal to the Negative Zone. In order to close it, the Human Torch sacrificed himself. This caused much anger in Valeria, who began coming up with plans to kill Annihilus.

After speaking with her grandfather Nathaniel, they introduced the induction of Doom in to the Foundation, much to the dismay of the remaining members of the Fantastic Four. Along with many other great villainous minds, they began to form their plan to stop the Council from their devious plans.

Future Foundation[]

Due the presence of Reed Richards of alternate universes, Mr. Fantastic made an alliance with many villains such as Mad Thinker, AIM, Wizard, Diablo and High Evolutionary to plan how to stop them. Then they lead the battle to High Evolutionary's city where one of the Richards escaped with Doctor Doom and the other two were captured by the Kree army. After returning to Baxter Building, Richards reunited other heroes to fight the Inhumans in Attilan, but at the time they where preparing to go to the rooftop, a Kree invasion started. Reed used Iron Man's armor and Sue's power to create a force field around Manhattan, at the same time the Cult of the Negative Zone opened the portal to the Negative Zone after Spider-Man failed to stop them, the portal revealed a living Johnny Storm with an enslaved Annihilus.[23]

Then Johnny gathered the team by drawing with fire the symbolical "4" at the sky above the Baxter Building. Reed, Sue, Ben and Peter boarded one of the Annihilation Wave's ships controlled by Johnny, who used the rest of the ships to battle the Kree army until the Inhumans asked to finish them by their own hand.[24] Meanwhile the other heroes on Earth where dealing with the scraps of the battle which could damage the Earth, Reed and Sue summons Galactus, who completely destroys the Kree army. That moment, the Mad Celestials appeared to destroy this universe. Although the team tried to use the Hub (a weapon of mass destruction created by the Reeds), they couldn't defeat the Mad Celestials. Then, a future version of Franklin and Valeria appeared as part of Nathaniel Richards's plan.[25] The Future Franklin used his power to heal Galactus and destroy the Mad Celestials, saving the day.[26] Later, the Baxter Building was easily rebuilt, Mr. Fantastic showed a new secondary headquarters called Foundation and new costumes were given to the members of the Future Foundation, also with the new additions of the future Franklin and Valeria.[27]

Later on during the "Death of the Invisible Woman" arc; it is revealed that Valeria has already been exhibiting her genius-level intellect through a conversation she has with her nanny, Tabitha Deneuve. Tabitha in reality is the future self of Sue. When the Baxter Building is under attack and Sue tries to protect Valeria and Tabitha, she is unaware that Tabitha was the one that set off the attack. Sue is knocked out by Tabitha's more powerful force field powers. When Tabitha is about to escape, Valeria follows her and asks her why she's doing this. And that's when Valeria reveals to everyone that Tabitha was Sue from the future.

After reviewing the events of what happened when Future Sue attacked, Reed watches video footage of Valeria and before the team goes off to fight the team of future super powered individuals, he exclaims to Valeria that he needs to speak to her. Later it is seen that Valeria has let her intelligence loose full force. Sue finds highly advanced books in Valeria's book stand. Along with that, Valeria is seen following in her father's steps by creating some highly advanced inventions.

Through some means the Invisible Woman "vanishes" and as a result Valeria and Franklin go to seek help from the mutant detective agency X-Factor Investigations. While there explaining the situation and showing her superior intellect, Valeria reminds many of the members of a young Layla Miller, and freaks out Darwin. They bring the group to the Baxter Building where half got into a fight with the Thing to provide a distraction, and the other half get the needed information.

Valeria chose to move to Latveria and reside with Doctor Doom. She planned to turn Doom into a force for good, which seemed to be working. With all the distrust she now had for her father, it seemed like the right choice for the time. When her mother attempted to take her by force from Doom, Sue began to show signs that Malice was returning to her psyche. While she originally refused to return home, she eventually decided that her family needed her.

The Multiversal Collapse[]

When the Multiverse was coming to its inevitable end, Valeria helped her father create a life raft to ensure the survival of humanity after the final incursion.[28] As the final incursion between Earth-616 and Earth-1610 took place, Reed gathered every person he could upon the life raft including members of the Future Foundation, and the rest of his family. When the vessel approached the central point of the incursion, it suffered a hull breach that led to the deaths of Reed's family, including Valeria.[29] Reed eventually gained the omnipotent power of the Beyonders from the Molecule Man after stripping them from Doctor Doom. With his new powers, Reed brought his family back to life. Valeria joined the Future Foundation in their journey to restore the Multiverse, while also creating entirely new realities.[30]

Years into the Future Foundation's multiversal adventures, Franklin became depleted of his ability to create entirely new realities. At this moment the team was confronted by an abstract entity called the Griever at the End of All Things who repudiated their mission of creating new universes. She caused the collapse of the hundreds of universes charted by the Future Foundation, forcing the team to make a final stand. The Fantastic Four were reunited when Mister Fantastic tricked the Griever into giving them the chance to summon the remaining members of the Fantastic Four for backup. In addition to teleporting Ben and Johnny, Reed also brought every superhero who had been part of the team at one point in order to defeat the new foe.[2] After the Griever was driven away, the Future Foundation continued their multiversal travels, but Reed, Sue, Franklin, and Valeria returned with Ben and Johnny to Earth, where only months had passed since the Foundation's years-long journey began.[31]

Along with the rest of the Fantastic Four's children and the Baxter Building, Reed Richards sent Valeria exactly one year into the future in a desperate ploy to defeat invaders from the Negative Zone, whose forces were sent six months into the past on the opposite side of the sun.[32] After a year passed, which for the children was just a moment, Valeria and the others finally returned and were reunited with their families. As the Fantastic Four had moved on from the Baxter Building, the entire family translocated to the Fantastic Farmhouse in Benson, Arizona where the children began attending a new school.[33]

One World Under Doom[]

Following the events of Blood Hunt, Doctor Doom would be made Sorcerer Supreme after tricking Doctor Strange into giving him the mantle to undo Varnae's machinations. Strange made him promise to return the mantle after Varnae's defeat, only for Doom to twist the promise so he gives Strange the mantle back after he saved the world from every threat.[34][35]

Valeria, alongside her brother, Franklin, Jo-Venn Masters-Grimm, and N'Kalla Masters-Grimm would be at home watching Doom battle the Fantastic Four, Avengers, and X-Men. Wanting to stop the fight, Valeria would build a device that would allow her to astral project into Doom's consciousness so she can try and get him to stop fighting.[7]

While in his mindscape, Valeria would thank Doom for sparing her father. She would then pleaded for Doom to stop, going on to say that he could not hold the entire population by force, to which he replied that he will prove it and sent her back to where she came from as he continued to fight the heroes. Doom would then enacted his final attack to create the fixed point in reality. He charged his armor with all his power and magic to ensure that his rule will stand for eternity. His armor would then explode off his body, sending shards flying throughout the battlefield and creating the fixed point. With the heroes defeated and Doom victorious, he did not realize that multiple shards of his armor stabbed into Valeria, who arrived at the battlefield moments before he performed the attack. She weakly attempted to call out his name before her head fell from her body, killing her.[7]

Horrified by what his actions caused, Doom used magic to hold her soul before she passed on and stitch her body back together. He tried to use his own powers in an attempt to resurrect her, only for it to fail. He opened a door to the Vishanti to make them resurrect her, only for them to refuse to hear him, to which he attacked them in an attempt to make them listen. With no options left, Doctor Doom traveled to the realm of the Living Tribunal. He would then bargain with them to break the fixed point in reality he created and resurrect Valeria in exchange for everything in his power. Initially, the Living Tribunal refused, only for Doom to let them see his soul and the love that he carried for her, willing to make any sacrifice to bring her back. In the end, the Living Tribunal agreed and revived Valeria, along with breaking the fixed point in reality Doom created, with Doom dying.[8]

Attributes

Power Grid[50]

Powers

Former Powers

Quasi-Psionic/Reality Energy Manipulation: On Earth-99315, Valeria von Doom, much like her brother Franklin Richards, had inherited the ability to channel and manipulate quasi-psionic and reality-altering energies for a variety of effects and purposes, ranging from the creation and manipulation of force-fields, generating concussive blasts, and enhancing her physical conditions. After being reborn in Earth-616, she lost all of her powers.[1]

  • Psionic Force-Field Generation: Valeria can also mentally generate a psionic field of invisible force apparently drawn from the same reality warping time and space quasi-psionic energies her brother uses, which she manipulates for a variety of effects. Most commonly, she generates near-indestructible invisible force fields around herself or other targets. She can vary the texture and tensile strength of her field to some extent, rendering it highly rigid or as soft and yielding as foam. She is capable of generating and manipulating multiple psionic force fields simultaneously. She may also use this power to create powerful force-bolts and levitate herself and other objects similar to Telekinesis.[1]
  • Concussive Blasts: She can focus her psionic energies into very powerful energy blasts of concussive and explosive force.[citation needed]
  • Superhuman Durability: Her aura is made of the same energies she uses to make her force fields.[citation needed]
  • Nullify Reality Alteration: She claimed she could block Franklin's reality-manipulating powers.[1]
  • Teleportation and Time Traveling: She has the ability to teleport herself and others though the space-time continuum through force of will.[1]

Abilities

  • Super-Genius Intelligence: Valeria possesses remarkable intelligence even as a child thanks to the cosmic mutation that she inherited from her parents.[6] As a toddler she was seen to complete a Rubik's Cube with her father saying that she will be playing chess by the time she is two.[36] She has claimed that she is smarter than her father.[15]

Notes

  • Similar to Franklin, Valeria's age is a subject of controversy. She is frequently drawn older, and sometimes she is drawn close in age to Franklin himself, despite them being born several years (or 34 years in real-life time) apart. In 2014, Tom Brevoort stated on Tumblr that Valeria was three years old,[37][38] while Franklin was eight years old,[39] indicating a difference of five years. However, time is also known to be compressed due to the sliding timescale.

Trivia

  • Similar to how Valeria's father, Reed Richards, is known as the "world's smartest man," she has proclaimed herself to be the "world's smartest girl."[3]
    • This title is challenged by Moon Girl, the only person who ever solved the Banner B.O.X. When the two of them met, Moon Girl informed Valeria of this fact and that she is smarter than Reed, though Valeria remained unimpressed and replied that she is also smarter than her dad.[40]
  • Doctor Doom considers Valeria to be the only person that has his favor, love and respect all at once.[8] He placed her under his royal protection.[12] He respects her opinions and cares for her happiness.[41][42][43] He lets her sleep over at Latveria.[13] He takes her out for ice cream when she asks.[14] Valeria has convinced Reed to help Doom,[44][45] and Doctor Doom is willing to help out Reed for Valeria's sake (and to prove his superiority over Reed).[46] Compared to Reed, Valeria is far more open to Doom's views and she tends to visit him, even in secret.[11][41][47]
  • Due to Doctor Doom's outside influence, Valeria's first word was "Doom."[48]
