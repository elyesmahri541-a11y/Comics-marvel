History

Zmora is one of the twin siblings known as the Night Terrors, born from the union between the Fear Lord Nightmare and Nemesis, the first nightmare ever dreamt. At some point, Zmora and her twin Morus were chained and hidden away by their father.[1]

Zmora and Morus were eventually released by Nightmare during his battle against Wiccan, Somnus, and the Sorcerer Supreme Scarlet Witch within Billy Kaplan's dreamscape. After the Scarlet Witch destroyed their cauldron, the Night Terrors subjected Wanda, Wiccan, and Somnus to a "poison peace," forcing them to relive their worst moments and turning their lives into despair and accusation. However, after Somnus temporarily awakened the people of the waking world, Nightmare became linked to humanity's collective consciousness, weakening him and giving the Night Terrors an opportunity to devour their father. In response, the Scarlet Witch and Wiccan cast a spell that manipulated humanity's ancient belief that salt repels evil and nightmares, using it against Nightmare and the Night Terrors, overwhelming them and knocking Nightmare unconscious. The Scarlet Witch then allowed the Night Terrors to retreat with their father rather than kill him.[1]

Attributes

Powers

Despite being the children of Nightmare, the Night Terrors regard their father as merely a lord, claiming their own power and influence vastly surpasses his due to being born from the Nightmare Primordial Nemesis. In their sphere of influence in a dream, they are able to counter the spells of the Scarlet Witch.[1]
