History

The Young Allies were at the scene of a USO show to entertain the troops. As the Allies were on the stage, General Manse and famous radio comedian Bob Carter watched the Allies show unaware that a strange old man was watching them as well. Suddenly, Carter got stricken with a sudden jealous rage and attacked General Manse, beating the superior officer to death. As the soldiers pulled Carter off the General, the Young Allies spotted the old man fleeing the scene and attempted to stop him. When questioning the old man, who rambled incomprehensible gibberish, Bucky shrugged him off as just a crank who was uninvolved with the sudden attack.[1]

However later, Carter's madness subsided and he was horrified by what he had done and explained how he had just suddenly lost control of his sanity. The Young Allies and Carter were visited by psychiatrist Wilton Wilkes who examined Carter and diagnosed him with a mental illness, however Kunckles was not sold on Wilke's professional assessment. Later in their bunkhouse, the Allies went over the day's strange events and recalled how Carter said something "hit him" and Bucky expressed his reevaluation of the old man's presence there.[1]
