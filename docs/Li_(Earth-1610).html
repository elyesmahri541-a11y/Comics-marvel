History

Li was the armed operative and enforcer of the Mandarin International, serving the two Caretakers, Taki and June. He participated in their efforts to regain control of Stark Industries, helping hack several of Tony Stark's inventions, including his Iron Man armor. After their attempt to subdue Stark failed, Li received orders from the organization's secret leader to kill Taki and June, then take his own life. He carried out the instructions, killing both women before committing suicide.[1]
