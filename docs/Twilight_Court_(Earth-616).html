History

Origin[]

The Twilight Court were created by the cryptic mastermind Myrddin to pursue the Missing Moment, a temporal anomaly that could not be reached through conventional time travel. Since only heroes could endure the Tribulation Events, Myrddin engineered his own paragons, forging them body and soul to embody archetypal roles. Drawing on the myths of King Arthur and the Knights of the Round Table, he shaped his champions as twisted reflections of the legends: Parsifal the Icon mirrored Sir Percival, Lancelot the Star reflected Sir Lancelot, Mordred the Witch stood in for Mordred, Bedivere the Engineer echoed Sir Bedivere, Bercilak the Construct recalled the Green Knight, and Galehaut the God paralleled Sir Galehaut.[1]

Confrontation with Kang[]

The Court's first mission came when Myrddin attacked Kang the Conqueror aboard his flagship, the Prospero. The knights struck in coordinated waves: Galehaut and Lancelot tried to restrain him, while Bedivere and Bercilak damaged his armor. Kang attempted to convince Mordred that Myrddin was manipulating them, but she delivered him to the Court for judgment. After Kang defeated Parsifal in single combat, Myrddin appeared and impaled Kang, forcing him to retreat into the Null-Time Zone.[1]

Impossible City[]

Later, the Twilight Court entered the Impossible City with Myrddin and engaged the Avengers. Parsifal dueled Captain America, Lancelot fought Thor, and Bedivere crossed blades with Iron Man. Myrddin eventually called them back, describing the fight as a trial.[2]

Soon afterward, Myrddin dispatched them to capture Kang from Avengers custody. The Court struck while most of the Avengers were trapped in slumber by Nightmare, though Scarlet Witch and the Vision resisted until their teammates awoke and repelled the Court.[3] They continued to serve as Myrddin’s enforcers within the City, battling heroes such as Thor and Black Panther, and later witnessed Myrddin erase Kang’s memories after reviving him from his coma.[4] During the Avengers' reclamation of the Impossible City, the Twilight Court fought again, with Lancelot dueling Captain America and Bedivere clashing against Iron Man. Despite their efforts, they were pulled back when Myrddin allowed the heroes a temporary victory.[5]

After Myrddin successfully destroyed the Grandmaster's Speculatorium, and unlocked the Missing Moment, he had no more use for the Twilight Court, and gave them to the Grandmaster as payment for the Spectulatorium's destruction.[5] The Grandmaster soon banished the Twilight Court to Earth-20208. Resolved to no longer serve Myrddin, and to instead become the heroes they were created to be, the Court sought a way to escape the dying reality. Happening across the ruins of Damocles Base, Bercilak and Bedivere reactivated the ship's A.I. and repaired its systems to escape, with Bedivere dubbing the A.I. Camelot, who served as their base of operations. Successfully escaping Earth-20208, the Court fought many battles for eight years, including imprisoning the Ashen Combine. However, during the battle against the Ambassador, the group sacrificed their lives to allow the escape of many others.[6]

Notes

  • The Twilight Court were patterned after the legends of King Arthur and the Knights of the Round Table, with each knight designed as a corrupted reflection of Arthurian legend.[1] Additionally, its members mirror the Avengers in terms of heroic iconicity.
  • Myrddin – creator and master of the Twilight Court, styled after Merlin.[1]
  • Artur the King – counterpart of King Arthur.[1]
  • Parsifal the Icon – counterpart of Sir Percival. Served as the Court's champion and judged Kang in combat.[1]
  • Lancelot the Star – counterpart of Sir Lancelot.[1]
  • Mordred the Witch – counterpart of Mordred.[1]
  • Bedivere the Engineer – counterpart of Sir Bedivere.[1]
  • Bercilak the Construct – counterpart of the Green Knight (Sir Bercilak de Hautdesert).[1]
  • Galehaut the God – counterpart of Sir Galehaut.[1]
