History

Officer Murphy was a security guard at Empire State University.

During the Inferno crisis, Murphy became possessed and lured Martha Connors and her son, Billy Connors, to the university library. Once they arrived, he unleashed a group of Demons against them. However, the Lizard and Spider-Man intervened, preventing the attack from claiming their lives.[1]
