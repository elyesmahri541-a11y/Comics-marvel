History

The Geheime Staatspolizei was the official secret police of Nazi Germany and its occupied territories.

Some of their plans included the invasion of Poland, which was thanks to the Destroyer not accomplished,[1] the attempt to defeat the Destroyer,[2] or the assassination attempt at government official Reginald Whitney.[3]
