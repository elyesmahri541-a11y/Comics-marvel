History

Zeke was a mercenary and an acquaintance of Deadpool. After not getting paid for his last two missions, Deadpool begrudgingly turned to Zeke for a job.[1] Zeke's wife had gone to Dr. Druek Lovosno, a plastic surgeon from Eastern Europe whose procedure required his patients to consume flesh to retain their vitality, otherwise they would become zombified. The procedure was incredibly expensive, and Zeke was unable to pay him. Because of that, his wife agreed with Dr. Lovosno to stay with him. When Deadpool came to Zeke, he proposed Dr. Lovosno a trade; to recover his wife in exchange of luring Deadpool to his castle, where Lovosno could use him as food source for his patients, since Deadpool's healing factor allowed him to regenerate his body.

Zeke's plan initially worked, until Dr. Lovosno discovered Deadpool's body was unsuitable as a source of sustenance. Deadpool offered Dr. Lovosno to lure Zeke and his men to his castle and use them for food. Claiming to have succeeded in retrieving his wife, Deadpool requested a rendezvous with Zeke at Dr. Lovosno's castle, much to his surprise. Expecting a confrontation with Deadpool since he didn't have cash to pay him the promised 1 million dollars for the job, Zeke brought his entire team of mercenaries to Lovosno's castle. They were quickly ambushed by the surgeon and his zombified patients. As Zeke's men were being devoured, he pleaded to his wife for his life. She ignored him as Dr. Lovosno told him he'd be saved for dessert. Zeke was gagged and Deadpool rebuked him for his betrayal before leaving. Deadpool additionally set off an explosive that destroyed Dr. Lovosno's castle, killing the zombies as well. Zeke was either devoured by the zombies before the destruction of the castle, or perished in the explosion.[2]
