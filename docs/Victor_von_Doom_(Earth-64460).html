History

Victor von Doom is the ruler of the nation of Latveria that equipped himself with a high-powered tech armor of his own design, occult skills and his genius intellect. As Doctor Doom, he developed a rivalry with Reed Richards and the Fantastic Four.

When the Annihilation Wave began sweeping across the cosmos, Doom became convinced that he could defeat Annihilus and save the galaxy himself, so he built the Cosmic Inductor, a powerful but unstable weapon that would gift Doom the powers of the stars.[1]
