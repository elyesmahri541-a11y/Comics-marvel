History

After the death of her grandfather Dietrich Trask at the hands of Logan and Nick Fury, the Trask family fled to South America. Her parents continue working on the Sentinel program as a means to keep people safe.[3] She continued working on the Sentinel program for the Consortium. When the X-Men and Nick Fury arrived at the factory, she managed to persuade them that she meant no harm. However, when the X-Men left to deal with more Sentinels, Zigfried pulled a gun on Nick Fury.[4]

Zigfried explained that she still blamed Nick for her grandfather's death and shot him in the leg. Daisy Dugan arrived and Zigfried evaded capture through the jungle. There, she met her New Sentinels[3] and took them with her when she rejoined her mother before seemingly being killed by a nuclear explosion in space.[5]

She was found alive and later placed as interim director of S.H.I.E.L.D..[6]
