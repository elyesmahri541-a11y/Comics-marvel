History

Kang the Conqueror foresaw the death of the Multiverse due to his fellow Kangs' temporal meddling, and so sparked the Multiversal War against them in an attempt to preserve the Multiverse, conquering some realities whilst destroying many others. In the process, he killed many alternate Avengers, including a version of Thor Odinson.[2] The war eventually allowed He Who Remains to discover and weaponize Alioth to defeat his alternate selves and put an end to the Multiversal War.[3]

With the connection of the Sacred Timeline re-established following the destruction of the Temporal Loom,[1] the Council of Kangs decided to exile Kang into the Quantum Realm with no means of escape. On his arrival, he met a fellow castaway Janet Van Dyne and convinced her to find their way home together. After many years of trial and error, they were able to repair the Multiversal Engine Core that powered Kang's Time Sphere, but upon seeing Kang's past through a telepathic feedback, Janet became terrified of him and used Pym Discs to enlarge the core to the dangerous proportions before fleeing. She joined the Freedom Fighters who opposed Kang, but with his recharged Kang Armor he was able to conquer the portion of the Quantum Realm and build an empire there. The only thing he lacked was the means by which he could escape and exact revenge on the Kangs who exiled him. He also found the disfigured Darren Cross and turned him into M.O.D.O.K..[2]

Janet was eventually rescued by her family but kept quiet about her experience in the Quantum Realm for the fear of Kang. This backfired in a disaster when Cassie Lang attempted to map the Quantum Realm, following Hank Pym's teachings. Her attempts were intercepted by M.O.D.O.K. and Kang, who opened a tunnel that sucked Hank, Janet, their daughter Hope, Cassie and her father Scott into the Quantum Realm. Kang's agents located and captured Scott and Cassie and Kang forced Scott to go into the Core and restore it to normality by threatening Cassie's life. Lang completed his end of the bargain but Kang reneged on his and captured Janet in addition to Cassie. The rest of the family attacked his stronghold alongside rebellious locals and Kang was forced to delay the launch of his invasion force to deal with them. After the ordinary defences fell, Kang entered the battlefield himself and killed many of the rebels before his defences were broken by the turncoat M.O.D.O.K. and he was overwhelmed by a swarm of giant ants. His base was destroyed and the humans were able to escape, but Kang caught Lang who was last and pummelled him with his bare hands, his armour being destroyed. Scott was only able to survive by pushing Kang into the now unstable Engine Core which sucked him in, seemingly killing him.[2]

Attributes

Abilities

  • Genius Intelligence
    • Hand-to-Hand Combat: Kang is an immensely skilled combatant, capable of completely overwhelming Scott Lang despite his armor being inoperable.
    • Mechanical Engineering: Kang designed the advanced technology of his Quantum Realm empire, which Janet Van Dyne notes as being centuries more advanced than anything Earth can dream of.

Paraphernalia

Equipment

  • Kang Armor
    • Superhuman Durability: Kang's armor allows him to shrug off stinger blasts from Wasp and punches from Ant-Man's giant form.
    • Energy Blasts: Using his armor, Kang can shoot beams of blue energy from his hands, powerful enough to instantly disintegrate the Freedom Fighters into puffs of blue smoke, leaving nothing behind.
    • Telekinesis: Kang's suit allows him to telekinetically move, freeze and strangle people with a simple gesture of his finger. He can also stop objects mid-air.
    • Force Field: Kang can generate a blue forcefield around himself to protect against attacks that his suit isn't strong enough to. Even a swarm of giant technologically advanced ants was incapable of dealing significant damage to the forcefield.
    • Portal Creation: Kang created a blue portal to transport himself, Cassie Lang and Scott from his citadel to his enlarged Multiversal Engine Core. He then created another one to bring Janet back to his citadel after freezing her in place, along with the restored Multiversal Engine Core.
