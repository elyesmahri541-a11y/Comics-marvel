History

Graves Mansion was an abandoned house used as a hideout by a group of Nazi spies posing as war bond salesmen. The criminals brought their proceeds there to finance their espionage activities. After discovering their true purpose, the Secret Stamp attacked them but was overpowered and captured. They tied him to a post beside a pit, intending to kill him by dissolving the bonds restraining his hands with acid and allowing him to fall into the pit. Roddy Colt managed to free himself before their plan could succeed.[1]
