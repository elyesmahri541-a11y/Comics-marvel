History

Origin[]

Born a titanic Eternal with the divergent gene, he became obsessed with the entity of Death and, seeking to impress it, wiped out millions of his race, including his own family.[1]

He amassed several armies, including the Chitauri,[2] and began his genocidal population control by going from planet to planet and slaughtering billions. Along the way, he found several young children on the worlds he razed that he took in as his own, creating the Black Order.[1]

Avengers Battles[]

In 2012, Thanos located the Space Stone, also called the Tesseract, on Earth, and so made a deal with Loki Laufeyson, for him to acquire the Tesseract for Thanos in exchange for a Chitauri army to conquer Earth with. Loki got the Tesseract and used it to open a wormhole for the Chitauri to come through and attack New York City, but unfortunately they were beaten back by Earth's mightiest defenders, a new group of superheroes known as the Avengers, leading Thanos to harbor a grudge against them.

Later, after the defeat of Ultron in 2015, he picked up the Infinity Gauntlet, frustrated and saying he would do it himself.[2]

The Build Stone[]

Beginning his quest for the Infinity Stones, Thanos partners with Ronan to find the Build Stone to use it to destroy half the universe.[3] After Ronan got the stone, Thanos goes to Earth to begin his destruction there, using the stone to build the giant BLT. The Guardians of the Galaxy would soon thwart his plan, taking the stone from him and sending him back to Sanctuary.[4]

Looking for Thane[]

After collecting all six Infinity Stones, Thanos stormed Attilan, hoping to learn the whereabouts of his son Thane from Black Bolt. He and the Black Order passed through several locations in Attilan until they reached the throne, where the Black Bolt was, but all of them would be defeated by the Black Bolt using his sonic scream.[1]

In some unknown way, the Loki got the Infinity Stones from Thanos.[5]

Trouble in Wakanda[]

Thanos would come to Earth with the aim of destroying it, but he would be easily defeated by the Avengers and Black Panther. After his defeat, Killmonger and Ulysses Klaue convince him that the Vibranium in Wakanda would give him more power, which makes him ally with them.[6] The three invade and dominate Wakanda, starting to steal Vibranium from the mines there.[7] With a large amount of power given by Vibranium, Thanos fought against Black Panther and the Avengers and was almost victorious, if T'Challa did not use the Vibranium mines against Thanos to open a portal that teleported him to the vacuum of space.[8]

Loki Training[]

Thanos would come to Earth again with Nebula and his army to pick up the Infinity Stones that were hidden in the Avengers tower by Loki. After his army took over the S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier and arrested the Avengers, he went to the Avengers Tower to get the stones, but what he didn't expect is that Loki would deceive the Chitauris in order to free the Avengers. The heroes fought Thanos, but he still managed to get the Mechfinity Gauntlet, however, Loki reveals that they were fake, and that he hid the true Infinity Stones elsewhere. Thanos was then defeated and imprisoned.[5]

Time Twisted[]

Thanos would join forces with Red Skull to steal the Ant-Van and travel back in time to bring Avengers' villains from different points in the timeline. First, he sent the Chitauri to recruit Obadiah Stane moments before he was killed in a confrontation with Tony. He later sent Red Skull to get the Tesseract in the 40's during Steven's confrontation with Red Skull himself, but Red Skull was unable to get the Tesseract and instead brought his past self into the present, which Thanos sent back to the past. Thanos goes to 2015 in the exact moment of the Avengers' final battle against Ultron to recruit the latter. After all recruited, Thanos would put his plan into practice: Defeat the Avengers, destroy their tower, destroy New York, dominate Earth and then the Universe. However, Ant-Man was hiding on the Hydra's island and that's why the Avengers knew Thanos' plan, so they all went there to stop him, and in the end the villains were defeated by being shrunk and placed in a bottle.[9]

Strange Tails[]

In unknown ways, Thanos managed to return to its original size and tries to subdue some of the Avengers' enemies at a Christmas meeting, but despite having the Infinity Gauntlet, he did not have the Infinity Stones to do so. Santa Claus would end up invading the meeting and fighting everyone, including Thanos, who thought Santa Claus was incapable of stopping him.[10]

Attributes

Powers

  • Titanian Eternal Physiology: Thanos is a superhuman mutant whose massive, heavy-bodied form was born with the capacity to synthesize cosmic energy for certain personal uses.[1]
    • Superhuman Strength: Thanos has super-strength as a big-fig that allows him to tear large rock masses out of the ground and pull on objects using special green handles.[11]
    • Energy Manipulation: Thanos can release a type of purple energy from his hands, energy that with one touch Thanos can disintegrate anyone.[11]
    • Superhuman Durability: Thanks to his extraordinary durability, Thanos is immune to radioactive goo and fire.[11] He is also immune to bullets[2] and physical attacks.[3]
    • Telekinesis: Thanos possesses strong psionic abilities.[2]
    • Energy Laser: Thanos can unleash a laser from his hands that has the capabilities of melting things or powering up machines.[2] This laser has varied colors, such as red[3] and yellow.[2]
    • Super Jump: Thanks to his immense strength, Thanos can leap to great heights.[2]
  • Vibranium Improvement: While energized with Vibranium, Thanos became much more powerful, having his powers increased to the point of being able to destroy the Hulkbuster.[8]
  • Superhuman Leaping

Abilities

  • Skilled Unarmed Combatant: Although he typically avoids physical combat, Thanos is a highly formidable hand to hand combatant.[11]
