History

Spider-Man attempted to stop a violent anti-mutant protest outside of Avengers Mansion. Realising he couldn't handle the situation, Spider-Man tried to flee but the crowd stopped him and violently beat him to death while he chose not to fight back.[1]

According to Wolverine's account, it took an army of Sentinels to bring Spider-Man down, and that he died in his arms.[2]
