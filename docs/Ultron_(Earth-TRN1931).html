History

Ultron was created by Tony Stark to create peace, though he went rogue. Ultron recruited the Maximoff twins Wanda and Pietro to assist him in exterminating the Avengers. The twins eventually saw through Ultron's plan and defected, with Pietro dying of unknown causes. Near the end of his crusade against the Avengers, he uploaded himself into the Avengers Tower mainframe. The Avengers, using the tower's neural interface, were able to pursue and defeat him once and for all.[1][2]
