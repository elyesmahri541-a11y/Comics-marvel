History

Peter Parker was the mutate hero known as Spider-Man. He used to be known as the "Menace" of New York, and to battle super villains such as Electro or the Sandman.[1]

When the Human Coalition discovered that powers could be inherited by children from their parents after encountering Nick Fury's Secret Warriors — as the Secret Warriors were super-powered kids born from super villains or super heroes — they decided to also target the post-muties, and to proceed termination and sterilization on them.[1]

With his wife, Mary Jane, pregnant with his child, he decided to make her leave, knowing he wouldn't be able to go through the modified DNA scanners.[1]

Mary Jane was able to get to the airport, fleeing to France with their child, but Peter was captured by the Exonims.[1]
