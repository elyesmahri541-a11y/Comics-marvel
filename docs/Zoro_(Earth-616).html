History

Zoro and his sister Sulani were the lion cubs of Zar and Sha. Shortly after their birth, Zoro was kidnapped by the ape named Bardak who was seeking a mirror taken by the jungle adventurer (and their adopted uncle) Ka-Zar (David Rand). Ka-Zar tracked down Bardak and slew him for the kidnapping, and returned the cub to their mother. Sha came to accept Ka-Zar as part of their family following this rescue.[2]

Zoro and Sulani's subsequent activities are unrevealed.
