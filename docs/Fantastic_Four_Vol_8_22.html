Solicit Synopsis

Something has gone wrong in (very) rural Canada, and the dead are rising to devour the living. Luckily, the Fantastic Four are there to help! And while it won’t be easy to stop this problem from spreading — much less fix it — luckily, the FF aren’t alone: WOLVERINE is there too. But why this town, and why now? And what plan of a figure from the Fantastic Four’s past could be responsible for all of it? Blood, gore and scientific adventure collide in this Fantastic Four tale like none other!
