Synopsis for 1st story

Following Doctor Doom's downfall, Thunderbolt Ross has seized control of Latveria. He plans to use a weapon left behind by Doom to impose democracy across the globe by force. Ross rechristens the nation "New America" and delivers a worldwide broadcast before deploying his forces to invade the neighboring nation of Symkaria. Reed Richards meets with U.N. Secretary-General Renata Riani at the Secretariat and offers his assistance, but she insists to keep super heroes out of the conflict.

After visiting a comatose Steve Rogers, still recovering from his recent clash with Ross, Bucky Barnes and Sam Wilson convene with the Avengers, the Fantastic Four, Namor and Spider-Man at Stark Tower. There, Tony Stark unveils his new mass-surveillance network, the Ironalert System, which has flagged Ross's operations as unusually effective and suggests the general is relying on predictive technology in his strategies.

As the Red Hulk, Ross and his Hulk Army clash with the Symkarian Armed Forces. Meanwhile, Stark and Bucky travel to New Orleans, where they fail to recruit Rogue and the X-Men. However, Wolverine joins them, promising to introduce them to a powerful ally. At the same time, the United Nations Security Council votes to assemble a military coalition against Ross. Wakandan ambassador Amara Azikiwe relays the development to the Black Panther.

In Virginia, David Colton storms the Pentagon to halt its super-soldier experiments. Captain America, Captain Marvel, the Vision and Wolverine intercept him. After destroying the laboratory, Colton agrees to help.

The Avengers and Fantastic Four finish prepping the Quinjet for departure to New America. Spider-Man hesitates to leave the home front unprotected, so Iron Man allows him to stay behind. Colton joins the team in a new green-and-yellow costume. Mister Fantastic receives a holographic call from Riani, who threatens the heroes with legal action should they intervene in New America. Iron Man ends the call and rallies his teammates with the Avengers' battle cry.
