History

Doctor Strange used his powerful magic to protect New York when Godzilla came out of the ocean. He tried to cast massive shields to stop the destruction, but he was overwhelmed when Knull arrived and sent massive symbiote swarms to Earth, which caused him to get bonded to a symbiote.[1] On Earth-72630. he opened a massive portal to bring Godzilla back to Earth-TRN1854 and into the city of New York.[2]
