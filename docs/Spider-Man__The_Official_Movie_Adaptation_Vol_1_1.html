Notes

  • While this comic is an adaption of Spider-Man, it features a few notable differences which make it incompatible with the film. These include:
    • While in the film Peter finds out Uncle Ben's been shot as he's dying on the street, the comic shows him finding out only after Ben's already died and is being loaded on to an ambulance.
    • The Quest Aerospace demonstration that the Green Goblin attacks takes place during the day instead of at night.
    • The Green Goblin's attack on the Daily Bugle happens at night instead of the day. This leads to there being a shorter amount a time in which Spider-Man is knocked out by the Goblin and awakens on a rooftop where the Goblin attempts to create a partnership between the two.
    • In the comic, Spider-Man and MJs kiss upside down, Green Goblin's attack in the burning building, and the Thanksgiving dinner all happen all within one night unlike the movie in which these events occur on at least two separate days.
    • Unlike in the movie, Peter's friends and family never sit down for Thanksgiving dinner as May notices Peter's scratch before dinner's served, leading Norman to immediately storm out.
