History

Officer Murphy was one of two NYPD officers assigned to transport Wrecker in a paddy wagon following his defeat by the Fantastic Four. Believing the Wrecker powerless without his enchanted crowbar, the officers restrained him only with special steel handcuffs. Once conscious, the Wrecker easily snapped his restraints and incapacitated both Murphy and his partner within moments before escaping custody.[1]

Officer Murphy was assigned again to guard members of the Wrecking Crew following their arrest. During the detention, Misty Knight specifically instructed him to keep a close watch on the prisoners. Murphy responded with confidence, assuring her that he would remain vigilant while overseeing the custody of the superhuman offenders.[2]
