Solicit Synopsis

BIGGEST ISSUE IN AMAZING SPIDER-MAN HISTORY!

Spider-Man faces his greatest fear and his most frightening new villain in decades… RAVAGE! Plus, some of the greatest creative minds on Earth honor one thousand issues of THE AMAZING SPIDER-MAN! NOAH HAWLEY (Alien: Earth, Fargo, LEGION) makes his comic book debut, Frank Miller and Peach Momoko collaborate for the first time, Stuart lmmonen and Brian Michael Bendis return to Spider-Man together, and some of the best ASM creators return to the title!

Notes

  • "I Can Never Rest" combines Baxter Bigelow (the security guard who Spider-Man refused to help stop the Burglar) and Officer Bernard O'Brien (the police officer who informed Peter Parker of his Uncle Ben's death) into a new character named Captain Arthur Simeksi.
