History

Mrs. Smith was a famous soap opera actress and the mother of Shia Smith, one of the young geniuses recruited by Janet Van Dyne for the G.I.R.L.. Mrs. Smith had even shared a screen kiss with Simon Williams in one of her films.

After a long period without seeing her mother, Shia reunited with her in Los Angeles, accompanied by her girlfriend, Ying Liu. Although initially reluctant to tell her mother about her homosexuality, Shia eventually found the courage to do so. Mrs. Smith reacted badly, proceeding to humiliate her daughter in public until Ying stepped in and verbally confronted her, forcing her to back down.[1]
