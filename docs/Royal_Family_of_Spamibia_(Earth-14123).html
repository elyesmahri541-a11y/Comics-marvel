History

After Fred clicked on a link in an email from an unknown and suspicious sender, Basemax was infected by a virus, and even though Hiro tried to quickly restart him, the process was corrupted, turning Basemax into the Queen of Spamibia, causing her to speak like a medieval character as she began attacking Big Hero 6 because they refused to kneel before her.[1]
