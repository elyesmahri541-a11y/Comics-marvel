History

Phin Mason convinced Miles Morales to attend a concert with her and Dazzler performing, after which he became a fan of hers.[1]

She was featured on a poster with Dazzler in Lowtown.[2]

After the Helix Sentinels brought destruction across the globe, mutant hate grew worldwide, causing Dazzler and Lila to cancel their reunion tour.[2]

Trivia

  • Among her songs included "Cannonball My Heart" and "Don't Want a Strong Guy."[2]
