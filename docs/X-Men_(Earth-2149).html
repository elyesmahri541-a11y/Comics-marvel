History

Little is detailed about X-Men’s history before the zombie plague arrived on Earth-2149, however it can be presumed that it followed much the same path as their Earth-616 counterparts.

After being attacked by an infected Alpha Flight early in the Zombie Contagion crisis, the surviving members joined forces with Magneto and joined the S.H.I.E.L.D. Resistance. Like many other superhero groups in this world, the organization quickly collapsed as all its members became infected, and later killed (Wolverine being the last remaining member).[1]
