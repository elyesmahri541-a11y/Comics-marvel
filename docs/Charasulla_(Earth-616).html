History

The New Mutants discovered a furry green alien called Charasūlla and fought with him. Despite their best efforts, the New Mutants struggled to overcome the alien until Lila began playing a riff on her guitar that caused his weapons and armor to explode and rendered him helpless before disappearing in a burst of light. Lila Cheney was soon contacted by the alien Charasūlla who appeared to have made a deal with her related to enslaving Earth's inhabitants. He sought a private arrangement with her but she insisted they followed the deal as prescribed but was soon betrayed by one of her band mates who lowered the defenses of their location and contacted the alien himself to greenlight an invasion of the sphere. The invasion by Charasūlla and his fellow Vrakanín commenced just as the other New Mutants arrived to the sphere.[1]
