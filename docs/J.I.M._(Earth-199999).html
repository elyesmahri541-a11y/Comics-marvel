History

J.I.M., also known as the Synthetic Man was an android created by Phineas Horton. In 1943, he was exhibited at the World Exposition of Tomorrow, where he was displayed inside a glass enclosure beneath a sign identifying him as Horton's creation.[2]

Legacy[]

Decades later, a painting seemingly depicting the Synthetic Man was displayed within Vision's Mindspace. Vision referred to the figure as the first of their kind, describing him as a cautionary tale about the dangers of allowing emotions to take control.[3]

Notes

  • In his original appearance, Horton described the Human Torch as a "synthetic man", although the phrase was used as a description of his creation rather than as a name.[4]
