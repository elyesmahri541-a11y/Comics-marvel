Professional History

Al Ewing is currently the writer of Thor (Vol. 7) and Venom (Vol. 6).

His other works for Marvel include Immortal Hulk, Immortal Thor, Mortal Thor, Venom (Vol. 5), Avengers Inc., Contest of Champions, Defenders (Vol. 6), Empyre, Guardians of the Galaxy (Vol. 7), Loki: Agent of Asgard, Mighty Avengers (Vol. 2), New Avengers (Vol. 4), S.W.O.R.D. (Vol. 2), Ultimates (Vol. 2), U.S.Avengers, and X-Men: Red (Vol. 2).
