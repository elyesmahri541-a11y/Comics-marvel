History

The Future Avengers are a group of young heroes that were raised by Hydra to fight the Avengers. When they discovered that it was actually Hydra that was evil, they betrayed the cause and became heroes.[1]

Trivia

  • The Future Avengers are based on the main characters of the Marvel/MADHOUSE anime production Marvel Future Avengers. The series was added onto the Disney+ streaming service on February 28th, 2020, four months after their comic appearance.
