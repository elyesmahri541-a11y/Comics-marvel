History

In Central Park at that very moment the Eternal Khoryphos and his Deviant lover Yrdisis were spending time seeing the sights. They too were interrupted by what appeared to be the Fantastic Four. However this time it turned out to be the Weird Sisters -- Truth, Beauty, and Charm -- in disguise. They attacked, managing to sting both Eternal and Deviant with a special device. However their attack was interrupted by the arrival of the real Fantastic Four. While they managed to rescue Khoryphos, the Weird Sisters managed to escape with Yrdisis as their prisoner.[1]

At the mansion once owned by Malcolm Stromberg, the Weird Sisters materialized with their prisoner Yrdisis. They took her down into a secret laboratory where Melstrom's minions had Karkas and Ransak as their prisoners. After hooking Yrdisis to a machine that would drain her kinetic energies, they finally had enough power to bring back a ghostly image of their fallen leader.[1]
