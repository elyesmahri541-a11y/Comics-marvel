Synopsis for "The Conqueror Worm"

The Avengers battle the Grim Reaper's Legion of the Unliving and they're not getting anywhere. The Vision slips away from the battle and confronts the Grim Reaper. He reminds the Grim Reaper that he had taken his own life while alive. This disrupts the Grim Reaper's confidence and hold on his underlings. The Legion of the Unliving attack the Grim Reaper, but are interrupted as the Nameless Ones claim him. The Avengers are swept back to their own dimension.
