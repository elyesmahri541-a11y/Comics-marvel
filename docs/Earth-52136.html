History

In this universe, Aunt May was killed by a burglar instead of Uncle Ben.[1]

Earth-52136/Characters

Notes

  • Prior to being given an official reality number, this universe was referred as Earth-TRN034 as a part of our Temporary Reality Numbers classification system.
  • The framing device of What If Aunt May Had Died Instead of Uncle Ben? #1 involves what is presented as a real-life comic book store owner making up this scenario and discussing it with a customer. Due to this, this reality's designation doesn't follow a pattern provided in Official Handbook of the Marvel Universe A to Z #5, where realities of the 2004 What If? stories are mentioned.
