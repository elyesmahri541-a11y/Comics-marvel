Synopsis for "Racing the Night"

Two weeks after the events of Legion Quest, Phoenix searches through the grounds surrounding the Xavier Institute. While doing so she encounters Wolverine. He has been living outside for over a week after piercing Sabretooth’s brain with his claws.[1] Wolverine claims that he’ll continue to live outside as long as Sabretooth, who has recovered albeit with some alteration to his personality, is kept alive and living in the mansion. Jean tries to reason with Logan saying that Sabretooth was starting to make progress, but Logan remains steadfast and tells Jean that Professor X and Cyclops can call on Wolverine’s help if they need it, otherwise he’ll remain outside. He then seems to vanish with Jean not entirely sure he was ever there at all.

Inside the mansion, Professor X speaks to Bishop about nightmares Bishop has been having since his return from the past during Legion Quest. Bishop says that they feel more like memories than dreams. This is because he has memories belonging to his alternate self from Earth-295 after they encountered each other.[2] Professor X suggests that perhaps these aren’t dreams or memories, but rather warnings of things that are yet to come.

Meanwhile in Yonkers, New York, Dennis Hogan disembarks from a train. He’s been travelling by various means of transport to Westchester County as he’s heard of a place there where people like himself, mutants, can receive help. Dennis struggles to contain a transformation that will reveal his mutant status to people. He enters the Den-Joe bar hoping to get a meal since he hasn’t eaten in two days.

At the CNBC News studio in Manhattan, Trish Tilby is getting ready to deliver a report about the existence of the Legacy Virus and the fact that it’s now a danger to mutants and humans alike. Her conscience is troubling her over this with it feeling like a betrayal of her friendship with Beast. A man, identified only as Clay, asks Trish if she’s sure she wants to go ahead with the report as it could lead to more hatred towards mutants.

At the Belle Fourche River Dam, which overshadows a Sentinels processing plant, X-Factor have managed to track down Mystique after looking for her for the last ten days. Whilst Forge deactivates a bomb planted by Mystique, in order to destroy the dam and flood the processing plant, Havok and Polaris evacuate the workers. Valerie Cooper has managed to track Mystique to a cavern near the dam and, after pretending not to hear Forge’s radio communication urging her to stay in place and wait for the rest of the team, prepares to enter the cavern. Inside the cavern Mystique is attempting to escape when she is attacked by a shadowy figure. Valerie finds Mystique in the aftermath, but the attacker has gone.

Back at the Xavier Institute food is being prepared by Cyclops and Beast for Storm as she keeps a vigil with a comatose Gambit. As they head down the corridor to deliver it they are met by Bishop. With memories of their Earth-295 counterparts clouding his mind Bishop attacks Cyclops and Beast. They manage to subdue him and he regains his senses, but is still worried about what is happening to him.

At the bar in Yonkers, Dennis has finished his meal, but still struggles to stop his transformation. He hopes to find a cure once he finds the X-Men. On a television in the bar Trish starts her report.

In a drainage pipe below the George Washington Bridge a homeless man, Jim Davis, meets his end at the hands of a mutant named Marrow. Originally one of the Morlocks who lived in the sewers, she has returned to Earth, after what for her has been twenty years, from another dimension she and other Morlocks were taken to by Mikhail Rasputin. The woman, formerly known as Sarah, is performing a ritual she calls “the Ceremony of Light” which involves bouncing the light of a single candle off multiple reflective surfaces. Marrow hopes this will act as a call to “the first among us”. She views this person as the saviour of the Morlocks.

Back at the mansion the trio of Cyclops, Beast and Bishop head down the corridor to deliver the meal for Storm. Bishop convinces Cyclops and Beast that they need to run more tests to find out what’s wrong with him, even though he’s been through many tests for two weeks. After briefly checking on Storm and delivering the food they take their leave. Storm sits by the bedside of Gambit and laments his kiss in Israel with Rogue which has left him in this condition.[3] Storm also wonders what secrets Rogue discovered in Gambit’s mind through their contact which were enough to make her run off from the team.

In Key Largo, Florida the errant Rogue, along with Iceman is dancing the night away to try and forget her troubles. After Bobby wards off the advances of a man approaching Rogue, risking skin-to-skin contact, the news report by Trish Tilby is still in progress. Trish informs the viewing audience that the Legacy Virus, verified by the Center for Disease Control, was unleashed by Stryfe months ago and was thought to only affect mutants.

Trish’s report is also being viewed at Xavier's School for Gifted Youngsters in Massachusetts. Trish continues that the virus, which spreads through the air, is now spreading to humans. As Synch, Husk and Jubilee watch the report with worry Jubilee calls out to Banshee and Emma Frost to come and watch the news.

Senator Robert Kelly, viewing the report in his office in Washington, D.C., upon hearing that Multiple Man of X-Factor has been killed by the Legacy Virus picks up the telephone and demands to be put in contact with Forge and Valerie Cooper.

At the Mutant Research Center on Muir Island, Scotland, Trish’s report is also being watched by Dr. Moira MacTaggert and Dr. Rory Campbell. Moira is identified in the report as the first human to be infected with the Legacy Virus. This leaves Moira unsure what to do next.

At the Den-Joe bar, Dennis leaves abruptly after hearing some customers talking about hunting and eradicating mutants. Unfortunately his departure does not go unnoticed.

At the Xavier Institute many of the X-Men are viewing Trish’s report as it concludes. The team tries to comfort Henry reassuring him that if Trish hadn’t told the public this news someone else would have. This does nothing to placate Beast and he leaves the room feeling betrayed by Trish. The team then discusses damage control since they know what reaction the news will get from humans. Charles instructs Cyclops to contact Cable. At that moment both Xavier and Psylocke are overcome with a psionic blast. Xavier feels certain Jean, despite not being amongst them, would have felt it too. Xavier tells the team what was experienced was a psionic presence falling to Earth through the atmosphere.

The “psionic presence” in question has landed in Switzerland. It is viewed on a computer screen by a being called Blaquesmith who logs coordinates of the temporal rift. The being who landed turns out to be Nate Grey of Earth-295.

In England at the Edgerton Estate a knock at the door is answered by the manservant, Niles, who finds Emplate on the doorstep. Emplate demands to see Lady Gayle Edgerton and, after threatening Niles, gets his chance to see her. Gayle heats a poker in the fire she’s been warming herself in front of. She then attacks Emplate with it jamming it into his chest. This has no effect on Emplate who wishes to discuss a mutual friend, the mutant, known as Chamber.

Three miles from the Den-Joe bar, Dennis is running alongside some railroad tracks. He trips and cries out that he just wants to live his life. The group from the bar have tracked him down and threaten him. This causes Dennis to panic and lose control which in turn causes him to fully transform into a reptilian looking version of himself. He attempts to escape lunging at one of the group which they perceive as an attack. One of the group hits him with a baseball bat. Dennis is able to flee, but the group gives chase.

Back at the mansion, Storm is bringing Charles tea while he recovers. Suddenly Charles senses Dennis’ distress and the situation he’s in. He gets Storm to carry him due North and sends out psionic commands to Logan asking for his help.

In the place known as Murderworld the team of X-Force are engaged in a training session. One which isn’t impressing their leader, Cable. Whilst Domino is observing from the control room an image of Arcade appears to her and warns her the place will explode in 60 seconds. She manages to alert the rest of the team and they make their escape before Murderworld explodes, ten seconds earlier than Arcade had said. They mention only just beginning to trust Sunspot again after his time as Reignfire. Once outside they’re greeted by Cyclops who tells them the Professor would like to talk to them. With no headquarters left Cable agrees to return with Cyclops.

Back in Wyoming, Polaris tells Forge that she and Havok will try to find who attacked Mystique. Forge suggests their priority should be securing Mystique and then finding the attacker. Forge finds Valerie Cooper who tells him that Mystique fought back against her and escaped. She then asks Forge to help her up. Since Cooper would never ask for help Forge sees through the deception and kicks “Cooper” rendering her unconscious and causes her to revert to her true form of Mystique. The real Cooper emerges from the cavern and suggests that she and Forge have plans for Mystique. They suspect that the mystery attacker was out to send a message rather than kill Mystique. As X-Factor prepares to leave the area with Mystique contained Havok seems overwhelmed by some kind of force. Suddenly a large explosion destroys the dam unleashing a torrent of water. Cooper questions Forge about whether he had disarmed the bomb. He thinks another must have been planted and worries about what happened to Havok and Polaris when the dam was destroyed. His answer comes from Polaris as she uses her magnetic powers to carry her and Havok into the air towards the X-Factor Plane. Polaris then tells Forge and Cooper that a bomb didn’t destroy the dam. It was Havok.

In Hammer Bay, Genosha, Shadowcat and Douglock of Excalibur and Peter Wisdom of Black Air are helping the first Mutate, Mr. Edgerton, to go through the notes of Doctor Moreau to see what the weapon is that’s being used against Mutates in a war taking place in Genosha. Unbeknownst to them they are being observed from somewhere else in Genosha by Sugar Man who arrived on Earth-616 from the Earth-295 timeline twenty years ago. To stop them getting too close to discovering his involvement in Genosha, Sugar Man vaporises Mr. Edgerton along with Moreau’s notes.

Out in deep space, Colossus is leading a spacewalk involving his fellow Acolytes Francisco Milan, Rusty Collins and Joanna Cargill. They transmit everything they can see to Exodus, onboard Avalon, who has been leader of the Acolytes since Magneto’s mind was wiped by Charles Xavier in a battle with the X-Men.[4] Exodus believes that what the spacewalk team have found in space is a portent of things to come. What they’ve found is a chunk of ice. Colossus believes it could pose a threat if it collides with Avalon. He instructs Rusty to melt the ice, but before Rusty can a flare of heat starts within the ice. The team realises there’s a living being inside the ice.

In the tunnels a dejected Marrow has been repeating the words of the Ceremony of Light numerous times with no results. Suddenly a furry hand extinguishes the candle claiming the light hurts their eyes. Henry McCoy, the Beast of Earth-295, greets Marrow as a descendant of his work, the original Morlocks, that he began when he arrived on Earth-616 twenty years ago and is pleased to learn that there are others like her waiting to follow his orders.

In the air above the railroad in Yonkers, Storm keeps Charles aloft while he tries to detect Dennis who is being savagely beaten. By reaching out with his mind Charles makes psionic contact with Dennis and shares his pain from the attack. Every moment of the attack is felt by Charles but with his powers not at their prime he is powerless to stop it. Eventually the group ends their attack and leaves in their vehicle seemingly proud of having killed a mutant.

Some time later Wolverine, Archangel Bishop, Jean, Storm and Psylocke find Dennis. Jean is able to sense Xavier’s astral presence is there too. Xavier’s astral self rejoins his physical body and he cradles the young mutant in his arms as Dennis is briefly pleased to see he found the X-Men. He then dies. Charles tells the gathered X-Men that they have to continue to fight for a better world because they can’t allow situations like this, humans killing mutants simply because they look different, to be their future.

Solicit Synopsis

The Age of Apocalypse has ended...but what new threats await the X-Men, X-Factor, X-Force and all mutants? Plus, what Age of Apocalypse heroes and villains have crossed over to our reality?
