History

The Doomjet is a jet owned by Doctor Doom. He and Bettle and rode it to attack the Daily Bugle building. Spider-Man destroyed it, with its remains falling to the ground.[1]

Doctor Doom intended to use the Doomjet to capture the Silver Surfer, but thanks to Iron Man's interference, Doom accidentally fired one of the craft's missiles, destroying the Surfer's board.[2]
