History

J. Jonah Duck is the editor-in-chief of the The Chronicle. He didn't always agree with the owner of the newspaper, Scrooge McDuck, such as when he decided to designate Minnie Mouse as the one who should found out who the heroine Captain Marvel was.[1]

Notes

  • Despite having the surname Duck, J. Jonah Duck is actually a chicken based on his design.
