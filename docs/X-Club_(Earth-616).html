History

Origin[]

In response to the decimation of the mutant population on M-Day, Beast and Angel assembled a team of scientific specialists to restore the X-Gene. This team, known as the X-Club, included Dr. Nemesis.[3] Madison Jeffries.[4] and Dr. Yuriko Takiguchi.[5] After resolving a misunderstanding with Takiguchi, who initially mistook them for Russian operatives, the group recruited Dr. Kavita Rao, a geneticist with experience in mutant biology.[6]

Nemesis proposed that studying pre-mutant DNA could unlock the key to reactivating the X-Gene, using his own parents as a test case.[7]

Time Travel and the Study of Mutation[]

To locate Cable and the Mutant Messiah in the distant future, the X-Club developed an experimental time-travel device. Due to its instability, biological acceleration forced time travelers to return within a strict thirty-three-and-a-half-hour window.[8]

Expanding upon this technology, Jeffries and Dr. Kavita Rao modified the device to journey into the past to study the origins of mutation. They traveled to 1906 San Francisco, seeking blood samples from Nemesis’ parents to analyze early genetic markers of mutation.[7]

Utopia and Scientific Challenges[]

With the mutant population relocating to Utopia, the X-Club turned its focus to ensuring the island’s stability. Alongside Magneto, they developed massive support structures to prevent Utopia from sinking while simultaneously creating a livable space for the Atlanteans.[9]

During this time, they uncovered invasive nanotechnology infiltrating Utopia and investigated Predator-X creatures that had breached the island’s defenses. The X-Club also assisted Magneto in retrieving the bullet that contained Kitty Pryde.

The team suffered a loss when Dr. Takiguchi passed away from natural causes.[9] Soon after, Beast, frustrated with Cyclops’ leadership, departed from both the X-Club and Utopia.[1]

Bastion’s Assault and the Siege of Utopia[]

The X-Club played a critical role in resisting Bastion’s assault on mutantkind. As Hope and Cable returned to the present, Bastion launched an all-out attack, cutting off Utopia and San Francisco within a massive red energy sphere. The X-Club, alongside the Avengers, attempted to breach the barrier, but even Thor’s power was unable to penetrate it.[10]

Confrontation with the Evolutionaries[]

When the X-Men faced the Evolutionaries once more, the X-Club was tasked with reconstructing a machine initially designed by Beast during their first encounter. To accomplish this, Psylocke extracted Cyclops’ memories and transferred them to Jeffries, allowing him to rebuild the device.[11]

Before the project was completed, Dragoness and Litterbug attempted to sabotage their efforts, but intervention from Match, Rockslide, and Anole secured the machine. Upon activation, it triggered a conflict with the Evolutionaries, whose leader dismissed its effectiveness due to Beast’s absence. It was revealed that the Evolutionaries had previously erased the memories of both the X-Men and the Brotherhood of Mutants regarding their past dealings.

Despite this, the machine ultimately eliminated all but one of the Evolutionaries, who was sent millions of years into the past, swearing vengeance against Cyclops.[12]

Reformation and Later Efforts[]

Following the dissolution of Utopia, the X-Club was reassembled with Dr. Kavita Rao and Madison Jeffries among its key members. They later assisted Wolverine by constructing a containment cell for Dr. Alba.[2]
