History

Renegades were a gang of superhuman outlaws operating in Texas, classified as Anormalocos by Texas Ranger Tombstone. Led by Padre, the group included Rose Red, Suicide King, Six-Gun Kid, Smith, and Vegas, who initially associated with them before eventually turning against his former companions. The Renegades used an abandoned warehouse known as Fort Misery as their headquarters and also operated from a location called Tierra Muerta. Their criminal activities brought them into conflict with Tombstone and ultimately with Vegas, after the gang abducted his sister Jane in connection with a device capable of manipulating probabilities.[1]
