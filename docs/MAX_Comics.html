MAX Comics

MAX Comics is an imprint of Marvel Comics formed in 2001 that specializes in adult-oriented comics. The adult content found in the MAX titles has often proved controversial, with everyone from religious watchdog groups to Stan Lee himself criticizing the imprint.[1] It was brought back in 2019 for the Punisher: Soviet limited series.
