History

Officer Murphy informed Cole North that two officers had brought a man into the precinct for B&E (breaking and entering). The suspect claimed to know where Spider-Man hung out and was attempting to negotiate some kind of deal in exchange for revealing the information. Murphy cautioned North that the man's story was probably nonsense, but thought he might want to hear him out anyway.[1]
