Notes

  • Originally slated to be released on May 2, 2025, then being delayed to May 1, 2026, and it has now been delayed to December 18, 2026.[5]

Trivia

  • The film was originally called Avengers: The Kang Dynasty, and Kang the Conqueror was set to be the antagonist. The film was reworked and renamed due to Kang's actor, Jonathan Majors, being fired due to domestic abuse allegations.[6][7]
  • Avengers: Doomsday is the first live-action film in which the Avengers, the Fantastic Four and the X-Men all appear.
  • Avengers: Doomsday story takes plot points from Time Runs Out.
