History

Zharkah was born in a planet governed by a strict caste system resulting from the interference of the Celestials. The Chosen were those gifted with magnificent abilities but no physical deviance, while the Rejects displayed phenotypical differences among themselves. As a Reject, Zharkah knew nothing but violence and disdain from the ruling class of Chosen, who saw her people as invalid and inferior creatures.[1] In constant war with her opponents, she became a valuable leadership figure of the Djade tribe. Tragically, she saw many of her offspring not survive birth.[2] When the group of alien mutants known as X-Factor were dragged onto her planet as a result of the machinations of the Celestials, Zharkah met Beast, who she believed to be one of the Rejects. X-Factor was separated due to the intervention of the Celestial Gammenon and Beast joined the Rejects in their city.[1] Having developed a strong connection with Beast, Zharkah was shocked to learn about the different members of X-Factor. Despite displaying loyalty, she was unable to reveal to her new ally she kept his teammate Jean Grey captive.[3] Zharkah specifically kept the information to test if Beast was a Reject or not. Upon finding Jean Grey being transported, he attempted to free her, prompting Zharkah to judge him as a traitorous infiltrator.[4] Beast was caged, but managed to escape. As he attempted to escape, it was revealed to Zharkah that Beast came from another planet, thus not being either a Reject or a Chosen. Knowing Beast's true intentions, Zharkah and the Djade tribe supported him in his quest to reunite X-Factor.[5] Zharkah led the Rejects into the citadel of the Chosen to end their ruthless and corrupted rule, implemented by the vile Palik. As the Celestials judged and threatened the planet, Zharkah decided to have the Rejects join the Chosen, X-Factor and all other factions to protect their existence. She made the difficult decision to embrace change and different ideologies to prosper in battle. This caused the Celestials to leave the planet and a united community was able to thrive in peace.[6]
