Synopsis for "Retribution!"

Ch'od helps Raza flee from Avengers mansion and the Avengers hunt them down. Binary reasons with Raza and explains that he was a victim of a Kree manipulation device. Black Knight forgives Raza and the StarJammers leave. However, Raza reveals to Hepzibah that Binary lied to protect him so that he may search for his son.

Notes

  • Binary decides to leave the Starjammers and stay on Earth.
  • Revealed that Raza has a son named Rion and a deceased wife.
