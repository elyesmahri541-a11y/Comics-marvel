History

Jia-Yi was the daughter of a man obsessed with robots, who was cold and distant to his child and pushed her to focus on her studies, which included lessons on time travel. She was contacted by a powerful telepath named David Haller, who through a series of clues led her to the location of his cult, where a man told her that she would be known as "Switch".

Her first conversation with David himself was interrupted by Division 3, who attacked and killed David, forcing Switch to travel back in time and warn him of the danger. Her first attempt to do so had failed and the second one led to an encounter with Amahl Farouk on the astral plane. However, Switch was able to escape Farouk and warn David in time for him to teleport his entire compound away from the forces of Division 3 that were on his doorstep.[2]

Attributes

Powers

  • Time Traveling: Switch possesses the ability to speed up time at will as well as open portals to an extradimensional plane she refers to as the "Hall of Time", from which she can reenter the timestream at a point before she left it and alter the past.[2][3]
    • Portal Creation: To generate her portals, Jia-Yi would twist one of her teeth with her fingers before drawing a glowing doorway in the air leading to the Hall of Time. Her portals could also lead to the astral plane[2] or the time between time.[4]
    • Teleportation: Following the death of her original body, Switch gained the ability to teleport between the Hall of Time and the timestream itself, no longer needing to create portals.[1]
  • Self-Resurrection: Switch was killed by pushing her original body past its physical limits with her powers. Shortly after this, she was reborn into a new body that could comfortably exist outside the timestream without the limitations of her old one.[1]

Abilities

  • Multilingual: Jia-Yi is fluent in both English and Mandarin Chinese.[2]

Weaknesses

Former Weaknesses
  • Physical Degradation: Creating portals and traveling back in time took a heavy toll on Switch's original body when done too frequently. As she pushed her limits, her teeth would fall out and she became increasingly exhausted and physically anguished.[5][6] This exhaustion ultimately killed her in the Hall of Time, though she was quickly reincarnated.[1]

Trivia

  • Switch's real name, Jia-Yi, translates to "plus one".
    • Based on his cryptic "What is one, plus one?" riddle to Amahl Farouk, it is possible Oliver Bird might have had direct knowledge of Switch or contact with her father, Kenzo Xiu, during his time in the astral plane; potentially setting the solution to David's troubled life in motion since the start of the series.[7]
