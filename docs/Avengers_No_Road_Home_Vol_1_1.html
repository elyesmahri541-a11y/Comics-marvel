Notes

Publication Notes[]

  • Though the No Road Home storyline occurs between issues Avengers (Vol. 8) #17 and Avengers (Vol. 8) #18 of Jason Aaron and Ed McGuinness' Avengers run, it is a spiritual sequel to the storyline Avengers: No Surrender, which was also written by Al Ewing, Mark Waid, and Jim Zub.
