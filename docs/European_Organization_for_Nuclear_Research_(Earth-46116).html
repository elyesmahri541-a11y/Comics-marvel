History

When Ant-Man was attacked by Dogpool inside the Avengers Tower, Sunny the Sentry Dog, came to his rescue. Sunny hit Dogpool so hard that he sent it to Europe where Dogpool landed in Geneva, in the CERN Research Center and there he used the Hadron Collider on Sunny, depowering him, but at the same time freeing the Void Mutt that was imprisoned inside Sunny, and thanks to this he told Dogpool that it would make his death less excruciating.[1]
