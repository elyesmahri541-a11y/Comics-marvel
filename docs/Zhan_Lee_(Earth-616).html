History

Zhan Lee (李战) and his wife, Jingyi Lee,[1] were prosperous Hong Kong immigrants living in the United States of America. The two had a daughter, Jubilation Lee, and together they lived in Beverly Hills, California.[3]

Zhan was the president of a bank and, when he found out people were laundering money through his bank, he threatened them to go to the police. That night, Zhan and Jingyi were both killed in a mysterious car accident, leaving Jubilee with no one to turn to.[4]

Many years later, Jubilee finally brought the ones responsible to justice with the help of her Gen X teammates.[4]
