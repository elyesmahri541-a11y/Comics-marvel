Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man is a superhero animated series created by Jeff Trammell and produced by Marvel Studios Animation. Premiering on Disney+ in 2025, it depicts the eponymous hero's origins and early adventures in a divergent timeline of the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

The series has received positive reviews by many fans and critics, mainly for its writing and voice acting.
