History

The Cleveland Cavaliers are a professional basketball team that were based out of Cleveland, Ohio. When their star player, LeBron James, left the team for the Miami Heat, the Cavs suffered through a decade of failure until an elderly LeBron returned to the team and brough them their first victory in the NBA Finals. Shortly thereafter, LeBron purchased the team and relocated them to Akron, redubbing them the Akron Cavaliers in the process.[1]
