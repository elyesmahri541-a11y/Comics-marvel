History

During the twenty-third Ragnarok cycle, the daughter of the Frost Giant slain by Thor over the life of the Enchantress came seeking reparations from Odin for slaughtering her daughter. Odin agreed to present three gifts to the female Frost Giant and sent Loki to Nidavellir to have them constructed by Dwarves Grerr, Alfrigg, and Dvalin. One of these items was a sword that could slay Frost Giants and Odin used it to kill the Frost Giant. The Dwarves then returned to Nidavellir with the other two items, a golden arm band and necklace.[1]
