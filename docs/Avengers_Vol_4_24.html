Synopsis for "The Great and Powerful Osborn!"

The Avengers' confrontation with Norman Osborn reaches its climax after his return to power. Backed by Hand ninjas enhanced with stolen superhuman abilities, Osborn personally enters the battle against the assembled Avengers, confronting Storm, Iron Man, Red Hulk, Spider-Woman, Hawkeye, and Quake.

As the fighting escalates, Osborn reveals the source of his newfound powers. Prior to launching his campaign against the Avengers, A.I.M. scientists granted him abilities derived from the Super-Adaptoid process, allowing him to absorb and duplicate the powers of others through physical contact. However, the procedure came with a significant flaw: absorbing too many powers at once could overwhelm his body and mind.

Empowered by a growing collection of abilities, Osborn initially appears unstoppable. He withstands attacks from multiple Avengers and repeatedly turns their own powers against them, forcing the heroes onto the defensive. Despite their disadvantage, the Avengers realize that Osborn's greatest strength may also be his greatest weakness.

Working together, they deliberately force Osborn to absorb more and more abilities. As additional powers accumulate within him, the strain becomes impossible to contain. The conflicting energies overload the adaptation process, causing Osborn to lose control and ultimately bringing him down. The Avengers succeed in defeating him and ending his attempt to dominate both the team and the government.

In the aftermath, it becomes clear that Madame Hydra had never been entirely committed to Osborn's cause. Having used him to advance her own agenda, she departs with the technology and resources that had made his operation possible, intending to use them to strengthen HYDRA's position in the future.
