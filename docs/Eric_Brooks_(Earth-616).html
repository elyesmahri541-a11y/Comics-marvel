History

Early Life[]

Eric Brooks was born in Soho, London, on October 24, 1929.[17] His father, Lucas Cross, a member of the secret society the Order of Tyrana, sent his pregnant wife, Tara, to England before he was taken prisoner in Latveria. There, she took the name "Vanessa Brooks" and found shelter with brothel owner Madame Vanity, another member of the Order of Tyrana.[18] Experiencing labor complications, Tara was forced to seek a doctor's assistance. The doctor, Deacon Frost, was actually a vampire who drank Tara's blood as she gave birth, passing on a series of enzymes that altered her baby. The enzymes entered the baby's bloodstream, transforming him into a being tainted by a vampire's bite, but not turned; in other words, a human/vampire hybrid that was also known as a dhampir. Frost was driven away before he could slay the baby, but Tara died, leaving the orphaned Eric Brooks to be raised at Madame Vanity's brothel.[6]

Training[]

While he was growing up on the streets of London, he ran into an American veteran vampire slayer named Jamal Afari and saved him from being killed by vampires that had a grudge against the old man. Afari soon learned about Eric and his origins and decided to take him under his wing, becoming his mentor and foster father and helping him to control his powers. Afari taught Eric everything he knew about hunting, fighting, and killing vampires.

Determined to avenge his mother's death, Eric fashioned himself into a vampire hunter like Afari while still a teenager and started calling himself Blade the Vampire-Slayer, after the sharp wooden weapons which he used to kill vampires. Finally, Blade and Afari split up when Afari willingly took the fall for murder when Blade killed a man who he thought was a vampire, but who was actually suffering from a derangement.[19]

World War II[]

In 1943, he joined the Secret Invaders on a mission on the Irish Sea, where he found and destroyed U-666, a Nazi U-boat being possessed by Mephisto. They turned up late due to fighting the Vampire Luftwaffe.[20]

1968[]

After stalking the night on his own for a number of years, Blade became a part of a small group of like-minded individuals hunting Dracula and consisting of Orji Jones (the leader of the group), Musenda, Ogun Strong, and Azu M'Dammen. Blade went to Dracula and told him he represented a group of men who believed that in another 60 years vampires would rule the world and, realizing that humanity had no chance against their "superiors," Blade would offer his and his associates assistance to do Dracula's bidding during the daytime. Blade further enticed Dracula with this idea by telling him that they had facilitated a plan that would speed up global conquest by vampires to only 10 years. Intrigued, Dracula agreed to go with Blade to meet his associates and met them: Orji, Musenda, Ogun, and Azu. However, Dracula would soon find that this was all a trap and that they were really a group of vampire hunters. They nearly succeeded in destroying Dracula by driving a wooden stake into his heart. It was during this battle that Blade's companion would try out wooden knives of his own design and found that they worked just as well as wooden stakes did. However, Dracula soon was resurrected by his servants and took his revenge by murdering the group of vampire hunters, leaving only Musenda and Blade alive.[21]

Mighty Avengers (1970s)[]

On November 7, 1972, the murder of a bat-like creature in New York brought Blade's attention. When looking for the carcass in a morgue, he discovered Kaluu investigating the body with magic, and attacked him believing him to be a vampire and an enemy. As soon as Detective James Lucas, Constance Molina, and Adam Brashear arrived on the scene, the fight was stopped when Blade realized he was among allies. They started investigating the murder as soon as the Bear appeared and presented herself as the killer of that creature in self-defense, as she wanted to prevent the Deathwalkers from using it for a sacrifice.[22]

The Bear explained that she herself was the result of a failed attempt of the Deathwalkers to extinguish humanity in 1908 and that they would try to do it again. With the mystery solved, the people present formed the Mighty Avengers, and went to find the Deathwalkers. Kaluu tracked down the magic they used in the creation of the werebat to a secret subway below City Hall. The Mighty Avengers then attacked the Deathwalkers before they could make their sacrifice. After Blade recovered the Talisman of Kamar-Taj, which was required to perform the sacrifice, Adam Brashear caused the subway to collapse above the Deathwalkers. With the Deathwalkers stopped, the team disbanded.[23]

Tomb of Dracula[]

When Blade was in need of money, he would often perform at nightclubs in London as a trumpeter. With time, he noticed there was a beautiful woman who would never miss any of his performances and one night, the two finally met after Blade intervened to protect her from a pimp named Ponce. After this Blade and the woman, named Safron Caulder, became romantically involved.[19]

Still operating as a vampire hunter, Blade would often fight alongside a team led by Quincy Harker, also a great vampire hunter. This team's members also included Rachel van Helsing and Frank Drake.[1] Blade and the team battled Dracula and attempted to kill him and rescue Elizabeth Langley.[24] Blade and the hunters chased Dracula across Europe. After being attacked by his thralls, they reached Dracula's mortuary. The team managed to outnumber him, and eventually Blade got close enough to the Lord of the Vampires to stake him through the heart, effectively killing Dracula.[25][26] However, his death was short-lived and he was resurrected by his minions.[27] Blade attempted to slay Dracula once more. Dracula managed to overpower Blade and fed upon the vampire hunter before leaving him for dead.[28] Quincy Harker came across the body of Blade and, seeing that he had been bitten by Dracula, thought him to be dead. Quincy Harker got out a wooden stake out and prepared to ram it through Blade's heart.[29] Before Quincy could strike, Blade suddenly revived and to Quincy's surprise, Blade had not changed into a vampire. Blade explained that he is immune to vampire bites due to the fact that his mother had been bitten by a vampire while Blade was being born. Blade vowed to use this immunity to his advantage.[30] They traveled to London, and at Quincy's home he tried to convince Blade to stay and continue their quest to destroy Dracula. Blade declined the offer, feeling that he should get back on the trail of the vampire responsible for killing his mother.[31] Blade returned to the flat that he shared with Safron to find that a vampire had broken in and was attempting to feed upon his girlfriend. Blade easily destroyed her attacker. A friend, Trudy Taylor, explained what happened and Blade agreed to go out looking for Dracula. After putting on his vampire hunting gear, Blade went out into the night and was soon attacked by Dracula in his bat form. Dracula knocked Blade down into the street with a car speeding toward him. Blade battled Dracula across the city, their fight taking them to a sporting goods store. Dracula was forced to retreat when Blade managed to partially impale the vampire lord in the chest with a broken ski pole, and then later with one of his wooden daggers. With the battle over, Scotland Yard let Blade walk away from the scene, and he returned home to Safron and Trudy to rest and told them he would explain everything in the morning.[32]

When Dracula traveled to Boston Blade was also there, still tracking down the vampire who had killed his mother. Blade attacked a vampire but was easily stopped in his tracks by Dracula who demanded that Blade accompany him back to Harold H. Harold's home to meet with Quincy Harker. Dracula explained that he had nothing to do with the murders and that the coven was acting on their own. Blade had no interest in getting involved in their battle with Doctor Sun, as he had come to destroy the vampire who had killed his mother. When Doctor Sun was about to kill Dracula, Blade arrived and offered his help in killing Doctor Sun.[33] The two worked together and destroyed Doctor Sun, but after the fight Blade threatened Dracula, telling him that if he did not help him find his mother's killer, he would die by his hand.[34] Dracula refused to be ordered around, leading to a battle between the two of them.[35]

Hannibal King[]

Blade, still looking for his mother's killer, found a clue leading to their apartment. Blade attacked the occupant, but the vampire was revealed to be Hannibal King, a vampire detective.[36] Hannibal easily defeated Blade but, due to their mutual mission, they decided to work together. They waited in Deacon Frost's apartment for his recent victim to rise as a vampire. When he awakened, he explained that he had dug up a coffin containing an exact duplicate of Blade.[37] As Blade and Hannibal continued their hunt for Deacon Frost, they wondered how Frost intended to take over the world with a duplicate of Blade.[38] After a while alone in London, Safron moved to the United States to stay with Blade in Boston, claiming that London "was a drag" without him there.[39]

Doppelgänger[]

Blade and King found the "Doppelgänger" and it revealed to them that it had been created to destroy the original.[40] The two fought, but Blade stopped when the two began to fuse together on contact. Hannibal King tried to rush to Blade's rescue; however, he was too late to stop the merge from completing itself. With the Doppelgänger in full control of their unified body, it then set its sights on King.[41] The Doppelgänger attacked King and, despite its savage fury, it was easily fought off by King who stabbed the phony Blade in the chest with one of Blade's wooden daggers, defenestrating the imitator and allowing Hannibal to escape.[42] King fled from the Doppelgänger and their fight crashed into Dracula's party with Deacon Frost in attendance. Furious at Blade's interruption of his party, Dracula attacked and was shocked to find out that his longtime vampire hunter enemy was now a vampire himself.[43] The Doppelgänger appeared to have the upper hand when it stabbed Dracula in the back with one of Blade's wooden daggers. However, Dracula turned around and, mocking the phony Blade's inferior skills when compared to the original, impaled the Doppelgänger in the chest with a wooden stake, destroying it. King enlisted the help of the Son of Satan, who exorcised Blade from the Doppelgänger and destroyed it with King's help. Blade and King eventually caught up with Frost and together they destroyed both the vampire and his vampiric doppelgängers, forging a lasting friendship in the process.[44]

Nightstalkers[]

Blade, Hannibal King, and Frank Drake formed a detective agency named BorderLine Investigative Services that specialized in supernatural cases, but the agency broke up after Drake left it and Blade was committed to a mental hospital after a battle with a temporarily resurrected Dracula. Doctor Strange later arranged the release of Blade so that he could join King and Drake in re-forming BorderLine Investigative Services as a team named the Nightstalkers.

Lilith the Mother of All Demons hired the team to kill the Ghost Rider - the leather-clad, motorcycle-riding Spirit of Vengeance - and bad boy Johnny Blaze, the fiery demon's former human host. After a protracted battle Blade, Drake, and King realized that they had been tricked. The team teamed up with the Ghost Rider and Blaze against Lilith, battling her monstrous minion Meatmarket.[45] Blade, Drake, and King continued their partnership with the Ghost Rider and Blaze. Along with Doctor Strange, Morbius the Living Vampire, and the Darkhold Redeemers, they formed the Midnight Sons, a group dedicated to preserving the boundaries between the mortal world and the dark realms. The Midnight Sons clashed with Lilith and her demonspawn, the Lilin, in a supernatural contest with Earth as the prize. The Midnight Sons proved victorious, destroying both Lilith and her brood.[46] Blade also rescued his girlfriend, Safron Caulder, from the cult of the Darkholders,[47] but shortly afterward they grew apart.[48]

Blade's desire to destroy supernatural enemies led him to be tricked into using a page of the Darkhold to do so. As "Switchblade", he attacked a number of supernatural beings and stole their power in the "Midnight Massacre". King and the Nightstalkers eventually stopped Blade and another page of the Darkhold was used to restore things back to normal.[49]

The Nightstalkers also battled other threats, such as Hydra's D.O.A. (Department of Occult Affairs).[50]

Upon the eventual weakening of the Montesi Formula and the return of vampires, Blade encountered and staked a former ally, a now-vampiric Taj Nital. Blade, Drake, and King, the latter of whom had again become a vampire, began their crusade anew to cleanse the world of all bloodsuckers. Blade and his partners clashed with the dread vampire lord Varnae, the first vampire and the one who had first visited the Hellish curse of vampirism upon men. King and Drake were slain in a bloody and futile battle. The loss of his compatriots only strengthened Blade's resolve to rid the world of vampires, regardless of the circumstances of their creation. Following their victory, King was again cured of his vampirism. Subsequently, Blade and King formed a new private investigation firm.[51]

Dracula Returns[]

Following the breaking up of the Nightstalkers, Blade left Boston and moved to Greenwich Village.[8] At some point before this time, Blade had a daughter with Safron named Brielle Brooks.[16][52]

Deacon Frost teamed up with Marie Laveau to take over organized crime in New Orleans as a staging area for greater conquests. Blade was alerted by Bible John and the two fought Dracula and, later, Marie Laveau who was attempting to resurrect Varnae. It appeared that King and Drake had somehow perviously been merged into Dracula when he was resurrected, but the two had since been freed.[53]

In New Orleans, Deacon Frost captured Blade, Hannibal King, and Brother Voodoo to establish his reputation among the undead in a power grab. The heroes freed themselves from the trap and Blade battled Frost to a stalemate. Frost escaped and Blade vowed again to hunt him down and destroy him.[48]

Daywalker[]

Later, Blade and Spider-Man encountered the vampire Henry Sage and learned of the development of the Daywalker Formula, a version of the Sunlight Serum.[54] Blade helped Spider-Man try to capture their former ally Morbius, who was under the control of a vampire known as the Hunger. During the battle, Morbius bit Blade, but his blood enzymes were not enough to protect him from Morbius' unique form of pseudo-vampirism. This transformed Blade into a Daywalker: a vampire that was able to move about in the sunlight and had none of the traditional weaknesses of vampires. Blade was now driven by bloodlust, however, but he was able to hold it back with a serum.[55]

Back in New Orleans, he was contacted by Dominique Levant,[56] who led him into the middle of a vampiric coup which involved Morbius and the powerful Ulysses Sojourner.[57] Sojourner sought to unify all vampires on the East Coast, but Blade was able to stop his plans.[58]

Silvereye[]

Blade was also temporarily in S.H.I.E.L.D. custody due to the machinations of Silvereye as they sought to use him. Their plans for Blade failed. However, Darius Venginian's true agenda remains unknown.[59]

Battling the Tryks[]

Blade encountered a new type of vampiric creature called a Tryk which was unafraid of vampires and hunted them down.[60] Cilla, the Queen of the Tryks, was interested in a ceremony which would make Blade her king and an organization called the Seven was introduced which pursued a policy of "vampiric equilibrium": they killed both vampires and the Tryks which preyed upon them, but only enough to regulate the numbers of both types. They were upset with Blade's recent escalation of vampire killings, as they did not want an all-out war spilling over into the streets, so they authorized his assassination.[61] The Seven revealed that Cilla wanted Blade for her mate so that she could devour him and produce another queen. Blade went on a date with his new girlfriend, Susan, and told her that the vampires tricked him into slaying his former lover with a sword by drugging her and outfitting her with contact lenses and phony fangs. The vampires captured Fofo, Blade's weapons supplier, and placed a tracking device on him so that he would lead them to Blade after they dumped him in the river. The vampires got the drop on Blade and captured him.[17]

The Tryks captured the Seven's agents who were sent to kill Blade, while Blade escaped from the vampires. The Tryks brought the agents to their queen after Blade received some I.D. from them, which he traced back to their leader. The boy (since the leaders of the Seven age backwards) made Blade the offer of a sword to invade the queen's stronghold and slay them so he could submerge himself in the queen's baths and become fully human.[62] After Blade had sex with Susan, he found out that she had been Cilla in disguise all along. Blade entered the queen's baths but did not change, so he realized the leader of the Seven was manipulating him. Blade then destroyed the Tryks' lair, but the now-pregnant queen escaped. Realizing the Tryk numbers had been severely thinned by Blade, the Seven countermanded its previous directive to assassinate Blade and let him have a free hand in the field.[15] Blade later helped a woman put to rest her husband who was killed by a vampire that was being pursued by him.[63]

Meeting the Punisher[]

Blade found the Punisher hiding on a rooftop, where he was viewing a deal between vampires and some thugs. The Punisher emerged from the shadows and the two drew their weapons; Blade eventually lowered his and told the Punisher that if he shot him, the vampires below would know. Then the Punisher attached a silencer to his gun and shot Blade in the back. Blade remained unharmed and they argued. They turned their attention to the crime below. Blade admitted that he admired the Punisher, but suddenly there was an explosion a few blocks away; the vampires thought it was the thugs and tried to drink their blood, but Blade and the Punisher jumped down from the roof to kill them all.[64]

Lucas Cross[]

Blade came into contact with Dracula again, and appeared to fully destroy the Lord of the Vampires once again aboard the S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier Pericles V.[6] After taking on a time travel mission for Doctor Doom, Blade received from Doom an elixir that would purportedly cure him of his thirst for human blood but would also remove the bloodlust he had killing the undead. During the mission in the past, Blade had fulfilled part of a prophecy by freeing his imprisoned father, Lucas Cross.[65]

Unknown to Blade, however, his wealthy father was responsible for Dracula's latest resurrection. Lucas kidnapped his son and tried to force Blade to feed on a young virgin girl by starving him in an attempt to fulfill the same prophecy that Blade had unwittingly begun. Blade escaped with the girl unharmed after biting through his own hand.[18]

Blade returned home to England, seeking answers about his past and the prophecy. Shortly after arriving, he used his S.H.I.E.L.D. connections to learn of vampires running illegal weaponry and killed them while procuring the guns that he was unable to bring into the country. The deaths of these vampires alerted a powerful vampire priest, Draconis, to Blade's presence. Draconis attacked Blade and was able to defeat him, as he had spent decades building up an immunity to vampiric weaknesses, including being staked in the heart, specifically to defeat Blade in vengeance for his time spent in London killing vampires when he was young.[66] Blade survived this encounter thanks to a talisman which made vampires immortal and was aware he could not take Draconis on alone; after gathering information on him, Blade called Hannibal King for help in taking him down. Blade fed upon Draconis to make killing him easier, thus feeding on virgin blood and unknowingly fulfilling the prophecy.[67]

While still in England, Blade dug up his mother's grave and within her casket marked "Tara Cross", he found a confession she had written about having been married to Lucas Cross as well as being a member of the Order of Tyrana, from whom she had been forced to flee. From Maria Hill he learned that the Order were connected to the Knights of Pendragon, of which he also learned Joseph Chapman, the current Union Jack, was a member as well as a vampire hunter. Chapman was already on Blade's trail and attempted to kill Hannibal for being a vampire. After getting Chapman to stop attacking them and speak with them instead, Blade learned more about the Order, including the fact that they were supposedly good people.[68]

Blade returned to America and tracked his father down to a social club party in Washington, D.C. to get answers. Blade learned both that his father was a vampire and that everything he had been doing up to this point was in service to the prophecy. Blade had distracted his father long enough for Hannibal to obtain a copy of the prophecy, the details of which Blade recognized in his recent actions, including the freeing of his father from a Latverian prison. Blade began working on stopping the prophecy which led him to try and track down Dracula's body; to aid him in this, he approached Spider-Man in New York City (whose uncle and aunt he was unaware that he had once saved from a vampire attack shortly after their marriage). Blade learned more about how Spider-Man was temporarily turned into a vampire, including the fact that Jamal Afari, an old friend and mentor of Blade's who he was forced to kill, had been the vampire to turn him.[69]

Blade spent weeks searching the world for Afari but was unable to find him, until his father broke into his home to offer his help. Blade travelled alongside his father and Hannibal to Transylvania, where his father assured him they would find Afari at Castle Dracula according to the prophecy, which he believed would give all vampires back their souls. Blade was not interested in potentially making vampires invulnerable by returning their souls and refused, at which point his father revealed he had been holding Afari hostage and would stake him dead if Blade did not cooperate. Desperately wanting the return of his soul, Hannibal turned against Blade, who quickly killed him.[70]

Moments after killing his friend, Blade was confronted by Dracula (whom Lucas had returned to life) and brokered a deal with him. Dracula tried to force Blade to complete the prophecy by returning a piece of the wooden stake that had used to kill him to the ground of his castle but Blade refused, causing Dracula to target Afari in an attempt to convince him. In the ensuing scuffle the sliver of the wooden stake touched the ground as Blade fumbled it, enacting the prophecy, though not in the way Lucas Cross had expected. The prophecy did not return vampires' lost souls to them but instead brought every dead vampire back to undead life, including Hannibal. Blade then decided not to kill his father and instead left him to live with his actions. Later, in exchange for having killed him, Blade gave Hannibal the potion that he obtained from Doctor Doom so Hannibal could live without bloodlust.[71]

Vanguard[]

Blade was recruited into the Vanguard, the name of a secret black ops team of super-heroes that carried out missions, including assassinations, on foreign soil. During one mission, Micromax was captured by Jaafar Yoosuf and was forced to tell him about the Vanguard and their most powerful operative, Colonel America (Trenton Craft). Yoosuf used his powers of body possession to cause police detective Stacy Dolan to murder Craft.[72] Her investigation led to her own arrest, but she survived a failed assassination attempt by the Vanguard's operative, Dominic Fortune.[73] Dolan discovered the entire Vanguard team and convinced them that she was innocent by exposing Yoosuf, who was summarily killed by Blade.[74] Craft arrived at their facility to reveal that his telekinetic powers allowed him to survive his own murder and autopsy, but that he had to destroy Dolan and the entire team to preserve the secret existence of the Vanguard.[75] The Vanguard members survived thanks to the intervention of Retcon, whose powers allowed Craft to think he had killed them, but they were all forced to go into hiding as a result of this.[76]

MI13[]

Following the Skrull invasion of the United Kingdom, Blade joined MI13 to aid with the resurgence of evil forces resulting from the Skrulls' defeat. Blade failed to ingratiate himself, however, when he attempted to kill fellow team member, and part-vampire, Spitfire. Both Spitfire and Blade, however, settled their difference and pursued a romantic relationship.[77]

Nightmare tried to conquer Earth and mind-controlled Blade and the world's most powerful supernatural beings. He then forced them to battle the former Sorcerer Supreme Jericho Drumm.[78]

Mutants vs. Vampires[]

Blade was forced to battle Xarus, the son of Dracula, as he united the various vampire sects.[79] The X-Men were attacked by a siege of vampires but fortunately, Blade was around to lend a hand. The vampires were now using technology to protect them from sunlight.[80][81] Dracula arrived at Utopia and offered his assistance in defeating his son.[82] With vampire activity in San Francisco escalating, Cyclops gave the order for the X-Men to battle their enemy in order to find out what their next move should be. He had Blade team up with the Angel in taking down a few vampires at a rock-and-roll concert.[83]

Blade and the X-Men had to battle wave after wave of attacking vampires.[84] With Dracula in the battle, Xarus tried ordering his minions to help him, but received no support. Blade did not see eye-to-eye with Cyclops and charged at Dracula, only to be knocked unconscious with an optic blast. Cyclops then reminded Dracula of their previous, unspoken agreement. After a short staredown, Dracula called Cyclops' bluff but, nonetheless, decided to end hostilities with mutants, even giving Jubilee back to them. Jubilee was then put in isolation. Blade believed that the only solution was to put her out of her misery. Wolverine warned him not to, prompting the vampire hunter to leave.[85]

Mighty Avengers[]

Blade secretly arrived at New York City in order to ask his associate Spectrum for help on a mission. However, they were interrupted by the invasion of Thanos the Mad Titan's army on Earth. Spectrum convinced Blade to help, but since he wanted to stay under the radar, he wore a phony Spider-Man suit that he had gotten from a super-hero costume shop. As "Spider Hero," Blade became part of a team of heroes named the Mighty Avengers after a Twitter hashtag used to describe them.[86]

Following the victory of Earth's heroes over Thanos' army, Blade adopted Hawkeye's former identity of Ronin.[2] As Ronin, Blade accompanied his teammates to Attilan. He recovered the Talisman of Kamar-Taj that the Deathwalkers were looking for and temporarily left the team during their fight against Lichidus' Hellhound.[87]

Days later, Ronin was attacked by ninja weresnakes, which were sent by the Deathwalkers who had discovered Blade's disguise, to get the Talisman of Kamar-Taj.[88] Numerous other creatures were sent to hunt down Blade, and he was ultimately defeated by wereroosters. Blade was then brought to the Deathwalkers, who planned to use him for a sacrifice.[89]

Even though he escaped captivity after being drained of a portion of his blood, and the Mighty Avengers located him, Blade could not prevent the Deathwalkers from finishing their ritual. The successfully-finished ceremony merged them together into the Deathwalker Prime, a creature with control over the four elements, thus it had power over the fifth: the spirit, which would be the key for it to destroy humankind.[90]

However, the Mighty Avengers managed to get hold of the cup that was used in the ritual of merging, and using a similar procedure merged themselves into the Avenger Prime, which managed to destroy the Deathwalker Prime, mainly because unlike the Deathwalker Prime, the different personas merged into the Avenger Prime were truly spiritually bonded, while the different personas merged into the Deathwalker Prime were fighting for control.[91]

Avengers[]

Blade was captured by vampires and eventually freed by the Wasp.[92] On the advice of Black Panther, Blade joined the Avengers and aided them in stemming a civil war between the vampire community being waged by the Shadow Colonel and the Legion of the Unliving, which turned out to be a ploy by Dracula to renew the Vampire Nation by removing weaker elements from it.[93]

Blade continued helping the Avengers to fight the Dark Council's invasion of Earth,[94] help rescue the Ghost Rider when he was sent to Hell,[95] help to determine the fate of the new wielder of the Starbrand,[96] stop Khonshu from taking over the world,[97] help deal with the Phoenix Force when it came to Earth to find a new host,[98] help subdue the Hulk,[99] and fight Knull when he attacked Earth by fighting Knull and asking for help from Dracula and his Vampire Nation.[100][101]

After Knull was defeated, the Vampire Nation was able to successfully lobby for recognition from the United Nations as a country within the Chernobyl exclusion zone as compensation for their help in fighting Knull. Blade chose to leave the Avengers and live in the Vampire Nation to keep the vampires in check with the full authority of both the Avengers and the United Nations.[102]

Heroes Reborn[]

One day, Blade woke up to discover that the entire world had changed to one where the Avengers did not exist and the Squadron Supreme was the world's premiere super-hero team. Wanting to change the world back to its proper state, Blade sought to create the Avengers. After failing to convince Thor to join him, Blade recovered Captain America still frozen in ice and convinced him to help him.[103] They then began gathering the members of the Avengers, starting with Echo.[104]

Blade and Captain America later recruited Black Panther, and Echo recruited Thor, who was able to recover his powers and memories. With the help of a Wakandan spaceship, Brandy Selby was discovered deep in space and returned to Earth. The Squadron Supreme eventually caught wind of the Avengers and began investigating them. When the Squadron found the Avengers in the hidden nation of Wakanda, Blade and the Avengers were ready to fight the Squadron.[105]

Blade fought Nighthawk, but was stunned by a Hawkarang that delivered a powerful electric shock. Blade was able to get to his feet, but was then knocked out by Hyperion. Blade eventually came to and helped Thor rise after he was knocked out by Hyperion. Blade and the other Avengers were about to fight Nighthawk when reality was returned to normal when the source of the reality warp was destroyed, President Coulson's Pandemonium Cube. Blade was returned to Earth-616 and awakened to find himself back in Chernobyl. Blade surmised that his supernatural nature was what protected him from the reality warp.[106]

Mother of Evil[]

In order to repay a favor he owed to an old acquaintance, Blade accepted a mission to protect a young woman from a masked Cambodian assassin wielding a magic sword. While Blade succeeded in killing the assassin in a duel, the woman he was hired to protect seized the assassin's sword before revealing her identity as the Adana: a primordial eldritch god who could only be stopped by the assassin Blade had just decapitated. Realizing that he had been tricked, Blade immediately tried to slay the Adana, but was effortlessly defeated by the god instead. Blade woke up 13 days later in the Cardamom Mountains, where he was imprisoned and tortured by a militant sect of warrior occultists as punishment for murdering their champion and dooming the entire world to annihilation at the Adana's hands. Fortunately, a teenage member of the sect named Rotha decided to defect from it and freed Blade from captivity so he could help her stop the Adana's apocalyptic machinations.[107]

In order to obtain another weapon capable of slaying the Adana, Blade and Rotha traveled to Japan to recruit the assistance of his ex-girlfriend, Tamlyn Hamato or Tulip, a clairvoyant Japanese arms dealer with a history of selling supernatural armaments to the highest bidder. After helping Tulip kill a local Yakuza boss she had swindled in an arms deal, Tulip informed Blade and Rotha that Lightbringer, the sword of Lucifer, was the weapon currently on Earth that had the best chance at slaying an eldritch entity like the Adana. But Lightbringer was in the possession of a vampire-turned-war-profiteer named Hamilton Achilles, who locked the sword inside a fortified Adamantium train that served as his fortress. Doctor Strange also arrived to confront Blade over his disastrous handling of the crisis, as Adana murdered over 12 of Stephen's subordinates trying to apprehend her, but Strange agreed to support Blade in his heist to steal Lightbringer.[108]

Boarding the train, Blade slashed his way forward to reach Hamilton and Lightbringer while Tulip and Rotha fought to free the captives kept to serve as livestock for its vampire passengers. While Hamilton attempted to fight Blade over the holy sword, the vampire boss was psychically compelled by the Adana to relinquish the weapon, and he committed suicide immediately afterwards. With Lucifer's flaming sword in his hands, Blade and his allies arrived just in time to confront the Adana while she was conducting a ritual, but it turned out to have been another trick by the eldritch horror, who duped Blade into delivering Lightbringer to her so she could carry out the next step of her plan.[109]

Blade and his allies failed to stop the Adana's ritual, resulting in a "primordial wave" that transformed millions of people across the planet into supernatural horrors. The Adana also bonded a demon to Tulip's soul, forcing the woman to perpetually suffer in agony seemingly to spite Blade. Overcome with guilt and a lust for revenge, Blade forced Doctor Strange to teleport him directly to the Adana for a desperate rematch, where she personally revealed to Blade that she had created an entirely new Hell from scratch to both rule over and serve as a display of her regained power. After effortlessly defeating Blade once more, the eldritch god decided to spare his life and cast him out of her Hell.

With the Adana now stronger than ever before, the world embroiled in chaos as new supernatural horrors run rampant, and Tulip continuing to suffer from her demonic possession, Blade left both Tulip and Rotha in Doctor Strange's care before traveling back to the Vampire Nation of Chernobyl. Realizing that he would never be able to defeat something as powerful as the Adana while suppressing his vampiric heritage, Blade decided to swallow his pride and seek mentorship from his oldest enemy: Dracula.[110]

After several sparring sessions, Dracula ascertained that the reason Blade had failed to stop the Adana was because he intentionally limited himself out of hatred for his own vampiric heritage, and that he would need to start embracing that side of himself if he wanted any real chance at defeating her. This culminated in Dracula convincing Blade to drink his blood, permanently evolving the latter into a "strigoi" of the same caliber as Dracula himself, granting the Daywalker access to a whole new array of supernatural abilities.[111] Blade then came to the aid of Bruce Banner, who had also been embroiled with combating supernatural monstrosities and needed the Daywalker's assistance in dealing with a demon that had possessed a teenager while overrunning a small town with its bloodthirsty kin. Blade manipulated the demon into leaving the teen's body and lured it and its demonic spawn to the outskirts of the town for the Hulk to safely destroy them.[112]

With that matter resolved, Blade and Rotha turned to Satana Hellstrom to send the Daywalker to Hell so he could assemble an army of the damned to march against the Adana, a plan that fell apart when a reaper named Abrath the Collector attempted to kill Blade for trespassing into the realm of the dead. Thankfully the spirit of Draven, the masked assassin Blade had mistakenly slain, killed Abrath before he could take Blade's life. Believing that they could potentially destroy the Adana if they worked together, the two escaped Hell and began working on the next phase of their plan.[113] Needing a way to get the Adana's attention, Satana pointed Blade to the sacred Archives of the Second World, which if defiled would force all of the supernatural evils of the world to come after him in retaliation. Blade massacred all of the Archives' occult attendants and leveled the entire facility with explosives, forcing the Adana to come after his head in accordance with the rules of the pact.[114]

With Tulip, Rotha, and Draven at his side, Blade slaughtered hordes of supernatural foes who were gunning for him in retaliation for destroying the Archives. After utilizing his new abilities to eliminate another wave of opponents, Satana arrived to reveal that she was able to pinpoint the Adana's location and opened a portal to send Blade and his allies to her, but not before warning Blade that, as one of the "Celestial Embodiments of Immortal Evil", the concept of self-preservation did not mean anything to an entity like the Adana, especially since she had already achieved everything she had set out to do since Draven's death.

Against all odds, Blade and his allies were able to slay the Adana's physical avatar with Lucifer's sword after coordinating with one another to pry it from the eldritch entity's possession. But in her dying breaths, the Adana boasted how it was already far too late, as her countless supernatural creations were still running rampant in the mortal world and Blade had to corrupt his very being physically and spiritually to best her. Blade sat down and lamented on how the Adana was right in the end and that he would have to dedicate the rest of his life undoing the chaos she had unleashed onto the world, but he invited Tulip to stay by his side and help him slay the horrific monstrosities who still remained.[115]

Blood Hunt[]

Sometime later, Blade came under the full possession of Varnae, essentially losing his body to the first vampire, with his consciousness trapped in the backseat of his mind.[116] In preparation for the worldwide vampire invasion Varnae, pretending to be Blade, went to meet some vampire hunters whom Blade had personally taught and, having raised no suspicion, caught them by surprise and managed to slaughter them all. The real Blade could do nothing but watch all of this as it unfolded, shedding a single tear for the loss of his proteges at the hands of his own body.[117]

Varnae continued masquerading as Blade, kickstarting the vampire invasion, making quick work of super-heroes who would have thwarted his plans early on such as the Avengers, Miles Morales and Doctor Strange. In addition to this, he dispatched his minions, the Bloodcoven, to capture Brielle since, due to being related to Blade, she could pose a threat to him.[118] Varnae also went to attack the re-formed Midnight Sons who were joined by Tulip, but was prevented from killing them by Blade fighting back against his control.[119]

Through a ritual millennia in the making, Varnae eventually imbued himself with the power of the Darkforce to ascend to godhood,[116] but was soon confronted by the Avengers, the Moon Knight, Dracula, Brielle and Miles. The latter two used their blood relation to Varnae and Blade to deal heavy damage to the first vampire. This paved way for Varnae to be exorcised from Blade's body by the Scarlet Witch, freeing Blade from Varnae's possession at last, but Blade was left in an injured state.[120] Blade managed to make a full recovery, and wanting to find peace and forget about what had happened he went to spend some time at a Buddhist temple in Thailand. There he was reunited with Tulip, and the two talked for a while.[121]

Powers

  • Dhampir Physiology: Blade was born a human/vampire hybrid that is also known as a dhampir; the enzymes in Blade's blood made him immune to normal vampire bites, uniquely attuned to sensing the supernatural and resistant to aging. Since being bitten by Morbius the Living Vampire, Blade has gained the traditional strengths of true undead vampires, but none of their weaknesses. He has superhuman strength, senses, and stamina, plus an accelerated healing factor.[122] After drinking the blood of Dracula, Blade became better connected to his powers, allowing him to unlock new abilities.[18]
    • Superhuman Senses: Blade's sense of sight and hearing are heightened to levels comparable to those possessed by true undead vampires. He is capable of seeing objects with perfect clarity at much greater distances than ordinary humans. He retains this same level of clarity even in near-total darkness. His hearing is similarly enhanced, allowing him to detect sounds that an ordinary human cannot hear and enable him to hear sounds an ordinary human can hear, but at greater distances. Blade is also able to sense the presence of supernatural beings or forces, whereas an ordinary human would be oblivious to them.[122]
      • Superhuman Smelling: Blade possesses an incredibly pronounced sense of smell, easily able to discern what is human and what is not even from a good distance away.[123] The She-Hulk likened his olfactory senses to those of Wolverine.
      • Superhuman Taste: Blade's sense of taste is enhanced to levels far greater than any ordinary human, allowing him to practically taste the lingering Frost Giant blood carried on the She‑Hulk’s body after battle.[124] Blade's half-vampire nature also imbues his sense of taste with some mystical properties.[125]
        • Blood Psychometry: By tasting spilled blood, Blade can access imprinted visions of past events, trauma, or emotional states tied to the moment of bloodshed.[125]
      • Extrasensory Perception: Blade possesses a sixth sense that allows him to sense Magic and supernatural beings.[126][127] He could also hear the Ghost Rider's Hell Charger laughing when no one else could.[128]
      • Superhuman Vision
        • Night Vision
        • Telescopic Vision
    • Superhuman Strength: Blade possesses the strength of a true undead vampire, allowing him to lift a ton of weight due to his half-vampire nature. Blade has overpowered a vampiric Spider-Man, routinely makes prodigious jumps of hundreds of feet through the air, and has sufficient strength to easily rip the head off another vampire with his bare hands.[122]
    • Superhuman Speed: Blade is capable of running and moving at speeds greater than even the finest human athlete. He can also accelerate his movements to cover short distances in an instant, faster than the human eye can see, but he is not as fast as a true undead vampire. His reflexes are similarly heightened. He moves so fast that everything else, when compared to him, appears to be moving in slow motion.
    • Superhuman Durability: Blade's human/vampire hybrid tissue allows him to sustain physical trauma to a certain extent. He can withstand powerful energy blasts, exposure to temperature extremes, and great impact forces without being injured. He has been seen tanking a blast from Lilith the Mother of All Demons herself and walked away from it only with minor burns and injuries. This also contributes to his healing factor, making him more resistant than a normal human.
    • Superhuman Agility: Blade's agility, balance, and bodily coordination are enhanced to levels that are beyond the natural physical limits of the finest human athlete. He can leap several feet into the air and is able to move, jump, climb, flip, and run at incredibly fast speeds without difficulty or exhaustion. He can perform above-human parkour feats and virtually always land on his feet.
    • Superhuman Reflexes: Blade's reflexes far superior to a normal human. He is able to easily react to and dodge point-blank gunfire from behind him without even looking.[129]
    • Superhuman Stamina: Blade's musculature generates less lactic acid (i.e., muscle fatigue) during physical activity than the musculature of an ordinary human. He can exert himself at peak capacity for several hours before fatigue begins to impair him.[122]
    • Regeneration: Blade, like true undead vampires, possesses an accelerated healing ability that allows him to heal mild to moderate injuries with much greater speed and efficiency than ordinary humans.[122] He is capable of healing from severe slashes and puncture wounds within a matter of minutes to hours depending on the severity of the wounds. Wolverine himself stated that his regeneration was almost on par with his during his fight with Blade in the Civil War. However, he is not able to regenerate missing limbs or organs.
    • Contaminant Transferal: Blade can take any kind of ingestible or even metaphysical ailments coursing in the system of another living being, or willingly introduced into his system and pass it along, using the same process that true undead vampires use to infect others.[130] He used this ability to drain away the infection which remade Robbie Reyes into a more monstrous form of the Ghost Rider.[131]
      • Healing: Blade can also use his vampire bite to deliver cures that he has contained within his bloodstream, as he did to nullify the Roxxon B.E.R.S.E.R.K.E.R. shocktrooper's Hulk-like transformation abilities.[131]
    • Advanced Longevity: Although not a true undead vampire, Blade does have the advantage of being a human/vampire hybrid. This includes a pseudo-Immortality, granting him a greatly enhanced lifespan. This has also been noted in the Superhuman Registration act as one of his abilities. Also, no change of his appearance has been seen during his time on Earth, besides sometimes losing his hair.[citation needed]
    • Immunity To Vampire Vulnerabilities: True undead vampires have numerous and very particular supernatural vulnerabilities, most of which Blade is totally immune to. For instance, Blade is unaffected by direct exposure to sunlight, whereas most true undead vampires are either rendered comatose during the daylight hours or are quickly disintegrated into dust when exposed to direct sunlight. True undead ampires are also highly vulnerable to silver, supposedly because of the metal's mystical purity. As a result, silver blades or bullets are capable of killing vampires. If not killed but merely injured, it will take a vampire much longer to heal than if the injury had been inflicted by another material. Blade himself could be injured by a silver weapon, much as an ordinary human would, but his healing factor would be able to heal it as if it were an ordinary wound. Blade is also immune to the effects of religious icons, such as crucifixes, whereas true undead vampires are rendered almost powerless when confronted by them.
    • Appearance Alteration: Blade seemingly possesses the ability to change to and from his human form into a more monstrous state of being by sheer force of will.[132] After drinking the blood of Dracula, Blade gained the ability to transform into a swarm of bats, a dark mist, and a wolf.[69][18][66]
      • Animal Form[69]
        • Winged Flight: Blade's transformed state comes with the added proclivity of bat wing-like appendages that enable him to fly at high speeds.[132]
      • Gas Mimicry: When in his mist form, Blade is capable of entering the bodies of multiple vampires and cause them to all explode at once.[69]
    • Self-Exorcism: Blade is capable of forcibly removing possessing entities from his own body, such as demons. He can also maintain control over his body while he is possessed.[66]
  • Magic: Though rarely displayed, Blade possesses some skill with magic. He knows one spell that allows him to trap and contain vampires inside a mystical artifact.[133]

Weaknesses

  • Blade does possess one weakness common to all vampires: the need to drink fresh blood in order to stay alive. However, rather than consume blood, he drinks or injects a specially designed serum that provides even better nourishment that blood could provide. If Blade does not drink the serum for an extended period of time, he will weaken and his self-control will be stretched to the limit. He would be forced to attack a human and drink his or her blood if this period is more than one week. Blade does have a special meditation to maintain his self-control and a huge amount of blood serum in his inventory. One helping of the serum is enough for 24 hours of extended combat or he can use it to quickly recover from grave wounds. However, an extended period of time without drinking either blood or the serum could prove fatal.[134][122]
  • Vulnerability to Red Sunlight: While not affected by normal (yellow) sunlight and immune to all the weaknesses of true undead vampires, Blade has been shown to be vulnerable to red sunlight, which is able to burn his skin. Longer exposure to red sunlight can be fatal.[135]
