History

When the Enforcer decides to kill Johnny Blaze, he takes him to a cliffside highway overlooking the Pacific Ocean where he has him tied to a stolen motorcycle which is then started and sent over the edge. However, Blaze survives by turning into the Ghost Rider. Later, after having defeated the Enforcer, the Ghost Rider ridees his Flame Cycle along the Pacific Coast Highway once again.[1]

Cyclops and Marvel Girl were chased by the X-Men down the Pacific Coast Highway.[2]
Helped by Shadowcat and Rachel Grey, they eventually managed to escape to Utopia.[3]

Alternate Realities[]

Earth-22206 (Wade Wilson's War)[]

Deadpool and Domino apparently escaped on the Pacific Coast Highway after killing Senator Sevier.[4]
