History

While stopping various violent convicts, Ironclad of the U-Foes was spotted. The Agent was assigned to the Thing to stop Ironclad and bring him back into custody. Ironclad, after being defeated, managed to escape by floating out on the East River and then sinking to the bottom. Stark suggested that cosmic radiation was the reason for Ironclad's ability to float at all.[1]
