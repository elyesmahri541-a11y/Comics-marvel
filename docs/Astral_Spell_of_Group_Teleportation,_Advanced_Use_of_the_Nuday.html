History

The Astral Spell of Group Teleportation, Advanced Use of the Nuday was a magical spell used by Doctor Strange to transport the New Avengers away from their confrontation with the Dark Avengers. Following the destruction of their aircraft and the appearance of an enormous illusory tidal wave, Strange teleported his teammates to a nearby rooftop, allowing them to escape the battlefield. The spell left him severely weakened, as he had already been poisoned by June Covington, and he needed time to recover after completing the transportation.[1]

The spell is detailed in the Book of the Vishanti at page 7653.
