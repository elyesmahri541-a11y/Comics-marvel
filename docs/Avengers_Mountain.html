Avengers Mountain is the corpse of a Celestial known as the Progenitor which was renovated into an Avengers base at the North Pole.[1]

During a conflict with Mephisto's Council of Red and Multiversal Masters of Evil, one of Avenger Prime's Deathloks uploaded himself into the Mountain.[2] Overhauled to look like a Deathlok, it went to the fight at the God Quarry and remained there as the new base for Avenger Prime and his Multiversal Avengers.[3][4][5]

History

The origins of the Avengers Mountain date back four billion years, when the Celestial known as the Progenitor came to Earth to die after being infected by the Horde.[6] His corpse laid unmoved within the Arctic Circle, and its resting place became the bottom of the Arctic Ocean at the North Pole.[7]

Following the defeat of the Final Host of Dark Celestials at the hands of the Avengers,[8] the resurrected Celestials brought forth the Progenitor from its watery grave and bestowed it upon the Avengers as a gift and a reminder of the wonders of life on Earth. The corpse was refurbished by the finest architects from Wakanda and technicians from Alpha Flight to make the mountain livable so it could serve as the Avengers' new headquarters.[1]

During a battle between the Council of Red's multiversal Masters of Evil, a badly-damaged Deathlok unit uploaded his consciousness into the Celestial's corpse.[2]

To prevent all-out war between the Eternals and mutants, Ajak, Makkari, Mister Sinister, and Iron Man combined their knowledge to reanimate the Progenitor's corpse and reprogram its consciousness to create a new Celestial that would stop the war, sacrificing Avengers Mountain in the process. However, the new Celestial deemed the sentient life of Earth to be unworthy and gave them twenty-four hours to change its mind or be destroyed.[9] Eventually, the heroes convinced the new Progenitor that it itself is unworthy to pass judgment, which caused it to undo everything and returned to its old form as Avengers Mountain.[10]

The Avengers used the Celestial's teleporter to go back in time to combat the Council of Red.[11] During this time, the Deathlok that uploaded himself into Avengers Mountain earlier came online. Remodelled into a Deathlok, it went to the God Quarry to fight alongside the Avengers against Mephisto and Doom Supreme.[3][4] After the battle was over, it stayed in the God Quarry with the Deathlok's master, Avenger Prime and his Multiversal Avengers, as their new base.[5]

Specifications[]

Avengers Mountain is one of the most sophisticated superhero headquarters on Earth, constructed within the remains of the Progenitor, an ancient Celestial buried beneath the Arctic Circle. Rather than simply occupying the Celestial's remains, the Avengers extensively retrofitted its anatomy into a fully operational headquarters, incorporating advanced human, Wakandan, alien, and Celestial technology.

Command & Administration[]

  • T'Challa's Corona Penthouse: Located at the crown of Avengers Mountain, this private penthouse serves as T'Challa's personal residence, office, and command suite. Its elevated position provides the Black Panther with direct access to the Mountain's principal command systems while overlooking the surrounding Arctic region.[12]
  • Brain Room Central Command and Servers: Constructed within the remains of the Progenitor's cerebrum, the Brain Room serves as Avengers Mountain's primary command, communications, intelligence, and data-processing center. Celestial brain tissue has been integrated into its computer systems, creating an extraordinarily powerful terminal capable of processing and decrypting immense quantities of information. The Avengers' teleportation and time-travel technology is also housed here.[13][14]
  • War Room: The Avengers' primary strategic planning and mission-briefing chamber. The War Room contains global and extraterrestrial monitoring systems, tactical displays, communications equipment, and holographic interfaces used to coordinate Avengers operations around the world and beyond.
  • Energy Monitoring Facility: A dedicated control center responsible for monitoring Avengers Mountain's immense energy requirements, Celestial systems, external power sources, and potentially dangerous fluctuations within the Progenitor's remains.

Transportation[]

  • Spinal Lift: Avengers Mountain's principal internal transportation system. Running through the Progenitor's spinal column, the sophisticated high-speed elevator connects the numerous levels, organs, facilities, and living areas spread throughout the Mountain.[1]
  • The Circulator Teleportation Room: Created by partially reanimating the Progenitor's circulatory system and harnessing cosmic energy from its remaining blood cells. Located within one of the Celestial's heart ventricles, the Circulator functions as a teleportation hub capable of transporting Avengers virtually anywhere on Earth.
  • Main Hangars: The fingers of the Progenitor's right hand have been converted into individualized aircraft hangars capable of housing Avengers Quinjets, spacecraft, support vehicles, and other specialized transports.[15]
  • Sea Hangar: Located within the Progenitor's right foot and accessible through a ground-level water passage, the Sea Hangar accommodates submarines, amphibious vehicles, Atlantean craft, and other underwater transportation.[1]
  • Underwater Hallway: A reinforced submerged passage connecting Avengers Mountain with its aquatic facilities and Sea Hangar, allowing personnel and specialized vehicles to enter or leave the headquarters beneath the Arctic waters.

Residential & Recreational Facilities[]

  • Living Quarters: Avengers Mountain contains private residential quarters for its active members, allowing Avengers to permanently or temporarily reside at headquarters. The accommodations can be customized to meet the biological, environmental, cultural, and security requirements of individual members.
  • Tony Stark's Loft and Forge: Tony Stark's personal living quarters and engineering workshop, situated within one of the Progenitor's eyes. The facility combines a private residence with an advanced fabrication laboratory where Stark can design, repair, modify, and maintain Iron Man technology.[16]
  • Eden Room: Created by transforming the Progenitor's appendix into an artificial forest and garden. The Eden Room provides a natural recreational environment within the otherwise technological headquarters and can also serve as a location for relaxation, meditation, ecological research, and cultivation.[12]
  • Hot Tub Room: A recreational facility where Avengers personnel can relax and recover between missions, supplementing the Mountain's more extensive residential and fitness facilities.

Training & Fitness[]

  • Avengers Strength and Conditioning Center: Located within the Progenitor's left bicep, this massive gymnasium provides physical-training equipment capable of accommodating Avengers ranging from ordinary human athletes to superhuman heavyweights.
  • Femoral Artery Jogging Track: A specialized running and endurance-training course constructed through the Progenitor's right calf and former vascular structures. Its considerable length allows Avengers to conduct cardiovascular and speed training without leaving headquarters.
  • Automated Mandroid Combat Range: Located within the Progenitor's left fist, this automated training facility deploys Mandroid units and other simulated opponents for combat exercises. Training scenarios can be adjusted for individual Avengers or entire teams and used to test tactics, weapons, powers, and coordinated combat techniques.[12]

Science, Medicine & Research[]

  • Medical Center: Avengers Mountain's principal hospital and emergency-treatment facility. Equipped to treat conventional injuries as well as superhuman, extraterrestrial, mystical, radiation-related, and other unusual medical conditions encountered by Avengers personnel.
  • Laboratories: Advanced scientific facilities used by Avengers scientists for research, engineering, biological analysis, extraterrestrial study, forensic investigation, and examination of unusual technology or materials recovered during missions.
  • Library of Intergalactic Law: Located within the Progenitor's right lung, this extensive archive contains legal records, treaties, diplomatic agreements, and information concerning the governments and laws of Earth, alien civilizations, and interstellar powers. It serves as an important resource when Avengers operations involve extraterrestrial jurisdictions.

Security & Containment[]

  • Detention Facility: Constructed within the Progenitor's intestinal tract, the detention center provides secure temporary containment for captured super-villains and other dangerous individuals. Specialized cells can be adapted for prisoners possessing superhuman powers or unusual biological requirements.[17]
  • Armory: A heavily secured weapons and equipment repository containing Avengers technology and emergency equipment. Its inventory includes reserve Iron Man Armor, Captain America's Shields, the Wakanda Hulkbuster, and weapons originating from Earth and numerous extraterrestrial civilizations.[18]
  • Storage Facilities: Secure warehouses distributed throughout Avengers Mountain for storing mission equipment, replacement machinery, scientific materials, recovered technology, emergency supplies, vehicles, and other Avengers resources.

Infrastructure[]

  • Power Line to Earth's Core: A massive energy conduit extending deep beneath Avengers Mountain toward Earth's interior, providing access to geothermal and planetary energy that can supplement the headquarters' Celestial-derived power systems.
  • Celestial Circulatory Network: Portions of the Progenitor's original circulatory system have been partially restored and incorporated into Avengers Mountain's infrastructure. Cosmic energy generated by the remaining Celestial blood cells powers several of the headquarters' most advanced systems, most notably the Circulator.
  • Internal Corridors: An extensive network of reinforced hallways connects the Mountain's rooms, laboratories, residential areas, hangars, command facilities, and converted anatomical structures. These corridors operate alongside the Spinal Lift to provide conventional access throughout the enormous headquarters.
Together, these facilities make Avengers Mountain more than a headquarters. It functions simultaneously as an Avengers command center, fortress, residence, scientific complex, training academy, transportation hub, armory, hospital, prison, and planetary defense installation.
