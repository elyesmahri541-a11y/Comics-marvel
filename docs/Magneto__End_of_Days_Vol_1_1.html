Solicit Synopsis

An all-new, lavishly illustrated look at the life of one of the most popular and controversial characters in the history of comics…Magneto. His expansive story told from a fresh illuminating perspective. X-MEN collaborators Brian Michael Bendis and Andrea Sorrentino (X-MEN, OLD MAN LOGAN) reunite with Eisner-winning colorist Dave Stewart for this unique look at an explosive life of contradictions that has defined huge parts of the entire Marvel Universe. Issue #1 takes us to the near future where the choices Magneto has made play out in very surprising ways. That means X-Men guest stars galore! Magneto will be featured in the blockbuster Avengers: Doomsday film, and this series will give new and old readers a chance to dive into the character’s powerful legacy.
