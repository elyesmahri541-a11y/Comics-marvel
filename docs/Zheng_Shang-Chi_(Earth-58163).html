History

Shang-Chi was the leader of the Dragons gang. He fought against the Human Resistance Movement in a gang war that was interrupted by the Brotherhood.[1] Thanks to Misty Knight turning sides, he and the Human Resistance Movement were able to escape before they could be apprehended. Later, Luke Cage managed to convince him that they were better off as allies.[2]

Shang-Chi and the Dragons broke into a warehouse in an attempt to steal the Mandarin's ten rings, only to find that they were forgeries and his team had been set up by the Brotherhood. The raid saw the death of his teammate Swordsman. At the arrival of the NYPD, Shang-Chi resisted arrest, causing a cop to beat him into submission.[3] Shang-Chi was soon freed by Luke, along many other sapien prisoners. After one final confrontation with the Brotherhood, the Human Resistance Movement left victorious. The next day, Thunderbird, the Brotherhood's leader was told by Magneto to let the sapien issue resolve itself, while the resistance fighters met at Josie's Bar to celebrate.[4]
