Synopsis for "Demon in the Armor, Part 3 of 4"

While investigating the Mandarin International, Tony Stark discovers a connection between the terrorist organization and his father’s past dealings with companies in Hong Kong. A flashback recalls a conversation between Tony and Nick Fury at Howard Stark’s gravesite. Following the trail, Tony tracks the Mandarin to an island in the South China Sea , but his opponent’s advanced nanotechnology proves more than a match for the Iron Man armor, overwhelming its systems and leaving Tony captured.
