Solicit Synopsis

CITIZEN V: UNMASKED!

The new Citizen V has been stalking Tony Stark. Is he friend of foe? Can Citizen V protect Iron Man from Whiplash’s final attack? And why has he taken on Baron Zemo’s old Thunderbolts name? Finally, this issue: Learn all his secrets and history with Iron Man!

Trivia

  • This issue's uncolored cover was inadvertently leaked by the comic art agency Felix Comic Art in early July 2026, weeks before it was formally announced during San Diego Comic-Con 2026. As representatives of cover artist Ryan Stegman, Felix Comic Art put up for sale several of his uncolored artwork, which included the covers for Iron Man (Vol. 7) #1–11.[1]
  • Artist InHyuk Lee has shared a process collage for his variant cover for this issue.[2]
