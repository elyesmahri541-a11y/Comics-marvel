History

Early Life & F.E.A.S.T.[]

Ricardo Villanueva is a resident of New York City who began dedicated volunteer work at the F.E.A.S.T. shelter network.[1] Known for his compassionate nature and steady presence, he took on community support responsibilities within the organization.

Relationship with May Parker[]

Through his volunteer work at F.E.A.S.T., Ricardo met May Parker (Aunt May). Over time, their shared commitment to helping others led to a close friendship that eventually blossomed into a romantic relationship.[1] Ricardo became a key supportive figure in May's personal life.
