MARVEL KADOKAWA

MARVEL KADOKAWA was a line of web manga launched by Kadokawa in collaboration with Marvel Comics. The line focused reimaginings of origins of various Marvel superheroes retold for modern Japanese sensibilities.[1] The first chapters for the line's initial five titles all launched on October 10, 2026, with further chapters being released weekly.
