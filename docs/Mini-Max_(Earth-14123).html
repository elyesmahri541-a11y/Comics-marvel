Personality

Mini-Max's personality is intentionally programmed to emulate many qualities of stereotypical superhero traits: such as his inflated sense and love of heroism, self-monologuing and speaking in a bold tone. Per his programming, he ensures that Fred causes as little collateral damage as possible, and he has a good sense of responsibility; telling Fred not to use his boot jets to toast marshmallows and reminding him about his parent's rules concerning pets in the house.
