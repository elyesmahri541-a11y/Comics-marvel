History

A former drug addict and mother to a baby she neglected which was taken from her by Jack Monroe. She later was taken to a clinic Giscard Epurer[1], who revealed he would train her to kill Jack Monroe[2], and eventually she set out to get her baby back by any means necessary. She hunted Jack to New Orleans, chasing the Undergrounder Blue Note to get information on him.[3] Following Jack, she steals Bucky back from him, injuring a clerk in the process. When he finds her, they fight, though Jack does not want to, and ultimately leaves Bucky with her.[4]

She was later killed by the Neo-Nazi 88.[5]
