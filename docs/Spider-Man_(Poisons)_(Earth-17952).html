History

Poison Spider-Man was the poison who assimilated the Venomized Spider-Man of the Resistance.[3] He fought against the Venom of the Prime Marvel Universe until he was overpowered by the rest of the Resistance and chose to flee.[2]

During Earth-41940's Venom initiation battle into the Resistance, the fight was brought to his reality. The poisons allied with Thanos in exchange for him sparing their species if he managed to destroyed half the universe. They were sent to obtain the Mind Stone from Iron Man, but were defeated by the combined forces of the Avengers, Resistance, and Spider-Men.

Stuck on Earth-41940, the poisons needed a new homeworld. They chose Klynatr, sending Poison Spider-Man and Poison Rhino to uproot their government. They locked up most of the symbiote in the Symbiote Prison. Riot had evaded capture and was able to free its brethren, starting the Poison/Symbiote War. In the end, they agreed to a truce, giving the Poisons their own territory.[1]

He was later one of the many poisons defending the Hive's ship in Earth-22249. He was killed by Venom of the Prime Marvel Universe as their final showdown.[4]
