History

Spider-Man: The New Animated Series (a.k.a. MTV's Spider-Man) was an animated series featuring Spider-Man, which ran for one season across 13 episodes. The show was made using computer-generated imagery (CGI) rendered in cel shading and was produced by Mainframe Entertainment (Executive Producer Brian Michael Bendis) for Sony Pictures Television and broadcast on MTV, albeit aired out of order.

The series was originally sold as being a continuation of the 2002 film Spider-Man.

Trivia

  • A 10-page digital comic tie-in by Brian Michael Bendis and Terry Dodson was in production, but seemingly was never released. The story would connect Spider-Man (2002 film) and this animated show.
