History

The Avengers shared a similar history to their Prime Universe counterpart. It was revealed to the Defenders that the Avengers and multiple other superbeings occurred on Earth because the Omegas had collected the ideas of beings from across the Multiverse and created the Concordance Engines to help mold the Earth to bring them all into one place to prevent the destruction of the universe by the Death Celestials.[1]
