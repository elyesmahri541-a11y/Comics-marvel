Professional History

Adams first broke into the American comic book industry with the 1985 Marvel Comics miniseries Longshot. His subsequent work includes a number of Marvel's major books, including The Uncanny X-Men, Excalibur, X-Factor, Fantastic Four, Hulk, and Ultimate X.
