Synopsis for "Skin Deep, Part Four"

Spider-Man discovers that Charlie has already committed two murders and set his house on fire. He confronts him but evades him, and after the fight, he faints. In his dream, he discovers the solution to stopping Charlie. Obtaining the necessary equipment from Tony, he manages to imprison Charlie by solidifying his armor. Unfortunately, he discovers that Weidermann has destroyed Aunt May's house.

Notes

  • Charlie burns down Peter's apartment and Aunt May's house.

Trivia

While hiding from Charlie, May and Mary Jane go to the Quesada Hotel, a reference to Joe Quesada.
