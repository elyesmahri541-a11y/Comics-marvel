History

Shaw accepts and had Tessa contact the White and Black Knights. Per the rules of the challenge, these soldiers acted as proxies for the dispute. While the White Knights were sent to eliminate J. Jonah Jameson, the Black Knights were tasked with stopping his assassination. At that moment, two White Knights teleported into the office to eliminate him. While the White Knights were distracted with Jonah, Peter slipped away so he could change into Spider-Man. While back at the Daily Bugle, the White Knights cornered Jameson in his office. When the wall-crawler tried to web-up the two White Knights, they teleported away.[1]

The mutants were drawn to the Daily Bugle where Spider-Man was busy fighting off the Black Knights. While he was distracted, the White Knights returned and blasted Jameson, sending him crashing out a window. That was when the Black and White Knights caught up and tried to take Jameson. Archangel and Psylocke tried to stop the White Knights but they managed to evade the two X-Men with their teleportation abilities. They managed to kill one of the Black Knights and grab Jameson. Archangel attempted to strike the two would-be assassins with his poison-tipped feathers, but they teleported away. He rejoined the X-Men who took down the last of the White and Black Knights.[1]
