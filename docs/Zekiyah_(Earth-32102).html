History

A young woman with aspirations of becoming a Michelin Star Chef, Zekiyah moved to New York City to begin her career. However, cost of living caused her financial hardship. Feeling cornered, a former member of Maki Matsumoto's organization offered her an opportunity as part of her Diabolical Darts. Seeing this as a Robin Hood-like chance to steal from the wealthy, Zekiyah agreed. However, after rising through the ranks to become Lady Bullseye's right hand, she realised she had strayed too far from her dream and left the organization.

Unfortunately, having already acquired a criminal record, Zekiyah had no opportunity to pursue baking once again and was forced to become a villain under the name Quick Whip where she repeatedly eluded Moon Girl. When Moon Girl finally apprehended Quick Whip, she intended to take her to S.H.I.E.L.D. headquarters uptown, but was pursued by Lady Bullseye and her minions attempting to capture Quick Whip for leaving their organisation.

After helping Moon Girl subdue the other villains, Lunella spoke with Agent Hill and Zekiyah became the inaugural member of Moon Girl's Good Word program, getting a job at a local bakery where she made a cake for the heroes.[1]

Attributes

Abilities

Quick Whip is skilled both in the use of whips in combat, and quick wit, repeatedly tricking others into letting down their guard in order to make a hasty escape. She is a skilled thief and lock picker, quickly escaping Moon Girl's handcuffs and Gotcha Glove in a matter of moments. She is also a skilled pâtissier, creating fusion desserts and baked goods.

Weaknesses

Quick Whip is an ordinary human with ordinary human weaknesses.

Paraphernalia

Weapons

Two manacles containing extendable metal whips; temporarily Moon Girl's Bubble Blaster.
