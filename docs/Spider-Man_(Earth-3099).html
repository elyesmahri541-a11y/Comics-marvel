History

In a far future where history was suppressed, Spider-Man believed himself to be the first to carry that name. He was summoned Abyssus to Earth-2099 to fight as one of his Abyssal Warriors. His natural immunity to the symbiotes allowed him to resist the bloodlust of the Knull seeds, which led him to abandon the Abyssal Warriors and side with the Champions of 2099, where he got to learn from his counterpart, Spider-Man 2099.[2]
