Solicit Synopsis

THE MARCH TO HULK WAR CONTINUES AS INFERNAL HULK CORRUPTS ALL IN HIS PATH!

The FANTASTIC FOUR and the BAXTER BUILDING have fallen to INFERNAL HULK, and NEW YORK is under siege! When the RICHARDS KIDS are taken too, SPIDER-MAN disobeys orders and goes on a suicide mission to save them at all costs…and to get the AVENGERS the weapon they need to stop Infernal Hulk and prevent the AGE OF MONSTERS. Hulk’s inevitable INFERNAL AVENGERS come one step closer to fruition in this unmissable penultimate issue!
