History

When he stumbled upon the original War Machine Armor when it resurfaced on Earth after being lost in the timestream, mercenary Parnell Jacobs brought it to Stuart Clarke. Clarke in turn suggested to bring it to Sunset Bain.[1] Bain had the War Machine Armor recreated with her company's own technology.[2]

Adopting the alias of War Machine, Jacobs used the armor to attack Astrodyne Systems for Bain's benefit. He was opposed by Iron Man and Warbird, but succeeded in his objective and destroyed Astrodyne's building. Iron Man tried to use a Negator Pack on War Machine, since he thought the suit to be the original armor, but was surprised to find the device having no effect on his enemy. Somehow, probably due to Clarke's help, the armor lacked any Stark technology.[3]

Jacobs continued with partnership with Bain, and was outfitted with an exoskeleton add-on to his suit.[4] War Machine confronted Iron Man again when Stark was investigating Baintronics.[1] War Machine almost defeated Iron Man, until Jim Rhodes, the former owner of the War Machine Armor and Jacobs' old military partner, convinced him to spare Iron Man for an owed favor. Jacobs quit working for Bain on the spot and ejected from the exoskeleton, flying away with the regular armor for himself.[5]
