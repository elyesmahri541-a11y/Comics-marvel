History

This Doom was a Man-Thing, as were all people on his world. His home reality of Earth-91 was attacked by Doom Supreme and his Masters of Evil in the past. With the deaths of prehistoric Avengers, it was overwritten with Earth-35521. Doom Supreme pulled Doom-Thing out of the timestream before he ceased to exist and subjected him to mental and physiological torture, turning him into one of his followers.[1]
