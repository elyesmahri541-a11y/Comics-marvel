Wonder Man is a superhero comedy television series created by Destin Daniel Cretton and Andrew Guest, and produced by Marvel Studios for Disney+. It is set in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, and follows struggling actors Simon Williams and Trevor Slattery as they try to earn roles in a remake of a superhero film.

History

On June 16, 2022, it was reported that a Wonder Man series was in development.[1] The series was released in its entirety on January 27, 2026.[2] On March 23, 2026, the show was renewed for a second season due to high critical and fan acclaim.[3] That decision was reversed and the show was cancelled four months later on July 30, 2026.[4]
