History

Dazzler was a mutant singer and performer. Phin Mason convinced Miles Morales to attend a concert with her and Lila Cheney performing, after which he became a fan of hers.[1]

A poster for her Revolution Tour could be seen in the Princess Bar in Lowtown.[2]

After the Helix Sentinels brought destruction across the globe, mutant hate grew worldwide, causing Dazzler and Lila to cancel their reunion tour.[2]

Trivia

  • Among her songs included "Last Stand in Discoland," "Turn Up The Light," "Sounds of Light and Fury," and "Songbird."[2]
