History

Jonathan Brace was a lab technician at Adarco, after his research grant kept being rejected he tried to sabotage the lab, that only resulted in his hands being irreparably damaged. Adarco struck a deal with him, that if he agreed to be part of an experimental surgery the company would give him cybernetic hands and support him for life. The surgery slowly destroyed his body, forcing the doctors to replace more and more his body parts with synthetic ones, by the end Brace had 80% of his body replaced.[1]
