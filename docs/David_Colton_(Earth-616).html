David Colton was born a frail, asthmatic and short kid. After he witnessed the September 11th attacks, David enlisted to the Army. Constantly bullied by the other recruits, his fate changed after a brutal beating by his sergeant. He was chosen to undergo the Super-Soldier Serum process.[4] New and fresh, Colton was dubbed "Captain America" and was deployed to both Afghanistan[3] and Iraq,[5] marking the beginning of his descent into cynicism.[6]

Years later, Colton was sent into Latveria on a rescue mission, with two members of the new Howling Commandos and a recently unfrozen Captain America.[3] After he discovered the US backed Doctor Doom's overthrow,[6] he finally snapped, taking on a barracks full of Latverian military and starting a fight with Captain America. Luckily, Colton survived the attack and was saved with Tony Stark's patented heart-saving technology.[7]

When he learned about Primewarrior, he started covertly attacking the labs and rescued a kid named Tyler Torrens alongside Wolverine.[8] However, Colton was shot during the escape, and Wolverine used an Origin Box that Tyler had stolen from Primewarrior to save his life, giving Colton the powers of Miracleman from Earth-6160.[9] Following his new power boost, Colton joined the Avengers[10] against the Red Hulk.[11] After Ross explodes, killing over 9,000 people, Colton, now transformed into Miracleman, fill with grief went helping the damage Ross caused,[12] and start searching answers about why he has developed those new powers.[13]

History

New Captain America[]

David Colton witnessed the September 11th attack firsthand. After what happened, he wanted to serve, but when he went enlist, he was rejected by the the United States Army because of his severe persistent asthma and frail physique. But this tenacious approach from Colton, gets the attention of one of the nearby sergeants, and he ended up getting in. On a phone call with his mom, she was unhappy with the idea, believing David was only driven by revenge, especially since he had already lost his older brother Brian in Kuwait. Given his size, David's mother thought he was just getting ready to get himself killed, and even proposed therapy. Some of the other recruits caught David crying on the phone and proceeded to threaten him with a beating, which he received. Later in the night, the sergeant found David with his flashlight on after lights out. He snatched him up and drag him outside and beat him up, saying that he was a mistake and an embarrassment and how the other recruits were right when they said David was going to get them killed. In the end, despite trying to fight back, David was knocked out.[4]

When he woke up, struggling to reach his inhaler, the sergeant was right there. Impressed by David not having stood down in the fight, he proposed a cure to his asthma with a replica of the Super-Soldier Serum,[4] actually coming from Primewarrior.[14] New and improved, David was first deployed to Afghanistan as the new Captain America.[3]

Afghanistan[]

While serving in Afghanistan, David rejoiced in the fall of the Taliban, believing that the people were finally free. Surprised that his fellow soldiers weren't celebrating, he questioned Qasim, a local who had been translating for them for months and David had come to trust him. Qasim told him that in the two months they had been there, America hadn't actually done any fighting; they had only dropped bombs. David truly believed that through their actions they have restored democracy to the region. Qasim was unconvinced. Where David has had violence brought to him, Qasim had grown up in it. Through civil wars and Soviet incursions, Qasim knew firsthand that violence only gives the illusion of victory. One day, David noticed something was off about Qasim when he spotted a man outside a building. Trained to suspect the worst, David tracked Qasim's phone to a neighborhood that David had never been to before. That's when he heard gunshots go off. David would fight his way through the building only to find Qasim bleeding to death. David would learn in that moment that the peace they had achieved was a total lie. The men David saw earlier in front of the building were working for a warlord who was in power before the Taliban, whose warlord had killed Qasim's sister. Shortly after, the military left the country.[3]

Iraq[]

Instead of coming home, David's new destination was Iraq, where the US government claimed to have found evidence of weapons of mass destruction. Whilst in Iraq, it was clear that his time abroad had begun to change David. Whilst his team talked about the downfall of Saddam Hussein, David was the only one quiet while the squad called the people ungrateful for their intervention. David knew that while, yes, they had taken out a dictator, all they'd really achieved was destabilizing the nation. This had taken a toll on his psyche. While the squad argued, David noticed a watching child and told the soldiers to rein it in. Out of nowhere, a car rammed into them and exploded. Death surrounded David. Most of his squad was dead, and while David was trying to help, his allies were shooting kids. David thought of his mom, apologized to her, and then drove his shield straight into the soldier's mouth and beat him to death.[5] Back in America, the sergeant who turned David into a super-soldier pressured his therapist into clearing him for duty, secured all of the footage of the incident, and assured him that he was still Captain America.[6]

After several years, David returned home, finally getting to see his mom. When they talked, she mourned that his entire life had been surrounded by death ever since 9/11. One day, David's mom came home to find him gone, with a book and a number for her to call him while he was abroad.[7]

Latveria[]

Shortly after Steve Rogers had been unfrozen, General Thaddeus Ross sent both Captains America to Latveria to rescue US diplomats from Victor von Doom. As they landed, satellites showed a clear path. When Steve questioned the satellites, David told him that America now watches everything and carries a big stick. They were interrupted by what appeared to be Doom, but was actually the rebels' with their own Doombot. They took them back to their base. Marius Wolf, one of the rebels, told them that Doom was vulnerable. They could sneak the ambassadors under the cover of night or they could just go kill Doom and walk across the border of a free Latveria, a big change to their plans that David did not approve. Rogers quickly intervened and took Colton outside to calm down. Rogers mediated with Colton by offering himself to help Wolf to take down Doom and to serve as a distraction while Colton and the Howlers went to free the hostages.[3]

Meanwhile, Colton, the Howlers, and the resistance stormed the U.S. embassy where it appeared to be lightly guarded. They breached the main room and found Latverian soldiers carrying the corpses of the diplomats. After neutralizing the soldiers, Colton found there were only ten bodies. He then threatened a Latverian soldier for the whereabouts of the other two hostages.[5] They fought their way to the two remaining hostages, with one of them being a little girl named Ainsley. It turned out the US backed Doom's takeover and that the embassy helped arrange it all, and the actual mission was to make sure none of that evidence made it out of Latveria. David was once again put into a situation where his orders directly conflicted what he believed to be right. From the perspective of his team, the job's the job. David didn't take this well, turning on his team and did everything he could to save the little girl. David chased down his teammate Slate and threw his shield into his former partner, dropping her to save Ainsley.[6]

Rogers entered the barracks to find Colton standing among dead Latverian soldiers. Colton told Rogers that he had no choice but they had kept losing their way, bringing “freedom”. He was correcting that by killing the soldiers who oppressed their people. Rogers tried to reason with Colton, stating that those soldiers were scared people who wanted to defend their nation, and they were in a sovereign nation that they cannot change by force, especially not by killing in cold blood. But Colton replied that he had too much blood on his hands, and that his actions have to mean something, and even if it means to keep bleeding until Latveria earned its freedom from Doom. Colton spotted a living soldier and threw his shield at him, only for Rogers to intercept it. This provoked a fight between Rogers and Colton until the former had the upper hand.[7]

After knocking Colton to the ground, Slate crept up behind them and attempted to gun them down, but Colton picked up Rogers's shield and blocked the bullets until Slate's gun ran out of ammunition. Colton threw the shield into Slate's head before suddenly collapsing in pain with his heart giving out from the stress enhanced by his super-soldier serum. Just as Ross's evacuation team arrived, Colton informed Rogers about the U.S. government's role in supporting Doom's coup. One month later, Ross was observing Colton undergoing surgery in which he is implanted with heart-saving technology granted by Tony Stark to counter the serum and thereby saving his life.[7]

Underground[]

Colton had been living in isolation since the Latverian mission. When he learned about Primewarrior, a private military operation who was using Weapon Plus research to create new super soldiers, he started covertly attacking the labs.[14] After freeing a super soldier subject, a mutant named Tyler Torrens, Colton gave him the names and info on his torturers. Soon after, Colton was tracked down by Wolverine and Teri O'Barnes, who was tipped off to Primewarrior and wanted to help that test subject, the kid with a mutant healing ability. Unfortunately, Wolverine led Nuke, Primewarrior's attack dog, to Colton's location.[15]

After Colton's safe house was destroyed, he joined Wolverine tracking down the super soldier. They tracked him back to Primewarrior, where he was looking for answeres and revenge. Standing between them and the kid was an enhanced clone of Nuke.[16] He was too much for them to handle until Nuke caught up with them and took out his own clone. He nearly turned on the others until Colton talked Nuke into being the soldier he always wanted to be.[9]

Nuke killed the leader of Primewarrior himself, saving the escaped super soldier from having to do it. The four super-soldiers still needed to escape. Fighting their way through more enhanced soldiers, Colton hesitated to use the shield due to his PTSD, leaving him open to getting shot. With no other choice to save Colton, Wolverine used an Origin Box from Earth-6160 that Primewarrior had in its possession to heal Colton and gave him the powers of an unknown hero from Earth-6160.[9]

Armageddon[]

Following his new power boost, Colton continued to shut down super soldier programs. Upon arriving at the Pentagon to destroy yet another program, he was intercepted by Captain Marvel, Captain America, the Vision, and Wolverine. Blasted by Captain Marvel and launched in the sky, Colton proceeded to knock her down with one punch, much to her surprise. Captain America and Wolverine then tried to recruit him for a mission to Latveria, now renamed "New America," to stop the Red Hulk, who was planning to remake other governments to his standard. He agreed, as long as they would not stop him from destroying the Pentagon's current super soldier experiment, and joined the Avengers.[10]

Surprised by Colton's immense powers, despite having a machine that could predict the future, the Red Hulk moved the battlefield to Kronlia, expecting Colton to hold back in a populated city. However, Colton brutally beat him anyway with no regard to collateral damage or casualties, nearly killing Ross but was stopped by the Avengers. Colton criticized the Avengers for being weak and flew off.[11]

Colton then began attacking nuclear bases around the world and seizing their warheads, before flying off to hurl them into the Sun. Heroes such as Captain Marvel, the Fantastic Four, and Namor tried to stop him, but with little success. Wolverine and the Winter Soldier subsequently visited Colton at his secluded cabin to confront him about his actions. While Wolverine disapproved of Colton's course of action, the Winter Soldier sided with him. Determined to put a stop to it all, Wolverine attempted to attack Colton; Colton grabbed him and propelled them both into space, where he warned Logan that he had no desire to harm him or the Avengers, but would do so if they tried to stop him. With Barnes's help, Colton retrieved the last of the nuclear warheads. As the two men talked Captain Marvel launched a surprise attack on Colton but was once again easily taken down, while Iron Man and Vision arrive to back Carol up.[17]

As a side effect of his newly acquired extradimensional superpowers, Colton began having visions of his mother and experiencing both a persistent subconscious urge to set things right and the certainty that he harbored something else within him—all while the Avengers, the Fantastic Four, and the X-Men intercepted the launched nuclear missiles. At that precise moment, the Winter Guard—along with Ross, transformed into Red Hulk—attacked him and Bucky. Upon coming face-to-face with the gamma-powered military man, Ross ended up exploding, as the Russian government had turned him into a nuclear weapon specifically designed to eliminate Colton. After barely surviving the explosion, another vision of his mother instructed him to utter a magic word; thus, Colton was completely reborn with the source of his powers—those of Miracleman.[2]

The explosion Ross generated kills 9,000 people, including the Winter Soldier, causing the United Nations to holds a meeting to create a plan to regulate superhumans. David Colton approached Wolverine in Russia, where he asked him to end his life because he didn't deserve his powers and due to his failure to stop Ross. Wolverine refuses and tells Colton not to let his failures weigh him down but learn from them and try to fix them if he can.[12]

Attributes

Powers

  • Super-Soldier Physiology: David Colton received a replicate of the Super-Soldier Serum from Primewarrior, enhancing his body above human abilities.[4]
    • Enhanced Strength: Colton possesses strength far exceeding that of a normal human, allowing him to lift heavy loads,[3] break structures,[5] and fight on equal terms with other super-soldiers[7] or powerful enemies.[9]
    • Enhanced Speed: Colton can run and move at high speeds. Wolverine noted that he was as quick as Steve Rogers.[14]
    • Enhanced Durability: His body can withstand major physical impacts, like an point blank range explosion.[5]
    • Healing Factor: Colton benefits from a rapid healing factor that allows him to recover from minor injuries in a matter of hours.[16]
  • Origin Box: After being injured, David was saved when Wolverine opened an Origin Box, granting him the powers of Miracleman from Earth-6160.[9][2] According to Captain Marvel, his power is comparable to the Sentry.[11]
    • Telekinetic Flight: Colton possesses the ability to fly at high-speeds.[10] Colton has proved capable of flying to space in a matter of seconds, surviving in the vacuum of space, and sitting in front of the Sun.[17]
    • Superhuman Strength: Colton has displayed an extremely high level of strength, equaling, if not surpassing the likes of Red Hulk.[18] He is capable of hurting powerful opponents such as Red Hulk to the point of make him turn back into his human form,[11] Captain Marvel twice,[2] once with a single punch,[10] and Spider-Man.[13] With a single touch, he destroyed Iron Man's armor.[11]
    • Nigh-Invulnerability: Colton's body can withstand handgun fire, as bullets bounce off his body and he showed no reaction to Captain Marvel's blasts.[10] He also showed no reaction to being knocked down by the Red Hulk, and instantly got up.[11] Despite his great durability extremely powerful forces can be fatal, as when the Red Hulk detonated and inflicted lethal damage to his original body.[2] Spider-Man's blow barely did something to him.[13]
    • Superhuman Senses: Colton possesses vastly enhanced senses. He could detect the Winter Soldier sneaking behind him.[11] He was able to hear a conversation between Iron Man and Mister Fantastic from an unknown distance.[17]
    • Body Switch: By saying the word "kimota" David was able to switch his dying body for a new one.[2]

Abilities

  • Master Combatant: His training incorporates the close combat methods of the American special forces, capable of easily defeating soldiers, and even exchanging a few blows with Steve Rogers.[7]
  • Master Shield Fighter: He possesses the reflexes and precision necessary to wield his shield with deadly efficiency, including complex ricochet throws.[3]
  • Master Weaponry: Although he primarily uses the shield in a manner similar to Steve Rogers (ricochet throws, defensive blocks, blunt force strikes), his training includes proficiency with firearms and modern tactical weapons.[14][15]

Paraphernalia

Equipment

  • Arc Reactor: Iron Man replaced Colton's weak heart with an arc reactor.[7]

Weapons

  • Captain America's Shield: While operating as Captain America Colton was given a replica of Roger's shield made of an unknown metal.[14]

Notes

  • David's existence is possible due to the sliding timescale. Despite the Heroic Age having started in 1961's Fantastic Four #1, and Captain America (Vol. 3) being in publication during 9/11, the sliding timescale establishes that all events between circa Fantastic Four #1 and present day happened in a window of time that began 15 years in the past. As such, when Captain America (Vol. 14) began publication in 2025, Steve Rogers had resurfaced from being frozen in ice around 2010.
