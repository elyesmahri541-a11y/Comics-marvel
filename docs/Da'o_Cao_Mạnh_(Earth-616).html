History

Da'o was a businesswoman from Vietnam and the half-sister of Karma of the X-Men.[citation needed]

Da'o was a mutant but was later depowered on M-Day. [1]

At some point, she took on the name Susan Hatchi and started a company, Hatchi Corporation, which sold advanced firearms, body armor, and ammunition to groups like S.H.I.E.L.D..[2]

During a standoff in which she held a gun on her half-sister and their father held a gun on Da'o, her sister brought Da'o inside her mind to show her the suffering they shared. While Karma was doing this, however, their father shot and killed her sister.[3]
