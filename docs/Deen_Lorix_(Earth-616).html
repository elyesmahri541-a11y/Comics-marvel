History

The man that who went by Deen Lorix was small scoundrel when he was recruited into the Nova Corps. With time, he became disillusioned with the Corps and began using his position for organized criminal endeavours, eventually being dishonorably discharged.[1]

At some point, he began using the alias Eden Rixlo and stole a telepathically-controlled ship, but wound up getting robbed himself, by the White Queen Emma Frost after trying to betray her; before leaving, she proceeded to erase most of the event from his memory. Without his full memory, it took him months, but he eventually found his way to the Solar System and confronted the White Queen.[1]

While having dinner aboard the ship, he betrayed her again and almost suceeded in killing her and the Marauders by ejecting them into space, but Iceman created an Ice sphere to protect them, and the rest of the team used their combined powers and smarts to stay alive and catch up with the ship. Kate Pryde then phased on-board and beat him up, before removing his psy-blockers, which allowed Frost to put an end to the adventure.[2]

Despite everything that ensued, Frost still decided to let him go, telling him he would get some money, and she would get the ship, thus concluding business between them.[3]

He was then hired by Star-Lord to steal Nova's ship.[4] Before betraying Star-lord to the crime syndicate known as the Kree-Skrull War and stealing his ship.[5]
