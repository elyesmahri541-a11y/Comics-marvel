Solicit Synopsis

Iron Man wasn’t always a hero. This Step 2 Step Into Reading leveled reader explores Tony Stark's journey to become the incredible Super Hero that he is today.

Tony Stark is a genius inventor and businessman. An unexpected accident leaves him with no choice but to create a suit that allows him to become Iron Man. Now Iron Man is a Super Hero, Avenger, and leader! Young readers will love learning about one of their favorite characters in this Step Into Reading leveled reader.

Step 2 readers use basic vocabulary and short sentences to tell simple stories. They are for children who recognize familiar words and can sound out new words with help.
