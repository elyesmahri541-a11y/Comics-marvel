History

3K[]

Joseph was a clone of Magneto that Astra created, different from another clone of Magneto she made, also named Joseph. He joined his creator as a member of 3K.[1]

When his mother wanted to obtain the role of Chairwoman of 3K after the Chairman briefly went silent, Joseph (now calling himself Magneto) supported her, attacking her rival Cassandra Nova, during a meeting, but he was quickly sedated by Nova and her apprentice, Myriad. The status quo went unchanged after the Chairman resurfaced and interrupted the fight.[2]

Joseph and Astra attended to Sebastian Shaw's Hellfire Gala. After Shaw was killed, the pair was framed for the murder, while the real killer, Emma Frost, wasn't apprehended. Joseph and Astra later teleported away.[4]

DNX[]

Joseph and the 3K X-Men attacked the X-Men's base as part of a diversion for the Chairman to confront Magneto at a temple. Despite Joseph being defeated, he and the 3K X-Men were teleported away when the Chairman completed his mission.[5]

When the X-Men attacked 3K's airship headquarters and Cassandra Nova was occupied with Quentin Quire, Astra opted not to have Joseph join the 3K X-Men in fighting the X-Men. They are then confronted by Human Torch and Thing who have been dispatched by Invisible Woman.[6] Despite initially defeating the Fantastic Four, Astra and Joseph were forced to flee the airship, leaving their team members behind, when the Human Torch seriously threatened to kill them by asphyxiation.[7]
