Earth-1048 is the universe that serves as the setting for various Marvel video games developed by Insomniac Games and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment for PlayStation consoles. It comprises Marvel's Spider-Man, Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales, Marvel's Spider-Man 2, and Marvel's Wolverine. The universe has also been expanded upon by various tie-in media such as the novels, comic books, and appearances in other media.[1]

History

In this reality, Peter Parker has been operating as Spider-Man for eight years. Assisted by NYPD Captain Yuri Watanabe, he helps combat crime in Manhattan. Following the fall of Wilson Fisk, the Kingpin of the underworld, the city was targeted by Mister Negative and his gang of Demons as part of a vendetta against Mayor Norman Osborn.

Following his initial defeat at the hands of Spider-Man with help from his partner and Daily Bugle reporter Mary Jane Watson, Mister Negative was broken out of the Raft and joined the plans of aggrieved scientist Doctor Octopus against Osborn, which involved the release of the plague known as Devil's Breath. In the process of tracking down an antidote for Devil's Breath, Spider-Man defeated some of his greatest foes, who had banded together as the Sinister Six, and restored order to the city by defeating countless of rioting Raft escapees. After defeating Doctor Octopus, a sample of the anti-serum in the villain's possession was used to synthesize more of it and restore the city to health.

Months later, during spring, Maggia leader Hammerhead collected several pendrives containing information on the Maggia Dons, and hired Felicia Hardy better known as The Black Cat. Felicia stole the pendrives inside The Maria, which was located in The Museum of Modern Art. That same night, Spider-Man was called by Mary Jane asking for help on her ongoing investigation of the return of the Maggia to the streets of New York.

Over an entire night, Peter finished all of the challenges that Screwball set up throughout the city for her Season One after getting out of jail, stopped different Maggia crimes, and found all of Walter Hardy, the original Black Cat's paintings by request of Detective Mackey, only to find out who he really was. He also continued to look for Felicia as she continued to steal USBs from different families across the city, and discovered, thanks to an encounter with her, that he had a possible son with her. At the end of the night, Peter discovered that his son never existed in the first place, and Black Cat was using him the whole time. In addition, Hammerhead managed to steal weapons that Sable International left behind after the Devil's Breath crisis, using it to arm his mafia and try to take over the city. Hammerhead also found something called "Project Olympus", a project made by Sable International to create super soldiers using mechanical exoskeletons, transforming himself into a cyborg.

Upon discovering that she had been stolen from, Silver Sable returned to New York City looking to retrieve her company's stolen equipment, and to kill Hammerhead. Spider-Man, who was also after Hammerhead at the time, tried to convince Sable to work alongside him to take down Hammerhead once and for all. However, due to an unexpected change of events, Sable was captured by Hammerhead, but was saved shortly thereafter by Spider-Man. Teaming up again, both Spider-Man and Sable managed to defeat Hammerhead after discovering his weakness to heat, thanks to Black Cat, who appeared again to give Peter one of the stolen pendrives that contained this information. Sable later returns to Symkaria with her retrieved stolen equipment.[2]

Around a year after the Devil's Breath crisis, Miles Morales, who had helped during the crisis alongside Spider-Man and Mary Jane, received powers after a spider similar to the one that bit Peter eight years prior bit him. Having been intensively trained over a year by the original Spider-Man, Miles took up the mantle alongside his mentor and quickly established himself as a unique version of the hero. When the original Spider-Man took a "working vacation," the responsibility of stopping a brewing battle between the Underground gang and corrupt energy corporation Roxxon fell at Morales' feet. The new Spider-Man took the challenge in stride, managing to fight back both sides and even save the city from a critical energy reactor which would have vaporized all of Harlem.[3]

A year later, the city of New York became the center of one last hunt orchestrated by Russian hunter and mercenary Kraven the Hunter, who chose to die in battle rather than from the cancer ravaging his body, aided by his army of soldiers of fortune. The Spider-Men teamed up to battle Kraven's threat, while a new street vigilante, Wraith, emerged to fight the arsonist Cult of the Flame and Harry Osborn returned to establish the Emily-May Foundation while serving as host to an extraterrestrial symbiote keeping his illness in check.[4]

Trivia

  • In a crossover with the mainstream Spider-Man after the events of the first game, 1048 Peter confirms that with the Avengers usually gone saving the world, there are usually half a dozen heroes in Manhattan. Considering this is before the Fantastic Four or X-Men were active, and Peter hasn't met Doctor Strange yet, that means that in addition to Peter there are five other street-level heroes regularly in New York, before Miles became a hero as well.
