History

During a nightmare caused by the homonymous magic entity, Spider-Man dreamt his aunt married the Green Goblin.[1]

Notes

  • In an early draft, May was depicted as a stripper with J. Jonah Jameson and Electro looking on, though in the final release the scene was changed to her marrying the Green Goblin instead.[2]
