History

Merrick trained Target as a mercenary when he was younger.[1]

Merrick was hired by General Coy to find people that were capable of guarding the Master Form.[1]

Merrick went to Rumika along with Target to drop off the Master Form for it to be safekept there for the sake of the world.[2]

Upon learning that a large ship with Mercenaries was detected headed towards Rumika he was ordered to do what "had to be done".

After having dropped off the Master Form he left the island for some time by the time he came back to check on it the village had been attacked and the Master Form stolen, Wolverine then reveals Merrick knew the people that attacked the village of Rumika, upon said reveal Target killed him.[3]
