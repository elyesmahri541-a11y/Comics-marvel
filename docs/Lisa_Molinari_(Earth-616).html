The multi-armed femme fatale known as Coat of Arms is Lisa Molinari, an artist who turned superheroism itself into her canvas. Empowered by the magical Tiboro's Coat, Lisa can manifest four additional arms and fly, abilities she used as a founding member of the Young Masters, a group that initially masqueraded as a new incarnation of the Young Avengers.[2]

After being released from juvenile detention, Lisa struggled to find employment until she discovered the mysterious Coat waiting for her in an alleyway. Rather than simply becoming a conventional superhero, she treated her new identity and the activities of her team as an ongoing art project examining the blurred line between heroes and villains. Lisa was also fascinated by Norman Osborn, particularly his notorious career as the Green Goblin, which inspired some of her artwork and eventually brought her into direct contact with him.[3]

Her adventures reunited her with Speed, whom she had known in juvenile detention and with whom she developed a mutual attraction. Despite being offered provisional membership in the genuine Young Avengers, Lisa remained loyal to her own teammates. She later worked with Jeremy Briggs, where her growing knowledge of magical artifacts proved useful, before returning to the Young Masters when they became affiliated with the Masters of Evil in Bagalia.[4][5]

History

Early Life[]

Lisa Molinari spent time in juvenile detention, where she knew Tommy Shepherd the reincarnated son of Scarlet Witch and Vision. After being paroled, she struggled to adjust to ordinary life and find employment, believing she was destined for something greater. Shortly afterwards, Lisa discovered a mysterious coat displayed on a rack in an alleyway beneath graffiti asking whether superheroes were good. Upon taking the garment, she discovered that it allowed her to manifest four additional arms and fly. Rather than simply treating the Coat as a source of power, Lisa saw its appearance as an opportunity to explore superheroism itself through her art.[6]

Lisa pursued art professionally and obtained materials from Cowley Fabrications, a business that produced both art installations and equipment for superhumans. There she encountered Norman Osborn, whose career as the Green Goblin fascinated her. Osborn initially admired Lisa's work and had purchased several of her pieces, but their encounter soured when she showed him artwork inspired by his Green Goblin identity. Unknown to Lisa, Osborn subsequently used the business card he gave her to monitor her activities.[3]

Young Masters[]

Adopting the identity Coat of Arms, Lisa joined Melter, Executioner, Big Zero, Enchantress, and Egghead in a group that initially called itself the Young Avengers. Lisa conceived the team's activities as a form of living or performance art, using actual superheroics to explore the distinction between heroes and villains and the way those roles were perceived by others. Even the team's interventions in crimes became part of the larger artistic concept rather than being motivated solely by conventional heroism.[7]

Their use of the Young Avengers name soon brought them into conflict with the established team. Rather than immediately dismissing the newcomers, the Young Avengers evaluated them individually to determine whether any could genuinely function as heroes. Lisa was paired with Speed against apparent Hydra forces and used the encounter to explain how she had found the Coat and why she had turned superheroism into an artistic project.[6]

The encounter also reunited Lisa with Tommy. Their earlier attraction resurfaced despite their opposing loyalties, and Speed encouraged her to abandon her teammates and join the genuine Young Avengers. Following the tests, Coat of Arms and Enchantress were the only members of Lisa's group offered provisional membership. Although Lisa was tempted by the possibility, she was unwilling to simply leave Melter and the others behind. Her loyalty to the group ultimately outweighed the opportunity to become part of an established superhero team.[4]

Lisa nevertheless continued spending time with Speed. She told him that she believed something significant was approaching and wanted to witness and document it as part of her work, illustrating the extent to which she continued to view the events surrounding both teams through the lens of an artist as well as a participant.[8]

Osborn's surveillance meanwhile revealed that Lisa's garment was Tiboro's Coat, an enchanted object associated with the extra-dimensional sorcerer Tiboro. After Melter contacted Osborn, the resulting confrontation drew the Young Avengers, Lisa's team, and members of the Dark Avengers into battle. Lisa initially remained outside the fighting, but intervened when Sentry attacked. Producing a mystical chain from one of the Coat's pockets, she helped Wiccan and Enchantress bind Sentry before he was banished through a magical vortex.[9]

Afterwards, Lisa and Speed simultaneously tried to persuade one another to switch teams. Their exchange made clear that neither had abandoned their feelings for the other, but neither was willing to give up their chosen path. They kissed before parting on good terms, and Lisa left with her teammates, who continued operating under the name "Young Masters".[10] Following Osborn's fall from power, Lisa remained with the Young Masters while the group hid at the Invisible Manor.[11]

Briggs Chemical[]

Lisa later joined Big Zero and Enchantress in working for Jeremy Briggs at Briggs Chemical LLC. Briggs had developed Clean Slate, a process capable of removing superhuman abilities, and planned to use it on the world's superhuman population before restoring powers only to those he considered responsible enough to possess them. Briggs claimed that Lisa and her teammates had repeatedly submitted to the process themselves to prove their commitment. Lisa supported the plan, regarding the destruction of the traditional division between heroes and villains as an extension of the ideas that had first inspired her career as Coat of Arms.[12] Because Clean Slate was ineffective against powers derived from magic, Lisa was tasked with examining mystical objects. In her mystic study at the Briggs Building, she investigated the Fossilized Amulet belonging to Reptil and the Amulets of Power belonging to White Tiger, seeking to understand why such artifacts could not be neutralized by Briggs' technology.[13]

When members of the Avengers Academy attempted to recover the stolen artifacts, Lisa used Reptil's and White Tiger's own amulets against them. The artifacts initially responded to Lisa, who argued that mystical objects were not passive tools but formed relationships with their wielders. According to Lisa, the wielder changed the artifact while the artifact in turn changed the person using it.[14] Lisa cited Tiboro's Coat as proof of this belief. She acknowledged that wearing the Coat had changed her since the day she discovered it, but did not regard that influence as harmful. Instead, she believed that both she and the Coat had become better through their relationship. Her willingness to accept the nature of the magical objects also temporarily allowed her to use Reptil's and White Tiger's artifacts more effectively against their owners.[14] During the battle, Lisa combined the powers of the stolen amulets with the additional arms provided by Tiboro's Coat, fighting several of the Academy students at once. Reptil and White Tiger ultimately re-established their respective bonds with their artifacts, causing them to reject Lisa and return to their original wielders. With Briggs' forces defeated, his attempt to impose Clean Slate on the world's superhuman population was stopped.[14]

Masters of Evil[]

Coat of Arms later returned to the Young Masters, who had become affiliated with the Masters of Evil in Bagalia. Lisa and the other young villains operated from Constrictor's Snakepit, where Constrictor acted as an employer and mentor. The Young Masters enjoyed the relative freedom offered by Bagalia, but were also sent on assignments on behalf of the Masters of Evil.[5] Lisa remained with the group as several survivors of Murderworld, including Chase Stein and Death Locket, became involved with the Young Masters. She continued operating with them as their activities expanded beyond Bagalia and the Murderworld survivors' conflict with the Masters of Evil ultimately brought the organization into confrontation with the Avengers and their allies.[15]

Attributes

Powers

Lisa Molinari possesses no innate superhuman powers. Her superhuman abilities are derived from Tiboro's Coat.[4]

Abilities

  • Artistry: Lisa is a talented artist whose work has explored superheroes, supervillains, and the nature of superheroism. She extended these concepts into her activities as Coat of Arms, treating her superhuman career as part of an ongoing artistic project.[7]
  • Knowledge of Magic: Lisa possesses knowledge of magical artifacts and their relationship with their wielders. While working for Jeremy Briggs, she maintained a mystic study and examined the magical amulets belonging to Reptil and White Tiger.[16]
  • Multi-Limbed Combat: Lisa is capable of coordinating the additional arms granted by Tiboro's Coat in combat, allowing her to wield several weapons simultaneously.[10][14]

Paraphernalia

Equipment

  • Tiboro's Coat: A magical garment associated with the extra-dimensional sorcerer Tiboro.[8]
    • Extra Limbs: The Coat allows Lisa to manifest four additional arms, giving her a total of six arms while wearing it. The additional limbs can be used independently, enabling her to wield several weapons or magical objects simultaneously.[6][14]
    • Flight: The Coat enables Lisa to fly.[6]
    • Magical Storage: The Coat's pockets contain an assortment of weapons and magical objects that Lisa can retrieve when needed. Lisa has implied that the Coat contains numerous such artifacts.[10][14]
      • Mystical Chain: Lisa retrieved a glowing magical chain from the Coat that was powerful enough to bind Sentry. With Sentry restrained, Wiccan and Enchantress were able to use their magic against him.[10]
    • Force Field: Lisa has been shown surrounded by a red protective force field while holding a small technological device. Whether the field was generated by the device, the Coat itself, or another object retrieved from the Coat was not established.[17]
    • Use of Magical Artifacts: The Coat's additional arms allow Lisa to employ several magical objects at the same time. After acquiring Reptil's Fossilized Amulet and White Tiger's Amulets of Power, she wielded both sets of artifacts simultaneously in combat. Reptil's amulet manifested a dinosaur-like head that attacked him, while White Tiger's amulets released mystical energy against their former wielder.[14]
    • Influence on Wearer: Lisa believes that magical artifacts and their wielders influence one another. She acknowledged that Tiboro's Coat had changed her since she first acquired it, but believed that both she and the Coat functioned better together as a result.[14]
