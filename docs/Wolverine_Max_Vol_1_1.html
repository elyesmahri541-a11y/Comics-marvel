Synopsis for "Permanent Rage (Chapter One)"

After a plane destined for Los Angeles crashes into the Pacific Ocean off the coast of Japan, a man named John Grant was the sole survivor. Struck with amnesia, John is unable to answer even the most basic of questions about himself: who he is, why he was on the plane, or how he knows the dead woman floating beside him in the wreckage. More importantly, he can't figure out how his legs grew back after being biten off by a shark -- or how he killed that shark with metal claws that emerged from his knuckles. When he sleeps, fragments of impossible memories from hundreds of years in the past plague his dreams. Confused, fustrated, and hunted by the Japanese police who have named him a suspect in the plane crash, John holed up in an anonymous motel, sure of only one thing: his real name isn't John Grant. It's Logan.

Here's what he doesn't know: A SWAT team was tipped off to his location and is about to bust down his door...
