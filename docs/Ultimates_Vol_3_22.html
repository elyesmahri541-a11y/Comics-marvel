Synopsis for 1st story

On July 4, 1923, Steve Rogers and Bucky Barnes were born on the same day and at the same hospital. Furthermore, they and their families lived in the same building, and the two grew up together and became more like brothers.

On March 18, 2026, Captain America led the Ultimates Network against the Red Skulls in Fatherlandville, Idaho. The fighting has been brutal and the Ultimates' casualties are being minimized by Pietro (who calls himself Quicksilver) and Wanda who use their powers to help the wounded. So far the Ultimates have been laying siege on the Red Skulls' fortress, where the Grand Skull and his forces are holding out after losing their support from the H.A.N.D. However, the Red Skulls are still armed with high-tech H.A.N.D. weapons while using babies as literal human shields.

Steve is later contacted by John Walker, who survived his ordeal with the Human Torch but his entire head had been badly burned to the point of resembling the original Red Skull, Johann Schmidt. Walker is now leading a breakaway faction of the Red Skulls and has claimed himself to be the "second coming" of the Red Skull, and proposes to Steve of aiding him to destroy the Grand Skull, whom Walker deemed as a pretender and false prophet. Steve is skeptical and he accuses Walker of using the Ultimates to settle his power struggle with the Grand Skull. But Walker insists that his offer is genuine as he reveals that he has followers inside Fatherlandville along with the fortress' schematics and a map to the Grand Skull with minimal bloodshed. The Ultimates reluctantly accept their alliance with Walker.

The Ultimates Network launch a diversion on the fortress while Steve, Hawkeye and Quicksilver infiltrate Fatherlandville via the underground escape tunnels provided by Walker. After the three gained access, Quicksilver uses his super-speed to rescue the children and women being held hostage, and allows Hawkeye to fight Red Skull soldiers while Steve engages the Grand Skull. During their fight, Steve inadvertently shatters the Grand Skull's helmet, revealing an elderly Bucky Barnes. Steve demands Bucky why he betrayed the American people and everything they had fought for to which Bucky angrily retorted that the American people took apart everything they had fought for.

After Steve was believed dead and World War II ended, Bucky saw the United States and its democratic institutions fall apart when the Maker came. He tried to make a difference to stop the dissolution of the U.S. by running for Congress many times but he ultimately failed, as the entire democratic system was declared a failure and the American people celebrated their incorporation into the North American Union. When Bucky gave up he tried settling for a normal life by becoming a car mechanic. But his job was rendered obsolete when advanced technology had made cars start repairing themselves, and Bucky could not find a new job to sustain himself as he was blacklisted by the Maker. When he got older he was diagnosed with terminal liver failure.

Unwilling to die for nothing, Bucky turned to working for the H.A.N.D. in exchange for getting a dose of Nick Fury's Infinity Formula which made him functionally immortal. But Bucky was assigned by Fury to serve as a long-term undercover agent in the Red Skulls, and secretly run them working for the H.A.N.D. Bucky was initially deeply disgusted with this task as he oversaw the Red Skulls commit many crimes and atrocities, but over time he grew to stop hating himself as a sort of survival mechanism before eventually embracing the group's extreme ideology.

Steve and Bucky continue their battle until the former finally beats him. Bucky tries to warn Steve that killing him will set off a dead man's trigger, as his heart is connected to a large bomb which will take out an entire room of pregnant women and children. But Steve replies that the Ultimates were warned about that by Walker, and Quicksilver had already rescued the hostages. Defeated, Bucky emotionally breaks down and admits to Steve how he is afraid of dying while trying to apologize and justify his actions. Steve embraces Bucky in a hug, promising that he will take care of him like they always do for each other. Just as Bucky tearfully thanks him, Steve reluctantly breaks his neck in a mercy kill to put him out of his misery. Then Steve mourned Bucky's death.
