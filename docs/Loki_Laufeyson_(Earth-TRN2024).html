History

The Good, the Bart, and the Loki[]

After Loki committed crimes against Asgard once again, Odin banished him to Springfield. Upon arriving in Springfield, Loki found himself in the backyard of 742 Evergreen Terrace, where he met Bart Simpson. Bart soon introduced Loki to the rest of his family, and Loki joined them for dinner. At dinner, Loki used magic to duplicate Homer Simpson's pork chop at his request, which led Homer to renounce all other gods and begin worshipping Loki. Loki then used his magic to banish Lisa Simpson to Asgard after Bart asked him to.

After finding Mjolnir in Asgard, Lisa returned to Earth to take down Loki with Thor's power. Just before Lisa confronted Loki, he used magic to disguise himself as Bart, and Bart as him. This led Lisa to banish "Loki" to parts unknown while "Bart" continued to live with the Simpsons. Later that night, Homer accidentally discovered Loki's ruse, but he didn't care. Homer kissed Loki good night and asked if he could turn Ned Flanders into a pork chop tomorrow, and Loki agreed.[1]

Further Exploits[]

Loki impersonated Moe Szyslak and gave all of the patrons at Moe's Tavern free drinks. When Barney Gumble questioned why "Moe" was giving them free drinks, Loki revealed himself and gave a brief rant about the negative effects of the patrons' alcoholic lifestyles. Barney was unfazed, however, and kept drinking.

Later, Loki found himself in Avengers Tower. There, Hulk repeatedly smashed him into the floor.

Sometime after this, the Time Variance Authority arrested Loki. Hunter B-15 brought Loki before Judge Ravonna Renslayer, who found Loki guilty of tampering with the Sacred Timeline.[1]
