History

Zofia was the first wife of Count Dracula, a Hungarian noblewoman, her marriage arranged by Dracula's father. Although Dracula absolutely despised Zofia, they had a child together named Lilith and the fact that he was given a female heir infuriated Dracula even more. Dracula routinely abused Zophia mentally and physically, while he had affairs with many women.[2]

Eventually, Dracula's treatment of Zophia worsened to the point where he could not even bare to stand her. After his father died, he stripped her of her royal title and demanded that she leave the castle with Lilith. Distraught, Zophia turned Lilith over to a gypsy woman named Gretchen to raise. With her daughter cared for, Zophia committed suicide in grief over her life situation.[2]

Zofia was survived by Lilith, who was in later years turned into a vampire even more powerful than Dracula (and without the same weaknesses and limitations of her father) by Gretchen as revenge against the cruel vampire king.[2] Over the centuries, Lilith often plagued her father in revenge for his treatment of her mother those many centuries ago.[citation needed]
