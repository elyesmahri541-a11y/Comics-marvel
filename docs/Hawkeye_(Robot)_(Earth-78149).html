History

Hawkeye was a robot created by Tony Stark that was based on Clint Barton. He was meant to lead the fight against Ultron, but was taken over by the villain. Once Hawkeye was freed, he was one of many heroes that joined S.T.R.I.K.E. to oppose the threat of Ultimus.[1]
