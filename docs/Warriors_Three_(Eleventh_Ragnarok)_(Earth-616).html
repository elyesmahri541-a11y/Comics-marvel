History

In the era of eleventh Ragnarök, Loki fooled Enchantress into being taken by a Frost Giant as his queen. The Asgardians then began to starve as there was nobody who could pick the Golden Apples from the Yggdrasill. The Warriors Three were among those who tried, but nobody had the strength to lift the apples, which instantly decayed at slightest touch.[1]
