Accolades

  • Clayton Cowles earned a nomination for Eisner Award in 2022 in "Best Lettering" category for his work on issues of this and other comic volumes. Ram V earned a nomination in "Best Writer" category for his work on issues of this and other comic volumes.[1]
    • Eisner Award is given each year for the works of the previous year. Nominations for 2022 listed "Venom", and while Ram V wrote issues #1-4, 6, and 7 of Venom (Vol. 5), and Clayton Cowles lettered all issues of this volume, only the first three issues were released in 2021 and could have been nominated for the Award in 2022.
  • Clayton Cowles won Eisner Award in 2025 in "Best Lettering" category for his work on issues of this and other comic volumes.[2]
    • Same as above, only issues released in 2024 could have earned a nomination for Eisner Award in 2025, so in this case issues #29—39.
