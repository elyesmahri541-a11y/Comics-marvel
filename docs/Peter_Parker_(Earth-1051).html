History

To an extent, Peter Parker's life presumably mirrors that of his counterpart from Earth-616 - becoming the vigilante superhero Spider-Man after the murder of his Uncle Ben; though he became a newspaper reporter rather than a photographer.[1]

At some point, Peter unwittingly bonded to a symbiote and brought it to Earth, forsaking and attempting to kill it upon learning of its true nature. At around that time, Peter uncovered the true identity of the Sin-Eater serial killer and wrote an article debunking that of Eddie Brock, who subsequently committed suicide. Eddie's ex-wife Anne Weying bonded to the symbiote and became the monstrous Venom, seeking to avenge Eddie's death.[1]

At some point, Peter left journalism and turned his talents towards science; and he Anne let go of their animosity and became close friends, to the extent that he was the first person she confided in when she learned she was pregnant.[2] When Anne's son Dylan became the supervillain Codex and began seeking to conquer the Earth in the name of the Symbiote Hive, Peter was recruited by Anne into the Sym-Soldier Program and bonded to a symbiote. However, the war against Codex proved to be a losing battle that saw many of the world's heroes and villains either slain or converted into Codex's army of symbiote-augments. As Codex conquered the Earth and began converting the populace into symbiote-augments linked to his hive-mind, Peter and the remaining Sym-Soldiers retreated to the old S.H.I.E.L.D. tunnels under the city of Manhattan.[1]

Alerted to an altercation involving the Avengers - now among Codex's top enforcers, Peter accompanied the other Sym-Soldiers on a scouting mission to investigate and came across the Venom (Eddie Brock) and Dylan Brock of Earth-616,[3] who had been warped to his universe by an interdimensional portal machine.[4] After expositing her origin story to Eddie, Anne introduced Eddie to the members of her team, Peter quipping that he was the "idiot reporter" Anne had mentioned. When a squad of Codex's minions attacked, Agent Spider-Man joined the others in holding them off until Agent Deadpool temporarily sacrificed himself to buy the team time to escape. When Eddie introduced his son, however, Peter was shocked and informed him of Codex's true identity.[1]

Recruited by Agent Venom to put an end to Carnage's multiversal killing spree, Agent Spider-Man fought it alongside Agent Deadpool and Agent Hawkeye; but was dragged into a Darkforce portal to the Spotted Dimension and cut in half when the portal was closed with him still inside.[5]

Personality

Peter possesses a wry, self-deprecating sense of humor, concurring with Anne's assessment of him as an "idiot reporter".[1]
