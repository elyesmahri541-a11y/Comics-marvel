Synopsis for "Brand New Day"

Peter Parker's fortunes appear to have changed when the issue opens with him sharing a kiss with a woman at a nightclub, but the moment proves to be only a brief exception to his usual string of bad luck. The previous day had already seen Peter dealing with a series of setbacks, beginning with reports of a criminal impersonating Spider-Man while committing armed robberies throughout the city. The media coverage of the so-called Amazing Spider-Mugger leads to J. Jonah Jameson blaming Spider-Man for inspiring criminals, while the declining success of the Daily Bugle raises questions about the newspaper's current situation.

Living at Aunt May's house in Forest Hills, Peter is forced to face his current circumstances when May insists that he stop wasting his days and find employment. With Betty Brant helping him locate an inexpensive apartment, Peter begins searching for a new job but repeatedly finds his past interfering with his chances. His unexplained absences as a teacher, his unusual photographic work, and the disappointment of a former scientific contact who remembers his potential all prevent him from securing a position.

As the day comes to an end, Peter reflects on the fact that he has abandoned his role as Spider-Man following the events surrounding the Super Hero Registration conflict, since continuing to operate would require revealing his identity. His isolation is interrupted when Harry Osborn invites him to spend the evening at the Mulberry nightclub. There, Harry is accompanied by Lily Hollister and her friend Carlie Cooper, who initially prefers staying home to study forensic odontology before being convinced to join them.

At the same time, corrupt Detective Willowby retrieves a mysterious artifact from police evidence storage and delivers it to associates of Mister Negative. After the criminals attempt to cheat the detective, Mister Negative eliminates them and personally completes the payment promised to Willowby. Examining the recovered object, a tablet covered with unfamiliar writing, Mister Negative realizes that he needs someone capable of translating its symbols.

Back at the club, Peter becomes the target of Mia Flores' attention, though her intentions are not entirely sincere, as she hopes a relationship with him will bring her closer to Harry. After Harry explains how he became involved with Lily while exploring a possible political campaign for her father, District Attorney Bill Hollister, he offers Peter financial help with his rent. Uncomfortable with Mia's advances, Peter escapes to the men's room and leaves the club through a window using his abilities.

Outside, Peter encounters Carlie just as the Amazing Spider-Mugger attacks again. When Carlie moves to protect him, Peter is unable to use his powers openly, allowing the criminal to steal his wallet and his web-shooter. Following the thief with a spider-tracer, Peter considers returning as Spider-Man but changes his mind after seeing a Daily Bugle headline proudly announcing the hero's 100-day absence. Determined not to help Jameson's newspaper, Peter pursues the criminal without his costume.

The chase eventually takes Peter to a homeless shelter, where his pursuit is interrupted by an unexpected encounter with Aunt May. Mistaken for someone in need of assistance, Peter struggles to explain what happened while the thief escapes. Left without money, Peter turns to the Daily Bugle as his final option, only to discover that the newspaper is in serious financial trouble and facing the possibility of being taken over. While Jonah works desperately to maintain control of the company, many employees remain loyal despite not being paid.

Frustrated by everything that has happened, Peter confronts Jameson and demands payment for all the work he has done for the Bugle over the years. The argument quickly escalates, with Peter expressing his anger more openly than he has in a long time. Before Jonah can respond, he suffers a heart attack and collapses in front of Peter.

Synopsis for "Park Avenue Interlude"

Jackpot responds to the hijacking of an armored truck transporting explosives as it speeds uncontrollably through the city. Catching up with the vehicle, she lands on its hood and breaks through the windshield, only to discover that no one is behind the wheel. Taking control, she manages to bring the truck to a safe stop, but the true culprit escapes when the rear doors burst open. Boarding what appears to be a Goblin Glider, the fleeing villain disappears into the night as Jackpot immediately sets off in pursuit.

Synopsis for "The Astonishing Aunt May!"

While volunteering at a homeless shelter, Aunt May takes a special interest in a troubled man known as Freak, whose severe drug addiction has left him in desperate condition. Although she tries to comfort him, Freak only asks her for money before a man named Raymond arrives looking for him. Sensing the confrontation could turn violent, May intervenes and calmly persuades Raymond to abandon his search. Before leaving, he is even convinced to make a generous donation to the shelter. As the situation settles down, Freak's attention turns toward the collection box, suggesting that his desperation has not diminished.

Synopsis for "Harry and the Hollisters"

Harry is having dinner with Lily's father, Bill Hollister. As a DA, Bill knows all about the Osborn family and (as might expect) isn't entirely thrilled with the prospect of Harry dating his daughter. Harry says that he was in Europe "wooing Swiss and German engineers" when his father went nuts. He doesn't plan to go down that road.

Hollister is suspicious of Harry's motives in bankrolling a feasibility study in to whether he should run for major. Did Lily ask him to do it? Is Harry's interest in politics because of Lily, or is his interest in Lily because of politics? Hollister makes it clear that no amount of money will give Harry influence over his campaign or his policies.

Then Lily arrives and the discussion between the two men is at an end. Harry has attempted to convince Hollister that he is nothing like his father. Bill Hollister will evidently take more convincing.
