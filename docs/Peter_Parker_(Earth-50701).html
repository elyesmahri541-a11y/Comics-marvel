History

Although Spider-Man today is a fierce opponent and active member of the New Avengers, he wasn't always so renowned. The bite of an irradiated spider granted high school student Peter Parker incredible, arachnid-like abilities. When a burglar killed his beloved Uncle Ben, a grief-stricken Peter vowed to use his amazing abilities to protect his fellow man. He has learned an invaluable lesson: with great power comes great responsibility.[2]
