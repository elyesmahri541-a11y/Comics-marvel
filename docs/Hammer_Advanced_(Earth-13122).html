History

Hammer Advanced is a weapons manufacturing company led by CEO Justin Hammer and is a rival of Stark Industries.

Justin was the "Environmental Atmosphere Awareness and Global Pollution Clean-up Coalition Conference" representing Hammer Industries.[1] After all the confusion surrounding Red Skull and A.I.M., Hammer Industries and Stark Industries began to partner.[3]
