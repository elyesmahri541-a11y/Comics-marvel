History

The Guardians of the Galaxy were formed after a confrontation with Ronan.[1]

The Guardians have visited Earth several times,[2][3] becoming popular there.[4][5]

The Build Stone[]

The Guardians stole the Build Stone from the Ravagers. In their escape, Ronan would try to get the Stone back, but the Guardians manage to escape through an unstable portal.[6] The instability of the portal caused Groot, Rocket and Drax to be teleported to other places.[7] Star Lord would find Rocket on Berhert,[8] and Gamora would find Drax in the Sanctuary where Thanos was.[9]

Yondu would take the Build Stone from Rocket, giving it to Ronan who takes it to Thanos.[8] Thanks to Drax and Gamora, the Guardians knew Thanos' plan, which was to destroy half the universe with a piece of equipment called the BLT starting with Earth.[9] Star-Lord, Rocket, Groot, Drax, Gamora and Mantis together would defeat Thanos and give Thor the Build Stone for him to keep in Asgard. As a celebration, the Guardians went for a BLT sandwich as a victory celebration.[10]

Facing Ego[]

At the request of Ayesha, high priestess of the Sovereign race, the Guardians will defend the Sovereign's precious anulax batteries from a multidimensional abilisk, offering them the newly captured Nebula in exchange once the monster has been destroyed.

The fight against the abilisk was a rough encounter for the group, but their triumph over it saw the Sovereign gladly handing over the prisoner, and had it not been for Rocket's theft of some of the batteries, the Guardians of the Galaxy could have called the mission a complete success. Instead they found themselves under attack by a vast fleet of drones intent on their destruction. The Milano sustained heavy damage in the ensuing fight, and it seemed that the crew were doomed, but then all parties were taken completely by surprise by the sudden arrival of a mysterious alien ship that destroyed every last drone of the Sovereign fleet.

The Guardians next met Ego, and after some events, the Guardians along with Kraglin and Yondu fought against Ego.[1]

Kang's Invasion[]

The Guardians of the Galaxy arriving on Xandar to help the Nova Corps fight against Kang the Conqueror. Kang calls in Eson the Searcher to fight the heroes. While the Guardians manage to defeat him, Kang takes Xandar away as the Guardians escape. The Guardians then receive a distress call from Earth of Nick Fury, so they travel there.

When they arrived on Earth, Nick Fury meets with the Guardians. Before the Guardians can warn Fury, Kang arrives and destroys the S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier, before taking Manhattan into Chronopolis, along with the Avengers, the Guardians, and other heroes.

The Guardians formed an alliance with the Avengers to defeat Kang. After Kang destroyed the Nexus of All Realities, Cosmo the Space Dog sends a message to Avengers Mansion, but the signal is too weak, so the Guardians head off to find something to help. This takes them to the Old West. While in a saloon, Star-Lord gets into a fight, and Gamora saves him. Star-Lord and Groot then go off while the rest of the Guardians are attacked by M.O.D.O.K., who has become the local sheriff and has the piece of tech they need. They team up with Old West Captain America and chase M.O.D.O.K. off. However, during the battle, Groot was captured by Captain Corbett, and in trying to free him, Rocket was also caught. Star-Lord helps Kid Colt and Arizona Annie take down Captain Corbett along with M.O.D.O.K. The Guardians, now in possession of the tech they need, return to Avengers Mansion.

Rocket uses the pieces to boost the signal, allowing Cosmo's message from Knowhere to come through. Tony Stark and Giant-Man believe that something on Knowhere can take down the shield surrounding Kang's Citadel. However, they'll need to find a way to bring it into Chronopolis. Doctor Strange suggests using the Nexus of All Realities' shards to do so.

After the Avengers locate the shards with help from Heimdall, the Guardians head toward Hala, where they fight against Ronan the Accuser and the Kree for the Nexus Shard, which Ronan planned to use to overthrow Kang. However, they are stopped by the Supreme Intelligence. The Guardians, under the control of Supreme Intelligence, battle Black Bolt and Medusa, but the two manage to free them from the villain's control. The Guardians ask the Inhumans for help against Kang, to which they agree.

In Avengers Mansion, Giant-Man and Tony Stark build a machine to bring Knowhere into Chronopolis, which can release an E.M.P. to disable Kang's shield. However, Kang, aware of their plans, sends Ego through instead. The heroes manage to send Ego back and bring in Knowhere. Cosmo then uses Knowhere to disable Kang's shields while the Nova Corps prepare to attack, but they are destroyed by Damocles Base, Kang's massive starship. Iron Man, Spider-Man, and Star-Lord travel into Damocles Base to bring it down. Kang plummets his starship to kill the heroes, sending in Korvac to keep them busy, but the heroes defeat Korvac and escape.

All the heroes in Chronopolis then fight off Kang's armies as Captain America, Doctor Strange, Captain Marvel, and Star-Lord attack Kang head-on. After the villain's defeat, the Guardians were present at the Avengers' victory party.[1]

Gamora's Party[]

Peter Quill planned to throw Gamora a big birthday party, as she never had one. Spider-Man and Captain America would help the Guardians in this mission, building a dance floor, a food table and a drum set for the Hulk to play music. The party really works and Gamora is happy.[1]

Notes

  • Initially, this version of the Guardians of the Galaxy was based on their Earth-616 counterpart. But as of LEGO Marvel Super Heroes 2, they have become similar to their Earth-199999 counterparts.
  • The character cards for Groot (Ravager), Kraglin, Tullk and Ego confirm that this version of the Guardians went through similar events to the movie Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2.
