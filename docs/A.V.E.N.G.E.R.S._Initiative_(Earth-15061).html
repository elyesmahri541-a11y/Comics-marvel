History

The A.V.E.N.G.E.R.S. Initiative was formed by Madame Natasha Romanova in the wake of the Zero Day, an event during which Thanos invaded Earth and killed roughly half of its heroes. The heroes who were left mobilized and managed to stop Thanos, killing him through a combination of magic and science.[2]

Madame Natasha's team expanded over the following years, and continued operating thirty years into the future. One of its members was Danielle Cage, who had taken up the mantle of Captain America by that time.[1]
