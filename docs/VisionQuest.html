VisionQuest is a superhero television miniseries created by Terry Matalas and produced by Marvel Studios for Disney+. A sequel to and spinoff of the miniseries WandaVision (2021) and Agatha All Along (2024), it stars Paul Bettany as the rebuilt synthezoid Vision, and follows his journey to understand himself while protecting a teenager who may host the soul of his son, Tommy Maximoff.

The eight-episode series ran on Disney+ from October 13 to November 10, 2026, as part of Phase Six of the Marvel Cinematic Universe franchise. It was well-received by critics and fans, who praised its writing and performances, particularly from Bettany and James Spader.
