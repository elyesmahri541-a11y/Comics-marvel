History

In the era of third Ragnarök, Asgard was under attack by the Frost Giants. All the gods were at battle as if it were soon to be the end of Asgard, the Warriors Three among them. In the end, it was Thor standing strong with his lightning winning the battle for Asgard. Thor brought down a mighty giant who fell and destroyed a giant wall of Asgard which stood as long as anyone could remember.[1]
