History

Sam was the chauffeur employed by Congressman Barlow during World War II. When his employer received an invitation to attend a séance hosted by Doctor Eternity, Sam drove him to the medium's country residence. Already unnerved by the circumstances, Sam was further frightened when another client arrived to contact her deceased son, and his scream attracted the attention of Captain America and Bucky. Later, after Doctor Eternity's Nazi accomplices arrived, Sam was taken prisoner and locked in an underground crypt alongside Barlow's corpse and the two heroes. The woman attending the séance eventually freed them, and Captain America and Bucky defeated the criminals and turned them over to the authorities.[1]
