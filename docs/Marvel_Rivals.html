Synopsis

The merciless clash between tyrannical dictator Doctor Doom and his future counterpart from the year 2099 has forced countless universes to collide in the Timestream Entanglement, creating new worlds and crises unknown.

Now, Super Heroes and Villains from across the multiverse must fight together and against one another as disparate groups each seek to best both Dooms before one achieves domination of these realities. Familiar faces become newfound foes while former enemies become the greatest of allies in Marvel Rivals!

Trivia

  • This marks the first instance of Galacta appearing in a Marvel audiovisual media.
  • One of the hoverboards in Tokyo 2099 had Miles Morales' design on its deck, and one of the drums had Ghost-Spider's print.
  • There was a fashion store called Timely Trend, named after Timely Comics.
  • Wolverine's Blood Berserker skin resembled Gabriel Van Helsing, who was portrayed by Wolverine actor Hugh Jackman in Van Helsing.
  • Moon Knight called Dracula a nerd who owed him money. This was a reference to the meme "I Know You're Here Dracula You Big @#$% Nerd."
  • The Malice spray was a homage to Griffith and his dark persona Femto from Berserk.
  • While Fortnite's Winter Soldier (Pen & Ink) outfit was based on the Winter Solider's appearance in Captain America: The Winter Soldier, his back bling was a Marvel Rivals reference. Its description was quoted from Bucky's ultimate ability and the arm was similarly split apart with retractable cables.
  • Ultron's emojis were also homages. Acceptable was a reference to T-800's death in Terminator 2: Judgment Day and Think, Human! parodied Omni-Man from Invincible S1E08, respectively.
  • The final frame of Hawkeye's Ronin MVP is based on a pose preformed by the Earth-199999 Ronin in Avengers: Endgame, though the skin itself was not based on its MCU counterpart.
  • The team-up between Jeff and Storm, It's Jeff-Nado!, was inspired by the Sharknado film series.
  • Numerous references to the Wesley Snipes' Blade were made:
    • One of Blade's lines when he knocks out Luna Snow is "Somebody's always trying to ice skate uphill." This is a reference to a line from 1998's Blade. When he knocks out an enemy Blade, he retorts "There's only one Blade." This is a reference to a line from Deadpool & Wolverine.
    • The Season 3 trailer and Blade's ultimate ability "Thousand-Fold Slash" play a track that homages New Order's Confusion (Pump Panel Reconstruction Mix).
    • The emojis "Blade Out" and "Full of Confidence" homage the Blade II poster and Blade catching a glaive in the blood rave scene respectively.
    • The final frame of Blade's default MVP Animation homages one of the Blade posters.
  • Human Torch and Thing's team-up, First Steps, was taken from an attack their Earth-828 counterparts used in The Fantastic Four: First Steps. Johnny even similarly asks Ben "what time is it, Ben?"
  • One of Gambit's voicelines "Been waitin' a long time for a battle like this, I tell you what." references a line from Deadpool & Wolverine. When he knocks out an enemy Wolverine, he retorts "Gambit see your bet, Logan, and raise it!" referencing a line said in the X-Men '97 episode "Remember It".
  • Deadpool makes numerous references to other marvel media including the Marvel Cinematic Universe, Deadpool, Deadpool & Wolverine, Loki, WandaVision, Blade & Deadpool/Batman #1. As well as other pop-culture references to Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, Dora the Explorer, No Sleep till Brooklyn, Ratatouille, Garfield, Star Wars, Oscars, Toto's Africa, Final Fantasy, Aerosmith's Livin' on the Edge, Super Mario, The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring, Stairway to Heaven, SpongeBob SquarePants, Neon Genesis Evangelion, Gundam, A Few Good Men, Pokémon, Moana (2016 film), The Magic School Bus, Jurassic Park, the song Firework, and Enda Mode from The Incredibles.
  • Deadpool called Morbius "Hollywood Gold" and said "It's Morbin' Time, Baby!" This was a reference to the meme "It's Morbin' Time", a satirical meme based on the Morbius film.
  • The Lower Manhattan map references the "Resist Rebel" graffiti seen in the second season of Daredevil: Born Again.
  • One of Devil Dinosaur's lore entries "Devilbot, Transform!" is a reference to Transformers.
  • In a reference to Spider-Man: No Way Home, Doctor Strange states he once made the entire world forget Spider-Man's secret identity.
  • One of Eddie Brock's interactions with Gorr describes his symbiote's hunger for chocolate and brains. This was a reference to the Venom film.
  • The God Quarry map has numerous pages and covers from the comics on full display.
  • With a few exceptions, most seasons are based on a volume or storyline from the comics:
    • Season 1 and the Blood Hunt PvE (Season 7.5) are based on Blood Hunt.
    • Season 2 is based on Hellfire Gala.
    • Season 3 is based on King in Black.
    • Season 4 and the K'un-Lun: Shenloong Arena map (Season 8.5) are loosely based on the Tournament of the Heavenly Cities from Immortal Iron Fist Vol 1 with elements of Iron Fist Vol 6 and Deadly Hands of K'un-Lun Vol 1.
    • Season 5 is loosely based on Mr. and Mrs. X Vol 1.
    • Season 6's pairing of Deadpool & Elsa Bloodstone is inspired by Deadpool Vol 7.
    • Season 7's pairing of Black Cat and White Fox pulls from Jed MacKay's run on Black Cat, while the main storyline is based on Devil's Reign.
