List of Avengers members

The Avengers are a team of Earth's mightiest heroes. Having originally assembled to fight for justice, the roster expanded over time and has, since its creation, featured a large number of characters in a variety of combinations, with the vast majority of the most prominent superheroes in the Marvel Universe having been a member of an Avengers team at some point in their career.

This list also includes the numerous spin-off Avengers teams that were at some point officially considered part of the main group. These teams are, in alphabetical order:

Characters in bold are currently active on one or more of the above, bolded Avengers teams, or on the main team (specifics indicated in the Notes section). Teams listed above in italics have not been officially confirmed as disbanded, but regardless have made no active appearance in at least two real-world years and thus can be assumed to be defunct until confirmed otherwise.

There are several different sub-conditions in which Avengers are added to the team as semi-members. These include: "provisional" members, who were promised a future position in a pertinent moment; "probationary" members, who were conditioned to an adaptation tenure as trainees before being accepted as full members; and "reservist" members, who only went to action when answering emergency crises. Most Avengers who are not active in an official team continue to operate as reservists, barring a complete severing of ties.

Non-member Avengers teams[]

The following teams were never officially listed as directly affiliated with the Avengers, but still worked with teams bearing the Avengers name in some capacity. As a result, members of the team are not officially recognized as Avengers, though it's possible some of them gained membership on an official team. These mostly consist of various villain groups who were fighting on the side of good for varying reasons, or teams that lacked recognition from previous Avengers members. More details on them can be found on their respective pages.

Teams called the "Avengers" in-universe[]

  • A.I.vengers: This was a group of androids created by Hank Pym for moral support. They never actively assisted Pym, or any other Avenger, outside of this.
  • Astonishing Avengers: This team consisted of villains who had been inverted during World War Hate. Because they were truly villains, although they assisted non-inverted heroes, they were never recognized as Avengers.
  • Avengers of the Supernatural: This team was created against their will by Mojo, and therefore never officially signed on as an Avengers team.
  • Brooklyn Avengers: Though under the guidance of actual Avenger Spider-Man, this team only operated in a specific area and never registered with the Avengers themselves.
  • Dark Avengers: This team was a group of villains disguised as heroes. Though seen as Avengers by the general public (unaware of their true nature), they were never true Avengers due to their allegiances.
  • Future Avengers: This team, consisting entirely of children, merely took the Avengers moniker and never fought with them.
  • Civil War Secret Avengers: This team, while consisting of many Avengers members (past, present, and future), never gained Avengers status due to their direct opposition of the Fifty-State Initiative.

Teams with editorial name "Avengers"[]

  • Agents of Wakanda (known as Secret Avengers)
  • Cosmic Avengers (never officially given a name)
  • Illuminati (known as New Avengers)
  • New Warriors (known as Avengers Resistance)
  • Thunderbolts (known as Dark Avengers)
