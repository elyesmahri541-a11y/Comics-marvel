History

Donald-Thor was a Ducksgardian who once suggested his uncle Scrooge-Odin to order Gladstone-Loki to tidy up his prizes from the Nine Realms lottery on the Isle of Silence. A furious Loki decided to get his revenge on his cousin and he discovered he had travelled to Midgard on a date with Daisy-Sif; Gladstone used his powers to control Goofy-Hulk forcing him to ruin Duckburg's bicentenary anniversary and when Donald heard the news, he started looking for Goofy, but he found an illusion of him instead. Understanding Gladstone was responsible of that, he reached him on the Isle of Silence and fought him, eventually defeating him. When back on Earth, he found Goofy-Hulk and other heroes who were looking for him and they decided to join into a superhero team known as the Avengers.[1]
