Solicit Synopsis

Step up to the terrible ten-in-one at the center of Clive Barker's dark carnival. Waiting for you in tonight's sinister sideshow...

Yowzah! Take a tour through the halls of hell, where love and pain become one!
Yowzie! South African Stormtroopers walk on a bed of burning humanity!
Yowzah! Holy Man oddity in the clutches of the devil!

Call them freaks, if you must, call them aberrations. Just be sure to call them yours...
