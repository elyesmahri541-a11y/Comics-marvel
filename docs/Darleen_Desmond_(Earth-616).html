History

Darleen Desmond was the girlfriend of Cody Pace prior to his journey through the Mystic Realms.

Cody and Darleen had gone on several dates, but their relationship was strained by Cody's behavior. On what would have been their third date, Cody informed Darleen that he was breaking their plans in order to see another woman, Judy-Ann.

Angered by Cody's treatment of her, Darleen threw dishes at him and ended their relationship. Cody subsequently left in his Corvette.[1]

Impersonation[]

While Cody was later stranded on Golgotha, he encountered a woman who appeared to be Darleen. She claimed that she had been struck by a car on Earth and had subsequently arrived on Golgotha in much the same way Cody had been transported between worlds after dying.

The woman was not the real Darleen, but a servant of Malevelos using her appearance in an attempt to manipulate Cody. The deception was exposed, and Ivory Darc appeared to Cody in a vision and provided him with an incantation capable of unleashing Devilthorn's power. Cody used the weapon against the impostor.[2]

Notes

  • Darleen Desmond was Cody Pace's girlfriend before ending their relationship shortly before Cody's first death.
  • The being resembling Darleen on Golgotha was a servant of Malevelos and not Darleen herself.
  • The ultimate fate of the real Darleen Desmond is unknown.
