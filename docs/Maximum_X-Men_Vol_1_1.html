AN ALL-NEW SERIES BEGINS IN THE AFTERMATH OF ARMAGEDDON!

Mutants by birth, heroes by choice! After the cataclysmic events of ARMAGEDDON, who’s left to save the world from an Avengers-level threat? In the absence of Earth’s Mightiest Heroes, Storm and Wolverine assemble a new team of Earth’s Mightiest Mutants to face the foes no single hero can handle. But they may quickly come to regret that decision when the ANNIHILATION WAVE arrives on Earth.
