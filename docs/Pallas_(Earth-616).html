History

Pallas was the sacred pet owl of the Olympian goddess of wisdom Athena,[3] who accompanied her on the field,[4][3] either perched on her shoulder or deployed to gather intelligene,[3] and otherwise stayed with her at her mansion[5][1][6] in the Green Mountains of Vermont.[5][6]

Paraphernalia

Transportation

  • Athena's car.[4]

Notes

  • Pallas is mentioned in Marvel Pets Handbook #1 to first appear in Incredible Hercules #115,[2] instead of #113 during which a (white) owl appears along Athena. This is alternatively an error, or Athena had multiple owls as pets.
    • That would explain why the owl switch back forth white[5][6] and brown-greyish[7][1][4] feathers (likely an artist oversight).
    • In the case of multiple owls, the brown one is confirmed to be Pallas.[1][2]
    • It is unknown if Athena's owl pet seen in New Warriors (Vol. 2) #10 is the same character.
  • Pallas is presumably based on the Owl of Athena concept and named after Athena's epithet "Pallas Athena", though it could also be a reference to the war titan Pallas or any of the many Greek mythological characters named Pallas.

Trivia

  • The word "Pallas" is from the Ancient Greek names "Παλλάς" ("Pallás") (feminine) and "Πάλλας" ("Pállas") (masculine), derived from "πάλλω" ("pállō", "to poise, sway, or swing").[8]
