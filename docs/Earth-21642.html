Earth-21642, also known as Universe-123-ABC, is the main setting of the children's animated series Marvel's Spidey and His Amazing Friends, its shorts, Meet Spidey and His Amazing Friends, and the spin-off series Marvel's Iron Man and His Awesome Friends and Marvel’s Avengers: Mightiest Friends.

Notes

  • Prior to being given an official reality number, this universe was referred as Earth-TRN888 as a part of our Temporary Reality Numbers classification system.
  • 21642, the reality's numeric designation, is close to the date 2021-6-24 (24 June 2021), the day Meet Spidey and His Amazing Friends S1E05 first aired. This episode notably introduced Green Goblin to the series.

Trivia

  • This universe is part of the "Disney Jr. Multiverse," which includes Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures, Sofia the First and Mickey Mouse Clubhouse, among others.[3]
  • In Spidey and His Amazing Friends, Iron Man and Black Panther are introduced as adults. However, in Iron Man and His Awesome Friends, they were retconned to be children like most other hero characters in this universe.
