History

Zoe was a student at Public School 20 Anna Silver. She's in the same science class, same physical education class, and same art class as both Lunella and Eduardo, and was present when Coach Hrbek accidentally activated the Omni-Wave Projector allowing the Killer-Folk and Devil Dinosaur to cross over from Earth-78411.[1]

When the school caught on fire due to Sasha, Lily, and another girl playing with matches, Zoe was one of Ms. Dominguez's students who used the Mutant Devil-Beast as a slide to escape the blaze.[2] She then witnessed the subsequent battle between Hulk and Devil Dinosaur, which destroyed several responding FDNY firetrucks, as well as part of the school.[3]
