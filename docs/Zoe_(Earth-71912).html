History

Zoe and Zachary moved to Marville and the Avengers and X-Men started fighting over the attention of the new residents of Marville.[1] They invited them to their secret clubhouses to convince them to join their crews. This also brought the Guardians of the Galaxy's interest in the twins as well.[2] However, the Guardians were far from being their last suitors. Eventually, the Inhumans also showed up to fight for the twins' friendship, but none of them were successful in their goal, as while they battled the Brotherhood of Evil Mutants, the Brotherhood was able to convince the twins to join their ranks.[3]
