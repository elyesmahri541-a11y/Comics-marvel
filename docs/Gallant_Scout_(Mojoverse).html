History

The Gallant Scout was a herald of Mojo and the King of Multiversal Content and Vertical Integration. When Mojo began delving into the world of streaming services, he decided to launch one called Mojo+ consisting of talent that were popular across the Multiverse. Since the Muppets were famous in countless universes, the Gallant Scout chose one of them, Rowlf the Dog of Earth-2409, to be one of the stars of the new streaming service, forcing him to play show tunes against his will. After taking Rowlf to the Mojoverse, he left a note for the Muppets telling them why he took their friend. After the Muppets travel to Earth-616 to gather super heroes to help fight Mojo and save their friend, they travel to the Mojoverse and attempt to stop Mojo, but this was revealed to be part of Mojo's plan since he wanted the muppets and super heroes to be a part of his new variety show. He forced them all to put on a show or else he'd keep Rowlf there, playing early 2000s trance music until his brain turned to mush. The heroes and Muppets all worked together to put on a show and Mojo claimed that the streaming numbers were through the roof, but the Gallant Scout waited until he saw the demographics to celebrate.

The heroes tried to distract their captors while Invisible Woman used her powers to turn Deadpool and Gonzo invisible in order to save Rowlf, but Mojo's men stopped Invisible Woman, causing the heroes to become visible again, leading to an all-out battle between the heroes, muppets, and Mojo's minions. The Gallant Scout used his gold surfboard to fly into the battle but was subdued by Animal. He tried to stop the Muppet by claiming that if he stopped hitting him, he could make him a star. Gallant Scout was defeated alongside Mojo and his minions.[1]
