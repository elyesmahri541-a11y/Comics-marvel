Welsh Publishing Group

Welsh Publishing Group (formerly Telepictures Publications and Lorimar-Telepictures Publications[1][2]) was a magazine publisher owned by Donald Welsh and bearing his name. It was bought by Marvel in 1994[3] and closed in 1995.
