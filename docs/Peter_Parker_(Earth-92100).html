History

In an effort to eliminate his powers, Peter concocted a potion that instead caused him to grow four extra arms.[6] The only cure for his predicament lied within Michael Morbius' blood. Unfortunately for Peter, Morbius was eaten by sharks and while fragments of him did wash up on the beach Peter was on, he did not see them.[2]

Peter went to every expert he could find, from Dr. Connors to Professor X, and finally Reed Richards, all of whom could not cure him. A subsequent tussle with Doctor Octopus that was wrapped up quickly made Peter realize that having a few extra arms wasn't all that bad. He then became very well respected and loved around the world, he was able to save Gwen Stacy from the Green Goblin.[2]

Spider-Man was recruited by the S.H.I.E.L.D. of Earth-41940 into the Spider-Men, a group of Spider-Man analogs assembled to combat the Multiversal Sinister Six.[7] Later, he was seen among a few other Spider-Men that Superior Spider-Man invited to defeat someone named Karn who had killed many other Spider-Totems from other universe.[8] Peter was later killed by Daemos in Earth-928 while buying time for Spider-Man 2099 and Lady Spider to escape.[4]
