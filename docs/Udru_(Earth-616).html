The Mother of Horrors created one of her first borne many, many eons prior to the coming of Gods and Men for the express intent of deposing the One Above All out of resentment of their lording over all of totality.[1] Having taken Earth and hidden it from them before siring her nescant kin in preparation for the omnipotent being's return, that being Eldest and her second spawn Udru himself. Whence upon the Golden One's realization of what She and her spawn had done; the primordial daemon mother Vinruviel's attempt at afflicting Him with her corrosion backfired, triggering Their prominant rise as His darkest self, the One Below All. Abjectly blinded with unbridled rage and fury, the dark side of the creator tore through Udru with all-consuming explosive malice. Pulverizing and dismembering The Mother's champion before setting about brutalizing the interloper who'd tainted His creation.[4][1] This would not be the end of the abominable matrons scion however, deeply unsettled and enraged at his defeat at the one He was meant to rival's hands. Brother Deep's anima lingered on well after his body was in shambles. Having spent untold millennia buried within the soil of the world His mother coveted. Udru would soon come under another name as a township of local miners would be disquieted by the tortured shrieks of the burgeoning atrocity resting beneath the earth where they'd set up home. The denizens would fanatically worship him as Brother Deep as they established a coal mining township which covered what they were really endeavoring. Digging up their new god from underneath his longstanding prison so he may challenge the almighty once again.[2]
