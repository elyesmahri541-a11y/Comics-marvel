History

Galactus is the Devourer of Worlds living on Earth-TRN1854. During the events following the monster's awakening, Galactus attempted to end the threat by eating Godzilla whole. However, the kaiju proved too powerful to contain, violently breaking out from inside Galactus and surviving the encounter.[1]
