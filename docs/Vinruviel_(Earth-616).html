From the ONE ABOVE ALL sprang all Creation, and all things within were made and governed by His will... ...save the single spark of life that sprang from no creator, only from itself: the MOTHER OF HORRORS.

—Eldest[source]

The Mother of Horrors is an entity that came from existence by herself, without any intervention from the One Above All, the creator of all reality and the Multiverse.[2] Resenting the One Above All for dominating reality, the Mother of Horrors claimed and hid the Earth from Him, and created numerous childrens to rule by her side like her Eldest child and her sons Ngathil and Udru, the latter who was created to directly challenge the One Above All. When the One Above All discovered the Earth and laid eyes on her children, He became enraged and confronted her. She then infected Him with her corruption and He manifested for the first time as His dark counterpart, the One Below All, who killed numerous of her children and ripped apart, blinded and disfigured Udru,[5] and later tortured and chained the Mother of Horrors.[1] However, Mother was since discouraged to continue, and when Eldest finally came to free her, possessing the Fractured Son's body, she ate Vinruviel instead, assimilating her powers.[6]

History

Origin[]

The One Above All created Earth and the Demiurge Primordial, who seeded Earth with life, all according to the One Above All's design.[1][7] The first of those creatures were the Elder Gods,[8][9] who shaped Earth according to His design.[1]

Vinruviel, the Mother of Horrors[10] was an entity that came to be from itself and not from the One Above All.[5][1] The Mother of Horrors resented the One Above All for dominating over all of existence, and decided that the planet Earth, to which she was bound to, would not know His rule, only hers and that of her children. She hid Earth from the One Above All, and began filling it with her creation.[5]

Early years[]

The Seed of Corruption[]

With her coming, life began to change.[7] She planted seeds of shadow within the sleeping Elder Gods.[1] As the Mother's corruption changed the creatures of Earth, the Elder Gods grew into fearsome and hungry creatures, many of them eventually becoming demons.[1][11]

Gaea was one of the few creatures uncorrupted, and she loved the Volantine Choir, a race of winged creatures living atop great sunlit forests. Vinruviel came to the Volantine, corrupting them, allowing them to also be diggers, and them leading to ravage their forests to built tunnels, burrows, and the first city on Earth. She also blessed with great size and strength one of them, whom she named Gologolthia ("keeper of my children").[7]

She created her oldest child.[12]

The Great Culling[]

The One Above All came to notice that His creation had been corrupted.[10][7] This is also attributed to Gaea who feared all life would be wiped out on Earth,[9] as the Elder Gods' constant fighting prevented even her hardiest creations survived during the Godwar-.[13] Either on Gaea's plea to the Demiurge,[8][13] or on the One Above All's decision, Gaea and Demiurge produced the Demogorge,[1][7] born as Atum.[8][9][13]

Wielding the powers of the Sun, Atum decimated the demons, absorbing their forms and energies.[8][9] Eventually, Atum transformed into Demogorge the God-Eater.[8][9]

Vinruviel observed as the God-Eater culled the children of the One Above All, and was glad of it.[1]

The God-Eater kept on the Great Culling, destroying one by one the creations of the Mother of Horrors. He eventually came to the city of the Volantine Choir, killed Gologolthia, but was unable to find the Volantine who had fled underground. Gologolthia would rise anew, becoming the Living City of the Volantine.[7]

Age of Monsters[]

After the Great Culling by the God-Eater, Vinruviel grew bolder, and she created life of her own.[1]

She spawned another being that she designed to mock the One Above All, which she named Udru, meaning "Brighter than He".[5]

Among the many children of the Mother of Horrors were Eldest's brothers Ngathil the God-Eater,[14] Fielaskil, Keeper of Lost Knowledge, Filekthiman, Beast of Riddles, Djurnele, Lord of the Iron Lords, and the Old Ones[11]/Great Old Ones,[15] her immortal children.[11]

The One Above All eventually notice life that was not His own, including Udru. He lay waste to Vinruviel's children, ripping apart, bliding and disfiguring Udru,[5] but could not find Vinruviel.[1]

The One Below All[]

As the Mother of Horrors grew bolder, the One Above All, enraged, eventually confronted her with a spear made of His own Light, splitting the Earth in half in order to see what was going on. She took this opening to infect Him with her corruption, turning Him into the One Below All.[1] In a rage, He proceeded to burn Vinruviel's children, then chaining the Mother of Horrors}[4][16][verification needed]in the Requiem Plain, letting the corpses of her children as a warning to anyone who tried to free her, and hanging the heads of the immortal Old Ones lamenting and asking their Mother to kill them around the city that was built around the door holding the Mother,[11] a door that could only be opened by the Green Flame.[2]


Eldest would spend the next eons trying to free her, but to no avail. She briefly succeeded circa 1600 BC when one of the One Below All's children, Enkidu, came to face her at Nineveh, Assyria. She managed to wear his flesh and was about to free Vinruviel, though the One Below All appeared and annihilated them.[17]

Modern Age[]

However, when the Green Door was shut, the One Below All was isolated from the rest of the Multiverse.[18] Later, a group of archaeologists found Enkidu's tomb, one of them being possessed by Eldest, returning to life and rekindling her quest to free mother Vinruviel, as a new Fractured Son was on the loose.[2]

The Eldest finally managed to possess him and open Vinruviel's door. However, she was unresponding, discouraged by the torture the One Below All inflicted upon her back then and the centuries of waiting. In a fit of rage, Eldest devoured her mother's flesh, assimilating her powers and becoming the Infernal Hulk.[6] Since Eldest also possessed Vinruviel - or the other way around -, the Mother of Horrors became trapped within the Hulkscape along with the Hulk personas and everyone else Eldest possessed or consumed.[19]

Attributes

Powers

Invisibility to the One Above All: The Mother of Horrors, at the time of her creation, was invisible to the One Above All's Omniscience, having defiled the Earth for a long time until He personally came to witness it.[2]

Immortality: Vinruviel was undying, as she was the embodiment of corruption itself, to the point even the One Below All couldn't kill her.[11] Even after being eaten by Eldest she seemingly lived on inside her.[6]

Corruption: She can corrupt other beings, as she did with the Elder Gods,[1][11] Gologolthia and the Volantine Choir,[7] or the One Above All Himself.[4]

Spawn Creation: Mother Vinruviel can seemingly reproduce asexually or through Matter Manipulation, as She made numerous spawns through the ages.[4][1][11]

Weaknesses

The One Below All/The Green Flame: Despite ger invisibility to the One Above All and having created His other half when She corrupted Him, She was particularly vulnerable to it and the Green Flame, as He easily defeated and tortured Her.[16] He would prevent ger from being free anytime Eldest came close to it.[20]

Notes

  • Considering the remarks of The Remembrances of Eldest in Incredible Hulk (Vol. 6) #29, which states that to consume ger would allow her to control one's flesh (in this instance, the One Below All), it's possible She retains control of Eldest as the Infernal Hulk.

Trivia

  • The Mother of Horrors doesn't consider only her biological spawn as her children, but also every being corrupted by her touch, such as murderer and abuser Henry Lang[3] or the sorcerer Sumanguru the Flesh-Weaver.[21]
