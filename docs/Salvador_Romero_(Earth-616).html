History

Sal Romero was a Spirit of Vengeance who fought in World War II.[2][3]

World War II[]

Sal Romero was a member of the 101st Airborne Division. During the D-Day landings, his transport plane was hit by a blast from Hauptmann Felix Bruckner's forces. Romero's entire squad was killed, including his buddy, Cruz. After his heart was yanked out by Bruckner, the deceased Romero was offered vengeance by the demon Zarathos to become a Ghost Rider.[7] Shortly after his transformation, Romero was found by Sgt. Nick Fury, Sebastian Szardos, and Peggy Carter who were hunting Bruckner and his men, and they invited him to join them.[7] Later, Szardos revealed that they had been looking for him, as Romero was linked to Bruckner on the astral plane and Romero would be able to lead them Bruckner.[6] The group then came across Logan who had slaughtered a group of German soldiers after his squad was killed. The five of them were then set upon by Logan's demonically corrupted undead comrades, which they were able to defeat.[6]

21st Century[]

Something reanimated Sal after his death to make him protect a boy named Leo, as everything in the world would somehow go wrong if the kid died.[8] He was one of the monsters who heard the Mother of Horrors' call to hunt the Fractured Son for her,[9] but he decided not to answer it for he had to protect Leo.[8]

One month after Sal protected the community of migrant workers amongst whom Leo lived from the first attack of Jinni Dagaal's War Devils, the Hulk and his travel companion Charlie Tidwell found shelter with them. When another War Devil approached their settlement, Sal sensed the threat to Leo's life, but mistakenly thought it was the Hulk who posed that threat, prompting him to attack the Jade Giant. The misunderstanding was eventually resolved and the War Devil was slain.[10] Sal and the Hulk later joined forces to defeat Jinni Dagaal and his War Devils once and for all.[8]
