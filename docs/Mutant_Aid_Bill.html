History

The Mutant Aid Bill was a bill sponsored and passed by the Senator Kelly. After Kelly was saved by mutants and superhumans while trapped in Arcade's Murderworld, Kelly sponsored the Mutant Aid Bill, that would allocate government funding help to create more schools for mutants. Those schools proved very effective in training young mutants to control their powers and usher in mutant acceptance in American society.
