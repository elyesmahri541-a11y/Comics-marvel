History

Origin[]

Tiboro's Coat was a magical garment formerly belonging to the extra-dimensional sorcerer Tiboro. How the Coat came to leave Tiboro's possession and arrive on Earth is unknown. Its connection to Tiboro was later identified by Norman Osborn.[1]

Coat of Arms[]

Lisa Molinari discovered the Coat displayed on a rack in an alleyway beneath graffiti questioning whether superheroes were good. After taking the garment, Lisa discovered that it allowed her to manifest four additional arms and fly. She subsequently adopted the identity Coat of Arms and made the garment a central part of her costumed persona.[2]

After Osborn began monitoring Lisa's activities, he recognized the garment as Tiboro's Coat, revealing its mystical provenance.[1]

The Coat was later shown to possess pockets capable of containing unusual objects. During a confrontation with Sentry, Lisa reached into the garment and produced a glowing mystical chain capable of restraining him, allowing Wiccan and Enchantress to use their magic against him. Lisa implied that other objects were contained within the Coat.[3]

While studying magical artifacts for Jeremy Briggs, Lisa expressed the belief that mystical objects formed relationships with their wielders in which each could influence the other. She cited Tiboro's Coat as her own example, stating that wearing it had changed her since she first acquired it, while believing that both she and the Coat had become better through their association.[4]

The Coat's additional arms also allowed Lisa to wield several other magical artifacts simultaneously. While in possession of Reptil's Fossilized Amulet and White Tiger's Amulets of Power, she used the Coat's extra limbs to employ both sets of artifacts at the same time.[5]

Properties

  • Extra Limbs: Tiboro's Coat allows its wearer to manifest four additional arms, giving Lisa six functional arms while wearing the garment. The additional limbs can be controlled independently, allowing multiple weapons or magical objects to be wielded simultaneously.[6]
    • Flight: The Coat grants its wearer the ability to fly.[2]
    • Magical Storage: The Coat possesses pockets capable of containing weapons and mystical objects. Lisa has indicated that additional objects are stored within the garment.[3]
    • Mystical Chain: Lisa retrieved a glowing magical chain from one of the Coat's pockets that was sufficiently powerful to restrain Sentry.[3]
    • Protective Field: Lisa has been shown surrounded by a red protective force field while wearing the Coat. However, she was holding a small technological device at the time, and it was not established whether the field originated from the Coat, the device, or another object retrieved from one of its pockets.[7]

Galactus exiled in the magic dimensions (Earth-59512)[]

An alternate version of Tiboro's Coat existed in the reality designated Earth-59512. The garment was associated with that reality's Tiboro and appeared within the Mystic Realms during the crisis caused when Galactus was displaced into the magical dimensions and began consuming their energies.[8] The Coat is worn during the conflict as it escalated and the mystical and cosmic forces of Earth-59512 mobilized against Galactus.[9] It was present alongside Tiboro and his Wondrous Wand of Tiboro during the final confrontation, when numerous sorcerers, mystical beings, superheroes, and cosmic forces attempted to oppose the increasingly powerful Galactus.[10]		 Doctor Strange Vol 5 13
