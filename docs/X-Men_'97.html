X-Men '97 is an animated superhero television series created by Beau DeMayo and produced by Marvel Animation. Premiering on Disney+ in 2024, it is a revival of X-Men: The Animated Series (1992–1997) and follows the titular team's adventures under the leadership of their former nemesis Magneto.

The series has been acclaimed by critics and audiences, with many praising its drama, social commentary, faithfulness to the comics, and updated animation style. DeMayo served as head writer for the first two seasons, while Matthew Chauncey has been confirmed to take over for the third.

History

The series premiered on March 20, 2024.

The show, like its predecessor, features a team line-up similar to that of the early 1990s X-Men comic books, including Cyclops, Jean Grey, Storm, Wolverine, Morph, Rogue, Beast, Gambit, Jubilee, and Bishop; largely resembling that of Cyclops' Blue Team, established in the early issues of X-Men (Vol. 2). However, unlike its predecessor, the lineup changes from episode to episode and is reflected by the opening titles.
