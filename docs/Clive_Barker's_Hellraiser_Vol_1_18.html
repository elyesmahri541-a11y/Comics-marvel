Solicit Synopsis

When Mark Twain said that 'a good woman's just a woman, but a cigar is a smoke' thing, he hadn't met these two Clive Barker firebrands...

A femme fatale so diabolical that she connives to outwit a Cenobite for his position and power, elevating the battle of the sexes to new proportions...
And Morte Mamme, the death mother, leading her cult of seven into battle against the legions of stormtroopers of the abyss. It's a battle from which some will not return...

These two ladies don't just smoke... they're looking to burn.
