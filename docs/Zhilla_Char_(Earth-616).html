History

Not much is known about Zhilla aside from the circumstances leading to her death. Approximately 800 years ago, when wandering the Astral Plane to digest the essence stolen from her victims, she wandered close to Nightmare's realm, attracting his attention. The predator became a victim, as Nightmare forced himself upon her, impregnating her in the process. Nightmare then left her in a lower realm from which she was unable to escape. Succubi mothers are replaced by their children, and so Zhilla's body burned away. In place was left her daughter, who inherited all of her mother's memories.[1]
