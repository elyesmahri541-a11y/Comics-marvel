Synopsis

Over their 60-year history, the X-Men have been many things. Outcasts. Teachers. Revolutionaries. Heroes.

Above all, they’re a species, and the future is still theirs for the taking!

Krakoa showed the world what homo superior is truly capable of, and now, the X-Men have to fight harder than ever to keep Professor X’s dream alive. The three core series will approach the X-Men’s vital mission with distinct and relevant perspectives while remaining faithful to the storytelling power of the mutant metaphor!

This is only the beginning.
