History

Peter Parker from this reality has had adventures that are very similar to his Earth-50302 counterpart during his early days as Spider-Man, and his origins are similar to the Peter Parker of Earth-616. Peter was bitten by a radioactive spider. He first used his powers to gain fame by competing in wrestling. He let a burglar get away, but the same burglar killed his Uncle Ben. Peter blamed himself for the murder and vowed to use his powers with a sense of responsibility.[8]

Spider-Man went on to date Sophia "Chat" Sanduval. when they started dating, Chat knew about him being Spider-Man from Emma Frost. But after the "Silencer" attacked them, Peter eventually Discovered the truth and he left Chat for a time though they kept communicating via birds when while making up. They eventually reconciled and Peter was happy that he has a girlfriend whom he could share his secret with.

He has been on many adventures since he became Spider-Man and all have been dangerous. One day, Peter was at Avengers Tower ordering Thai food when he was visited by She-Hulk who was there to visit Tigra. They invited Peter out to go shopping with them, so they left and began walking down the streets of New York City when they noticed a dog causing chaos. They tried to stop it but were unsuccessful. S.H.I.E.L.D. agents then flew in and informed them that the dog was actually a Life Model Decoy named Bebop that The Leader had hacked into to download information from S.H.I.E.L.D.'s computers, but instead of giving it a simple "download" command, he wanted to make a joke and used the command "fetch," given the LMD's canine appearance. Because his brainwaves were based on those of an actual dog, this triggered Bebop to run away. Peter, She-Hulk, and Tigra were tasked with finding and returning the Life Model Doggie. While trying to find him, they were all attacked by other Life Model Decoys hacked into by the Leader that looked like animals such as birds and horses. Peter used his Avengers communicator to block their signal, effectively shutting them all down. They all then were attacked by the Leader himself in a mech suit, and with the help of Bebop, they were able to defeat him and return both him and Bebop to S.H.I.E.L.D. custody, allowing them to continue their shopping day.[9] Some time later, Peter climbed outside someone's apartment building in order to watch Hollywood's Biggest Night Awards ceremony on a TV through their window. Hulk and Tigra were guests at the ceremony because they were nominated for their documentary film called "Don't look at the Camera: Three days in the life of the Hulk."[10] Peter had a bounty on his head from The Torino Crime family. As Spider-Man became an infamous figure, Peter attempted to start a new career under the codename Mace. This identity didn't go over well with the public; however, as they questioned why he didn't carry a mace. Still under threat of the Torino Crime Family, Peter repurposed the suit into a new secondary identity of Agent Nine.[11]

Peter was recruited by the S.H.I.E.L.D. of Earth-41940 into the Spider-Men, a group of Spider-Man analogs assembled to combat the Multiversal Sinister Six. To differentiate himself, he used his alternate identity of Agent Nine while operating on this parallel world.[12]
