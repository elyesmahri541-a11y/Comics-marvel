History

The F.A.A. sent their Inspector Henderson to the crash site of Flight 4-1-7 who, with the help of the Avengers, investigated the anomalous crash, which without the FAA knowing, was caused by the Grim Reaper who shot it down to thank Lloigoroth for the powers he had granted him.[1]
