Solicit Synopsis

SPECIAL "PLANET X" FINALE! The final double-sized chapter in Grant Morrison's most ambitious story to date... Professor X and his X-Men make their final stand against Magneto, who was hiding under their noses disguised as team member Xorn for months. And an X-Man dies in the showdown![1]

Trivia

  • Last issue Toad's tongue was sliced off by Wolverine, yet this issue it is back and it is unexplained how this happened. Perhaps he can regrow lost limbs, similar to Anole, or it is just his tongue. Later on in New Mutants (Vol. 3) #14, his right pointer finger is blown off by a Nimrod.
