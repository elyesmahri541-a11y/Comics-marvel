History

In June 1963, A.J Dohertz slipped a “wildly inappropriate” image into Captain Fancy Issue 188 when doing the artwork. It wasn’t until after the issue was published that everyone discovered the image. Because of him the entire run of Issue 188 was pulped except for one issue copy that was smuggled to safety.

Sometime around 2027-2030 it was available on an online auction. Frederick Frederickson IV tried to purchase it but his rival Richardson Mole cut the power to his house before he could place his bid. Due to this and the issue being smuggled over the decades, very few people had seen it. In spring 2032 Go Go Tomago had to beat Richardson’s high score in order trot Fred to get information needed to defeat High Voltage. [1]

Personality

Fred described Dohertz as a disgruntled artist, probably because of him slipping an offensive image into Issue 188 and subsequently nearly getting the whole printing run pulped.
