History

Smith took a letter shown by Iron Man, in which Stark gave Iron Man control of Stark Industries while he was away on a secret government mission, to analyse in the a laboratory. When he reported back to Inspector Flint, he noted noted there were no fingerprints on the note, and the handwriting seemed oddly shaky, casting doubt on the letter's authorship.[1]
