History

Not much is known about Michael Rhodes' history, other than that he took over as Iron Man after the previous Iron Man, his father James Rhodes, had a stroke. He is also the only mortal active member of the Avengers(which by this point consisted of himself, Thor, Sunturion, and Jocasta).
