History

When Butch of the Bratpack mentioned that Daredevil would protect them, Sabretooth decided to teach them a lesson by defeating the hero. Their cat caught a mouse by it’s tail and proudly presented it to the family, yet it did not kill or eat it. Butch’s father explained that even though the cat was regularly fed and living in the city, it still hunted mice because of it’s instincts.[1]

Attributes

Powers

Like Fatboy, he exudes an aura capable of making people around him fight with each other. It's not known if it's his natural mutant ability to grow up or if it was given to him by Mojo.
