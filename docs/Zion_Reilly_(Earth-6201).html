History

Young Zion Reilly volunteered for an experimental program which implanted a device called "Spider" under her skin to access the network without the need for carrying a physical device. The procedure went wrong and put Zion in a coma for eight months, from which she emerged with powers. Kicked from the network by Electro, Zion assumed the identity of Recluse and went on to wage a war against the company and its head, Maxwell Dillon. After stopping the Spider program from going worldwide, she was recruited by Captain America to deal with a threat of a higher order.[1]
