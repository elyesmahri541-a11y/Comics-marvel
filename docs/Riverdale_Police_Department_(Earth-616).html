History

Riverdale Police Department was the law enforcement agency responsible for maintaining order in Riverdale. It arrested a group of Nazi spies who had been fraudulently selling war bonds to finance their espionage activities. After gathering evidence of their criminal operation, Roddy Colt, operating as the Secret Stamp, alerted the police by telegram and confronted the spies in Riverdale, where officers subsequently took them into custody.[1]
