History

Zip Zephyr first appeared after the fallout of Gang War in Brooklyn, challenging the young Spider-Man while he was on patrol with Ms. Marvel and Shift. Zip Zephyr first fires his magical bag of wind currents known as sakkos at Miles which the Spider-Man dodges. While fighting the trio of superheroes Zip Zephyr brags about his demi-god status while ridiculing so called "mortals" like Miles. Though Zephyr possessed great strength and agility he was no match for all three of the young heroes and was quickly debilitated from a monstrous punch from shift, being webbed up by Miles for the local authorities to retrieve him.[3]

Zip Zephyr was shown terrorizing the NYPD in hopes of proving he deserves his place in the lineage of the Anemoi, While releasing Destructive air waves strong enough to dismantle swat cars and buildings. Just when Zip Zephyr thought he achieved victory his rival Miles Morales arrived with a vicious kick to the demi-god's face. Zephyr, while frustrated by the young spider's kick and taunts, also saw this as a opportunity to prove his godly might and status. The fight between the two was evenly matched, Miles even calling Zephyr's strength "otherworldly".[citation needed]

During the fight Miles made Zephyr bleed a golden ichor from his mouth due to his barrage of attacks, thus further proving Zephyr is actually descended from gods. Seeking to wrap up the fight quickly Miles used his "maxxed mode" and charges a devastating punch wrapped in his venom which rendered Zephyr unconscious. Miles said this was the hardest punch he had ever thrown seeing somewhat impressed that the demi-god was only knocked out cold and not worse. Zip Zephyr is then left in the battlefield for the NYPD to retrieve him.[4]

Zip was incarcerated in the Raft, where he was kept in check with an Inhibitor Collar. While Zip was bemoaning his fate, he was approached by Ares, who offered to help break Zip out and promote him from Theoi to Olympian in exchange for Zip swearing fealty to him and killing Kwaku Anansi's latest herald, Miles. Zip agreed and attacked Miles at the Brooklyn Visions Academy, nearly killing him by sucking the air out of his lungs before being stopped by Thor and Hercules. The battered Zip was then recovered by Ares and War-Cry.[2]

Attributes

Powers

Olympian Demigod Physiology: As an Olympian Demigod, Zip Zephyr presumably has the typical powers and superhuman physical attributes common to them.[3]

Frictionless Skin: Due to bathing in the sweat oils of Theseus, Zip Zephyr has slippery skin that makes him difficult to grab and can resist adhesive substances such as Web Fluid.[3]
