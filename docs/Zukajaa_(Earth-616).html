History

"Zukajaa", conceived as a child to fulfill a prophecy where the Zukajaa, the Seventh Lord of Duske would be conceived by the Tyrk Queen and a Daywalker,and would bring the Tryks to their rightful place as the rulers of the twin realms in which he would rule. To this end Cilla tricked Blade into impregnating her and conceived a child.[1] The fate of the child is unknown.

Notes

  • Cilla was said to have been pregnant and potentially given birth. However, the validity in this is unknown and the fate of the potential child is also unknown.
