History

Goofy-Hulk was a gentle but feared super hero, who was controlled by Gladstone-Loki in order to lead his cousin Donald-Thor back on Ducksgard. After realizing he had been tricked, Goofy-Hulk moved in a desertic zone where several superheroes reached him to be sure he wasn't a threat anymore, at that point the heroes decised to join their forces, founding the Avengers.[1]

Notes

  • Despite the alternate cover of What If...? Mickey & Friends Became the Avengers showed Goofy turning from a dog scientist to his Hulk form, it's unclear if Goofy-Hulk actually had a depowered identity, due to the fact that, unlike his mainstream counterpart, he wasn't powered by rage and he never reverted his transformation in the story where he appeared.

Trivia

  • Goofy posed as Hulk in another universe, where he helped Mickey-Hawkeye and Donlad-Wolverine against Pete Skull.
