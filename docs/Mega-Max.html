History

Mega-Max was first built by Big Hero 6 to battle Trina, Noodle Burger Boy and their army of Buddy Guardians. The mech was operated by Roddy Blair and Mini-Max to assist the team. Mega-Max was used the thrown Trina away, greatly damaging her. After Trina commanded the Buddy Guardians to attack Mega-Max, Hiro activated his Neurotransmitter, which released an army of Megabots from Mega-Max to disable the Buddy Guardians.[2]

Mega-Max was later piloted by the team after they were challenged by boy band 4 2 Sing to a competition to see who was the best superhero team in San Fransokyo. After 4 2 Sing's Dragon Mech was hijacked by Baron Von Steamer, Mega-Max was used to destroy the dragon.[3]

Later, Fred opened a scam email containing a virus that infected Basemax, causing her to believe she is the "Queen of Spamibia". Mega-Max, alongside Baymax, Mini-Max, Skymax and Sim-Max subsequently also got infected with said virus. Mega-Max was dubbed the "Ogre King of Spamibia" and attacked Big Hero 6 as they tried to turn Basemax's power off. Mega-Max was returned to normal afted Fred successfully rebooted the system.[1]
