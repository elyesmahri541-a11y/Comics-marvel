History

Mr. B was an associate of Katrina Weston and an employee of Strand Cybertech. He worked alongside Katrina, behind the scenes, in gathering and selling the prototype weapons (Neutron Gun, Indestructible Fighting Man, Holographic Display Unit, Anti-Gravity Belt) that four engineers (McDermott, Roland Yates, Jason Weems, and Raleigh Jordan) had developed. When Mr. B came to visit Weston to celebrate the transactions, they began to toast to the end of the superhero Nightman, but they were interrupted by Nightman's return. Mr. B tried to intimidate Nightman but the latter grabbed him by the throat and tossed him out a window onto the cliffs below.[1]

Notes

  • While his last name may start with B, his full name was never revealed.

Trivia

  • Nightman kills Mr. B and quips "Life's a hassle, isn't it?" in reference to the character being portrayed by David Hasselhoff, a known collaborator of show creator Glen A. Larson.
  • Hasselhoff would later go on to play a version of himself in Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 (film).
