History

Rosa, also known as Fawn, was a mutant hunted and then captured by the Hag in Oregon, and then brought to the Graymalkin Prison, where she was imprisoned.[3]

Like other mutants, after being detained, she was turned into a Graymalkin Trustee by Doctor Cornia Ellis. She follows the orders of the Hag, and alongside other "children" of the Hag, she attacked the X-Men.[4][5]

She was subsequently freed from the prison thanks to the X-Men.[6]
