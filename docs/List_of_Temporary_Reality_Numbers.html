List of Temporary Reality Numbers

Click here for the CATEGORY of Temporary Reality Numbers.

The Temporary Reality Numbers (TRN for short) are a system used by the Marvel Database for the categorization of alternate realities which have not yet received an official numeric designation by Marvel. Please note that TRNs are entirely unofficial designations, and should not be used outside the Marvel Database. If you believe that a new TRN should be assigned and are committed to creating related pages, please consult the rules and leave a message here.
