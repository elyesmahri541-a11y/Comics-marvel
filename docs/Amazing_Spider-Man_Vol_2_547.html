Synopsis for "Crimes of the Heart"

Peter administers first aid to Jonah until paramedics arrive. Knowing that this is what his friend would like, Robbie decides to continue working on the release of the next issue of the Bugle and asks Peter to bring a photo of Spider-Man and, filled with remorse, the latter puts on his costume and goes to the search for the thief who robbed him. The latter discovers by chance that he has taken possession of one of Spider-Man's web-shooters and launches a spider-tracer on a mafioso who is then kidnapped by Mister Negative's men. The latter finds the spider-tracer and foresees the arrival of Spider-Man who discovers that Mister Negative has the tablet of Death and Entropy, but having to rescue the kidnapped he is forced to let him escape. Meanwhile, the thief begins to use the web-shoteer to commit crimes, but realizes that he has robbed the real Spider-Man and that he could trace his identity, while Marla decides to sell the Bugle, to avoid further stress for Jonah. After saving Bruno Karnelli, Spider-Man discovers that there will be a meeting of two mafia families and he guesses that Mister Negative wants to eliminate them. Not being able to let anyone die, not even some criminals Spider-Man reaches them and tries to warn them, but Mister Negative releases a poisonous gas shortly after his arrival.
