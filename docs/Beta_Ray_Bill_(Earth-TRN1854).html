History

Beta Ray Bill is a Korbinite warrior on Earth-TRN1854. Before entering the battle against the cosmic threat, he met with Thor and shook his hand. When the Symbiote-Bonded Godzilla and Knull attacked the cosmic realms, Bill joined the defenses to protect Asgard. However, during the intense conflict, he was overwhelmed by Knull's forces and captured, trapped inside a symbiote cocoon alongside other powerful warriors and Monarchs near the World Tree.[1]
