Plot

Hulk has a new chase truck, and he's ready to put it to the test by challenging Iron Man to a race. The stage is set with Hulk in his smash truck and Iron Man in his car. But just as the race is about to begin, Venom shows up, revs his engine, and takes off at full speed! Hulk and Iron Man chase after him through the streets of NYC, but Venom is just too fast for them. Can they catch up? Watch the LEGO action unfold in this epic chase with a few surprise appearances along the way!
