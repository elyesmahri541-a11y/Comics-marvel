History

In this reality, when Hiro Hamada tried to upload Fred's data into Baymax's databank, the process got corrupted, leading to a glitch entering his mind. Baymax and Fred attempted to stop the glitch as it continued to glitch out Baymax.[1]

Notes

  • This reality was seen as a "dream" by Baymax of Earth-14123.
