History

Peter Parker was bitten by a radioactive spider which gave him amazing abilities which he used for selfish reasons, this led to him letting a thief escape who would later murder his beloved Uncle Ben. After discovering that the murderer was the same thief he didn't stop when he had the chance, he would learn that with great power comes great responsibility, and thus he became the crime fighter known as Spider-Man.

As Spider-Man, he fought many threats to the citizens of New York, including Doctor Octopus, Venom, Carnage, Green Goblin, Vulture, Electro and Kraven the Hunter. He would later marry Mary Jane Watson and live happily together.

While working at the Daily Bugle and while covering John Jameson's launch to Counter-Earth he spotted Venom and Carnage boarding the shuttle. Quickly Peter changed into his classic suit and fought the two symbiotes. In the end, Spider-Man failed to stop Venom and Carnage and was blamed when Jameson's ship was supposedly sabotaged and destroyed. The public began to hunt the wall-crawler and was believed to be dead when rumble fell on him from a burning building. Peter Parker returned to Mary Jane. While he recovered, Peter discovered that Jameson is still alive on Counter-Earth. Peter stole a Wrist Morpher from Reed Richards which utilizes nano-technology allowing him to change into his new suit in seconds.[1]

Spider-Man snuck aboard another shuttle headed for Counter-Earth, with Peter Parker supposedly along to document his adventures. Spider-Man succeeded, but soon crashed on Counter-Earth, and after the battle with the High Evolutionary's Machine Men and the Knights of Wundagore, Spider-Man was rescued by the Resistance and eventually found his way to John Jameson, now fighting alongside the Resistance against the High Evolutionary. Unfortunately, a battle erupted when their base was uncovered by the Knight Lady Vermin. A fight ensued between humans and beastials. Soon, Venom and Carnage arrived and after a fierce struggle, Spider-Man used his sonics to drive the symbiotes away. After a debate between Spider-Man, Jameson, and Karen O'Malley, Spider-Man decided to join Jameson and the Resistance. Karen handed Spider-Man a bag with all sorts of luggage inside and Spider-Man web-slung away. Later, Peter Parker in his new clothes found a child being attacked by a Machine Man, he saved him and in return for saving Shayne, the boy's mother Naoko Yamada-Jones gave Peter a free room for two weeks. Peter Parker thanked her and sat down and says he promises to get back to Mary Jane. Peter Parker eventually found work as a photographer at the Daily Byte and began to make money like he used to on his Earth.

Spider-Man's main focus turned into fighting against the High Evolutionary and his Beastial army while also dealing with Venom and Carnage's attempts to take over the planet, with Synoptic.[3]

At some point, Peter got recruited to join Miguel O'Hara's Spider Society to help protect the Multiverse from anomalies. He was one of the many members of this team to try to stop Miles Morales from returning to his home dimension under Miguel's orders.[4]

Notes

  • Peter Parker is voiced by Rino Romano in Spider-Man Unlimited.
    • Romano also voiced Peter Parker in Spider-Man, Spider-Man 2: Enter Electro and Peter Parker in a bonus scene in X2: Wolverine's Revenge.
      • Romano also voiced Batman in the The Batman 2004 animated series.
  • In some ways, many events in his life mirrored those of his Earth-92131 counterpart, including his first encounter with the Venom symbiote but, despite sharing similarities, there are clear differences. For example, S.H.I.E.L.D. handled Venom's imprisonment and Dormammu had nothing to do with this Carnage's origin.
  • This new costume was created exclusively for the series, although it also appeared in the Earth-616 when Spider-Man fought Blastaar in the Negative Zone alongside the third Dusk.[5]
