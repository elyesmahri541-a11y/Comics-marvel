History

Mickey Mouse-Iron Man was listening to the police's radio frequencies, when he heard that his old friend Goofy-Hulk had ruined Duckburg's bicentenary anniversary, so Mickey wore his armor and flew to Duckburg's park, where he met other heroes looking for Goofy. He was unable to locate his friend because the satellite he was using had been damaged, so Minnie-Wasp sent some ants in search of the emerald giant, eventually finding him in a desertic zone, where he went after breaking free from Gladstone-Loki's control. After those events, the heroes joined their forces, founding the Avengers.[1]
