History

Peter Parker was Miles Morales' mentor before he was killed by Doctor Octopus[1] when the villains became organized and seized control of the country.[2]

His mask and a set of his Web-Shooters were in a display case in the Red Skull's trophy room.[3] Another set was own by his granddaughter, Ashley Barton.[2] She took on the name Spider-Woman, becoming her grandfather's spiritual successor.[4]
