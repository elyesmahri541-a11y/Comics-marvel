History

Taki was one of the two Caretakers of the Mandarin International, alongside June. She participated in the organization's efforts to regain control of Stark Industries, helping hack several of Tony Stark's inventions, including his Iron Man armor. After their attempt to subdue Stark failed, the organization's secret leader ordered Li to execute both Caretakers. Li carried out the order, killing Taki and June before taking his own life.[1]
