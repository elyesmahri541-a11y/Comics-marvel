History

The Superior Avengers began as an idea by Kristoff Vernard called the "Avengers Initiative", which consisted of using the Time Platform to scan for future timelines where Doctor Doom's absolute rule over the world had been prosperous and recruiting superhumans from those futures to prove to the public that Doom could be a benevolent philanthropist. Following some failed attempts, Vernard ended up recruiting a group of heroes from more than a century into the future, consisting of the brutal Killmonger, the elusive Doctor Octopus, the lethal Malekith, the macabre Onslaught, the loyal Abomination, and the cryptic Ghost. The team of Avengers were introduced to the public as efficient replacements for the traditional heroes and symbolized the reformation of villainous mantles by exhibiting their might when defeating Annihilus and his Annihilation Wave.[1]

In fact, the group of Avengers was formed by traitorous acolytes of Doctor Doom, who in their timeline had betrayed and executed the ruler they considered to be a tyrant. They secretly hid in Prison 42 deep in the Negative Zone and out of Doom's influence,[1] where they built the Arcane Warhead, a powerful device fueled by magical energy. During a mission to defeat the criminal fugitive Diablo, the Avengers siphoned his mystic power to energize their device. In order to fool Vernard, Doctor Octopus created mechanical replicas of the members of the Avengers to interact with him while they were away, while Killmonger managed to seduce him.[2] The Superior Avengers' ultimate plan was to use the Arcane Warhead to obliterate their own version of Annihilus, who had risen to power following Doctor Doom's demise.[3]

During one of their missions to capture another fugitive, Graviton, the Superior Avengers clashed with their predecessors, the original Avengers. Despite sharing a common goal, the two groups immediately engaged in a battle against each other due to their deeply different approaches.[4] The Ghost was mortally wounded in battle by the Vision, revealing she was, unbeknownst to all, one of Doctor Octopus' drones. This prompted the Superior Avengers to abandon their mission and promptly seek out the Ghost to find answers and stop a possible betrayer. Cunningly, the Ghost exposed the true nature of the Superior Avengers to Kristoff Vernard, claiming she believed Doctor Doom could help with destroying Annihilus in the future.[3]
