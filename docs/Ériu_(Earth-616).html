History

Origins[]

Ériu was a goddess[3] of the Tuatha de Danaan[1] and the sister of Nuada, the ruler of the Tuatha.[4] As Nuada's sister, she was also believed to be the sibling of the Dagda, Leir, Badb, Macha, Morrigan,[5] and Ogma.[6] This connection also suggests that she was the daughter of Iarbonel and Danu.[5]

Progeny[]

Around 1896 BC, the Tuatha de Danaan migrated from the Otherworld to Earth, where they encountered the Fomorians, a rival group with whom they had a long-standing conflict. Despite the animosity between the two factions, Ériu secretly coupled with Elathan, a Fomorian prince.[1] From this union, she bore a son named Bres, but concealed Bres' Fomorian heritage.[1][7]

Tuatha on Earth[]

When Ériu's brother, Nuada,[4] suffered a severe injury, he was compelled to abdicate. Bres, who had matured unusually quickly, was chosen as his successor. However, his seven-year reign was marked by tyranny, leading to his eventual overthrow by the Tuatha. Upon his deposition, Ériu revealed Bres' true parentage, prompting him to leave the Tuatha and join his father, Elathan, among the Fomorians.[1]

12th Century[]

In the 12th century, the Fomorians launched an assault on Avalon, the sacred land of the Tuatha. During the ensuing battle, Amergin Glúingel, the High Druid of Avalon, confronted Bres, reminding him of his human mother and urging him not to bring harm to her people. Bres rejected this plea, declaring that he identified solely with his Fomorian father and his lineage.[8]

Notes

Nuada's and the Tuatha de Danaan's entries in Thor & Hercules: Encyclopaedia Mythologica #1 states that Bres was the nephew of Nuada, meaning that Ériu was seemingly sister to Nuada. As Nuada's sister, she was also believed to be the sibling of the Dagda, Leir, Badb, Macha, Morrigan,[5] and Ogma.[6]
