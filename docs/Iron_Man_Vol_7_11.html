Trivia

  • This issue's uncolored cover was inadvertently leaked by the comic art agency Felix Comic Art in early July 2026, weeks before it was formally announced during San Diego Comic-Con 2026. As representatives of cover artist Ryan Stegman, Felix Comic Art put up for sale several of his uncolored artwork, which included the covers for Iron Man (Vol. 7) #1–11. The black-and-white artwork included the cover bleed, and the cover for this issue was labelled for issue #12 instead.[1]
