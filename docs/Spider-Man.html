Spider-Man

Peter Parker
The friendly neighborhood Spider-Man is an altruistic super hero who lives up to using his spider-like powers with great responsibility. Peter Parker has gained said powers through the bite of a radioactive spider as a high school student and started to swing across the city to fight crime and protect innocents after his selfishness indirectly resulted in the death of his beloved Uncle Ben. In an alternate universe where Parker had been killed in combat, young Miles Morales followed in his footsteps after being bitten by a spider injected with the OZ Serum. Both Spider-Men have embraced their duty to crime-fighting and operate simultaneously under its amazing, spectacular and sensational mantle.
