History

The Stark Drones were unmanned aerial vehicles developed by Tony Stark. While stored at the Air Force Drone Test Field in Nevada, they were hacked by operatives of the Mandarin International, who remotely activated them and turned them against their creator. Iron Man was forced to destroy the drones to stop their attack.[1]
