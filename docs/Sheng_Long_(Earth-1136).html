History

Sheng Long is the former master of Ryu and Ken Masters.[1]

Notes

  • Sheng Long originates from Capcom's Street Fighter video game series. Sheng Long was first referenced in one of Ryu's early victory quotes from Street Fighter II: "You must defeat Sheng Long to stand a chance." The manual for the SNES port of Street Fighter II later expanded on this and referred to "Master Sheng Long" as Ryu and Ken's teacher. However, Shen Long originated as a mistranslation of "Shoryuken" and no such character existed in the Japanese version of Street Fighter II. Ryu and Ken's master would later be revealed to be a separate character, Gouken.
