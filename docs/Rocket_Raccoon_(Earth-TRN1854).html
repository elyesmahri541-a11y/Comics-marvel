History

Rocket Raccoon is a highly intelligent, cybernetically enhanced raccoon who fought against the symbiote invasion on Earth-TRN1854. He used his massive futuristic blasters and tactical skills to defend the galaxy when Godzilla and Knull began wiping out entire planets.[1] [2] [3]
