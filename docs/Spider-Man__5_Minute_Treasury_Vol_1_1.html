Solicit Synopsis

Jump into action-packed stories with the Avengers in this 5-Minute Treasury! When the planet is threatened by sinister Super Villains, Earth's Mightiest Heroes must come together to fight for good. With 12 stories, each just the right length to read aloud in five minutes, this treasury is perfect for saving the day in time for bed.
