History

Alternate Universes[]

LEGO Marvel Super Heroes (Earth-13122)[]

The A.I.M. Drones were used by A.I.M. to distract Captain America from their plan of stealing Tony Stark's climate machine. The drones were destroyed by Captain America.[1]

The drone were mounted on the A.I.M. Motor-Trike and were used to fight against the Avengers.[2]
