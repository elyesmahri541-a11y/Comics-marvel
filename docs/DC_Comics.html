DC Comics

For the fictional DC Universe in which the DC characters live, see DC Universe.

Founded

1934

Formed by

Malcolm Wheeler-Nicholson

Location

Burbank, California

DC Comics is an American comic book publishing house and the most notable rival of Marvel Comics. DC is known for creating popular super heroes such as Superman, Batman, Wonder Woman, the Flash, and Green Lantern. Over the years, Marvel Comics and DC Comics have had numerous crossovers between their properties, including the Amalgam comics imprint that the two companies jointly founded and shared.

For many years, Marvel Comics leader Stan Lee in his Stan's Soapbox columns and letters pages would refer to DC as the "Distinguished Competition", a play on their initials.[1]

Since the beginning and continuing to the present day, it has been common practice for many comic creators to do work for both Marvel and DC over their careers. Well-known creators including Jack Kirby,[2], Marv Wolfman,[3] Frank Miller,[4] Todd McFarlane,[5] Alan Moore,[6] and Brian Michael Bendis[7] have all worked at both DC and Marvel in their lifetimes.
