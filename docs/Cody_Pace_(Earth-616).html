History

Cody Aaron Pace was a bonded security courier living in Hoboken, New Jersey. After repeatedly breaking dates with his girlfriend Darleen Desmond, including one in order to go out with a woman named Judy-Ann, Darleen ended their relationship.

While working in Manhattan, Cody was hired to transport a secured briefcase from the Petroleum Mediation League to Al Hasan Fabib at the United Nations Headquarters. Unknown to Cody, the men who gave him the case were terrorists and the briefcase contained a bomb. After security detected the explosive, Cody was placed inside a secure vault while awaiting the bomb squad. The bomb detonated, killing him.

Instead of remaining dead, Cody awoke on a world called Peregrine Magi, dressed in unfamiliar clothing and carrying a mystical battlepike called Devilthorn. There he encountered Ort and, initially believing his surroundings to be a dream or hallucination, fought off a group of hostile creatures. Cody later encountered Ivory Darc and became involved in her conflict with the Owkons. After being seriously wounded, Cody realized that the world around him was real.

Cody, Ort and Ivory were later joined by Jabot Wafloon. Hoping to find a way home, Cody traveled with them to seek the Slivigoth, three powerful beings who might possess the ability to return him to Earth. The key to their lair was held by Meugot Lawless, forcing the group to infiltrate his keep. During the resulting battle, Ivory sacrificed her captive husband and children in order to defeat Meugot. Horrified by both Meugot's actions and Ivory's willingness to sacrifice her family for the greater good, Cody killed Meugot with Devilthorn.

The group subsequently reached the lair of the Slivigoth, where they were joined by Gaelen Piecekeeper. Cody eventually discovered that the physical bodies of the Slivigoth were not their true forms and concluded that an orb above their thrones contained their actual selves. He hurled Devilthorn into the orb, destroying it and causing the Slivigoth to crumble into dust. Shortly afterward, a surviving Guard of the Grotto impaled Cody from behind, killing him.

Cody resurrected on a futuristic Earth-like world populated by mechanical replicas of humans. His companions eventually found him there and revealed that the Triad had enslaved the world's human population. Increasingly suspicious of his companions and their unexplained knowledge of his situation, Cody demanded answers. One of the mysterious gods responsible for his journey appeared and showed him Golgotha, the home of the demon Malevelos.

Cody learned that Malevelos had formerly wielded Devilthorn and sought to reclaim it. Malevelos worshiped Death and intended to use Devilthorn to open a gateway through which the embodiment of Death could enter the worlds of the living. Cody was also told that the only true doorway back to his own world was located on Golgotha.

Cody and his companions encountered Ambrose-7, an automaton possessing transferred human brain patterns who offered to help overthrow the Triad. Cody initially refused to participate, but ultimately returned in time to prevent his companions from being executed. After the Triad was defeated, Ambrose-7 betrayed the group, intending to deliver them and Devilthorn to Malevelos in exchange for being made fully human.

Jabot's barge drones attacked Ambrose-7, who attempted to defend himself with Devilthorn. The weapon's power caused Ambrose-7 to explode. Cody and his companions subsequently discovered the enslaved humans beneath the world and freed them from stasis. Cody then entered a portal leading to Golgotha, but Malevelos closed it before his companions could follow him.

Alone on Golgotha, Cody finally confronted Malevelos. The demon offered to return Cody to Earth in exchange for Devilthorn. Cody attempted to delay him while waiting for his companions, but was attacked by previous fallen heroes serving Malevelos. Cody escaped by using Devilthorn to blast through a wall of Malevelos' castle.

While fleeing, Cody encountered what appeared to be Darleen Desmond. She claimed to have arrived on Golgotha after being struck by a car on Earth, but was revealed to be a servant of Malevelos. Ivory appeared to Cody in a vision and taught him an incantation capable of unleashing Devilthorn's power, allowing him to destroy the creature.

Angered by the repeated manipulation and deception of those who had guided his journey, Cody returned to Malevelos and agreed to surrender Devilthorn in exchange for Earth being spared. Malevelos used the weapon to open portals between worlds, allowing the embodiment of Death to enter the worlds of the living. Cody witnessed Death travel from world to world and slaughter their inhabitants.

Ivory revealed that Cody's life was necessary for Malevelos' spell to continue and that, after surrendering Devilthorn, his own death was the only remaining way to stop it. Realizing the consequences of his actions, Cody seized Devilthorn and deliberately impaled himself with the battlepike. His sacrifice defeated Malevelos and caused the dimensional portals to begin closing. Death then pulled Cody's corpse into its own dimension.

Back on Earth, Cody's landlady, believing that he had simply abandoned his apartment without paying his rent, had it redecorated for a new tenant.[1]

Attributes

Powers

Cody Pace was mystically enhanced by the forces responsible for sending him across the various worlds.

  • Self-Resurrection: Following his first death on Earth, Cody was unable to permanently die for most of his journey. Whenever Cody was killed, he would resurrect on another world, restored to full health. This ability persisted until his final death on Golgotha.

Abilities

  • Martial Arts: Cody claimed to possess black belt martial arts training and demonstrated considerable hand-to-hand fighting ability during his adventures.
  • Physical Fitness: Prior to his mystical journey, Cody was a generally fit human male.
  • Weapon Proficiency: Despite having no known previous experience with Devilthorn, Cody became an effective combatant with the mystical battlepike during his journey.

Weaknesses

Despite his temporary inability to permanently die, Cody could still be injured, experience pain, and be killed. His resurrection transported him to another world rather than immediately restoring him at the location of his death.

Paraphernalia

Equipment

  • Devilthorn - A mystical battlepike formerly wielded by Malevelos. Devilthorn was capable of killing immortal beings, projecting energy and, through the use of spells, opening portals to other dimensions.

Weapons

  • Devilthorn

Notes

  • Cody's ability to resurrect following death was distinct from the powers of Devilthorn.
  • During his journey, Cody's companions and the beings responsible for his mission frequently withheld information from him concerning the true purpose of his quest.
