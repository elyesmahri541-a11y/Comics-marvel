Trivia

  • The Roy robots employed by Hank Pym in the Ant Hill are stated to be reprogrammed A.I.M. robots. However, his dialogue implies that he will need to replace them with upgraded versions at some point.
    • The Roy bots may very well be named after Roy Thomas, the real-life creator of Ultron.
