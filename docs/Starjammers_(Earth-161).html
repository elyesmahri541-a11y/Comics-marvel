History

While Robyn took Nathan to Mr. Sinister, Corsair told the Starjammers to find his son. However, the Marauders brought down their ship. While the two teams fought, Corsair teleported to Xavier's School and pulled a gun on Sabretooth, who then sported a cyborg hand.[1]

Corsair told the X-Men that the Marauders were back. They teleported back to Alaska and joined the Starjammers]in fighting the Marauders. Cyclops told 'Ro and Lockheed to go to his house and Hepzibah volunteered to join them.[2]

From aboard the Starjammer, Corsair told Cyclops to gather his team and instructed Alex to gathered everyone from the house and rendezvous with Cyclops. Jean Grey put up a shield around the X-Men and Starjammers. Corsair used the Starjammer to create an earthquake, killing most of the Marauders.[3]
