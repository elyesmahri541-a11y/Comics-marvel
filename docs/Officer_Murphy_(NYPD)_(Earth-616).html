History

Murphy was an officer with the New York City Police Department. He was among the police who confronted Bruce Banner after the Absorbing Man, possessing a young girl, murdered her parents and framed Banner for the crime. During the ensuing standoff, Banner persuaded the supervising police sergeant to let him leave in order to protect the child. When Murphy began to object to the decision, the sergeant abruptly silenced him and ordered him to stand down.[1]
