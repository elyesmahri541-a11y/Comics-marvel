Golden-Age Characters

The following is a list of characters and teams appearing during the Golden Age, both from a publication standpoint as well as an in-universe standpoint. While Marvel's Golden Age is generally accepted as having ended in 1950 with Captain America #75, and the Silver Age of comics is accepted as starting in 1956 with DC Comics' introduction of The Flash (Barry Allen), Marvel treats its Golden Age as anything published prior to Fantastic Four #1 in 1961, likewise the in-universe Golden Age is treated as being from the Great Depression up until the debut of the Fantastic Four. Due to the Sliding Timescale employed by Marvel Comics, this means that the Golden Age of Superheroes extends from the exploits of the Mystery Men in 1932 all the way up to roughly 15 years before the present day.

The Lost Generation[]

In 2000, Marvel released Marvel: The Lost Generation, a miniseries counting down from #12 to #1. It featured many new characters and their associations with the First Line, and was set between the 1950s and the 1980s. Due to this, the term The Lost Generation has now come to be synonymous in fan spaces with heroes active between the times of the All-Winners Squad in the post-War 1940's and the Fantastic Four roughly 15 years ago, despite the miniseries' original focus on the First Line.
