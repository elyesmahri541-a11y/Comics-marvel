Synopsis for "What Could Have Been: A Street Fighter Epilogue"

A series of character profiles detailing what would have happened to them if the series had been allowed to continue.

Notes

  • This volume was loosely based on Street Fighter II. The series was canceled after the third issue; seemingly because Capcom (owner of Street Fighter) was not pleased with the portrayal. In the final issue, the superhero Ferret meet one of the Street Fighter character and referenced an early Ferret adventure, confirming that this story happened in Earth-1136, home to the Protectors.
  • The series was successful in Brazil, and publisher Escala continued with issues 4 and 5; however, a new chronology began with issue 6.
