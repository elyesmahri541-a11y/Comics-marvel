History

The Spider-Man from this universe retained the powers of the Enigma Force.[1]

Into the Multiverse[]

Peter was recruited by the S.H.I.E.L.D. of Earth-41940 into the Spider-Men, a group of Spider-Man analogs assembled to combat the Multiversal Sinister Six.[5]

Sometime during Spider-UK's search for the source behind the death of the spiders, he found this reality, and it became the refuge for many of the Spider-Men, whom were hunted by the Inheritors. As strong as the Inheritors were, they were still severely outclassed when compared to someone with such power, and therefore did not dare to step foot into this dimension. However, he could only assist them to this extent as he would lose the Enigma Force if he were to leave his universe. This resulted in the resistance of Spider-heroes to be based in the Central Park of Earth-13.[1]

Back on Earth-41940, Cosmic Spider-Man was among the Spider-Men not fond of the Superior Spider-Army's methods with dealing the Inheritors. He decided to join a rival group of spiders, Team Amazing.[5]

With the help of a blackmailed Lizard-Spider and a brainwashed J. Jonah Jameson, who was infected with the Spider-Virus, Karven the Hunter hunted down Cosmic Spider-Man. Peter was badly hurt by his former allies and was then executed by Kraven. He would be resurrected by his fellow Spider-Men not long after.[5]

Totem War[]

When the Inheritors attacked Earth-13, Peter confronted their leader, Solus. Even though he was powerful, Peter's power derived from pure life force, which was what the Inheritors feasted on; although Solus acknowledged that his power was too great for his children, Solus himself was powerful enough to drain the Enigma Force from Peter, killing him in the process.[6]

His corpse was found by Lady Spider and Miguel O'Hara, who were off-world at the time of the attack.[7] A statue of Cosmic Spider-Man was later erected in Central Park in his honor.[8]
