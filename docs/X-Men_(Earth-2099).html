History

In the year 2099, where the Krakoan Era of mutants became just a legend, the world became worst for the mutant population and the X-Nation needed protectors. Cerebra became the leader of the new X-Men which included in their ranks Bloodhawk, Cyclops, Deadpool, Krystalin, Mer-Max, Northstar, Phoenix, Skullfire, and veteran mutant adventurers Rogue and Tulkas.[1] They were later joined by Cable, who was a survivor of the recent Avengers massacre at the hands of the Masters of Evil.[2][3]

The team and most mutants originally lived in the Savage Land until they were kicked out after an attack from the Cabal, an illuminati group that was above the law thanks to their Black Cards and used its Sentinels to make the mutants escape their home. Northstar would eventually find the Celestial Garden, an oasis with a mutant at its core in Ontario that was created after the death of a Celestial named Jovion, which the X-Men believed to be a place they could call home.[2]
