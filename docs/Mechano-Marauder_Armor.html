History

MK I[]

The first armor was a battlesuit that only provided its user superhuman strength. Iron Man defeated Fabian almost effortlessly, while the inventor was ignored by the rest of the Avengers.[2]

MK II[]

The second armor was more complex: it had two giant robotic hands and its wheel allowed the user to move quickly on almost every surface. When the external shell was destroyed by She-Hulk, Fabian revealed a new armor inside that, more similar to his first one, but smaller and provided with two crab-like arms; however, this second armor was too heavy and he started sinking into the ground, that allowed Wasp to enter Fabian's battlesuit, finally defeating him.[3]

MK III[]

Fabian utilized a modified suit (provided with repulsor rays and stretching hands) in an attempt to protect Avengers Island from Doctor Doom's Robots, but the island was sunk.[4]

Properties

  • Superhuman Strength
  • Superhuman Durability
  • Energy Projection: The suit can shoot repulsor rays from Its palms.[5]
