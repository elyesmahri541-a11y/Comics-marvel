History

The Aizukotetsu-kai were a Yakuza federation based in Kyoto. They possessed cloning technology, which they used to create "Widdle Wade" a miniature clone of Deadpool. He became unhinged and went rogue, forcing the oyabun to hire Deadpool to kill the clone. Deadpool then boarded the oyabun's yacht and killed him for using his genes to create a clone.[2]
