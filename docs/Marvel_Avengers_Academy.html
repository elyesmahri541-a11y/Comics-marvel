Trivia

  • The news report about the Academy considering allowing pets is a reference to the Pet Avengers.
  • When Loki mentioned that Asgard had many serpents, he may have been referencing either Cur Borson, the Midgard Serpent, or Fin Fang Foom.
  • Rank 3 and 4 Captain America's design is reminiscent to the Commander Rogers uniform.
  • Captain America's problem with beaches is due to D-Day.
  • Iron Man having "non-business" briefcases is a reference to the Iron Man Armors that can be stored in briefcases.
  • The Red Hulk's design alludes to his military background in the comics.
  • Iron Man offering Rescue a job at Stark Industries is a reference to her comic book counterpart.
  • Star-Lord's talk of a dance battle is a callback to his Earth-199999 counterpart's mock dance battle with Ronan the Accuser in the 2014 film Guardians of the Galaxy.
  • The Wasp made a reference to the daytime soap opera Days of Our Lives.
  • Captain America meeting T'Chaka is a reference to Black Panther (Vol. 3) #30.
  • Pym wishing that he had come up with Ultron references to him being its creator in the comics and Ultron naming itself.
  • The Black Panther's time in Hell's Kitchen is a reference to his time as protector of the kitchen in Black Panther: The Man Without Fear.
  • The Winter Soldier repeating the phrase, "I'm with you 'till the end of the line..." is a direct quote from the 2014 film Captain America: The Winter Soldier.
  • Loki's nickname for the Black Panther, "the Dark Leopard," is a reference to when the character's name was briefly changed to "the Black Leopard" in order to distance the character from the real life Black Panther Party.
  • Baron Zemo saying that he planned to create a "Thunderbolts" team is a reference to Hawkeye's tenure as the Thunderbolts' leader in the Marvel Universe.
  • Stark calling the Starstones "magically delicious" is a reference to the breakfast cereal Lucky Charms.
  • Spider-Man's requiring 2099 Oscoins to unlock is a reference to Spider-Man 2099, who was later added to the game.
  • When Spider-Man proposed to create a think tank; the Black Panther proposed the Illuminati, a super-hero think tank that his Earth-616 counterpart had served on; while the Leader proposed the Supreme Intelligence, the ruler of the Kree Empire.
  • Director Fury saying that the public prefers serious and mean heroes to those who joke around and have fun is a play on the differences between Marvel's Cinematic Universe and DC's Extended Universe.
  • The battle at the top of the Oscorp Tower might be a nod at a similar encounter from the third act of the 2012 film The Amazing Spider-Man.
  • Mary Jane being kidnapped by Osborn and taken to the George Washington Bridge is a reference to "Turning Point", an episode of Spider-Man (the 1994 animated series), which itself is a reference to Amazing Spider-Man #121.
  • The shared action "Share a Moment" between the Iron Spider and Spider-Man and between the Wasp and Spider-Ham is a reference to the prior's kiss in the 2002 film Spider-Man.
  • Spider-Man being afraid of being cloned is a reference to the Clone Saga.
  • Spider-Man 2099's story of Iron Man's future is the same story that Andros Stark told in "Iron Man 2099", an episode of the 2009-2012 animated TV series Iron Man: Armored Adventures.
  • When asking about the future, the Wasp refers to the 1962-1963 and, later, 1985-1987 animated TV series The Jetsons.
  • Spider-Gwen is mentioned to be both a bodega clerk and a Web-Warrior, dictating that she chronically came from some time between Spider-Verse (Vol. 2) #1 and Spider-Gwen (Vol. 2) #1/Web Warriors #1.
  • Spider-Man 2099's rank 3-4 outfit resembling a Mexican luchador's is a reference to how his costume was a Day of the Dead outfit and how the original Spider-Man was a wrestler.
  • Gwen's second conversation with Spider-Man is similar to the conversation that she had with his Earth-616 counterpart in Amazing Spider-Man (Vol. 3) #11.
  • The inclusion of the Spider-Mech is a reference to the Japanese TV series Supaidāman.
  • The Goblin Gargoyles item have the design of the Goblin Armor from the 2002 film Spider-Man.
  • Mysterio questioned the line "To be, or not to be" from the play Hamlet, written by William Shakespeare.
  • Venom describing his teeth by saying "And what big teeth we have..." is a reference to the story Little Red Riding Hood.
  • Carnage calling Spider-Man the Gosh Dang Spider-Man is a reference to the Gosh Darn Batman, which itself is a reference to the Goddamn Batman.
  • Daredevil referenced his "I'm Not Daredevil" T-shirt.
  • The Wasp's action "Meet My Little Friend" is a reference to the quote "Say hello to my little friend!" from the 1983 film Scarface.
  • Nick Fury telling Loki "You can't handle the truth!" is a quote from the 1992 film A Few Good Men.
  • The Sheriff Steve Rogers outfit is based on Captain America's Earth-51920 counterpart.
  • The Gladiator Captain America outfit is based on Captain America's Earth-51518 counterpart.
  • Spider-Girl's rank 1-4 outfits are reminiscent of her Araña costume.
  • The description for the Superior Doctor Octopus outfit includes a line from the song "Anything You Can Do".
  • Wiccan asked if the Time-Ship was "bigger on the inside," a common description of the TARDIS.
  • Stinger made a reference to the Back to the Future film trilogy.
    • Iron Lad not understanding Stinger's reference, but Captain America understanding it, was a joke from the 2012 film Marvel's The Avengers.
  • Odin's catchphrase, "Go big or go to Muspelheim," is a play on words of the saying "Go big or go home."
  • Gladiator Black Widow's bio is a referenced to the line "Are you not entertained?" from the 2000 film Gladiator.
  • The Thunder Hulk outfit is based on the version of Thor on the cover of Hulked-Out Heroes #1.
  • The game's creators, TinyCo, said that they shut the game down because they wanted to focus on other projects, but this was actually the last game released by TinyCo before they were bought by JamCity.
