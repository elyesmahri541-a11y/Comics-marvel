Synopsis for "Bitter Lesson"

Spider-Man's origin is reimagined!

Peter Parker develops the strength and agility of a spider, when an experiment conducted by Doctor Octavius goes awry in "Bitter Lesson."

Notes

  • This series updates Spider-Man's origin to modern times. Issue #1 adapts Amazing Fantasy #15, incorporating elements of Dr. Octopus's origin from Amazing Spider-Man #3.

Trivia

  • The cover is homage to Amazing Fantasy #15, the first appearance of Spider-Man.
