History

Murray was a high-ranking official associated with the Weapon X Program, involved in the attempted recapture of a mutant woman known as the Native and the exploitation of her genetic potential. Operating alongside Mr. Willoughby, he oversaw the deployment of retrieval teams to British Columbia after the Native evaded capture by Sabretooth. Murray ordered the mobilization of a complete mutant suppression unit, instructed his subordinates to prepare Vapor for reprogramming, and demanded that the Native be taken alive, while both Sabretooth and Wolverine were marked for elimination. After the Native was captured and transported to the Weapon X facility known as the Workshop in Montana, Murray supervised the operation intended to harvest her eggs for use in breeding genetically engineered weapons. When Vapor discovered that the Native was pregnant, Murray insisted that the extraction proceed before deciding whether to preserve her life. He subsequently attempted to enlist Sabretooth to eliminate Wolverine, offering him five million dollars, but the arrangement backfired when Creed turned against the Weapon X executives, killing both Murray and Willoughby during the assault on the Workshop.[1]
