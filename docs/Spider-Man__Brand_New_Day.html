Plot

May Parker

You're a really special person, and I would hope that that would always make you feel really great about yourself, but I know sometimes it doesn't. Sometimes you'll be really lonely. And sometimes - because you're special - people can do dark, monstrous things to you. But the people who love you, we love you not because you're special - we love you because you're you. I want you to remember that, even when it feels like you're totally alone. That's your deepest responsibility. Never forget that, no matter how powerful you become.

With a magic spell, Peter Parker erased his identity from the public consciousness. As his former best friend Ned Leeds and ex-girlfriend Michelle "MJ" Jones moved out to attend the M.I.T., Parker devoted nearly every second of his life to fighting crime as New York City's famed vigilante Spider-Man. He had written a letter for Jones intended to make her remember his identity and their relationship. Over a period of four years, Parker began collaborating with NYPD detective Jean DeWolff as he rounded up New York's organized crime scene, apprehending individuals like Mac Gargan (who now knows as Scorpion) and the Hand, and New York's crime rate dropped to a record low.

Parker responds to a police chase targeting a stolen tank that crashes into the Damage Control Detention Center, but when Parker opens the tank's hatch, he finds an elderly woman inside. Nearby guards begin to act erratically in succession, and Parker determines the woman and the guards are being possessed by a telepath. He chases the telepath to a helipad but fails to stop them from redirecting a helicopter carrying Gargan to Bellevue Hospital. Parker repels the telepath from possessing him, but a bomb inside the tank explodes, and the telepath escapes. DeWolff notifies Parker that Gargan was freed by a guard upon landing at Bellevue. Parker meets with Yelena Belova at her office to talk about the telepath, who informs him that the tank was the fourth attack on Damage Control in the past month, with the assailant searching their files for “V-Max”. She also refuses to lend support from the New Avengers to investigate.

As Parker and DeWolff attempt to locate Gargan, Parker runs into Leeds, who had moved back to New York City with Jones after they both graduated from M.I.T. He follows him to a party and finds his bulletin board on Spider-Man's identity, hoping to one day meet and thank him for previously saving his and his friends' lives. He had also invented the Spidey Tracker app to pinpoint Spider-Man sightings. Parker also meets Jones, but experiences a panic attack upon seeing her with her new boyfriend, and he hastily leaves the party. He passes out when he returns home, waking up the next day in a cocoon of his own organic webbing. Parker's arachnid DNA had caused a biological mutation that led to organic web spinnerets developing in his wrists and his Spider-Sense becoming extremely sensitive to stimuli. He engages an enthralled Gargan in search of V-Max, but Parker's arachnid DNA causes him to fall into a blind rage, and he violently lashes out against him. However, his actions nearly kill a police officer.

Hoping to control himself, Parker approaches Dr. Bruce Banner after his lecture at Empire State University to consult him on his Gamma Inhibitor, which allows him to remain in human form. He asks Banner if his inhibitor device could be applied to his mutating DNA, to which Banner responds that it is possible, but warns him that a universal inhibitor that could target any mutation would be dangerous. Returning home, Parker tests an inhibitor prototype but it overheats during a field test. He runs into a man controlled by the telepath and learns that the telepath had entered his apartment and found his letter for Jones. They then leave for Jones' apartment, finding her address through an online posting from Leeds. Parker isolates the telepath, then barges into Jones' apartment and convinces her to go with him. Parker brings Jones to a rooftop and kisses her after she apparently remembers their history. However, Jones reveals herself to be under the telepath's thrall and provokes Parker into attacking her. Returning to his senses, he saves Jones from falling before conceding to the telepath. She gives Parker back his letter before leaving in a taxi for Empire State University. Parker sees her face and takes a picture of her with his suit's HUD.

Taking Jones to Frank Castle's hideout, Parker convinces him to keep her safe in exchange for previously saving his life on Staten Island. After running scans on the photos he took of the telepath, Parker finds heavily redacted information but is able to find a name: Jean Grey. Parker is able to finish two inhibitors with Jones' bio-engineering knowledge. He uses one of himself and intends to use the other on Grey, who attacks Damage Control Headquarters to reach director Bill Metzger inside the vault. Jones convinces Castle to accompany Parker there, where they are attacked by an enthralled Banner, whose inhibitor device was removed by Grey. Parker and Castle free Banner from Grey's control, but Banner is unable to prevent the Hulk from breaking out. Parker reveals his identity to the Hulk, getting him to surrender. Castle targets Grey but is enthralled and used to break open the vault. Before Grey could interrogate Metzger, Parker attaches the inhibitor on Grey, rendering her powerless. Grey is tranquilized by Damage Control while the Hulk reverts to Banner, who is sent to a psychiatric ward. Before passing out, Grey utters a name: Sara. Parker inquires Metzger about Sara, to which he claims that Sara was a recently-passed co-worker that Grey found when she entered his mind.

As Damage Control transfers Grey to their facility on Roosevelt Island, Parker returns to Castle's base and finds Jones with his letter. She gets him to unmask and see his face, to which she states she has no memory of and does not reciprocate his romantic feelings. Parker agrees to take Jones home and meets Leeds there, and the three spend time together bonding. He apologizes to Jones for kissing her, but she reveals she does not remember anything from when she was under Grey's enthrallment. Realizing Metzger lied to him, Parker calls Belova about Sara's relation to Grey and learns that she was her sister who was kidnapped by Damage Control and experimented on. As Parker leaves for Roosevelt Island to rescue Grey, Jones accidentally reveals his identity to Leeds.

While unconscious, Grey recalls her last memory with Sara. They were at Central Park practicing their telepathic abilities when Damage Control, under Metzger's orders, arrived to arrest Sara, who enthralled her sister to not intervene in order to keep her safe. Grey later received a final telepathic message from Sara — “V-Max”. She wakes up inside a test chamber, where Metzger experiments on her to understand and potentially replicate her telepathic abilities. Metzger reveals that Sara died during these experiments, and Grey notices a vent with the label “Vent Max” on it, realizing that V-Max was the last thing Sara saw. Enraged, Grey's telepathy grows even more powerful, evolving into telekinesis. She produces a psionic blast that engulfs New York City, freezing everyone caught in it.

Castle avoids the psionic field, which expands deeper into the city. He perches a sniper rifle on a rooftop with a direct line of sight at the test chamber. Meanwhile, Parker is unaffected by the psionic freeze and forces his way into Roosevelt Island. He fights the enthralled Hand, who possess weapons capable of neutralizing Parker's synthetic webbing. Parker discards his inhibitor and embraces his arachnid mutations, allowing him to subdue them and break into the test chamber. Grey threatens to kill Metzger, but Parker allows Grey to enter his mind, and she finds a memory of his aunt May inside. Putting herself in Parker's place, she finds warmth in May's message about being different, and Parker comforts Grey over their shared feelings of loss. Suddenly, Parker senses a sniper rifle going off, and is able to act in time to take the hit for Grey. A distraught Castle, having fired the shot, takes Parker to the Metro-General Hospital and remains by his side as Grey keeps him alive by telepathically forcing him to breathe. As he recovers, the people of New York show their support for Spider-Man, and a crowd of supporters gather at the hospital. After he wakes up, Castle dons the Spider-Man mask to distract his supporters and allow Parker to secretly leave the hospital.

The sun rises on a brand new day. Jones toys with the black dahlia necklace she received as a gift from Parker in Europe and smiles. Grey leaves New York on a bus and Parker visits May's grave, telling her he will not continue his heroics alone. Later, DeWolff informs Parker that Metzger had disappeared and Belova is helping her investigate Damage Control before encouraging him to rest. Parker then goes to buy flowers when he notices Leeds in the flower shop monitoring the Spidey Tracker. He greets him by introducing himself as “Peter” before performing their old handshake. Leeds responds back with astonishment, to Parker's delight.

In the post-credits scene, the Spidey Tracker locates a Spider-Man somewhere in outer space.

Notes

  • On November 29, 2021, it was announced that a fourth Spider-Man film was in development, with Tom Holland and Zendaya returning.[3]
  • This is the first Spider-Man film to be a fourth installment in a series, after Spider-Man 4 starring Tobey Maguire was cancelled in January 2010.[4]

Trivia

  • Rosario Dawson was originally set to reprise the role of Claire Temple from Marvel's Netflix shows in this film, but suddently her scene was cut out.[5] Her scene was originally supposed to be takes place right after the encounter between Spider-Man and Scorpion, when Spider-Man interacted with her in the hospital for a brief scene. Unfortunately, it was cut "for time" according to screenwriters Chris McKenna and Erik Sommers.[6]
  • According to Ramrod actor Billy Clements, there were more scenes of him and "a lot more recognised actors", but the scenes were cut out for unknown reasons.[7]
  • There was an idea about a cameo of the character Paste-Pot Pete that was originally supposed to be appear in the opening montage, but his scene was scrapped according to director Destin Daniel Cretton.[8]
