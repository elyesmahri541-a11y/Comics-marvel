History

X-Factor is a US government-sponsored secret team of mutants led by Valerie Cooper.

Iceman broke into X-Factor's facility to find his girlfriend Lorna Dane and ran afoul of the X-Men. Afterward, when it came to a battle against the X-Factor, Forge said it was to boost his team's confidence.[1]

Post E-Day[]

After E-Day, X-Factor began rounding up young mutants, claiming to be relocating them for their own safety. X-Factor would later come into conflict with X-Force, when they ambushed them, captured one of their members, and ended up losing all the mutant youths they had captured.[2]

On a covert mission to intercept the Purifiers, Polaris took it a step too far, resulting in Val Cooper kicking her off the team. X-Factor later announced that they would go after X-Corp.[3]
