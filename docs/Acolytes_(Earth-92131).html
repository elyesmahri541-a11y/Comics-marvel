History

The Acolytes consisted of Fabian Cortez, Amelia Voght, Delgado, Chrome, Carmella Unuscione, Joanna Cargill, and Byron Calley. The Acolytes aided Magneto in the liberation of the Genoshan mutant slaves from the hands of the Genoshan Magistrates. After Magneto is betrayed by Cortez and believed to have been killed by the X-Men, the Acolytes pledged their loyalty to Cortez.[1]

After Cortez' plot was exposed, the Acolytes turned on him and subsequently disbanded. [2]

In the wake of the Genoshan genocide and Magneto's death, the Acolytes reformed under the leadership of Exodus and recruited several new members.[3]
