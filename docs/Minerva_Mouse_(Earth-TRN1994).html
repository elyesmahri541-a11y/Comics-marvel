History

After hearing of Goofy-Hulk's rampage at Duckburg's bicentenary anniversary, Minnie-Wasp decided to look for the emerald giant with Pluto Ant-Man and after reaching Duckburg's park with other heroes, she told the ants to find Goofy. They found him in a desertic zone, where he went after breaking free from Gladstone-Loki's control. After those events, the heroes joined their forces, founding the Avengers.[1]
