History

When the alien Vaengr, a non-humanoid being disguised as a golden-skinned emissary offering humanity a path to utopia, landed in New York City, Nick Fury​ and a contingent of S.H.I.E.L.D. agents were dispatched to meet him. Fury quickly uncovered the deception, and after Vaengr dropped the act and slaughtered the other agents, Fury wrestled with the creature, tearing off a circlet that Vaengr wore. Without it, he transformed back into his hideous, natural appearance, which Fury sought to fight by donning the same circlet, thus transforming himself into a similar monstrous shape that could contend against the invader. As they fought however, Vaengr's proposed miracle machine was activated, its effects revealing itself to be a doomsday device.[1]
