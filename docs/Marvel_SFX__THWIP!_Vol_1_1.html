Solicit Synopsis

READY, SET, THWIP!

One of comics’ best innovations is the visual sound effect, and the House of Ideas is celebrating the best of all SFX with our new anthology THWIP! Some of comics’ best creators alongside luminaries from outside the field bring their Thwipiest stories to bear! Multi-Bram Stoker Award- and Nebula Award-winning author STEPHEN GRAHAM JONES (Buffalo Hunter Hunter) is joined by Marvel Stormbreaker ALESSANDRO CAPPUCCIO in this kickoff issue, also featuring JED MacKAY with LUCAS WERNECK and RAINBOW ROWELL. Future issues feature ERIK LARSEN, SIMONE DE MEO and so many more!!!
