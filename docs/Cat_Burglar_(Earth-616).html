History

The Cat Burglar was a criminal who clashed with Spider-Man. After robbing J. Jonah Jameson's home, Jameson set out a reward for his capture. He was captured by the police.[1] Years later, he stole the equipment designed by Hobie Brown, the original Prowler, and used it to become the new Prowler, in the employ of the criminal Belladonna.

Unfortunately, during a break-in of a rival of Ravanna's, this Prowler accidentally killed a guard while attempting to escape, and witnesses mistook his silhouette in the night as Spider-Man's. Now feeling guilty for the accident and frightened of both being charged with "Murder in Commission of a Felony" and Spider-Man looking for him to clear his own name, he fell even more under the influence of Belladonna.

They conspired to trap and kill Spider-Man, but the Prowler's employer betrayed and almost killed him, so he sought revenge, but both this Prowler and Belladonna were eventually captured by Spider-Man and jailed.[2]

Later he was seen on Wilbur Day's funeral in his original Cat Bruglar costume, but the original Prowler called him a "costume stealing copycat bastard" and then attacked. However, they ended up laughing like old buddies and the copycat issue was solved. After it turned out that the Punisher was their bartender, the bar was blown up and it's unknown if Cat Bruglar survived or not.[3]
