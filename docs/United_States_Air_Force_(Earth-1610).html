History

The United States Air Force (USAF) is the aerial warfare branch of the United States Armed Forces, responsible for conducting military air operations and maintaining the country's air defense capabilities.

One of its facilities was the Air Force Drone Test Field in Nevada, where the Stark Drones were stored before being hacked and activated by operatives of the Mandarin International.[1]
