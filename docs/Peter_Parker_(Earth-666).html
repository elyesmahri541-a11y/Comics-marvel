History

Spider-Man was a member of the Avengers.[3]

When The Great Hunt began, Bora of the Inheritors used the Master Weaver to track him down across the Multiverse, and hunt him down. He was brought to Earth-001, where his essence was devoured as part of a banquet for the Inheritors, with each consuming their own captured spider.[1] He was later resurrected when Silk cut open Morlun with the Totem Dagger.[2]
