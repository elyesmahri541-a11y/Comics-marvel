History

The Palace Theatre was a historic theater located in New York City.

During World War II, Sam, the chauffeur of Congressman Barlow, suggested that his employer spend the evening at the Palace Theatre rather than visit the unsettling Vault of the Departed Spirits, where Barlow had arranged to meet the supposed medium Doctor Eternity. Barlow disregarded the suggestion and proceeded with his visit.[1]
