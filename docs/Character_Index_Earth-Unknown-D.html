Darius Wentworth was one of five wealthy passengers aboard a space yacht that was severely damaged after passing through a meteor swarm, causing a critical loss of oxygen. The remaining supply could sustain only five of the six men onboard, including the pilot. Unable to choose directly, the pilot devised a test to expose the most selfish passenger. He claimed that entering a certain chamber containing the oxygen reserve would guarantee survival. Seeking to save himself, Wentworth revealed his intentions, drew a weapon, and entered the room. Unknown to his crew, Morgan had ulterior motives: he intended to steal a priceless Venusian artifact known as the Diamond Horn, a jewel so valuable that the Venusians refused to touch it, a fact Morgan misinterpreted as reverence rather than fear. Using deception, Morgan succeeded in stealing the Diamond Horn and returned to Earth, planning to sell it and become wealthy. Shortly after his return, however, he began suffering severe physical illness. A news broadcast revealed that the Diamond Horn emitted deadly Delta Radiation, explaining why the Venusians avoided it. The same report stated that only one specialist possessed the knowledge required to treat Delta Radiation poisoning: Dr. Siblus Slado. Believing the treatment would require a large sum of money, Morgan attempted to rob a wealthy-looking elderly passerby. During the struggle, the man suffered a fatal heart attack. Morgan stole his wallet, discovering it contained significant cash along with a business card identifying the victim as Dr. Siblus Slado, the only person capable of saving his life. Realizing he had killed his sole chance of survival, Morgan was left doomed by his own actions.[59]

Appearances: Carnage: Black, White & Blood #4

Dylan Brock lived to some extent the same story as his Earth-616 counterpart. Dylan Brock wakes up late one night to find Anne Weying, an alternate version of his late mother and Agent Venom from another universe, standing over him. Saying she doesn't have much time to explain, Anne claims that after Dylan and Eddie left, she spent years waiting for her son - the Dylan Brock of her universe - to awaken from the coma he'd been left in after his connection to Knull was severed. Finally, Anne gave up hope... only to discover one day that the Carnage symbiote had abandoned Cletus Kasady and was seeking Dylan's comatose body as its next host. Restoring the powers Dylan possessed as Codex, Carnage quickly conquered the world, killing nearly everyone and keeping a handful of people - Anne included - alive to torture. Laughing in disgust, "Anne" is revealed to be Dylan, taken over by the Carnage symbiote and transformed into a monster.

The two Dylan fought until Venom's host was severely damaged by Carnage / Codex, while Venom heals him, Dylan angrily says he would have given anything to meet his own mother, but after enduring unbearable loneliness he has had to settle for his adopted family. Countless symbiotes appear and are destroyed by Carnage leaving Codex's body lifeless. Later, Dylan sits next to a comatose Eddie Brock, who is intubated in a hospital bed, and tells him that he has been able to sense that Dylan and Ana's alternate codices have merged inside the symbiote Hive-Mind. Shedding tears, Dylan says that Eddie has suffered enough from the return of his cancer, and that he can always see him again in the Hive. [87]

Appearances: Carnage: Black, White & Blood #4

Dylan Brock lived the same story as his Earth-1051 counterpart to some extent. Dylan remained in a coma for several years, until the Carnage symbiote abandoned Cletus Kasady and sought him out as its new host. After joining the Carnage symbiote, Dylan's powers as Codex were restored and Carnage quickly conquered the world, killing almost everyone and keeping others alive to torture them, including Annie.

After killing his mother, the Carnage-ized Dylan went to Earth-616 to attack his counterpart, who had since become the host of his own world's version of the Venom symbiote. Disguised as Anne, Carnage quickly gained the upper hand and badly wounded Dylan, infecting Venom with an offshoot of his symbiote. However, Dylan's dormant powers reawakened and he used them to summon a horde of symbiotes that forced the Carnage symbiote off of his counterpart's body, which had been reduced into a desiccated corpse under the creature's control. Using his powers, Dylan then obliterated the Carnage symbiote. [87]

Appearances: Fantastic Four (Vol. 7) #7

When a time-traveller Doctor Doom tried to change the past to save Valeria's life, he fought against all of the superheroes of Earth and killed them all, including Dylan.[6]
