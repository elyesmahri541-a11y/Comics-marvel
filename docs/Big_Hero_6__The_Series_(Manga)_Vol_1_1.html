The gang is back in an all-new adventure! Join Hiro, Baymax, Go Go, Wasabi, Honey Lemon and Fed as they fight to protect the streets of San Fransokyo.

Notes

  • Due to differences in the story, this story must occur in a different reality.
    • And due to significant differences from the movie's plot both here and in the manga adaptation of the movie, this is a separate reality from the movie's manga adaptation.
  • While a Volume 2 is teased, it is unknown if it is still releasing. Originally it was planned to release June 20th 2023, however date was changed to December 31st 2026 around this time. But the publisher's website no longer shows the listing, so it is unknown if volume 2 is still releasing.
  • Assuming this reality also shares the same present day as Earth-14123, as well if the cherry blossoms are a timeline indicator, the the manga takes place around mid-March to mid-April 2032.
  • The series is in chronological order, except for Baymax Returns Part I being the third and final story. It could be that Baymax Returns is a flashback, with Volume 2 presumably finishing it with Part II.
  • Also Globby appears despite Big Roommates 2 not being adapted. Although he does seemingly appear as Dibs in chapter 3, as he did in the TV episode version of Baymax Returns.
