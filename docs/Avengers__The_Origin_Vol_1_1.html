Solicit Synopsis

The true story of the Avengers is revealed right here! This five-issue extravaganza reveals previously untold details of the historic first meeting of Thor, Iron Man, the Hulk, Ant-Man and the Wasp! You only THINK you know the full story. From Joe Casey (AVENGERS: EARTH'S MIGHTIEST HEROES) and artist extraordinaire Phil Noto!

Notes

  • Retells and updates events from Avengers #1. Each story of this mini-series will give more detail (and sometimes contradictions) to Avengers #1. Most changes result from updating the time frame from 1963 to a more contemporary date.

Trivia

  • Tony Stark's security password is his mother's maiden name.
