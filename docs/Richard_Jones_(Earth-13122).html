History

Rick Jones was once exposed to a gamma bomb, but was saved by Bruce Banner. He would later be kidnapped by M.O.D.O.K. to be transformed into his own Hulk and follow the villain's orders, having part of Bruce's blood injected into him, but Rick managed to escape and become the hero "A-Bomb".[2]

Pym vs Pym[]

Hulk, A-Bomb, and Wonder Man were at Avengers Mansion when Hank Pym's Yellowjacket from the future captured "himself" as Ant-Man. To rescue Hank Pym from himself, Hulk, A-Bomb, and Wonder Man worked to find a way to lower the force field protecting both Yellowjacket and Ant-Man. When the force field was deactivated, Yellowjacket escaped into the vents and Ant-Man pursued him. Yellowjacket then fought Ant-Man, Hulk, A-Bomb, and Wonder Man in a yellowjacket-themed robot that emitted lasers, eventually leading to Yellowjacket being taken into custody.[2]

Hulk Selfie[]

In Sokovia, S.H.I.E.L.D. scientists were able to find a cure for Rick, but he didn't want to be cured at the time because he was going to take a Hulk's selfie as A-Bomb for Aunt Polly. With the help of an ally he escapes the scientists and takes his selfie, but he's not cured because all the scientists were knocked out.[1]

Kang's invasion[]

After being transported to Chronopolis, he is extremely annoyed at the fact that he is taken from home by Kang, so he and an ally defeat Kang's numerous henchmen to calm him down.[2]
