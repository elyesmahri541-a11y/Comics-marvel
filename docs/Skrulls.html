Skrulls

Deviant Skrulls
A long time ago, the Celestials visited Skrullos and experimented on the genetics of a native life form, creating three branches among that species: the Prime (or "Normals"), the Eternals and the Deviants.

The Deviant Skrulls became the dominant species after waging war against their cousin races and exterminating them. They are commonly known as Skrulls, and the Skrull Empire.[1][2]
