History

Matt Murdock was born around 1972[2] and raised in Hell's Kitchen in New York City by his father, boxer Jack Murdock. In 1984, Jack's criminal activities led to Matt stumbling across one of his business deals, and being blinded by radioactive chemicals when he tried to run away through a warehouse facility. The radiation also drastically heightened his remaining senses and gave him a radar-sense that allowed him to perceive his surroundings.

His father is killed not long after this moment, he vows to seek justice by any means. At night, Murdock became Daredevil, and took justice into his hands. Throughout his adult life, Murdock dealt with the moral implications of being a vigilante, and tried to prove that he was not a villain. He dealt with the case of the murder of Lisa Tazio, which was done by Wesley Welch rather than Daunte Jackson. His decision to spare Kingpin was a means of proving the latter to himself, on the grounds that a villain would have killed his opponent. After the battle, Murdock found a good luck charm that was similar to the one Elektra showed him, suggesting that she was still alive. Later that night, Murdock was seen outside Ben Urich's workplace. After he saw him, Urich realized that a man like Daredevil was needed, deciding at the last moment to delete the article he had composed, and even encouraging Murdock to carry on with his fight.[1]

Sometime later in life both Matt and Elektra were both taken by the TVA for unknown reasons, they would ultimately prune both of them and send them to the Void. When they were in the Void they found other heroes who were also there building a Resistance against the malevolent ruler of the Void, Cassandra Nova. On one of these occasions when the team were trying to stop her, Matt had apparently died in the process of trying to defend Elektra.[3]

Attributes

Powers

  • Superhuman Senses: Though Daredevil had lost his sight, his remaining four senses function with superhuman sharpness. As he puts it, his sense of sound gives off "a kind of Radar Sense". He trained his senses, with his acute sense of touch giving him superhuman both strength and balance.
  • Superhuman Hearing

Weaknesses

  • Intense Sound: Due to Daredevil's superhuman hearing, he is vulnerable to extremely loud noise, for example, when noises occur at a distance, they can interfere with his radar sense, causing him to perceive multiple individuals and objects simultaneously, making completely impossible for Daredevil to pin-point the location of any specific individuals or objects. But at close-range, loud noises can temporarily incapacitate Daredevil, leading to him feeling the intense physical pain in both of his ears.

Notes

  • Ben Affleck portrays Daredevil in the film Daredevil, while Scott Terra portrays a young Matt in the same film.
    • Affleck appears as Matt in a deleted dream sequence in Elektra.
  • The film novelization indicates that Matt Murdock lost his sight in 1984 at the age of 12, and notes that it was 19 years ago. This would make Murdock around 30 or 31 in the present-day of the film in 2003.

Trivia

  • This version of Daredevil was planned to appear in Deadpool & Wolverine before being cut from the film. In a concept art, he was supposed to fight the Deadpool Corps alongside the Resistance, including the surviving member Human Torch and Ghost Rider, the latter character being cut from the film entirely.[4] The sequence was ultimately scrapped due to budget constraints. Much like with Nicolas Cage, Deadpool actor Ryan Reynolds, tried to contact Ben Affleck to return in the role for the film, but was unsuccessful.[5]
