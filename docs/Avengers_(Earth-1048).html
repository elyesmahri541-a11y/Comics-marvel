History

The Avengers were an organization of heroes stationed at the Avengers Tower in Manhattan; however, due to their constant adventures, they were not around often to make much use of it. They fought Taskmaster at one point.[1]

Trivia

  • In Marvel's Spider-Man, after taking a picture of the Avengers Tower for the first time, Spider-Man says the following: "Avengers Tower is so cool... but they're never around to use it. I think they're somewhere in the West Coast right now. Probably surfing or something," which is also a nod to the West Coast Avengers.
  • In Marvel's Spider-Man 2, a flashback sequence involving a 15-year old Peter Parker sneaking into Midtown High School with Harry Osborn revealed that Parker grew up "worshipping" the Avengers, and had previously photographed Avengers Tower at some point before getting his powers.
