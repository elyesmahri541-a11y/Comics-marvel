The Void is the dark and destructive entity that bonded to Bob Reynolds when he became the Sentry, and acts as a counterforce and embodiment of his negative aspects. It is more of a force than a person but can take any shape it chooses. When talking to and teasing Sentry, it normally uses human form. He possesses enough power to destroy the entire universe and has stated an interest in spreading destruction beyond, to the Multiverse. Bob fights a constant inner battle to keep the Void at bay as it comes into being every time he uses his powers and is his eternal nemesis, born in his own psyche.

History

Origin[]

Though the exact origin of the Void is unknown, his first activities were seemingly traced all the way back to circa 1600 BCE, when Moses pleaded for every Hebrew doorpost to be marked with lamb's blood in order to protect the Jewish firstborn from the tenth and final plague God was going to bring on Pharaoh Ramses and on Egypt: the Angel of Death, believed to be the Void himself[12][9] (though other beings have been told to be that entity).

The Sentry[]

According to Lindy Reynolds,[12] Norman Osborn,[9] the Silver Surfer,[13] and the Void itself,[12] the Void is a dark entity that bonded to Robert Reynolds when he consumed the serum that turned him into the Sentry. However, according to Robert himself, the supposed first time the Void entered his heart was when he learned of the death of Laika, a little Russian dog who died on a Soviet Union space exploration mission. This was the first time Reynolds felt great sadness, a situation that would lead to the threat of the Void's emergence when the evil entity began to torment the future hero, who at the time was only seven years old.[7] From then on, the Void would only grow within his heart as Robert was forced to face many tragedies in his life which he was unable to do anything about.[14]

The Void next appeared a few years after the Sentry became famous; causing Scout to lose his left forearm when he nearly killed him, driving the Hulk into a rampage, and murdering over one million people in Manhattan.[citation needed] The Void also triggered a crime wave in New York's underworld by allying with Wilson Fisk, the Kingpin. Realizing this, the Sentry sought Spider-Man's help, given the hero's experience dealing with Fisk. The duo found Fisk in the company of the Void, but the situation was not what they had expected. Fisk was impaled by one of the Void's intangible tendrils. The Void was not working with the Kingpin, but rather using its powers to manipulate his mind. Fisk was saved by Spider-Man, who freed him from the tendril. During the fight, the Void caused Spider-Man to have visions of his own past, present, and future by piercing him with an intangible tentacle.[15] Upon discovering that the Void was a part of himself,[16] the creature of the blackest night being the counterpart of the golden guardian of good,[17] the Sentry teamed up with Reed Richards and Doctor Strange to create a system capable of making the Earth's entire population, themselves included, forget all about him. With the Sentry inactive and forgotten, the Void vanished.[16]

Years later, Robert's memories slowly returned along with the Void, who rampaged across Europe, murdering many members of the Super-Heroes of Europe. The Sentry contacted his former colleagues, but the only one who recalled their shared history was the Hulk. As the Void returned to Manhattan, the pieces began to fall in place and the Sentry's former friends assembled to defend him and the city. Once again, the Void's goal of wreaking destruction on a universal scale, and beyond, would be thwarted by the golden guardian of good, the Sentry. Remembering the dual nature of his powers, the Sentry and Reed Richards reactivated the Watchtower, dispelling the Void and making the world forget the Sentry once again.[18][19]

New Avengers[]

The Sentry was later confronted by the New Avengers and S.H.I.E.L.D., along with the Inhumans, the Fantastic Four and the X-Men. He cried, saying that he had warned them of the Void's coming and that it was too late now, before the group was suddenly attacked by the Void. Reed Richards showed Reynolds a recording he had made himself as a plea for help. He had asked that, should Mister Fantastic believe Reynolds was beyond saving, as a precaution against his powers spiraling out of control due to his current condition, Richards take measures such as sending him to the Negative Zone, launching him into space toward Skrull Empire territory, or aiming the Ultimate Nullifier at his head and using it. Hoping that he would eventually view the recording alongside Richards, Reynolds had instructed his future self to do whatever Richards and his allies said. Robert, with the help of Emma Frost, discovered that the reason no one remembered him was not because of his own attempts to erase the Void, but because of the intervention and manipulation of his mind by Mastermind, who was hired by the General to use his great mental powers to contain the Sentry's abilities and force everyone (himself included) to forget of his existence. Should the Sentry ever remember his life, he would be struck by a fear of the Void attacking the world. With this newfound knowledge, the Void disappeared, and Emma Frost resurrected the Sentry. The Avengers offered him membership, partly to keep an eye on him and partly because they would needed his power, and he accepted.[20]

In a battle against Yelena Belova, the Sentry had his powers absorbed by her. This would prove to be her downfall, however, as it also exposed her to a manifestation of the Void, who enveloped and incapacitated her. The Sentry told Belova that he would send the Void away if she answered his questions, but she was liquefied by her employers before she could answer any of them.[21]

When the Scarlet Witch used her powers to alter reality and create a world where mutants rose to power, the Sentry, along with many others, had his life and mind changed to fit the new reality. He was an ordinary man with a wife, Lindy, and a son. Reynolds began to question his sanity after mutants came to power. Having partially resisted the reality alteration, he remembered flying and possessing incredible superpowers, but this seemed to be a symptom of "Dead-End Syndrome"—a condition becoming common among humanity due to a sense of inadequacy following the rise of Magneto and mutantkind across the world. Unable to keep his job, he developed agoraphobia. At his wife's insistence, Reynolds began seeing a psychologist, Dr. Stephen Strange, who, prior to the reality shift, had been the great magic-user Doctor Strange. The two discussed his psychosis, but no significant progress was made. Like the Sentry, his dark counterpart, the Void, also resisted the reality shift and began to manifest as a wall of blackness that followed Robert everywhere. Reynolds believed this blackness was real but remained uncertain, unable to distinguish between reality and madness. Eventually, the Scarlet Witch altered reality once more, restoring everything to the way it had been before.[22][23]

Returned to the world, the Sentry captured the hearts of the public, with newspapers referring to him as the Golden Guardian as he saved hundreds of lives on a daily basis. Reynolds' psychological problems, however, had worsened. Unable to reconcile that Robert Reynolds, Sentry and the Void were the same being, the Sentry contained the Void in a vault in the Watchtower. At C.L.O.C.'s urging, Reynolds' psychiatrist Dr. Cornelius Worth entered the vault only to find a chair and a mirror. When Cornelius confronted Reynolds with this, Reynolds became confused and ran to the fairgrounds where he first gained his powers.[citation needed]

Cornelius followed Reynolds to the fairgrounds, finding the Sentry and the Void fighting. The Void claimed that Reynolds transformed into the Void rather than the Sentry, and that the Sentry was merely a guilt-borne byproduct of that transformation. With only half of Reynolds' memories (the Void possessing the other half), the Sentry was not sure whether the Void was wrong or not. The Sentry chased the Void away and then explained to Cornelius why he had hired him in the first place: Reynolds subconsciously wanted someone to reveal the Void's existence to the world so he would no longer have to hide that aspect of himself. Sentry then told Cornelius how he really gained his powers as a teenager: by stealing the Professor's serum and ingesting it to get high. He also told Cornelius that he knew all along that the Void was never in the Watchtower's vault. He knew that if Reynolds believed the Void was in the Watchtower, the Void would be. This kept the world safe from the Void for as long as Sentry could keep up the charade.[24]

The Sentry then visited Doctor Strange to determine if he was real or not. Strange tricked the Sentry into traveling to his old Professor's research facility where the two men confined the Sentry in an illusionary realm where he was a mental patient and superheroes did not exist in an attempt to prevent any further destruction. The Sentry broke free of the illusion and after confronting the Professor who had made the original serum, traveled to Antarctica to confront the Void and discover what memories of Reynolds the Void had in his mind.[25]

The Void revealed that Reynolds had actually ingested a super-saturated, exponentially more potent version of the Super-Soldier Serum that created Captain America. This was considered dangerous by the government because Sentry's blood could be used to create more of the serum, enough for the entire world. Several failed attempts were made to kill both personas. Enraged by this revelation, Sentry threw the Void into the Sun, telling his enemy that he no longer needed him to balance his own actions of good, but the Void promised to return.[26]

During the Secret Invasion of the Skrulls, the Sentry was manipulated by a Skrull disguised as the Void, making him believe that he himself had unintentionally caused the invasion as punishment for those who had forgotten him in the past. This caused the Sentry to abandon the fight and leave Earth. As a result, the Void created an equal and opposite reaction, appearing to fight the Skrulls and save Lindy Reynolds, promising to do anything the Sentry refused to do. Although the Void said he would take care of Lindy and embraced her, she was terrified in the presence of the evil entity.[27]

Dark Reign[]

After becoming head of national security, Norman Osborn visited the Sentry, , manipulating him psychologically and giving him a vial of the same serum that had originally granted him his powers. As soon as he drank it, the Void took control, though Osborn did not realize it, and stated that he owed him. Osborn agreed, noting that this was why the Sentry had become his secret weapon to defeat his enemies and keep his newly formed Cabal in line.[28][12]

When the Sentry participated in an Avengers attack on the X-Men, Emma Frost entered his mind, combining her mental powers with those of Charles Xavier, to stop him, as this seemed to be the only way to combat his extraordinary powers. While inside the Sentry's mind, Frost was attacked by the Void, whom she considered absolute evil,[29] but managed to withstand the assault by shifting into her diamond form. After a mental breakdown, she recovered. Although its diamond form granted it immense resistance to mental attacks, the presence of a Void sliver within her posed a significant risk. Consequently, Cyclops entered his beloved's mind with Xavier's assistance to aid her. The Void attempted to expel Cyclops but ultimately gave up and transferred itself into his mind instead.[30] The Void tried to persuade Cyclops to surrender control of his mind, promising to make him the leader he aspired to be by offering him power, strength, and greatness. It manifested as multiple versions of Jean Grey, knowing that taking the form of someone so significant to Cyclops could destabilize him. Frost entered Cyclops's mind to help him combat what Xavier called evil incarnate. With her help, and using the mental knowledge he had gained over the years as the romantic partner of Jean Grey, a powerful telepath, Cyclops managed to contain the Void sliver within a psychic prison inside his own mind.[31]

When the Sentry faced Absorbing Man, who had acquired the power of a Cosmic Cube, the Void was separated from him by the immense power Absorbing Man now wielded. The separation altered the appearance of both, reverting them to the forms they had held in the past. The Sentry immediately battled the Void, and the two merged once more as soon as the confrontation ended.[32]

When Lindy tried to kill her husband with an alien weapon, fearing what he was becoming and his refusal to let her go, she then began forming theories about the Void's origins. Unfortunately, the Void asserted control of her husband, reviving him. A terrified Lindy pleaded for the Void to end her misery, to which the evil entity complied, only for Robert to intervene, reminding his counterpart of their deal of not touching her. Robert flew far from Earth, intending to commit suicide by plunging himself into the Sun, only to regenerate. The Void then taunted his efforts and pointed out that for all intents and purposes, he should give him complete control. He returned to New York City and started to destroy it, but was convinced not to by Osborn. The Void agreed to work for Osborn. When the Cabal gathered, a disagreement arose between Osborn and Doctor Doom, leading Osborn to order the Void to kill him. The Void hurled Doom through a wall and struck him with a blast of energy after Osborn ordered him to attack again, following Doom's own energy blast against Taskmaster. However, that was not the real Victor von Doom. It was a robot sent by Doom to impersonate him. Several insect-like robots emerged from the Doombot. Osborn told the Void that he could not be seen, so he instructed the Void to let the Sentry take control. The Void protested but agreed.[33][34]

The Siege of Asgard[]

The intensity and brutality of the Siege of Asgard were steadily increasing the likelihood of the Void’s emergence. As a result, the yellow light emanating from a wound the Sentry had sustained while fighting Ares turned purple for a brief moment. Before long, the Void would take control.[35] The Void emerged after the Sentry was sent by Osborn to defeat Thor. Before the fight ended, Osborn, who had been subdued by the forces of the Avengers, the Secret Warriors, and many other heroes, unaware that the Void had taken control, ordered the Sentry to destroy Asgard. The Void broke the base of Asgard, completely destroying the city. Osborn, defeated and crazed, told the heroes that they would all be killed as the Void took on a monstrous form. After Loki helped the heroes by increasing their strength, the Void unleashed its wrath upon Thor's brother, killing him. Thor engaged the Void, but began to lose, until Iron Man took control of a damaged H.A.M.M.E.R. Helicarrier in the area and used it as a bullet to strike the Void. The Void was finally defeated when Thor struck it with a powerful attack from Mjolnir after Robert pleaded him to be killed. Thor then took the Sentry's charred body and tossed it into the Sun.[36] There, Robert's body was constantly regrown from an atom just to be burned down again afterwards. Bored of the cycle, the Void left him behind and went to the White Hot Room.[10]

At some point, the Void (still separated from Robert) was captured by Doctor Strange and locked up inside the Sanctum Sanctorum, from where he was later released by Strange himself to be used against Loki, who had become the new Sorcerer Supreme of Earth.[37] After revealing to Loki that the Exile of Singhsoon - a spell that transfers all the world's magic to its caster which Loki had been looking for - was inside Zelma Stanton's soul, the Void was separated from Strange. Loki and Strange fought together, but their magical attacks were practically useless against the Void. The two were saved by the arrival of the Sentry. Although the Void showed resistance, the three were able to imprison it once again within the Sanctorum.[38]

Sentry World[]

The Void was trapped within the Sentry World - a pocket dimension created inside Robert's psyche by Doctor Strange.[39] As a result of the machinations of his his former protégé, Billy Turner, Robert fully embraced the Void and became one with it, transforming into a dark version of the Sentry.[40] However, that didn't last long. The Void took over Robert again and steered him into conflict with Black Panther and the Agents of Wakanda in the small town of Pawhuska. While meditating, the Sentry, unwittingly, created an energy shield in the town that warped reality. The Void used this shield to sow chaos. Inside it, storms and visions of suffering manifested, alongside attacks by monsters created by the Void. Fat Cobra stabilized the Sentry by extracting a piece of the Void and consuming it.[41]

Scourge of the Negative Zone[]

Consequently, the Sentry sought out Mister Fantastic and begged him to free him from the Void forever. As there was no way to accomplish such a feat under the laws of their universe, Reed sent him to the Negative Zone in the hopes that he would succeed there.[5] Upon soaking up the negative cosmic rays, the Sentry separated from the Void but was left in the powerless form of Bob Reynolds, unable to transform into his superhero alter ego.[42] The explosion caused by their separation tore a hole in space that bridged the Negative Zone to the Cancerverse, a realm where nothing could die. As the Void shared a mutual interest with the Cancerverse forces,[13] he became their apparent new leader, all while impersonating and taking on the appearance of the Sentry.

Planning to return to his own universe to spread the Cancerverse's infection even further,[3] the Void tore a crack in reality made by Annihilus,[43] who used it to escape to the Positive Zone and seek help,[3] into a rift with his bare hands. The Cancerverse forces tried to infect a planet near the rift, but Beta Ray Bill stopped them at their tracks and sacrificed his hammer to trap the Void in the Negative Zone again before the rift closed itself.[43] While searching for the Human Torch,[44] who went to the Negative Zone with the rest of the Fantastic Four to help its inhabitants,[5] the Void ended up finding Nova instead. He defeated the hero and handed him to the Revengers, but Nova managed to escape them before they were able to infect him again.[44]

After Reed Richards opened a portal to assemble the galaxy's greatest heroes against the Cancerverse, a group of heroes held the Void still so the Silver Surfer could merge Bob Reynolds to him again, returning the Sentry to his blackened form. They hoped that Bob would call off the invasion once in control, but that wasn't possible as the Void - who now shared control of Reynolds' body with the Sentry - wasn't truly the Cancerverse's leader and it was now hellbent on getting Nova back to the fold. With no other option, Nova attracted the Cancerverse's infection back to the tear it was coming from and swallowed it whole. The merged Sentry then killed Nova, thus ending the scourge and closing the rift. While all the other heroes returned to the Positive Zone, the Sentry remained behind to watch over the universe he almost killed.[13]

King in Black[]

The Sentry was recalled to Earth to help fight Knull - the primordial dark god who had created the Symbiotes - and attempted to fly him into space and rip him in half as he once had Carnage. However, Knull easily overpowered the Sentry and ripped him apart. Unleashed from its mortal vessel, the Void submitted to Knull and was assimilated by him.[45] After Eddie Brock became the new host of the God of Light and turn into a cosmic version of Venom, he overpowered Knull. Using the Uni-Power, Venom destroyed Knull's body on the Sun and the living abyss inside of Dylan Brock that tried to take over his body, seemingly defeating the dark god and the Void with him.[11]

Return[]

Sentry eventually returned to life, and returned to Earth after having spend some time exploring the galaxy. Unfortunately, the Void came back as well, and as he traveled through space the Void devastated various alien civilizations, filling Robert with heavy guilt. The Void had in fact been gaining more control over Robert, as unlike previously, he began causing destruction which Robert had fix later. The Void would cause a lot of trouble for a powerful New York criminal, someone he hated.[7] The Void launched a wave of devastating attacks against Kingpin's criminal enterprise, inflicting massive losses on the crime boss and leaving him at death's door at Madison Hill hospital after infecting him with Ebola,[14] though he ultimately cured him, telling Fisk that he would see him again.[17]

Personality

Why hadn't you heard? I want it all... The entire universe and more. To devour the entirety of soulfood in this place... Hers first, perhaps? Perhaps last, just so you'll know it's coming. Every single one of them, Sentry... All the way through to you.

—The Void[source]
Being the dark side of the Sentry, the Void is the opposite of the hero Reynolds represents. In essence, he does the opposite of what the Sentry does. As Reynolds said, every time he uses his powers to try to save the world, the Void emerges to fulfill its purpose: to attack it.[46] His goal is destruction, stating that the world would end by his hand, being the bringer of destruction and death.[47] His thirst for destruction is so great that he stated he wanted to act in more than just the entire universe , meaning the Multiverse is also his target.[48] While the Sentry saw himself as the golden guardian of good, he saw the Void as the creature of the blackest night.[17] The Void is extremely brutal and cruel, as demonstrated when it broke several of Hulk's bones while insulting him by calling him the Sentry's pet monkey. Its hatred for Hulk stemmed from the Sentry loving his friend. As his name suggests, the Void is basically devoid of things like compassion, being a dark, destructive, and deceitful evil entity.[6] Charles Xavier considered him evil incarnate.[31] Although the Void hated the Sentry for preventing him from achieving his goal, considering making his wife, Lindy Reynolds, his first or last victim, whichever would cause the Sentry more suffering,[48] in a way, he loved the endless struggle between the two, going so far as to say that he loved him, and that due to the bond between them and the apparent inability of one to kill the other, the Void wanted the Sentry to consider him his friend. This twisted friendship that the Void had with the Sentry showed that the dark entity was capable of feeling some affection, despite its strong desire to do things like devour the Sentry's soul, put acid under its eyelids, and devour its dog.[26] The Void appeared to also have a soft spot for pets just like Robert, as during a catastrophe he was caused, he held a stray cat with him and made sure no harm came to it.[7]

Attributes

Powers

How our universe will end.

—Doctor Strange talking about the destructive potential of the Void's powers[source]

As the evil and dark counterpart of the Sentry, the Void possesses powers that make him a threat not only to the universe itself but also to the multiverse.[48] With limitless powers that make it omnipotent, often the only one truly capable of opposing the Void is the Sentry.[49] Teams of super-beings, such as the Avengers, the Fantastic Four, the X-Men, and the Inhumans, working together, have proven virtually useless against the vast powers of the dark entity.[47] Void himself compared himself to Galactus, the Devourer of Worlds, a being of great power.[12] The fact that the Sentry, the counterpart to the Void, fought Galactus to a standstill, who was fully powered at the time, makes this comparison more plausible, demonstrating that the Void’s powers are comparable to those of such beings.[50] The Void can draw power from external factors, such as the Negative Zone, making its powers, and consequently the threat it represents, greater.[6] The destructive potential of the Void's powers and its desire for chaos and destruction led the Sentry to believe that the risk of using the Ultimate Nullifier, a weapon capable of causing comparable destruction, against him, was worthwhile.[47]

  • Matter Manipulation: The Void possesses similar matter and Energy Manipulation abilities as the Sentry. Having used it to entrap the Avengers within an inescapable shroud of black matter he had conjured up, material easily able to hold the likes of Thor and Giant-Man with ease.[49] At a more concentrated level of these abilities, the Void can cause total atomic cancellation in a target, as it did when it pierced Loki with one of its tentacles and disintegrated him.[9]
  • Reality Warping: Much like the Sentry, the Void possesses a great capacity for reality warping. On one occasion, while the Sentry was meditating, he unknowingly created an energy shield in a small town in Oklahoma. The Void used this energy shield to manifest things such as storms and visions of suffering.[51]
  • Regeneration: The Void can heal himself from virtually all injuries.
  • Immortality: For all intents and purposes, the Void cannot and will not ever truly die even long after being hurled into the Sun by Robert[26] and later killed by a bolt of lightning by Thor.[52] That the dark aspect of the Sentry cannot expire in any sense, coming to be an existing entity all its own; it even went so far as to abandon the Sentry's body after becoming bored by the constant cycle of destruction and regeneration in the Sun.[10]
  • Resurrection: The Void appears to be able to resurrect himself and to bring the dead back to life.
  • Superhuman Strength: The Void's physical abilities are a direct mirror reflection of his counterpart.[26] He has enough physical power to easily man handle gods and monsters with little effort on his part, be it shattering the all but unbreakable bones of the Incredible Hulk[6] or even knocking around the Sentry through a host of carnival rides with relative ease.[1] He was even able to pry open an interdimensional portal linking the Posiverse and the Negative Zone with his bare hands.[43] A group of extremely strong beings, including Captain Marvel, Beta Ray Bill, Gladiator and others, had great difficulty holding back the Void for only a few moments.[13]
  • Warping Speed: Void also boasts the ability to think, move, run, and react at beyond superhuman speeds. As a thought effigy born of living shadow, he may actually be faster than his good side.[1] The Dark Sentry can appear anywhere at any given time across the world and beyond with or without the Sentry knowing it, capable of moving and traveling thousands to trillions of miles at the drop of a hat before returning to his creator's shattered psyche.[26]
  • Superhuman Stamina: Dark Sentry has an advanced musculature greater than that of any human and most known superhumans. Being a being of thought, his muscles produce no fatigue toxins, affording him all but unlimited physical stamina.[26]
  • Superhuman Agility: Void's natural balance, agility, and body coordination are enhanced to levels that are far beyond the natural physical limits of even the finest human athlete.[26]
  • Superhuman Reflexes: The Void can act and react at above superhuman speeds exceeding the equivalent of several times the speed of sound. As such, he/it is quick enough to match the highly adept Sentry in the art of superpowered fisticuffs with little difficulty.[26] Void is easily quick witted enough to counterattack Ares as he pounced on him from behind[53] and is capable of battling whole scores of Marvel's superhuman community with ease and without so much as a wasted movement.[54]
  • Superhuman Senses: It, like the Sentry, is able to hear almost any sound at any decibel, pitch, and frequency. The only Earth creature who can detect sounds at the frequencies he can is a blue whale (0.01-200,000 Hz). He has shown enough control to block out ambient sounds to focus on a specific source/frequency (He has demonstrated hearing a butterfly's heartbeat in Africa). The range of his eyesight is also far greater than that of a human being. He is able to detect a single person from hundreds of miles in the air and above cloud level. The full extent of Void's other senses superhuman capabilities is unknown.[55]
    • Sensory Scrying: The Void's senses are tied to those of the Sentry's senses. Everything Reynolds sees and does, it will know instantly.[26]
    • Clairvoyance: On its own, the Void also possesses extra sensory perception of unknown potency. Being more attuned with the world around him than just of the tangible spectrum, such as sensing if and when his human self has been disturbed by the presence of another force[54] or ascertaining what Loki had done to empower the heroes standing against him.[9] The Void appears capable of perceiving events across the timeline. When he pierced Spider-Man with one of his intangible tentacles, he caused the hero to experience visions of his own past, present, and future. He saw Gwen Stacy looking deeply sorrowful, a stark contrast to how he usually remembered her: happy and alive. Alongside this, he caught a glimpse of the Green Goblin, which seemed to foreshadow Stacy’s eventual death at the hands of the villain. He also saw a beautiful, green-eyed redhead whom he was destined to lose. It was Mary Jane Watson, though the hero had not yet met her.[15]
  • Nigh-Invulnerability: The Void is, for all intents and purposes, almost completely impervious to harm. He is highly resistant to magical attacks. When Doctor Strange and Loki Laufeyson confronted him, their attacks were unable to stop the dark entity. Their lives were saved by the Sentry, who joined the fight. Even combining his magical powers with the powers of the Void's luminous counterpart, the entity was able to offer resistance.[38] When the Scarlet Witch rewrote reality using chaos magic to create a world where mutants were dominant, the Void partially resisted, manifesting in the new reality as a wall of darkness that constantly pursued Robert Reynolds.[23][22]
  • Psionics: The Void possesses telepathic, empathetic, and possibly cyberpathic capabilities.[56] The level of which being unknown but quite possibly rivaling that of the Sentry himself.[15] Having once used such a faculty of his powers to taunt his heroic self through a television screen, showcasing some mildly impressive remote technological operation.[57] Attacking the Void's mind can be extremely dangerous. Besides being resistant to mental attacks, the Void can cause a mental breakdown in who attempts to do so. When Emma Frost entered the Sentry's mind, the Void attacked her, an attack that could have cost her her life if she hadn't remained in her diamond form.[58]
  • Flight: The Void also has the natural ability of aviation, used in conjunction with his superhuman speed to fly far above hyper sonic to trans-light speeds.[1] He can match Sentry in a contest of flight speed as well as sit stationary in mid-air if need be.[32][59] He can even fly at speeds high enough to outpace even a god of Asgard, battering him into near unconsciousness in a single stroke.[52]
  • Light Generation: While a dark element, The Void in tandem with the Sentry often emitted light within darkness.
  • Weather Manipulation: The Void has massive control over the weather and can create destructive storms.
  • Appearance Alteration: The Void is a highly adept shapeshifter, constantly changing and alternating his appearance on the fly. With varying forms ranging from a shadowy, trench coat-wearing villain to a shell skinned mega beast, monstrous flame effigy, an obese pustule atrocity, or massive hurricane of darkness.[54] He can even turn into a copy of the Sentry.[3][42]
  • Adaptive Possession: The Void is capable of hosting most any individual he desires and endowing them with his powers, as he did with Dr. Strange during his battle with Loki over his title of Sorcerer Supreme.[60]
  • Infini-Tendrils: The Void can create deadly infini-tendrils. Those impaled on these tendrils experience traumatic visions. The Tendrils are also capable of causing physical damage, for example, when they broke many of the Hulk's bones.
  • Darkness Manipulation: The Void has massive control over darkness.
    • Army Creation: The Void could create soldiers to form a shadow army.[41]
  • Intangibility: Sentry's dark personality can physically change and alternate molecular/atomic density and cohesion in order to phase through objects and people while inflicting tremendous pain on a subject. The Void demonstrated this ability by impaling many with its tentacles during the Siege of Asgard.[9]
  • Night Augmentation: The Void is at his strongest during the night.[citation needed]
  • Negative Zone Augmentation: Just as the Sentry can draw power from external factors like the Sun[49] and the Microverse,[61] the Void can do the same, with the Negative Zone being a great source of power for the dark entity.[6]
  • Biological Manipulation: Like its counterpart the Sentry, the Void has an as of yet untapped capacity to affect organic matter in undisclosed but seemingly unlimited ways. The Void persona uses such attributes to malignant ends, while his lighter half acclimates more beneficial ones. Its power over such aspects is so great that the Void afflicted a women with breast cancer a world away.[57] The Void used his powers to cause Wilson Fisk, the Kingpin, to contract Ebola and bring him to the brink of death, as well as to cure him.[14][17]

Abilities

Deception: The Void is very deceitful, especially with Sentry. This was demonstrated when he went back on the deal he made with the Sentry, which consisted of not harming his wife. He tried to justify breaking the deal with the weak argument that Lindy had asked him to kill her, although this had clearly been a result of his state of terror caused by the Void.[12]

Weaknesses

  • Magic: The Void can be sealed with powerful magic, as was done by Doctor Strange.[38]
  • Chi: The Void was proven to be vulnerable to spiritual energy attacks like Chi.[51]
  • Darkness Domination: Since the Void is technically a dark entity, someone with control over dark beings, such as the King in Black Knull, a deity, can bend it to their will.[45]

Notes

  • Void does not seem to have a defined form as such, being only an amorphous entity.
  • In Defenders (Vol. 6) #5, the Defenders travel back to the Third Cosmos and help destroy Anti-All, a primordial manifestation of nothingness, shattering it into countless fragments. Doctor Strange theorizes that the pieces of Anti-All's essence scattered across the Multiverse and that when the heroes fight against "the great Nothing" in their own times they're fighting back against Anti-All as well, implying that dark entities like the Void were born out of Anti-All's fractured essence.
    • When asked if Anti-All was the source of the Sentry's Void, Al Ewing responded that he would rather leave such matters up to interpretation than provide a boring answer that would just be retconned if he changed his mind or a later writer wanted to do something different.[62]

Trivia

  • The exact nature of the Void is unclear, as while it is intrinsically linked to the Sentry, it has existed as a separate entity on multiple occasions and was referred to by Norman Osborn as the Biblical angel of death.[9]
    • Lindy Reynolds, the Sentry's wife, had a theory that Bob accidentally opened himself up to something of Biblical proportions when he drank the drug that gave him his powers, even going as far as to identify this something as possibly being God. She, however, had no proof of it and thought it crazy, though the Void mockingly told Robert her theory was indeed true.[12]
  • Despite the Void having displayed some symbiote-like traits - such as appearing as a monster with fanged jaws, claws, and tentacles made of darkness,[63] and bonding to Dr. Strange to fight Loki - Donny Cates has stated that it is not a creation of Knull.[64] Despite this, in King in Black #1, the Void was shown being controlled by and bonding to Knull much like a symbiote, with Knull boasting that the Void serves him. This could be because, Knull could control both physical and metaphorical darkness, like the inner darkness of people.[65]
  • The Sentry not being in his dark form in King in Black #1 is a continuity error owing to Donny Cates and Ryan Stegman not having been aware of his fusion with the Void at the time the issue was written and illustrated, and Marvel's editorial department not correcting it prior to publication.[66]
