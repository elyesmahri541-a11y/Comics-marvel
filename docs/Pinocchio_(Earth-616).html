History

Pinocchio fought Jessica Drew on the palace on the orders of Dr. Page defeating her, later Wolverine came for revenge for Jessica and also fought with Pinocchio, Pinocchio also won the fight with the assistance of Dr. Page blinding Wolverine temporarily.[1]

Then it was revealed that Pinocchio was a robot created by Dr. Page and Broker Andover implanted with the mind of a young boy named Rick who was a childhood friend of Target.[2]

Later on when Wolverine came back to the palace they fight again until Target stopped them, realizing that it has Rick's mind, and turned Pinocchio against Dr. Page.[2]
