History

Fall from Grace[]

This Spider-Man's history mirrors that of his Earth-616 counterpart up to at least the Grim Hunt, where Peter saw a vision of a future where he killed Kraven the Hunter shortly after his resurrection. That decision made drastic changes to his life, making Peter darker and deadlier. He ended up hurting his loved ones and friends and was expelled from the Avengers.

He adopted a new attitude and costume as anti-hero and made choices like killing Doctor Octopus, passing his own tentacle through his heart.[3]

Despite Earth-616's Peter rejecting to kill Kraven, and thus seemingly assuring that he would never become that future version of himself, this Spider-Man was shown as part of Peter's possible future when he fought the Scions of Cyttorak later in his life, which could suggest that this Peter (or one similar to him) could still be one of his possible futures, branching off from a different point than the Grim Hunt now.[4]

Multiversal Adventures[]

At an unspecified point in his life, Peter was recruited by the S.H.I.E.L.D. of Earth-41940 into the Spider-Men, a group of Spider-Man analogs assembled to combat the Multiversal Sinister Six.[5] During the Totem War, Spider-Man joined Team Amazing as he ironically wasn't fond of the Superior Spider-Army's methods for dealing with the Inheritors.[5]

At some point, he joined Miguel O'Hara's Spider Society based on Earth-928. He was seen with Tarantula, LEGO Spider-Man and Spidercide as they got into Peter Parkedcar to be taken into the transporter room so they could aid in dealing with the Spot situation.[6]

Last Stand[]

Years later, Peter, an old man on the run from the NYPD, visited his Aunt May's grave, aware that his younger self was hiding in the shadows. Peter was confronted by the NYPD, and though he was given the choice to surrender and admit to his crimes, he refused. He fought the NYPD before finally being shot down and killed.[1]
