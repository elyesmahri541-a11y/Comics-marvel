The Spectacular Spider-Man is an animated superhero television series developed by Victor Cook and Greg Weisman, and co-produced by Marvel Entertainment. It follows the titular hero's early adventures while also trying to balance his high school life. The series' tone and style mainly draws inspiration from Stan Lee, Steve Ditko and John Romita Sr.'s work on The Amazing Spider-Man, though also includes elements from other Spider-Man media.

While five seasons were planned, the series was canceled after only two due to legal troubles between Disney and Sony Pictures Television. Despite this, it garnered critical acclaim for its writing, animation style, voice acting and faithfulness to its source material. Many fans refer to it as among the best Spider-Man adaptations.

Full Cast

Main[]

  • Josh Keaton as Peter Parker / Spider-Man
  • Lacey Chabert as Gwen Stacy
  • James Arnold Taylor as Harry Osborn
    • Taylor also voiced Frederick Foswell on the series
  • Daran Norris as J. Jonah Jameson
    • Norris also voiced John Jameson / Colonel Jupiter on the series
  • Vanessa Marshall as Mary Jane Watson
  • Joshua LeBar as Flash Thompson
  • Alanna Ubach as Liz Allan
  • Clancy Brown as Capt. George Stacy
    • Brown also voices Alex O'Hirn / The Rhino
  • Alan Rachins as Norman Osborn

Recurring[]

  • Benjamin Diskin as Eddie Brock / Venom
  • Dee Bradley Baker as Dr. Curt Connors / The Lizard
  • Deborah Strang as May Parker
  • Steve Blum as the Green Goblin

  • Grey DeLisle as Betty Brant and Sally Avril
  • Cree Summer as Glory Grant
  • Peter MacNicol as Otto Octavius / Doctor Octopus
  • Kevin Michael Richardson as L. Thompson Lincoln / Tombstone

  • Phil LaMarr as Randy Robertson and Robbie Robertson
  • Ed Asner as Ben Parker
  • Andrew Kishino as Kenny Kong
  • Tricia Helfer as Felicia Hardy / Black Cat
  • John DiMaggio as Hammerhead and Flint Marko / Sandman
  • Thom Adcox-Hernandez as Phineas Mason / The Thinker
