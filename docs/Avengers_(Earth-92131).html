History

The Avengers were mentioned along with the Fantastic Four and the Defenders as Spider-Man entered a sewer to track down the Lizard and complained that this never happened to said teams.[1]

After X-Force freed several young mutant prisoners from X-Factor's Helicarrier [2], the Avengers faced off against them near a mountain. After a brief battle, both sides agreed to disagree on what was to be done and went their separate ways.[3]
